# NU7

> NU7 is the next Zcash network upgrade. Mainnet activation is targeted for November 5, 2026, and the activation block height is set on October 20.

In a coinholder survey from August 25 to September 14, 2026, about 2.4 million ZEC voted on the main choices for NU7. The infographic shows what they backed.

![NU7 coinholder survey results: about 2.4M ZEC took part and the 1,000,000 ZEC threshold was reached. Coinholders backed 25-second blocks, kept the halving schedule, disabled Sprout v4 transactions at NU7 and set NSM reissuance to begin in February 2031. Mainnet activation is targeted for November 5, 2026.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu7-coinholder-survey.png)

- **Hold Sprout funds?** Move them before activation, since version 4 transactions stop working at NU7. See [Recovering Funds](../using-zcash/recovering-funds).
- **Faster blocks:** [What 25-second blocks actually change](../research/what-25-second-blocks-actually-change)
- **How the vote stayed private:** [Shielded Coinholder Voting](../zcash-tech/shielded-coinholder-voting)

The survey is non-binding, and the rules change through ZIPs. Scope: [ZIP 259](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0259). Dates: [forum announcement](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/nu7-timeline/57655).
