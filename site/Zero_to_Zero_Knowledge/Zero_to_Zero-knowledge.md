# Zero to Zero Knowledge

A short series on the ideas behind private digital cash. Start at the top.

| # | Topic | What you will learn |
|---|-------|---------------------|

| 3 | [Zcash Addresses](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/zcash-addresses) | Transparent and shielded transactions, and Unified Addresses. |
| 4 | [Trusted Execution Environments](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/tees) | Hardware that keeps computation private, and where it falls short. |
