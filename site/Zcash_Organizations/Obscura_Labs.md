## Mission Statement

Obscura Labs is an independent organization registered in Nigeria with a strategic focus on Africa and other emerging markets.

Its mission is to build infrastructure, tools, and practical adoption pathways that improve the accessibility, utility, decentralization, and long-term resilience of the Zcash ecosystem across Africa.

The organization aims to support the use of privacy-preserving financial technology by improving access to reliable infrastructure, supporting developers and businesses, educating users, and strengthening local Zcash communities.

## Background

Obscura Labs was founded by Chidi Olisa, the lead of Zcash Nigeria.

Before establishing Obscura Labs, Chidi contributed to the Zcash ecosystem through community building, education, advocacy, localized content creation, user support, and initiatives designed to introduce people in Nigeria and other parts of Africa to financial privacy and Zcash.

Engagement with users, students, developers, businesses, and community members highlighted several barriers to broader Zcash adoption across Africa. These included limited infrastructure, difficulty accessing ZEC through local currencies, insufficient developer support, a shortage of localized educational resources, and limited integration with everyday payment systems.

Obscura Labs was created in response to these challenges.

The organization is based on the view that awareness alone is not sufficient for sustainable adoption. Communities also require reliable infrastructure, accessible products, developer tools, financial access, education, and long-term institutional support.

## Vision

Obscura Labs vision is to help make Africa one of the strongest regions for Zcash infrastructure, adoption, and ecosystem growth.

The organization aspires to become a leading African contributor to the Zcash ecosystem by:

* Operating reliable Zcash infrastructure.
* Improving access to ZEC and privacy-preserving payments.
* Supporting users, developers, businesses, and community organizations.
* Building practical tools and services.
* Encouraging the development of sustainable Zcash communities.
* Increasing the geographic diversity of Zcash infrastructure and ecosystem participation.

## Strategic Areas

Obscura Labs initially plans to focus its work across four strategic areas.

### Infrastructure Development

Reliable infrastructure is necessary for wallets, applications, developers, exchanges, payment services, and users to interact with the Zcash network.

Obscura Labs operates and develops Zcash infrastructure within Africa to improve network accessibility, geographic diversity, reliability, and resilience. This work is intended to reduce reliance on a small number of infrastructure operators and provide dependable services for users and developers in African markets.

Infrastructure initiatives may include:

* Public Zcash mainnet RPC endpoints.
* Public Zcash testnet RPC endpoints.
* Zebra full nodes.
* Lightwalletd services.
* DNS seeders.
* Public infrastructure monitoring.
* Developer-focused APIs.
* Integration tools and documentation.
* Additional services that reduce the barriers to building on Zcash.

By making infrastructure more accessible, Obscura Labs aims to support developers, wallets, applications, businesses, and services integrating with the Zcash ecosystem.

### Adoption and Financial Access

For Zcash to be useful in everyday situations, people must be able to acquire, store, use, and exchange ZEC without unnecessary friction.

Obscura Labs supports initiatives intended to improve:

* Access between ZEC and African local currencies.
* Fiat on-ramps and off-ramps.
* Merchant adoption.
* Payment integrations.
* Cross-border payments.
* User onboarding.
* Wallet accessibility.
* Practical use of ZEC by individuals and businesses.

Particular attention is given to services that address challenges commonly experienced in African markets, including currency instability, financial exclusion, expensive cross-border transfers, limited access to global payment systems, and increasing financial surveillance.

## Current Initiatives

Although Obscura Labs is in an early stage of development, several initiatives have already begun.

### African DNS Seeder Infrastructure

Obscura Labs operates DNS seeder infrastructure from Africa.

DNS seeders help new Zcash nodes discover peers when connecting to the network. Increasing the geographic distribution of seeders contributes to the decentralization and resilience of the Zcash peer-to-peer network.

### Zebra Node Infrastructure

The organization operates Zebra nodes within Africa.

Zebra is an independent implementation of the Zcash consensus protocol. Operating Zebra infrastructure contributes to network accessibility, implementation diversity, and the geographic distribution of Zcash nodes.

### Lightwalletd Services

Obscura Labs operates Lightwalletd infrastructure to support Zcash wallet connectivity.

Lightwalletd provides blockchain data to light-client wallets without requiring each wallet user to operate a complete Zcash node. Reliable Lightwalletd services are therefore important for mobile wallets and other lightweight Zcash applications.

### Infrastructure Monitoring

Obscura Labs is developing public monitoring and status systems for its infrastructure.

These systems are intended to improve operational transparency by allowing users and developers to check the availability and status of publicly accessible services.

### ZecFiat

ZecFiat is an Obscura Labs initiative focused on improving access between ZEC and African local currencies.

The project is intended to reduce barriers for individuals and businesses seeking to acquire, use, or exchange ZEC. Its broader objective is to make Zcash more practical within markets where access to cryptocurrency services and international financial systems may be limited.

Further information about ZecFiat’s structure, supported markets, availability, and operating model is expected as the initiative develops.
