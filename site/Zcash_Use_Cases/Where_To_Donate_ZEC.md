> Last checked: October 27, 2026, on each organization's own donate page. Addresses change, so this page links to the donate pages instead of copying addresses. Always copy the address from the organization's own site. Tax notes are what each organization says about itself, not tax advice.

## TL;DR

- Nine nonprofits publish a ZEC address. Five of them use a shielded address, so your gift stays private on-chain
- Four well-known nonprofits accept ZEC only at a transparent address: EFF, Freedom of the Press Foundation, the Tor Project and the Internet Archive
- Donation platforms like The Giving Block and Every.org accept ZEC for thousands of charities, but none of them says it supports shielded ZEC
- If you need a tax receipt, check each organization's note below before you give

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-lock-2f8e221321.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="lock icon"/> Shielded or transparent: why it matters

When you send ZEC from a shielded wallet to a **shielded** address, the amount, the sender and the receiver all stay private.

When you send to a **transparent** address, the gift is visible on the blockchain: anyone can see the amount, when it arrived and the organization's balance. Your shielded wallet's other activity stays private, but the gift itself is public. See [The Turnstile](/zcash-tech/the-turnstile) for what becomes public when ZEC leaves a shielded pool.

Both still support the organization. Shielded just keeps the gift private.

## <img src="/content-images/chain-for-links-svgrepo-com-117ee0dec1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="chain-links icon"/> Donation platforms

Platforms accept ZEC on behalf of many charities, usually convert it to dollars, and send you the receipt. They are an easy way to give ZEC to a charity that doesn't hold crypto itself. None of them says it accepts shielded ZEC, so expect to send from or to a transparent address.

| Platform | What it is | ZEC |
|---|---|---|
| [The Giving Block](https://thegivingblock.com/resources/cryptocurrency-we-accept/) | Crypto donation service used by many US nonprofits | Listed |
| [Every.org](https://support.every.org/hc/en-us/articles/1500007829861-What-cryptocurrencies-coins-and-exchanges-does-Every-org-support) | Donation platform for US charities | Listed |
| [The Signatry](https://thesignatry.com/asset-giving/cryptocurrency/) | Christian donor-advised fund | Mentioned on its crypto page; ask them first |

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> How to give

1. Pick an organization above and open its donate page
2. Copy the ZEC address from that page, not from this wiki or a screenshot
3. Check the first and last few characters after you paste it
4. If you want a receipt, add your email in the memo (shielded only) or follow the organization's note above
5. Send from a [shielded wallet](/wallets)

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Common mistakes to avoid

- Copying an address from a forum post, social media or an old screenshot instead of the organization's own page
- Sending from an exchange, which can link the gift to your account
- Expecting a tax receipt without checking the organization's note first
- Sending to a Sapling address from a wallet that can't send to Sapling
