## Ìwádìí àti ìdàgbàsókè

Ẹgbẹ naa ti ṣe itọsọna awọn aṣeyọri idanimọ ni ọpọlọpọ igba, akọkọ jẹ [ifilọlẹ Zcash](https://electriccoin.co/blog/zcash-begins/) gẹ́gẹ́ bí ìlo àwọn ẹ̀rí tí kò ní ìmọ̀ fún ọjà lágbàáyé.

Diẹ diẹ sii tẹle bi awọn [Sapling nẹtiwọki igbesoke](https://electriccoin.co/blog/sapling-activation-complete/) allowing for lighter, shielded transactions with a time reduction of 90% for constructing transactions, and a memory reduction of over 97%, enabling for shielded transactions on a mobile device for the first time.

Ìgbésẹ̀ tó ṣe pàtàkì jùlọ tí wọ́n gbé ni èyí tó ṣẹṣẹ̀ wáyé. [Ìṣèdá àti lílo àwọn ohun èlò náà](https://electriccoin.co/blog/nu5-activates-on-mainnet-eliminating-trusted-setup-and-launching-a-new-era-for-zcash/) ti halo2 trustless proving system in Network Upgrade 5 for Zcash. Eyi yọ igbaradi igbẹkẹle kuro o si ṣe ifilọlẹ akoko tuntun fun Zcash . Niwon 2022, eyi ti n ṣiṣẹ lori Mainnet Zcash ati gba awọn olumulo laaye lati lo aabo diẹ sii & iṣiro to rọrun julọ fun ilana isanwo wọn. 

## Ìfọ̀rọ̀wánilẹ́nuwò àti Àjọṣepọ̀ 

Ẹgbẹ ECC ṣe atilẹyin ati ṣetọrẹ si ilolupo eda abemi Zcash ni ọpọlọpọ awọn ọna, ṣiṣẹpọ pẹlu mejeeji inu & ẹgbẹ ita nigbagbogbo lati ṣe iranlọwọ tọju ati mu ilọsiwaju ilana ti Zcash. 

Partnerships include working with Gemini to enable shielded transactions on their platform, Flexa to enable easy Zcash mobile payments at retal locations, hardware wallet companies like Keystone to enable shielded cold storage of ZEC, Brave for Zcash support in the browser, & other teams in the Zcash ecosystem to bring imrovements to Zcash. 

ECC tun jẹ onigbọwọ igberaga ti [PGP fun Crypto](https://x.com/pgpforcrypto) a ṣe àpérò tí ó kó àwọn ògbóǹkangí, olùfẹ́ àti onímọ̀ ní àyè cryptocurrency láti jíròrò ìdàgbàsókè, pínpín ìmọ̀ràn, kí wọ́n sì gbé ìgbésẹ̀ fún àjọṣepọ̀ àwùjọ. PGPforCrypto ni olórí rẹ̀ jẹ ọmọ ẹgbẹ́ Board ati VP of Strategic Alliances at Electric Coin Co., Paul Brigner .

## Ìlànà àti Òfin

Ìjìnlẹ̀ òye àti ipò adarí tí ECC ní nípa ìpamọ́ra nínú ẹ̀rọ-ìpèsè àtàwọn ohun tó ń ṣe fún ààbò ara ẹni àti ti orílẹ̀ èdè jẹ́ èyí tó wúlò gan an fáwọn olùṣe ìlànà. A ti sa gbogbo ipá láti darí ètò ìṣèlú lọnà yíyẹ níbi tí ó bá ṣeé ṣe látìgbà tí a dá ECC sílẹ̀. 

Examples of outreach include dialogue with Financial Action Task Force (FATF) taking part in meetings which resulted in the organizations recommendations for the cryptocurrency sector. As a result of these efforts Zcash became approved by the New York State Department of Financial Services (NYDFS), the only privacy-protecting cryptocurrency to do so.
