# Bí Zcash ṣe wà ní ètò-ìpínlẹ̀ rẹ̀

## TL;DR

- Zcash kìí ṣe ilé-iṣẹ́ kan ló kọ ó, àwọn àjọ tó dá wà tí kò sí ẹnìkan nínú wọn ni wọ́n kó o jọ èyí sì ní apá ọ̀tọ̀ọ̀tọ̣ lára iṣẹ́ náà.
- Fun julọ ti awọn oniwe-itan meji ajo asiwaju idagbasoke, ni Electric Coin Company ati Zcash Foundation
- Ní January 2026 gbogbo ẹgbẹ́ Electric Coin Company fiṣẹ̀ sílẹ̀ lẹ́yìn àríyànjiyàn nípa ìṣàkóso, àti ètò ìsálú ayé tún ṣe sí àwọn ẹgbẹ́ aládé tí ó dá dúró bíi mélòó kan.
- Loni awọn ilana, awọn node software, apamọwọ, iwadii, scaling, ati owo ti wa ni mu nipa lọtọ ẹgbẹ
- Kò sí àjọ kan ṣoṣo tó ń ṣàkóso Zcash, àkànṣe ìsọfúnni ni ó wà ní ṣíṣí sílẹ̀ tí kò sì gba àṣẹ kankan. Ó sì máa ń ṣiṣẹ́ bó ṣe yẹ nígbà gbogbo lákòókò yípadà yìí.

<br/>

## Ta ni èyí wà fún?

- Àwọn tí wọ́n ṣẹ̀ṣẹ̀ dé ń gbìyànjú láti mọ ẹni tó ṣe Zcash àti àwọn tó máa ń tọ́jú rẹ̀.
- Ẹnikẹni ti o ba dàrú nipa ọpọlọpọ awọn orukọ ajọṣepọ ninu eto ilolupo eda abemi.
- Àwọn olùkópa tí ó ń pinnu ẹni ti wọn yóò bá ṣiṣẹ́ tàbí ibi tí wọ́n á rán àbá kan lọ.

<br/>

## Ìdí tí èyí fi ṣe pàtàkì

Ifá pé ó yé ewéko náà á mú kí gbogbo nǹkan rọrùn. Ó sọ fún ọ ẹni tí ń tọ́jú kóòdì tó o gbára lé, ta ni láti súnmọ́ fún owó ìrànwọ́ àti ti ó jẹ̀bi apá kan nínú nẹtiwòrìkì tí ìwọ bìkítà nípa rẹ. O tún fi hànkan lára àwọn agbára Zcash: nítorí iṣẹ́ náà pín sí orí àwùjọ aládàáni, kò si ibi ìkọlù kankan lè gba tàbí dá ètò náà dúró.

Ojúewé yìí jẹ́ àwòrán ojú-ìpín. fún àjọ kọ̀ọ̀kan tí ó ti ní ojúewé kan tó kún lórí wiki, ẹ óò rí àlàyé ṣókí àti ìjápọ láti ka àwọn nǹkan mìíràn dípò kí a tún ohun tí wọ́n kọ níbẹ̀ sọ padà.

## Bí ó ṣe máa ń ṣiṣẹ́ nígbà kan rí

Ní gbogbo ìgbà tí Zcash ti ń gbèrú, àjọ méjì ló wà nídìí rẹ̀.

The Electric Coin Company launched Zcash in 2016 and employed much of the core development team. It was overseen by Bootstrap, a nonprofit board created to support Zcash. The Zcash Foundation worked alongside it as an independent nonprofit, focused on stewardship of the protocol and on building an independent node. Both were funded largely by a portion of the block reward set aside for development.

This two-pillar structure held for years, but it depended on that shared funding and on the two organizations staying aligned. As the original development funding evolved and its long term future became less certain, the question of how to pay for ongoing work grew more pressing. That funding question sits in the background of much of what changed next, and it is part of why some teams now raise outside capital while others rely on grants.

<br/>

## Àtúntò ọdún 2026

Ní January 2026, ètò náà yí padà pátápátá. ní 7 January, olórí ilé-iṣẹ́ Electric Coin Company Josh Swihart kéde lórí X pé gbogbo ẹgbẹ́ iléeṣẹ́ ti fi ipò sílẹ̀.

Bootstrap was a nonprofit created in 2020 to govern the Electric Coin Company, which had become a wholly owned subsidiary of it. The disagreement between the company team and this board built up over time and touched several issues, including the direction of the organization, how development should be funded, and the future of the Zashi wallet, which the team wanted to move into a private company to raise outside capital. Swihart described the departure as a constructive discharge, a legal term meaning conditions were changed so severely that resignation was effectively forced, and said a majority of the board had moved out of alignment with Zcash's mission.

The other side of the account matters for fairness. Bootstrap framed the conflict as a matter of governance and nonprofit legal compliance. Zcash's founder, Zooko Wilcox, publicly defended the board members named in the dispute, saying he had worked with them for many years and regarded them as people of high integrity, while making clear he was not taking a side on the disagreement itself.

Two things were not in dispute. No party alleged any criminal conduct, so this was a corporate and governance disagreement rather than a legal case. And the Zcash network itself was unaffected, it stayed open source, permissionless, secure, and fully operational throughout, a point both Swihart and Wilcox stressed to users.

Ohun ti o tẹle ni atunṣe dipo idaamu. Ẹgbẹ ile-iṣẹ iṣaaju lọ lati ṣe agbekalẹ ZODL nigbamii ni 2026, ati lọtọ awọn ọmọ ẹgbẹ igbimọ Bootstrap mẹta tẹlẹ ṣẹda Sovright . Idagbasoke yanju sinu apẹrẹ pinpin diẹ sii kọja ọpọlọpọ awọn ẹgbẹ ominira.

Àwọn ìkéde tí a ṣàpèjúwe níbí yìí ni Josh Swihart (@jswihart) àti Zooko Wilcox (@zooko), ṣe láwùjọ lórí X, January 7, 2026, níbi ti ẹ lè ka gbogbo àwọn àkọlé náà.

All of these organizations maintain open source repositories, so their work can be read, checked, and built on by anyone. And the organizations are not the whole story. Many significant contributions come from individuals and from contracted companies funded through grants, rather than from the core organizations alone. Alongside them sit wallet teams, regional communities, independent developers, and investors who hold and support ZEC without building the protocol. The list above is the backbone, not the full picture.

## Ibi tó yẹ kó o ti bẹ̀rẹ̀ sí í ṣe òṣìṣẹ́ tuntun ni.

Ìjọ wo ló ṣe pàtàkì sí ọ, ó sinmi lórí ohun tó o fẹ́ láti máa ṣe.

1. Lati lo Zcash, o nilo apamọwọ kan, nitorinaa ZODL ati apamọ rẹ jẹ aaye ibẹrẹ ti ara ẹni.
2. To run a node or understand the network software, look to the Zcash Foundation and its Zebra node.
3. Láti fi owó ṣètìlẹyìn fún iṣẹ́ kan tàbí láti ṣe ìtìlẹyìn iṣẹ́ tí a sanwó sí, wo Zcash Community Grants.
4. Láti tẹ̀lé ìwádìí àti ọjọ́ iwájú ìlànà náà, ẹ tọ àwọn ilé-ìdánwò Shielded Labs, Àkànṣe Tachyon, àti Ẹgbẹ Valar.

## Àwọn èrò òdì tó wọ́pọ̀ nípa àwọn èèyàn

- Zcash kì í ṣe ohun ìní tàbí tí ó wà lábẹ́ àkóso àjọ kan ṣoṣo, kò sí ètò kankan tó lè yí ẹ̀rọ náà padà tàbí kó dá a dúró fúnra rẹ̀.
- Àríyànjiyàn 2026 kò ní ipa lórí ẹ̀rọ, owó tàbí ìpamọ́ra. Ìdàgbàsókè ètò ni ó jẹ́ àti pé ìlànà náà ń ṣiṣẹ bó ṣe yẹ láàrín gbogbo àkókò tí a fi wà nínú ilé ìṣọ́ yìí.
- The team leaving the Electric Coin Company did not end Zcash, the work moved to new independent organizations
- Níní àwọn àjọ púpọ̀ jẹ́ okun, kìí ṣe àìlera. Ó ń mú ìkùdíẹ̀ káàtó kúrò ó sì máa ń jẹ́ kí iṣẹ́ náà dúró sán-ún
- Ṣiṣakoso tabi igbega ZEC kii ṣe kanna bi kikọ Zcash, awọn oludokoowo ati awọn ajíhìnrere jẹ apakan ti agbegbe ṣugbọn o yatọ si awọn ẹgbẹ ti o dagbasoke ilana naa.
