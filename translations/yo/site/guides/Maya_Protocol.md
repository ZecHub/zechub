## Kí ni Maya Protocol?

Maya jẹ́ ọmọbìnrin kan tó ń gbé nílùú New York. [àdàpọ̀ tí kò dáwọ́ lé](https://nym.com/blog/what-is-dex) (DEX) system that enables trading of cryptocurrencies across different blockchains. You can, for example, swap Bitcoin (BTC) on the Bitcoin blockchain with Ethereum (ETH) on the Ethereum blockchain in an easy way, without holding the assets or involving any centralized authorities or Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures.

Maya Protocol ni a ṣe agbekalẹ nipa lilo Cosmos Software Development Kit (Cosmos SDK) ati ṣiṣẹ lori Ẹri ti Bond (PoB) ilana ifọkanbalẹ. Atilẹyin naa jẹ nipasẹ "Node Operators", ẹniti o npa olu sinu eto naa ki o si gba awọn ipadabọ bi ẹsan fun alabapin wọn ati igbiyanju wọn. Ni pataki, awọn akopọ jẹ kọnputa ṣiṣe sọfitiwia ti o fọwọsi paṣipaarọ olumulo ati abojuto ohun-ini ninu awọn adirẹsi ti a yan kọja oriṣiriṣi blockchains .

To complete a swap, the supported cryptocurrency must be received in one of Maya's addresses, sent by a user, and then an equivalent amount is sent from another of Maya's addresses on a different blockchain. This process is managed and approved by at least two-thirds of the nodes, particularly ensuring that the funds are properly received.

Ni ọna yii, awọn olumulo le firanṣẹ iru ami kan lori blockchain kan ki o gba oriṣiriṣi oriṣi miiran lori ẹlomiran blockchains, gbogbo abinibi ati laisi lilo awọn aami ti a fi sinu.

## Kí Ni Ẹ̀rí Ìdè?

Proof of Bond (PoB) jẹ́ ètò ìfọwọ̀kan tí àwọn oníṣẹ́-òpó ní láti ṣe àdéhùn ìdánilójú kan (ní ọ̀nà ti àdàkọ ẹbí nẹtiwọọki náà) kí wọ́n lè kópa nínú àjọṣepọ̀. Idaduro yìí ń ṣiṣẹ́ bí ọ̀nà ìṣúnná owó, ó sì mú un dáni lójú pé àwọn nodes máa hùwà láìlábòsí àti tọpinpin àìlálèébù àwùjọ.2 Bí nódù bá gbìyànjú láti hu ìwà burúkú tàbí kìíṣe iṣẹ́ rẹ̀ dáadáa, a le gé idaduro rè é kúrò, èyí túmọ̀ sí wípé apá ibì kan lára è ni yóò di ohun ìyàtọ̀ fún wọn.3

In Maya Protocol, this mechanism helps to produce economic value from the staked resources of node operators, increasing capital efficiency. Similarly, in Thorchain, node operators bond RUNE (the native token) to secure the network and ensure cooperation among participants.

## Ìyàtọ̀ láàrin Maya àti THORChain

Maya jẹ ẹ̀ka ti THORChain ṣùgbọ́n ó kún fún àwọn àfikún àti iṣẹ́-ṣiṣe tuntun tí wọ́n ń ṣiṣẹ́ bí ọ̀nà yíyẹ. Àwọn tó ṣe pàtàkì jùlọ ni:

### Àwọn Ìkànnì Owó-ìṣúná

Rather than following the Pure Bond Model, Maya is contemplating a shift to a Liquidity Nodes model. In this system, nodes are enabled to directly contribute liquidity, bonding it to the network. This approach means node operators face a significant risk: if they misuse funds, they incur losses, acting as a powerful deterrent. As a result, node operators use Liquidity Units from Liquidity Pools, which simultaneously provide liquidity and bolster network security.

### Ààbò Ìdánù Tí Kò Dúró Ṣinṣin

Eto ti o daabobo awọn olupese oloomi lati isonu igba diẹ (LPs) wọn le ni iriri nigbati wọn ba pese oloomi, nitori iyipada nigbagbogbo ninu awọn idiyele ti awọn ohun-ini crypto.
ILP ni 10% ti ipese $ CACAO (10 million $CACA O) ati pe o tun ṣe atunṣe nigbagbogbo nipasẹ 10% awọn owo-owo ilana. ILP di lọwọlọwọ 50 ọjọ lẹhin idogo iṣuna, pẹlu idaabobo to 100%.

Iye akoko ti ILP bo da lori iṣẹ-ṣiṣe ASSET ati $ CACAO. A ṣe iṣeduro kikun lẹhin ọjọ 150 bi ASSETS ba ṣiṣẹ dara julọ, ati lẹhin awọn ọjọ 450 ti o ba jẹ pe $ CCAO nṣiṣẹ daradara. ILP ni a sanwo mejeeji ki o tun pada sipo nigbati idasilẹ pipe ṣugbọn ko ni ipa nipasẹ awọn yiyọ kuro diẹ ninu apakan. Fun afikun, ILP ti wa ni atunbere ṣugbọn kii ṣe isanwo.

### Àpẹẹrẹ ìkápá tó yàtọ̀ sí ti tẹ́lẹ̀

The Liquidity Auction was a 21-day event designed to distribute $CACAO tokens among participants. During the event, users deposited supported assets to a specific address. At the conclusion of the auction, 90% of the $CACAO tokens were allocated to participants in proportion to their liquidity contributions, while the remaining 10% was allocated to the ILP reserve. The participants became liquidity providers, with their deposited assets and $CACAO tokens placed into Maya's pools, enabling them to earn a share of the generated fees.

### Ọ̀nà Mìíràn Láti Máa Lo Àwọn Àkáǹtì Owó Ìpamọ́

At the genesis of Maya Protocol, the available CACAO reserves were only 10% of the total supply, compared to 44% for THORChain, and were primarily intended for Impermanent Loss Protection (ILP). Maya does not have block emissions; and if Protocol Owned Liquidity and Lending are implemented, they will feature a different design, as in THORChain, these aspects are closely integrated with the Reserves.

Síbẹ̀síbẹ̀, láìfi ìyàtọ̀ rẹ̀ pè Maya tún ṣiṣẹ́ gẹ́gẹ́ bí àfikún ojútùú sí THORChain, ó ńfúnni ní ìmúpọ̀jù, ìtẹsiwaju àti ìdánilójú, tí yóò sì ṣe àkópọ̀ àwọn nẹtiwọọki tuntun ti kò si nínú ìṣèṣe THORCHAIN tó wà lọ́wọ́lọ́wọ.

Pẹlupẹlu, ibi-afẹde Maya ni lati di * backend * fun awọn iṣẹ miiran lati kọ lori rẹ, pẹlu ireti ti ri ọpọlọpọ awọn titun * frontends*, tabi awọn iṣẹ DEX ti a ṣe sori amayederun Maya.
