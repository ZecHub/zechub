Ndị ọrụ nwere ike iziga/nata ZEC ugbu a n'ebughị ikpughe nguzozi ma ọ bụ metadata - ha niile n'ime ihe nchọgharị Brave.

## Akụkụ 1: Ịtọlite Brave Wallet

**Nzọụkwụ ndị a kpuchiri:**

1. Mepee ihe nchọgharị Brave (naanị desktop ugbu a)

2. Pịa akara ngosi Wallet ma ọ bụ gaa na brave://wallet

3. Tọọ e-akpa ego gị (mepụta ma ọ bụ bubata)

4. Nweta dashboard crypto

## Akụkụ 2: Ịgbakwunye Zcash (ZEC) na Brave Wallet

**Nzọụkwụ ndị a kpuchiri:**

1. N'ime dashboard Wallet, pịa "Manage Assets"

2. Chọọ ZEC ma mee ka ọ rụọ ọrụ

3. ZEC apụtala ugbu a n'ime e-akpa ego gị

## Akụkụ 3: Ịnata ZEC nke e chebere echebe (Adreesị Ejikọtara Ọnụ)

**Nzọụkwụ ndị a kpuchiri:**

1. Pịa ZEC n'ime akụ gị

2. Họrọ "Receive"

3. Brave na-emepụta Adreesị Ejikọtara Ọnụ (UA) - otu Adreesị akpa ego nke na-akwado ma Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe ma nke Oha

4. Detuo ma ọ bụ nyochaa koodu QR

5. Kesaa UA gị na onye na-ezipụ ego



![img3](/content-images/53c940b6-1a03-4fa7-aefa-d3478f678a88-23f68a3855.webp)



## Akụkụ 4: Izipu ZEC nke e chebere echebe na Nzuzo

**Nzọụkwụ ndị a kpuchiri:**

1. Họrọ ZEC -> Send

2. Tapawa Adreesị Ejikọtara Ọnụ ma ọ bụ Adreesị akpa ego nke e chebere echebe nke onye nnata

3. Tinye ego ole

4. Kwenye nkọwa Azụmahia ahụ

5. Pịa Send - ebufela ego ugbu a na Nzuzo, na-eji pool Orchard

## Ụdị Adreesị akpa ego

Nke a bụ nkọwa maka ụdị Adreesị akpa ego Zcash ọ bụla:

**t-addr (t1...)** - Adreesị akpa ego Oha na-arụ ọrụ dịka Adreesị akpa ego Bitcoin, nke pụtara na Azụmahia bụ nke ọhaneze na blockchain. Ọ dị mfe iji ya na e-akpa ego na-abụghị Zcash, mana ha anaghị enye Nzuzo.

**z-addr (zs...)** - Adreesị akpa ego nke e chebere echebe na-eji teknụzụ Nzuzo nke Zcash (Sprout/Sapling) zoo onye na-ezipụ, onye nnata, na ego ole. Naanị viewing key ma ọ bụ onye nwe ya nwere ike ikpughe nkọwa.

**UA (u... or QR)** - Adreesị Ejikọtara Ọnụ na-ejikọta ọtụtụ ụdị Adreesị akpa ego (Oha, nke e chebere echebe, Orchard) n'ime otu. Nke a na-enye ndakọrịta kacha elu ma na-ahọrọ Nzuzo ebe o kwere omume.
