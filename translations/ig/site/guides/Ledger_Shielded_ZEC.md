# Ledger Nke e chebere echebe [Shielded] ZEC Nduzi Onye Ọrụ

N'ụbọchị 23 Septemba 2026, Ledger gbakwụnyere ZEC nke e chebere echebe n'onwe ya na Ledger Wallet Desktop. Otu akaụntụ Zcash na-ejide ugbu a nguzozi Oha na nguzozi nzuzo n'ime pool Ironwood nke Zcash. Igodo nzuzo gị na-anọgide na Ledger gị, a na-enyochakwa ma bịanye aka na Azụmahia [transaction] ọ bụla n'elu ngwaọrụ ahụ.

**Naanị Desktop.** Ọkwa Ledger na-ekwu na ZEC nke e chebere echebe bụ "dị naanị na Ledger Wallet Desktop. Nkwado mkpanaka adịghị ugbu a, mana ọ na-abịa n'oge adịghị anya." Ngwa desktop ahụ na-egosikwa otu ọkwa ahụ. Enyochara ya n'ụbọchị 15 Ọktoba 2026.

## Nkwadebe

- Ngwaọrụ Ledger nke na-agba ngwa Zcash: Nano S Plus, Nano X, Stax ma ọ bụ Flex. Ewubereghị ngwa Zcash ugbu a maka Nano S mbụ.
- Ledger Wallet Desktop 4.21 ma ọ bụ nke ka ọhụrụ, ebudatara naanị site na [ledger.com](https://www.ledger.com). Nduzi a jiri ụdị 4.24.0.
- Ngwa Zcash kachasị ọhụrụ dị na Ledger gị (3.9.4 mgbe edere nduzi a).
- Obere ego ZEC iji nwalee. Emere nzọụkwụ ọ bụla dị n'okpuru na mainnet na 0.001 ZEC. Azụmahia ọ bụla na-efu obere ugwo netwọk (0.00015 ZEC n'ule anyị).
- Nkebiokwu mgbake mkpụrụokwu 24 gị ga-anọgide n'akwụkwọ. Etinyela ya n'ime kọmputa ma ọ bụ kesaa ya.

## Nduzi Nzọụkwụ site na Nzọụkwụ (Ledger Wallet Desktop)

**1. Wụnye ma ọ bụ melite ngwa Zcash**
- Mepee Ledger Wallet, jikọọ ma kpọghee Ledger gị, ma mepee **My Ledger**. Kwe ka njikọ ahụ dị na ngwaọrụ.
- Chọọ **Zcash** n'ime katalọgụ ngwa, wee pịa **Install**, ma ọ bụ **Update** ma ọ bụrụ na arụnyelarị ya.
- Mepee ngwa Zcash na Ledger gị. Ihuenyo ahụ na-agụ "ngwa Zcash dị njikere". Pịgharịa gaa n'aka nri ruo **App info** iji lelee ụdị ahụ.

**2. Tinye akaụntụ Zcash**
- Na Ledger Wallet, họrọ **Add account**. Na **Select asset**, chọọ Zcash ma họrọ **Zcash (ZEC)**, ọ bụghị token e kechiri ekechi dịka Binance-Peg Zcash Token.
- Mgbe ngwa Zcash meghere na Ledger gị, Ledger Wallet na-achọta akaụntụ gị. Kanye ya n'okpuru **Select the accounts you want to add** wee pịa **Confirm**.
- Ibe akaụntụ ahụ na-egosi **Available balance**, **Transparent balance** na **Private balance**, tinyere bọtịnụ **Enable shielded**.

![Zcash account in Ledger Wallet Desktop showing the desktop-only notice, transparent and private balances, and the Enable shielded button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-01-account-desktop-only.png)

**3. Mee ka nguzozi nzuzo rụọ ọrụ**
- Pịa **Enable shielded**. Windo **Enable Zcash private balance** nwere nzọụkwụ anọ: Birthday, UFVK, Device na Confirmation.
- **Birthday:** maka akaụntụ ọhụrụ, debe ịdị elu ụbọchị ọmụmụ n'ụbọchị taa. Kanye naanị "This account received shielded ZEC in the past" ma ọ bụrụ na ị maara na o mere, n'ihi na inyocha site na block [block] 0 nwere ike iwe ogologo oge.
- **UFVK na Device:** Ledger Wallet na-arịọ Ledger gị ka ọ bubata Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK). Gụọ ihuenyo ndị dị na ngwaọrụ ahụ ma kwado. Ọ bụrụ na ị hụ "Device connection lost", jide n'aka na ngwa Zcash meghere, kwado ozugbo ma pịa **Retry**.
- Mgbe ị hụrụ **UFVK successfully imported**, pịa **Start sync**. Mgbe mmekọrịta gwụchara, bọtịnụ ahụ na-agbanwe gaa **Update balance** ma nwee oge "Last sync".
- viewing key [viewing key] na-anọgide na kọmputa gị. Ọ nwere ike ịchọta ego gị nke e chebere echebe mana ọ pụghị imefu ya.

![Enable Zcash private balance window showing UFVK successfully imported and the Start sync button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-02-ufvk-imported.png)

**4. Nata ZEC ma nyochaa Adreesị akpa ego abụọ ahụ na Ledger gị**
- Pịa **Receive** ma họrọ akaụntụ Zcash gị. Ledger Wallet na-egosi **Address for Zcash 1** (Oha, nke ji t1 amalite) na **Private address for Zcash 1** (nke ji u1 amalite). Adreesị akpa ego ndị mbụ ka dị irè.
- Ledger gị na-egosi "Verify Zcash addresses", **Private address** gafee ibe abụọ, emesịa **Public Address**. Tụlee mkpụrụedemede ọ bụla na ihuenyo tupu ị kesaa Adreesị akpa ego ọ bụla, wee kwado na ngwaọrụ ahụ.
- Họrọ Adreesị akpa ego nzuzo; jiri nke Oha naanị mgbe onye zitere ya anaghị akwado ihe ọzọ.

![Ledger Wallet Receive window showing the transparent address and the private address for Zcash 1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-03-receive-addresses.png)

![Ledger device screens: Verify Zcash addresses, Private address in two pages, then Public Address](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-04-device-verify-addresses.png)

**5. Bugharịa ZEC Oha gaa n'ime nguzozi nzuzo gị**
- Pịa **Send**. N'okpuru **Self transfer**, họrọ **Send to private balance**.
- Tinye ego ma ọ bụ jiri 25%, 50%, 75% ma ọ bụ **Max**. Lelee ugwo netwọk, wee pịa **Review**.
- Na Ledger gị, gafee usoro ndị a: "Review transaction to send ZEC", "Transfer from public to private address", ego ahụ, Adreesị akpa ego mmepụta n'ibe abụọ, ugwo ahụ na ịdị elu ngafe oge. Lelee na Adreesị akpa ego mmepụta dabara na Adreesị akpa ego nzuzo gị sitere na nzọụkwụ 4, wee pịa bọtịnụ abụọ na **Sign transaction**.
- Ledger Wallet na-egosi **Transaction sent**. Na nkọwa Azụmahia ahụ, ụdị ya na-agụ **Private (Ironwood)**.
- Ledger Wallet na-ekwu na nnyefe onwe onye na-ejikọ ego gị nke e chebere echebe na Adreesị akpa ego Oha gị. Ịnata ego ozugbo na Adreesị akpa ego nzuzo gị na-eme ka ha ghara ijikọta.

![Send ZEC window in Ledger Wallet with the Self transfer option Send to private balance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-05-send-to-private-balance.png)

![Ledger device review screens for moving ZEC from the transparent balance to the private address, ending with Sign transaction and Transaction signed](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-06-device-review-shielding.png)

- Mgbe Azụmahia ahụ kwadoro, nguzozi Oha na-agbada, nguzozi nzuzo ebili. Ego ọhụrụ e chebere echebe na-egosi dịka "maturing" ruo mgbe ha nwere nkwenye zuru ezu iji mefu.

![Zcash account after shielding: transparent balance reduced, private balance increased and shown as maturing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-07-balances-after-shielding.png)

**6. Ziga ZEC site na nguzozi nzuzo gị**
- Ozugbo enwere ike imefu nguzozi nzuzo, pịa **Send** ma debe **Transfer from** na **Private balance**.
- Mado Adreesị akpa ego onye nnata ma tinye ego ahụ. Ị nwere ike itinye **Memo (optional)** ezoro ezo, nke ruru bytes 512, nke naanị onye nnata nwere ike ịgụ.
- Nyochaa onye nnata, ego na ugwo na Ledger gị, wee pịa bọtịnụ abụọ na **Sign transaction**.

![Sent transaction from the private balance in Ledger Wallet transaction details](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-08-private-send.png)

## Ihe Dị Mma Ịmara

- ZEC nke e chebere echebe na Ledger bụ naanị desktop ugbu a (enyochara n'ụbọchị 15 Ọktoba 2026).
- Nguzozi nzuzo Ledger Wallet na-eji pool Ironwood.
- Ledger na-ekwu na ị nwere ike kesaa onye ndụmọdụ ụtụ isi ohere naanị ịgụ akụkọ Azụmahia gị. Onye ọ bụla nwere viewing key gị nwere ike ịhụ akụkọ gị nke e chebere echebe, yabụ kesaa ya naanị n'ebumnuche.
- E jidere ihuenyo ngwaọrụ ndị dị na nduzi a site na emulator Speculos Ledger (Nano S Plus) na-agba ngwa Zcash 3.9.4, jikọtara na Ledger Wallet Desktop 4.24.0 na mainnet Zcash. Ledger anụ ahụ na-agba otu ngwa ahụ ma na-egosi otu ihuenyo ndị ahụ.
