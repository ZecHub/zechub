# Free2Z: Ịtọlite akaụntụ + nkuzi mgbasa ozi dị ndụ

## Okwu mmalite

Free2Z bụ ikpo okwu [platform] ndị okike [creators] na ndị nkwado [supporters] ji ejikọta ma mezuo ebumnuche ha. Free2Z na-enye ngwá ọrụ maka ndị okike igosi nkà ha ma nweta ego maka ọrụ ha na-enweghị imebi Nzuzo [privacy]. Ndị nkwado nwere ike ịchọta ma kwado ebumnuche ha kwenyere na ya.

## Mepụta akaụntụ gị

Ugbu a, e nwere ụzọ abụọ iji mepụta akaụntụ [account] gị na Free2Z:  
1. Họrọ aha njirimara na okwuntughe.  
2. Jikọọ akaụntụ Twitter gị.  

Iji mepụta akaụntụ ma ọ bụ banye, gaa na https://free2z.cash/ ma pịa bọtịnụ 'CREATE'.

![Login/signup form](/content-images/image-2023-11-19-133101013-3ee51e3b6f.webp)

Ụdị nbanye/ndebanye aha ga-apụta na nchọgharị ịntanetị gị. Họrọ aha njirimara gị na okwuntughe siri ike - nke a ga-abụ ozi nbanye gị. Dozie captcha ahụ ma pịa bọtịnụ 'Enter' iji mepụta akaụntụ gị.

![Username and password entry form](/content-images/image-2023-11-19-133141052-0387984be1.webp)

N'aka ọzọ, ị nwere ike ịmepụta akaụntụ Free2Z site na ijikọta akaụntụ Twitter gị. Iji mee nke a, pịa akara ngosi Twitter dị n'akụkụ 'Login With', a ga-arịọkwa gị ka ị jikọọ akaụntụ Twitter gị.

![Twitter login option](/content-images/image-2023-11-19-133210103-f65246bbee.webp)

Pịa **Authorize app** iji jikọọ akaụntụ Twitter gị na Free2Z.

## Hazie profaịlụ gị

Mgbe emechara akaụntụ gị, a ga-eduga gị na ibe profaịlụ Free2Z gị. Ọ bụrụ na ị mepụtara akaụntụ gị site na ijikọta Twitter gị, a ga-ejuputa ọtụtụ ubi ozugbo. Ọ bụrụ na ị mepụtara akaụntụ site n'itinye aha njirimara na okwuntughe, ị kwesịrị itinye ozi gị ebe a. Ị nwere ike iji aha nzuzo kama aha gị n'ezie.

![Default empty profile header](/content-images/default-header-a11eb8328f.webp)

Ọ na-agwụ ike ugbu a, ya mere ka anyị mee ka ọ masịkwuo site n'ịtọlite avatar na banner. Ọdịdị Free2Z na-eme ka nzọụkwụ a dị mfe nke ukwuu site n'iji bọtịnụ bulite faịlụ (akara akụ elu dị n'aka nri).

![Avatar and banner upload buttons](/content-images/avatar-and-banner-bd20d0bbfb.webp)

Ị nwere ike ịhụ nsonaazụ ya n'elu ibe ahụ.

![Profile page after customization](/content-images/resultatpage-f4ddacb8b8.webp)

Aah, ọ ka mma! Ọ bụrụ na ị nwere nkà nka, ị nwere ike ime ka ibe profaịlụ Free2Z gị maa mma nke ukwuu! Dee nkọwa mara mma maka ibe gị. Ị nwere ike ikwu gbasara onwe gị ma ọ bụ kọwaa ụdị ọdịnaya ndị ọzọ nwere ike ịtụ anya n'aka gị. (Ndụmọdụ: Jiri usoro okwu markdown hazie nkọwa gị!)

![Editing the description field](/content-images/edit-description-b52afabec1.webp)

Echefula itinye Adreesị akpa ego nke e chebere echebe nke Zcash gị, ka ndị nkwado nwee ike inye gị onyinye ozugbo!

![Zcash shielded address field](/content-images/p2p-address-b89f035bd9.webp)

Nzọụkwụ ikpeazụ iji mezue nhazi ibe gị bụ ịtọ 'Member Price'. Nke a bụ ọnụahịa n'ime 2Zs (Tuzies) ndị ọrụ ndị ọzọ ga-akwụ iji denye aha na ibe gị! (A ga-ekwu maka 2Zs karịa ma emechaa).

![Member price setting](/content-images/member-price-2dafd7e399.webp)

N'ikpeazụ, pịa bọtịnụ 'Update Profile' iji mechaa nhazi profaịlụ gị!

## Ndebanye aha na 2Zs

Free2Z na-enye ndị ọrụ ohere idenye aha n'aka ndị okike ha hụrụ n'anya ma nweta njirimara pụrụ iche na ọdịnaya naanị ndị otu nwere ike ịhụ. Onye okike kwesịrị ịhazi opekempe ego 2Zs maka ndebanye aha.  

2Zs ma ọ bụ Tuzies bụ kredit dijitalụ n'ime ngwa a na-eji na Free2Z maka ọtụtụ ebumnuche, gụnyere:  
1. Idenyere ndị okike aha  
2. Ịkwalite ọhụhụ nke ibe Free2Z  
3. Inye ndị okike onyinye  

Ị nwere ike ịhụ kredit 2Zs gị n'isi ibe profaịlụ gị.

![2Zs / Tuzies balance in header](/content-images/tuzis-balance-9d6a9eff7f.webp)

Ị nwere ike ịzụta 2Zs ndị ọzọ site na Zcash site n'ịpị n'itule 2Zs gị. Opekempe ego a ga-azụta bụ 0.05 ZEC, ọnụego mgbanwe ugbu a bụkwa 0.05 ZEC = 10 2Zs.

![Buying 2Zs interface](/content-images/buy-tuzies-e82c626047.webp)

## Mgbasa ozi dị ndụ

Ikpo okwu Free2Z na-enye njirimara magburu onwe ya a kpọrọ Free2Z Live. Site na ngwá ọrụ a, ndị okike nwere ike ịmalite mgbasa ozi dị ndụ maka ndị otu debanyere aha. Ụfọdụ njirimara dị mkpa nke Free2Z Live gụnyere:  
- Ọnụ na vidio  
- Ịkekọrịta ihuenyo  
- Idezi koodu ọnụ  
- Mkparịta ụka  
- Ide ihe na bọọdụ ọcha  
- Na ọtụtụ ihe ndị ọzọ!

### Gịnị ka ị nwere ike iji Free2Z Live mee

Free2Z Live bụ ngwá ọrụ dị ike, nke na-enye ndị okike ohere ịgbasa ụdị ọdịnaya ọ bụla. Lee ọmụmaatụ ole na ole nke ụdị ọdịnaya ị nwere ike iji Free2Z Live gbasaa:  
- Nkuzi na ngosipụta  
- Ngosi na mmemme  
- Ọrụ imekọrịta ọnụ  
- Akụkọ na ntaakụkọ dị ndụ  
- Ihe okike gị bụ naanị oke!

### Otu esi amalite mgbasa ozi dị ndụ

Ọ dị mfe nke ukwuu ịmalite mgbasa ozi dị ndụ site na Free2Z Live. Ma e nwere ihe ole na ole a chọrọ tupu ịmalite mgbasa ozi gị.  
1. Onye okike **ga-enwerịrị** opekempe **ọnụahịa ndị otu** nke 10 2Zs.  
2. Onye okike **ga-enwerịrị** itule opekata mpe 150 2Zs na akaụntụ ya.  

Ọ bụrụ na onye okike mezuru **ọnọdụ abụọ ahụ** dị n'elu, akara ngosi a kpọrọ **Go Live** ga-adị ya na ibe profaịlụ ya.

![Go Live button](/content-images/go-live-4c8ac67505.webp)

Iji malite mgbasa ozi dị ndụ, naanị pịa bọtịnụ **Go Live**, ngwa Free2Z ga-arịọkwa ikike iji igwe okwu na webcam gị (ọ bụrụ na ị nwere otu). Mgbe ị nyesịrị ikike ndị dị mkpa, Free2Z ga-arịọ gị ka ị tinye aha otutu ịchọrọ iji n'oge mgbasa ozi dị ndụ. Ozugbo ịhọrọ aha otutu, pịa **Join** iji malite mgbasa ozi dị ndụ gị.

![Select nickname for live stream](/content-images/select-nickname-414444b992.webp)

Ma emechaala! Mgbe ị malitere mgbasa ozi dị ndụ, profaịlụ gị ga-egosi akara ngosi na-egosi na ị nọ na mgbasa ozi, ka ndị debanyere aha gị nwee ike isonyere mgbasa ozi dị ndụ ahụ.

![Joining a livestream](/content-images/join-livestream-679334ba53.webp)

Mgbe ndị mmadụ sonyere mgbasa ozi dị ndụ, ha nwere ike ikwurịta okwu, rịọ ka ha sonye n'elu ikpo okwu, soro site n'ịtụ vootu na ntuli aka, na ihe ndị ọzọ.

![Example of a Free2Z Live session](/content-images/free2z-live-example-eecb2ae553.webp)

_(Ọmụmatụ nke oge Free2Z Live)_

Free2Z Live na-agbanwe agbanwe ma dị ike. N'akụkụ na-esonụ, aga m enye nkọwa zuru ezu banyere ọrụ ya na ngwá ọrụ ya.

### Ịchọpụta Free2Z Live

Wiki-doc a abụghị ntụzịaka zuru ezu gbasara Free2Z ma ọ bụ Free2Z Live. Ma lee nkọwa izugbe nke ngwá ọrụ ya.

N'akụkụ ala ekpe nke ihuenyo, anyị na-ahụ bọtịnụ ole na ole.

![Bottom left controls](/content-images/free2z-live-left-60b450b01c.webp)

Ọrụ mara mma dị ebe a bụ ikike ịkekọrịta ihuenyo gị ka ndị ọrụ ndị ọzọ hụ ihe ị na-eme.

N'akụkụ ala, anyị nwere ike ịhụ nhọrọ mgbasa ozi dị ndụ.

![Bottom toolbar](/content-images/free2z-live-bottom-ae64a6960c.webp)

Ụfọdụ nhọrọ ndị dị mkpa ebe a bụ:  
- Mechie / meghee igwe okwu  
- Kwado / gbanyụọ webcam  
- Hapụ elu ikpo okwu  
- Mechie ụda ndị na-ekwu okwu niile  
- Hapụ  

Iji kwụsị mgbasa ozi dị ndụ, ị kwesịrị ịpị bọtịnụ 'Leave', wee pịa 'End meeting for all' iji kwụsị nzukọ ahụ maka ndị niile sonyere.

![Bottom right interaction panel](/content-images/free2z-live-right-c83cb6d209.webp)

Bọtịnụ ndị dị n'aka nri ala nwere ọrụ metụtara mmekorita mgbasa ozi dị ndụ, dịka:  
- **Chat**: Nke a na-enye ndị sonyere ohere ikwurịta okwu n'etiti onwe ha na onye ọbịa n'oge mgbasa ozi dị ndụ.  
- **Polls**: Nke a na-enye onye ọbịa ohere ịmepụta ntuli aka ma nakọta nzaghachi n'aka ndị sonyere.  
- **List of participants**: Nke a na-egosi ndepụta ndị sonyere na mgbasa ozi dị ndụ.  
- **Plugins**: Nke a na-enye onye ọbịa ohere ịgbakwunye plugins na ndọtị iji meziwanye ahụmịhe mgbasa ozi dị ndụ.

![Plugins panel](/content-images/free2z-live-plugins-e9a30ee355.webp)

Ebumnuche wiki-doc a abụghị ịkọwa plugins niile, mana ana m akwadosi gị ike ka ịnwale nke ọ bụla, n'ihi na ha niile na-enye nnukwu uru mmekorita n'etiti ndị okike na ndị na-ekiri. Iji malite plugin, naanị pịa bọtịnụ 'rocket' dị n'aka nri.
