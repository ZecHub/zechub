# Ịhụ Adreesị Zcash

Ọ bụrụ na ị na-amụ maka Zcash maka oge mbụ, ị ga-aghọta ozugbo na e nwere ụdị abụọ [azụmahịa](https://zechub.wiki/using-zcash/transactions) nke nwere ike ime: *ihe doro anya* na *ihe e chebere*.
Ọzọkwa, ọ bụrụ na ị na-agbaso ihe ndị kachasị ọhụrụ na gburugburu ebe obibi Zcash, o nwere ike ị mụtala gbasara ya [Adreesị Ndị Ejikọtara Ọnụ](https://web.archive.org/web/20260823012524/https://electriccoin.co/blog/unified-addresses-in-zcash-explained/), or UA's.
Mgbe ndị mmadụ nọ n'ụlọ ọrụ Zcash na-ekwu maka azụmahịa *echekwara echekwa*, ha pụtara azụmahịa ndị gụnyere adreesị e dere maka usoro osisi ma ọ bụ ubi mkpụrụ osisi. 
E mere UA iji jikọta *ụdị azụmahịa ọ bụla* nke echekwara ma ọ bụ nke doro anya ka ọ bụrụ otu adreesị. Nkọwapụta a bụ isi ihe na-eme ka UX dị mfe n'ihu. Ebumnuche nke ntuziaka a bụ ịgbakwunye nghọta nke UA site na iji ihe atụ anya doro anya.

Ihe mbụ ị ga-achọpụta bụ otú ogologo nke ụdị adreesị ọ bụla si dị iche. Ị nwere ike ịhụ nke a anya site na ọnụọgụ mkpụrụedemede dị na eriri adreesị *ma ọ bụ* site na ilele koodu QR ndị metụtara ya. Ka ogologo adreesị ahụ na-abawanye, koodu QR na-eme ka ọ dịkwuo elu ma tinye data karịa n'ime square ahụ.

* `t1goiSyw2JinFCmUnfiwwp72LEZzD42TyYu` dị mkpụrụedemede iri atọ na ise n'ogologo
* `zs1cpf4prtmnqpg6x2ngcrwelu9a39z9l9lqukq9fwagnaqrknk34a7n3szwxpjuxfjdxkuzykel53` ogologo mkpụrụedemede iri asaa na asatọ
* `u1ckeydud0996ftppqrnpdsqyeq4e57qcyjr4raht4dc8j3njuyj3gmm9yk7hq9k88cdkqfuqusgpcpjfhwu3plm2vrd32g8du78kzkm5un357r4vkhz4vhxd4yfl8zvszk99cmsc89qv4trd7jzkcs8h6lukzgy25j8cv76p0g603nrrg6yt6cxsh2v8rmkasskd69ylfyphhjyv0cxs` ogologo mkpụrụedemede 213

Ihe nke abụọ ị ga-achọpụta bụ mmalite nke eriri adreesị ọ bụla -- mmalite doro anya na *t*, osisi nwere *zs*, na n'ikpeazụ UA nwere *u1*.

Ọ dị mkpa ịmara:

#### "Adreesị ịkwụ ụgwọ Orchard enweghị koodu eriri nke na-eguzo onwe ya. Kama, anyị na-akọwa "adreesị ejikọtara ọnụ" nke nwere ike ijikọ adreesị nke ụdị dị iche iche ọnụ, gụnyere Orchard. Adreesị ejikọtara ọnụ nwere akụkụ "u" nke a na-agụ n'ebe mmadụ nọ na Mainnet, ya bụ, ha ga-enwe prefix "u1". "
