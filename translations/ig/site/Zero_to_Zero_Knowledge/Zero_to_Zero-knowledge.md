| # | Isiokwu | Ihe ị ga-amụta |
|---|-------|---------------------|
| 1 | [Ọrụ Hash](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/hash-functions) | Ntinye ọ bụla na-aghọ mmepụta edobere. Njirimara atọ nke hash ga-enwerịrị. |
| 2 | [Mweghachi Mkpụrụ Okwu Mweghachi](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/mnemonic-seed-phrases) | Otu ndepụta okwu si aghọ isi ihe na-ejide ego gị. |
