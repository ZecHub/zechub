# Zero to Zero Knowledge

Usoro dị mkpirikpi banyere echiche dị n’azụ ego dijitalụ nkeonwe [private digital cash]. Malite n’elu.

| # | Isiokwu | Ihe ị ga-amụta |
|---|-------|---------------------|
| 1 | [Ọrụ Hash](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/hash-functions) | Ntinye ọ bụla na-aghọ mmepụta a kapịrị ọnụ. Njirimara atọ hash ga-enwerịrị. |
| 2 | [Mkpuru mgbake](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/mnemonic-seed-phrases) | Otu ndepụta okwu si aghọ mkpịsị ugodi na-ejide ego gị. |
| 3 | [Zcash Adreesị akpa ego](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/zcash-addresses) | Azụmahia Oha na nke e chebere echebe, na Adreesị akpa ego ejikọrọ [Unified Addresses]. |
| 4 | [Gburugburu Ọrụ ntụkwasị obi](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/tees) | Ngwaike na-edobe mgbakọ na mwepụ na nzuzo, na ebe ọ na-adịghị ezu. |
| 5 | [Cryptonote](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/cryptonote) | Usoro Nzuzo dị n’azụ Monero, na otú o si dị iche na Zcash. |
| 6 | [Lelantus](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/lelantus) | Usoro nkwukọrịta dị n’azụ Firo, na otú e si atụnyere ya na Zcash. |
