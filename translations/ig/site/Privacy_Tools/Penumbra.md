# Penumbra 

Penumbra bụ netwọkụ layer-1 nke e chebere echebe kpamkpam, proof-of-stake, na decentralized exchange n'ime gburugburu ebe obibi Cosmos. Interchain pool nke e chebere echebe ya nwere ike ijide akụ sitere na chain ndị ejikọrọ na IBC. Ndị ọrụ nwere ike ibufe, mee Mkpọchi ego dijitalụ, Mgbawe, ma nye liquidity n'ime Penumbra na-enweghị ibipụta nguzozi akaụntụ nke onye ọ bụla ma ọ bụ jikọta omume ndị ahụ na njirimara ha. Nke a apụtaghị na akụkụ ọ bụla nke data netwọkụ ma ọ bụ DEX bụ nke nzuzo.

## Teknụzụ e ji na Penumbra IBC 

Mmekọrịta IBC: Usoro Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC) na-ejikọta Penumbra na chain ndị ọzọ IBC kwadoro. Mbufe IBC na-abata na-echebe ego mgbe ha banyere Penumbra; mbufe na-apụ na-ewepụ nchebe ha mgbe ha na-apụ. Omume dị na chain onye mmekọ nke Oha na-anọgide bụrụ nke ọha, gụnyere Adreesị akpa ego ebe ego nkwụnye na-abata na-aga. [ókè Nzuzo na-eso ókè IBC ahụ](https://guide.penumbra.zone/overview/interchain-privacy).

Proof Zero-Knowledge (ZKP): Penumbra na-eji proof zero-knowledge n'akụkụ onye ahịa gosi na mmelite ọnọdụ e ji Nzobe ederede zobe dị irè n'ebughị data Azụmahia nzuzo nke onye ọrụ pụta ìhè. E-akpa ego na-enyocha ma na-emeghe Nzobe ederede data ha na-ahụ n'ógbè ha; node ọha enweghị ike iweghachite nguzozi nzuzo nke onye ọrụ naanị otu a.

Decentralized Exchange nwere Nzuzo: Penumbra gụnyere DEX maka ịzụ ahịa akụ dị n'ime pool nke e chebere echebe ya. Nsonaazụ Mgbawe nke onye ọ bụla bụ nke nzuzo, ebe nsonaazụ mmezu batch na ọnọdụ liquidity na-enweghị aha bụ nke ọha. Ọdịiche ndị a dị mkpa mgbe a na-akọwa ihe exchange ahụ na-ezobe.

## Ojiji [Use Cases]

Azụmahia echekwara: Mbufe dị n'ime pool nke e chebere echebe nke Penumbra na-echebe onye zitere, onye natara, ego ole, na ụdị akụ. Ndị ọrụ nwere ike ikpughere ndị ọzọ enyere ikike omume ha n'ụzọ nhọrọ. Ịkekọrịta naanị Adreesị akpa ego nnata anaghị ekpughe nguzozi onye nwe ya. Njirimara ndị a anaghị agbatị na omume ọha dị na chain ọzọ mgbe ewepụrụ ego.

Mkpọchi ego dijitalụ: Token Mkpọchi ego dijitalụ nke Penumbra bụ **UM**. Ịnyefe UM n'aka validator na-eweghachite token delegation na-anọchi anya òkè dị na pool delegation validator ahụ. Mkpokọta stake dị na pool validator ọ bụla bụ nke ọha, ebe delegation nke onye ọ bụla enweghị aha. Iwepụ delegation na-emepụta token unbonding pụrụ iche maka validator mbụ, ndị na-anọgide n'ihe ize ndụ slashing ruo mgbe oge igbu njikọ kwụsịrị. Lee ntuziaka [Mkpọchi ego dijitalụ nke e chebere echebe](https://guide.penumbra.zone/overview/staking) gọọmentị.

Mgbawe token: A na-achịkọta ebumnuche Mgbawe site na ụzọ abụọ azụmahịa ma mezuo ha dịka batch na ngwụcha block ọ bụla. Nke a na-ewepụ nhazi n'ime block nke ebumnuche Mgbawe dịka ụzọ ndị ahịa nwere ike isi rite uru; ọ bụ usoro kpọmkwem, ọ bụghị nkwa na ụdị MEV ọbụla enweghị ike ịdị. A na-ede nsonaazụ batch n'ọnọdụ chain ọha, ndị ọrụ na-anatakwa òkè nke nsonaazụ ha n'onwe ha n'ime pool nke e chebere echebe. Ntuziaka [DEX](https://guide.penumbra.zone/overview/dex) na-akọwa mmezu batch na arbitrage akpaaka nke usoro ahụ.

Ịmepụta ahịa: Ndị na-enye liquidity na-emepụta ọnọdụ liquidity etinyere n'otu ebe na-enweghị aha. Liquidity na ọrụ azụmahịa nke ọnọdụ ndị a bụ nke ọha, mana ejikọtaghị ha na njirimara onye na-enye ha.
