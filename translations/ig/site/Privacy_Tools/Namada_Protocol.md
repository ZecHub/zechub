Usoro Namada na-arụ ọrụ dịka ikpo okwu Layer 1 nke dabere na consensus proof-of-stake, e mere iji nye Nzuzo [privacy] nke na-adịghị elebara ụdị akụ anya n'etiti chain. Site na protocol Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC), Namada na-ejikọta nke ọma na chain ndị nwere nkwubi-azụ ngwa ngwa, na-eme ka mmekọrịta dị n'etiti ha dị mfe. Ọzọkwa, Namada na-ewulite bridge ụzọ abụọ a na-atụkwasịghị ntụkwasị obi na Ethereum, nke na-eme ka nkwurịta okwu dị nchebe ma bụrụ nke a pụrụ ịdabere na ya n'etiti netwọk abụọ ahụ.

Namada na-ebute Nzuzo ụzọ site n'itinye ụdị circuit Multi-Asset Shielded Pool (MASP) emelitere. Ụdị a kwalitere na-enye ụdị akụ niile, gụnyere token ndị a pụrụ ịgbanwe na ndị a na-apụghị ịgbanwe, ohere iji otu set nke e chebere echebe [shielded] nke ha na Zcash. N'ihi ya, ịnyefe akụ ndị akwadoro na Namada na-adị iche n'ihi na ọ na-esiri ike ịchọpụta ya, n'ihi ọkwa Nzuzo dị elu metụtara ya. Ọzọkwa, mmelite kacha ọhụrụ nke circuit Multi Asset Shielded Pool na-eme ka ụgwọ ọrụ set nke e chebere echebe kwe omume, bụ nke bụ atụmatụ ma ọ bụ mkpali ọhụrụ pụrụ iche nke na-ekesa akụrụngwa iji kwalite Nzuzo dịka ihe ọha na eze bara uru.

## Ethereum Bridge + IBC Kwekọrọ

Ijikọta Ethereum bridge na Namada na-ewepụ mkpa protocol dị iche, ebe ọ na-aghọ akụkụ dị mkpa nke gburugburu obibi Namada. A tụkwasịrị validators nọ n'ime Namada obi ka ha gbaa bridge ahụ yana protocol isi Namada. Validators ndị a na-arụkwa ọrụ dị ka relayers mgbe a na-ebufe akụ na Namada, nke na-eme ka itinye ndị ọzọ ghara ịdị mkpa. N'aka nke ọzọ, mgbe a na-ebufe akụ na Ethereum, ndị ọzọ nọ n'èzí, a maara dịka relayers, na-esonye, n'agbanyeghị na ha enweghị ọrụ ịkwado ma ọ bụ ichekwa bridge ahụ.

![Ethereum Bridge Diagram](/content-images/image-0fd8f754ba.webp)

Usoro Namada nwekwara ike ijikọ nke ọma na chain ọ bụla nwere nkwubi-azụ ngwa ngwa nke na-akwado protocol Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC). Mgbe a bịara n'ịmekọrịta na Ethereum, Namada na-etinye Ethereum bridge pụrụ iche ma dị nchebe nke na-arụ ọrụ n'enweghị ntụkwasị obi. E ji nlezianya mepụta bridge a iji bute nchekwa ụzọ site n'ịmanye njikwa usoro maka njikọ bridge niile na site n'ile mbufe Ethereum ọ bụla nwere ntụpọ anya dịka mmejọ dị njọ nke pụrụ ibute ntaramahụhụ slashing.

## Ụgwọ Ọrụ Set Nke E Chebere Echebe

Na mmelite kacha ọhụrụ nke Usoro [Namada](https://namada.net/blog/what-is-namada), a na-enye ndị ọrụ nwere akụ ndị e chebere echebe mkpali ka ha sonye n'ọrụ na set nke e chebere echebe a na-ekekọrịta. Nke a ga-ekwe omume site n'ijikọta circuit MASP emelitere, nke gụnyere ugbu a Convert Circuit ọhụrụ. Site n'iji atụmatụ ọhụrụ a, Namada na-agba ndị ọrụ ume ka ha tinye aka na set nke e chebere echebe a na-ekekọrịta site n'inwe akụ ndị e chebere echebe.

Na Namada, a na-ewere set nke e chebere echebe dịka ihe ọha na eze bara uru nke na-abụghị nke naanị mmadụ nwere ma na-adịghị asọ mpi. Nke a pụtara na ka ọtụtụ mmadụ na-eji mbufe ndị e chebere echebe, ọkwa nkwa Nzuzo na-akawanye mma maka onye ọ bụla sonyere. Protocol ahụ na-amata mkpa nnabata na ntinye aka ọnụ iji kwalite Nzuzo maka ndị ọrụ niile. Ya mere, site n'ịkpali ndị ọrụ ka ha nwee akụ ndị e chebere echebe ma tinye aka na set nke e chebere echebe a na-ekekọrịta, Namada na-akwalite gburugburu obibi Nzuzo siri ike karị ma dịkwa ike.

## Azụmahia Akụ Ndị E Chebere Echebe

Mgbe a bịara na mbufe ndị e chebere echebe, ma ọ gụnyere token Ethereum na-enweghị ike ịgbanwe (NFT), ATOM, ma ọ bụ NAM, ha enweghị ike ịdị iche na ibe ha. Nke a pụtara na atụmatụ na-echekwa Nzuzo nke MASP (Modified Accumulator Sapling Protocol), ụdị circuit Zcash Sapling emelitere, na-emetụta ụdị akụ niile n'otu aka ahụ. Circuit MASP na-eme ka akụ niile dị n'ime gburugburu obibi Namada keta otu set nke e chebere echebe. Usoro a na-ahụ na e kewaghị nkwa Nzuzo n'etiti akụ nke ọ bụla. N'agbanyeghị olu Azụmahia metụtara otu akụ pụrụ iche, nchedo Nzuzo ahụ na-adịgide otu ma na-anọọrọ onwe ya.

![Shielded Assets Transaction Diagram](/content-images/image-1-512c639d56.webp)

Site n'ijikọta set nke e chebere echebe n'ofe akụ dị iche iche, Namada na-ahụ na a na-echekwa Nzuzo n'otu aka ahụ, n'agbanyeghị ụdị akụ pụrụ iche metụtara na mbufe nke e chebere echebe. Usoro a na-akwalite usoro Nzuzo jikọtara ọnụ n'ime protocol ahụ ma na-eme ka nzuzo nke Azụmahia metụtara Ethereum NFTs, ATOM, NAM, na akụ ndị ọzọ akwadoro sikwuo ike. Namada na-emekwa ka mbufe token ndị a pụrụ ịgbanwe na ndị a na-apụghị ịgbanwe n'onwe ha kwe omume site n'iji zk-SNARKs ọhụrụ, na-ahụ na e chebere nzuzo token ndị sitere na ya na ndị na-esighị na ya dịka a na-eme na Zcash.

## Ugwo Dị Ntakịrị na Azụmahia Ngwa Ngwa

Namada na-ejikọta ihe abụọ dị mkpa iji nye ọsọ Azụmahia ngwa ngwa na nkwubi-azụ: mmepụta proof ngwa ngwa na consensus Byzantine Fault Tolerant (BFT) nke oge a. Atụmatụ abụọ a na-eme ka Namada rute ogo nhazi Azụmahia yiri nke Visa, netwọk Kwụọ ụhwọ a ma ama nke a maara maka ike ya ịhazigharị nnukwu ọnụọgụ. Mmepụta proof ngwa ngwa na-ezo aka n'ịmepụta proofs cryptographic nke ọma, nke na-akwado izi ezi na iguzosi ike n'ezi ihe nke Azụmahia na Blockchain. Site n'iji usoro dị elu na nkwalite arụmọrụ, Usoro Namada na-ebelata ọrụ mgbakọ dị mkpa iji mepụta proofs ndị a, nke na-ebute nkwado na nkwenye Azụmahia ngwa ngwa.

Ọzọkwa, Namada na-eji algọridim consensus BFT nke oge a, nke na-ahụ na Azụmahia dị mma nakwa na e kwekọrịtara na ha n'ofe netwọk. Usoro consensus ndị a na-eme ka Namada nwee ike iru consensus gbasara usoro na izi ezi nke Azụmahia, na-enye nkwa siri ike nke nkwubi-azụ. Site na nkwubi-azụ, a na-ewere Azụmahia dịka ndị a na-apụghị ịtụgharị azụ, na-ebelata ihe ize ndụ nke iji otu ego ugboro abụọ ma ọ bụ ịtụgharị Azụmahia azụ. Namada na-agbaso usoro yiri nke Anoma, protocol ọzọ a maara maka azịza scaling ya. Namada na-anabata fractal instances, nke na-enye ohere ịmepụta chain ndị agbakwunyere n'ime blockchain isi. Ọdịdị fractal a na-eme ka scaling kwụ n'akụkụ kwe omume site n'ikesa ibu ọrụ n'ofe instances ọtụtụ, na-eme ka ikike na arụmọrụ netwọk n'ozuzu ya dịkwuo mma.

## Njikọ Mmasị Atụmatụ nke Namada na Zcash

Dị ka akwụkwọ e bipụtara n'oge na-adịbeghị anya, nke a pụrụ ịhụ na [Blọọgụ Usoro Namada](https://namada.net/blog/rfc-proposal-for-a-strategic-alliance-between-namada-and-zcash), otu nọ n'azụ Usoro Namada nwere obi ụtọ iweta atụmatụ na arịrịọ maka nkọwa (RFC) maka njikọ mmasị atụmatụ n'etiti akụ, chain, na obodo Namada na Zcash.

![Namada-Zcash Strategic Alliance Diagram](/content-images/image-2-68804c60f3.webp)

Njikọ a tụrụ aro gụnyere ihe atọ bụ isi. Nke mbụ, a ga-emepụta pool ikike iji nye ego maka ọrụ ndị na-eweta uru nye Zcash na Namada abụọ ahụ. Nke abụọ, a ga-ekesa airdrop token NAM nye ndị ji ZEC. N'ikpeazụ, e nwere atụmatụ iji guzobe bridge nke na-ebelata ntụkwasị obi nke jikọtara Zcash na Namada. Ozugbo etinyere ya n'ọrụ, bridge a ga-eme ka ndị ji ZEC, a na-akpọ Zolders, jiri ZEC ha na Namada. Ọzọkwa, Zolders ga-enwe ohere ịnweta gburugburu obibi Cosmos ka ukwuu na Ethereum site na Namada. Ị nwere ike ịmụtakwu banyere njikọ mmasị atụmatụ ahụ na [Nzukọ Obodo Zcash](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/rfc-proposal-for-a-strategic-alliance-between-namada-and-zcash/44372)
