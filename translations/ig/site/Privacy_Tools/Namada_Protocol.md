Usoro Namada na-eje ozi dị ka ikpo okwu Layer 1 dabere na nkwekọrịta ihe akaebe nke ihe onwunwe, nke e mere iji nye nzuzo nke ihe onwunwe na-enweghị ike ịchọta. Site na usoro Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC), Namada na-ejikọta nke ọma na agbụ ngwa ngwa, na-eme ka mmekọrịta dị mfe. Na mgbakwunye, Namada na-eguzobe akwa abụọ na-enweghị ntụkwasị obi na Ethereum, na-eme ka nkwukọrịta dị nchebe na nke a pụrụ ịtụkwasị obi dị n'etiti netwọk abụọ ahụ.

Namada na-ebute ụzọ maka nzuzo site n'itinye usoro nchekwa nke Multi-Asset Shielded Pool (MASP) emelitere. Ụdị a emelitere na-enye ụdị akụ niile, gụnyere ma tokens nke a na-akpọ fungible na nke a na-akpọ non-fungible, ohere iji otu ihe nchekwa nke a na-akpọ set protected kpọmkwem dịka nke Zcash. N'ihi ya, usoro nke ịnyefe akụ ndị a na-akwado na Namada na-apụta ìhè ka ọ na-esi ike ịchọpụta n'ihi ọkwa nzuzo dị elu. Ọzọkwa, mmelite kachasị ọhụrụ na sekit Multi Asset Shielded Pool na-enye ohere maka ụgwọ ọrụ nchekwa nke bụ atụmatụ ma ọ bụ ihe mkpali na-enye akụrụngwa iji kwalite nzuzo dị ka ihe ọma ọha.

## Akwa Ethereum + IBC dakọtara

Njikọta nke àkwà mmiri Ethereum na Namada na-ewepụ mkpa ọ dị maka usoro dị iche, ebe ọ na-aghọ akụkụ dị mkpa nke usoro gburugburu ebe obibi Namada. Ndị na-akwado ihe n'ime Namada nwere ọrụ ịgba ọsọ àkwà mmiri ahụ n'akụkụ usoro Namada bụ isi. Ndị na-akwado ihe ndị a na-ejekwa ozi dị ka ndị na-enyefe ihe mgbe a bịara n'inyefe ihe onwunwe na Namada, na-eme ka itinye aka nke ndị ọzọ na-enweghị isi. N'aka nke ọzọ, mgbe a na-ebufe ihe onwunwe na Ethereum, ndị ọzọ (a maara dị ka ndị na-enyefe ihe) na-etinye aka, ọ bụ ezie na ha enweghị ibu ọrụ maka ịkwado ma ọ bụ ịchekwa àkwà mmiri ahụ.

![Ethereum Bridge Diagram](/content-images/image-0fd8f754ba.webp)

Namada Protocol nwekwara ike ijikọ nke ọma na usoro ọ bụla dị ngwa nke na-akwado usoro Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC). Maka mmekọrịta na Ethereum, Namada na-etinye akwa Ethereum pụrụ iche ma dị nchebe nke na-arụ ọrụ n'ụzọ a na-apụghị ịtụkwasị obi. E ji nlezianya hazie akwa a iji mee ka nchekwa dị mkpa site n'ịmanye njikwa mmiri maka njikọ akwa niile ma na-ewere mbufe Ethereum ọ bụla na-adịghị mma dị ka nnukwu mpụ nke nwere ike ibute mbelata ntaramahụhụ.

## Ụgwọ Ọrụ Setịpụ Ekpuchiri Echebe

Na mmelite kachasị ọhụrụ nke [Usoro Namada](https://namada.net/blog/what-is-namada), a na-agba ndị ọrụ nwere akụ nchekwa ume isonye na set nchekwa ekekọrịtara. Nke a na-eme site na njikọta nke sekit MASP emelitere, nke gụnyere ugbu a Convert Circuit ọhụrụ. Site n'iji atụmatụ ọhụrụ a eme ihe, Namada na-agba ndị ọrụ ume ka ha tinye aka na set nchekwa ekekọrịtara site na ijide akụ nchekwa ekekọrịtara.

Na Namada, a na-ewere ihe nchekwa ahụ dị ka ihe na-abụghị nke pụrụ iche ma na-emegide ndị ọzọ. Nke a pụtara na ka ọtụtụ mmadụ na-eji nnyefe nchekwa, ọkwa nke nkwa nzuzo na-akawanye mma maka onye ọ bụla sonyere. Usoro a na-amata mkpa ọ dị ịnabata na isonye n'ịkwalite nzuzo maka ndị ọrụ niile. Ya mere, site n'ịgba ndị ọrụ ume ijide akụ echekwara ma tinye aka na ihe nchekwa ekekọrịtara, Namada na-akwalite usoro nzuzo siri ike ma sie ike karị.

## Azụmahịa Akụ E Chebere

Maka mbufe nchekwa, ma ọ bụ ihe e ji aka kpuchie, ma ọ bụ ihe e ji aka kpuchie Ethereum nke a na-akpọ "Non-Fungible Token" (NFT), ATOM, ma ọ bụ NAM, ha enweghị ike ịmata ọdịiche dị n'etiti onwe ha. Nke a pụtara na atụmatụ nchekwa nzuzo nke MASP (Modified Accumulator Sapling Protocol) nyere, nke bụ ụdị Zcash Sapling emelitere, na-emetụta ụdị akụ niile. Sekit MASP na-enye akụ niile dị na gburugburu Namada ohere ịkekọrịta otu ihe e ji aka kpuchie. Ụzọ a na-eme ka nkwa nzuzo ghara ịgbasa n'etiti akụ nkeonwe. N'agbanyeghị olu azụmahịa metụtara otu akụ, nchekwa nzuzo na-anọgide na-agbanwe agbanwe ma na-adabereghị onwe ya.

![Shielded Assets Transaction Diagram](/content-images/image-1-512c639d56.webp)

Site n'ịjikọta ihe nchekwa ahụ n'ofe akụ dị iche iche, Namada na-ahụ na a na-akwado nzuzo n'otu aka ahụ, n'agbanyeghị ụdị akụ akọwapụtara nke metụtara mbufe nchekwa. Ụzọ a na-akwalite usoro nzuzo jikọtara ọnụ n'ime usoro ahụ ma na-eme ka nzuzo nke azụmahịa metụtara Ethereum NFTs, ATOM, NAM, na akụ ndị ọzọ akwadoro dịkwuo mma. Namada na-enyekwa aka ịnyefe ihe mgbochi fungible na nke na-abụghị nke a na-agbanwe agbanwe site na iji zk-SNARKs, na-ahụ na nzuzo maka ihe mgbochi ndị obodo na nke na-abụghị nke obodo dịka e si eme ya na Zcash.

## Ụgwọ dị obere na azụmahịa ngwa ngwa

Namada na-ejikọta ihe abụọ dị mkpa iji nye ọsọ azụmahịa ngwa ngwa na njedebe: mmepụta ngwa ngwa na nkwekọrịta Byzantine Fault Tolerant (BFT) nke oge a. Atụmatụ abụọ a na-enyere Namada aka iru ọnụego nhazi azụmahịa yiri Visa, netwọk ịkwụ ụgwọ a ma ama nke a maara maka ikike mmepụta ya dị elu. Mmepụta ngwa ngwa na-ezo aka na mmepụta dị irè nke ihe akaebe nzuzo nke na-egosi izi ezi na iguzosi ike n'ezi ihe nke azụmahịa na Blockchain. Site na iji usoro na njikarịcha dị elu, Namada Protocol na-ebelata oke ego mgbakọ na mwepụ achọrọ iji mepụta ihe akaebe ndị a, na-ebute nkwenye ngwa ngwa na nkwenye nke azụmahịa.

Na mgbakwunye, Namada na-eji algọridim nkwekọrịta BFT nke oge a, nke na-ahụ na iguzosi ike n'ezi ihe na nkwekọrịta nke azụmahịa n'ofe netwọk ahụ. Usoro nkwekọrịta ndị a na-enyere Namada aka iru nkwekọrịta na usoro na izi ezi nke azụmahịa, na-enye nkwa siri ike nke njedebe. Na njedebe, a na-ewere azụmahịa dị ka ihe a na-apụghị ịgbagha agbagha, na-ebelata ihe egwu nke mmefu ego abụọ ma ọ bụ nlọghachi azụmaahịa. Namada na-agbaso ụzọ yiri nke ahụ na Anoma, usoro ọzọ a maara maka ngwọta nhazi ya. Namada na-anabata ọnọdụ fractal, nke na-enye ohere maka imepụta agbụ ndị a kpọchiri akpọchi n'ime isi blockchain. Nhazi fractal a na-enye ohere maka nhazi kwụ ọtọ site na ikesa ibu ahụ n'ọtụtụ ọnọdụ, na-eme ka ikike na arụmọrụ nke netwọk ahụ ka mma.

## Njikọ Atụmatụ Namada na Zcash

Dịka akwụkwọ e bipụtara n'oge na-adịbeghị anya si kwuo, enwere ike ịchọta ya [Blọọgụ Usoro Namada](https://namada.net/blog/rfc-proposal-for-a-strategic-alliance-between-namada-and-zcash), ndị otu nọ n'azụ Namada Protocol nwere obi ụtọ ịnye atụmatụ na arịrịọ maka okwu (RFC) maka njikọ aka dị n'etiti akụ, agbụ, na obodo Namada na Zcash.

![Namada-Zcash Strategic Alliance Diagram](/content-images/image-2-68804c60f3.webp)

Njikọ a tụrụ aro gụnyere ihe atọ dị mkpa. Nke mbụ, enwere ebe a ga-enye onyinye iji nye ego maka ọrụ ndị ga-eweta uru nye ma Zcash ma Namada. Nke abụọ, a ga-ekenye ndị nwere ZEC ohere ikuku nke tokens NAM. N'ikpeazụ, a na-eme atụmatụ ịtọlite akwa mmiri nke a na-ebelata ntụkwasị obi nke na-ejikọ Zcash na Namada. Ozugbo etinyere ya, àkwà mmiri a ga-enye ndị nwere ZEC, nke a na-akpọ Zolders, ohere iji ZEC ha na Namada. Ọzọkwa, Zolders ga-enwe ohere ịnweta usoro gburugburu ebe obibi Cosmos na Ethereum sara mbara site na Namada. Ị nwere ike ịmụtakwu gbasara njikọ aka atụmatụ na [Ọgbakọ Obodo Zcash](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/rfc-proposal-for-a-strategic-alliance-between-namada-and-zcash/44372)
