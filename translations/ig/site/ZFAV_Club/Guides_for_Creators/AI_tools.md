# Ngwaọrụ AI: Ederede, Foto, Vidiyo, Mmepụta ọdịyo (n'ịntanetị)

Lee ndepụta ga-eme ka ndụ dịkwuo mfe. Otu esi eji ha eme ihe bụ ihe ọzọ.

Ide ihe mkpali kacha mma nwere usoro mmụta. O kwere omume ịmụta ihe ndị bụ isi ngwa ngwa.

## Mmepụta/Ndị enyemaka ederede

[Ihe nyocha ngwa ngwa](https://novelai.net/tokenizer) (tokenizer) iji mụta otu esi arụ ọrụ mkpali na ọnụọgụgụ tokens ha na-eji.

Ị nwere ike ịrịọ ndị enyemaka Chatbot mgbe niile ka ha nyere gị aka ịmepụta ezigbo ndụmọdụ maka gị.

### Ngwaọrụ efu:

- [Claude nke Anthropic](https://claude.ai/) - nwere ike ịchọ VPN iji debanye aha (naanị na US)
- [ChatGPT 3.5](https://chat.openai.com/) - rịọrọ maka nọmba ekwentị
- [Nkata Bing (GPT4)](https://www.bing.com/search?q=Bing+AI&showconv=1&FORM=hpcodx) - obere ntụziaka kwa ụbọchị
- [Llama 2 - 70B](https://www.llama2.ai/) - n'efu na weebụsaịtị

### Ngwaọrụ akwụ ụgwọ:

- [Nkata2Z Free2Z](https://free2z.com/ai) - Kwụọ ụgwọ site na ZEC / 2Z maka GPT-3.5 na GPT-4 senti/ngwa ngwa
- [Nkata GPT-4](https://chat.openai.com/auth/login) - $20/ọnwa.
