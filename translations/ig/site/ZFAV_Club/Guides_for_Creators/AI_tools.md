# Ngwá ọrụ [tools] AI: Ịmepụta Ederede, Onyonyo, Vidio, na Ọdịyo (n'ịntanetị)

Nke a bụ ndepụta iji mee ka ndụ dị mfe. Otú e si eji ha eme ihe bụ okwu ọzọ.

Ide prompt kacha mma nwere usoro mmụta. Ọ ga-ekwe omume ịmụta ihe ndị bụ isi ngwa ngwa.

## Ịmepụta Ederede/ Ndị Nnyemaaka

[Nyochaa prompt](https://novelai.net/tokenizer) (tokenizer) iji mụta otú prompt si arụ ọrụ na token ole ha na-eji.

Ị nwere ike ịjụ ndị nnyemaaka Chatbot mgbe niile ka ha nyere gị aka ịmepụta prompt ọma.

### Ngwá ọrụ efu:

- [Claude nke Anthropic](https://claude.ai/) - ị nwere ike ịchọ VPN iji debanye aha (naanị US)
- [ChatGPT 3.5](https://chat.openai.com/) - na-arịọ nọmba ekwentị
- [Bing Chat (GPT4)](https://www.bing.com/search?q=Bing+AI&showconv=1&FORM=hpcodx) - prompt a na-ekwe kwa ụbọchị dị ole na ole
- [Llama 2 - 70B](https://www.llama2.ai/) - efu na weebụsaịtị

### Ngwá ọrụ akwụ ụgwọ:

- [Free2Z Chat2Z](https://free2z.com/ai) - Jiri ZEC kwụọ ụgwọ / 2Zs maka GPT-3.5 na GPT-4 cents/prompt
- [Chat GPT-4](https://chat.openai.com/auth/login) - $20/ọnwa.
