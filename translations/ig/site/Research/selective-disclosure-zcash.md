Na Ọgọọst 17, kọmitii Zcash Community Grants zukọrọ iji tụlee atụmatụ a kpọrọ Shielded Compliance Bridge. Deborah Olaboye, onye na-atụnye aka na open-source nke ọrụ ya gụnyere ebe nchekwa execution-specs nke Ethereum, ewulitela proof of concept nke na-anabata unified full viewing key, na-enyocha compact blocks megide testnet indexer, ma na-eburu "compliance reference" n'ime mpaghara memo [memo] nke Zcash na-ejikọta mbufe nke e chebere echebe na ndekọ Travel Rule nke nọ na mpụga chain. Ọ rịọrọ ikike [grant] iji mezue ya, ma kọmitii ahụ jụrụ.

[echiche ha](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-community-grants-meeting-minutes-8-17-2026/57119) bụ na njikọta nrubeisi na-adabere na exchange [exchange] ma ọ bụ ụlọ ọrụ kpọmkwem ga-eji ha, na ụkpụrụ ọnụ ha niile nwere ike iwulite dabere na ha adịghị, nakwa na ma ọ bụrụ na onye mmekọ arịọbeghị ngwá ọrụ [tools] ahụ, o yighị ka a ga-anabata ya. N'otu nzukọ ahụ, a jụkwara atụmatụ ndị ọzọ maka ihe ndị yiri ya, otu maka igwe mebere emepe "programmable selective disclosure", na usoro owuwu Nzuzo na ntụkwasị obi, nke gụnyere map nke okwu iwu US.

N'ezie, mkpebi ndị a abụghị ihe ịrịba ama na ọ dịghị onye chọrọ nke a. Mgbe e jiri ha tụnyere ihe ndị ọzọ mere n'afọ ahụ, ọ bụ ihe megidere ya. Binance rịọrọ n'ogo protocol na 2024 ma nweta ZIP n'ime afọ ahụ. Gemini na-agba withdrawals nke e chebere echebe n'okpuru nlekọta iwu kemgbe 2020. Ecosystem ahụ hibere otu Washington maka amụma na June na ụlọ ahịa njikọta ụlọ ọrụ na Ọgọọst. Ihe kọmitii ahụ kwuru, n'ụzọ dị irè, bụ na edebeghị mkpa ahụ ka ọ bụrụ ihe achọrọ onye ọ bụla pụrụ iwulite dabere na ya.

## Otu sistemụ ahụ si arụ ọrụ

Adreesị akpa ego [address] nke e chebere echebe nke Zcash bụ Igodo ego [spending key] na-achịkwa ya. A na-enweta viewing keys site na ya, nke nwere ike ịgụ data Azụmahia ma enweghị ike ibugharị ego. Full viewing key na-ahụ Azụmahia na-abata na nke na-apụ maka akaụntụ ahụ, incoming viewing key na-ahụ naanị ego anataara, ebe outgoing viewing key na-enye onye zitere ego ohere iwughachi ihe o zitere. Kemgbe [ZIP 316](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0316), a chịkọtara ndị a n'ime unified viewing keys, otu full na otu incoming, nke kpọmkwem maka akaụntụ, ebe akaụntụ bụ ọnọdụ n'ime osisi igodo e-akpa ego [wallet] n'okpuru [ZIP 32](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0032). Unified full viewing key na-achịkọta akụkụ per-pool maka pool [pool] ọ bụla e chebere echebe nke akaụntụ ahụ ji, ọ nwekwara transparent extended public key nke akaụntụ ahụ, ya mere ikesa otu na-ekpughe chain [chain] Oha [transparent] yana akụkọ nke e chebere echebe.

Viewing key n'echiche a na-alaghachi azụ n'oge. Ọ na-ewepụta Nzobe ederede [encryption] nke akụkọ akaụntụ ahụ dum kemgbe mgbe e kere akaụntụ ahụ, a pụghịkwa iweghachite ya ozugbo e kesara ya, belụsọ ibuga ego ahụ n'akaụntụ ọhụrụ. Enweghị viewing key maka Azụmahia ọ bụla n'otu n'otu ma ọ bụ nke oge ya nwere oke. Josh Swihart, onye isi nchịkwa nke ZODL, ụlọ ọrụ e hibere n'afọ a site n'aka otu injinia ECC mbụ nke protocol, kwadoro nke a, ma kwuokwa na ikpughe Kwụọ ụhwọ [payment] kwa Azụmahia “adịghị emejuputa ebe ọ bụla.”

zcashd, reference node mbụ, wepụtara ụzọ RPC abụọ, `z_getpaymentdisclosure` na `z_validatepaymentdisclosure`, nke na-enye onye zitere ego ohere igosi proof [proof] nke otu Kwụọ ụhwọ nye onye nke atọ. [Akwụkwọ RPC](https://zcash.github.io/rpc/z_getpaymentdisclosure.html) na-akpọ ha nnwale, nke gbanyụrụ ma ọ gwụla ma onye na-arụ ọrụ setịpụrụ akara experimental-features, ma na-emejuputa "Payment Disclosure ZIP nke ka bụ ọrụ a na-arụ." ZIP ahụ, [303](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0303), ka akara ugbu a dị ka nke a wepụrụ, ebe onye nọchiri ya, [ZIP 311](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0311), ka bụ draft na-enweghị mmejuputa ọ bụla agbakwunyere. Ụzọ ndị ahụ kpuchiri naanị pool Sprout, pool mbụ e chebere echebe nke 2016, ma agbatịghị ha ruo Sapling ma ọ bụ Orchard, ya bụ, pool ndị na-ebu Azụmahia ọ bụla nke e chebere echebe nke ugbu a. zcashd n'onwe ya [rutere njedebe ndụ](https://zcash.github.io/zcash/user/end-of-life.html) na Julaị 18, 2026, nkwụsị e mere atụmatụ tupu upgrade Ironwood. Ma Zebra, consensus node [node] nke Zcash Foundation, ma ọ bụ Zallet, e-akpa ego onye nọchiri ya, [depụtara](https://github.com/zcash/zallet/releases) usoro ikpughe. Maka pool ndị dị mkpa, ikpughe kwa Azụmahia ka bụ ebumnuche imewe.

Ebumnuche ahụ ka dị ndụ, ma ọ dị anya karịa ka o siri yie na Julaị. [ZIP 231](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0231), memo bundles, bụ draft nke ga-emepụta ohere maka ya: Swihart kọwara memo bundles dịka ntọala ga-adị n'ikpeazụ maka authenticated reply-to nke nwere ike ịrụ ọrụ dị ka proof of payment, ebe draft nke dabere na ya, [authenticated reply addresses](https://zips.z.cash/draft-ecc-authenticated-reply-addrs), chọrọ proofs buru ibu karịa mpaghara memo ugbu a. Memo bundles bụ mgbanwe usoro Azụmahia, na òtù ise na-ahazi votu coinholder [NU7](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/nu7-coinholder-vote/56912) kwetara na Ọgọọst na mgbanwe usoro ahụ anọghị n'ókè NU7 ma dị n'ókè upgrade nke na-esote. Votu ahụ n'onwe ya, nke na-agba site n'Ọgọọst 25 ruo Septemba 14, tinyere issuance, amụma ugwo [fee], ohere n'etiti block [block] na mwepụ Sprout n'aka ndị jidere; ikpughe anọghị na ballot ahụ. Ụzọ ochie, ZIP 311, achọghị mgbanwe consensus [consensus] mana ka bụ draft na-enweghị mmejuputa na-enweghị mkpuchi Orchard.

Ya mere, ihe a na-ewepụta taa bụ viewing keys nke oke ha bụ akaụntụ, nke full ma ọ bụ incoming naanị, na-alaghachi azụ n'oge, a pụghị iweghachite ha, ma na-ekesara ha site n'uche onye ji akaụntụ, ebe ikpughe n'ogo Azụmahia dị na roadmap.

## Ịghọta ikpughe

Enwere ike idobe usoro ikpughe, na Zcash ma ọ bụ n'ebe ọ bụla ọzọ, n'ihe ndị a: Ihe a na-ekpughe (oke). Onye a na-enye ya (onye natara ikike). Mgbe ikike ahụ malitere ịrụ ọrụ na oke oge gara aga ọ ruru (oge). Ma enwere ike iweghachite ya (ikike iweghachite). Na onye malitere ya, onye data ahụ metụtara ma ọ bụ ọchịchị (mmalite).

Usoro nrubeisi bụ ihe achọrọ n'ihe ndị ahụ, ebe owuwu sistemụ bụ ọnọdụ e nwere gbasara ha. Viewing keys nke Zcash nwere, site n'usoro imewe, oke sara mbara, onye ji akaụntụ họọrọ onye natara ikike, ha na-alaghachi azụ n'oge kpamkpam, a pụghị iweghachite ha, onye ọrụ na-amalite ha. Akụkụ ihe achọrọ, dịka exchanges na ndị nlekọta si etinye ya n'ọrụ, na-enwekarị oke n'otu mmekọrịta ma ọ bụ oge, a na-enye ya ụlọ ọrụ a kpọrọ aha ma ọ bụ onye na-eweta analytics ya, na-aga n'ihu site n'oge onboarding gaa n'ihu, a na-eweghachite ya mgbe mmekọrịta kwụsịrị, ụlọ ọrụ ahụ na-amalitekwa ya dịka ọnọdụ ọrụ.

A na-akpọ ihe abụọ ndị a Mpughe ahọpụtara. N'ụwa credential, Mpughe ahọpụtara pụtara igosi otu akụkụ àgwà sitere na credential ejiri signature [signature] bịanye aka n'ekwupụghị ndị fọdụrụ, nke bụ ihe [SD-JWT](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc9901/), ISO mdoc na BBS+ signatures wuru iji mee. Nkeji ikpughe bụ àgwà, oge ya bụ oge ngosi, onye ji ya na-ekpebi kwa mmekọrịta.

N'ụwa Kwụọ ụhwọ e chebere echebe, ahịrịokwu ahụ pụtara ikpughe data Azụmahia nye onye a họọrọ, ebe nkeji ya bụ akụkọ akaụntụ na oge ya bụ mgbe ọ bụla e nyefere igodo ahụ.

## Ihe akụkụ ihe achọrọ rịọrọ

Swihart kwuru na naanị arịrịọ nrubeisi n'ogo protocol otu injinia ahụ natara bụ nke sitere na Binance, nke na Jenụwarị 2024 chọrọ nkwa ebe deposit si n'ụzọ Oha. Azịza otu ahụ bụ [ZIP 320](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0320), transparent-source-only addresses, na ederede ZIP na-edekwa n'ezie mmalite “dịka Binance chọrọ.” Adreesị TEX bụ ụzọ Oha nwaoge dị mkpirikpi nke na-ekwe nkwa na exchange na-anata ego sitere na isi mmalite Oha. Ọ na-enye exchange ụzọ nloghachi na-enyeghị ya akụkọ onye zitere ego. Otu mgbanwe protocol ahụ mere maka exchange na-ekpughe kpọmkwem ihe exchange ahụ rịọrọ, ọ dịghịkwa ọzọ.

Kemgbe upgrade Sapling, Swihart na-ekwu na ọ nweghịzị ihe kpatara teknụzụ exchange agaghị akwado Azụmahia e chebere echebe, n'ihi na ịmepụta proofs na-adị ngwa, a mataworị ndị ahịa exchange n'oge onboarding, ma enhanced due diligence dị maka ikpe ndị chọrọ ya. N'echiche ahụ, ọdịiche dị n'etiti igwe ọrụ na usoro nrubeisi abụghị primitive na-efu efu, kama ọ bụ mkparịta ụka na-efu efu.

Divij Pandya na-arụ ọrụ n'akụkụ nke ọzọ nke mkparịta ụka ahụ. Ọ bụ onye isi nchịkwa Pretty Good Policy maka Zcash, òtù Washington maka amụma e hibere na June dịka n'ihu nke atụmatụ mbụ ECC duziri, ọrụ òtù ya bụ ime ka a mata ikpughe viewing key dịka usoro nrubeisi a na-anakwere n'ime akwụkwọ onye ọrụ nrubeisi nwere ike ịkpọtụrụ. Dị ka o si mara, o kwuru, ọ dịbeghị onye nlekọta ọ bụla metụla viewing keys aka dị ka ngwá ọrụ. Oke ọrụ PGPZ, n'ezie, bụ nnabata kama nduzi maka ndị na-ewu ihe. Enweghị ike ịkọwapụta ngwá ọrụ megide ihe achọrọ ruo mgbe onye nwere ikike ekpebiela maka ngwá ọrụ ahụ, ya mere nnabata bụ nzọụkwụ dị n'elu. Paul Brigner bịanyere aka na ndebanye òtù ahụ dịka onye guzobere PGPZ na dịka onye isi amụma na iwu nke ZODL, ya mere mgbalị amụma na injinia na-ekekọrịta onye isi na thesis.

Nkwupụta Julaị 21 maka ndekọ nye obere kọmitii House banyere nlekọta FinCEN kọwara Zcash dịka nke nwere “ikike Mpughe ahọpụtara, na-enye ndị ọrụ ohere iji aka ha kesaa nkọwa Azụmahia site n'iji viewing keys n'etiti auditors, ndị ọchịchị ụtụ isi, ma ọ bụ ndị ọrụ nrubeisi,” ma na-ekwu maka zero-knowledge [zero-knowledge] proofs “ejikọrọ na selective viewing keys” iji gosi eziokwu akọwapụtara. Akwụkwọ ozi nkọwa na docket FinCEN, [FINCEN-2026-0101-0050](https://www.regulations.gov/comment/FINCEN-2026-0101-0050), na-ekwu, dịka Pandya siri kwuo, na viewing keys na Mpughe ahọpụtara kwesịrị ịbụ usoro nrubeisi a na-anakwere. Ajụjụ mepere emepe ha jụrụ onye nlekọta bụ nke granularity, ma ikpughe oke ya bụ akaụntụ, nke gụnyere akụkọ niile, ezuru maka “ozi Azụmahia ezubere iche,” ma ọ bụ ma azịza ya na-eche ZIP 231.

Ruo mgbe e kpebiri nke ahụ, òtù nrubeisi na-eche mmachi nke onye biputere n'ogbe mkparịta ụka pseudonymous, strahncryptography, [kọwara](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-shielded-compliance-bridge/56862) n'ime mkparịta ụka banyere atụmatụ Olaboye: ha na-agbaso iwu ahụ n'ụzọ zuru ezu, ha agaghị anakwa ụzọ a na-amataghị ma onye nlekọta ma ọ bụ ụlọ ọrụ iwu ekwenyeghị na ya, n'agbanyeghị otu cryptography siri sie ike. Nhazi ikpughe ọ bụla na-ejikọta reference na Azụmahia na-egosi naanị hop ikpeazụ, hop ikpeazụ ahụ nwekwara ike ịbụ onye na-edebe ego n'onwe ya. Viewing key nwere otu mmachi ahụ. Ọ na-ekpughe Azụmahia onye ji akaụntụ, ọ bụghị ebe ego ndị counterparties sitere, nke bụ ajụjụ sanctions screen na-ajụ.

Viewing keys na-emezu ihe achọrọ ebe ọ bụla ihe a chọrọ bụ proof nke onye data ahụ metụtara nyere maka ihe mere, dịka provenance nke deposit onye debere ego họọrọ ịkọwa, audit afọ ofufo, ndebanye ụtụ isi, ma ọ bụ proof na e mere Kwụọ ụhwọ kpọmkwem. Ndị a bụ ikpughe onye ọrụ malitere, na-alaghachi azụ n'oge, nke otu ugboro. Ha anaghị emezu ihe achọrọ ebe ọ bụ continuous monitoring site na onboarding gaa n'ihu, sanctions screening megide counterparties onye ji akaụntụ anaghị achịkwa, itinye oke n'oge ma ọ bụ mmekọrịta, ma ọ bụ iweghachite n'oge offboarding.

Gemini anabatala withdrawals gaa Adreesị akpa ego e chebere echebe kemgbe [Septemba 2020](https://www.gemini.com/blog/gemini-adds-support-for-shielded-zcash-transactions), mgbe onye na-ekwuchitere ya nyere mkparịta ụka na-aga n'ihu na ndị nlekọta otuto, gbasaa nke ahụ ruo pool Orchard na Nọvemba 2025, na ruo pool post-Ironwood ụbọchị abụọ ka activation gasịrị na Julaị 2026. Ọ na-anabata deposits sitere na Adreesị akpa ego e chebere echebe banye n'ime njide Oha. Ndepụta Adreesị akpa ego withdrawal nke Kraken, [](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360001474826-supported-address-formats-for-cryptocurrency-withdrawal), na-akwado naanị Adreesị akpa ego ZEC Oha, Coinbase bụkwa naanị Oha, ebe Binance chọrọ TEX. Ihe nnukwu exchange ọ bụla a na-achịkwa na-enyeghị ugbu a bụ njide e chebere echebe, Adreesị akpa ego deposit e chebere echebe, ma ọ bụ usoro ọrụ a ga-etinye viewing key onye ahịa n'ime ya.

A na-ewu ingestion otu oyi akwa n'okpuru. N'ime atụmatụ ndị dị na gburugburu ikike retroactive nke coinholder duziri [a na-atụle na Septemba a](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/call-for-proposals-coinholder-directed-retroactive-grants-program-q3/56885) bụ ZecBooks, ngwá ọrụ bookkeeping Mac nke na-ebubata view-only key, na-ekewa notes [note], ma na-ebupụ ngwugwu ahịrị nwere oke maka accountant n'enyeghị ohere na-adịgide adịgide n'e-akpa ego; ZecLedger, ngwá ọrụ read-only maka cost-basis na reconciliation, nke wuru gburugburu unified full viewing key, ma nwalee ya megide e-akpa ego mainnet; na seer-sync, engine chain-sync nke na-ewere viewing key ka payment processor ma ọ bụ sistemụ accounting nwee ike ịhụ ọrụ e chebere echebe n'enweghị ijide Igodo ego. Ọ dịghị nke bụ usoro ọrụ exchange, ọ dịghịkwa nke enwetala nlebara anya onye nlekọta. Nke ọ bụla bụ ọkara nsogbu ahụ chere accountant ihu, nke otu ma ọ bụ mmadụ abụọ wuru na-enweghị onye mmekọ rịọrọ ya, na scoped export nke ZecBooks bụ Mpughe ahọpụtara na-arụ ọrụ n'interface ebe a na-achọkarị ya mbụ: n'etiti onye ọrụ e chebere echebe na onye na-edobe akwụkwọ ya.

## Klaasị ahụ, ọ bụghị coin

Ndị na-ewu ihe Zcash na-arụ ọrụ na nsogbu nke architecture Nzuzo ọ bụla na-ekekọrịta.

N'ime imewe confidential-EVM Zama, access control list na-ekpebi Adreesị akpa ego ndị nwere ike ịrịọ Nzobe ederede ciphertext ka ewepụtara, na network threshold nke ndị na-arụ ọrụ key-management na-eme Nzobe ederede ahụ na arịrịọ. Nke ahụ na-ebufe axis onye natara ikike site n'igodo onye ọrụ na-enyefe gaa n'ikike contract na-edekọ, ma mee ka mmalite bụrụ programmable, ebe contract nwere ike inye auditor ikike iwepụta Nzobe ederede dịka ọnọdụ iji ya. Ọ naghị eme ka ikpughe bụrụ nke a pụrụ iweghachite n'echiche onye ọrụ nrubeisi pụtara, n'ihi na ciphertext e wepụtalarị Nzobe ederede ya na-anọgide otu ahụ, ọ na-agbanwere njide igodo onye ọrụ maka ịkwụwa aka ọtọ nke ndị na-arụ ọrụ dịka ntọala ntụkwasị obi. Igodo auditor n'ogo asset, nke ụdị dị na confidential transfer extension nke Solana na imewe ERC ezoro ezo nke Avalanche, na-edobe onye natara ikike n'ogo token [token], n'ihi na issuer ma ọ bụ auditor a họpụtara nwere ike ịgụ balances na transfers maka asset ahụ.

Inco ebipụtala [usoro owuwu](https://www.inco.org/blog/programmable-view-access) maka viewing e buru ụzọ kọwaa, ebe a na-edobe ohere n'ime key material n'oge Azụmahia, ma e jiri ya tụnyere programmable view access, ebe ikike viewing bụ ọnọdụ contract enwere ike inye, tinye oke ma weghachite ka e mesịrị.

## Ihe mere o ji sie ike nke ukwuu

Gịnị na-ekewa viewing key na usoro nrubeisi?

Viewing key na-ekpughe akụkọ nke onye ji akaụntụ n'onwe ya. Sanctions screening na continuous monitoring na-ajụ maka counterparties, ebe ego dị n'akụkụ nke ọzọ nke Azụmahia ọ bụla sitere, nke ahụ bụ kpọmkwem ozi pool e chebere echebe dị iji zoo. Ọ dịbeghị onye gosiri imewe na-aza ajụjụ counterparty na-enweghị ledger Oha ma ọ bụ onye nke atọ a tụkwasịrị obi.

Ọ bụrụ na memo bundles abata na upgrade nke na-esote NU7 ma draft reply-address eso ya, “ozi Azụmahia ezubere iche” n'ime ndebanye PGPZ ga-abụ ihe nkịtị. Nke ahụ dịkarịa ala upgrades abụọ dị anya, azịza nlekọta na-abịakwa n'usoro oge onye nlekọta.

A na-ahụ ọchịchọ ahụ n'ụzọ dị mfe n'ọnụọgụ ndị mmadụ na-ewu ihe na-aga n'akụkụ ya. Ihe na-efu bụ ihe achọrọ kwụ ọtọ maka ikpughe na-echekwa Nzuzo. Zcash bụ ecosystem ebe a na-akparịta mkpebi ahụ n'ihu ọha, site n'aka injinia, ndị nkwado, ndị na-ewu ihe, exchange na kọmitii ikike ndị yiri ka ha kwenyere na ebe aga mana na-arụrịta ụka maka usoro nzọụkwụ. Ndị onye mmepụta [developer] naanị ha malitere ịrụkwa ya n'oyi akwa accounting na-echeghị ka e dee ihe achọrọ ahụ.
