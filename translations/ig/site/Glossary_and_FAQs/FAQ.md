## Gịnị bụ Zcash?

<div className="rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card p-5 my-4">

Zcash bụ ego dijitalụ nke nwere Azụmahia [transaction] ngwa ngwa, nke nzuzo, na ugwo [fee] dị ala. Nzuzo [privacy] bụ njirimara bụ isi nke Zcash. Ọ bụ ya bidoro iji proof zero-knowledge [zero-knowledge] zoo Azụmahia niile.

Ọtụtụ e-akpa ego [wallet] dị maka Kwụọ ụhwọ ngwa ngwa, nke mkpanaka, nke nchekwa, na nke nzuzo: [E-akpa ego](/using-zcash/wallets)

</div>

## Kedu ka m ga-esi nweta Zcash?

<div className="rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card p-5 my-4">

Ị nwere ike ịzụta ZEC na [exchange Onye nlekota](/using-zcash/custodial-exchanges), [DEX](/using-zcash/dex), ma ọ bụ [ikpo okwu Mgbawe etiti](/using-zcash/centralized-swaps).

Ị nwekwara ike ịzụta Zcash n’etiti mmadụ na mmadụ ma ọ bụ nweta ya site n’ịgwọpụta [mining].

</div>

## Gịnị bụ ọdịiche dị n’etiti Zcash na ego dijitalụ ndị ọzọ?

<div className="rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card p-5 my-4">

Zcash nwere Nzuzo karịa Bitcoin ma ọ bụ Ethereum n’ụzọ bụ isi. Ọ na-enye oge block ngwa ngwa (sekọnd 75), ugwo dị ala, na mmelite mgbe niile.

Ndị ọrụ nwere ike ịhọrọ n’etiti Azụmahia **Oha [transparent]** ma ọ bụ **nke e chebere echebe [shielded]**. Maka ozi ndị ọzọ, lee [Usoro gburugburu nke e chebere echebe](https://electriccoin.co/blog/shielded-ecosystem).

</div>

## Kedu ka e si achị protocol Zcash?

<div className="rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card p-5 my-4">

A na-achị protocol ahụ site na usoro **Atụmatụ Mmelite Zcash (ZIP)**. Onye ọbụla nwere ike izipu mbipụta mbụ nke ZIP. Ndị obodo na-arụrịta ụka gbasara mbipụta ndị ahụ, ndị ndezi ZIP na-anakwere ma ọ bụ na-ajụ ha:

- [Daira Hopwood](https://twitter.com/feministPLT) (Electric Coin Company)
- [Deirdre Connolly](https://twitter.com/durumcrustulum) (Zcash Foundation)

A na-ede mkpebi n’ime nkọwapụta ahụ ma kwado ha on-chain mgbe network nakweere ha.

</div>

## Ebee ka Azụmahia m nọ?

<div className="rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card p-5 my-4">

Buru ụzọ gụọ [ntuziaka anyị gbasara ndị nyocha block](/guides/blockchain-explorers). Mgbe ahụ lelee [Zcash Onye Nchọpụta Block](https://mainnet.zcashexplorer.app).

Enweghị oge ngwụcha nkeji 25 a kapịrị ọnụ ma ọ bụ oge nkwụghachi ego akpaka. Lelee ogo ngwụcha e dekọrọ na e-akpa ego [wallet] gị na ma egwupụtara Kwụọ ụhwọ mbụ. Ọ bụrụ na ọ gwụrụ n'egwupụtaghị ya, emeghị ya na on-chain [on-chain]; ngwụcha anaghị emepụta Azụmahia nkwụghachi ego. Kwe ka e-akpa ego ahụ mekọrịta tupu ị soro ntụziaka ịnwale ọzọ ya. Hụ [nchọpụta nsogbu Azụmahia](/using-zcash/transactions#troubleshooting-stuck-transactions) na [ZIP 203](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0203).

**Ihe ndị a na-ahụkarị mere Azụmahia nwere ike ghara ịpụta:**

- Njikọ ịntanetị kwụsịrị
- Ugwo Azụmahia dị oke ala
- Network juputara
- Ntinye Oha dị ọtụtụ nke ukwuu (ogo ya dị ukwuu)

**Ndụmọdụ iji nwee ihe ịga nke ọma:**

- Jiri njikọ kwụsiri ike
- Kwụọ ugwo ọkọlọtọ (ma ọ bụ karịa maka mkpa pụrụ iche)
- Chere ma gbalịa ọzọ mgbe e mesịrị
- Jiri ntinye ole na ole mee ka Azụmahia dị ntakịrị

</div>

## Zcash ọ bụ n’ezie nke Nzuzo?

<div className="rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card p-5 my-4">

**Ee.** Zcash na-ezo ozi onye zitere, ọnụọgụ ego, na onye natara maka Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe.

Zcash **anaghị**:

- Ezo Azụmahia nwere ọtụtụ mbinye aka (njikọta FROST ka na-echere)
- Echebe megide njikọ ya na Azụmahia Oha
- Zoo Adreesị IP

Ọgụgụ ọzọ: [Usoro gburugburu nke e chebere echebe](https://web.archive.org/web/20260903010654/https://electriccoin.co/blog/shielded-ecosystem/)

</div>

## zcashd akwụsịla ịrụ ọrụ, gịnị ka m ga-agba ugbu a?

<div className="rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card p-5 my-4">

zcashd akwụsịla. Mbipụta 6.20.0 mechiri onwe ya na block 3417100 n’ụbọchị 18 Julaị 2026, ọ kwadoghịkwa Ironwood (NU6.3) mgbe ọ bụla, yabụ enweghị mbipụta ọzọ ị ga-emelite gaa na ya.

Kama nke ahụ, gbaa stack Z3. Mmemme atọ kewara ọrụ zcashd na-arụbu naanị ya:

- **[Zebra](/zcash-tech/zebra-full-node)** na-akwado chain
- **[Zaino](/zcash-tech/zaino)** na-edepụta ya ma na-enye data e-akpa ego
- **[Zallet](/zcash-tech/zallet)** na-echekwa ego gị

[Zakura](/zcash-tech/zakura-node) bụ full node ọzọ. Ọ na-agbakwa zcashd n’elu onwe ya, nke na-enyere aka ma ọ bụrụ na sọftụwia ndị ọzọ ka na-atụ anya interface ochie ahụ.

Malite na [ntuziaka mbugharị](/guides/migration-guide-zcashd-to-zebrad-zallet).

</div>

## Kedu ka m ga-esi bugharịa e-akpa ego zcashd m gaa na Zallet?

<div className="rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card p-5 my-4">

Zallet na-ebuga `wallet.dat` dịbu, yabụ ị na-edobe ego gị na Adreesị akpa ego gị.

Buru ụzọ mepụta ndabere nke `wallet.dat`, sorozie [ntuziaka mbugharị](/guides/migration-guide-zcashd-to-zebrad-zallet). Debe ndabere ahụ ruo mgbe ị kwadoro nguzo ego gị na Zallet.

[Zallet ntụaka ngwa ngwa](/using-zcash/zallet-quick-reference-guide) na-ekpuchi iwu ndị a na-eji kwa ụbọchị ozugbo ị gafere.

</div>

## Gịnị bụ Ironwood, ọ dịkwa ihe m ga-eme?

<div className="rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card p-5 my-4">

Ironwood bụ network upgrade NU6.3, nke dị na mainnet kemgbe block 3,428,143 n’ụbọchị 28 Julaị 2026. Ọ na-agbakwunye pool nke e chebere echebe nke e wuru na circuit e doziri, ma mechie pool [Orchard](/using-zcash/shielded-pools) nke ochie, ka onye ọbụla wee nwee ike ịchọpụta na ọ dịghị ZEC e mepụtara n’ụzọ aghụghọ.

**Ọ bụrụ na ị jidere ego nke e chebere echebe, ọ pụrụ ịdị mkpa ka a bugharịa ya.** Uru na-apụ na Orchard naanị site na [turnstile](/zcash-tech/the-turnstile), e-akpa ego dị iche iche na-ejikwa mbugharị ahụ n’oge na n’ụzọ dị iche iche. Jụọ onye na-enye gị e-akpa ego tupu iji aka gị bugharịa ihe ọ bụla.

Gụọ [Ironwood](/zcash-tech/ironwood) maka ihe gbanwere na ihe kpatara ya.

</div>

## Kedu full node ka m kwesịrị ịgba?

<div className="rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card p-5 my-4">

**[Zebra](/zcash-tech/zebra-full-node)** bụ onye nnọchi anya kpọmkwem nke zcashd, bụrụkwa node nke ntụziaka mbugharị na-atụ anya. Họrọ ya ma ọ bụrụ na ịnweghị ihe kpatara ịghara ime ya.

**[Zakura](/zcash-tech/zakura-node)** bụ mmejuputa nke abụọ ezubere maka ọnụ ọgụgụ buru ibu, nwere sync ngwa ngwa na nkwachapụ block nke e wuru n’ime ya. Họrọ ya ma ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ ihe ndị ahụ, ma ọ bụ ọ bụrụ na ị dabere na sọftụwia ka na-agwa zcashd okwu.

Ha abụọ na-akwado otu chain ahụ, yabụ nke a bụ nhọrọ ọrụ karịa nke consensus.

</div>
