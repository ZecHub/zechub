# Ihe Ndị Dị Mkpa Maka Zcash

## Kedu ihe bụ isi gbasara akụnụba nke Zcash?

Isi ego Zcash's bụ otu ihe ahụ Bitcoin's na-enye nde ZEC iri abụọ na otu nde. Kwa sekọnd iri asaa na ise, a na-enweta ego ọhụrụ [ngọngọ](https://zcash.readthedocs.io/en/latest/rtd_pages/glossary.html#:~:text=Block,mempool%20in%20an%20unconfirmed%20state.) A na-egwupụta ya na blockchain Zcash, ụgwọ ọrụ blọk nke 1.5625 ZEC na-abata na mgbasa ozi. A na-ekesa ụgwọ ọrụ blọk a nye ndị na-egwuputa ihe na ndị na-egwuputa ihe na [Ego mmepe Zcash](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1014).

Ọnụọgụ ụgwọ ọrụ nke ngọngọ ahụ na-ebelata ọkara ihe dị ka afọ anọ ọ bụla ruo mgbe nde ZEC 21 niile na-agbasa. Ọnụọgụ Zcash fọrọ nke nta ka ọ bụrụ kpọmkwem yiri nke Bitcoin. Ọ dị mkpa ịmara na ka a na-emepụta mkpụrụ ego ọhụrụ, mmụba na-ebelata, na ọkara nke ọ bụla, ọnụego ahụ na-ebelata nke ukwuu.
