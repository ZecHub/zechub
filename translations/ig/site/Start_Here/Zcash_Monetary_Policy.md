# Amụma Ego Zcash

## Nchịkọta

* Zcash nwere ọnụọgụ ZEC nde 21 a kapịrị ọnụ, otu oke ahụ dị ka nke Bitcoin.
* Kemgbe NU7 malitere ọrụ na 6 November 2026, block ọhụrụ na-abịa ihe dị ka sekọnd 25 ọ bụla ma na-emepụta ZEC 0.52083333.
* Nke a na-agbakọta ruo ihe dị ka ZEC 1,800 ọhụrụ kwa ụbọchị, otu ọnụọgụ kwa ụbọchị ahụ dị ka tupu NU7.
* A na-ekesa ụgwọ block ọ bụla: 80% nye onye na-egwụputa, 8% nye Zcash Community Grants, na 12% nye igbe nchekwa akpọchiri akpọchi.
* A na-ebelata ụgwọ block na ọkara ihe dị ka afọ anọ ọ bụla. A na-atụ anya halving nke na-abịa n’ihe dị ka ngwụcha November 2028.

## Nkọwa bụ Isi

### Gịnị bụ akụnụba nke Zcash?

Ebe ego Zcash bụ otu ihe ahụ dị ka ọnụọgụ nkeji ego ZEC nde 21 a kapịrị ọnụ nke Bitcoin. Ná nkezi, sekọnd 25 ọ bụla (sekọnd 75 tupu NU7 amalite ọrụ na 6 November 2026), a na-egwụputa [block](https://zcash.readthedocs.io/en/latest/rtd_pages/glossary.html#:~:text=Block,mempool%20in%20an%20unconfirmed%20state.) ọhụrụ n’ime blockchain Zcash, ụgwọ block nke ZEC 0.52083333 na-abanyekwa n’ọrụ. Tupu NU7, ụgwọ ahụ bụ ZEC 1.5625 kwa block; [ZIP 218](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0218) kewara ya ụzọ atọ, ya na oge block, ya mere ọnụọgụ ZEC ọhụrụ kwa ụbọchị agbanwebeghị. Kemgbe NU7, a na-ekesa ZEC 0.52083333 dị ka ndị a: 80% nye onye na-egwụputa (ZEC 0.41666668, tinyere ugwo Azụmahịa), 8% nye Zcash Community Grants (ZEC 0.04166666), na 12% nye igbe nchekwa akpọchiri akpọchi (ZEC 0.06249999), dịka [ZIP 1015](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1015) si dị.

A na-ebelata ọnụ ahịa ụgwọ block na ọkara ihe dị ka afọ anọ ọ bụla ruo mgbe ZEC nde 21 niile ga-adị n’ọrụ. Mmụba ọnụ ahịa Zcash na-agbaso nke Bitcoin ihe fọrọ nke nta ka ọ bụrụ n’ụzọ zuru oke. Ọ dị mkpa ịma na ka a na-emepụta mkpụrụ ego ọhụrụ, mmụba ọnụ ahịa na-ebelata, na n’oge halving ọ bụla, ọnụego ahụ na-ada nke ukwuu.

## Ihe Oyiyi / Ihe Nlereanya

Were ya na e nwere igwe na-enye mkpụrụ ego n’ihu ebe nchekwa a kaa akara. Ebe nchekwa ahụ nwere ZEC nde 21 na mkpokọta, ọ nweghịkwa onye nwere ike itinye otu mkpụrụ ego ọzọ n’ime ya.

Sekọnd 25 ọ bụla, igwe ahụ na-awụpụ obere ụyọkọ mkpụrụ ego ọhụrụ, ZEC 0.52083333. Onye na-egwụputa nke chọtara block na-ewere 80% nke ụyọkọ ahụ. Zcash Community Grants na-enweta 8%. A na-etinye 12% fọdụrụ n’ime igbe nchekwa.

Ihe dị ka afọ anọ ọ bụla, ụyọkọ ahụ na-adị ọkara nke nha mbụ ya. Nke ahụ bụ halving.

NU7 mere ka ụyọkọ ndị ahụ na-abịa ugboro atọ karịa, ma mee ka nke ọ bụla dị obere ugboro atọ. Ọnụọgụ mkpụrụ ego ọhụrụ kwa ụbọchị nọgidere otu.

## Nkọwa Miri Emi

### ZEC ọhụrụ kwa ụbọchị, oge nke ọ bụla

Halving na-ebelata ụgwọ kwa block. Block ndị na-eme ngwa ngwa (Blossom na 2019, NU7 na 2026) na-ebelatakwa ụgwọ kwa block, yana oge block, ya mere ha na-eme ka ọnụọgụ kwa ụbọchị nọgide otu.

| Oge | Oge block | Ụgwọ kwa block | ZEC ọhụrụ kwa ụbọchị |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Oge mmalite, 2016 ruo 2019 | sekọnd 150 | ZEC 12.5 | 7,200 |
| [Blossom](/zcash-tech/blossom), Dec 2019 ruo Nov 2020 | sekọnd 75 | ZEC 6.25 | 7,200 |
| Halving nke mbụ ([Canopy](/zcash-tech/canopy)), Nov 2020 ruo Nov 2024 | sekọnd 75 | ZEC 3.125 | 3,600 |
| Halving nke abụọ ([NU6](/zcash-tech/nu6)), Nov 2024 ruo Nov 2026 | sekọnd 75 | ZEC 1.5625 | 1,800 |
| [NU7](/zcash-tech/nu7), Nov 2026 ruo ngwụcha 2028 | sekọnd 25 | ZEC 0.52083333 | 1,800 |
| Halving nke atọ, a na-atụ anya ya na ngwụcha Nov 2028 | sekọnd 25 | ọkara nke ụgwọ dị ugbu a | ihe dị ka 900 |

A na-enweta ọnụ ọgụgụ kwa ụbọchị nke ọ bụla site n’ịmụba ụgwọ kwa block na ọnụ ọgụgụ block dị n’otu ụbọchị (sekọnd 86,400 kewara site n’oge block). Oge block n’ezie na-adịgasị ntakịrị, ya mere ọnụọgụ a na-ewepụta kwa ụbọchị na-agbanwe ntakịrị gburugburu ọnụọgụ ndị a.

### Mgbe halving nke na-abịa ga-eme

NU7 mere ka block na-abịa ugboro atọ karịa, ya mere nọmba block nke halving na-abịa gbanwere. E debere halving na-abịa maka block 6,132,390; a na-atụ anya ya n’ihe dị ka ngwụcha November 2028 ([ZIP 259](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0259)). Edemede ochie na-egosi block 4,406,400. Nọmba ahụ ziri ezi tupu NU7.

### Ugwo na Usoro Ịkwado Nkwụsi Ike Netwọk

Kemgbe NU7, a na-ewepụ 60% nke ugwo Azụmahịa dị na block ọ bụla n’ọrụ ma debe ya n’ime nchekwa ọha. Malite na block 8,939,664, nke a na-atụ anya n’ihe dị ka February 2031, block ọ bụla ga-akwụ ndị na-egwụputa akụkụ a kapịrị ọnụ nke nchekwa ahụ, gbakwunyere ụgwọ block nkịtị. Oke mkpụrụ ego nde 21 agaghị agbanwe. Ibe [Network Sustainability Mechanism](/zcash-tech/network-sustainability-mechanism) na-akọwa otú o si arụ ọrụ.
