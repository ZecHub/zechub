# Ego Mmepe Zcash (Development Fund)

## Nchịkọta nkenke (TL;DR)

- Zcash na-akwụ ụgwọ mmepe nke ya site n'ụgwọ ọrụ block (otu ngwugwu azụmahịa e dekọrọ n'ime blockchain), ya mere ndị na-ewu ya adịghị adabere na ndị ọchụnta ego si n'èzí
- Taa, 8% nke ụgwọ ọrụ block ọ bụla na-aga na Zcash Community Grants, 12% na-agakwa n'otu ego nke ndị nwe mkpụrụ ego na-achịkwa. Onye na-egwụputa (miner) na-ewere ihe fọdụrụ
- Ego a agbanweela na halving (mgbe a na-ebelata ụgwọ ọrụ block ọkara) ọ bụla: ụgwọ ọrụ ndị guzobere (founders' reward) mgbe a malitere, Ego Mmepe site na 2020, igbe nchekwa (lockbox) site na 2024, na njikwa ndị nwe mkpụrụ ego kemgbe Nọvemba 2025
- Ndị nwe mkpụrụ ego na-atụ vootu banyere otu a ga-esi jiri ego 12% ahụ. Zcash Community Grants na-ekpebi ikike (grant) nke ya
- Unu chọrọ ego? Tinyenụ akwụkwọ arịrịọ na Zcash Community Grants maka ọrụ ọhụrụ, ma ọ bụ na ikike azụ (retroactive grants) nke ndị nwe mkpụrụ ego maka ọrụ unu rụchaala

## Onye ka nke a bụ maka

- Ndị ọhụrụ na-eji Zcash chọrọ ịmata onye na-akwụ ụgwọ maka Zcash
- Ndị na-ewu ihe, ndị edemede na ndị ntụgharị asụsụ na-achọ ego
- Ndị nwe mkpụrụ ego chọrọ ịghọta ihe ha na-atụ vootu na ya

## Ihe kpatara nke a ji dị mkpa

Zcash enweghị ụlọ ọrụ nwe ya. Mana mmadụ ka ga-akwụrịrị ụgwọ maka e-akpa ego (wallet), ngwanrọ node, nchọpụta na agụmakwụkwọ. Kama ịchịkọta ego n'aka ndị ọchụnta ego, Zcash na-edebe akụkụ nke ụgwọ ọrụ block ọhụrụ ọ bụla maka mmepe. Nke a na-eme ka ndị na-ewu Zcash lekwasị anya n'ebe ndị ọrụ ya nọ na n'ebumnobi Nzuzo (privacy) ya.

## Otu o si gbanwee

| Oge | Ihe mere | Nkesa nke ụgwọ ọrụ block ọ bụla |
|---|---|---|
| 2016 ruo 2020 | **Ụgwọ ọrụ ndị guzobere.** Kwụrụ ndị guzobere Zcash, ndị ọchụnta ego mbụ, ndị ọrụ na Zcash Foundation ụgwọ, ruo mgbe halving mbụ | 20% gaa n'ụgwọ ọrụ ndị guzobere |
| Nọv 2020 ruo Nọv 2024 | **Ego Mmepe**, nke [Canopy](/zcash-tech/canopy) mepụtara ([ZIP 1014](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1014)) | 7% Electric Coin Company, 5% Zcash Foundation, 8% Ikike Ndị Bụ Isi (Major Grants) (emesịa Zcash Community Grants) |
| Nọv 2024 ruo Nọv 2025 | **Igbe nchekwa**, nke [NU6](/zcash-tech/nu6) mepụtara ([ZIP 1015](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1015)) | 8% Zcash Community Grants, 12% banye n'Igbe Nchekwa Ego Mmepe A Na-egbu Oge (Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox), e debere ruo mgbe obodo kpebiri otu a ga-esi jiri ya |
| Nọv 2025 ruo ugbu a | **Njikwa ndị nwe mkpụrụ ego**, nke [NU6.1](/zcash-tech/nu6-1) guzobere ([ZIP 1016](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1016)) | 8% Zcash Community Grants, 12% gaa n'Ego Ndị Nwe Mkpụrụ Ego Na-achịkwa (Coinholder-Controlled Fund), nke e ji ego dị n'igbe nchekwa malite |

N'oge ọ bụla, onye na-egwụputa na-ewere 80% fọdụrụ.

## Otu o si arụ ọrụ taa

Kemgbe NU6.1 malitere ịrụ ọrụ na Nọvemba 24, 2025, ụzọ abụọ ego si na-esi n'ụgwọ ọrụ block ọ bụla:

- **8% na-aga na [Zcash Community Grants](/zcash-organizations/zcash-community-grants)** (ZCG). Otu kọmitii nwere onwe ya na-akwụ ụgwọ maka ọrụ ọhụrụ n'ime usoro Zcash: e-akpa ego, ngwá ọrụ, nchọpụta, agụmakwụkwọ na mmemme.
- **12% na-aga n'Ego Ndị Nwe Mkpụrụ Ego Na-achịkwa.** Ọ malitere site na mkpụrụ ego e chekwara n'igbe nchekwa. Ndị nwe mkpụrụ ego na-atụ vootu ma a ga-esi na ya kwụọ ikike ma ọ bụ hapụ ya ka ọ nọrọ. Òtù Ndị Na-ejide Igodo (Key-Holder Organizations) na-ejide ego ahụ, iwu na-ejikọkwa ha iji ya naanị dịka ndị nwe mkpụrụ ego si kpebie ([ZIP 1016](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1016)).

Usoro a ga-aga n'ihu ruo halving nke atọ nke Zcash. ZIP 1016 na-ekwe ka ụbọchị ahụ gbanwee, n'ihi na NU7 na-agbanwe oge block. Obodo ga-ekpebi mgbe ahụ ma a ga-edobe ya, gbanwee ya ma ọ bụ kwụsị ya.

## Onye na-ekpebi

- **Zcash Community Grants** na-enyocha akwụkwọ arịrịọ ma na-atụ vootu na ikike ọ bụla.
- **Ndị nwe mkpụrụ ego** na-eji ZEC ha atụ vootu na atụmatụ maka ego 12% ahụ. Ha anaghị akpọchi mkpụrụ ego ha iji tụọ vootu.
- **Mgbanwe n'ego n'onwe ya** chọrọ ZIP ọhụrụ na network upgrade (mmelite netwọk), mgbe obodo kparịtachara ya na [Zcash Ebe Mkparịta Ụka Obodo](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com).

Maka nghọta zuru oke, lee [Zcash Gọọmentị (governance)](/zcash-community/zcash-governance) na [Otu e si hazie Zcash](/start-here/how-zcash-is-organized).

## Otu esi enweta ego

- **Ọrụ ọhụrụ unu chọrọ ịrụ:** tinyenụ akwụkwọ arịrịọ na [Zcash Community Grants](/zcash-organizations/zcash-community-grants).
- **Ọrụ unu rụchaala:** tinyenụ akwụkwọ arịrịọ na [Ikike Azụ Ndị Nwe Mkpụrụ Ego Na-eduzi](/zcash-organizations/coinholder-directed-retroactive-grants), nke na-eme kwa ọnwa atọ.
- **Obere onyinye n'ime wiki a:** ZecHub na-enye ndị na-enye aka obere ụgwọ na ZEC. Lee [Nyere aka wuo ZecHub](/contribute/help-build-zechub).
