# Gịnị bụ ZecHub?
---

ZecHub bụ ọdụ mmụta decentralized maka Zcash. Ebumnuche nke [ZecHub](http://zechub.xyz) bụ ịnye ikpo okwu mmụta ebe ndị otu obodo nwere ike ịrụkọ ọrụ n'ịmepụta, ịkwado izi ezi, na ịkwalite ọdịnaya nke na-akwado usoro gburugburu ebe obibi [ecosystem] nke Zcash. Anyị na-eme nke a site n’ụfọdụ usoro.

1. [Nkuzi](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6_epn0lASLFF28ePj7P5TvtMG_WE7p3O)
2. [Akwụkwọ wiki](https://zechub.wiki/)
3. [Pọdkastị na-agba ndị otu obodo ajụjụ ọnụ](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6_epn0lASLHlNCMtUErX8UfaJK6N9K5O).
4. [Akwụkwọ Akụkọ Zcash kwa izu.](https://zechub.substack.com/)

Nkuzi, blọọgụ dị mkpirikpi, na Akwụkwọ Akụkọ bụ ndị e meghere isi mmalite [open sourced] ma debe ha na GitHub, nke pụtara na onye ọ bụla n’obodo nwere ike inye onyinye. Nke a bụ iji hụ na ọdịnaya ziri ezi nakwa na ọwa ahụ n’onwe ya enweghị otu ebe ọdịda [single point of failure].

Ọ bụrụ na ị bụ onye ọhụrụ na Zcash, jiri [ntuziaka a](/start-here/new-user-guide).****

## Otu esi eme mgbanwe na ZecHub

---

1. Gaa na ebe nchekwa [ZecHub nke GitHub](https://github.com/ZecHub/zechub)
   
2. Mepụta akaụntụ GitHub ọhụrụ ma ọ bụ jiri nke gị banye
   
3. Soro ntuziaka a ka ịmụta otu esi eme pull request:
