## Zodl, nke a na-akpọbu Zashi: [iOS]](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zashi-zcash-wallet/id1672392439) - [Android]](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.electriccoin.zcash) - [F-Droid] (ụda olu)](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/co.electriccoin.zcash.foss/)

Built for Private Payments. Zashi was a self-custody shielded ZEC wallet for encrypted digital transactions, built by ECC and released in 2024.

Ezubere ya iji tinye ndị enyi na ezinụlọ n'ime Zcash na ịkwụ ụgwọ dijitalụ nkeonwe, yana interface dị mfe maka ndị mmadụ enweghị ahụmịhe crypto tupu ha ahazi obere akpa ego, nweta ZEC ma bido azụmahịa na nkeji.

A gbanwere aha obere akpa ahụ ** Zodl** na February 2026 mgbe ndị otu kwagara ZODL. Ndị ọrụ dị adị enweghị ihe ọ bụla ha ga-eme, dịka ngwa a gbanwere ya na mmelite ọzọ ya, nke mere njikọ ụlọ ahịa n'elu mepee Zod l Ọ bụ ugbu a ka echekwara ebe ahụ kama site ECC, yabụ lee [Peeji ZOD L]](/zcash-organizations/zodl) maka ozi dị ugbu a.

Mmekọrịta e wuru n'afọ ECC:
- [Mkpụrụ ego:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/coinbase-zashi-1-2-release/) ndị ọhụrụ crypto nwere ike ịzụta ZEC ozugbo n'ime obere akpa ahụ na-agbafe nsogbu nke ndebanye aha akaụntụ, KYC, ma wepụ mkpụrụ ego site na mgbanwe.

- [Flexa:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/zashi-flexa-integration-is-here-spend-zec-at-thousands-of-retailers/) makes it simple to actually use ZEC, allowing new users to spend it at thousands of retail locations like Barnes & Noble, GameStop, and Chipotle.

- [Keystone:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/drumroll-please-introducing-zashi-keystone-hardware-wallet-integration-for-shielded-zec/) nnukwu ihe ọganihu maka obodo Zcash nke were ọtụtụ afọ na-eme. Njikọ a gosipụtara mgbanwe dị ịrịba ama n'iji ojiji Zcash site na ikwe ka nchekwa oyi nke ZEC kpuchie ya.

## Nnyocha na Mmepe

Ndị otu ahụ eduziwo ihe ndị a ma ama na ọtụtụ oge, nke mbụ bụ [ntọhapụ nke Zcash]](https://electriccoin.co/blog/zcash-begins/) dị ka ụwa mbụ azụmahịa ojiji nke Zero-Ihe Ọmụma Ihe Akaebe.

Ọtụtụ ndị ọzọ na-eso dị ka [Sapling netwọk nkwalite]](https://electriccoin.co/blog/sapling-activation-complete/) na-ekwe ka mfe, kpuchie azụmahịa na a oge Mbelata nke 90% maka ewu azụmahịa, na ebe nchekwa mbelata nke n'elu 97%, ikwe ekpuchi azụmahịa on mobile ngwaọrụ maka mbụ.

Ihe kachasị mkpa bụ ihe kacha ọhụrụ. [Ihe e kere eke na nkesa nke usoro a dị ka ndị ahịa si emepụta ma melite ya]](https://electriccoin.co/blog/nu5-activates-on-mainnet-eliminating-trusted-setup-and-launching-a-new-era-for-zcash/) nke usoro nkwenye na-enweghị ntụkwasị obi halo2 na Network Upgrade 5 maka Zcash. Nke a kpochapụrụ ntọala tụkwasịrị obi ma malite oge ọhụrụ maka Z Cash. Kemgbe 2022, nke a arụ ọrụ na Zcash Mainnet wee nye ndị ọrụ ohere iji nweta nchebe dị mfe & ngwa ngwa emelitere maka usoro ịkwụ ụgwọ ha. 

## Mmekọrịta na mmekọrịta ndị mmekọ 

The ECC team supports and contributes to the Zcash ecosystem in many ways, collaborating with both internal & external teams regularly to help maintain and improve the Zcash protocol. 

Partnerships include working with Gemini to enable shielded transactions on their platform, Flexa to enable easy Zcash mobile payments at retal locations, hardware wallet companies like Keystone to enable shielded cold storage of ZEC, Brave for Zcash support in the browser, & other teams in the Zcash ecosystem to bring imrovements to Zcash. 

ECC bụkwa onye nkwado dị mpako nke [PGPforCrypto]](https://x.com/pgpforcrypto) usoro nzukọ nke na-eme ka ndị ọkachamara, ndị nwere mmasị, na ndị ọkachamara n'ime oghere cryptocurrency iji kwurịta mmepe, ịkekọrịta nghọta, ma kwalite njikọ aka obodo. Onye isi oche PGPforCrypto bụ onye otu Board na VP of Strategic Alliances at Electric Coin Co., Paul Brigner.

## Iwu na Usoro Ọchịchị

Nghọta na nduzi nke ECC gburugburu nzuzo dị n'elu-agbụ yana ihe ọ pụtara maka nchekwa onwe onye na mba bara ezigbo uru nye ndị na-eme iwu. E meela mgbalị siri ike iji duzie usoro ziri ezi ebe o kwere omume kemgbe e guzobere ECC. 

Ihe atụ nke mgbasa ozi gụnyere mkparịta ụka na Financial Action Task Force (FATF) isonye n'ọgbakọ ndị rụpụtara ndụmọdụ maka ngalaba cryptocurrency. N'ihi mbọ a, Zcash ka New York State Department of Financial Services (NYDFS), naanị nzuzo-echebe cryptocurrency iji mee ya.
