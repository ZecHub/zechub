## Nkwupụta Ebumnuche

Ebumnuche na ECC bụ ime ka nnwere onwe akụ na ụba sie ike. Nzuzo ego [financial privacy] bụ isi nke mbọ a, ma dị mkpa iji chebe ùgwù mmadụ, nchekwa, ikike mmadụ ịchị onwe ya, na nkwenye. Inweta Nzuzo ego kwesịkwara ịdị mfe dịka imechi ákwà mgbochi n'ụlọ gị. Ma mgbe Web2 pụtara, ike aghọọla nke etinyere n'aka mmadụ ole na ole, ndị na-achịkwa ya abanyeworị n'ime ime ụlọ gị. Ị nweghị ike imechi ákwà mgbochi ahụ. Anyị ga-ewu ihe ọhụrụ.
Usoro ọhụrụ a nke ngwá ọrụ ego, nke mmadụ niile nwere ike iji, ga-adị mfe maka ndị ọrụ, nwee Nzuzo zuru ezu ma nwee nhọrọ maka ikpughe ozi, akụrụngwa na-akwado ya ga-agbasakwa ma sie ike.

## Zodl, nke a na-akpọbu Zashi:  [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zashi-zcash-wallet/id1672392439) - [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.electriccoin.zcash) - [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/co.electriccoin.zcash.foss/)

E wuru maka Kwụọ ụhwọ Nzuzo [Private Payments]. Zashi bụ e-akpa ego [wallet] ZEC nke e chebere echebe [shielded] maka Azụmahia dijitalụ nwere Nzobe ederede [encrypted digital transactions], nke ECC wuru ma wepụta na 2024.

E mere ya iji kpọbata ndị enyi na ezinụlọ n'ime Zcash na ịkwụ ụgwọ dijitalụ nke Nzuzo, site na njirimara dị mfe nke ga-eme ka ndị na-enwebughị ahụmịhe crypto nwee ike ịhazi e-akpa ego, nweta ZEC, ma malite Azụmahia n'ime nkeji ole na ole.

A gbanwere aha e-akpa ego ahụ ka ọ bụrụ **Zodl** na Febrụwarị 2026 mgbe ndị otu ahụ kwagara ZODL. Ndị ọrụ dịbu adịghị mkpa ime ihe ọ bụla, ebe ngwa ahụ gbanwere aha ya n'oge mmelite ya ọzọ, nke mere njikọ ụlọ ahịa ndị dị n'elu ji emeghe Zodl. A na-elekọta ya ugbu a n'ebe ahụ kama ịbụ ECC, ya mere lee ibe [ZODL ahụ](/zcash-organizations/zodl) maka ozi ugbu a.

Njikọta e wuru n'oge afọ ECC:
- [Coinbase:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/coinbase-zashi-1-2-release/) ndị ọhụrụ na crypto nwere ike ịzụta ZEC ozugbo n'ime e-akpa ego ahụ, na-enweghị nsogbu nke ịdebanye aha akaụntụ, KYC, na iwepụ mkpụrụ ego na exchange.

- [Flexa:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/zashi-flexa-integration-is-here-spend-zec-at-thousands-of-retailers/) na-eme ka iji ZEC n'ezie dị mfe, na-enye ndị ọrụ ọhụrụ ohere imefu ya n'ọtụtụ puku ebe azụmaahịa dịka Barnes & Noble, GameStop, na Chipotle.

- [Keystone:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/drumroll-please-introducing-zashi-keystone-hardware-wallet-integration-for-shielded-zec/) bụ nnukwu ọganihu maka obodo Zcash nke e jirila ọtụtụ afọ wulite. Njikọta a na-egosi nnukwu mmalite ọhụrụ n'ịdị mfe iji Zcash, site n'ikwe ka e debe ZEC nke e chebere echebe n'ebe nchekwa oyi.

## Nnyocha na Mmepe

Ndị otu ahụ edugola ọganihu ndị a ma ama n'ọtụtụ oge, nke mbụ bụ [mwepụta Zcash](https://electriccoin.co/blog/zcash-begins/) dịka ojiji azụmahịa mbụ n'ụwa nke Zero-Knowledge Proofs.

Ọtụtụ ndị ọzọ sochiri, dịka [Sapling network upgrade](https://electriccoin.co/blog/sapling-activation-complete/) nke na-enye Azụmahia dị mfe karị, nke e chebere echebe, yana mbelata oge nke 90% maka iwulite Azụmahia, na mbelata ebe nchekwa karịrị 97%, nke mere ka Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe kwe omume na ngwaọrụ mkpanaaka maka oge mbụ.

Ọganihu kachasị mkpa bụ nke kachasị ọhụrụ. [Ịmepụta na itinye n'ọrụ](https://electriccoin.co/blog/nu5-activates-on-mainnet-eliminating-trusted-setup-and-launching-a-new-era-for-zcash/) usoro proof halo2 na-enweghị ntụkwasị obi na Network Upgrade 5 maka Zcash. Nke a wepụrụ trusted setup ma malite oge ọhụrụ maka Zcash. Kemgbe 2022, nke a na-arụ ọrụ na Mainnet Zcash ma na-enye ndị ọrụ ohere iji usoro dị nchebe karịa ma dị mfe imelite maka protocol Kwụọ ụhwọ ha. 

## Mmekọrịta & Mmekorita 

Ndị otu ECC na-akwado ma na-atụnye aka n'usoro ndụ Zcash n'ọtụtụ ụzọ, na-esokwa ndị otu dị n'ime na ndị dị n'èzí arụkọ ọrụ mgbe niile iji nyere aka lekọta ma melite protocol Zcash. 

Mmekọrịta gụnyere iso Gemini arụ ọrụ iji mee Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe n'elu ikpo okwu ha, Flexa iji mee ka ịkwụ ụgwọ mkpanaaka Zcash dị mfe n'ebe azụmaahịa, ụlọ ọrụ hardware wallet dịka Keystone iji mee nchekwa oyi nke ZEC nke e chebere echebe, Brave maka nkwado Zcash na ihe nchọgharị, na ndị otu ndị ọzọ n'usoro ndụ Zcash iji wetara Zcash nkwalite. 

ECC bụkwa onye nkwado mpako nke [PGPforCrypto](https://x.com/pgpforcrypto), usoro nzukọ na-achịkọta ndị ọkachamara, ndị nwere mmasị, na ndị ọzụzụ na mpaghara cryptocurrency iji kparịta mmepe, kesaa nghọta, ma kwalite ntinye aka obodo. Onye otu Board na VP of Strategic Alliances na Electric Coin Co, Paul Brigner, na-eduga PGPforCrypto.

## Iwu & Usoro Nchịkwa

Nghọta na nduzi ECC gbasara Nzuzo on-chain na ihe ọ pụtara maka nchekwa onwe onye na nke mba bara uru nke ukwuu nye ndị na-eme iwu. E meela mbọ mgbe niile iji duzie iwu n'ụzọ kwesịrị ekwesị ebe o kwere omume kemgbe e hiwere ECC. 

Ihe atụ nke iru ndị ọzọ gụnyere mkparịta ụka na Financial Action Task Force (FATF), na isonye n'nzukọ ndị butere aro nzukọ ahụ maka ngalaba cryptocurrency. N'ihi mbọ ndị a, Zcash ghọrọ nke New York State Department of Financial Services (NYDFS) kwadoro, cryptocurrency naanị ya na-echebe Nzuzo nke mere otú ahụ.

Ma Bootstrap na Electric Coin Company nwere board of directors, ma Electric Coin Company nwekwara Scientific Advisory Group.
