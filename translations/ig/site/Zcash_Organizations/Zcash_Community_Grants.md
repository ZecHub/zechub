## Nkwupụta Ebumnuche

Mmemme Zcash Community Grants na-enye ndị otu nọọrọ onwe ha na-abanye n'usoro ihe dị ndụ nke Zcash ego, ka ha rụọ nnukwu mmepe na-aga n'ihu (ma ọ bụ ọrụ ọzọ) maka ọdịmma ọha nke usoro ihe dị ndụ nke Zcash. 

## Ndị mmadụ na Ndị otu

Otu kọmitii, nke mmadụ ise mejupụtara ugbu a, na-ahụ maka Zcash Community Grants. Obodo a na-enyocha ma na-akwado akwụkwọ arịrịọ ikike sitere n'aka ndị otu nke atọ chọrọ iwulite n'elu protocol nke Zcash. Ozi ndị ọzọ gbasara [Ndị otu](https://zcashcommunitygrants.org/committee/)

Ọrụ ndị e kwadoro n'oge na-adịbeghị anya site na ZCG bụ Zcash Ngwá akụ nke e chebere echebe (ZSA) nke ndị otu QEDIT na-edu. Iwebata DeFi na ZEC site na Zcash, yana protocol Kwụọ ụhwọ [payment] ọhụrụ na-agbakwunye atụmatụ ndị ọzọ na mainnet nke Zcash. 

Akwụkwọ ngosi dị mkpirikpi nke Zcash Media, nke nwere ndị Zcasher a ma ama dịka Edward Snowden, Zooko na Deirdre Connolly, nwere ebumnuche imepụta vidio dabara adaba, na-atọ ụtọ, na-akụzi ihe ka e kesaa ya n'ọtụtụ ebe. 

Mmekọrịta hardware wallet nke Ledger iji kwado Azụmahia [transaction] nke e chebere echebe nke Sapling n'uju; nke a na-enye ohere ka echekwa Zcash na nzuzo na n'enweghị nsogbu. Ndị otu Zondax rụrụ nke a. 

SDK maka usoro Kwụọ ụhwọ nke e chebere echebe, nke dị mfe iji otu pịa, na usoro ebe a na-ere ahịa maka ụlọ ahịa nkịtị, nke ZGo na-arụ. 

Mmemme Onye nnọchi anya zuru ụwa ọnụ na-enyere Zcash aka inweta nnọchite anya sara mbara n'ụwa niile nakwa maka mmemme obodo a na-eme n'onwe mmadụ gburugburu ụwa. 

Kọmitii ikike nke Zcash enyochala ọtụtụ ọrụ ndị ọzọ; lee [Ndepụta zuru ezu, emelitere](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1FQ28rDCyRW0TiNxrm3rgD8ai2KGUsXAjPieQmI1kKKg/edit?gid=135980745#gid=135980745) nke ọrụ ndị e nyefere.

## Nhazi Ọgbakọ

Zcash Community Grants bụ ọgbakọ nọọrọ onwe ya na Electric Coin Company na Zcash Foundation. 

## Ego

Zcash Community Grants na-enweta 8% nke onyinye block, n'okpuru usoro ego e guzobere na NU6.1 site na [ZIP 1016](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1016). Mgbasa ahụ malitere mgbe mgbasa mbụ nke [ZIP 1015](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1015) kwụsịrị, ma ọ ga-adị ruo halving nke atọ nke Zcash na block 4,406,400 n'okpuru oghere block dị ugbu a. ZCG na-eji ego ndị a akwado ndị otu nọọrọ onwe ha n'ofe usoro ihe dị ndụ nke Zcash.

### Nzọụkwụ 1: Kwadebe Aro Gị
Mepụta aro doro anya gụnyere:
- Aha ọrụ na nkọwa onye na-achọ arịrịọ
- Nkwupụta nsogbu na azịza a tụrụ aro
- Ihe a ga-ebunye, usoro oge, na ihe ịrịba ama nke ọganihu
- Mmefu ego na arịrịọ maka ego
- Ahụmịhe dị mkpa na ihe ize ndụ

---

### Nzọụkwụ 2: Nyefee na GitHub
1. Gaa na **Zcash Community Grants ebe nchekwa GitHub**
2. Mepee taabụ **Issues** ma pịa **New Issue**
3. Họrọ **template akwụkwọ arịrịọ ikike** kwesịrị ekwesị
4. Dejupụta oghere niile achọrọ ma nyefee ya

Issue nke GitHub bụ **ndekọ gọọmentị nke akwụkwọ arịrịọ**.

---

### Nzọụkwụ 3: Mepụta Post na Forum Obodo
1. Gaa na **Forum Obodo nke Zcash**
2. Mepụta thread ọhụrụ n'ụdị **Zcash Community Grants**
3. Jiri otu aha ahụ dị na issue nke GitHub
4. Tinye nchịkọta dị mkpirikpi ma tinye **njikọ gaa na issue nke GitHub**

---

### Nzọụkwụ 4: Soro na Nnyocha
- Nyochaa issue nke GitHub na thread forum
- Zaa ajụjụ na nzaghachi sitere na Kọmitii ZCG na obodo

---

### Nzọụkwụ 5: Nsonaazụ
- Aro a kwadoro na-aga n'ihu n'inweta ego na ịkọ akụkọ
- Aro a na-akwadoghị **NWERE IKE** idegharị ma nyefee ọzọ
