# Sovright
![Sovright](/content-images/sovright-16a27c0d24.webp)

**Sovright** bụ òtù na-enweghị uru US 501(c3) raara onwe ya nye ịkwado nnwere onwe mmadụ. Malite n'ilekwasị anya na akụrụngwa Zcash, ịgwụputa, mmepe protocol, na nkwado ecosystem, ọrụ a na-ewu ngwá ọrụ na ọrụ ezubere iji wusie akụrụngwa ego dijitalụ nkeonwe ike ma kwado nguzogide ogologo oge nke netwọk Zcash.

A maara Sovright na mbụ dị ka **Bootstrap**. Aha mbụ ahụ gosipụtara ebumnuche mbụ nke òtù ahụ ịmalite ụlọ ọrụ akụrụngwa Zcash site na mmalite.

[Gaa na saịtị](https://sovright.com/)

## Aha

Aha **Sovright** bụ ngwakọta nke **sovereign** na **right**. Ọ na-egosipụta ilekwasị anya nke òtù ahụ na ọchịchị onwe onye, Nzuzo [privacy], na ikike ịhọrọ otú e si achịkwa data onwe onye, ego, nkwukọrịta, compute, na ọrụ ịntanetị.

Ọrụ Sovright malitere na Zcash, cryptocurrency na-echekwa Nzuzo nke na-eji cryptography zero-knowledge akwado Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe. Ka oge na-aga, ilekwasị anya nke òtù ahụ gbasapụrụ karịa ego nkeonwe iji tinye nchegbu akụrụngwa sara mbara, gụnyere nkwukọrịta echekwara, netwọk na-adịgide adịgide, compute nkeonwe, na mmekorita AI echekwara.

## Pool Ịgwụputa

Sovright na-arụ ọrụ pool ịgwụputa Zcash na:

[mining.sovright.com](https://mining.sovright.com/)

E mere Pool Ịgwụputa Sovright iji kwado ọchịchị onwe onye nke onye na-egwụputa ma belata itinye uche pool ịgwụputa n'otu ebe. Itinye uche pool ịgwụputa n'otu ebe bụ nchegbu na-emegharị emegharị n'ime netwọk proof-of-work n'ihi na ọnụ ọgụgụ nta nke pool nwere ike mechaa chịkwaa nnukwu akụkụ nke hashrate [hashrate] netwọk. Nke a nwere ike ime ka decentralized ghara ịdị ike ma mụbaa ịdabere n'omume ndị na-ahụ maka nnukwu pool.

Pool Ịgwụputa Sovright bụ maka ndị na-egwụputa naanị ha, ndị ọrụ nta, na ndị sonyere ọzọ chọrọ ịkwado ecosystem ịgwụputa Zcash nke decentralized karịa. Pool ahụ na-emesi ike n'usoro ịkwụ ụgwọ Oha, ikike nyocha, na ugwo ọrụ dị ntakịrị.

Ụdị testnet dị ugbu a anaghị enye ịkwụ ụgwọ ma ọ bụ naanị maka nnwale.

## (Na-abịa n'oge adịghị anya)

### Nkwụsi Ike Netwọk

Nkwụsi ike netwọk na-ezo aka n'ịdabere na ike nke akụrụngwa na-ebufe Azụmahia Zcash. Azụmahia nkeonwe anaghị adabere naanị na cryptography, kama ọ daberekwa na nnweta na ntụkwasị obi nke akụrụngwa netwọk dị n'okpuru ha.

Sovright na-achọpụta nkwụsi ike netwọk dị ka mpaghara ọrụ ntọala maka ijigide nchekwa na ojiji bara uru nke Zcash.

### Ọchịchị Onwe Ego

Ọchịchị onwe ego na-ezo aka n'ikike ndị mmadụ nwere ịchịkwa ma nweta ego nke ha n'enweghị ịdabere na nkwenye sitere n'aka ndị etiti. N'ọnọdụ Zcash, nke a gụnyere Kwụọ ụhwọ nkeonwe, ijide ego n'onwe, na akụrụngwa nke na-enye ndị ọrụ ohere ime Azụmahia n'usoro nke ha.

Sovright na-ewere inweta ego na ego ndị ọrụ na-achịkwa dị ka akụkụ bụ isi nke ọchịchị onwe onye.
