# Financial Privacy Foundation (FPF)
![FPF](/content-images/FPFgrants-226bdea4bb.webp)

## Banyere FPF
Financial Privacy Foundation (FPF) bụ òtù na-adịghị akwụ ụgwọ e hibere na Cayman Islands. Ebumnuche bụ isi FPF bụ ịkụziri na inye ndị mmadụ n'otu n'otu na obodo ike ka ha chịkwaa Nzuzo ego [financial privacy] na nchekwa ha. FPF kwenyekwara n'ịkwalite usoro ego ziri ezi na nke nhatanha nke na-akwanyere Nzuzo onwe onye ùgwù. Ọrụ bụ isi FPF metụtara Nzuzo ego bụ inye ndị mmadụ n'otu n'otu na obodo ozi, akụrụngwa na nkwado iji chebe ma bulie Nzuzo ego ha elu. Financial Privacy Fund (FPF) na-emepụta ugbu a mmemme ikike [grant] akpọrọ FPF X FPF.

##  Ozi gbasara FPF X FPF

 FPF X FPF gbasara mmemme ikike nnwale mbụ FPF, Financial Privacy Fund (FPF x FPF). Mmemme a ga-akwado ego maka ọrụ injinịa dị n'ime usoro gburugburu ebe obibi Zcash ndị fọrọ obere ka ha gwụchaa, ma ọ bụ ọrụ ndị ga-enye aka ozugbo na usoro gburugburu ebe obibi ahụ ma nwee ike gwụchaa n'ime oge na oke ego akọwapụtara. FPF X FPF ji ego pere mpe malite iji mee ka usoro inye ikike dị mma, ma nwee olileanya ịmalite agba n'ọdịnihu.

## Nkọwa Mmemme FPF X FPF 

Nkọwa Mmemme FPF X FPF: FPF nwere ọtụtụ nkọwa n'ime mmemme ya, ndị a bụ:

- Ọnụ Ego: Ruo $50,000 USD
- Arịrịọ Kachasị Elu: $25,000 USD
- Ihe KYC Chọrọ: Ọ dịghị
- Ụdị Ọrụ: Naanị ọrụ injinịa ndị nwere ike ịbara usoro gburugburu ebe obibi Zcash uru kpọmkwem ma nwee ike gwụchaa nke ọma ka a ga-atụle.
- Kwụọ ụhwọ [Payment]: Mgbe arụchara ọrụ ahụ nke ọma
- Ụbọchị Mmalite: March 18, 2024

## Usoro Itinye Akwụkwọ Maka Ikike 

Usoro itinye akwụkwọ maka ikike FPF X FPF gụnyere ọtụtụ usoro. Nke mbụ, ndị na-achọ itinye akwụkwọ ga-etinye akwụkwọ maka ikike ahụ site n'ụdị echekwara enyere. Mgbe ahụ, FPF ga-enyocha akwụkwọ arịrịọ ahụ maka ntozu, a ga-agwakwa ndị na-achọ itinye akwụkwọ ọnọdụ ha n'ime ụbọchị kalenda 5 mgbe oge njedebe itinye akwụkwọ gachara. Ọzọ, kọmitii nyocha pụrụ iche ga-enyocha ma kpebie maka onyinye ikike n'ime ụbọchị kalenda 20 mgbe oge njedebe nnyefe gachara. A ga-ebipụta nkọwa ikike e nyere na webụsaịtị FPF maka nghọta na ịza ajụjụ.

## Ozi Ndị Ọzọ Banyere FPF X FPF 

Enwere ike ịhụ ozi ndị ọzọ gbasara FPF X FPF na webụsaịtị Financial Privacy Fun (FPF) [ebe a](https://www.financialprivacyfoundation.org/grants); ọ bụrụkwa na ị ka nwere mmasị ịmata gbasara mmemme FPF X FPF, ị nwere ike ịga na akwụkwọ Ajụjụ A Na-ajụkarị ebe a: [Ajụjụ A Na-ajụkarị](https://www.financialprivacyfoundation.org/_files/ugd/9ba471_16b6c85b5eb645c9847536d2fdb72434.pdf). 

Ọ bụrụ na ị nwere mmasị ịbụ Onye Nyocha Afọ Ofufo maka agba a nke mmemme FPF X FPF, biko gaa na [njikọ](https://www.financialprivacyfoundation.org/_files/ugd/9ba471_679a364148984518be3b728cf1df04d8.pdf) a. Maka ajụjụ ndị ọzọ gbasara Mmemme Ikike FPF X FPF, ị nwere ike ịjụ ozugbo site n'iziga ozi-e [FPF](mailto:info@financialprivacyfoundation.org).
