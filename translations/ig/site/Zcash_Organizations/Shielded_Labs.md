# <img src="/content-images/Shielded-labs-shield-white-2-27aef199ae.webp" alt="Shielded Labs logo" width="50"/> Shielded Labs

[Shielded Labs](https://shieldedlabs.net/) bụ otu nkwado Zcash nọọrọ onwe ya, nke e ji onyinye kwado, nke dị na Switzerland. Ọrụ ya bụ iwulite ego nkeonwe a na-apụghị ịkwụsị akwụsị nke na-enye nnwere onwe mmadụ na ikike onwe onye, na-elekwasị anya n'ọrụ na-akwalite nkwado, nchekwa, na nhazi nke netwọk Zcash.

Shielded Labs na-agbakwụnye ikike injinia n'onwe ya na gburugburu Zcash. Ọ bụghị akụkụ nke Electric Coin Company, Zcash Foundation, ma ọ bụ Zcash Community Grants, ọ na-akọwakwa onwe ya dị ka nzukọ usoro Zcash mbụ nke na-enwetaghị ego kpọmkwem ma ọ bụ na-apụtaghị ìhè site na Development Fund ma ọ bụ ụgwọ ọrụ ngọngọ.

## Ọrụ na Ọrụ

Shielded Labs lekwasịrị anya na usoro na ọrụ akụrụngwa nke nwere ike ime ka Zcash sie ike karịa ogologo oge. Ihe ndị a na-ekwu maka ọha na eze na-emesi ike:

- Nnwere onwe mmadụ na nzuzo ego
- Ego nnwere onwe na-abụghị nke Mmepe Ego Mmepe
- Nkwanye ùgwù maka ndị nwere ZEC na ntinye aka nke onye ji ego
- Nnwere onwe onye na ikike nke onye ọrụ
- Mmepụta teknụzụ maka nkwado, nchekwa, na nhazi
- Mmekọrịta na ndị ọzọ na-enye aka Zcash

Òtù a na-enye aka site na injinịa kpọmkwem, nnyocha ọha na eze, nkwado akụrụngwa, na nhazi gburugburu ebe obibi. Ọ na-ejikwa akụrụngwa ọha maka ntuli aka ọchịchị na ego ndị nwere ego na-eduzi, na-enyere ndị obodo Zcash aka ịghọta mgbe na otu ha ga-esi sonye na mgbaàmà ọchịchị.

## Ọrụ Ndị Dị Ugbu A

Shielded Labs depụtara ọtụtụ ebe ọrụ na-arụ ọrụ n'ihu ọha:

- [Usoro Nkwado Netwọk](https://shieldedlabs.net/nsm/) - Otu mgbanwe ndị a tụrụ aro iji kwado mmefu ego nchekwa ogologo oge Zcash's ma chekwaa oke mmanụ ZEC ruru nde 21. Ọrụ NSM gụnyere ọkụ ZEC n'afọ ofufo, iwepụ ihe mkpofu, na atụmatụ iweta ego nke akọwara na ZIPs 233, 234, na 235.
- [Crosslink](https://shieldedlabs.net/crosslink-faq/) - A tụrụ aro ka e mee nkwalite nkwekọrịta ngwakọ nke ga-ejikọta mmepụta ngọngọ Zcash's na nkwenye nke ihe akaebe, ka ndị nwere ZEC wee nwee ike inye aka chekwaa netwọk ahụ ebe ndị na-egwuputa ihe na-aga n'ihu na-emepụta ngọngọ.
- [Ụgwọ Mgbanwe](https://shieldedlabs.net/fees/) - Nyocha na ngwaọrụ maka ụgwọ azụmahịa Zcash ndị a pụrụ ịkọ nkọ, ndị dị mfe iji, na ndị na-adịgide adịgide ka netwọk ahụ na-agbanwe.
- [Efu](https://shieldedlabs.net/zero/) - Ngwanrọ akụrụngwa Zcash nke mepere emepe maka mgbanwe, ọdọ mmiri igwu ala, ndị na-enye obere akpa ego, na òtù ndị ọzọ dabere na akụrụngwa Zcash.
- [Ironwood](https://shieldedlabs.net/ironwood/) - Mmelite netwọk Zcash na ọdọ mmiri e ji kpuchie nke e mepụtara iji meghachi omume na adịghị ike nke Orchard adịgboroja, nke e mere iji weghachite ịdị mma nke ọkọnọ Zcash's na-ekesa n'onwe ya.
- [Epoch](https://shieldedlabs.net/epoch-future-proof-cryptography-for-zcash/) - A mara ọkwa ọrụ nyocha na injinia na Ọktoba 2026 iji mepụta cryptography post-quantum dị njikere maka mmepụta, nke nwere nkwa dị elu maka Zcash ma gbasaa nkwenye iwu nke akụkụ cryptographic dị mkpa. Ọrụ a na-elekwasị anya na mmejuputa dị njikere maka mmepụta ka ọ na-erule njedebe nke 2027 nke nwere ike ịbụ ntọala nke mmelite netwọk n'ọdịnihu. Ọ na-emeju Project Tachyon na mgbalị post-quantum ndị ọzọ n'ofe gburugburu ebe obibi.

## Ndị otu Epoch na cryptography

Na Ọktoba 2026, Shielded Labs wepụtara Epoch, mgbalị siri ike iji kwado Zcash n'ọdịnihu megide kọmputa quantum, mwakpo ndị AI na-enyere aka, na ihe iyi egwu ndị ọzọ dị elu. Ọrụ a na-achọ iji dochie akụkụ nzuzo nke quantum na-adịghị ike ma tinye nkwenye iwu na akụkụ dị mkpa nke usoro ahụ.

Ndị otu mmadụ atọ na-ahụ maka nzuzo na-edu Epoch:

- **Ulrich Haböck** – Onyeisi Ndị Na-ede Akwụkwọ Ndekọ (onye bụbu onye na StarkWare; onye bụbu onye na Polygon Zero). Ọkachamara n'ihe akaebe ihe ọmụma efu na nzuzo post-quantum; onye dere arụmụka logUp searchup na onye so dee akwụkwọ Circle STARKs.
- **Luke Edwards** – Injinia Cryptography (onye bụbu onye ndu teknụzụ nke ndị otu injinia cryptography na Aztec). Nwere PhD na Applied Mathematics site na Mahadum Arizona.
- **Suyash Bagad** – Injinia Cryptography (onye bụbu onye Aztec, na-arụ ọrụ na ọbá akwụkwọ Barretenberg na ezinụlọ PLONK nke sistemu ihe akaebe). Nwere akara ugo mmụta BTech na MTech site na IIT Bombay; nyocha gụsịrị akwụkwọ lekwasịrị anya na ihe akaebe nchekwa nzuzo nke nchekwa.

Nkebi nke 1 lekwasịrị anya n'inyocha nnyocha ndị dị ugbu a, inyocha ihe owuwu ndị a chọrọ maka nchekwa Zcash na arụmọrụ, yana imepụta atụmatụ doro anya mgbe a rụchara ọrụ. A na-ezube imezu ihe dị njikere maka mmepụta na njedebe nke 2027.

## Onyinye Ironwood

Na 2026, Shielded Labs rụrụ ọrụ dị mkpa na nzaghachi gburugburu ebe obibi maka adịghị ike nke Orchard adịgboroja. Taylor Hornby, onye nyocha nchekwa nke Shielded Labs, chọpụtara adịghị ike ahụ ma kpugheere Zcash Open Development Lab (ZODL), bụ́ ndị haziri ndozi mberede na ndị ọzọ na-enye aka Zcash.

Mgbe e mechara ndozi mberede ahụ, obodo ahụ ka chọrọ ụzọ isi weghachite obi ike onwe ya na mmiri Zcash's na-ekesa. Ironwood lebara nke ahụ anya site n'ịmepụta ọdọ mmiri ọhụrụ a na-echebe site na iji usoro Orchard edoziri na site n'ịkpọchi ọdọ mmiri Orchard mbụ ka ego wee pụọ naanị site na ntụgharị. Shielded Labs rụkọrọ ọrụ na Project Tachyon, Valar Group, ZODL, na Zcash Foundation na nhazi, nyocha, na nkwadebe gburugburu ebe obibi maka Ironwood.

Ọrụ a gosiri otu n'ime ọrụ ndị bụ isi Shielded Labs' na Zcash: ịgbakwunye ikike nchekwa nkeonwe na mmepe protocol na mpụga òtù mmepe Zcash mbụ.

## Ọrụ Ọchịchị

Shielded Labs na-ebipụtakwa ozi gbasara ọchịchị maka ndị otu obodo Zcash. Ibe nchịkwa ya na-enyocha ntuli aka ọchịchị usoro, isonye na ZCAP, ego ndị nwere ego na-eduzi, na obere ego ntuli aka akwadoro. Nke a na-enyere aka ime mkpebi Zcash dị mfe ịgbaso maka ndị nwere ego, ndị otu ZCAP, na ndị sonyere na obodo sara mbara.

Zcash anaghị eji ọchịchị akpaka n'usoro maka mgbanwe usoro. Ntụli aka na ntinye obodo bụ ihe mgbaàmà ndụmọdụ nke na-abanye n'ime usoro nkwekọrịta sara mbara. Ọrụ nchịkwa Shielded Labs' na-enyere aka ime ka ihe mgbaàmà ndị ahụ dịkwuo mfe ịnweta ma dị mfe ịchọpụta.
