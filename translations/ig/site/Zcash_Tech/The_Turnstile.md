# turnstile

## Nchịkọta

- turnstile bụ iwu ndekọ ego ọha nke na-enyocha uru ole na-abanye ma na-apụ na pool nke ọ bụla nke e chebere echebe
- Ọ na-eme ka onye ọ bụla nwee ike nyochaa na pool anaghị akwụpụta uru karịrị nke etinyere n'ime ya, ọbụna ma Azụmahia ndị dị n'ime ya bụ nke nzuzo
- Nke a na-echebe ọkọnọ ZEC pụọ n'ajọ njehie zoro ezo, n'ihi na mkpụrụ ego adịgboroja agaghị apụ na pool n'emebighị ndekọ ahụ
- Kwụọ ụhwọ n'ime pool na-anọgide bụrụ nke nzuzo. Azụmahia nke na-ebufe ZEC n'etiti pool na-egosi ego ọ na-ebufe
- turnstile bụ ihe mere mbugharị Orchard gaa Ironwood ji nwee ike igosi na ọkọnọ nke e chebere echebe siri ike

<br/>

## Onye ka nke a bụ maka ya

- Onye ọ bụla chọrọ ịghọta otu Zcash si eme ka a tụkwasị ọkọnọ nzuzo ya obi
- Ndị ọrụ na-eso mbugharị Orchard gaa Ironwood ma na-eche otu o si egosi na ọkọnọ ahụ bụ eziokwu
- Ndị mbido nwere mmasị ịmata otú usoro ego nzuzo ka nwere ike isi nyochaa

<br/>

## Ihe ịma aka ahụ

Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe Zcash na-ezo ego, ndị zitere ya, na ndị natara ya. Nzuzo ahụ bụ isi ihe ya. Mana ọ na-ebute ajụjụ siri ike: ọ bụrụ na ọ nweghị onye nwere ike ịhụ ihe dị n'ime pool nke e chebere echebe, kedu ka onye ọ bụla ga-esi mara na ngụkọta ego ZEC ziri ezi? Kedu ka ị ga-esi nyochaa ego ị na-apụghị ịhụ?

Ọ bụrụ na njehie ekwe ka mmadụ mepụta mkpụrụ ego adịgboroja n'ime pool nke e chebere echebe, aghụghọ ahụ ga-ezo site n'otu Nzuzo ahụ na-echebe ndị ọrụ eziokwu. Enweghị nchedo, ejighị n'aka ahụ ga-emebi ntụkwasị obi n'ọkọnọ dum. turnstile bụ nchedo na-edozi nke a.

<br/>

## Ihe turnstile bụ

Chee pool nke ọ bụla nke e chebere echebe dịka ụlọ nwere otu ọnụ ụzọ a na-agụ. Mgbe ọ bụla uru si n'èzí abanye na pool, ma ọ bụ hapụ ya gaa ebe ọzọ, ọ na-agafe ọnụ ụzọ ahụ, a na-agụkwa ya n'ihu ọha. Azụmahia dị n'ime ụlọ ahụ na-anọgide nzuzo, mana ngụkọta na-aga n'ihu n'ọnụ ụzọ ahụ na-ahụ onye ọ bụla.

Iwu ahụ dị mfe: pool enweghị ike ịhapụ uru karịrị nke batara n'ime ya. Nodes na-ajụ block ọ bụla ga-eme ka nguzozi pool daa n'okpuru efu. A maara uru e kweere dị n'ime pool mgbe niile, n'ihi na ọ bụ naanị ngụkọta nke batara e wepụrụ ngụkọta nke hapụrụ. Ndekọ ọha a bụ turnstile.

<br/>

## Otu o si arụ ọrụ

Zcash enweela ọtụtụ pool nke e chebere echebe n'akụkọ ya, dịka Sprout, Sapling, na Orchard. Uru na-agagharị n'etiti chain Oha na pool ndị a, ma mgbe ụfọdụ n'etiti pool ndị ahụ n'onwe ha. turnstile na-ele mmegharị ndị ahụ anya:

1. Mgbe ZEC batara n'ime pool nke e chebere echebe, a na-agbakwunye ego ahụ na nguzozi ọha pool ahụ
2. Mgbe ZEC pụtara na pool, a na-ewepụ ego ahụ
3. Network na-ajụ block ọ bụla ga-eme ka nguzozi pool ghọọ nọmba na-adịghị mma, nke pụtara na ihe hapụrụ karịrị ihe ọ bụla batara
4. Azụmahia ọ bụla na-egosi naanị mmịgharị uru net ya n'ime ma ọ bụ n'èzí pool, ya bụ nguzozi uru ya. Maka Kwụọ ụhwọ n'ime otu pool, nke ahụ bụ naanị ugwo

Network na-enyocha nguzozi maka value pool ọ bụla n'ụzọ a, gụnyere Sprout, Sapling, Orchard, pool ọhụrụ Ironwood, na nguzozi Oha na lockbox. N'ihi nke a, ọbụna ma kpọmkwem ihe dị n'ime pool zoro ezo, ihe kacha ukwuu nwere ike isi na ya pụta bụ nke batara n'ime ya. Ọ dịghị mbawanye ego zoro ezo nwere ike gbapụ banye n'ọrụ.

<br/>

## Otu esi enyocha nguzozi uru

Ndekọ dị n'ọnụ ụzọ ahụ bụ naanị nke a pụrụ ịtụkwasị obi n'ihi na a na-amanye Azụmahia ọ bụla igosi na o bufere ego eziokwu, ọbụna ma ego ahụ n'onwe ya zoro ezo. Azụmahia ọ bụla nke e chebere echebe na-ebipụta otu nọmba eziokwu: uru net ọ na-ebuga n'ime ma ọ bụ n'èzí pool, a na-akpọ ya nguzozi uru [value balance]. Nguzozi uru dị mma pụtara na ego hapụrụ pool gaa n'akụkụ Oha, nke na-adịghị mma pụtara na ego batara. Nkọwa nzuzo na-anọgide mechiri emechi, mana ọnụ ọgụgụ net a bụ ọha, ọ bụkwa ya ka turnstile na-agbakọta.

Akụkụ amamihe dị na ya bụ otú Azụmahia si egosi na nọmba ọha ahụ bụ eziokwu n'ekpugheghị ego nzuzo dị n'azụ ya. Usoro ahụ dị iche dabere na pool, nke a bụkwa ezigbo ọrụ igwe nke turnstile.

N'ime pool Sprout mbụ, Azụmahia ọ bụla na-eji JoinSplit. JoinSplit na-emefu notes abụọ zoro ezo wee mepụta abụọ ọhụrụ, ma ọ na-eburu mpaghara ọha abụọ: vpub_old, uru na-abanye na pool nke e chebere echebe site n'akụkụ Oha, na vpub_new, uru na-ahapụ pool laghachi n'akụkụ Oha. JoinSplit ọ bụla ga-enwe nguzozi n'onwe ya, proof zero-knowledge ya na-ekwe nkwa na ntinye zoro ezo na mmepụta zoro ezo na-agbakọta nke ọma. Nguzozi pool Sprout bụ naanị ngụkọta na-aga n'ihu nke vpub_old niile e wepụrụ vpub_new niile n'ofe chain. Nke a bụ ihe mere Sprout ji bụrụ ihe atụ bara uru n'ikpeazụ: n'ihi na vpub_old bụ naanị ụzọ uru nwere ike isi banye n'ime pool, otu iwu na-akwụsị ya nwere ike mechie pool ahụ ruo mgbe ebighị ebi.

Na Sapling, Orchard, na Ironwood, a na-egosi nguzozi n'ụzọ amamihe ka ukwuu, site n'iji binding signature [binding signature]. Kama mbufe ọ bụla inwe nguzozi naanị ya, Azụmahia dum na-eme commitment [commitment] maka ego ọ bụla zoro ezo site n'iji value commitment. Value commitment bụ envelopu mechiri emechi maka nọmba, nke e wuru site na homomorphic Pedersen commitment, nke nwere njirimara pụrụ iche: a pụrụ ịgbakwunye ma wepụ envelopu ndị ahụ na-emegheghị ha. Network na-agbakọta commitments ntinye niile, wepụ commitments mmepụta niile, ma jiri nsonaazụ ya tụnyere ọnụ ọgụgụ net otu Azụmahia kwupụtara, mpaghara valueBalance ya. Naanị Azụmahia nke ego nzuzo ya kwekọrọ n'eziokwu na valueBalance ọha ahụ nwere ike imepụta binding signature ziri ezi n'elu commitments agbakọtara. Ọ bụrụ na mmadụ anwaa ibugharị uru karịa nke ha kwupụtara, commitments agaghị agbakọta, binding signature agaghị enyocha nke ọma, a ga-ajụkwa Azụmahia ahụ. Ironwood na-eji otu protocol Orchard, ya mere ọ na-arụkwa ọrụ n'otu ụzọ ahụ.

Nke a bụkwa ihe na-eme ka mbufe n'etiti pool dị mfe nyocha. Mgbe ego si n'otu pool nke e chebere echebe gaa na nke ọzọ, dịka site na Orchard banye Ironwood, Azụmahia ahụ enweghị ike izochi ego ahụ pụọ na ndekọ. Pool ọ bụla nwere nguzozi uru nke ya nke proofs nke ya ga-emeju: akụkụ Orchard ga-egosi mwepu kwekọrọ site na binding signature ya, akụkụ Ironwood ga-egosikwa ntinye kwekọrọ site na nke ya. A na-egosi uru na-ahapụ otu pool na uru na-abanye na nke ọzọ nke ọ bụla n'onwe ya, ya mere mmegharị n'etiti pool bụ n'ezie ịgafe turnstile ugboro abụọ n'ime otu Azụmahia, otu na-apụ, otu na-abanye, a na-agụkwa ha abụọ n'ihu ọha ọbụna ma ego ndị dị n'okpuru ha nọgide nzuzo.

Ya mere turnstile abụghị ntụkwasị obi. Azụmahia ọ bụla na-egosi n'onwe ya na mgbakọ na mwepụ mmetụta net ya, network na-agbakọta mmetụta net ndị egosiri kwa pool, iwu consensus (ZIP 209) na-ajụ block ọ bụla ga-eme ka nguzozi pool daa n'okpuru efu. Proof n'ọkwa Azụmahia, mmanye n'ọkwa chain.

<br/>

## Ihe mere o ji dị mkpa

turnstile na-enye Zcash ihe atọ n'otu oge.

Nke mbụ, ọ na-ekewa ihe ize ndụ. Njehie cryptographic n'otu pool na-anọ naanị na pool ahụ, n'ihi na turnstile na-akwụsị uru adịgboroja ịgafe banye n'ọkọnọ sara mbara.

Nke abụọ, ọ na-eme ka obodo nwee ike nyochaa ọkọnọ n'azụ. Ọ bụrụ na achọpụta njehie n'ikpeazụ, ndekọ turnstile na-egosi ma uru karịrị nke batara ọ pụtala n'otu pool. Ndekọ dị ọcha bụ ihe akaebe siri ike na ejighị aghụghọ mkpụrụ ego mee ihe.

Nke atọ, ọ na-edobe Kwụọ ụhwọ n'ime pool nzuzo ka ọ na-eme ihe ndị a niile. Azụmahia ọ bụla na-ebipụta naanị mmịgharị uru net ya n'ime ma ọ bụ n'èzí pool, nke maka Kwụọ ụhwọ n'ime otu pool bụ naanị ugwo. Mbufe n'etiti pool, dịka site na Orchard gaa Ironwood, na-egosi ego na-agafe. Ike nyocha na Nzuzo na-ebikọ ọnụ, nke a na-adịghị ahụkebe ma bụ otu n'ime ike dị jụụ nke Zcash.

<br/>

## turnstile n'ọrụ

turnstile abụghị ihe ọhụrụ, ejirila ya mee ihe n'oge ndị dị mkpa n'akụkọ Zcash.

Mgbe Zcash si na pool Sprout mbụ gaa n'ihu n'ebe pool Sapling ọhụrụ dị, turnstile chebere mgbanwe ahụ. E mechara gbochie pool Sprout ka ọ ghara ịnata ntinye ọhụrụ, nke gbara ndị ọrụ ume ịkwaga ebe turnstile na-edobe ndekọ eziokwu. Ọtụtụ afọ ka e mesịrị, eziokwu na ọ nweghị uru hapụrụ Sprout n'ụzọ na-ekwesịghị ekwesị bụ ihe akaebe na ejitụbeghị cryptography mbụ ya mee ihe nke ọma n'ụzọ aghụghọ.

Otu imewe ahụ ugbu a na-echebe mmegharị sitere na Orchard gaa Ironwood. N'afọ 2026, a chọpụtara ma dozie njehie soundness na usoro proving Orchard. Ọ nweghị ihe akaebe na ejitụla ya mee ihe, mana n'ihi na ọrụ nke e chebere echebe bụ nke nzuzo, ijide n'aka agaghị ekwe omume. Nzaghachi ya bụ imechi pool Orchard ochie ma mee ka onye ọ bụla kwaga ego ya site na turnstile banye Ironwood, pool ọhụrụ na-eji protocol e deziri. Ịmanye ego ka ọ gafee turnstile pụtara na mkpụrụ ego adịgboroja ọ bụla e chepụtara nke fọdụrụ agaghị eso, ma mgbe mbugharị gwụchara, onye ọ bụla nwere ike ikwenye na ọkọnọ nke e chebere echebe siri ike.

<br/>

## Iwepụ pool n'otu ụzọ

turnstile na-eme ka o kwe omume iwepụ pool ochie n'enweghị nsogbu, n'otu ụzọ naanị, n'emebighị nkwa ọkọnọ. Aghụghọ ya bụ imechi ọnụ ụzọ mbata ma hapụ ọnụ ụzọ ọpụpụ ka o ghe oghe.

Sprout bụ ihe atụ kacha doo anya. Iji wepụ ya, ZIP 211 gbakwụnyere otu iwu consensus: site n'ịdị elu mmalite ya, mpaghara vpub_old nke JoinSplit ọ bụla ga-abụrịrị efu. Ebe vpub_old bụ naanị ụzọ uru nwere ike isi banye Sprout, ịmanye ya ka ọ bụrụ efu pụtara na ọ dịghị uru ọhụrụ nwere ike ịbanye ọzọ, ebe uru ka nwere ike ịpụ gaa n'akụkụ Oha ma ọ bụ gaa n'ihu Sapling. Pool ahụ ghọrọ nke otu ụzọ. Ọ nwere ike ịpụ naanị, ọ gaghị ejupụta ọzọ. turnstile na-aga n'ihu na-agụ oge niile, ya mere nguzozi nwere ike ịda ka ego na-apụ mana enweghị ike ịrị elu, ọ gaghị enwekwa ike ịda n'okpuru efu.

Mbugharị Orchard gaa Ironwood na-eji otu echiche ahụ. Na upgrade NU6.3, a na-emechi pool Orchard ka ntinye ọhụrụ ghara ịbanye, a na-agwakwa e-akpa ego ka ha ziga ego Orchard gafee turnstile banye pool Ironwood ọhụrụ. Orchard ghọọ pool otu ụzọ nke nwere ike ịpụ naanị. N'ihi na ọpụpụ ọ bụla bụ ịgafe turnstile nke a ga-egosi na ọ ziri ezi, uru adịgboroja ọ bụla e chepụtara nke fọdụrụ na Orchard enweghị ike iso ego eziokwu pụọ nwayọọ. Ọ rapaara n'ime pool nke na-apụ naanị ma a na-ele ya anya n'ọnụ ụzọ. Ka oge na-aga, nke a na-eme ka pool ochie ahụ na-aga n'ebe ọ tọgbọ chakoo ma na-eme ka onye ọ bụla nwee ike ikwenye na uru pụtara adịghị karịa uru batara n'eziokwu.

Nke a bụ ihe kpatara turnstile ji dị mkpa karịa ndekọ ego dị mfe. Ọ bụ usoro na-eme ka Zcash nwee ike iwepụ pool nke e chebere echebe, ma ọ bụ iji belata mgbagwoju anya dịka na Sprout, ma ọ bụ iji gbakee n'ajọ njehie achọpụtara dịka na Orchard, ebe ọ na-edobe nkwa na-aga n'ihu, ọha, na nke enwere ike igosi maka ọkọnọ.

<br/>

## Nghọtahie ndị a na-ahụkarị

- turnstile anaghị ekpughe onye zitere ihe nye onye. Ọ na-egosi ego Azụmahia ọ bụla na-ebufe ZEC n'etiti pool
- Ọ naghị eji aha achọpụta onye na-eme mkpụrụ ego adịgboroja. Ọ na-amachi ego ole nwere ike ịpụ na pool, nke bụ ihe na-echebe ọkọnọ
- Ọ bụghị ihe ọhụrụ maka Ironwood. Ọ chebela mgbanwe pool niile bụ isi nke e chebere echebe n'akụkọ Zcash
- Ngụkọta pool ọha anaghị ebelata Nzuzo maka Kwụọ ụhwọ n'ime pool. Ịbugharị ZEC n'etiti pool na-egosi ego e bugara, nke mere ZIP 318 ji arịọ e-akpa ego ka ha kwaga n'ego ọkọlọtọ ka oge na-aga

## Akụrụngwa

1. [ZIP 209: Machibido Nguzozi Value Pool Chain Karịrị Oke](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0209) - iwu consensus dị n'azụ turnstile
2. [ZIP 211: Ịkwụsị Ịgbakwunye Uru Ọhụrụ na Sprout Chain Value Pool](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0211) - otu e si mechie pool Sprout maka nkwụnye ego ọhụrụ
3. [ZIP 258: NU6.3](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0258) - upgrade na-ewebata pool Ironwood ma na-eduga uru gafee turnstile
4. [Mmanye Turnstile Megide Ime Mkpụrụ Ego Adịgboroja](https://electriccoin.co/blog/turnstile-enforcement-against-counterfeiting/) - nkọwa mbụ sitere na Electric Coin Company
5. [Nkọwapụta Protocol Zcash](https://zips.z.cash/protocol/protocol.pdf) - lee ngalaba gbasara nguzozi na binding signature maka nkọwa zuru ezu
6. [Value Pools, Akwụkwọ Zebra](https://zebra.zfnd.org/dev/rfcs/0012-value-pools.html) - otu node si enyocha nguzozi uru pool ọ bụla
