# NU6.2

> NU6.2 malitere ịrụ ọrụ na Zcash mainnet na block 3,364,600 (June 3, 2026 UTC).

Ihe ị ga-amụta: ihe NU6.2 doziri, ihe mere Zcash ji kwụsị Azụmahia [transaction] nke e chebere echebe [shielded] ruo ihe dị ka otu ụbọchị, na otú network si mechie oghere ahụ n'enweghị ego ọ bụla furu efu.

NU6.2 bụ Zcash [network upgrade](../start-here/network-upgrades), nke akọwara n'usoro iwu na [ZIP 257](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0257), nke nyeere protocol nke Orchard nke e chebere echebe aka ịrụ ọrụ ọzọ mgbe nkwụsị mberede gasịrị. Ọ doziri njehie izi-ezi [soundness bug] n'ime circuit zero-knowledge nke Orchard ma weghachite Azụmahia [shielded](../using-zcash/shielded-pools). Were ya dị ka isiakwụkwọ nrụzi mberede nke akụkọ njehie Orchard. Network upgrade sochirinụ, [Ironwood (NU6.3)](../zcash-tech/ironwood), mechara tinye pool ọhụrụ na nyocha ọhaneze nke nke ochie.

Ihe kpatara nke a ji dị mkpa. Orchard bụ ebe ọtụtụ Kwụọ ụhwọ [payment] nzuzo [privacy] Zcash na-eme. Mgbe achọpụtara ntụpọ na mgbakọ na mwepụ nke na-eme ka pool ahụ bụrụ nke a pụrụ ịtụkwasị obi, network chere nhọrọ siri ike ihu: ịhapụ Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe ka ha na-aga n'ihu n'ihe ize ndụ a maara, ma ọ bụ kwụsị ha ruo mgbe nrụzi dị njikere. NU6.2 bụ otú Zcash si họrọ nchekwa mbụ ma ka weghachite Nzuzo n'ime otu ụbọchị.

![NU6.2 emergency timeline: the Orchard bug was found on May 29, Orchard was paused by an emergency soft fork on June 2, and NU6.2 re-enabled it on a corrected circuit on June 3](/content-images/nu6-2-timeline-0b8de7fe26.webp)

## TL;DR (Nchịkọta dị mkpirikpi)

- NU6.2 malitere ọrụ na mainnet na block 3,364,600 (June 3, 2026 UTC), nke a kọwara na ZIP 257.
- Ọ mere ka Orchard rụọ ọrụ ọzọ mgbe a kwụsịrị ya nwa oge n'ihi ihe mberede: a chọpụtara njehie izi-ezi na Action circuit nke Orchard n'oge nyocha Shielded Labs, ma kọọ ya n'ụzọ kwesịrị ekwesị na ngwụcha May 2026.
- Soft fork mberede na June 2, 2026 (zcashd v6.12.5, zebra v4.5.3) mere ka Azụmahia Orchard kwụsị nwa oge; Azụmahia Oha (transparent) na Sapling nọgidere na-arụ ọrụ.
- Hard fork nke NU6.2 dozire circuit ahụ (tinyere constraint ahụ na-efu efu), mepụta verifying key ọhụrụ nke na-ajụ proof ọjọọ ochie, ma mee ka ogologo proof ziri ezi bụrụ iwu consensus.
- O nweghị ego furu efu ma ọ bụ e kpọchiri akpọchi, o nweghịkwa ihe akaebe na e jiri njehie ahụ zuo ihe; ọrụ ịnyocha uru dị na circuit ochie bụ nke Ironwood (NU6.3), site na pool ọhụrụ na nyocha ọha.

## Ihe atụ a na-ahụ anya

NU6.2 bụ isi nrụzi mberede nke akụkọ Orchard. Chee echiche otu ebe nchekwa ego n'ụlọ akụ nke a chọpụtara na mkpọchi ya nwere ntụpọ dị nzuzo: ụlọ akụ ahụ akwụsị iji ebe nchekwa ahụ otu ụbọchị (nkwụsịtụ ahụ), tinye mkpọchi e meziri emezi (circuit ọhụrụ na igodo nyocha [verifying key]), wee meghee ya ọzọ. Ihe niile e debere tupu nrụzi ahụ ka nọ ebe ahụ, ọ dịghịkwa onye metụrụ ha aka. Ma a ga-enyocha ha ma bufee ha n'ebe nchekwa ọhụrụ kpam kpam (pool ọhụrụ nke Ironwood) n'okpuru nlekọta ọha.

## Ihe mere

Ihe kpalitere ya bụ njehie izi-ezi na Action circuit nke Orchard, bụ mgbakọ na mwepụ kwesịịrị igosi na Azụmahia ọ bụla nke e chebere echebe na-erube isi n'iwu. Onye nchọpụta nọọrọ onwe ya Taylor Hornby chọpụtara ya n'oge nyocha [Shielded Labs](../zcash-organizations/shielded-labs) ma kọọ ya n'ụzọ dị mkpa na ngwụcha May 2026. Ọ nọgidere na-ahụghị ya kemgbe Orchard malitere na 2022, ma n'echiche ọ pụrụ ikwe ka Azụmahia na-adịghị irè gafee dị ka nke dị irè, nke pụrụ ikwe ka ezuru ego ma ọ bụ mefuo ya ugboro abụọ n'ime pool Orchard. Nchịkọta turnstile nke Zcash ka na-egbochi oke uru ọ bụla pụrụ isi na Orchard pụọ, ya mere a pụghị ịbawanye ngụkọta ọkọnọ ZEC n'onwe ya, mana ntụpọ na mgbakọ na mwepụ nke na-eme ka ego e chebere echebe bụrụ nke a pụrụ ịtụkwasị obi dị njọ, ya mere network mere ihe n'ime ụbọchị ole na ole. Ibe [Ironwood](../zcash-tech/ironwood) na-akọwa njehie ahụ na otú ọ pụrụ isi mepụta uru n'uju.

## Nkwụsị mberede

Na June 2, 2026, soft fork mberede kwụsịrị Azụmahia Orchard niile nwa oge. E zipụrụ ya dịka hotfix na zcashd v6.12.5 na zebra v4.5.3.

1. Ezumike Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe Orchard na nnata ego kwụsịrị, ya mere enweghị proof ọhụrụ Orchard a pụrụ imepụta ma ọ bụ nabata.
2. Azụmahia Oha [transparent] na Sapling nọgidere na-arụ ọrụ dịka ọ dị na mbụ.
3. Ebugharịghị, kefriza, ma ọ bụ tufuo ego ọ bụla. Nkwụsị ahụ kwụsịrị naanị ọrụ ọhụrụ Orchard ka a na-emecha nrụzi n'ezie.

![What paused and what kept working: transparent and Sapling transactions ran the whole time, while only Orchard paused for about a day between June 2 and June 3](/content-images/nu6-2-what-paused-37bfa471d9.webp)

## Ihe NU6.2 gbanwere

N'ụbọchị na-esote, June 3, 2026, hard fork NU6.2 malitere na block 3,364,600 ma nyeere Orchard aka ịrụ ọrụ ọzọ n'elu circuit e doziri.

![The NU6.2 circuit fix: the flawed circuit had a missing constraint and an old verifying key, and NU6.2 added the constraint to produce a corrected circuit with a new verifying key that rejects bad proofs](/content-images/nu6-2-the-fix-3038b4eb7e.webp)

1. Ọ doziri gadget variable-base scalar multiplication nke Action circuit site n'itinye constraints ndị na-adịghị.
2. Nke ahụ mepụtara verifying key ọhụrụ, ya mere proofs emere n'okpuru circuit ochie, nke nwere ntụpọ, anaghịzi adị irè, ma proof ọhụrụ ọ bụla ga-eji nke e doziri.
3. Ọ mere ogologo proof kpọmkwem ka ọ bụrụ iwu consensus, mechie oghere nke anaghị etinye iwu tupu ahụ.

E zipụrụ koodu e doziri na zcashd v6.20.0 na zebra v5.0.0, nke ewuru na halo2_gadgets v0.5.0 na orchard crate v0.14.0.

## Ihe NU6.2 na-emebeghị

NU6.2 chebere circuit maka Azụmahia ọhụrụ ọ bụla, mana uru e mepụtara n'okpuru iwu ochie ka nọ na pool Orchard. Inyocha uru ahụ na ibuga ya na pool dị ọcha bụ ọrụ dị iche, nke ahụ bụkwa ihe [Ironwood (NU6.3)](../zcash-tech/ironwood) na-eji pool ọhụrụ ya na turnstile eme.

## Ọ nwere ihe furu efu

Mba. Enweghị ihe na-egosi na ejirila njehie ahụ mee ihe, enweghị imepụta uru na-enweghị ikike, ma ọ dịghị mmetụta ọ bụla n'ego ndị ọrụ. Nchịkọta turnstile nke Zcash kwadoro na ngụkọta ọkọnọ ahụ nọgidere n'ọrụ nke ọma n'oge ihe omume ahụ dum.

## Mmejọ A Na-emekarị

- **Ikwere na Zcash nke e chebere echebe (shielded) adịghị arụ ọrụ otu ụbọchị.** Ọ bụ naanị Orchard kwụsịrị nwa oge; Azụmahia (transactions) Oha (transparent) na Sapling nọgidere na-arụ ọrụ n'oge ahụ niile.
- **Iche na nkwụsị ahụ kpọchiri ma ọ bụ bibie ego.** Ọ dịghị ego a kpaliri, kpọchie, ma ọ bụ tufuo. Nkwụsị ahụ kwụsịrị naanị ọrụ ọhụrụ nke Orchard.
- **Iche na bug ahụ mere ka ndị mwakpo nwee ike ibipụta ZEC.** Ndekọ turnstile kpachiri ngụkọta uru na-apụ na Orchard, ya mere ngụkọta ọnụọgụ ZEC dị adị enweghị ike ịba ụba. Ihe ize ndụ ahụ dị n'ime pool ahụ, ọ bụghị n'ọnụọgụ ego dị adị.
- **Ịgwakọta soft fork mberede (June 2) na NU6.2 (June 3).** Soft fork ahụ gbanyụrụ Orchard. NU6.2 bụ hard fork dozigharịrị circuit ahụ ma mee ka ọ malite ịrụ ọrụ ọzọ.
- **Ịtụ anya na NU6.2 ga-ehicha uru nke circuit ochie.** NU6.2 na-echekwa Azụmahia ọhụrụ ọ bụla. Inyocha na ịkwaga uru ochie bụ ọrụ dị iche nke Ironwood.

## Nkọwa okwu

| Okwu | Nkọwa dị mfe n'asụsụ Bekee |
|---|---|
| Shielded pool | Nhazi ego nke zero-knowledge cryptography na-ezo ego ya na ndị nwe ya |
| Orchard | Protocol nke e chebere echebe nke NU5 webatara. Ọ bụ pool bụ isi nke e chebere echebe nke Zcash n'oge NU6.2; Ironwood dochiri ya na NU6.3, ma Orchard ugbu a bụ naanị maka imefu |
| Soundness bug | Ntụpọ nke na-ekwe ka Azụmahia na-adịghị irè gafee nyocha proof dịka a ga-asị na ọ dị irè |
| Verifying key | Uru ọhaneze network na-eji elele na proof dị irè. Idozi circuit gbanwere ya |
| Soft fork | Mgbanwe iwu kwekọrọ na ụdị ochie, nke kwụsịrị Orchard n'enweghị ikewa network |
| Hard fork | Mgbanwe iwu ahaziri ahazi nke node niile ga-emelite maka ya, nke amalitere na block e setịpụrụ |

## Ajụjụ a na-ajụkarị

Enwere m ohere ịnweta e-akpa ego [wallet] ZEC m furu efu? Mba. Ruo ihe dị ka otu ụbọchị, Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe Orchard kwụsịrị, mana ego ọ bụla efughị ma ọ bụ nọ n'ihe ize ndụ, Azụmahia Oha na Sapling nọgidere na-arụ ọrụ.

Gịnị mere e ji kwụsị ya kama idozi ya na nzuzo? Njehie izi-ezi pụrụ ikwe ka ezuru ego ma ọ bụ mefuo ya ugboro abụọ n'ime pool Orchard, ya mere ihe nchekwa kwesịrị ekwesị bụ ịkwụsị Azụmahia ọhụrụ Orchard ruo mgbe circuit e doziri amalite.

Proofs ochie nke e chebere echebe ka na-arụ ọrụ? Proofs emere n'okpuru circuit ochie anaghịzi adị irè mgbe NU6.2 gasịrị. E-akpa ego ndị nọ na release akwadoro na-eji circuit e doziri na-akpaghị aka.

E jirila njehie ahụ mee ihe? Enweghị ihe na-egosi na e jirila ya mee ihe. Nchọpụta nchekwa jidere ya ma dozie ya tupu mmerụ ọ bụla a maara.

Kedu ka NU6.2 si dị iche na Ironwood? NU6.2 doziri circuit ma mee ka Orchard rụọ ọrụ ọzọ. [Ironwood (NU6.3)](../zcash-tech/ironwood) mechara tinye pool ọhụrụ na turnstile ọhaneze iji nyocha ma bugharịa uru pool ochie ahụ.

<summary>Azịza</summary>

N'ihi na ntụpọ ahụ bụ njehie izi-ezi, nke pụtara na Azụmahia na-adịghị irè pụrụ ịgafe dị ka nke dị irè. Ịhapụ Orchard ghe oghe gaara ahapụ ụzọ maka izu ohi na imefu ugboro abụọ n'ime pool ahụ. Ịkwụsị Azụmahia ọhụrụ Orchard mechiri ụzọ ahụ ruo mgbe circuit e doziri malitere na NU6.2.
