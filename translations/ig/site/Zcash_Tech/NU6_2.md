# NU6.2

> NU6.2 malitere na Zcash mainnet n'ogige 3,364,600 (June 3, 2026 UTC).

Ihe ị ga-ewepụ: ihe NU6.2 doziri, gịnị kpatara Zcash ji kwụsịtụ azụmahịa echekwara maka ụbọchị otu, na etu netwọkụ si mechie oghere ahụ n'efunahụghị ego ọ bụla.

NU6.2 bụ Zcash. [nkwalite netwọkụ.](../start-here/network-upgrades), nke akọwapụtara n'ụzọ iwu kwadoro na [ZIP 257 Ụlọ Ọrụ Na-ezipụ Akwụkwọ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0257), nke na-reenabled Orchard echebe protocol mgbe ihe mberede kwusi. Ọ gbazie a soundness ahụhụ n'ime Orchard si efu-ihe ọmụma circuit ma weghachite [e chebere echebe](../using-zcash/shielded-pools) Chee echiche banyere ya dị ka isi nke mmezi mberede na akụkọ ahụhụ Orchard. Nwelite n'elu, bụ ihe ndị a ga-eji mee atụmatụ maka usoro ahụ ma ọ bụrụ na ị nwere ike imepụta otu ngwaike iji mezie nsogbu gị niile. [Ironwood (NU6.3) Ihe na-eme ka ọ dị mma.](../zcash-tech/ironwood), tinyezie ọdọ mmiri ọhụrụ na nyocha ọha nke ochie.

Why this matters. Orchard is where most private Zcash payments happen. When a flaw was found in the math that keeps that pool honest, the network faced a hard choice: keep shielded transactions running with a known risk, or pause them until the fix was ready. NU6.2 is how Zcash chose safety first and still restored privacy within a day.

![NU6.2 emergency timeline: the Orchard bug was found on May 29, Orchard was paused by an emergency soft fork on June 2, and NU6.2 re-enabled it on a corrected circuit on June 3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-2-timeline.png)

## Gịnị mere?

The trigger was a soundness bug in Orchard's Action circuit, the math that is supposed to prove every shielded transaction obeys the rules. Independent researcher Taylor Hornby found it during a [Ụlọ nyocha echekwara](../zcash-organizations/shielded-labs) audit and disclosed it responsibly in late May 2026. It had sat unnoticed since Orchard launched in 2022, and in theory it could let an invalid transaction pass as valid, which could allow funds to be stolen or double-spent inside the Orchard pool. Zcash's turnstile accounting still capped how much value could ever leave Orchard, so the total ZEC supply itself could not be inflated, but a flaw in the math that keeps shielded funds honest is serious, so the network acted within days. The [Osisi ígwè](../zcash-tech/ironwood) peeji na-akọwa ahụhụ ahụ nakwa etu o si nwee ike ịmepụta uru n'ụzọ zuru ezu.

## Oge nkwụsị mberede.

On June 2, 2026, an emergency soft fork temporarily disabled all Orchard transactions. It shipped as a hotfix in zcashd v6.12.5 and zebra v4.5.3.

1. A kwụsịtụrụ izipu na ịnata Shielded Orchard, yabụ enweghị ike ịmepụta ma ọ bụ nabata ihe akaebe ọhụrụ nke Orchard.
2. Mmekọrịta Transparent na Sapling nọgidere na-arụ ọrụ nke ọma.
3. Enweghị ego ọ bụla a kpaliri, kpọnwụrụ akpọnwụ ma ọ bụ furu efu. Oge nkwụsị ahụ kwụsịrị ọrụ Orchard ọhụrụ mgbe emechara ezigbo ndozi ahụ.

![What paused and what kept working: transparent and Sapling transactions ran the whole time, while only Orchard paused for about a day between June 2 and June 3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-2-what-paused.png)

## Ihe NU6.2 gbanwere

N'echi ya, June 3, 2026, a na-arụ ọrụ ike NU6.2 n'ogige 3.364.600 ma weghachite Orchard na sekit gbanwere.

![The NU6.2 circuit fix: the flawed circuit had a missing constraint and an old verifying key, and NU6.2 added the constraint to produce a corrected circuit with a new verifying key that rejects bad proofs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-2-the-fix.png)

1. O doziri ihe na-agbanwe agbanwe nke usoro Action site n'ịgbakwunye mgbochi ndị a kọrọ.
2. Nke ahụ mere ka e nwee igodo ọhụrụ maka ime nchọpụta, n'ihi ya ihe àmà ndị a na-eme site ná usoro ochie nke nwere ntụpọ adịghịzi irè, ọ bụkwa onye e meziri emezi ga-abụrịrị onye ọhụụ.
3. O mere ka ogologo ihe akaebe ahụ bụrụ iwu a na-agbakọ, nke mechiri oghere nke a na - etinyebeghị n'ọrụ tupu mgbe ahụ.

Koodu a gbaziri agbazi na zcashd v6.20.0 na zebra v5.0.0, wuru halo2_gadgets v0.5.0 na ogige ubi mkpụrụ osisi v0.14.0.

## Ihe NU6.2 emeghị

NU6.2 nwetara okirikiri maka azụmahịa ọhụrụ ọ bụla, mana uru e kere n'okpuru iwu ochie ka nọ na ọdọ mmiri Orchard. Nyochaa uru ahụ ma bugharịa ya gaa ebe dị ọcha bụ ọrụ ọzọ, nke a bụkwa ihe mere ndị ahịa ji eme atụmatụ iji mepụta ego ha chọrọ ịkwụ ụgwọ site na itinye aka na usoro nnweta akụnụba mba ụwa (NUA). [Ironwood (NU6.3) Ihe na-eme ka ọ dị mma.](../zcash-tech/ironwood) na-ejide ya ọhụrụ ọdọ mmiri na turnstile.

## Ọ̀ dị ihe furu efu ?

Mba. Enweghị ihe akaebe na-egosi na a kụrụ ahụhụ ahụ, enweghị mmepụta uru ọ bụla akwadoghị ya, yana enweghi mmetụta maka ego ndị ọrụ. Akụkọ ndekọ nke Zcash gosipụtara ngụkọta ọkọnọ nọgidere n'enweghị nsogbu site na mmemme niile.

## Akwụkwọ ọkọwa okwu

Okwu. N'asụsụ Bekee nkịtị pụtara:
|---|---|
❖ Ebe a na-edebe ego ndị e zoro ezo. A naghị eji ihe ọ bụla amata onye nwe ha ma ọ bụ otú o si dịrị ya.
Orchard. Zcash si ugbu a na-echebe protocol, ebe kasị onwe azụmahịa emee.
◯ Nrụpụta nke ezi uche. Njehie na-eme ka azụmahịa adịghị mma gafere nyocha nkwenye dị ka a ga - asị na ọ bụ ezigbo ya.
◯ Igodo nyocha ❖ Ụkpụrụ ọha na eze nke netwọkụ ahụ ji enyocha ma ihe akaebe ọ dị irè. Ịgbazi sekit gbanwere ya ❑ Ọ bụrụ na ị nwere ike ịhụ data ndị ọzọ, pịa bọtịnụ nchọpụta ka i wee hụ otu n'ime ha.
Soft fork. Mgbanwe iwu na-agbaso azụ, nke kwụsịtụrụ Orchard n'enweghị ikewa netwọkụ ahụ.
 Hard fork  Mgbanwe iwu jikọtara ọnụ nke niile nodes ga-emelite maka, arụ ọrụ na ngọngọ setịpụrụ.

## Ajụjụ ndị a na-ajụkarị

Did I lose access to my ZEC? No. For about a day, shielded Orchard transactions were paused, but no funds were lost or at risk, and transparent and Sapling transactions kept working.

Gịnị mere ị ga-eji kwụsị kama idozi ya n'udo? Ihe ọjọọ na-eme ka ego zuo ohi ma ọ bụ jiri okpukpu abụọ mee ihe n'ime ọdọ mmiri Orchard, yabụ ụzọ dị mma iji gbochie azụmahịa ọhụrụ nke Orchard ruo mgbe usoro a gbaziri agbazigharị.

Ihe akaebe ochie a na-echekwa ka ọ ga - arụ ọrụ? Ihe akaụntụ e mere n'okpuru usoro ihe ochie ahụ anaghịzi adị irè mgbe NU6.2. Wallets on a supported release automatically use the corrected circuit.

Ọ bụ ihe nchọgharị nche na-ejide ya ma dozie tupu ọ bụla mara nsogbu.

Kedu ka NU6.2 si dị iche na Ironwood? NU6.2, gbaziri sekit ahụ ma nyeghachi Orchard ike. [Ironwood (NU6.3) Ihe na-eme ka ọ dị mma.](../zcash-tech/ironwood) e tinyeziri ọdọ mmiri ọhụrụ na ụlọ ntụrụndụ ọha mmadụ iji nyochaa ma kwalite uru nke ebe ochie ahụ.

<summary>Answer</summary>

N'ihi na ntụpọ ahụ bụ ihe dị mma, nke pụtara azụmahịa ndị a na-adịghị arụ ọrụ nwere ike ịgafe dịka iwu. Ịhapụ Orchard ga - emepe ụzọ maka ohi ma jiri okpukpu abụọ n'ime ọdọ mmiri ahụ. ịkwụsịtụ ahịa ọhụrụ Orchard mechiri ọnụ ụzọ ruo mgbe agbanyere usoro nhazi ahụ na NU6.2.
