# NU6.2

> NU6.2 malitere ọrụ na Zcash mainnet na ngọngọ 3,364,600 (June 3, 2026 UTC).

Ihe ị ga-ewepụ: ihe NU6.2 doziri, ihe mere Zcash ji kwụsị azụmahịa echekwara ihe dị ka otu ụbọchị, na otu netwọk si mechie oghere ahụ n'efughị ego ọ bụla.

NU6.2 bụ Zcash [mmelite netwọk](../start-here/network-upgrades), nke a kọwara n'ụzọ doro anya na [ZIP 257](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0257), nke mere ka usoro nchekwa Orchard ahụ rụọ ọrụ ọzọ mgbe nkwụsịtụ mberede gasịrị. O doziri nsogbu ahụike na sekit enweghị ihe ọmụma Orchard's ma weghachi ya [echebe](../using-zcash/shielded-pools) Azụmahịa. Were ya dị ka isi ihe ndozi mberede nke akụkọ ahụhụ Orchard. Mmelite na-esote, [Ironwood (NU6.3)](../zcash-tech/ironwood), wee tinye ọdọ mmiri ọhụrụ na nyocha ọha nke nke ochie ahụ.

Ihe kpatara nke a ji dị mkpa. Orchard bụ ebe ọtụtụ ụgwọ Zcash nkeonwe na-eme. Mgbe achọpụtara ntụpọ na mgbakọ na mwepụ nke na-eme ka ọdọ mmiri ahụ bụrụ eziokwu, netwọk ahụ chere nhọrọ siri ike ihu: mee ka azụmahịa echekwara na-aga n'ihu na ihe egwu a maara, ma ọ bụ kwụsịtụ ha ruo mgbe ndozi ahụ dị njikere. NU6.2 bụ otú Zcash si họrọ nchekwa mbụ ma ka weghachite nzuzo n'ime otu ụbọchị.

![NU6.2 emergency timeline: the Orchard bug was found on May 29, Orchard was paused by an emergency soft fork on June 2, and NU6.2 re-enabled it on a corrected circuit on June 3](/content-images/nu6-2-timeline-0b8de7fe26.webp)

## Kedu ihe mere

Ihe na-akpali ya bụ nsogbu ahụike dị na sekit Orchard's Action, mgbakọ na mwepụ nke a na-eche na ọ ga-egosi na azụmahịa ọ bụla echekwara na-agbaso iwu ndị ahụ. Onye nchọpụta nọọrọ onwe ya bụ Taylor Hornby chọpụtara ya n'oge a na-eme nnyocha [Ụlọ nyocha echekwara](../zcash-organizations/shielded-labs) nyochaa ma kpughee ya nke ọma na ngwụcha Mee 2026. Ọ nọghị ebe a na-ahụghị ya kemgbe Orchard malitere na 2022, n'echiche, ọ nwere ike ikwe ka azụmahịa na-ezighi ezi gafere dị ka nke ziri ezi, nke nwere ike ikwe ka e zuru ego ma ọ bụ mefuo ya okpukpu abụọ n'ime ọdọ mmiri Orchard. Akaụntụ Zcash's turnstile ka na-ekpuchi ego ole ọ ga-efu Orchard, yabụ enweghị ike ibuli mkpokọta ZEC n'onwe ya, mana ntụpọ dị na mgbakọ na mwepụ nke na-eme ka ego echekwara bụrụ eziokwu dị oke njọ, yabụ netwọk ahụ mere ihe n'ime ụbọchị ole na ole [Ironwood](../zcash-tech/ironwood) peeji na-akọwa ahụhụ ahụ na otu o si emepụta uru zuru oke.

## Nkwụsịtụ mberede ahụ

Na June 2, 2026, otu ngwa enyemaka mberede kwụsịrị azụmahịa niile Orchard nwa oge. E zigara ya dị ka ihe ngwọta dị mma na zcashd v6.12.5 na zebra v4.5.3.

1. E kwụsịtụrụ izipu na ịnata ihe Orchard nke e chebere echebe, yabụ enweghị ike ịmepụta ma ọ bụ nabata ihe akaebe ọhụrụ Orchard.
2. Azụmahịa ndị na-egosi ihe doro anya na nke na-eme ka Sapling na-arụ ọrụ nke ọma.
3. E meghị ego ọ bụla, e kpọchiri ya, ma ọ bụ furu efu. Nkwụsịtụ ahụ kwụsịrị ọrụ ọhụrụ Orchard mgbe e mechara ndozi ahụ.

![What paused and what kept working: transparent and Sapling transactions ran the whole time, while only Orchard paused for about a day between June 2 and June 3](/content-images/nu6-2-what-paused-37bfa471d9.webp)

## Ihe NU6.2 gbanwere

N'echi ya, Juun 3, 2026, a gbanyere ike NU6.2 na blọk 3,364,600 wee mee ka Orchard rụọ ọrụ ọzọ na sekit edoziri.

![The NU6.2 circuit fix: the flawed circuit had a missing constraint and an old verifying key, and NU6.2 added the constraint to produce a corrected circuit with a new verifying key that rejects bad proofs](/content-images/nu6-2-the-fix-3038b4eb7e.webp)

1. O doziri ngwaọrụ mmụba scalar nke mgbanwe nke sekit Action site na ịgbakwunye ihe mgbochi ndị na-efu.
2. Nke ahụ mepụtara igodo nkwenye ọhụrụ, yabụ ihe akaebe emere n'okpuru sekit ochie ahụ, nke nwere ntụpọ, anaghịzi akwado, ihe akaebe ọhụrụ ọ bụla ga-ejikwa nke edoziri.
3. O mere ka ogologo ihe akaebe ahụ bụrụ iwu nkwekọrịta, na-emechi oghere a na-emebeghị nke ọma na mbụ.

E zigara koodu edoziri na zcashd v6.20.0 na zebra v5.0.0, nke e wuru na halo2_gadgets v0.5.0 na ubi osisi v0.14.0.

## Ihe NU6.2 emeghị

NU6.2 kwadoro sekit ahụ maka azụmahịa ọhụrụ ọ bụla, mana uru e kere n'okpuru iwu ochie ka dị n'ọdọ mmiri Orchard. Ịnyocha uru ahụ na ibuga ya n'ọdọ mmiri dị ọcha bụ ọrụ dị iche, nke ahụ bụkwa ihe ọ bụ [Ironwood (NU6.3)](../zcash-tech/ironwood) ejiri aka ya na ọdọ mmiri ọhụrụ ya na ihe e ji atụgharị ya.

## Ò furu efu ihe ọ bụla?

Mba. Enweghị ihe akaebe na e jiri ahụhụ ahụ mee ihe, enweghị mmepụta uru na-enweghị ikike, ọ nweghịkwa mmetụta ọ bụla na ego ndị ọrụ. Akaụntụ Zcash's turnstile kwadoro na mkpokọta ego ahụ nọgidere na-adịgide ruo mgbe ihe omume ahụ dum gasịrị.

## Ajụjụ Ndị A Na-ajụkarị

Èfuola m ohere ịnweta ZEC? Mba. Ruo ihe dị ka otu ụbọchị, a kwụsịtụrụ azụmahịa Orchard echekwara, mana ego ọ bụla furu efu ma ọ bụ nọrọ n'ihe ize ndụ, azụmahịa Sapling na-agakwa n'ihu na-arụ ọrụ.

Gịnị mere a ga-eji kwụsị kama ịkwụsịtụ nwayọ? Nsogbu ahụike nwere ike ikwe ka e zuru ego ma ọ bụ mefuo ya okpukpu abụọ n'ime ọdọ mmiri Orchard, yabụ ịkwaga n'enweghị nsogbu bụ ịkwụsị azụmahịa ọhụrụ Orchard ruo mgbe sekit edoziri agbanwere.

Ihe akaebe ochie e ji kpuchie ekpuchi ka na-arụ ọrụ? Ihe akaebe emere n'okpuru sekit ochie anaghịzi ekwenye mgbe NU6.2. Obere akpa dị na ntọhapụ akwadoro na-eji sekit edoziri emezi na akpaghị aka.

E jirila ahụhụ ahụ mee ihe? Enweghị ihe akaebe ọ bụla. Nnyocha nchekwa jidere ya ma dozie ya tupu ihe ọjọọ ọ bụla a maara.

Kedu ka NU6.2 si dị iche na Ironwood? NU6.2 doziri sekit ahụ ma mee ka Orchard. [Ironwood (NU6.3)](../zcash-tech/ironwood) wee tinye ọdọ mmiri ọhụrụ na ebe a na-atụgharị ihe n'ihu ọha iji nyochaa ma gbanwee uru ọdọ mmiri ochie ahụ.
