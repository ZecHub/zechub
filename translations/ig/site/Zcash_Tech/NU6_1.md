# NU6.1

> NU6.1 malitere ịrụ ọrụ na mainnet Zcash na block 3,146,400 (Nọvemba 24, 2025 UTC).

Ihe ị ga-amụta: otu Zcash si akwụ ụgwọ maka mmepe nke ya n’enweghị ụlọ ọrụ na-achị ya, na otu NU6.1 si nye obodo na ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego ohere ikwu okwu kpọmkwem banyere ego ahụ.

NU6.1 bụ Zcash [network upgrade](../start-here/network-upgrades), nke akọwara na [ZIP 255](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0255), nke guzobere usoro ọhụrụ maka itinye ego na Gọọmentị maka mmepe Zcash. Ọ na-ekpebi ebe akụkụ nke ụgwọ ọrụ block ga-aga na onye na-achịkwa ya, ma tinyekwara mmezi nka ole na ole dị nta. Ndị Electric Coin Company na Zcash Foundation kwadoro NU6.1.

Ihe kpatara nke a ji dị mkpa. Ọtụtụ sọftụwia na-enweta ego site n’ụlọ ọrụ. Zcash bụ network decentralized nke na-enweghị otu onye nwe ya, ya mere ọ na-eche ajụjụ siri ike ihu: olee otú ọ ga-esi kwụọ ndị na-aga n’ihu iwulite ya ụgwọ, n’ụzọ obodo tụkwasịrị obi? NU6.1 bụ azịza Zcash ugbu a. Ọ na-eduga akụkụ nke ụgwọ ọrụ block ọ bụla n’ime pool abụọ, otu maka ikike obodo na otu nke ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego n’onwe ha na-achịkwa.

![The NU6.1 funding split: most of each block reward goes to miners, 8 percent to Zcash Community Grants, and 12 percent to a coin-holder-controlled fund](/content-images/nu6-1-funding-split-890dcf6881.webp)

## Usoro itinye ego

NU6.1 na-ekewa ụgwọ ọrụ block ọ bụla n’akụkụ atọ.

1. Akụkụ kasị ukwuu, ihe dịka pasent 80, na-aga n’aka ndị onye na-egwụputa na-echekwa network ahụ.
2. Pasent 8 na-aga Zcash Community Grants (ZCG), nke na-akwado ọrụ nke obodo na maka obodo.
3. Pasent 12 na-aga n’ime ego ọhụrụ nke ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego na-achịkwa, nke Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox nyere mmalite.

A kọwara iwu nke usoro a na [ZIP 1016](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1016), ha ga-adịgidekwa ruo halving nke atọ Zcash, nke na-enye obodo oge ịhụ ka ọ si arụ ọrụ tupu ha eme ya ka ọ bụrụ nke na-adịgide adịgide ma ọ bụ gbanwee ya.

## Onye na-achịkwa ego ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego

Usoro pasent 12 ahụ bụ echiche ọhụrụ. Kama ụlọ ọrụ ma ọ bụ ntọala ikpebi otu esi emefu ya, ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego na-ekpebi ọnụ ma hà ga-ahapụ ego ahụ ma ọ bụ debe ya.

1. Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox, nke e kere na network upgrade NU6 gara aga, na-enye ego a mmalite.
2. Ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego na-ekpebi ọnụ ma hà ga-ekesa ego ahụ maka ọrụ ecosystem ma ọ bụ debe ya ka akpọchiri.
3. Nke a na-enye ndị na-enwe ZEC olu kpọmkwem n’ụzọ Zcash si akwado ọdịnihu ya.

![The NU6.1 governance flow: the Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox seeds the coin-holder fund, and coin holders decide whether to distribute grants or hold the funds](/content-images/nu6-1-governance-21210bcc35.webp)

## Mmezi ndị nta

NU6.1 mekwara ka ihe nka abụọ dị ọcha.

1. Tupu NU6.1, ụfọdụ usoro RPC anaghị amata ego a na-ewepụ na nchebe nke ọma site na pool Orchard, ya mere nguzozi na ugwo nwere ike ịpụta ezighị ezi na e-akpa ego na ndị na-enyocha azụmahịa. NU6.1 doziri nke ahụ.
2. Ọ gbanwere ndabara e-akpa ego zcashd ka o wuo Azụmahia ụdị 5, usoro e webatara mbụ na NU5, kama ụdị 4. Azụmahia ụdị 4 ka dị irè.

## Otu itinye ego Zcash si gbanwee

NU6.1 bụ otu nzọụkwụ n’akụkọ ogologo banyere otu Zcash si akwụ ụgwọ maka mmepe nke ya.

1. Canopy (2020) kwụsịrị ụgwọ ọrụ ndị guzobere mbụ ma kee [ego mmepe](../start-here/development-fund).
2. NU6 (2024) guzobere Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox, debe akụkụ nke mmepụta maka ikike ndị obodo ga-ekpebi n’ọdịnihu.
3. NU6.1 (2025) zara ajụjụ NU6 hapụrụ ghe oghe, onye na-ekpebi na olee otú, site n’ịga n’ihu na-enye pasent 8 nke ụgwọ ọrụ block maka ikike obodo na iduga pasent 12 n’ime ego ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego na-achịkwa nke e nyere mmalite site na nchekwa ahụ.

![How Zcash funding evolved: Canopy created the development fund, NU6 set up the lockbox, and NU6.1 set the rules for who controls it](/content-images/nu6-1-funding-history-bcf6a94ad4.webp)

## Nkọwa okwu

| Okwu | Nkọwa dị mfe n’asụsụ Bekee |
|---|---|
| Block reward | ZEC ọhụrụ a na-emepụta ma na-akwụ ka a na-egwụputa block ọ bụla |
| Zcash Community Grants (ZCG) | Kọmitii na-akwado ọrụ nke obodo Zcash na maka ya |
| Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox | Nchekwa e guzobere na NU6 nke na-edobe akụkụ nke mmepụta maka ikike ndị obodo ga-ekpebi n’ọdịnihu |
| Coin-holder fund | Ego NU6.1, nke lockbox nyere mmalite, nke ndị nwere ZEC na-achịkọta ọnụ na-achịkwa |
| Halving | Ihe omume a haziri ahazi ebe a na-egbutu ụgwọ ọrụ block n’ọkara |
| Network upgrade (NU) | Mgbanwe ahaziri ọnụ n’iwu consensus Zcash, nke a na-eme ka ọ rụọ ọrụ n’ogologo block e setịpụrụ |

## Ajụjụ ndị a na-ajụkarị

NU6.1 ọ na-agbanwe ZEC m ma ọ bụ Nzuzo m? Mba. NU6.1 gbasara otu e si akwado mmepe ego, ọ bụghị gbasara Azụmahia ma ọ bụ Nzuzo. Ego gị na Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe anaghị emetụta.

Ebee ka ego itinye ego si abịa? Ọ na-abịa na ụgwọ ọrụ block, ZEC ọhụrụ a na-ewepụta ka a na-egwụputa block. A na-eduga akụkụ ya n’ime ego ikike na ego ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego kama ka ọ niile gaa n’aka ndị onye na-egwụputa.

Gịnị ka ego ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego bụ maka? Ikike ecosystem nke ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego na-ahọrọ ọnụ ịkwado ego, ma ọ bụ idebe ya ma ọ bụrụ na ha ahọrọ ichere.

Nke a ọ bụ nke na-adịgide adịgide? Mba. Usoro a na-aga ruo halving nke atọ, mgbe ahụ obodo nwere ike idobe ya, gbanwee ya, ma ọ bụ dochie ya.

Olee otú NU6.1 si dị iche na NU6? NU6 kere lockbox ahụ. NU6.1 setịpụrụ iwu maka onye na-achịkwa ya na otu esi ekewa ego ahụ.

## Nwalee nghọta gị

NU6 debeere pool ego na lockbox mana hapụrụ otu nnukwu ajụjụ n’azaghị. Gịnị ka ọ bụ, oleekwa otú NU6.1 siri zaa ya?
