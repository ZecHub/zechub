# NU6.1

> NU6.1 malitere na Zcash mainnet n'ụlọ 3,146,400 (November 24, 2025 UTC).

Ihe ị ga-ewepụ: otu Zcash si akwụ ụgwọ maka mmepe nke ya n'enweghị ụlọ ọrụ na-elekọta, yana etu NU6.1 siri nye obodo na ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego okwu ozugbo.

NU6.1 bụ Zcash. [nkwalite netwọkụ.](../start-here/network-upgrades), nke a kọwara na [ZIP 255 Ụlọ ọrụ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0255), that set up a new funding and governance model for Zcash's development. It decides where a slice of the block rewards goes and who controls it, and it also shipped a few smaller technical fixes. NU6.1 was endorsed by both the Electric Coin Company and the Zcash Foundation.

Why this matters. Most software is paid for by a company. Zcash is a decentralized network with no single owner, so it faces a hard question: how does it fund the people who keep building it, in a way the community trusts? NU6.1 is Zcash's current answer. It routes part of every block reward into two pools, one for community grants and one that coin holders themselves control.

![The NU6.1 funding split: most of each block reward goes to miners, 8 percent to Zcash Community Grants, and 12 percent to a coin-holder-controlled fund](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-1-funding-split.png)

## Ụdị ego a ga-eji na-eme ya bụ:

NU6.1 na-ekewa ụgwọ ọrụ ọ bụla n'ime akụkụ atọ.

1. Ihe ka ukwuu, ihe dị ka pasent 80 na-agara ndị ọrụ mịna bụ́ ndị na-echebe netwọk ahụ nche.
2. Pasent 8 na-aga Zcash Community Grants (ZCG), nke a na-enye ego maka ọrụ ndị obodo.
3. Pasent 12 na-aga n'ụlọakụ ọhụrụ nke ndị ji mkpụrụ ego ahụ chịkwaa, bụ́ nke Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox malitere.

A kọwara iwu nke ụdị a na: [ZIP 1016 (mkpọka)](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1016), ha na-anọgide ruo mgbe nke atọ Zcash halving, nke na-enye obodo oge iji hụ otú ọ na-arụ ọrụ tupu eme ya adịgide ma ọ bụ ịgbanwe ya.

## Onye na-achịkwa ego ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego ahụ?

Ihe ọhụrụ e mepụtara bụ na kama ụlọ ọrụ ma ọ bụ òtù dị iche iche ga-ekpebi otú ha ga-esi emefu ego, ndị ji mkpụrụ ego ahụ ekpebie ka a hapụ ya n'aka ha.

1. The Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox, kere na mbụ NU6 upgrade, mkpụrụ a enweta ego.
2. Ndị ji mkpụrụ ego ahụ na-ekpebi ma hà ga-ekenye ha maka ịrụ ọrụ n'ebe ndị ọzọ, ka ọ̀ bụ ha agaghị ekesa ya.
3. Nke a na-enye ndị mmadụ nwere ZEC olu ozugbo n'ụzọ Zcash si enweta ego ọdịnihu ya.

![The NU6.1 governance flow: the Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox seeds the coin-holder fund, and coin holders decide whether to distribute grants or hold the funds](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-1-governance.png)

## Ihe ndị dị nta e dozirila .

NU6.1 mekwara ka ihe abụọ dị na teknụzụ.

1. Before NU6.1, some RPC methods did not correctly recognize funds being unshielded from the Orchard pool, so balances and fees could look wrong in wallets and explorers. NU6.1 fixed that.
2. Ọ gbanwere ndabara nke obere akpa zcashd iji wuo usoro 5 azụmahịa, ụdị mbụ e webatara na NU5, kama nsụgharị 4. Usoro 4 azụmahịa ka dị irè.

## Etu ego Zcash si mepụta ya

NU6.1 bụ otu nzọụkwụ n'ime akụkọ toro ogologo banyere etu Zcash si akwụ ụgwọ maka mmepe nke ya.

1. Canopy (2020) kwụsịrị ụgwọ ọrụ ndị guzobere mbụ ma mepụta a [ego mmepe](../start-here/development-fund).
2. NU6 (2024) guzobere Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox, na-echekwa oke nke mbipụta maka onyinye ndị obodo kpebiri n'ọdịnihu.
3. NU6.1 (2025) zara ajụjụ nke NU6 hapụrụ, onye kpebiri na otu esi eme ya site n'ịnọgide na pasent 8 nke ụgwọ ọrụ nchịkọta maka onyinye obodo ma duzie 12 percent gaa ego ndị nwe mkpụrụ ego.

![How Zcash funding evolved: Canopy created the development fund, NU6 set up the lockbox, and NU6.1 set the rules for who controls it](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-1-funding-history.png)

## Ajụjụ ndị a na-ajụkarị

NU6.1 na-agbanwe ZEC m ma ọ bụ nzuzo m? Nke NO.NU6.1 gbasara etu esi enweta ego mmepe, ọbụghị azụmahịa maọbụ nkeonwe gị.Ego gi na ahia echekwara emetụtaghi ya.

Where does the funding money come from? From block rewards, the new ZEC issued as blocks are mined. A share is routed to the grant and coin-holder funds instead of all of it going to miners.

Gịnị bụ ego ndị na-ejide mkpụrụ ego maka? Onyinye Ecosystem nke ndị nwere mkpụrụ ego họọrọ ka ha kwụọ ụgwọ, ma ọ bụ ijigide ya ma ọ bụrụ na ha chọrọ ichere.

Is this permanent? No. The model runs until the third halving, and then the community can keep it, change it, or replace it.

Kedu ka NU6.1 si dị iche na NU6? NU6 kere igbe mkpọchi. NU6.1, setịpụrụ iwu maka onye na-achịkwa ya yana otu esi eke ego ahụ.

## Nwalee nghọta gị .

NU6 debere ego n'ime igbe ma hapụ otu nnukwu ajụjụ a na-azaghị. Kedu ihe ọ bụ, oleekwa otú NU6.1 si zaa ya?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The open question was who decides how the lockbox funds are used. NU6.1 answered it by splitting the money: 8 percent to Zcash Community Grants, and 12 percent to a fund that coin holders themselves control, choosing whether to distribute it or hold it.
</details>
