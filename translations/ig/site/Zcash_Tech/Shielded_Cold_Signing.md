# Mbinye Aka Oyi Nke E Chebere Echebe

> **Isi echiche:** E-akpa ego ibe ya na-ahụ ihe niile dị mkpa iji wuo ma nyochaa Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe. Naanị hardware wallet na-ejide ikike ịkwado mmefu ego. Nkewa ahụ bụ isi ihe mbinye aka oyi nke e chebere echebe pụtara.

Ichekwa ego na Zcash gụnyere ibufe ego site na Adreesị akpa ego Oha gaa na nke onwe, nke e chebere echebe, na-ezo nkọwa Azụmahia site n’iji atụmatụ Nzuzo [privacy] nke Zcash.

### Gịnị na-agbanwe mgbe ị chebere ZEC?

Site na Azụmahia Zcash Oha, enwere ike ịhụ ozi dịka:

- Adreesị akpa ego onye zitere
- Adreesị akpa ego onye nata
- ego e bufere

na blockchain.

Mgbe ị na-echebe ZEC, ego gị na-abanye na pool nke onwe (dịka Ironwood). Blockchain nwere ike ikwenye na Azụmahia dị irè n’egosịghị nkọwa Azụmahia nkeonwe gị. Kemgbe mmelite NU6.3 na 28 Julaị 2026, ego a ka chebere ọhụrụ na-abanye na pool Ironwood ebe pool Orchard ochie anaghịzi anabata nkwụnye ego ọhụrụ, mana ọ ka na-ekwe ka a wepụta ego.

### Akụkụ Nke Azụmahia E Chebere Echebe

### 1. Note (Ndekọ Ego Fọdụrụ Nke E Chebere Echebe)

Note bụ ndekọ nzuzo, nke cryptographic, na-anọchi anya ego ZEC a na-emefubeghị nke onye ọrụ nwere.

N’adịghị ka blockchain ọha, ebe a na-edepụta ego fọdụrụ n’ụzọ doro anya, note nke e chebere echebe na-ezochi uru na nkọwa nwe ya ka ndị na-ekiri on-chain ghara ịhụ onye nwere ego ahụ.

Mgbe a malitere Azụmahia, e-akpa ego ibe ya na-enyocha ledger, na-achọpụta note onye ọrụ a na-emefubeghị, ma họrọ note ndị a ga-emefu.

### 2. Nullifier (Usoro Ịchọpụta Mmefu Ugboro Abụọ)

Ebe ọ bụ na ezoro nkọwa note n’aka ledger ọha, network chọrọ usoro iji chọpụta mmefu ugboro abụọ na-ekpugheghị note a na-emefu.

Nullifier bụ akara cryptographic pụrụ iche esitere na note kpọmkwem. Mgbe a na-emefu note, a na-ebipụta nullifier ya na blockchain.

Network na-achọpụta na edebeghị nullifier ahụ na mbụ. Ọ bụrụ na edebeghị ya, enwere ike ịnakwere mmefu ahụ. Nke dị mkpa bụ na onye na-ekiri n’èzí enweghị ike iji nullifier mata note kpọmkwem o siri na ya pụta.

E-akpa ego ibe ya na-esite ma na-ewu nullifier kwesịrị ekwesị dị ka akụkụ nke ịchịkọta data Azụmahia.

## Igodo

Igodo bụ mkpụrụ ozi cryptographic na-achịkwa ohere ịnweta ego gị ma kwe ka e-akpa ego gị rụọ ọrụ dịka ịnata, ịhụ, na imefu ZEC.

E-akpa ego na-ejide ọtụtụ Igodo dị iche iche, nke ọ bụla nwere ebumnuche dị iche, dịka otu akaụntụ nwere ike inwe nzere dị iche maka ohere ịgụ na ohere ide.

Igodo bụ etiti mmekọrịta dị n’etiti e-akpa ego ibe ya na hardware wallet, n’ihi na Igodo dị iche na-arụ ọrụ dị iche, ma naanị otu n’ime ha kwesịrị ịnọgide kpọchiri.

Ọ dị mkpa ịmara na pool ọ bụla nke e chebere echebe na-ewu Igodo ya dịka osisi mwepụta, ebe akụ na-atụ aka site n’otu akụkụ gaa n’ihe ọ bụla a pụrụ isite na ya nweta. Ndị ọrụ chọrọ ịnata Kwụọ ụhwọ nke e chebere echebe ga-enwerịrị Adreesị akpa ego Kwụọ ụhwọ nke e chebere echebe, nke a na-emepụta site na Igodo ego.

### Ọkwa Igodo

**1. Igodo ego**

Nke a bụ Igodo kacha metụta nchekwa. Ọ na-enye ikike cryptographic iji kwado mmefu ZEC.

Nke a bụ Igodo a na-ekwu maka ya mgbe ọ bụla e kwuru na “hardware wallet na-ejide Igodo” n’akụkụ ndị ọzọ nke akụkọ a.

N’usoro mbinye aka oyi, a na-edobe Igodo ego n’ime hardware wallet ma ghara ikpughere ya e-akpa ego ibe ya. E-akpa ego ibe ya ekwesịghị inwe Igodo a ma ọlị.

Na Sapling na Orchard abụọ ahụ, ọkwa Igodo na-esite na Igodo ego mgbọrọgwụ wepụta ikike mmefu na ikike ịhụ dịka alaka abụọ dị iche:

- **Sapling:** Igodo ego a gbasapụrụ (𝑎𝑠𝑘,𝑛𝑠𝑘,𝑜𝑣𝑘) na-esitepụta Igodo ikikere proof (𝑎𝑘,𝑛𝑠𝑘) na Full Viewing Key (𝑎𝑘,𝑛𝑘,𝑜𝑣𝑘), ndị nke a na-esitepụta ọzọ viewing key mbata (𝑖𝑣𝑘) na Adreesị akpa ego dịgasị iche.
- **Orchard:** Igodo ego mgbọrọgwụ (𝑠𝑘) na-ekewa ozugbo gaa na Igodo ikikere mmefu (𝑎𝑠𝑘) na Full Viewing Key (𝑎𝑘,𝑛𝑘,𝑟𝑖𝑣𝑘).

Nkewa nhazi a na-enye ntọala cryptographic iwu kwadoro maka ikewapụ Igodo hardware, nke gụnyere e-akpa ego ibupụ alaka viewing (UFVK) gaa na kọmputa nnabata maka imekọrịta ego fọdụrụ na iwulite Azụmahia, ebe a na-edobe alaka ikikere mmefu kpọchiri kpamkpam n’ime ngwaọrụ.

Mwepụta Igodo na-aga naanị n’otu ụzọ. Nke a pụtara na enweghị ike ịtụgharị viewing key azụ iji wughachi ikike mmefu. Enweghị ike ịtụgharị ya n’ụzọ cryptographic na-eme ka ibupụ Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK) bụrụ nke nchekwa n’ihi na ọ na-enye ọhụhụ e-akpa ego zuru ezu n’ekpugheghị ihe nzuzo mmefu dị n’ime ngwaọrụ.

**2. Viewing key**

Zcash na-ekewa ikike ịhụ ọrụ e chebere echebe na ikike imefu ya. Viewing key na-enye e-akpa ego ma ọ bụ akụkụ ọzọ enyere ikike ohere ịhụ ozi gbasara Azụmahia e chebere echebe n’enyeghị ikike mmefu.

- **Full Viewing Key (FVK), nke a kpọchiri dịka Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK):** a kpachapụrụ anya isite ya na Igodo ego, ọ na-eme ka sọftụwia nwee ike ịchọpụta ma mepee ọrụ e chebere echebe nke e-akpa ego, wee si otú ahụ mata Azụmahia. Ọ na-enyekwa ya ohere ịgbakọ ego fọdụrụ n’enweghị ike ịkwado mmefu. A kọwara ya nke ọma dịka ihe sitere na Igodo ego nke Nzuzo ya dị ntakịrị, ọ bụghị mbipụta na-esighị ike nke seed.

Full Viewing Key (FVK) na-enye ọhụhụ zuru ezu naanị maka ịgụ, ebe ọ na-edobe nkewa nchekwa siri ike. Site n’imepe ciphertext note na-abata na nke na-apụ (site n’akụkụ outgoing-viewing-key ya), e-akpa ego ibe ya nke nwere naanị FVK nwere ike igosi akụkọ Azụmahia zuru ezu, nke a kara akara, karịa naanị ego fọdụrụ na-agbanwe agbanwe.

Ọtụtụ Adreesị akpa ego Kwụọ ụhwọ dịgasị iche nke Ihe na-apughi ijikọ ọnụ na-ekekọrịta otu FVK na viewing key mbata (IVK), na-ekwe ka emepụta Adreesị akpa ego na-enweghị oke n’enweghị ịbawanye ibu nyocha blockchain.

Nkewa ọrụ a bụ ntọala usoro ọrụ mbinye aka oyi. A na-ebufe nyocha chain na-aga n’ihu, nsuso akụkọ ihe mere eme, na njikwa Adreesị akpa ego niile na sọftụwia nnabata, ebe ikike mmefu na-anọpụrụ iche n’ime ngwaọrụ hardware.

### Ọdịiche dị oke mkpa: UFVK na Igodo ego

> **Enwere ike ikekọrịta UFVK. Igodo ego ga-anọgide na hardware wallet.**
>
> E-akpa ego ibe ya na-eji viewing key nyochaa e-akpa ego, chọpụta ego na-abata, mata note, ma nyere aka ịkwadebe Azụmahia. Ọ nweghị Igodo nkeonwe dị mkpa iji kwado mmefu.

Dịka ekwuru na mbụ, nkewa Igodo bụ ntọala nhazi mbinye aka oyi.

E-akpa ego ibe ya nwere ike ịrụ ọtụtụ ọrụ achọrọ iji kwadebe Azụmahia n’enweghị mgbe ọ bụla ọ ga-achịkwa ego ahụ. Hardware wallet chọrọ naanị ịnata Azụmahia a kwadebere ma rụọ ọrụ mbinye aka ikpeazụ.

Ndị na-emepụta hardware wallet ekwesịghị imepụta nkewa a n’onwe ha. Nhazi Igodo Zcash ekewalarị ikike ịhụ na ikike mmefu na ọkwa protocol, ndị na-emepụta ya na-ewukwa naanị ọrụ mbinye aka na mbupụ ha n’elu nhazi dị adị ahụ. Protocol na-ekwe ka esite na Igodo ego nweta ozi dịka Adreesị akpa ego nke e chebere echebe ma ọ bụ full viewing key, ebe Igodo ego n’onwe ya na-anọgide na nzuzo, na-eme ka hardware wallet dịka Ledger, Keystone, na Hito nwee ike inye sọftụwia ibe ya ozi viewing ọ chọrọ n’ekpugheghị Igodo nkeonwe nwere ike imefu ego ahụ.

Ọ dị mkpa ịmara na mkpa iwulite Azụmahia abụghị otu ihe na mkpa Igodo ego nkeonwe. Nkewa ahụ bụ otu n’ime isi echiche nchekwa dị n’azụ njikọta hardware wallet.

## Iwulite na Ikikere Azụmahia E Chebere Echebe

Ndị a bụ usoro dị n’ịmepụta na inye Azụmahia ikikere. N’okwu dị mfe, usoro ndị a bụ ihe e-akpa ego na-eji data ahụ eme iji zipu ego:

### Iwulite Azụmahia (Ịchịkọta Azụmahia E Chebere Echebe)

Iwulite Azụmahia bụ usoro ịchịkọta ozi achọrọ iji mepụta Azụmahia Zcash dị irè site na Kwụọ ụhwọ onye ọrụ bu n’obi.

Usoro iwulite Azụmahia na-ejikọta note ahọpụtara, ndị nata, ego, ugwo, nullifier, na data Azụmahia ndị ọzọ achọrọ n’ime nhazi Azụmahia. Maka Azụmahia e chebere echebe, ọ na-akwadokwa ozi achọrọ maka imepụta zero-knowledge proof na signature ndị dị mkpa.

E-akpa ego ibe ya na-arụkarị ọkwa a. Ọ

- Na-ahọrọ note ndị a ga-emefu
- Na-ekpebi ntinye na mwepụta Azụmahia
- Na-agbakọ uru ndị dị mkpa
- Na-akwadebe data Azụmahia a ga-emecha gosipụta na binye aka.

N’usoro mbinye aka oyi, enwere ike ikpokọta data Azụmahia nke si na ya pụta n’ime PCZT ma nyefee ya hardware wallet maka nzọụkwụ mbinye aka dị mkpa maka nchekwa.

### Igosipụta (Igosi Na Protocol Dị Irè)

Igosipụta na-ezo aka n’imepụta zero-knowledge proof (dịka Halo 2 ma ọ bụ Groth16) nke na-egosi na Azụmahia na-agbaso iwu protocol Zcash.

Proof ahụ na-enyere network aka ijide n’aka:

- Onye na-emefu nwere ikike imefu note ndị ahụ
- Note ndị ahụ dị adị n’ezie
- Nullifier kwekọrọ na ya pụrụ iche
- Ngụkọta ntinye hà ngụkọta mwepụta gbakwunyere ugwo.

Ọ na-arụ ihe ndị a niile n’ekpugheghị nkọwa nzuzo ọ bụla dị n’okpuru.

Ime proof chọrọ nnukwu ike mgbakọ. Ibufe ọrụ a n’aka ngwaọrụ ibe ya na-egbochi hardware wallet ịkwụsị ọrụ ma ọ bụ ịgwụ ebe nchekwa.

### Mbinye Aka (Ikikere Mmefu)

Mbinye aka dị nnọọ iche na igosipụta. Signature dijitalụ na-arụ ọrụ dịka akara ikikere doro anya nke onye ji Igodo nkeonwe mepụtara.

Ebe zero-knowledge proof na-achọpụta ịdị irè Azụmahia, signature dijitalụ na-egosi ikike mmefu. Signature na-akwado na ezigbo onye nwe ya kwadoro oke mbufe a kapịrị ọnụ.

Mbinye aka chọrọ obere ike mgbakọ, nke na-eme ka ọ dabara nke ọma maka hardware wallet.

### Gịnị bụ mbinye aka oyi nke e chebere echebe?

Mbinye aka oyi nke e chebere echebe bụ usoro iji kwado Azụmahia Zcash nke e chebere echebe ebe a na-edobe Igodo ego nkeonwe iche na e-akpa ego ibe ya ejikọrọ na ịntanetị. Enwere ike iwulite ma kwadebe Azụmahia n’ime kọmputa n’ịntanetị, ebe hardware wallet dị iche, nke a na-edobe offline ma ọ bụ n’ụzọ ọzọ a kewapụrụ, na-eji Igodo ya echebere kwado Azụmahia ahụ.

## E-Akpa Ego Ibe Ya Na Hardware Wallet

Azụmahia e chebere echebe nwere ike ịgụnye e-akpa ego ibe ya na hardware wallet, nke nke ọ bụla na-arụ ọrụ pụrụ iche.

### E-Akpa Ego Ibe Ya

Gịnị bụ e-akpa ego ibe ya?

E-akpa ego ibe ya na-arụ ọrụ na kọmputa ma ọ bụ ngwaọrụ mkpanaka ma na-arụ ọtụtụ ọrụ dị n’ịmepụta Azụmahia, dịka:

- Ịhọrọ note ndị a ga-emefu
- Iwulite Azụmahia
- Ịgbakọ data Azụmahia dị mkpa
- Ịhazi imepụta zero-knowledge proof ndị achọrọ.

### Gịnị mere e-akpa ego ibe ya ji baa uru?

E-akpa ego ibe ya na-ahụ maka usoro imepụta Azụmahia, mana ọ bụrụ na malware abanye na kọmputa, onye mwakpo nwere ike:

- ịhụ ihe ị na-eme,
- gbanwee Azụmahia a na-ebinyebeghị aka,
- gbalịa iduhie gị ka ị kwado ihe.

E-akpa ego ibe ya adịghị mkpa inwe ohere ịnweta Igodo nkeonwe echekwara na hardware wallet; ya mere, ọ nwere ike ijikwa iwulite Azụmahia na ọrụ mgbakọ ndị ọzọ ebe hardware wallet na-enye oke nchekwa maka Igodo nkeonwe.

### Hardware Wallet

Hardware wallet bụ ngwaọrụ anụ ahụ offline nke na-ekewapụ Igodo nkeonwe gị na ekwentị ma ọ bụ kọmputa ejikọrọ, na-arụ ọrụ dịka ebe nchekwa dị nchebe maka akụ crypto gị.

### Gịnị mere hardware wallet ji baa uru?

Ọ bụrụ na malware abanye na kọmputa gị, onye mwakpo nwere ike:

- ịhụ ihe ị na-eme,
- gbanwee Azụmahia a na-ebinyebeghị aka,
- gbalịa iduhie gị ka ị kwado ihe.

Enweghị ike iwepụta Igodo nkeonwe naanị site na hardware wallet, ya mere ngwaọrụ ahụ nwere ike rụọ ọrụ dịka oke nchekwa.

Onyonyo ikike e-akpa ego ibe ya megide hardware

Nkewa dị n’etiti e-akpa ego abụọ a dị mkpa karịsịa maka Azụmahia e chebere echebe n’ihi na ha dị mgbagwoju anya karịa Azụmahia Oha ma na-agụnye akụkụ dịka note e chebere echebe, nullifier, commitment, zero-knowledge proof, na signature Azụmahia.

Ọ gaghị adị irè ka hardware wallet rụọ ọrụ mgbakọ ọ bụla. Kama nke ahụ, e-akpa ego ibe ya na-akwadebe Azụmahia ma nye hardware wallet ozi dị mkpa maka mbinye aka, ebe Igodo ego nkeonwe na-anọpụrụ iche n’ime ngwaọrụ hardware.

## Ikewapụ Igodo

> Ikewapụ igodo pụtara na igodo nzuzo ndị nwere ike inye ikike imefu ego anaghị ahapụ hardware wallet. Onye enyemaka nwere ike ịkwadebe Azụmahia ahụ; ọ nweghị ike ịnye signature ya.

Ikewapụ igodo na-ekwu maka idobe igodo nzuzo achọrọ iji nye ikike Azụmahia iche na e-akpa ego enyemaka na ngwaọrụ ọ na-arụ ọrụ na ya. N’usoro mbinye signature oyi nke e chebere echebe, e-akpa ego enyemaka nwere ike iwulite Azụmahia ma hazie proof ndị achọrọ, ebe hardware wallet na-edobe igodo ego nzuzo dị mkpa n’iche.

Ọ bụrụ na e mebie kọmputa ma ọ bụ ekwentị enyemaka, onye mwakpo nwere ike iso Azụmahia ahụ kparịta tupu e nye ya signature, ma n’uche dị mma o nweghị ike iwepụta igodo nzuzo ahụ na hardware wallet. Ya mere, igodo ahụ na-anọ n’ime oke nchekwa nke ngwaọrụ hardware.

Nkewa a na-enye e-akpa ego enyemaka ohere ịrụ ọrụ iwulite Azụmahia na ọrụ mgbakọ na mwepụ na-enyeghị ya ohere ịnweta ihe igodo achọrọ iji nye Azụmahia ahụ ikike ozugbo.

Ihe dị mkpa ịmata ọdịiche ya bụ na “viewing key” apụtaghị na ngwa ahụ na-anata ụdị seed dị ike ntakịrị.

Na Zcash, a Full Viewing Key (FVK/UFVK) sitere n’Igodo ego n’ụzọ a kpachapụrụ anya, ma na-enye onye ji ya ikike ịchọpụta na ịkọwa ihe omume nke e chebere echebe metụtara ya, ma ọ bụghị inye ikike imefu ego. FVK bụ ihe sitere n’Igodo ego nke nzuzo ya dị ntakịrị.

**Otu ịdọ aka ná ntị Nzuzo dị mkpa:**

Ya mere, a ga-amara na ngwa ahụ enweghị ike izuru ego ahụ naanị site na UFVK, mana ọ nwere ike ịhụ ihe omume nzuzo nke e chebere echebe nke e-akpa ego ahụ.

Nke a mere o ji zie ezi karị ịkọwa ngwa enyemaka dị ka nke nwere *ikike ịhụ*, karịa ikwu naanị na ọ nwere “ozi Oha.”

Ụzọ kachasị mfe isi kwuo echiche a dum bụ na e-akpa ego enyemaka na-enweta ihe igodo zuru iji mata ihe na-eme n’e-akpa ego, ma ọ naghị enweta ihe igodo zuru iji nye ikike maka ihe na-eme n’ego e-akpa ego ahụ. Hardware wallet, n’aka nke ọzọ, na-edobe ikike imefu ego n’iche ma jiri ya naanị mgbe onye ọrụ kwadoro Azụmahia.

Nke ahụ bụ isi ihe nlereanya ikewapụ igodo dị n’azụ atụmatụ hardware wallet Zcash ndị a nke e chebere echebe, ọ bụkwa otu ihe nlereanya ahụ na-apụta, n’ụzọ mmejuputa ọ bụla, na Ledger, Keystone, na Hito ndị a ga-ekpuchi n’oge adịghị anya.

## PCZT

PCZT pụtara Partially Created Zcash Transaction. Ọ bụ ngwugwu na-eburu Azụmahia Zcash na ozi achọrọ iji mechaa ya site n’otu ọkwa ruo n’ọkwa ọzọ.

Ọ bụ usoro ahaziri ahazi maka Azụmahia Zcash mgbe a ka na-ewu Azụmahia ahụ. Ọ na-enye ndị sonyere ma ọ bụ ngwaọrụ dị iche iche ohere ịrụ akụkụ dị iche iche nke usoro ịmepụta Azụmahia ma nyefee Azụmahia e mechara naanị akụkụ ya n’etiti ha.

Iwulite Azụmahia dị mgbagwoju anya nke ukwuu karịa inye signature maka nnyefe Oha dị mfe n’usoro cryptocurrency zero-knowledge dị ka Zcash. Azụmahia ndị e chebere echebe chọrọ proof zero-knowledge na-achọ mgbakọ siri ike, dịka ndị e mepụtara site na Halo 2 ma ọ bụ Groth16. N’ihi ya, ngwaọrụ nwere ike dị ala dịka hardware wallet nwere ike ghara inwe ebe nchekwa na ikike nhazi zuru iji jikwaa usoro Azụmahia dum.

Iwebata PCZT na-edozi ọtụtụ ihe ịma aka bụ isi na nhazi crypto nke e chebere echebe. PCZT bụ usoro data etiti ahaziri ahazi nke na-ekewa ịmepụta Azụmahia n’ọrụ dị iche iche, nweere onwe ha. Ọ na-enye kọmputa enyemaka dị ike ohere ijikwa nchịkọta Azụmahia na imepụta proof, ebe ọ na-amachibido hardware wallet anọghị n’ịntanetị naanị n’inye ikike na inye signature.

### Mmachi Hardware na Nchekwa Oyi

Ọ bụ ezie na hardware wallet na-enye gburugburu nchekwa maka ichekwa igodo ego nzuzo, ha na-enwekarị RAM, ike mgbakọ, na ikike izipu data dị obere ma e jiri ha tụnyere kọmputa enyemaka. Mmachi ndị a na-eme ka ọ ghara ịdị irè ka ha mepụta proof zero-knowledge na-achọ mgbakọ siri ike ma ọ bụ debe ọnọdụ Azụmahia zuru ezu achọrọ n’oge usoro iwulite Azụmahia.

N’ihi ya, e-akpa ego enyemaka nwere ike ijikwa ọrụ na-achọ mgbakọ siri ike, ebe hardware wallet na-anọgide lekwasịrị anya n’ọrụ nchekwa dị oke mkpa nke inye Azụmahia ikike site n’iji Igodo ego ya echekwara.

Ọ na-eme ka mmadụ jụọ, ọ bụrụ na ngwaọrụ a nwere mmachi dị otu a, kedu ka ọ ka si eme nyocha *zuru oke* iji bụrụ nke a pụrụ ịtụkwasị obi?

N’ụzọ dị mfe, hardware wallet enweghị ike ịnakwere uru ọ bụla na-enweghị usoro sitere na kọmputa enyemaka ma nye ya signature. Ọ chọrọ ozi zuru iji gosi na uru a na-enye signature kwekọrọ na Azụmahia onye ọrụ chọrọ inye ikike. Nke a na-emepụta nguzozi siri ike n’ihi na ngwaọrụ ahụ ga-eme nyocha na ntule zuru iji chebe usoro inye signature, ebe ọ na-arụ ọrụ n’ime mmachi siri ike banyere ebe nchekwa, mgbakọ, nha firmware, na mgbagwoju anya mmejuputa.

Ijikwa Orchard PCZT nke Ledger bụ ihe atụ nke ihe ịma aka a, na-egosi na ntule pụrụ iche dabere na circuit nwere ike ime ka mmejuputa dị mfe ijikwa n’ụgbọọrụ nwere mmachi, mana o nwekwara ike ịchọ mgbanwe firmware mgbe usoro Azụmahia ma ọ bụ circuit akwadoro gbanwere.

Mgbalị abụọ na-aga n’ihu na-egosi azịza dị iche maka otu mmachi a.

E webatara atụmatụ nyocha banyere pipeline proving oge na-adịgide adịgide zuru oke nke dabara maka nhazi hardware wallet mepere emepe na kọmputa nkịtị na Grant #423. Ebumnuche ya bụ ịchọpụta akụrụngwa cryptographic enwere ike imejuputa n’enweghị nsogbu na gburugburu dịka C ma ọ bụ Rust, ebe a na-ebelata mgbagwoju anya na ibu ọrụ nlekọta nke nkwado Zcash e chebere echebe.

Otú ọ dị, ụzọ ọzọ bụ **Zcash SeedSigner** a tụrụ aro, nke bụ signer mepere emepe, kewapụrụ n’ikuku, e wuru site na akụrụngwa dị ọnụ ala a na-enweta ngwa ngwa. Usoro ọrụ a tụrụ aro na-edobe kọmputa enyemaka iche na ngwaọrụ inye signature, na-ebufe PCZT gafee oghere ikuku maka nyocha na inye signature n’elu ngwaọrụ.

### PCZT Na-eme ka Nhazi E Kewara Ekewa Kwesị Ekwe

Ụkpụrụ Partially Created Zcash Transaction (PCZT) bụ nhazi data iwu kwadoro nke na-ejikọta e-akpa ego enyemaka na hardware wallet.

E-akpa ego enyemaka nwere ike ijikwa ịhọrọ note, chọpụta nullifier, na imepụta proof zero-knowledge n’onwe ya. Nke a kwe omume n’ihi na ozi achọrọ maka usoro ndị a, tinyere igodo eji ewepụta nullifier, sitere na viewing key kama ihe ọ bụla ejikọrọ na ikike imefu ego. E-akpa ego enyemaka na-etinyezi ihe ndị a n’ime akpa PCZT.

A na-enye hardware wallet PCZT, nke na-agụ ọnọdụ Azụmahia ahụ, dịka ọnụọgụ na Adreesị akpa ego ndị nnata, tinye signature site n’Igodo ego ya echekwara, ma nye ngwaọrụ enyemaka ahụ PCZT emelitere.

Nhazi a na-eguzobe oke nchekwa siri ike: hardware onye ọbịa dị ike nwere ike ịrụ mgbakọ zero-knowledge siri ike na-enweghị ịchọ ohere ịnweta Igodo ego, ebe ngwaọrụ nchekwa oyi nwere ike dị ala nwere ike idobe nchekwa ego na-enweghị ịchọ ịgbakọ cryptography dị arọ.

### Nkwekọrịta Nzuzo nke Prover

Ọ bụ ezie na inyefe e-akpa ego enyemaka ma ọ bụ prover ọzọ ọrụ imepụta proof zero-knowledge na-edobe Igodo ego n’iche, ọ ka chọrọ ka prover ahụ nweta ozi Azụmahia dị mkpa, dịka uru note, Adreesị akpa ego ndị nnata, na commitment.

PCZT na-edobe nkewa nhazi ahụ site n’ịhụ na enwere ike inye ozi a maka proving na-ebunyeghị Igodo ego nzuzo achọrọ maka inye ikike.

Nke a na-ekwe nkwa na ikike proving dị iche na ikike imefu ego. Prover ahụ nwere ike ịrụ ọrụ imepụta proof na-achọ mgbakọ siri ike na-enweghị inweta ikike cryptographic iji mefuo ego onye ọrụ.

### Mmekọrịta PCZT

Ka PCZT na-aghọ akụkụ dị mkpa nke usoro ọrụ hardware wallet, multisignature, na inye signature kewapụrụ n’ikuku nke Zcash, mmekọrịta n’etiti mmejuputa onwe ha na-adịwanye mkpa.

Atụmatụ June 2026 nke AngryDavee kwuru na mmejuputa PCZT adịworị n’ọrụ dị iche iche gụnyere Ledger, Keystone, Hito, OneKey, na mmejuputa ntụaka dị na librustzcash. Otú ọ dị, atụmatụ ahụ chọpụtara na nnwale a na-ekekọrịta iji gosi na mmejuputa ndị a na-akọwa PCZT n’otu ụzọ adịghị.

Azịza a tụrụ aro bụ usoro nnwale mmekọrịta mepere emepe nwere vector nnwale PCZT iwu kwadoro na nnwale n’etiti mmejuputa nke na-ekpuchi usoro ọrụ zuru ezu create → sign → finalize. Ọ tụkwara aro ịnwale oke ọrụ dịka Combiner, ebe enwere ike ikpokọta ozi e nyegoro signature n’akụkụ sitere na signer dị iche iche tupu emecha Azụmahia.

Nke a dị mkpa karịsịa nye hardware wallet n’ihi na PCZT na-enye ohere ikewapụ iwulite Azụmahia na inye signature: e-akpa ego enyemaka nwere ike iwulite Azụmahia ma kwadebe PCZT, ebe ngwaọrụ hardware na-anata ozi inye signature achọrọ ma rụọ ọrụ inye signature dị oke mkpa maka nchekwa.

Emejuputaghị atụmatụ ahụ n’ihi na Zcash Community Grants kpebiri ịghara ịga n’ihu na ikike ahụ mgbe ha nyochachara atụmatụ ahụ ma tụlee nzaghachi obodo. N’ihi ya, emepụtaghị usoro nnwale n’etiti mmejuputa ahụ site n’ike a. Ya mere, ọ bụ azịza a tụrụ aro maka ihe ịma aka mmekọrịta PCZT achọpụtara, ọ bụghị ụkpụrụ dị ugbu a n’ofe Zcash.

### Ledger dị ka Gburugburu Inye Signature Hardware

Ledger bụ ụlọ ọrụ hardware wallet. E wuru ngwaọrụ ya gburugburu Secure Element, nke bụ chip pụrụ iche e mere iji jide ihe igodo nzuzo ma debe ya n’iche n’anụ ahụ na ngwaọrụ ndị ọzọ, nakwa n’ihe ọ bụla ejikọrọ na ya. Secure Element ahụ bụ isi ihe mere ngwaọrụ Ledger ji bụrụ nke a pụrụ ịtụkwasị obi maka nchekwa oyi. Nke a pụtara na ọbụna ma ọ bụrụ na e mebie kọmputa ma ọ bụ ekwentị e jikọtara na ya, a pụghị iwepụta igodo dị n’ime Secure Element.

N’onwe ya, ngwaọrụ Ledger enweghị ihe gbasara cryptocurrency ọ bụla kpọmkwem. Ọ chọrọ ngwa etinyere n’ime ya nke maara otu esi ejikwa cryptography pụrụ iche nke ego ahụ.

Ngwaọrụ Ledger enweghị ihe gbasara cryptocurrency ọ bụla kpọmkwem, ya mere ọ chọrọ ngwa maara cryptography nke ego ahụ. Maka Zcash, e nweela ọgbọ abụọ:

- Ngwa “Zcash Shielded” mbụ nke Zondax wuru, nke e ji e-akpa ego enyemaka dịka Zkool arụ ọrụ ma a na-ewepụ ya na 5 November 2026 na,
- Ngwa Zcash ọhụrụ nke Ledger (3.9.4+), nke Ledger Wallet Desktop 4.21+ na-eji n’onwe ya maka Ironwood ZEC e chebere echebe.

Ihe nlereanya ikewapụ igodo dị n’okpuru metụtara ha abụọ.

Zkool bụ e-akpa ego enyemaka dị n’ekwentị ma ọ bụ kọmputa gị nke na-agwa ngwa ahụ okwu. Ọ na-ewulite Azụmahia, zigara ya Ledger, ngwa Zondax dị n’elu ngwaọrụ ahụ enyezie ya signature ma zighachi signature ahụ.

Ya mere, Zondax bụ software na-arụ ọrụ na hardware, Zkool bụ software na-arụ ọrụ n’èzí ya nke na-eji ngwa Zondax ekwurịta okwu na ngwaọrụ ahụ.

### Gịnị na-ahapụ Ledger?

Software Zcash nke Ledger nwere ọrụ pụrụ iche maka ibupụ viewing key nke e-akpa ego, bụ igodo na-enye software ohere ịhụ ego fọdụrụ na Azụmahia na-enweghị ike imefu ihe ọ bụla.

Ọrụ a nwere ike iwepụta otu n’ime ihe abụọ:

- Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK), nke bụ viewing key zuru ezu na-ekpuchi pool niile e chebere echebe nke Zcash n’otu oge, ma ọ bụ
- Orchard Full Viewing Key (FVK)

N’ụzọ ọ bụla, ihe na-apụta bụ naanị viewing key. N’agbanyeghị ụdị ị rịọrọ, ọrụ a enweghị ụzọ isi wepụta otu mpempe ozi nke ga-eme ka mmadụ nwee ike ibugharị ego n’ezie, bụ Igodo ego.

Nke ahụ bụ isi ebumnuche otu e siri wuo ya; e mere ya ka naanị ihe ọ nwere ike ibupụ bụrụ ihe dị nchebe inye ngwa enyemaka.

A haziri usoro igodo Zcash ka maka pool ọ bụla e chebere echebe, Igodo ego si na ya ewepụta ihe abụọ dị iche:

- Igodo inye ikike imefu ego, na
- Full viewing key

FVK nwere ike isi na ya wepụta ikike ndị ọzọ metụtara nlele, mana ọ dịghị mgbe ụzọ mmepụta ahụ laghachiri n’ọrụ ikike imefu ego. Ozugbo igodo abanyela n’akụkụ nlele nke usoro ahụ, ọ na-anọ ebe ahụ, a pụghịkwa iji ya wughachi igodo ikike imefu ego o sitere na ya.

Ironwood na-agbaso otu usoro a, n’ihi na ọ na-eji ihe owuwu igodo na note dị n’ala nke Orchard eme ihe ọzọ. Otú ọ dị, ọ bụ pool dị iche n’ọkwa consensus, nwere osisi commitment na setị nullifier nke ya. Nke a pụtara na UFVK bụ n’ezie ngwugwu viewing key kwa-pool, Oha, Sapling, Orchard, na ugbu a Ironwood, ọ bụghị otu igodo kwụ ọtọ.

UFVK e bupụrụ tupu Ironwood amalite agaghị enwe viewing key Ironwood, n’ihi na pool ahụ adịghị mgbe ahụ. Nke a bụ kpọmkwem ihe mere e-akpa ego ji dị mkpa imelite iji mata ma wepụta UFVK nwere ike Ironwood mgbe network upgrade NU6.3 gasịrị.

### Gịnị na-anọ na Ledger?

Ikike imefu ego na-anọ n’elu ngwaọrụ ahụ.

Signer Ledger na-anata Azụmahia/PCZT site n’aka host ma mepụta signature ikike imefu ego dabere na pool Azụmahia ahụ na-emefu site na ya, pool Ironwood n’ihi na njikọta ugbu a nke Ledger na-akwado naanị Ironwood. A na-emepụtazi signature ahụ n’elu ngwaọrụ ahụ, host na-anatakwa naanị signature, ọ bụghị Igodo ego nzuzo.

Nke a kwekọrọ na ihe nlereanya nchekwa n’ozuzu nke Ledger. Secure Element ya na-ekewapụ ihe igodo nzuzo, na ihe nchekwa ngwa Ledger chọrọ na-amachibido ngwa ikpughere host nzuzo ndị ahụ.

Signer Ledger nwere ike ibupụ FVK/UFVK ebe ọ na-enye signature Azụmahia n’onwe ya. A na-enye ikike abụọ ahụ site n’ọrụ dị iche iche, ọ bụghị dị ka otu ngwugwu.

### Gịnị ka nke ahụ pụtara n’omume?

Were ya na Ledger gị nwere ZEC 10.

E-akpa ego enyemaka nwere ike inwe UFVK ma si otú ahụ chọpụta: “E-akpa ego a anatala ZEC 10.” Ọ nwere ike inyocha ma gosipụta Azụmahia na ego fọdụrụ.

Mana ọ bụrụ na mmadụ emebie e-akpa ego enyemaka, UFVK naanị ya anaghị enye ha ike imepụta signature ikike imefu ego.

Ebumnuche signature ikike imefu ego dị iche bụ ime ka ngwaọrụ nwere mmachi dịka hardware wallet nwee ike inye mmefu e chebere echebe ikike na-enweghị ime proof zk-SNARK zuru ezu n’onwe ha.

### Ngwa Zondax Ledger; akụkụ hardware wallet

#### Gịnị bụ Zondax?

Zondax bụ ụlọ ọrụ mmepe ndị ọzọ wuru ngwa Ledger Zcash. Ọ bụ ngwa firmware n’ezie na-arụ ọrụ n’hardware Ledger ma na-enye ngwaọrụ ahụ ikike ịghọta na ịhazi Azụmahia Zcash na mbụ.

Zondax bụ onye mmepụta nọ n’èzí. Hardware Ledger enweghị ikike ebum n’obi iji hazie Azụmahia Zcash, ikike ahụ sitere kpamkpam na software Zondax.

### Mmejuputa mbụ Zcash Ledger

Ebe nchekwa ngwa Zondax Ledger Zcash bụ software na-arụ ọrụ n’elu ngwaọrụ Ledger. Ngwa ahụ lekwasịrị anya na mbụ na Azụmahia Sapling e chebere echebe, tupu Orchard adị dịka pool e chebere echebe bụ isi nke Zcash; ya mere nkwado Sapling bụ ókè ihe ngwaọrụ Ledger nwere ike ime na ZEC e chebere echebe.

Nhazi ya na-egosi nkewa ahụ, ebe akụkụ host/enyi na-akwadebe ozi Azụmahia, ebe Ledger na-ejikwa ọrụ igodo dị mkpa na inye signature.

Ọbụna n’ụdị mbụ a nke nkwado hardware wallet Zcash, ụkpụrụ bụ isi, ya bụ iwulite na proving na host ma mesịa inye signature n’iche n’elu ngwaọrụ, abụrụla atụmatụ ahụ.

### Mmegharị gaa n’ihu Orchard

Ọrụ Ledger kwagara n’ihu Orchard + PCZT. Nke a bụ mgbanwe bara uru karịa obere mmelite, ebe Orchard na-eji usoro proving dị iche, Halo 2, ma e jiri ya tụnyere Groth16 nke Sapling, yana ihe owuwu note na igodo dị iche; ya mere ịgbatị firmware naanị Sapling ka ọ jikwaa inye signature Orchard chọrọ ntọala cryptographic ọhụrụ n’akụkụ ngwaọrụ, ọ bụghị naanị patch firmware.

Otú ọ dị, ka Zcash na-aga n’ihu Ironwood, Zondax na Ledger gbanwere nlekwasị anya mmepe ha iji kwado Ironwood ozugbo, kama imecha mmejuputa Orchard naanị iji megharịa ya maka Ironwood n’oge ọzọ. Nke a na-anọchi anya ọkwa ọzọ na mmalite mmejuputa Ledger, ebe Ironwood na-ewuli n’elu nhazi cryptographic Orchard ma na-ewebata pool e chebere echebe dị iche na mgbanwe protocol ndị ọzọ.

### Ihe na-eme n’ime ngwa Ledger a

A kọwara usoro nke si na ya pụta dị ka:

1. Host na-ewulite PCZT.
2. Host na-eziga ozi Azụmahia dị mkpa na ngwaọrụ Ledger.
3. Ngwaọrụ ahụ na-emepụta signature ikike imefu ego Orchard.
4. Host na-ejikwa ihe niile na-esote nke ahụ, gụnyere binding signature na nchịkọta Azụmahia ikpeazụ.

Ngwa na-arụ ọrụ n’ime Ledger anaghị ahụ ma ọ bụ mepụta ihe ọ bụla gafere signature ikike imefu ego n’onwe ya, ọrụ niile dị arọ n’usoro mgbakọ ma ọ bụ chọrọ data buru ibu, proving, binding, na nchịkọta, na-anọ n’aka host, ebe ọ dịghị mkpa ịtụkwasị ya obi n’ihe igodo.

### Njikọta e chebere echebe nke Ledger; Ledger Wallet Desktop

Njikọta Zcash kachasị ọhụrụ nke Ledger na-enye ihe atụ doro anya nke otu e nwere ike isi kewaa iwulite Azụmahia e chebere echebe na inye signature dị oke mkpa maka nchekwa n’etiti software enyemaka na hardware wallet.

Site na Ledger Wallet Desktop 4.21+ na ngwa ngwaọrụ Zcash 3.9.4+, ndị ọrụ nwere ike izipu ma nata Ironwood ZEC e chebere echebe ozugbo site na Ledger Wallet Desktop. Njikọta a na-akwado nnyefe nzuzo gaa nzuzo, ichebe ego site na pool Oha, iwepụ nchebe gaa na pool Oha, Adreesị akpa ego nnata nzuzo, na memo. A na-enyocha Azụmahia ma nye ya signature n’elu ngwaọrụ Ledger kama ịdabere naanị n’ozi kọmputa gosipụtara.

Akụkụ dị mkpa nke nhazi a bụ nkewa ikike nlele na imefu ego. N’oge mmekọrịta ego fọdụrụ nzuzo, Ledger na-ebupụ Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK), nke echekwara n’obodo na kọmputa. UFVK na-enye Ledger Wallet Desktop ohere ịchọpụta Azụmahia e chebere echebe metụtara ya na ịchọpụta ego fọdụrụ n’e-akpa ego, mana ọ naghị enye ikike imefu ego ahụ. Ikike imefu ego dị oke mkpa maka nchekwa na-anọ n’elu ngwaọrụ Ledger, nke na-arụ ọrụ inye signature ikpeazụ. Nke a na-emepụta oke doro anya n’etiti kọmputa, nke na-ejikwa ọnọdụ e-akpa ego ma na-ewulite Azụmahia, na ngwaọrụ hardware, nke na-echebe ikike achọrọ iji nye mmefu ego ikike.

Otú ọ dị, njikọta ugbu a bụ naanị Ironwood. Ọ naghị egosipụta ma ọ bụ jikwaa ego Orchard ma ọ bụ Sapling site na Ledger Wallet Desktop. Nke a dị mkpa n’akụkọ ihe mere eme n’ihi na mmepe Zcash mbụ nke Ledger jikọtara na nkwado e chebere echebe Orchard tupu mmejuputa ahụ agaa n’ihu protocol Ironwood.

Mgbanwe ahụ na-egosikwa mkpa ndakọrịta igodo na akaụntụ. Ledger nwere ngwa “Zcash Shielded” ochie nke Zondax mepụtara ma njikọta dịka YWallet na zkool ji arụ ọrụ, nke a na-ewepụ ugbu a. N’ihi na ngwa ochie ahụ jiri usoro mmepụta dị iche maka akaụntụ e chebere echebe, a pụghị iji ngwa Ledger ọhụrụ megharịa akaụntụ Sapling ya naanị.

Ya mere, njikọta a na-egosi ma ọganihu ma mmachi ndị fọdụrụ na nkwado hardware wallet e chebere echebe. Ledger nwere ike ugbu a inye ahụmịhe Ironwood zuru ezu nke software enyemaka na-ejikwa mmekọrịta e-akpa ego na nkwadebe Azụmahia, ebe ngwaọrụ hardware ka bụ oke nchekwa maka imefu ego.

N’otu oge ahụ, ndakọrịta akaụntụ n’akụkọ ihe mere eme, nkwado naanị maka pool e chebere echebe ụfọdụ, mmachi nha Azụmahia hardware, mmachi platform, na mmachi mgbakọ na firmware metụtara ịkọwa na inyocha Azụmahia e chebere echebe na-egosi na nkwado hardware wallet maka Zcash adabereghị naanị n’ikike protocol, kama ọ daberekwa na nhazi na akụrụngwa nke mmejuputa ọ bụla n’otu n’otu.

### Ihe mere njikọta ahụ ji dị mkpa

Inwe cryptography na koodu mbinye aka ezughị. Njikọta hardware wallet abụghị naanị nsogbu cryptography; ọ bụkwa nsogbu nkesa na platformu a na-apụghị izere ezere. Dịkarịa ala, ọ gụnyere:

- Nkwado/nkesa ngwa — ma ụlọ ahịa ngwa gọọmentị nke onye nrụpụta hardware ga-anabata ma kesaa ngwa ahụ
- Nche firmware/ngwa — ma ngwa ahụ mezuru ihe nche nke onye nrụpụta chọrọ maka ijikwa ihe Igodo ego
- Protocols nkwurịta okwu — otu e-akpa ego onye mmekọ na ngwaọrụ si agbanweta data Azụmahia n'ezie
- Ndakọrịta na e-akpa ego — ma ụdị data na usoro ngwa mbinye aka dakọtara na ihe e-akpa ego onye mmekọ tụrụ anya ya
- Pool ndị e chebere echebe akwadoro — nke n'ime Sapling, Orchard, (na ugbu a Ironwood) ngwa ahụ nwere ike ịbịanye aka maka ha n'ezie

Ngwa Ywallet Ledger nwe ya mezuru ihe cryptography na ihe owuwu chọrọ; ọ pụrụ ịbịanye aka na Azụmahia Zcash nke e chebere echebe, mana anabataghị ya n'ime ụlọ ahịa ngwa gọọmentị nke Ledger.

## 2. Keystone

**Gịnị bụ Keystone?**

Keystone bụ hardware wallet nwere air gap. Ngwaọrụ mbinye aka ya enweghị njikọ kpọmkwem site na waya ma ọ bụ ikuku na ngwa onye mmekọ n'oge usoro mbinye aka n'onwe ya; kama nke ahụ, ha na-ekwurịta okwu site na QR codes. A jikọtara ya ebe a na Zashi (nke e megharịrị aha ya ugbu a bụrụ Zodl), e-akpa ego onye mmekọ nke ala Zcash, njikọta ahụ na-eme ka ụkpụrụ ikewapụ Igodo ego doo anya nke ukwuu ma dị mfe ịchọpụta.

**Usoro Keystone/Zashi**

Njikọta Keystone nke Zcash na Zashi na-eme ka nkewa a doo anya nke ukwuu. Mgbe ị jikọtara Keystone na Zashi, a na-ebufe Unified Full Viewing Key site na Keystone gaa na Zashi.

**Gịnị ka Zashi nwere ike ime na UFVK?**

Zashi nwere ike iji ya:

- chọpụta Azụmahia ndị e chebere echebe na-abata
- kpebie nguzozi
- nweta ozi gbasara ọrụ nke e chebere echebe

Mana ọ pụghị imefu ego ahụ nanị n'ihi na o nwere UFVK.

UFVK bụ “ikike nlele” nke na-enye ohere ịmata nguzozi na Azụmahia, mana anaghị enye ohere imefu.

### Gịnị na-anọgide n'ime Keystone?

Seed/ikike imefu na-anọgide na Keystone. Zashi anaghị anata seed, Igodo ego, ma ọ bụ spend authorization private key. Ọ na-anata UFVK, ozi Adreesị akpa ego, na ozi dị mkpa maka ọrụ nlele naanị.

Ọnụ, ọkara abụọ a na-emezu nkọwa ahụ. Ihe na-ahapụ ngwaọrụ ahụ, dịka UFVK, adreesị, na ozi watch only, zuru Zashi iji nyochaa akaụntụ ahụ, mana ọ dịghị ihe na-ahapụ ngwaọrụ ahụ zuru iji bugharịa ego site n'ime ya.

Nke a bụ ihe mere njikọta Zashi na Keystone ji nwee ike inye nchekwa oyi nke e chebere echebe. Ngwa e-akpa ego ahụ nwere ike nyochaa akaụntụ na-enweghị inwe ikike achọrọ iji si na ya mefu. E wepụtara njikọta ahụ kpọmkwem iji kwado nchekwa oyi nke ZEC e chebere echebe.

## 3. Hito

Hito na-agbaso ụdị ikewapụ Igodo ego nke yiri nke Keystone, mana mmejuputa ya dị ọhụrụ. Kama ijikọta ya na e-akpa ego onye mmekọ dị adị dịka Zashi, Hito ji e-akpa ego Android nke ya abịa, a na-edekwa usoro onboarding n'etiti ngwaọrụ hardware ahụ na ngwa Android ahụ n'ime ihe ịga nke ọma mmepe Hito.

**Onboarding: ihe ngwa Hito na-anata**

E-akpa ego Android na-eji:

- Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK)
- Unified Address
- mkpịsịaka seed ZIP-32
- ozi network

mgbe a na-eme onboarding akaụntụ hardware.

Rịba ama na ngwa Android anaghị anata seed hardware wallet ma ọ bụ Igodo ego nzuzo.

![Hito onboarding](/content-images/hito-onboarding.webp)

**Gịnị ka ngwa Hito na-enweta?**

Ozi onboarding edere gụnyere:

- UFVK
- Unified Address
- mkpịsịaka seed ZIP-32
- Network

UFVK na-enye e-akpa ego Android ozi ọ chọrọ iji rụọ akaụntụ ahụ dịka e-akpa ego nwere ike ilele n'enwetaghị seed. Mkpịsịaka seed bụ njirimara ogologo ya kwụsiri ike e si na seed nweta, a na-eji ya amata akaụntụ nke ọ bụla; ọ bụghị seed n'onwe ya, ọ naghị enyekwa ikike imefu ọ bụla, nke mere ọnụnọ ya anaghị emegide nkwupụta “anaghị anata seed” dị n'elu.

**Gịnị na-anọgide na Hito?**

Seed n'ezie, Igodo ego nzuzo, na ikike mbinye aka na-anọgide na ngwaọrụ hardware. Hito na-emezi mbinye aka offline.

Ihe ịga nke ọma Hito ugbu a na-ekwu na usoro dum nke imepụta, mbupụ, mbinye aka offline, na mbubata na-arụ ọrụ, ma nwee nnyefe BLE na mbupụ BBQr QR nke PCZT e binyere aka.

Usoro ahụ kwesịrị ịkpọ aha n'ụzọ doro anya, ebe ọ bụ ihe atụ pụtara ìhè nke okirikiri zuru ezu kpuchiri na mbụ na akụkọ a: e-akpa ego Android na-emepụta PCZT na-enweghị signature, bupu ya gaa Hito, Hito binye aka offline site n'ihe Igodo ego ya kewapụrụ, a na-ebubatakwa PCZT e binyere aka n'ime e-akpa ego Android.

N'oge ọ bụla, seed ma ọ bụ Igodo ego anaghị agafe oke ahụ n'akụkụ ọ bụla.

## Orchard ruo Ironwood: Ntolite nke Nkwado hardware wallet

Orchard bụ pool nke e chebere echebe [shielded] n'ime protocol Zcash nke na-enyere ndị ọrụ ịchekwa ma mee Azụmahia [transaction] ZEC n'ịdị Nzuzo [privacy] ka elu. E webatara ya na Network Upgrade 5 (NU5), Orchard na-eji usoro proof [proof] Halo 2 na nhazi cryptographic emegharịrị iji kwado Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe na-enweghị ikpughe ozi Azụmahia dị mkpa dịka onye zitere, onye nnata, ma ọ bụ ego ole.

Ironwood bụ pool nke e chebere echebe [shielded] ewebatara na network upgrade NU6.3 nke Zcash dịka onye nọchiri pool Orchard. Ọ na-eji protocol nzuzo [cryptographic protocol] Orchard, ma na-eweta mgbanwe e mere iji meziwanye nchekwa ogologo oge nke ego nke e chebere echebe, gụnyere ike iweghachi megide quantum [quantum recoverability] na nkwa siri ike gbasara nguzosi ike n'ezi ihe nke ọkọnọ [supply integrity].

Na mmalite, ọ dị mkpa ịkọwa na Ironwood ejighị usoro cryptographic ọhụrụ kpamkpam dochie protocol Orchard. E webatara Ironwood dị ka pool ọhụrụ nke e chebere echebe, nke e wuru site na protocol Orchard dị adị. Nke a pụtara na ha nwere otu nhazi Action, otu usoro proof Halo 2, na otu nhazi note na igodo.

Ironwood nwere osisi note commitment nke ya, set nullifier, anchor, na pool uru nke ya, ya mere na ọkwa consensus, ọ bụ pool dị iche n'ezie. Mana n'akụkụ cryptographic, ọ bụ ndọtị nke Orchard karịa iwughachi ya.

E mere ya otu a iji:

1. **Ịdị mma nke supply.** Na Mee 29, 2026, onye nyocha nchekwa Taylor Hornby kpughere nzuzo ntụpọ soundness dị oke egwu na sistemụ proof zero-knowledge nke Orchard, nke nwere ike ikwe ka e mepụta ZEC adịgboroja na-enweghị ịhapụ akara on-chain. N'ihi na data Azụmahia nke Orchard bụ nke nzuzo, ọ dịghị onye nwere ike ịchọpụta n'onwe ya na e mepụtaghị ZEC adịgboroja tupu ndozi ahụ. Ịmechi pool ochie ahụ ma malite nke ọhụrụ na efu na-edozi nsogbu ahụ n'ụzọ ndozi Orchard n'ime ya agaghị enwe ike.
2. **Hụ na mgbake Quantum dị.** E mere Ironwood ka ego ya nwee ike isonye na usoro mgbake n'ọdịnihu ma ọ bụrụ na a mebie echiche discrete log dị n'okpuru ya.
3. **Belata mgbanwe mmejuputa.** Kama ịmepụta protocol nke e chebere echebe ọhụrụ kpamkpam, e jirila nhazi Orchard a nwalere eme ihe ọzọ. Nke a pụtara na achọrọ ide obere koodu Azụmahia ọhụrụ, proof, e-akpa ego [wallet], na hardware wallet site na mmalite.

### Usoro oge ọha

- Mee 29: A na-ekpughe adịghị ike Orchard.
- Juun: A machibidoro Azụmahia Orchard nwa oge mgbe a na-ewepụta ndozi mbụ site na NU6.2. Na Juun 12, onye mmepụta isi lekwasịrị Julaị 21 anya maka upgrade zuru oke; emesịa ọ gafeere otu izu. E welitere nchegbu obodo na ndị nwe Keystone ga-abụ ndị ikpeazụ ịkwaga, onye mmepụta isi wee zaa na Keystone ga-akwado Ironwood site n'ụbọchị mbụ, na firmware a tụrụ anya maka Julaị 3, na-echere constants signing emechara na mgbanwe API Orchard sitere na core Zcash.
- Mmalite Julaị: NU6.3 rutere testnet; Zebra 6.0.0 pụtara na Julaị 10.
- Julaị 27–28: Ọrụ signing Ironwood PCZT v2 nke Ledger jikọtara na branch integration. Ironwood (NU6.3) malitere na Julaị 28 na block 3,428,143, mechie pool mbụ Orchard n'azụ turnstile nke na-agbachi ego ole ZEC nwere ike isi na Orchard pụta n'ego etinyere n'ime ya n'ụzọ ziri ezi, ma webata ụdị Azụmahia ọhụrụ.

Zebra ghọrọ node a chọrọ, n'ihi na zcashd anaghị akwado iwu consensus ọhụrụ. Adreesị akpa ego [address] anaghị agbanwe n'ihi na Ironwood ji nhazi receiver nke Orchard eme ihe ọzọ, ya mere e-akpa ego na-eduga Adreesị akpa ego ndị dị adị n'ime pool ọhụrụ na-akpaghị aka. Ihe e-akpa ego chọrọ bụ ọrụ ịkwaga ego, ebe ọ bụ na ndị nwe ha ga-emecha bugharịa ego Orchard n'ime Ironwood, a machibidokwala outputs ọhụrụ na-anata Orchard kpamkpam site na consensus.

### Ihe gbanwere na teknụzụ

Ụdị Azụmahia siri na version 5 gaa na version 6, ya mere otu Azụmahia nwere ike iburu component Ironwood n'akụkụ components Sapling/Orchard/Oha [transparent] dị adị. E gbatịrị PCZT n'otu usoro:

- Encoding siri na PCZT v1 gaa v2.
- Bundle Ironwood dị iche ugbu a nọ n'akụkụ nke Sapling na Orchard.
- A na-akwado Azụmahia v6 na sighash v6 ọhụrụ.
- Plaintexts note nwetara version ọhụrụ (V3).

Mgbanwe sighash bụ ihe kacha mkpa kpọmkwem maka hardware wallet. Na v6, a naghị etinye anchors Orchard na Ironwood n'ime signature hash, ha na-enye naanị authorizing data digest. Nke ahụ bụ mgbanwe firmware n'ezie, ọ bụghị naanị ịkwalite version. Device ga-emejuputa sighash ọhụrụ ahụ nke ọma ma gbakọọ ya ọzọ n'onwe ya kama ịtụkwasị obi n'ihe app ibe ya kwuru na ọ na-akwado.

Nkwekọrịta ikewapụ n'onwe ya agbanweghị: device ka na-eweghachi naanị signature nkwado imefu, ọ dịghị mgbe ọ na-eweghachi `alpha`, ọ dịghịkwa emepụta signature binding; host ka na-ahụkwa proof na ihe ọ bụla ọzọ.

Otú ọ dị, otu iwu signer gbanwere na v6: IO Finalizer na-abịanye dummy padding spends aka kama device, n'ihi na device enweghị ụzọ isi nyochaa ha megide data note n'ezie. Ọrụ Signer nke device na-awụfe ha kama ịrịọ ya ka ọ bịanye aka n'ihe ọ na-enweghị ike ịlele. Ugwo [fee] a na-egosi ugbu a na-agbakọ balances uru Orchard na Ironwood ọnụ, n'ihi na Azụmahia v6 nwere ike isi na pool ọ bụla mefuo ego n'otu Azụmahia ahụ.

Inweta mgbakọ pool ahụ nke ọma dị mkpa n'omume, ọ bụghị naanị n'echiche. Njehie mere n'oge na-adịbeghị anya n'ime e-akpa ego Zcash nke Brave n'onwe ya bụ ezigbo atụ: mgbakọ ugwo ya chere na inputs niile nke e chebere echebe sitere na pool Orchard ochie, nke butere actions emegharị na atụmatụ ugwo ezighi ezi mgbe ọ bụla Azụmahia metụtara Ironwood n'ezie. Nke ahụ bụ kpọmkwem ụdị njehie ụkpụrụ …gbakọọ ya ọzọ n'onwe gị, atụkwasịla host obi… n'azụ signing hardware wallet dị iji jide. E-akpa ego ibe ya mehie mgbakọ pool, naanị device na-enyocha mgbakọ nke ya n'adabereghị ga-ahụ nke ahụ tupu ịbịanye aka na ya.

### Ọnọdụ signer ọ bụla

**Ledger agbanweela ọnọdụ ahụ dum.** Na Septemba 23, 2026, Ledger wepụtara nkwado Zcash nke e chebere echebe nke obodo ozugbo n'ime Ledger Wallet Desktop; app ibe ọzọ adịghịzi mkpa. Igodo ego gị na-anọgide na device, kọmputa na-arụ mgbakọ Azụmahia, ị na-akwadokwa ihe niile na ihuenyo tupu ebinye ya aka. Ọ na-akwado naanị Ironwood, ọ bụghị pool Orchard ma ọ bụ Sapling ochie. Ọghọm ya bụ na app …Zcash Shielded… ochie nke Zondax wuru, nke chọrọ e-akpa ego ibe dịka Zkool iji rụọ ọrụ, na-akwụsị. Ledger na-ewepụ app ochie ahụ n'ebe ọ dị na Novemba 5, ya mere onye ọ bụla ka na-eji ya ga-ebuga ego ya tupu ụbọchị ahụ.

**Keystone nọ n'ọnọdụ ọma.** Firmware 3.0.2 gbakwunyere nkwado Ironwood zuru oke na Julaị, nke ahụ ka bụ ihe achọrọ ugbu a ma kwụsie ike. Ma version ahụ ma ọ bụ nke dị ọhụrụ ka achọrọ iji bịanye aka n'ihe ọ bụla.

**Hito agaala n'ihu karịa ọkwa testnet mbụ.** Ọ binyere aka ma nyefee Azụmahia mbụ ya n'ezie sitere na Orchard gaa Ironwood na hardware anụ ahụ (na mbụ na testnet). Mmelite ndị sochirinụ kwadoro flows mainnet (Ironwood→Ironwood, Ironwood→Transparent, na ndị ọzọ) na hardware mmepụta. Firmware kwụsiri ike na mwepụta sara mbara ka na-aga n'ihu.

Zondax na Zkool nọ ugbu a n'ọnọdụ mgbanwe. App Zondax bụ nke a na-ewepụ na Ledger. Zkool, nke dabere na app ahụ iji kparịta ụka na devices Ledger, egosibeghị ihe ga-eme ọrụ ahụ mgbe app ochie ahụ ga-apụ n'anya. O doghị anya ma ọ ga-anọgide bara uru maka ndị ọrụ Ledger n'ihu, ma ọ bụ ma ọdịnihu ya ga-abụkarị na devices ndị ọzọ.

Ịkwaga ego n'onwe ya bụ mmetụta kacha ukwuu. Ebe ego na-agafe pools bụ ihe ọha, e-akpa ego na-agbasa transfers n'ofe denominations ọkọlọtọ ka oge na-aga kama ịkwaga balance n'otu mkpụrụ nke a na-ahụ anya; nke a pụtara ọtụtụ Azụmahia a ga-abịanye aka n'oge ịkwaga ego, ọ bụkwa kpọmkwem ihe mere signing batch PCZT ji dị nnukwu mkpa maka hardware wallet.

Ọrụ firmware Ledger nwekwara ihe mgbochi hardware: imefu note n'ezie nwere ike iru iri puku syscall cryptographic n'otu command, na-akpalite watchdog device. Ndozi ahụ bufere mgbakọ field n'ime software ma chọọ ọnọdụ scratch e kerịta iji daba n'ime mgbochi stack nke Nano X.
