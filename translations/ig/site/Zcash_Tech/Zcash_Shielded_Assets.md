# Zcash Akụ nke e chebere echebe [Shielded Assets]

## Nchịkọta

Zcash Akụ nke e chebere echebe (ZSA) bụ mgbatị protocol a tụrụ aro nke ga-ekwe ka akụ **na-abụghị ZEC** — stablecoins, token Gọọmentị, ma ọ bụ akụ omenala ọ bụla — biri n'ime pool nke e chebere echebe nke Zcash, ebe a na-edobe onye zitere, onye natara, na ego ahụ na Nzuzo [privacy].

- **Ihe ọ bụ:** Akụ omenala n'ụdị ERC-20, mana nke e chebere echebe na ndabara.
- **Onye na-ewu ya:** [QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/), n'okpuru ikike sitere na Zcash Foundation, na mmekorita ya na Electric Coin Company.
- **Otu e si kọwaa ya:** [ZIP 226](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0226) (mbufe na ịkụ ọkụ) yana [ZIP 227](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0227) (iwepụta).
- **Ọnọdụ:** ọ dịbeghị ndụ na mainnet, ọ bụghịkwa akụkụ nke Network Upgrade 7 (NU7). [ZIP 259](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0259), nke depụtara ihe niile NU7 na-ebunye, anaghị agụnye ZIP 226 ma ọ bụ ZIP 227, ma edobeghị ụbọchị mmalite ZSA.
- **Ugwo:** a na-akwụ ha mgbe niile na ZEC, n'agbanyeghị akụ a na-ebufe.

## Nkọwa Isi

Zcash Akụ nke e chebere echebe (ZSA) bụ ndozi a tụrụ aro maka protocol Zcash nke ga-eme ka e nwee ike ịmepụta, ibufe, na ịkụ ọkụ nke akụ omenala na chain Zcash.

Ọ bụrụ na ị maara ụkpụrụ token [ERC-20](https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/standards/tokens/erc-20/) n'elu blockchain Ethereum, ZSA dị na Zcash dịka token ERC-20 dị na Ethereum.

Zcash Akụ nke e chebere echebe ga-eme ka e nwee ike ịmepụta token omenala na blockchain Zcash, si otú a kwe ka token ndị ọzọ na-abụghị [ZEC](/guides/using-zec-privately) rite uru na amaghi onye na Nzuzo nke Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe na blockchain Zcash.

Otu nnukwu ojiji ZSA pụrụ inwe bụ iwepụta stablecoins na protocol Zcash. Stablecoins bụ cryptocurrencies na-ejikọta uru ha na ego fiat, dịka US Dollar ma ọ bụ Euro. Ugbu a, ụfọdụ stablecoins a na-ekesa n'ọtụtụ ebe bụ token ERC-20 dịka [USDC](https://www.circle.com/en/usdc) na [Dai](https://docs.makerdao.com/).

Ojiji ọzọ ZSA pụrụ inwe bụ iwepụta token Gọọmentị. Dịka ọmụmaatụ, ZecHub (onye na-ebipụta wiki a) bụ Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO), ma nwee ike ịmepụta ma nye ndị otu ya ZSA maka ịtụ vootu maka atụmatụ na mkpebi Gọọmentị.

[QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/) na-emepụta ZSA, n'okpuru nnukwu ikike sitere na [Zcash Foundation](/zcash-organizations/zcash-foundation) na mmekorita ya na [Electric Coin Company](/zcash-organizations/electric-coin-company). Ebe ọ bụ na a ka na-emepụta ọrụ a nke ọma, a na-ebipụta mmelite na [eriokwu a](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-update-zcash-shielded-assets-monthly-updates/41153) nke forum Zcash. Ngwa ikike ZSA [](https://zcashgrants.org/gallery/25215916-53ea-4041-a3b2-6d00c487917d/33106640/) nke QEDIT dị na weebụsaịtị ikike Zcash Foundation.

## Anya / Ntụnyere

### Akwụkwọ ozi e mechiri emechi

Chee Azụmahia Zcash nke e chebere echebe n'echiche dịka akwụkwọ ozi dị larịị, e mechiri emechi, a tụbara n'ime igbe ozi ọha. Onye ọ bụla pụrụ ịhụ na e zitere akwụkwọ ozi. Ọ dịghị onye pụrụ ịhụ onye zitere ya, onye nakọtara ya, ma ọ bụ ihe dị n'ime ya — akwụkwọ ozi ọ bụla yikwara nke ọ bụla ọzọ.

Taa, akwụkwọ ozi n'elu network Zcash nwere ike ibu naanị otu ihe: ZEC.

ZSA anaghị agbanwe akwụkwọ ozi ahụ. Ọ na-agbanwe **ihe a na-ekwe ka ọ dị n'ime ya**. Mgbe ZSA gasịrị, otu akwụkwọ ozi ahụ e mechiri emechi nwere ike ibu stablecoin, token Gọọmentị DAO, ma ọ bụ isi ihe iguzosi ike n'ihe nke ụlọọrụ — ma n'èzí, ọ ga-adị ka akwụkwọ ozi ọ bụla ọzọ na network ahụ.

Otu nkọwa kwesịrị ka e cheta: **a na-akwụ ugwo nzipu ozi mgbe niile na ZEC**, n'agbanyeghị ihe dị n'ime akwụkwọ ozi ahụ.

### Ihe onye na-ekiri n'èzí pụrụ ịhụ

| Onye na-ekiri pụrụ ịhụ... | ERC-20 na Ethereum | ZSA na Zcash |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onye zitere ya | Ọha | Nke e chebere echebe |
| Onye natara ya | Ọha | Nke e chebere echebe |
| Ego ole e bugara | Ọha | Nke e chebere echebe |
| Ọnụ ego onye ọ bụla | Ọha | Nke e chebere echebe |
| Mkpokọta ọkọnọ nke akụ ahụ | Ọha | **Ọha — ụma** |
| Ego a na-akwụ ugwo na ya | ETH | ZEC |

### Ihe mere ahịrị ọkọnọ ahụ abụghị njehie

Ahịrị abụọ ikpeazụ nke tebụl ahụ bụ ebe ZSA na-adọrọ mmasị.

ZIP 227 na-edobe **iwepụta n'Oha** ụma, ka e wee nwee ike soro ọkọnọ na-ekesa nke akụ ọ bụla na on-chain. Ihe onye ọ bụla ji na Kwụọ ụhwọ nke onye ọ bụla na-anọgide na Nzuzo; ọnụ ọgụgụ token niile dị adịghị.

Maka onye na-ewepụta stablecoin, nchikota ahụ bụ isi ihe karịa nkwekọrịta. Enwere ike inyocha nchekwa megide ọkọnọ a pụrụ ikwenye n'ihu ọha, ebe ndị mmadụ na-eji token ahụ n'ezie na-edobe ego ha na Kwụọ ụhwọ ha n'onwe ha.

### Otu akụ, otu njirimara

Akụ ọ bụla na-enweta **Njirimara Akụ** pụrụ iche, nke sitere na igodo iwepụta nke onye na-ewepụta ya tinyere nkọwa ederede banyere akụ ahụ. Ndị na-ewepụta abụọ dị iche apụghị ịmepụta otu njirimara ahụ, ma ịmepụta token ma ọ bụ ịgbanwe akụ chọrọ nkwenye cryptographic sitere n'aka onye wepụtara ya. N'okwu akwụkwọ ozi: onye ọ bụla nwere ike izipu akwụkwọ ozi, mana naanị ụlọ ọrụ na-emepụta ihe nwere otu akụ nwere ike ibipụta ihe ọzọ n'ime ya.

### Atụmatụ Ndozi Zcash (ZIPs)

- [ZIP 226](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0226): Mbufe na ịkụ ọkụ nke Akụ Zcash nke e chebere echebe
- [ZIP 227](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0227): Iwepụta Akụ Zcash nke e chebere echebe
- [ZIP 230](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0230): Usoro Azụmahia mbipụta 6

> **Rịba ama banyere ZIP 230:** Ewepụla ZIP 230 kemgbe ahụ, a gaghịkwa ebunye ya. Ugbu a, [ZIP 229](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0229) na-akọwa Azụmahia mbipụta 6. Lee ọkwa dị n'elu peeji [ZIP 230](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0230).

ZIP 226 na-akọwa protocol OrchardZSA — mgbatị protocol Orchard nke na-ebu mbugharị na ịkụ ọkụ nke akụ omenala. ZIP 227 na-akọwa otu e si emepụta akụ ndị ahụ na mbụ, a ga-etinyekwa ya naanị yana ZIP 226.

### Atụmatụ Ikike ZSA

Ndị otu [QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/) wepụtara atụmatụ ZSA maka Akụ nke e chebere echebe (ZSA/UDA) iji wuo akụ nke e chebere echebe n'ozuzu na blockchain Zcash. A na-akpọkarị ndị a User Defined Assets (UDA) ma ọ bụ Akụ Zcash nke e chebere echebe (ZSA).

Site n'atụmatụ a, ndị otu nọ na [QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/) na-ezube iweta DeFi n'ime gburugburu Zcash ma, n'otu oge ahụ, mee ka e jiri teknụzụ Nzuzo kacha mma n'ime gburugburu DeFi dị ugbu a. N'ime nyocha ntuli aka, ndị otu jụrụ, ndị obodo zaghakwara na [akụ nke e chebere echebe n'ozuzu (ZSA/UDA) bụ atụmatụ a na-arịọkarị ugbu a](https://twitter.com/BenarrochDaniel/status/1428327864034791429).

Atụmatụ ndị a na-agbaso ụkpụrụ nkà na ụzụ nke nkọwapụta Atụmatụ Ndozi [Zcash (ZIP)](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0000), a kọwakwara ha na ZIP 226 & ZIP 227.

1. [ZIP 226](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0226): Mbufe na ịkụ ọkụ nke Akụ Zcash nke e chebere echebe
2. [ZIP 227](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0227): Iwepụta Akụ Zcash nke e chebere echebe

## Mmetụta Bara Uru

**Ọ bụrụ na ị na-ejide ma ọ bụ na-eji ZEC**

- A kọwara ZSA dịka mgbatị nke Orchard ("OrchardZSA"), yabụ ha ga-ekekọrịta usoro nke e chebere echebe ZEC jirila. E-akpa ego gị ga-achọ nkwado ZSA kpọmkwem tupu o nwee ike ijide ma ọ bụ ziga ha.
- Ị ga-achọ ịnwe ụfọdụ ZEC n'aka mgbe niile. A na-akwụ ugwo maka iwepụta na ibufe ZSA na ZEC, ọ bụghị n'ime akụ ahụ n'onwe ya.
- Ọ dịghị ihe na-agbanwe gbasara Azụmahia ZEC gị dị ugbu a.

**Ọ bụrụ na ị bụ onye nwere ike iwepụta ya — stablecoin, DAO, ụlọọrụ**

- Iwepụta akụ chọrọ nkwenye cryptographic ejikọtara na igodo iwepụta, yabụ naanị gị nwere ike ịmepụta token ma ọ bụ gbanwee njirimara nke akụ gị.
- Ọkọnọ akụ gị na-ekesa bụ nke a pụrụ inyocha n'ihu ọha, ebe ego na mbugharị ndị ọrụ gị abụghị. Maka onye na-ewepụta n'okpuru iwu, nke a na-abụkarị kpọmkwem nchikota achọrọ.
- Otu Azụmahia iwepụta nwere ike ịmepụta ihe karịrị otu akụ n'otu oge.

**Maka gburugburu ebe obibi**

- N'ihi na a na-egosi ugwo ZSA ọ bụla na ZEC, ọrụ n'ime akụ ọ bụla n'ọdịnihu e wepụtara na Zcash na-emepụta ọchịchọ maka ZEC n'onwe ya.
