### Nzọụkwụ 1: Mepụta E-akpa ego Pụrụ Iche

- Jiri e-akpa ego naanị maka ọrụ nwere mmetụta
- Ejikọtala ya na Azụmahia nke onwe gị

### Nzọụkwụ 2: Jiri Naanị Adreesị akpa ego nke e chebere echebe

- Na-eme Azụmahia mgbe niile site na iji z-addresses
- Zere Azụmahia Oha kpamkpam


### Nzọụkwụ 3: Chebe Kwụọ ụhwọ Maka Ebe Isi Mmalite

Ọ bụrụ na ị na-akwụ ebe isi mmalite:
- Jiri Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe (z → z)
- Zere usoro oge a pụrụ ibu amụma
- Ejikwaghachi Adreesị akpa ego


### Nzọụkwụ 4: Kpachara Anya na Memo

- ETINYELA ozi nwere mmetụta na memo
- Jiri ntụaka na-enweghị mmetụta ma ọ bụ nke e dere n'uzo nzuzo ma ọ bụrụ na ọ dị mkpa


### Nzọụkwụ 5: Debe Nchekwa Ọrụ

- Zere ijikọ e-akpa ego na njirimara gị
- Ekwenyela Azụmahia n'ihu ọha
- Jiri ọwa dị iche iche maka nkwukọrịta
