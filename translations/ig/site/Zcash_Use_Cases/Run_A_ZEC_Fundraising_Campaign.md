## Nchịkọta nkenke (TL;DR)

- Nye mkpọsa ahụ akaụntụ e-akpa ego (wallet) nke ya na Adreesị akpa ego (address) nke e chebere echebe (shielded), ka ọ ghara ịgwakọta ya na ego ndị ọzọ
- Kesaa njikọ arịrịọ Kwụọ ụhwọ (payment) ma ọ bụ koodu QR nwere ego a tụrụ aro yana memo e dejupụtalarị
- Gosi ọganihu n'ekpugheghị ndị nyere onyinye: bipụta mkpokọta ego ahụ n'onwe gị, ma ọ bụ nye otu onye nyocha a tụkwasịrị obi viewing key
- viewing key na-ekpuchi akaụntụ ahụ dum, ọ bụghị naanị otu Adreesị akpa ego, nke a bụ ihe kpatara mkpọsa ahụ ji nwee akaụntụ nke ya
- Mechie mkpọsa ahụ n'ụbọchị i kwere nkwa ya, ma bipụta otú e si jiri ego ahụ mee ihe

## <img src="/content-images/user-svgrepo-com-21adf62b7c.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="user icon"/> Onye ka nke a dịịrị?

- Otu obodo, ọrụ ngwanrọ mmeghe (open-source) na ndị okike na-achịkọta ego maka otu ebumnobi
- Ụlọ ọrụ anaghị akpata uru na-eme arịrịọ njedebe afọ ma ọ bụ nke mberede na ZEC
- Onye ọ bụla chọrọ ka ndị na-enye onyinye tụkwasị mkpọsa ahụ obi n'emeghị ka onyinye ọ bụla pụta ìhè n'ihu ọha

Ọ bụrụ na ị bụ ụlọ ọrụ anaghị akpata uru nọ na US, buru ụzọ gụọ [Nabata Onyinye Nzuzo dịka Ụlọ Ọrụ Anaghị Akpata Uru nke US](/zcash-use-cases/accept-private-donations-as-a-us-nonprofit) maka akwụkwọ nnata na fọm IRS.

## <img src="/content-images/warning-error-svgrepo-com-b7ea8a50da.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="warning icon"/> Nsogbu ahụ

Ọtụtụ mkpọsa nchịkọta ego crypto na-eji Adreesị akpa ego Oha (transparent) ka ndị na-enye onyinye nwee ike ịhụ ka mkpokọta ego ahụ si eto. Nke a na-egosikwa onyinye ọ bụla, ego ole ọ bụ, mgbe ọ bịarutere na ebe ego ahụ na-aga ọzọ. Ndị na-enye onyinye ha n'ụzọ nzuzo n'ebumnobi na-atụfu Nzuzo (privacy) ahụ, onye ọ bụla nwekwara ike ịgbaso ego ahụ mgbe mkpọsa ahụ gachara.

Mkpọsa nke zuru ezu na Nzuzo nwere nsogbu nke ọzọ: ndị na-enye onyinye enweghị ike ịhụ ma e ruru ebumnobi ahụ ma ọ bụ otú e si mefuo ego ahụ.

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-lock-2f8e221321.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="lock icon"/> Gịnị mere Zcash?

Site na ZEC nke e chebere echebe, onyinye ọ bụla na-anọgide na Nzuzo on-chain, gị onwe gị na-ekpebi ihe ị ga-egosi: mkpokọta ego, ogwe ọganihu, ma ọ bụ ohere naanị ịgụ maka onye nyocha. Ndị na-enye onyinye nwere ike itinye ozi na [memo](/using-zcash/memos), ị nwekwara ike ikele ha n'enweghị onye ọzọ hụrụ onye nyere onyinye.

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Ntuziaka nzọụkwụ na nzọụkwụ

### Nzọụkwụ 1: Tọọ ebumnobi na iwu

Dee ihe ndị a tupu ị malite:
- Ego ole ị na-achịkọta na ihe ọ ga-akwụ ụgwọ ya
- Ụbọchị njedebe
- Ihe ga-eme ma ọ bụrụ na ị chịkọtara karịa ma ọ bụ erughị ebumnobi ahụ
- Onye na-achịkwa ego ahụ, na ma ọ dị mkpa ka ihe karịrị otu mmadụ kwado imefu ego (lee [Ijikwa Private Community Treasury](/zcash-use-cases/private-community-treasury))

Bipụta ihe ndị a n'ibe mkpọsa ahụ. Ndị na-enye onyinye na-atụkwasị obi na mkpọsa nke na-ekwupụta site na mbido ihe ọ ga-eme.

### Nzọụkwụ 2: Mepụta akaụntụ dị iche maka mkpọsa ahụ

Mepụta akaụntụ ọhụrụ n'ime e-akpa ego gị, nke a ga-eji naanị maka mkpọsa a, ma detuo Adreesị akpa ego nke e chebere echebe site na ya. Idebe mkpọsa ahụ iche na-eme ka ọ dị mfe ịgụ mkpokọta ego, ọ ga-adịkwa mkpa ma emechaa ma ọ bụrụ na ị kesaa viewing key, n'ihi na viewing key na-egosi ihe niile na-eme n'akaụntụ ahụ dum ([Viewing Keys](/zcash-tech/viewing-keys)).

### Nzọụkwụ 3: Mepụta njikọ arịrịọ Kwụọ ụhwọ na koodu QR

[URI arịrịọ Kwụọ ụhwọ](/using-zcash/payment-request-uris) bụ njikọ `zcash:` nke na-ejupụta Adreesị akpa ego, ego a tụrụ aro na memo maka onye na-enye onyinye. Jiri [ngwá ọrụ na-emepụta arịrịọ Kwụọ ụhwọ](/tools) nke ZecHub mepụta otu, wee kesaa ya dịka njikọ na koodu QR.

Memo dịka "Aha mkpọsa, zaghachi na: email gị ma ọ bụ aha njirimara gị" na-enyere ndị na-enye onyinye chọrọ ekele ma ọ bụ akwụkwọ nnata aka. Kwuo n'ibe ahụ na memo abụghị iwu.

### Nzọụkwụ 4: Malite ibe mkpọsa ahụ

Tinye n'ibe ahụ:
- Ebumnobi, ụbọchị njedebe na iwu sitere na Nzọụkwụ 1
- Adreesị akpa ego nke e chebere echebe, koodu QR na njikọ ahụ
- Otú e si enye onyinye site na e-akpa ego nke e chebere echebe, na ebe a ga-enweta otu
- Otú ị ga-esi kọọ ọganihu (Nzọụkwụ 5)

Kpachie Adreesị akpa ego ahụ n'otu ebe ma gwa ndị na-enye onyinye ka ha detuo ya naanị site n'ebe ahụ.

### Nzọụkwụ 5: Kọọ ọganihu n'ekpugheghị ndị nyere onyinye

Họrọ otu ma kwuo nke ị họọrọ n'ibe ahụ:
- **Bipụta mkpokọta ego ahụ n'onwe gị**, dịka ọmụmaatụ otu ugboro n'izu, site n'ego dị n'akaụntụ mkpọsa ahụ n'ime e-akpa ego gị
- **Nye otu onye nyocha a tụkwasịrị obi viewing key**, dịka onye otu bọọdụ ma ọ bụ onye obodo ndị na-enye onyinye maara, onye ga-akwado mkpokọta ego ahụ n'ihu ọha. Incoming viewing key na-egosi onyinye a natara; full viewing key na-egosikwa mmefu ([Viewing Keys](/zcash-tech/viewing-keys))

Ebipụtala viewing key n'ibe mkpọsa ahụ. Onye ọ bụla nwere ya nwere ike ịhụ ego na memo nke onyinye ọ bụla, gụnyere nkọwa kọntaktị nke ndị nyere onyinye.

### Nzọụkwụ 6: Kelee ndị nyere onyinye

Zaghachi ndị nyere onyinye hapụrụ kọntaktị na memo. Ebipụtala aha onye nyere onyinye ma ọ bụ ego o nyere n'ajụghị ya ajụjụ mbụ.

### Nzọụkwụ 7: Mechie ma kọọ akụkọ

N'ụbọchị njedebe:
- Kwuo na e mechiela mkpọsa ahụ, ma wepụ ma ọ bụ melite Adreesị akpa ego na koodu QR
- Bipụta mkpokọta ego ikpeazụ na ihe ego ahụ kwụrụ ụgwọ ya
- Ọ bụrụ na ị kesara viewing key, rịọ onye nyocha ahụ ka o kwado mkpokọta ego ikpeazụ na mmefu ya

[Nkpughe Kwụọ ụhwọ](/zcash-tech/payment-disclosures) nwere ike n'otu ụbọchị ime ka i gosipụta otu Kwụọ ụhwọ n'enweghị viewing key, mana ụkpụrụ ahụ ka bụ ndepụta, ya mere adaberela na ya ugbu a.
