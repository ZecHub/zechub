- Mepụta Adreesị akpa ego nke e chebere echebe (z)

### Nzọụkwụ 2: Gosipụta Ntuziaka Kwụọ ụhwọ

- Gosipụta z-adreesị gị

### Nzọụkwụ 4: Kwenye Kwụọ ụhwọ

### Nzọụkwụ 5: Debe Omume Nzuzo
