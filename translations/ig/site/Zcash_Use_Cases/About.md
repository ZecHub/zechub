# Jiri Zcash mee ihe n'ụwa n'ezie

Zcash abụghị naanị maka nzuzo n'echiche - ọ bụ gbasara **nnwere onwe ego kwa ụbọchị**.

Nkebi a na-egosi gị **otu esi eji Zcash eme ihe n'ọnọdụ ndụ n'ezie**, yana ntuziaka nzọụkwụ site na nzọụkwụ na omume kacha mma.

## Ihe Ị Ga-amụta

- Otu esi echebe nzuzo ego gị
- Otu esi zere mmejọ ndị a na-emekarị nke na-ekpughe data gị
- Otu esi etinye Zcash n'ọrụ n'ọnọdụ ụwa n'ezie

## Otu esi eji ntuziaka ndị a

Akwụkwọ egwuregwu ọ bụla bụ:
- Obere (nkeji 5-7)
- Nzọụkwụ site na nzọụkwụ
- Lekwasị anya n'ụwa n'ezie 


Ọ bụrụ na ị bụ onye ọhụrụ, bido ebe a: [Kedu ihe bụ Zcash](/start-here/what-is-zec-and-zcash)


##  Ụzọ akwadoro

Soro njem nzọụkwụ a iji mara ojiji Zcash n'ezie:

###  [Nara onyinye n'onwe gị](/zcash-use-cases/receive-donations-privately)
Mụta otu esi anabata ego n'ekpugheghị njirimara gị ma ọ bụ akụkọ ego gị.


###  [Zipu Ego Na-enweghị Njikọta Njirimara](/zcash-use-cases/send-money-without-linking-identity) 
Zere ikpughe obere akpa ego gị, njirimara gị, ma ọ bụ eserese azụmahịa gị mgbe ị na-eziga ego.


###  [Ntọala Nzuzo nke Onye Nwe Onwe Ya](/zcash-use-cases/freelance-privacy-setup)
Nweta ụgwọ na Zcash ka ị na-edebe ndị ahịa gị na ego gị n'onwe gị.

###  [Gbaa Akụ Obodo nkeonwe](/zcash-use-cases/private-community-treasury)
Jiri adreesị echekwara iji jide ego ekekọrịtara ma belata visibiliti nke nguzozi na azụmahịa

###  [Nabata onyinye nkeonwe dị ka otu ndị na-abụghị ndị ọrụ ebere nke US](/zcash-use-cases/accept-private-donations-as-a-us-nonprofit)

###  [Ebee ka a ga-enye onyinye ZEC](/zcash-use-cases/where-to-donate-zec)
Chọta ndị na-anaghị akwụ ụgwọ nke na-anabata ZEC, na onye n'ime ha na-anabata onyinye nchekwa

###  [Ntọala Nzuzo Onye Nta Akụkọ](/zcash-use-cases/journalist-privacy-setup)
