> Ibe a bụ ozi izugbe, ọ bụghị ndụmọdụ iwu ma ọ bụ ụtụ isi. Iwu ụtụ isi US na-agbanwe, yabụ lelee ọnọdụ gị na onye ndụmọdụ ụtụ isi. Iwu ọ bụla dị n'okpuru a jikọtara na isi mmalite IRS ya, nke enyochara n'Ọktoba 2026 megide Publication 526 (maka nkwụghachi ụtụ isi 2025) na ibe IRS gbasara iwu ụtụ isi 2025.

## Na nkenke

- IRS na-ewere ZEC dị ka akụ, yabụ onyinye ZEC bụ onyinye na-abụghị ego nkịtị
- Ọ bụrụ na i jidere ZEC ihe karịrị otu afọ, ị nwere ike n'ozuzu wepụ uru ahịa ziri ezi ya ma ghara ịkwụ ụtụ isi uru isi obodo n'ihi mmụba ya
- Ọ bụrụ na i jidere ya otu afọ ma ọ bụ ihe na-erughị ya, mwepụ gị bụ nke pere mpe n'etiti ihe ị kwụrụ maka ya na uru ahịa ya
- Maka onyinye ruru $250 ma ọ bụ karịa, ị chọrọ nkwenye edere ede sitere n'aka ọrụ ebere ahụ
- Ọ bụrụ karịa $500, ị ga-etinye Form 8283. Ọ bụrụ karịa $5,000, ị ga-achọkwa ntule uru sitere n'aka ọkachamara ruru eru
- Ị ga-enweta mwepụ ahụ naanị ma ọ bụrụ na ị na-edepụta mwepụ gị n'otu n'otu. Mwepụ ọhụrụ maka ndị na-adịghị edepụta mwepụ n'otu n'otu, nke malitere na 2026, na-ekpuchi naanị onyinye ego nkịtị, ọ bụghị ZEC

## <img src="/content-images/user-svgrepo-com-21adf62b7c.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="user icon"/> Ònye ka nke a bụ maka ya?

- Ndị na-akwụ ụtụ isi US chọrọ inye ọrụ ebere ZEC
- Ndị na-enye onyinye chọrọ ka onyinye ha nọrọ na nzuzo on-chain ma ka na-ekwu mwepụ ụtụ isi

Ọ bụrụ na ị na-elekọta ọrụ ebere ahụ, lee [Nabata Onyinye Nzuzo dịka Òtù Na-adịghị Maka Uru na US](/zcash-use-cases/accept-private-donations-as-a-us-nonprofit).

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-lock-2f8e221321.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="lock icon"/> Nzuzo on-chain, mana ọrụ ebere ahụ maara

Onyinye nke e chebere echebe na-eme ka ego ahụ, Adreesị akpa ego gị, na nguzozi ọrụ ebere ahụ ghara ịdị n'ihe ndekọ ọha. Mwepụ ụtụ isi bụ ụdị Nzuzo ọzọ: maka onyinye ọ bụla ruru $250 ma ọ bụ karịa, ọrụ ebere ahụ ga-amarịrị onye ị bụ iji zitere gị nkwenye, ma karịa $5,000, onye na-atụle uru gị na ọrụ ebere ahụ ga-abịanye aka na Form 8283 gị.

Yabụ, ị nwere ike ịnọ na nzuzo n'ihu ọha ma ka na-ekwu mwepụ ahụ. Ị pụghị ịnọ na-amaghị aha n'ebe ọrụ ebere ahụ nọ ma kwuo ya maka onyinye ruru $250 ma ọ bụ karịa.

- Ọrụ ebere bụ òtù ruru eru (lelee ya na [IRS Ọchụchọ Òtù Ahapụrụ Ụtụ Isi](https://apps.irs.gov/app/eos/)) ma nabata ZEC. Lee [Ebe Ị Ga-enye Onyinye ZEC](/zcash-use-cases/where-to-donate-zec)

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Nduzi nzọụkwụ site na nzọụkwụ

### Nzọụkwụ 1: Lelee ogologo oge i jirila ZEC

- **Ihe karịrị otu afọ:** ZEC bụ akụ isi obodo, yabụ ọ bụ ihe onwunwe uru isi obodo. Ị nwere ike n'ozuzu wepụ uru ahịa ziri ezi ya n'ụbọchị ị nyere ya ([Publication 526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p526.pdf), [IRS Ajụjụ A Na-ajụkarị, Q34–Q35](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions))
- **Otu afọ ma ọ bụ ihe na-erughị ya:** mwepụ gị bụ nke pere mpe n'etiti ndabere ọnụ ahịa gị (na-adịkarị ihe ị kwụrụ) na uru ahịa ziri ezi ya n'ụbọchị ahụ ([IRS Ajụjụ A Na-ajụkarị, Q35](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions))

Ọ bụrụ na ị zụtara ZEC n'oge dị iche iche, nye site na otu ị jiderela ihe karịrị otu afọ ma debe note banyere nke ị jiri.

### Nzọụkwụ 2: Lelee ọrụ ebere ahụ

Hụ na òtù ahụ nwere ike ịnata onyinye nwere ike iwepụ ụtụ isi, ma gụọ ihe ọ na-ekwu gbasara nnata crypto. Ụfọdụ ọrụ ebere anaghị eziga nkwenye maka crypto ma ọlị. [Ebe Ị Ga-enye Onyinye ZEC](/zcash-use-cases/where-to-donate-zec) depụtara ihe nke ọ bụla kwuru.

### Nzọụkwụ 3: Zipu onyinye ahụ ma gwa ọrụ ebere ahụ onye ị bụ

- Detuo Adreesị akpa ego ahụ site na ibe onyinye nke ọrụ ebere ahụ n'onwe ya
- Zipu site na e-akpa ego gị nke e chebere echebe
- Ọ bụrụ na ọrụ ebere ahụ nwere Adreesị akpa ego nke e chebere echebe, tinye aha na email gị na [memo](/using-zcash/memos), ma ọ bụ jiri usoro ịkpọtụrụ dị na ibe onyinye ya

### Nzọụkwụ 4: Dee uru ya n'ụbọchị ahụ

Dekọọ ụbọchị na oge, ọnụọgụ ZEC, uru dollar US ya n'oge ahụ, na ebe ị nwetara ọnụahịa ahụ. Chekwaa ID Azụmahia ahụ. Lee [Idobe ndekọ na ZEC nke e chebere echebe](/zcash-use-cases/keeping-records-with-shielded-zec).

### Nzọụkwụ 5: Nweta nkwenye edere ede

Maka onyinye ọ bụla otu ugboro ruru $250 ma ọ bụ karịa, ị chọrọ nkwenye edere ede n'oge kwesịrị ekwesị sitere n'aka ọrụ ebere ahụ. Ọ na-akọwa ZEC ị nyere, mana ọ naghị etinye uru na ya. Ị chọrọ ya tupu ụbọchị ị tinyere nkwụghachi ụtụ isi gị, ma ọ bụ ụbọchị ikpeazụ tinyere mgbatị oge ma ọ bụrụ na nke ahụ dị tupu ya ([Publication 526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p526.pdf)).

### Nzọụkwụ 6: Dejupụta Form 8283

- **Karịrị $500 ruo $5,000:** dejupụta [Form 8283](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8283.pdf), Nkebi A
- **Karịrị $5,000:** dejupụta Nkebi B. Ị chọrọ ntule uru ruru eru sitere n'aka onye na-atụle uru ruru eru, ọrụ ebere ahụ ga-abịanyekwa aka na fọm ahụ. Crypto abụghị nchekwa a na-ere n'ihu ọha maka iwu a, yabụ ntule uru ahụ dị mkpa ọbụna agbanyeghị na ZEC nwere ọnụahịa ahịa ([Publication 526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p526.pdf), [CCA 202302012](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-wd/202302012.pdf)). [Publication 561](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p561.pdf) na-akọwa ihe a na-ewere dị ka ntule uru ruru eru na mgbe a ga-eme ya

### Nzọụkwụ 7: Kwuo ya na nkwụghachi ụtụ isi gị

Kwuo mwepụ ahụ na Schedule A, nke pụtara na ị na-edepụta mwepụ gị n'otu n'otu. Onyinye nke ihe onwunwe uru isi obodo nye ọtụtụ ọrụ ebere ọha na-adịkarị oke na 30% nke ego a gbanwere agbanwe gị, ị nwekwara ike ibufe nke karịrị oke ahụ gaa n'ihu ([Publication 526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p526.pdf)).

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/user-svgrepo-com-21adf62b7c.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="user icon"/> Ọ bụrụ na ị naghị edepụta mwepụ n'otu n'otu

Site n'afọ ụtụ isi 2026, ndị na-adịghị edepụta mwepụ n'otu n'otu nwere ike iwepụ onyinye ọrụ ebere ruru $1,000 ($2,000 maka di na nwunye na-etinye nkwụghachi ọnụ). Mwepụ ahụ bụ naanị maka ego nkịtị. Onyinye na-abụghị ego nkịtị dịka ZEC erughị eru ([IRS: Ndokwa One Big Beautiful Bill](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/one-big-beautiful-bill-provisions)).

Ọ bụrụ na ị naghị edepụta mwepụ n'otu n'otu, onyinye ZEC ka na-akwado ọrụ ebere ahụ, mana ọ gaghị ebelata ụtụ isi gị.

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Njehie ndị a na-ahụkarị a ga-ezere

- Ibu ụzọ ree ZEC ma nye dollar, nke nwere ike ime ka ị kwụọ ụtụ isi uru isi obodo ị gaara ezere
- Inye ZEC nke i jidere otu afọ ma ọ bụ ihe na-erughị ya ma wepụ uru ahịa ya dum
- Inye n'amaghị aha ma mesịa tụọ anya nnata
- Ịwụpụ ntule uru maka onyinye karịrị $5,000 n'ihi na ZEC nwere ọnụahịa ahịa
- Ifunahụ ndekọ nke mgbe ị zụtara ZEC i nyere
