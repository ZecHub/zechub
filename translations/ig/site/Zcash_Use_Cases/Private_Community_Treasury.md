## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Ntuziaka Nzọụkwụ-site-na-Nzọụkwụ

### Nzọụkwụ 1: Mepụta Adreesị akpa ego Ebe nchekwa ego Nke E Chebere Echebe

- Mepụta **z-address** maka Ebe nchekwa ego
- Nke a ga-abụ akaụntụ bụ isi na-ejide ego


### Nzọụkwụ 2: Kọwaa Ọrụ

Kewaa ibu ọrụ:
- Onye na-edebe ego (na-achịkwa ego)
- Ndị na-enye onyinye (na-arịọ mmefu)
- Ndị nyocha (na-anabata n'ime)

Zere ijikọ ọrụ ndị a na njirimara ọha.



### Nzọụkwụ 3: Nata Ego na Nzuzo

- Nata onyinye site na Adreesị akpa ego nke e chebere echebe
- Gbaa ndị nyere onyinye ume izipu site na e-akpa ego nke e chebere echebe



### Nzọụkwụ 4: Soro Azụmahia n'Ọdịdị N'ime

Jiri:
- Memo e-akpa ego
- Ndekọ off-chain (dịka ọmụmaatụ, akwụkwọ a na-ekekọrịta)

Ihe atụ memo: 
 - Ikike #12 - Kwụọ ụgwọ onye mmepụta [developer]



### Nzọụkwụ 5: Mee Kwụọ ụhwọ na Nzuzo

- Zipu ego site na Azụmahia nke e chebere echebe (z → z)
- Zere ikpughe njirimara ndị nnata n'ihu ọha
