## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Step-by-Step Guide

### Nzọụkwụ 1: Mepụta Adreesị Akụba echekwara

- Mepụta ** z-adreesị** maka akụnụba.
- Nke a na-aghọ isi akaụntụ njide ego.


### Nke Abụọ: Kpebie Ọrụ Ndị I Kwesịrị Ịrụ n'Ọgbakọ

Ọrụ dị iche:
- Onye na-ahụ maka ego (na-achịkwa ego)
- Ndị na-enye onyinye (na-arịọ maka mmefu)
- Ndị na-enyocha (na-akwado n'ime)

Zere ijikọta ọrụ ndị a na njirimara ọha.



### Nzọụkwụ 3: Nweta Ego na Ntị Gị Nanị Gị

- Nabata onyinye site na adreesị echekwara
- Gbaa ndị na-enye onyinye ume ka ha si n'akpa ego e chebere zipu ihe.



### Nzọụkwụ 4: Soro azụmahịa n'ime ụlọ gị

Jiri:
- Ihe ncheta obere akpa ego.
- Mpempe akwụkwọ mpụga (dịka, dọkịkọm na-ekerịta)

Ihe atụ memo: 
 - Onyinye #12 - Ịkwụ Ụgwọ Onye Mmepụta Ihe



### Nzọụkwụ 5: Na-akwụ ụgwọ na nzuzo

- Ziga ego site na azụmahịa echekwara (z → z)
- Zere ikpughe njirimara ndị nnata n'ihu ọha.
