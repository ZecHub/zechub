> Ibe a bụ ozi izugbe, ọ bụghị ndụmọdụ iwu ma ọ bụ ụtụ isi. Iwu ụtụ isi US na-agbanwe, ya mere jiri onye na-ahụ maka akaụntụ gị ma ọ bụ onye ndụmọdụ ụtụ isi nyochaa usoro ọ bụla. Iwu ọ bụla dị n'okpuru a jikọtara na isi mmalite IRS ya, nke enyochara n’Ọktoba 2026 megide Publication 526 (maka nkwụghachi ụtụ isi 2025) na Form 8283 (ntụgharị Disemba 2025). Edere FAQ ego mebere IRS maka Azụmahia tupu 2025, ya mere nyochaa ya megide nduzi dị ugbu a.

## Na nkenke

- Debe onyinye n’e-akpa ego [wallet] nke e chebere echebe nke nzukọ gị na-achịkwa, ọ bụghị e-akpa ego nkeonwe nke onye ọrụ
- IRS na-ewere ZEC dịka akụ, ya mere onyinye ZEC bụ onyinye na-abụghị ego nkịtị
- Maka onyinye $250 ma ọ bụ karịa, ziga onye nyere onyinye nkwenye edere ede nke kọwara ZEC mana etinyeghị uru dollar na ya
- Maka onyinye karịrị $5,000, onye nyere onyinye chọrọ nyocha ọnụ ahịa ruru eru, ị ga-abịanyekwa aka na Form 8283 ha
- Ọ bụrụ na ị ree ZEC n’ime afọ atọ ị natara ya ma ị bịanyere aka na Nkebi B nke Form 8283 onye nyere onyinye, nyefee Form 8282
- Onye nyere onyinye nke na-amaghị aha ya n’ebe ị nọ n’ozuzu enweghị ike ịrịọ mwepụ ụtụ isi nke $250 ma ọ bụ karịa

- Ndị ọrụ ebere US 501(c)(3), ụka, na otu obodo chọrọ ịnakwere ZEC

## <img src="/content-images/warning-error-svgrepo-com-b7ea8a50da.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="warning icon"/> Nsogbu ahụ

Ọtụtụ ọrụ onyinye crypto maka ndị ọrụ ebere US na-enye gị Adreesị akpa ego Oha, ma ọ bụ gbanwee onyinye ahụ maka gị site n'otu. N’Adreesị akpa ego Oha, onye ọ bụla nwere ike ịhụ onyinye ọ bụla, ego ya, na nguzozi gị. Onye nyere onyinye n’ezube inye ya na nzuzo na-atụfu Nzuzo ahụ ozugbo onyinye ahụ rutere.

Nzukọ anaghị akwụ uru nwekwara akwụkwọ ndị mmadụ n'otu n'otu enweghị: nnata onye nyere onyinye, fọm IRS maka onyinye buru ibu, na nkwụghachi kwa afọ. Nzuzo na akwụkwọ ndị ahụ nwere ike ịrụkọ ọrụ, ma ọ bụrụ na ị hazie ha tupu onyinye mbụ abịa.

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Nduzi nzọụkwụ site na nzọụkwụ

### Nzọụkwụ 1: Kpebie iwu gị

Detuo ihe ndị a, ma mee ka bọọdụ gị kwado ha:
- Ònye nwere ike ịkwaga ego, yana ma mmadụ abụọ ga-akwadorịrị mbugharị ọ bụla
- Ma ị na-ere onyinye ọ bụla ozugbo ma ọ bụ na-edobe ụfọdụ ZEC
- Ma ị na-anabata onyinye amaghị aha, yana ruo ego ole

### Nzọụkwụ 2: Hazie e-akpa ego nke nzukọ na-achịkwa

- Mepụta e-akpa ego ahụ na ngwaọrụ nzukọ, ọ bụghị ekwentị nkeonwe
- Debe Mkpuru mgbake [seed phrase] na-anọghị n'ịntanetị, n'aka ndị isi abụọ nke onye ọ bụla nwere ike iru ya
- Maka njikwa ọnụ, lee [Gbaa Private Community Treasury](/zcash-use-cases/private-community-treasury)

### Nzọụkwụ 3: Bipụta Adreesị akpa ego nke e chebere echebe

- Kekọrịta Adreesị akpa ego nke e chebere echebe naanị ya na ibe onyinye gị. [Adreesị akpa ego jikọtara ọnụ](/using-zcash/unified-addresses) na-amalite na `u1`, mana ọ nwekwara ike ịnwe onye nnata Oha, onyinye si n’e-akpa ego naanị-Oha wee daa n’ọha. Jiri Adreesị akpa ego naanị-nke e chebere echebe nke e-akpa ego gị ma ọ bụrụ na ọ na-enye otu.
- Ebipụtala Adreesị akpa ego Oha (ọ na-amalite na `t1` ma ọ bụ `t3`)
- Gwa ndị nyere onyinye chọrọ nnata ka ha tinye email ha na [memo](/using-zcash/memos). E ji Nzobe ederede [encryption] chebe memo ahụ, ya mere naanị nzukọ gị nwere ike ịgụ ya.

### Nzọụkwụ 4: Dekọọ onyinye ọ bụla

Maka onyinye ọ bụla, dekọọ:
- Ụbọchị na oge enwetara ya
- Ego na ZEC
- Uru ya na dollar US n'oge ahụ, na isi mmalite ọnụahịa i jiri
- ID Azụmahia na memo

IRS na-ewere ZEC dịka akụ ([Notice 2014-21](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-14-21.pdf)), ya mere nke a bụ onyinye na-abụghị ego nkịtị n’akwụkwọ gị. Iji nye onye na-ahụ maka akaụntụ gị ohere ịgụ naanị, lee [Idebe ndekọ site na ZEC nke e chebere echebe](/zcash-use-cases/keeping-records-with-shielded-zec).

### Nzọụkwụ 5: Kwenye onyinye nke $250 ma ọ bụ karịa

Onye nyere onyinye enweghị ike iwepụ otu onyinye nke $250 ma ọ bụ karịa n’enweghị nkwenye edere ede sitere n'aka gị ([Publication 1771](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1771.pdf)). Maka onyinye ZEC, tinye:
- Aha nzukọ gị
- Ụbọchị ị natara ya
- Nkọwa onyinye ahụ, dịka ọmụmaatụ “1.5 ZEC”. Etinyela uru dollar na ya: ịtụ ọnụ ahịa onyinye ahụ bụ ọrụ onye nyere onyinye.
- Ma ị nyere ihe ọ bụla na nzaghachi, dịka ọmụmaatụ “Enyereghị ngwongwo ma ọ bụ ọrụ ọ bụla n’ọnọdụ onyinye a.”

Onye nyere onyinye ga-enwerịrị ya tupu ha etinye nkwụghachi ụtụ isi ha ([Publication 526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p526.pdf)). Nzukọ na-ezigakarị ya tupu Jenụwarị 31 nke afọ na-esote; iziga ya ozugbo dị mfe karịa.

### Nzọụkwụ 6: Bịanye aka na Form 8283 maka onyinye karịrị $5,000

Ọ bụrụ na onye nyere onyinye rịọrọ ihe karịrị $5,000, ha chọrọ nyocha ọnụ ahịa ruru eru. Crypto anaghị agụta dịka nchekwa a na-ere n’ahịa ọha, ya mere nyocha ọnụ ahịa ahụ metụtara ya ([Publication 526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p526.pdf), [CCA 202302012](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-wd/202302012.pdf)). Onye nyere onyinye na-ewetara gị [Form 8283](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8283.pdf), ị ga-abịanye aka n’akụkụ onye nata onyinye nke Nkebi B. signature gị na-akwado na ị natara ZEC n’ụbọchị ahụ; ọ naghị akwado uru onye nyere onyinye kwuru ([IRS FAQ, Q36](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions)).

### Nzọụkwụ 7: Gbanwee ka ọ bụrụ dollar, ma ọ bụrụ na ị chọrọ

- Lelee exchange ndị na-anabata nkwụnye ego nke e chebere echebe na [Custodial Exchanges](/using-zcash/custodial-exchanges). Ọtụtụ na-anabata naanị nkwụnye ego Oha.
- Ọ bụrụ na nke gị bụ naanị-Oha, bugharịa ZEC site n’e-akpa ego gị nke e chebere echebe gaa na exchange n’otu nzọụkwụ, n’oge ị rere ya. Ego ahụ aghọọ ọha, mana onyinye ndị ọzọ na nguzozi gị na-anọgide na nzuzo.
- Ọ bụrụ na ị ree ZEC n’ime afọ atọ ị natara ya, ma ị bịanyere aka na Nkebi B nke Form 8283 onye nyere onyinye, nyefee [Form 8282](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8282.pdf) n’ime ụbọchị 125 mgbe ire ahụ gasịrị ma ziga onye nyere onyinye otu ya ([IRS FAQ, Q37](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions)).

### Nzọụkwụ 8: Kọọ ya na nkwụghachi kwa afọ gị

Kọọ onyinye ZEC dịka onyinye na-abụghị ego nkịtị na nkwụghachi Form 990-series gị, tinyere [Schedule M](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-schedule-m-form-990) ma ọ bụrụ na ọ metụtara gị. Jụọ onye na-ahụ maka akaụntụ gị otu esi edepụta onyinye amaghị aha.

## <img src="/content-images/user-svgrepo-com-21adf62b7c.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="user icon"/> Ndị nyere onyinye amaghị aha: ihe ha nwere na enweghị ike ịrịọ

- Nzuzo n'aka ọha abụghị otu ihe na amaghị aha n’ebe ị nọ. Onye nyere onyinye nwere ike inye na nzuzo ma ka kesaa email ha na memo iji nweta nnata.
- Onye nyere onyinye nke na-amaghị aha ya n’ebe ị nọ enweghị ike inweta nkwenye, ya mere ha enweghị ike iwepụ otu onyinye nke $250 ma ọ bụ karịa.
- Karịa $5,000, onye nyere onyinye chọkwara nyocha ọnụ ahịa na signature gị na Form 8283, nke pụtara ịgwa gị onye ha bụ.
- Maka onye nyere onyinye, onyinye ZEC ejidere ihe karịrị otu afọ na-adịkarị mfe iwepụ n’uru ahịa ziri ezi, na-enweghị ụtụ isi uru isi obodo na onyinye ahụ ([IRS FAQ, Q34–Q35](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions)).

Kwuo nke a na ibe onyinye gị, ka ndị nyere onyinye wee họrọ tupu ha enye.

- Idebe onyinye n’e-akpa ego nkeonwe nke onye ọrụ
