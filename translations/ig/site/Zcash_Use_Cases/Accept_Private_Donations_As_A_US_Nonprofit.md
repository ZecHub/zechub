> Ibe a bụ ozi izugbe, ọ bụghị ndụmọdụ iwu ma ọ bụ ụtụ isi. Iwu ụtụ isi US na-agbanwe, yabụ lelee nzọụkwụ ọ bụla na onye na-ahụ maka akaụntụ gị ma ọ bụ onye ndụmọdụ ụtụ isi. Iwu ọ bụla dị n'okpuru njikọ na isi mmalite IRS ya, nke a lere anya na Ọktoba 2026 megide Mbipụta 526 (maka nloghachi 2025) na Fọm 8283 (mmegharị Disemba 2025). E dere FAQ maka ego mebere emebere IRS maka azụmahịa tupu 2025, yabụ lelee ya anya na ntuziaka dị ugbu a.

## TL;DR

- Jide onyinye n'ime obere akpa nchekwa nke nzukọ gị na-achịkwa, ọ bụghị obere akpa nke onye ọrụ
- IRS na-emeso ZEC dị ka ihe onwunwe, yabụ onyinye ZEC bụ onyinye na-abụghị ego
- Maka onyinye nke $250 ma ọ bụ karịa, zigara onye nyere onyinye akwụkwọ nkwenye ederede nke na-akọwa ZEC mana ọ naghị etinye uru dollar na ya
- Maka onyinye karịrị $5,000, onye nyere onyinye ahụ chọrọ nyocha ruru eru, ị ga-abịanye aka na Fọm 8283 ha
- Ọ bụrụ na ị ree ZEC n'ime afọ atọ nke ịnata ya ma ị bịanye aka na Nkebi B nke Fọm 8283 nke onye nyere onyinye, tinye Fọm 8282
- Onye na-enye onyinye nke na-anaghị akọwa aha gị n'ozuzu ya enweghị ike ịrịọ maka mbelata nke $250 ma ọ bụ karịa

## <img src="/content-images/user-svgrepo-com-21adf62b7c.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="user icon"/> Who is this for?

- Òtù ọrụ ebere US 501(c)(3) ndị chọrọ ịnabata ZEC
- Ndị na-ahụ maka ego, ndị na-ede akwụkwọ na ndị otu bọọdụ na-ahazi onyinye ego crypto
- Ndị na-enye onyinye chọrọ ịma ihe otu òtù na-anaghị akwụ ụgwọ chọrọ n'aka ha

Maka ndị mmadụ n'otu n'otu na ndị okike, lee [Nara onyinye n'onwe gị](/zcash-use-cases/receive-donations-privately).

## <img src="/content-images/warning-error-svgrepo-com-b7ea8a50da.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="warning icon"/> The problem

Ọtụtụ ọrụ onyinye ego maka ndị ọrụ ebere nke US na-enye gị adreesị doro anya, ma ọ bụ gbanwee onyinye ahụ maka gị site na otu. Na adreesị doro anya, onye ọ bụla nwere ike ịhụ onyinye ọ bụla, ọnụọgụ ya na ego ị ga-enweta. Onye nyere onyinye nke na-enye onyinye n'onwe ya na-efunahụ nzuzo ahụ ozugbo onyinye ahụ rutere.

Òtù na-anaghị akwụ ụgwọ nwekwara akwụkwọ ndị mmadụ na-anaghị akwụ ụgwọ ha: akwụkwọ nnata onyinye, akwụkwọ IRS maka onyinye buru ibu, na akwụkwọ ndị ọzọ kwa afọ. Nzuzo na akwụkwọ ndị ahụ nwere ike ịrụ ọrụ, ma ọ bụrụhaala na ị na-eme atụmatụ maka ha tupu onyinye mbụ abịa.

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Step-by-step guide

### Nzọụkwụ nke 1: Kpebie iwu gị

Dee ya, ma mee ka bọọdụ gị kwado:
- Ònye nwere ike ibugharị ego, na ma mmadụ abụọ ga-akwado mbugharị ọ bụla
- Ma ị rere onyinye ọ bụla ozugbo ma ọ bụ jide ụfọdụ ZEC
- Ma ị na-anabata onyinye ndị a na-amaghị aha ha, na ego ole ka ị na-anabata?

### Nzọụkwụ nke 2: Mepụta obere akpa njikwa nke nzukọ ahụ

- Mepụta obere akpa ego na ngwaọrụ nzukọ, ọ bụghị ekwentị nkeonwe
- Debe mkpụrụ okwu ahụ n'ịntanetị, ya na ndị uwe ojii abụọ nwere ike iru ya
- Maka njikwa a na-ekerịta, lee [Gbaa Akụ Obodo nkeonwe](/zcash-use-cases/private-community-treasury)

### Nzọụkwụ nke 3: Bipụta adreesị echekwara

- Kesaa adreesị naanị nke e chebere na ibe onyinye gị [adreesị ejikọtara ọnụ](/using-zcash/unified-addresses) na-amalite na `u1`, mana o nwekwara ike ịnwe onye nnata doro anya, onyinye sitere na obere akpa doro anya na-adaba n'ihu ọha. Jiri adreesị nchekwa naanị nke obere akpa gị ma ọ bụrụ na ọ nwere otu.
- E bipụtala adreesị doro anya (ọ na-amalite site na `t1` or `t3`)
- Jụọ ndị nyere onyinye chọrọ akwụkwọ nnata ka ha tinye email ha na [ihe ncheta](/using-zcash/memos)E zoro ezo ihe ndetu ahụ, yabụ naanị otu gị nwere ike ịgụ ya.

### Nzọụkwụ nke 4: Dekọọ onyinye ọ bụla

Maka onyinye ọ bụla, detuo:
- Ụbọchị na oge e natara
- Ọnụọgụ dị na ZEC
- Uru dị na dollar US n'oge ahụ, na ebe ị si enweta ọnụahịa ị jiri
- NJ azụmahịa na memo

IRS na-emeso ZEC dị ka ihe onwunwe ([Ọkwa 2014-21](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-14-21.pdf)), yabụ nke a bụ onyinye na-abụghị ego n'akwụkwọ gị. Iji nye onye na-ahụ maka akaụntụ gị ohere ịgụ naanị, lee [Idebe ndekọ na ZEC echekwara](/zcash-use-cases/keeping-records-with-shielded-zec).

### Nzọụkwụ nke 5: Kweta onyinye nke $250 ma ọ bụ karịa

Onye nyere onyinye enweghị ike iwepụ otu onyinye nke $250 ma ọ bụ karịa na-enweghị nkwenye ederede n'aka gị ([Mbipụta 1771](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1771.pdf)Maka onyinye ZEC, tinye:
- Aha ụlọ ọrụ gị
- Ụbọchị ị natara ya
- Nkọwa nke onyinye ahụ, dịka ọmụmaatụ "1.5 ZEC". Etinyela ya otu dollar: iji onyinye ahụ kpọrọ ihe bụ ọrụ onye nyere ya.
- Ma ị nyere ihe ọ bụla n'ọnọdụ ya, dịka ọmụmaatụ "E nyeghị ngwaahịa ma ọ bụ ọrụ ọ bụla n'ọnọdụ onyinye a."

Onye nyere onyinye ga-enwerịrị ya tupu o tinye akwụkwọ ụtụ isi ya ([Mbipụta 526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p526.pdf)) Ndị otu na-ezigakarị ya tupu Jenụwarị 31 nke afọ na-abịa; izipu ya ozugbo bụ ihe kachasị mfe.

### Nzọụkwụ nke 6: Bịanye aka na Fọm 8283 maka onyinye karịrị $5,000

Ọ bụrụ na onye nyere onyinye na-ekwu na ihe karịrị $5,000, ha chọrọ nyocha ruru eru. Crypto anaghị agụta nchekwa a na-ere n'ihu ọha, yabụ nyocha ahụ metụtara ya ([Mbipụta 526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p526.pdf), [CCA 202302012](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-wd/202302012.pdf)Onye nyere gị onyinye na-ewetara gị [Fọm 8283](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8283.pdf), ị bịanye aka n'akụkụ nke e mechara nke Nkebi B. Mbinye aka gị na-egosi na ị natara ZEC n'ụbọchị ahụ; ọ naghị akwado uru onye nyere onyinye ahụ ([Ajụjụ Ndị A Na-ajụkarị Banyere IRS, Q36](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions)).

### Nzọụkwụ nke 7: Gbanwee gaa na dollar, ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ

- Lelee mgbanwe ndị na-anabata nkwụnye ego echekwara na ha [Mgbanwe Ụlọ Nlereanya](/using-zcash/custodial-exchanges)Ọtụtụ na-anabata naanị ego nkwụnye ego doro anya.
- Ọ bụrụ na nke gị doro anya, bufee ZEC site na obere akpa nchekwa gị gaa na mgbanwe ahụ n'otu nzọụkwụ, n'oge ị rere ya. Ego ahụ ga-abụ nke ọha, mana onyinye ndị ọzọ na ego gị ga-anọgide na nzuzo.
- Ọ bụrụ na ị ree ZEC n'ime afọ atọ nke ịnata ya, ma ị bịanye aka na Nkebi B nke Fọm 8283 nke onye nyere onyinye, tinye akwụkwọ [Fọm 8282](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8282.pdf) n'ime ụbọchị 125 nke ire ere ahụ wee ziga onye nyere onyinye otu ([Ajụjụ Ndị A Na-ajụkarị Banyere IRS, Q37](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions)).

### Nzọụkwụ nke 8: Kpesa akụkọ gbasara nloghachi gị kwa afọ

Kpesa onyinye ZEC dị ka onyinye na-abụghị ego na nloghachi usoro Form 990 gị, yana [Usoro M](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-schedule-m-form-990) ọ bụrụ na ọ metụtara gị. Jụọ onye na-ahụ maka akaụntụ gị otu esi edepụta onyinye ndị a na-amaghị aha ha.

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/user-svgrepo-com-21adf62b7c.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="user icon"/> Anonymous donors: what they can and can't claim

- Nzuzo n'ebe ọha na eze nọ abụghị otu ihe ahụ dịka nzuzo n'ebe ị nọ. Onye nyere onyinye nwere ike inye ihe n'onwe ya ma ka kesaa ozi email ya n'ime akwụkwọ ozi ahụ iji nweta nnata.
- Onye nyere onyinye nke na-anaghị akọwa aha ya agaghị enweta otuto, yabụ na ha enweghị ike iwepụ otu onyinye nke $250 ma ọ bụ karịa.
- Ihe karịrị $5,000, onye nyere onyinye ahụ chọkwara ka a nyochaa ya ma bịnye aka gị na Fọm 8283, nke pụtara ịgwa gị onye ha bụ.
- Maka onye nyere onyinye, a na-ewepụkarị onyinye ZEC nke e ji ihe karịrị otu afọ na uru ahịa ziri ezi, na-enweghị ụtụ isi ego na onyinye ahụ ([Ajụjụ Ndị A Na-ajụkarị Banyere IRS, Q34–Q35](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions)).

Kwuo nke a na ibe onyinye gị, ka ndị na-enye onyinye wee nwee ike ịhọrọ tupu ha enye.

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Common mistakes to avoid

- Ijide onyinye n'ime obere akpa onye ọrụ
- Ibipụta adreesị doro anya, ma ọ bụ adreesị nkwụnye ego mgbanwe, dị ka adreesị onyinye gị
- Itinye uru dollar na nkwenye ahụ
- Ire ihe n'ime afọ atọ ma chefuo Fọm 8282
- Nkwa maka mbelata ụtụ isi nye ndị na-enye onyinye a na-amaghị aha
