# MetaMask Zcash Ntuziaka Njikọta Snap

> Jide ma ziga ZEC nke e chebere echebe site na mgbatị ihe nchọgharị MetaMask, site n'iji Zcash Snap nke ChainSafe na e-akpa ego weebụ WebZjs.

## Na nkenke

* ChainSafe Zcash Snap na-agbakwunye ZEC nke e chebere echebe [shielded] na MetaMask n’elu kọmputa (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). Ọ dịghị mkpa inwe sọftụwia Zcash iche.
* A na-enweta igodo Zcash gị site na Mkpuru mgbake [recovery phrase] MetaMask gị. Otu mkpụrụokwu ahụ na-echekwa ma ETH gị ma ZEC gị ugbu a.
* Ị na-ejikwa ZEC site n’e-akpa ego [wallet] WebZjs na webzjs.chainsafe.dev, ọ bụghị n’ime ndepụta akụ nke MetaMask.
* Akaụntụ gị na-enweta Adreesị akpa ego [address] ejikọrọ ọnụ, ya mere ndị zitere ego na-akwụ gị n’ime pool kacha nwee Nzuzo [privacy] ha kwadoro.
* Tinye ego na ya site n’ịgbawe [swap] (dịka ọmụmaatụ ETH gaa na ZEC) ma ọ bụ site n’iwepụ ZEC na exchange gaa na Adreesị akpa ego gị nke e chebere echebe.
* Mgbake: tinyegharịa MetaMask na Snap, wee bubata Mkpuru mgbake gị dị ugbu a.

## Nkọwa Isianyị

MetaMask Snap bụ obere mgbakwunye e mechiri n'ime sandbox nke na-akụziri MetaMask blockchain [blockchain] ọhụrụ. Zcash Snap, nke ChainSafe wuru ma Hacken nyochara, na-eme ka MetaMask nweta igodo Zcash site na mkpụrụ mgbake [recovery phrase] gị dịbu ma binye aka na Azụmahia [transactions] Zcash.

MetaMask n'onwe ya anaghị egosi nguzozi ZEC. Kama nke ahụ, e-akpa ego webụ WebZjs na-ejikọ na Snap, na-emekọrịta na netwọkụ Zcash site na sava lightwalletd, ma gosi Adreesị akpa ego na nguzozi gị. A na-agbakọ proof nke e chebere echebe na ihe nchọgharị gị site na WebAssembly, nke mere na izipu nwere ike iwe obere oge.

Nzọụkwụ ndị dị n'okpuru na-ekpuchi iwunye Snap, ijikọ WebZjs, itinye ego n'ime e-akpa ego [wallet], izipu na ịnata, na ịlele akụkụ nke Adreesị akpa ego ejikọtara ọnụ gị.

## **Ihe ndị a chọrọ tupu amalite**


> [**Mgbakwunye MetaMask**](https://snaps.metamask.io/snap/npm/chainsafe/webzjs-zcash-snap/) (naanị na kọmputa) - Chrome, Edge, ma ọ bụ Firefox.
> Akaụntụ MetaMask - Chekwaa Mkpuru mgbake gị; Snap na-enweta igodo Zcash site na ya.  
> Njikọ Ịntanetị kwụsiri ike - Maka ịmekọrịta na netwọk Zcash.  
> Ego - ETH iji gbaa maka ZEC ma ọ bụ ZEC sitere na exchange.

> **Ndụmọdụ:** Chebe mkpụrụokwu mgbake MetaMask gị - ọ na-achịkwa ma ETH ma ZEC.

## **1. Wụnye Snap Zcash**

1. Gaa na [**akwụkwọ ndekọ Snaps MetaMask**](https://snaps.metamask.io/snap/npm/chainsafe/webzjs-zcash-snap/).  
2. Chọọ [**"Zcash Shielded Wallet"**](https://snaps.metamask.io/snap/npm/chainsafe/webzjs-zcash-snap/) ma ọ bụ [**"WebZjs Zcash Snap"**](https://snaps.metamask.io/snap/npm/chainsafe/webzjs-zcash-snap/).  
3. Pịa **Wụnye/Tinye na MetaMask**.
4. Nabata ikike dịka:
   ```
      Manage Zcash accounts 
      Store data on your device
   ```

![Zcash-snap-install](/content-images/Hy5MSG2Oex-42d0c5b346.webp)


---

## **2. (Nhọrọ) Tinye Netwọk Zcash**

Na MetaMask, họrọ **Tinye Netwọk** ma tinye:

Maka **BNB SmartChain**;
```markdown
-  Name: BNB Smart Chain
-  RPC URL: https://bsc-dataseed.binance.org
-  Chain ID: 56
-  Symbol: BNB
-  Block Explorer URL: https://bscscan.com
```
Nke a na-enyere ozi netwọk na njikọ explorer aka.
![Add-a-custom-Net....](/content-images/S1hq7f2Oel-e1ca8b9044.webp)

Maka **Mainnet Zcash**;
```markdown
- Name: Zcash Mainnet  
- RPC URL: https://zjs.zec.rocks 
- Symbol: ZEC
```
`https://zjs.zec.rocks` bụ sava lightwalletd dakọtara na WebZjs (gRPC-web) nke [zec.rocks](https://zec.rocks) (@emersonian) na-agba. Maka testnet, jiri `https://zjs.zec.rocks/testnet`. Ọ bụrụ na ị na-agba e-akpa ego weebụ WebZjs n'onwe gị, nke a bụ uru a ga-edobe dịka `LIGHTWALLETD_PROXY`.

5. Chere ka mmekọrịta kwụsị.

## **5. Zipu / Nata ZEC**

1. Na **WebZjs**, gaa na **Nyefee Nha Ego**.  
2. Tinye:
```
   - Shielded recipient address  
   - Amount
```
   ![Transfer-Balance](/content-images/rkvcFfhdex-bd55d079eb.webp)

4. Kwenye Azụmahia ahụ na MetaMask (binyere Azụmahia ahụ aka).  
5. Ego ị natara ga-apụta na WebZjs mgbe nkwenye gasịrị.

## **6. Nyochaa / Dozie Nsogbu**

> Lelee **WebZjs** maka nha ego emelitere **(MetaMask edepụtabeghị ZEC ozugbo)** .  
> Ọ bụrụ na nsogbu emee:
  ```
  - Confirm you have the official ChainSafe Snap.  
  - Check correct network settings.  
  - Ensure correct address format.  
  - Reconnect via **Connect Snap** if needed.
  ``` 

> **Ndụmọdụ Nchekwa:** Naanị wụnye **ChainSafe Snap enyochala**; nyochaa ikike tupu ịnakwere.

## **7. Lelee Akụkụ Adreesị**

1. Gaa na ngalaba **Nata** - Adreesị Ejikọrọ Ọnụ gị ga-egosi na ndabara.  
2. Detuo Adreesị Ejikọrọ Ọnụ ahụ ma gaa na [Zcash Block Explorer](https://mainnet.zcashexplorer.app/).  
3. Tapawa Adreesị Ejikọrọ Ọnụ gị n'ime ogwe ọchụchọ.  
4. Ugbu a ị ga-ahụ akụkụ niile nke Adreesị Ejikọrọ Ọnụ, nke gụnyere:
``` 
   Orchard Address  
   Sapling Address  
   Transparent Address
```
