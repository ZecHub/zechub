# Faucets

<img width="2138" height="878" alt="Nseta ihuenyo_2025-10-30_10-36-45" src="/content-images/bba0da3b-7064-4680-9535-a0dfcf50cc00-1827fccf4b.webp" />


Faucets bụ ọrụ ndị na-enye obere ego cryptocurrency [cryptocurrency] n’efu. Ndị onye mmepụta na-ejikarị ha eme nnwale na cryptocurrency, ndị ọrụ na-ejikwa ha enweta obere ego cryptocurrency n’emeghị ka ha zụta ya. Ha na-enwekarị oke ego cryptocurrency onye ọrụ nwere ike ịnata n’ime oge e nyere.

Testnet: <a href="https://fauzec.com/">fauzec.com</a>
