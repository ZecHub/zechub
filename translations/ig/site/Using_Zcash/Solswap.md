# **Otu esi agba Mgbawe maka ZEC na e-akpa ego Phantom**

![img1](/content-images/SJOlnt-ceg-34468cfecd.webp)

Ị nweelarị ZEC na Solana (dịka ọmụmaatụ, site na token nke na-akwụ ndị ji ya ụgwọ na ZEC)? Agbagwala ya Mgbawe. Bugharịa token ahụ na e-akpa ego Zcash nke e chebere echebe site na [Ị nwetara ZEC na Solana? Bugharịa ya na Zcash nke e chebere echebe](/using-zcash/solana-zec-to-shielded).

## **ZEC nke sitere n'usoro ahụ ka ọ bụ token ZEC?**

“ZEC” na Phantom nwere ike ịpụta akụ abụọ dị iche, ya mere mara nke ị na-akwụ ụgwọ maka ya.

- Bọtịnụ Mgbawe arụnyere n'ime **Phantom** na-enye gị nnọchite token nke ZEC na Solana (ma ọ bụ netwọk ọzọ Phantom na-akwado). Ọ bụghị ZEC nke sitere n'usoro ahụ. Ọ nọ na Adreesị akpa ego Phantom gị, ọ nweghị ọrụ Zcash nke e chebere echebe, e-akpa ego Zcash enweghịkwa ike ịhụ ya ma ọ bụ chebe ya.
- **ZEC nke sitere n'usoro ahụ** dị naanị na blockchain Zcash ma eziga ya na Adreesị akpa ego Zcash. Iji nweta ya, ịchọrọ ọrụ na-arịọ maka Adreesị akpa ego Zcash gị, dị ka Mgbawe n'ime [ZODL](https://zodl.com), otu n'ime nhọrọ dị na ibe [DEX](/dex), ma ọ bụ solswap.org sochiri iwepụ ego na e-akpa ego Zcash gị (Nzọụkwụ 8).

### Lelee tupu ị kwụọ ụgwọ

- **Netwọk:** ZEC ị natara kwesịrị ịdị na netwọk **Zcash**. Ọ bụrụ na ọ na-ekwu Solana, Ethereum ma ọ bụ Base, ọ bụ token.
- **Akụ:** ZEC nke sitere n'usoro ahụ enweghị nkwekọrịta token ma ọ bụ Adreesị mint. Ọ bụrụ na nke gị gosiri otu, ọ bụ token. E nwekwara ọtụtụ token “ZEC” ndị yiri ibe ha na Solana, ya mere atụkwasịla aha ahụ naanị obi. Token OmniBridge na Solana bụ `A7bdiYdS5GjqGFtxf17ppRHtDKPkkRqbKtR27dxvQXaS`; nke ahụ ka bụ token, ọ bụghị ZEC nke sitere n'usoro ahụ.
- **Adreesị akpa ego:** ZEC nke sitere n'usoro ahụ na-aga na Adreesị akpa ego Zcash, nke na-amalite na `t1`, `u1` ma ọ bụ `zs`. Ọ bụrụ na a na-eziga ZEC na Adreesị akpa ego Phantom gị, ị na-enweta token.

##  **Nzọụkwụ 1: Mepee Mpaghara Mgbawe**
Mepee **ngwa Phantom** wee gaa na **[solswap.org](https://solswap.org/)** site na ihe nchọgharị Phantom. Jiri aka gị dee Adreesị ahụ. Saịtị ahụ na-arụ ọrụ na NEAR Intents ma nwee ike iziga ZEC gaa na Adreesị akpa ego Zcash.

Bọtịnụ **Mgbawe** nke Phantom nwekwara ZEC n'usoro, mana nke ahụ na-enye gị token a kọwara n'elu, ọ bụghị ZEC nke sitere n'usoro ahụ.

##  **Nzọụkwụ 4: Lelee Gas & Ugwo [fee]**
- Debe token gas chain mmalite zuru ezu na Phantom iji kwado ntinye ego ahụ (*SOL* na Solana, *ETH* na Ethereum).
- Gụọ ahịrị ugwo dị na ọnụ ahịa solswap tupu ị kwadoo. Mgbawe arụnyere n'ime Phantom na-eji usoro ugwo nke ya (na mbụ, ugwo Phantom nke 0.85% tinyere gas netwọk na ugwo ijikọta chain). Ọnụ ọgụgụ ndị ahụ anaghị emetụta ntinye ego solswap.org.

##  **Nzọụkwụ 5: Gbanwee Ntọala (Ọ bụrụ na Ọ Dị Mkpa)**
Na solswap.org, nyochaa slippage na ọnụ ego kacha nta a kpọtụrụ aha na ihuenyo ahụ tupu itinye ego.

Ọ bụrụ na ị na-ele akwụkwọ **Mgbawe** nke Phantom anya kama, ị nọ n'okporo ụzọ token sitere na mmalite ibe a. Mechie ya wee mepee `solswap.org` na ihe nchọgharị Phantom.

## **Nzọụkwụ 8: Wepụ ZEC Nke Sitere n'Usoro Ahụ Gaa na e-akpa ego Zcash Gị**
Mgbe Mgbawe gasịrị, ZEC gị na-apụta na nguzozi **Account** solswap.org gị. Ọ kabeghị na netwọk Zcash, ọ nọghịkwa na Phantom.

1. Mepee e-akpa ego Zcash nke ndekọ [directory](/wallets) ji akara **Ironwood: Njikere**. Detuo `u1` nke e-akpa ego gị kpọrọ nke e chebere echebe. `t1` na-arụkwa ọrụ, mana ntinye ego ahụ bụ nke ọha ruo mgbe ị chebere ya.
2. Na solswap.org, gaa na **Account** wee pịa **Withdraw**. Họrọ **ZEC**, tọọ netwọk ka ọ bụrụ **Zcash**, mado Adreesị akpa ego ahụ ma lelee mkpụrụedemede mbụ na nke ikpeazụ tupu ị kwadoo.
3. Ọ bụrụ na **Received amount** na **Fee** anọgide na “–” ma bọtịnụ ahụ emeghị ihe ọ bụla, nguzozi ahụ efughị. Ọ nọ na NEAR Intents n'okpuru igodo Phantom gị. Mezue na [near.com](https://near.com): jiri otu e-akpa ego Phantom banye, mepee **Move legacy assets**, pịa **Withdraw** n'ahịrị ZEC (ọ bụghị **Move**), tọọ netwọk ka ọ bụrụ **Zcash**, ma mado otu `u1` ahụ. Phantom ga-arịọ gị ka ị **Sign Message**. Kwadọọ naanị ma ọ bụrụ na arịrịọ ahụ sitere na `near.com` nakwa na ozi ahụ kpọrọ `"verifying_contract": "intents.near"`. Ihuenyo zuru ezu maka ụzọ mgbapụ ahụ dị na [Ị nwetara ZEC na Solana? Bugharịa ya na Zcash nke e chebere echebe](/using-zcash/solana-zec-to-shielded).
