Ị na-ejide ZEC na Solana (dịka ọmụmaatụ site na akara ngosi nke na-akwụ ndị nwere ụgwọ na ZEC)? Egbanwela ya. Bugharịa akara ngosi ahụ gaa na obere akpa Zcash echekwara na ya [Ị nwere ZEC na Solana? Bugharịa ya na Zcash echekwara](/using-zcash/solana-zec-to-shielded).

## **ZEC nke obodo ka ọ bụ ihe nnọchianya ZEC?**

"ZEC" na Phantom nwere ike ịpụta ihe abụọ dị iche iche, yabụ mara nke ị na-akwụ ụgwọ maka ya.

- **Phantom's arụnyere n'ime** na-enye gị ihe nnọchianya nke ZEC na Solana (ma ọ bụ netwọk ọzọ na-akwado Phantom). Ọ bụghị ZEC. Ọ dị na adreesị Phantom gị, ọ nweghị ọrụ nchekwa Zcash, obere akpa Zcash enweghị ike ịhụ ya ma ọ bụ chebe ya.
- **Native ZEC** dị naanị na Zcash blockchain ma ezigara ya na adreesị Zcash. Iji nweta ya, ịchọrọ ọrụ nke na-ajụ adreesị Zcash gị, dị ka mgbanwe n'ime [ZODL](https://zodl.com), otu n'ime nhọrọ dị na [Ibe DEX](/dex), ma ọ bụ solswap.org wee wepụ ego na obere akpa Zcash gị (Nzọụkwụ nke 8).

### Lelee tupu ị kwụọ ụgwọ

- **Netwọk:** ZEC ị na-enweta kwesịrị ịdị na netwọk **Zcash**. Ọ bụrụ na ọ na-ekwu Solana, Ethereum ma ọ bụ Base, ọ bụ token.
- **Akụ̀:** ZEC nke obodo enweghị nkwekọrịta akara ma ọ bụ adreesị mint. Ọ bụrụ na nke gị egosi otu, ọ bụ akara. E nwekwara ọtụtụ akara "ZEC" yiri nke Solana, yabụ echekwala aha naanị gị. Akara OmniBridge dị na Solana bụ `A7bdiYdS5GjqGFtxf17ppRHtDKPkkRqbKtR27dxvQXaS`; nke ahụ ka bụ ihe akaebe, ọ bụghị nke obodo ZEC.
- **Adreesị:** ZEC nke obodo na-aga na adreesị Zcash, nke na-amalite na `t1`, `u1` or `zs`Ọ bụrụ na a na-eziga ZEC na adreesị Phantom gị, ị ga-enweta akara ngosi.

##  **Nzọụkwụ nke 1: Mepee Nhazi Mgbanwe**
Mepee ngwa **Phantom ** wee gaa na **[solswap.org](https://solswap.org/)** site na ihe nchọgharị Phantom. Dee adreesị ahụ n'onwe gị. Saịtị ahụ na-agba ọsọ na NEAR Intents ma nwee ike izipu ZEC na adreesị Zcash.

Bọtịnụ **Swap** Phantom's depụtara ZEC, mana nke ahụ na-eme ka ị nweta akara ngosi akọwara n'elu, ọ bụghị nke ZEC.

##  **Nzọụkwụ nke 4: Lelee Gas na Ụgwọ**
- Debe ihe nrịbama gas zuru oke na Phantom iji kwado nkwụnye ego ahụ (*SOL* na Solana, *ETH* na Ethereum).
- Gụọ akara ụgwọ dị na ọnụahịa solswap tupu ị kwado ya. Swap arụnyere n'ime Phantom's na-eji usoro ụgwọ nke ya (n'akụkọ ihe mere eme, ọ bụ 0.85% ụgwọ Phantom tinyere gas netwọk na ụgwọ njikọ). Ọnụọgụ ndị ahụ anaghị emetụta nkwụnye ego solswap.org.

## **Nzọụkwụ nke 8: Wepụ ZEC nke Obodo gị na obere akpa Zcash gị**
Mgbe mgbanwe ahụ gasịrị, ZEC gị ga-apụta na nguzozi akaụntụ solswap.org gị. Ọ dịghị na netwọk Zcash, ọ dịghịkwa na Phantom.

1. Mepee obere akpa Zcash [ndekọ aha](/wallets) akara **Ironwood: Dị njikere**. Detuo a `u1` akara akpa gị dị ka ihe e ji kpuchie `t1` ọ na-arụkwa ọrụ, mana ego ahụ ga-abụ nke ọha ruo mgbe ị ga-echebe ya.
2. Na solswap.org, gaa na **Akaụntụ** wee pịa **Dọpụ**. Họrọ **ZEC**, tọọ netwọk ahụ ka ọ bụrụ **Zcash**, mado adreesị ahụ wee lelee mkpụrụedemede mbụ na nke ikpeazụ tupu ị kwado.
3. Ọ bụrụ na **ego enwetara** na **Ụgwọ** nọrọ na "–" ma bọtịnụ ahụ anaghị eme ihe ọ bụla, nguzozi agaghị efu. Ọ dị na NEAR Intents n'okpuru igodo Phantom gị. Mechaa na [near.com](https://near.com): banye na otu obere akpa Phantom ahụ, mepee **Bugharịa ihe onwunwe ochie**, pịa **Wepụ** n'ahịrị ZEC (ọ bụghị **Bugharịa**), tọọ netwọk ahụ ka ọ bụrụ **Zcash**, wee mado otu ihe ahụ `u1`Phantom ga-agwa gị ka ị bịanye aka na ozi**. Kwenye naanị ma ọ bụrụ na arịrịọ ahụ si n'aka `near.com` na aha ozi ahụ `"verifying_contract": "intents.near"`Ihuenyo zuru oke maka ngwọta ahụ dị na [Ị nwere ZEC na Solana? Bugharịa ya na Zcash echekwara](/using-zcash/solana-zec-to-shielded).
