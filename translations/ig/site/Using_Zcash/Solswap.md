## **ZEC nke obodo ka ọ bụ ihe nnọchianya ZEC?**

"ZEC" na Phantom nwere ike ịpụta ihe abụọ dị iche iche, yabụ mara nke ị na-akwụ ụgwọ maka ya.

- **Bọtịnụ Mgbanwe nke Phantom arụnyere n'ime** na-enye gị ihe nnọchianya nke ZEC na Solana (ma ọ bụ netwọk ọzọ na-akwado Phantom). Ọ bụghị ZEC. Ọ dị na adreesị Phantom gị, ọ nweghị ọrụ nchekwa Zcash, obere akpa Zcash enweghị ike ịhụ ya ma ọ bụ chebe ya.
- **Native ZEC** dị naanị na Zcash blockchain ma ezigara ya na adreesị Zcash. Iji nweta ya, ịchọrọ ọrụ nke na-ajụ adreesị Zcash gị, dị ka mgbanwe n'ime [ZODL](https://zodl.com), otu n'ime nhọrọ dị na [Ibe DEX](/dex), ma ọ bụ solswap.org wee wepụ ego na obere akpa Zcash gị (Nzọụkwụ nke 8).

### Lelee tupu ị kwụọ ụgwọ

- **Netwọk:** ZEC ị na-enweta kwesịrị ịdị na netwọk **Zcash**. Ọ bụrụ na ọ na-ekwu Solana, Ethereum ma ọ bụ Base, ọ bụ token.
- **Akụ̀:** ZEC nke obodo enweghị nkwekọrịta akara ma ọ bụ adreesị mint. Ọ bụrụ na nke gị egosi otu, ọ bụ akara. E nwekwara ọtụtụ akara "ZEC" yiri nke Solana, yabụ echekwala aha naanị gị.
- **Adreesị:** ZEC nke obodo na-aga na adreesị Zcash, nke na-amalite na `t1`, `u1` or `zs`Ọ bụrụ na a na-eziga ZEC na adreesị Phantom gị, ị ga-enweta akara ngosi.

##  **Nzọụkwụ nke 1: Mepee Nhazi Mgbanwe** 
Mepee ngwa **Phantom** wee gaa na **[solswap.org](https://solswap.org/)** site na ihe nchọgharị Phantom. Saịtị ahụ na-agba ọsọ na Near Intents ma nwee ike izipu ZEC na adreesị Zcash. 

Bọtịnụ **Swap** nke Phantom depụtara ZEC, mana nke ahụ na-eme ka ị nweta akara ngosi akọwara n'elu, ọ bụghị nke ZEC.

##  **Nzọụkwụ nke 4: Lelee Gas na Ụgwọ** 
- Maka **mgbanwe otu agbụ ígwè**, hụ na ị nwere ihe akaebe gas nke obodo zuru oke (*ETH maka Ethereum, SOL maka Solana*). 
- **Mgbanwe mgbanwe nke agbụ ígwè** chọrọ gas na agbụ isi iyi na ebe a na-aga. 
- Lelee nhazi ụgwọ ahụ: 
  - Ụgwọ Phantom: **0.85%** 
  - Gas netwọk 
  - Ụgwọ Ndị Na-enye Ihe Mgbakwunye (~**0.3%**)

## **Nzọụkwụ nke 8: Wepụ ZEC nke Obodo gị na obere akpa Zcash gị** 
Mgbe mgbanwe ahụ gasịrị, ZEC gị ga-apụta na nguzozi akaụntụ solswap.org gị. Ọ dịghị na netwọk Zcash, ọ dịghịkwa na Phantom. Iji bufee ya: 
- Mepee obere akpa Zcash dịka [ZODL](https://zodl.com) ma detuo adreesị nnata gị. Fọm mwepụ ahụ na-anabata nkọwa doro anya (`t1`) ma ọ bụ otu (`u1`) adreesị. 
- Na solswap.org, gaa na **Akaụntụ** wee pịa **Dọpụ**. 
- Họrọ **ZEC**, tọọ netwọk ahụ ka ọ bụrụ **Zcash**, mado adreesị gị wee lelee ya ugboro abụọ tupu ị kwado.

## **Nzọụkwụ Ndị Ọzọ** 
Ozugbo ZEC nke obodo gị dị na obere akpa Zcash gị, ị nwere ike iji ya kpuchie ya [ntuziaka a](/guides/using-zec-privately).  

Enweghị ike ichebe ihe ngosi ZEC nke e ji bọtịnụ Swap nke Phantom zụta n'ụzọ dị otu a, n'ihi na ọ dịghị na netwọk Zcash. Ị ga-ebu ụzọ gbanwee ya maka ZEC nke e zigara na adreesị Zcash.
