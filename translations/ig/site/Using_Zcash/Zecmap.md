# ZecMap (n'asụsụ Bekee)

ZecMap bụ akwụkwọ ndekọ aha zuru ụwa ọnụ, nke obodo na-eme maka ịchọpụta azụmahịa na ọrụ ndị nabatara Zcash (ZEC). E wuru gburugburu interface map mmekọrịta, ọ na - enyere ndị nwere ZEC aka ịza ajụjụ bara uru: ** "Ebee ka m ga - eji zụọ ego ZEC?"**

Ebe nrụọrụ weebụ: [zecmap.com](https://zecmap.com/)

---

## TL;DR

- ZecMap bụ ** free, ọha directory** nke ndị ahịa na-anabata ZEC  azụmahịa mpaghara na ụlọ ahịa n'ịntanetị.
- Chọgharịa na map mmekọrịta iji chọta ndị ahịa ZEC-enyi na enyi dị gị nso ma ọ bụ n'ebe ọbụla n'ụwa.
- Onye ọ bụla nwere ike itinye azụmaahịa; ndepụta gafere nyocha obodo tupu ịpụta n'ihu ọha.
- A **Contributor Rewards Program** na-akpali ịgbakwunye ma nyochaa data ndị ahịa.
- ZecMap na-adịbeghị anya jikọrọ aka ya na ndị ọrụ. [CipherPay](https://x.com/ZecMap/status/2059622324958093616) iji gbasaa njikọta ndị ahịa.

## Ihe ZecMap Na-eme

ZecMap na-ahazi ụlọ ọrụ ndị nabatara Zcash n'elu map ụwa. Ndepụta ọ bụla gosipụtara aha azụmahịa, ụdị, ọnọdụ ma ọ bụ weebụsaịtị, yana otu esi anabata ZEC. Nke a na - eme ka ọ dịrị ndị nwere ZEC mfe:

- Chọọ ụlọ oriri na ọṅụṅụ, ebe a na-eri nri, họtel, ahịa ma ọ bụ ọrụ dị nso nke ga-anabata ZEC tupu ị gawa njem.
- Chọpụta ụlọ ahịa dị n'ịntanetị, ndị na-enye VPN, freelancers ma ọ bụ ọrụ dijitalụ nke kwadoro ịkwụ ụgwọ Zcash.
- Chọgharịa mpaghara Zcash site na obodo, mba ma ọ bụ ụzọ njem.
- Nyere aka mee ka akwụkwọ ndekọ ahụ dị ọhụrụ dịka azụmahịa ndị ọzọ na-amalite ịnakwere ZEC.

## Nnyocha na Nkwenye

Ndepụta na-agafe usoro nyocha tupu ha apụta n'ihu ọha:

1. Onye na-enye onyinye ga-ewepụta azụmahịa nwere ozi ọha zuru ezu iji nyochaa.
2. A na-enyocha ndepụta ahụ maka mbipụta abụọ na izi ezi bụ isi.
3. A na-ekwenye nnabata ZEC site n'aka onye isi ọha ma ọ bụ kọntaktị ahịa.
4. A na-egosi ndepụta ndị a kwadoro n'ime map.
5. Ndị otu obodo nwere ike ịdebanye aha ochie ma ọ bụ ezighi ezi ka ndekọ ahụ wee dị ọhụrụ.

Ebe ọ bụ na ụzọ ndị ahịa si akwụ ụgwọ nwere ike ịgbanwe, were ya ka ihe ọmụma obodo ahụ kwadoro kama ịbụ nkwa. Jụọ onye ahịa tupu ị gaa njem ma ọ bụ zụta nnukwu ihe.

## Mmemme Ụgwọ Ọrụ Ndị Na-enye Aka

ZecMap na-akwụ ndị ọrụ aka ụgwọ maka inye aka n'ịzụlite ma debe akwụkwọ ndekọ aha. A haziri onyinye iji gbaa ume:

- ** Ndepụta ọhụrụ**  na-agbakwunye ụlọ ọrụ ndị nabatara ZEC ma ha anọghị na map.
- **Nyocha**  na-akwado na ndepụta ndị dị ugbu a ka nọ n'ọrụ ma zie ezi.
- **Nchekwa**  imelite ozi ochie ma ọ bụ ịkọwa ụlọ ọrụ mechiri emechi.
- ** Mgbasawanye mkpuchi**  na-agbakwunye ndị ahịa n'obodo, mpaghara ma ọ bụ ụdị ntanetị.

Maka iwu ụgwọ ọrụ ugbu a na otu esi ekwu, gaa n'ebe ahụ. [zecmap.com](https://zecmap.com/) ma ọ bụ lelee ọkwa ZecMap na Zcash obodo.

## Mmekọrịta na Ecosystems

ZecMap accepts merchants that accept Zcash payments via **Flexa**, a payment network used by a growing number of retail locations. This expands the number of real-world merchants listed on the map beyond those that accept Zcash natively.

ZecMap ekwupụtakwala mmekọrịta ya na ndị ọrụ nchekwa. [CipherPay](https://cipherpay.app) iji mee ka njikọta nke usoro ịkwụ ụgwọ maka ndị ahịa na ndekọ ahụ dị omimi.
