# Nduzi Ngwuputa Zcash: Isonyere ọdọ mmiri Ngwuputa na ngwaike nkeonwe

## Okwu Mmalite

Zcash (ZEC) bụ ego dijitalụ lekwasịrị anya na nzuzo nke na-eji algọridim Equihash proof-of-work maka igwu ala. Igwu Zcash gụnyere iji ike mgbakọ na mwepụ dozie nsogbu mgbakọ na mwepụ dị mgbagwoju anya, ịkwado azụmahịa, na ịchekwa netwọk ahụ n'ọnọdụ ụgwọ ọrụ ZEC. N'ihi nnukwu ihe isi ike nke netwọk ahụ, a naghị atụ aro ka ọtụtụ ndị ọrụ igwu ala naanị ha. Isonyere ọdọ mmiri igwu ala bụ ụzọ kachasị mma iji nweta ụgwọ ọrụ na-aga n'ihu site na ijikọta ike hash gị na ndị ọzọ.

Ntuziaka a na-elekwasị anya n'ịgwupụta Zcash site na iji ngwaike nkeonwe (dịka ọmụmaatụ, PC ụlọ nwere GPU ma ọ bụ ASICs dị n'ọkwa ntinye). Rịba ama na ọ bụ ezie na GPU ka nwere ike igwu Zcash, ASICs ka dị irè ma baa uru karịa na 2026 n'ihi nsogbu netwọk. Na-elele uru ugbu a mgbe niile site na iji ngwaọrụ dịka WhatToMine.com, ebe ihe dị ka ọnụ ahịa ọkụ eletrik, ọnụ ahịa ngwaike, na uru ZEC na-emetụta ndụ. Ịgwupụta nwere ike ọ gaghị aba uru maka onye ọ bụla; nyochaa iwu mpaghara na ọnụego ike (chọọ maka < $0.08/kWh).


## Ihe achọrọ

### Akụrụngwa
- **Ịmepụta GPU (Ntọala nkeonwe akwadoro maka ndị mbido):**
  - NVIDIA ma ọ bụ AMD GPUs nwere opekata mpe 4GB VRAM (dịka ọmụmaatụ, NVIDIA GTX 1070, RTX 3060; AMD RX 580 ma ọ bụ karịa).
  - Motherboard dakọtara, PSU zuru oke (ọ dịkarịa ala 750W maka ọtụtụ GPU), yana ezigbo oyi iji gbochie oke okpomọkụ.
  - Ngwakọta ọtụtụ GPU bụ ihe a na-ahụkarị maka ọnụego hash ka mma (dịka ọmụmaatụ, GPU 6x nwere ike iru 1-2 kSol/s).
- **Ịgwuputa ASIC (Ọ dị irè karịa mana ọ na-efu ọnụ ahịa dị elu):**
  - ASIC ndị dakọtara na Equihash dịka Bitmain Antminer Z15 (420 kSol/s) ma ọ bụ Innosilicon A9 (50 kSol/s).
  - Ndị a na-ada ụda, na-ekpo ọkụ karịa, ma na-eri ike karịa (dịka ọmụmaatụ, 1500W+); dabara adaba maka oghere raara onwe ya nye. Zụta site na ebe a ma ama dịka Bitmain.com ma ọ bụ ndị na-ere ahịa (Blockware Mining).
- **Izugbe:** Ịntanetị kwụsiri ike, kọmputa maka ịtọlite/nlekota. ASICs na-achịkwa netwọk ahụ (ihe dị ka 13 GSol/s mkpokọta hashrate na 2026), na-eme ka igwu ala GPU ghara ịsọ mpi mana ọ ka ga-ekwe omume maka ndị na-enwe mmasị na ya.

### Ngwanrọ
- **Sistemụ Ọrụ:** Windows 10/11, Linux (Ubuntu akwadoro maka nkwụsi ike).
- **Ngwanrọ Ngwuputa:**
  - Maka GPU: lolMiner (na-akwado AMD/NVIDIA), GMiner, ma ọ bụ miniZ (NVIDIA-focused). Budata ya na ebe nchekwa GitHub gọọmentị (dịka ọmụmaatụ, github.com/Lolliedieb/lolMiner-releases).
  - Maka ASICs: Jiri firmware/dashboard nke onye nrụpụta wuru n'ime ya (dịka ọmụmaatụ, interface weebụ Bitmain).
- **Akpa ego:** Akpa ego Zcash iji nweta ụgwọ ọrụ. A na-atụ aro:
  - Ekpuchiri (nkeonwe): ZODL Wallet, Zingo (Mkpanaka/Desktọpụ), Zkool (mkpanaka/desktọpụ).
  - Ihe doro anya (dị mfe mana ọ dịghị ka nkeonwe): Edge Wallet, Zecwallet Lite.
  - Budata ya na [obere akpa ego](https://zechub.wiki/wallets)Mepụta adreesị echekwara (malite na 'zs') maka nzuzo ma ọ bụrụ na ọdọ mmiri ahụ kwadoro ya.

### Ndị ọzọ
- Ọkụ eletrik: Gbakọọ ọnụ ahịa ya. GPU na-eji 150-300W kwa kaadị; ASICs 1000W+.
- Antivirus: Gbanyụọ mgbe ị na-ahazi ya n'ihi na ọ nwere ike igosi ndị na-egwuputa ihe dị ka ihe iyi egwu.

## Ntuziaka Nzọụkwụ Site na Nzọụkwụ Maka Ịbanye n'Ọdọ Mmiri Ngwuputa

### Nzọụkwụ nke 1: Hazie obere akpa Zcash gị
1. Budata ma wụnye obere akpa ego site na weebụsaịtị Zcash gọọmentị [obere akpa ego](https://zechub.wiki/wallets).
2. Mepụta obere akpa ego ọhụrụ ma chekwaa mkpụrụ okwu gị n'enweghị nsogbu.
3. Mepụta adreesị nnata (ọ kacha mma ka e chebe ya maka nzuzo). Dee ya ebe a, dịka ọmụmaatụ, `zs1exampleaddress...`.
4. Ọ bụrụ na ị na-eji adreesị doro anya (malite na 't'), ọ dị mfe karịa mana ọ naghị enye nzuzo.

### Nzọụkwụ nke 2: Kwadebe Ngwaike Gị
- Maka GPU:
  1. Wụnye GPU na PC gị ma melite draịva (NVIDIA: Ahụmịhe GeForce; AMD: Radeon Software).
  2. Overclock ma ọ bụrụ na ị nwere ahụmịhe (jiri MSI Afterburner mee ka ọ kwụsie ike; chọọ +100-200 isi elekere, -500 ebe nchekwa maka arụmọrụ).
- Maka ndị ASIC:
  1. Jikọọ ASIC na ike na Ethernet.
  2. Chọta adreesị IP ya site na iji ngwaọrụ dị ka Advanced IP Scanner ma ọ bụ ngwa nke onye nrụpụta.
  3. Nweta dashboard weebụ (dịka ọmụmaatụ, tinye IP na ihe nchọgharị, nbanye ndabara: mgbọrọgwụ/mgbọrọgwụ maka Bitmain).

**Ịdọ Aka Na Ntị:** Hụ na ikuku na-abata nke ọma; igwu ala na-eme ka okpomọkụ dị. Malite obere ka ị nwalee ya.

### Nzọụkwụ nke 3: Họrọ ma sonye na ọdọ mmiri igwu ala
Ọdọ mmiri ndị na-egwupụta ihe na-ekesa ọrụ ma na-ekerịta ụgwọ ọrụ dabere na hashrate gị enyere. Họrọ dabere na ụgwọ (0-2%), obere ụgwọ ọrụ (0.01-0.1 ZEC), ebe (obere ping), na ntụkwasị obi.

**Ọdọ Mmiri Akwadoro (Dabere na Hashrate, Ụgwọ, na Nyocha):**
- **2Miners (zec.2miners.com)**: 1% ụgwọ, PPLNS ụgwọ, na-akwado GPU/ASIC/NiceHash. Hashrate dị elu (~1.17 GSol/s), sava a pụrụ ịtụkwasị obi.
- **F2Pool (zec.f2pool.com)**: ụgwọ 2%, ụgwọ PPS+, nkwado mkpụrụ ego dị iche iche. Ọdọ mmiri buru ibu (~2.57 GSol/s).
- **ViaBTC (zec.viabtc.com)**: 2% ụgwọ (PPS+), dashboard enyi na enyi, sava zuru ụwa ọnụ.
- **AntPool (zec.antpool.com)**: 1% ụgwọ, site na Bitmain, dị mma maka ASICs (~494 MSol/s).
- **Ọdọ mmiri Zcash Foundry (foundrydigital.com/foundry-zcash-pool/)**: Ọdọ mmiri Zcash ọkachamara nke Foundry Digital na-egwupụta. Ọ na-eji ụgwọ PPLNS, na-enye nleba anya ụgwọ ọrụ doro anya na nkwado ọkwa ụlọ ọrụ. Ọ kacha mma maka ndị na-egwupụta ASIC n'ụlọ ọrụ na nnukwu ụlọ ọrụ; ọ chọrọ nkwenye akaụntụ.
- **Sovright (mining.sovright.com)**: Ọdọ mmiri Zcash e wuru na Stratum V2, nke na-arụ ọrụ ugbu a dị ka netwọk nnwale ọha. Enwebeghị ụgwọ ZEC dị ndụ, yabụ were ya dị ka ụzọ ị ga-esi nwalee ntọala gị kama isi mmalite ego. Lee ngalaba raara onwe ya nye n'okpuru maka nkọwa ndị ọzọ.
- Ndị ọzọ: Kryptex Pool, Luxor (lelee poolwatch.io/coin/zcash maka ọnụọgụgụ oge).

1. Gaa na weebụsaịtị ọdọ mmiri ahụ wee mepụta akaụntụ (email ma ọ bụ na-enweghị ndebanye aha maka ụfọdụ dịka 2Miners).
2. Tinye adreesị obere akpa Zcash gị na ntọala maka ịkwụ ụgwọ.
3. Rịba ama ihe nkesa stratum nke ọdọ mmiri ahụ (dịka ọmụmaatụ, zec.2miners.com:1010) na ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri.

### Nzọụkwụ nke 4: Wụnye ma hazie ngwanrọ Ngwuputa
- Maka GPU (Ọmụmaatụ: lolMiner na Windows/Linux):
  1. Budata lolMiner site na GitHub (ụdị kachasị ọhụrụ, dịka ọmụmaatụ, 1.88).
  2. Wepụta na folda.
  3. Mepụta faịlụ otu (start.bat) site na nhazi:
     ```
     lolMiner.exe --coin ZEC --pool zec.2miners.com:1010 --user YOUR_WALLET_ADDRESS.WORKER_NAME --pass x
     ```
     - Dochie `YOUR_WALLET_ADDRESS` na adreesị ZEC gị.
     - `WORKER_NAME`: Aha maka ngwa gị (dịka ọmụmaatụ, Rig1).
     - Maka sava EU: eu.zec.2miners.com:1010.
  4. Gbaa faịlụ ogbe ahụ. Ọ ga-ejikọ na ọdọ mmiri wee malite igwu ala.
- Maka ASICs (Ọmụmaatụ: Bitmain Antminer):
  1. Banye na dashboard weebụ.
  2. Gaa na Nhazi Miner.
  3. Tinye nkọwa ọdọ mmiri:
     - URL: stratum+tcp://zec.2miners.com:1010
     - Aha njirimara: YOUR_WALLET_ADDRESS.WORKER_NAME
     - Paswọọdụ: x (ma ọ bụ oghere).
  4. Chekwaa ma malitegharịa ihe na-egwuputa ihe.
- Maka ngwanrọ ndị ọzọ (dịka ọmụmaatụ, GMiner):
  ```
  miner.exe --algo 125_4 --server zec.2miners.com:1010 --user YOUR_WALLET_ADDRESS.WORKER_NAME --pass x
  ```

**Nwale:** Gbaa ọsọ ruo nkeji iri-na-iri na ise; lelee njikwa maka òkè ndị a nabatara wee jiri hashrate.

### Nzọụkwụ nke 5: Malite Ngwuputa na Nyocha
1. Mepee igwe na-egwuputa ihe: ọ ga-ejikọ na ọdọ mmiri wee malite izipu òkè.
2. Nyochaa site na:
   - Dashboard ọdọ mmiri: Tinye adreesị obere akpa ego gị iji hụ hashrate, ego a na-akwụghị ụgwọ, na ọnụ ọgụgụ.
   - Ngwa njikwa ngwanrọ: Lelee maka njehie, okpomọkụ (dobe < 80 degrees C).
   - Ngwaọrụ: Jiri HiveOS maọbụ SimpleMining OS maka njikwa rig dị anya.
3. Ụgwọ: Ọtụtụ ọdọ mmiri na-akwụ ụgwọ na akpaghị aka mgbe ị ruru nke kacha nta (dịka ọmụmaatụ, 0.05 ZEC). Lelee iwu ọdọ mmiri.

## Ịgwuputa ihe megide ebe Zebra nke gị

Ihe niile dị n'elu na-egosi onye na-egwuputa ihe gị n'ọdọ mmiri onye ọzọ. Ụzọ ọzọ bụ ịgba ọsọ otu n'onwe gị ma mee ka ngwanrọ igwuputa ihe gị rịọ ya ka ọ rụọ ọrụ. Ebe ọ bụ na zcashd kwụsịrị na 18 Julaị 2026, nke ahụ pụtara [Zebra](/zcash-tech/zebra-full-node), node nke ngalaba a na-ekpuchi.

Akụkụ Zebra's dị obere ma dị iche: ọ na-ewulite ndebiri ngọngọ ma na-anabata ngọngọ edoziri n'elu njikọ RPC ya. A na-eme hashing site na ngwanrọ igwu ala, na ịkekọrịta ụgwọ ọrụ n'etiti ọtụtụ ndị na-egwu ala bụ ọrụ nke ngwanrọ ọdọ mmiri. Ma igwu ala naanị ya na-achọta ngọngọ dabere na òkè gị nke ọnụego hash nke netwọk ahụ, nke ngalaba a anaghị anwa ime atụmatụ. Ọ bụ ya mere mmeghe na ibe a anaghị akwado igwu ala naanị maka ọtụtụ ndị ọrụ.

Nzọụkwụ ndị a na-eso ibe akwụkwọ Zebra abụọ, [Ịgwupụta Zcash na Zebra](https://zebra.zfnd.org/user/mining.html) na [Ịgwuputa ihe na Zebra na Docker](https://zebra.zfnd.org/user/mining-docker.html), dịka ha dị na 8 Ọktoba 2026. Aha ndị bụ isi na ndabara na-agbanwe n'etiti mwepụta, yabụ lelee Akwụkwọ ahụ tupu ị dabere na ya.

### Tọọ adreesị nke natara ụgwọ ọrụ ahụ

Mepụta faịlụ nhazi nke nwere ntọala ndabara, wee dezie ya:

```bash
mkdir -p ~/.config
zebrad generate -o ~/.config/zebrad.toml
```

A chọrọ adreesị onye na-egwuputa ihe ([Akwụkwọ: Adreesị onye na-egwuputa ihe](https://zebra.zfnd.org/user/mining.html#miner-address)):

```toml
[mining]
miner_address = "YOUR_ADDRESS"
```

Zebra na-anabata adreesị P2PKH ma ọ bụ P2SH doro anya, adreesị Sapling, ma ọ bụ Unified Address. Maka Unified Address nwere ihe karịrị otu onye nnata, Akwụkwọ ahụ kwuru na Zebra na-akwụ otu onye nnata ụgwọ, "na-ahọrọ Orchard, wee Sapling, wee họrọ transparent".

Igodo nhọrọ abụọ na-aga n'otu `[mining]` ngalaba:

| Igodo | Ihe ọ na-eme | Oke |
|---|---|---|
| `extra_coinbase_data` | Na-agbakwụnye akara ọha, dị ka aha ọdọ mmiri, na ntinye ego nke blọk ọ bụla ị na-egwupụta, mgbe akara Zebra's gasịrị ([Akwụkwọ](https://zebra.zfnd.org/user/mining.html#extra-coinbase-data)) | 86 bytes. N'elu nke ahụ, "Zebra jụrụ ịmalite" |
| `miner_memo` | Na-etinye ihe ncheta echekwara na ihe nrite ahụ. N'adịghị ka `extra_coinbase_data` ọ bụghị nke ọha: naanị onye nwere igodo nlele maka adreesị nnata nwere ike ịgụ ya ([Akwụkwọ](https://zebra.zfnd.org/user/mining.html#miner-memo)) | 512 bytes. Ọ na-arụ ọrụ naanị mgbe ụgwọ ọrụ ahụ gara onye nnata echekwara. Site na adreesị doro anya, ọ "enweghị mmetụta ọ bụla n'ime nwayọ" |

### Mepee ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri RPC

Ngwanrọ igwu ala na-agwa Zebra okwu gbasara JSON-RPC, nke na-agaghị ekwe omume ruo mgbe ị nyere ya adreesị ị ga-ege ntị na ya ([Akwụkwọ: ngalaba RPC](https://zebra.zfnd.org/user/mining.html#rpc-section)):

```toml
[rpc]
listen_addr = "127.0.0.1:8232"
```

8232 bụ ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri RPC ọkọlọtọ na Mainnet. `127.0.0.1` na-eme ka ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri ahụ dị mfe iru site na otu igwe ahụ naanị.

Ebe ọ bụ na Zebra 2.0.0 kuki na-echebe ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri RPC, usoro yiri nke zcashd ji. Zebra na-ede kuki ahụ mgbe njedebe RPC malitere ma hichapụ ya mgbe a na-emechi ya. Dịka ọmụmaatụ, faịlụ ahụ na-anọ na ndekọ nchekwa Zebra's `/home/user/.cache/zebra/.cookie` na Linux, ma nwee otu ahịrị:

```text
__cookie__:PASSWORD
```

`rpc.cookie_dir` na-ebugharị faịlụ ahụ. `rpc.enable_cookie_auth = false` gbanyụọ chek ahụ, naanị ihe akaebe nke ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri RPC nwere bụ: ihe ọ bụla nwere ike iru ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri ahụ nwere ike ịkpọ ya.

### Malite Zebra ma hapụ ya ka ọ mekọrịta

```bash
zebrad
```

Zebra na-agụ nhazi ahụ site na ebe ndabara `-c /path/to/zebrad.toml` iji faịlụ ọzọ. Chere ka mmekọrịta ahụ gwụchaa. Ndekọ ahụ na-egosi `sync_percent=100.000%` mgbe ọ dị ([Akwụkwọ: Zebra Na-agba Ọsọ](https://zebra.zfnd.org/user/mining.html#running-zebra)). [Zebra zuru oke](/zcash-tech/zebra-full-node) na-ekpuchi ngwaike na oghere diski.

### Lelee ya na getblocktemplate

`getblocktemplate` ọ bụ oku ngwanrọ ngwuputa na-eme iji nweta ọrụ, yabụ ọ bụkwa ule kachasị ọsọ ([Akwụkwọ: Nnwale ntọala ahụ](https://zebra.zfnd.org/user/mining.html#testing-the-setup)) Tinye ihe dị n'ime faịlụ kuki gị n'ọnọdụ `__cookie__:PASSWORD`:

```bash
curl --silent --data-binary '{"jsonrpc": "1.0", "id":"curltest", "method": "getblocktemplate", "params": [] }' -H 'Content-type: application/json' http://__cookie__:PASSWORD@127.0.0.1:8232/ | jq
```

Nhazi ọrụ na-eweghachi otu `result` na-emegide ubi onye na-egwuputa ihe chọrọ, n'ime ha `previousblockhash`, `coinbasetxn`, `target` na `height`Ọ bụrụ na ị setịpụrụ `extra_coinbase_data`Akwụkwọ ahụ kọwara otu esi achọta akara gị na ya `coinbasetxn.data`.

### Tinyere ngwanrọ igwu ala gị na ya

Nye ngwanrọ igwu ala ma ọ bụ ọdọ mmiri gị njedebe RPC, `127.0.0.1:8232`, na aha njirimara na paswọọdụ sitere na kuki ahụ. Akwụkwọ ahụ kwuru na Zebra "na-akwado usoro RPC dị mkpa iji rụọ ọrụ ọtụtụ ngwanrọ ọdọ mmiri igwu ala" ([Akwụkwọ: Gbaa ọdọ mmiri igwu ala](https://zebra.zfnd.org/user/mining.html#run-a-mining-pool)).

Ihe ndetu abụọ sitere n'Akwụkwọ ahụ tupu ịhọrọ ngwanrọ:

- **s-nomp bụ maka nnwale naanị.** Akwụkwọ ahụ kpọrọ ntọala s-nomp ya "nnwale" ma kwuo na s-nomp "adakọghị na NU5, yabụ ụfọdụ ọrụ igwu ala agbanyụọla" [Nduzi netwọk ule](https://zebra.zfnd.org/user/mining-testnet-s-nomp.html) na-agbakwụnye na s-nomp "emelitebeghị ya n'ihu ọha maka mmelite netwọkụ NU5 ma ọ bụ ndị ọzọ" ma na-atụ aka na ndị na-egwupụta ihe na-emepụta ihe na ngwanrọ nke na-akwado mmelite dị ugbu a.
- **Zebra na-ebuga ihe eji eme ihe dị njikere.** Ebe nchekwa Zebra nwere ntọala Docker Compose na [`docker/mining/`](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zebra/tree/main/docker/mining) nke ahụ na-amalite Zebra yana ọdọ mmiri igwu ala, ọ bụrụkwa na ị rịọ maka ya, onye na-egwuputa CPU. Ọdọ mmiri dị na ya bụ s-nomp ma ọ na-amalite na Testnet ọ gwụla ma ị gbanwere ya, yabụ were ya dị ka ụzọ isi mụta akụkụ ndị na-agagharị.

### Ụzọ Docker

Ụzọ kacha mkpụmkpụ nke Akwụkwọ ahụ bụ `zfnd/zebra` Foto Docker, nke e tinyere adreesị na ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri RPC dị ka mgbanwe gburugburu ebe obibi ([Akwụkwọ: Ịgwuputa ihe na Zebra na Docker](https://zebra.zfnd.org/user/mining-docker.html)Iwu dị n'okpuru bụ nke Akwụkwọ ahụ nwere otu mgbanwe: ọ na-ebipụta ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri RPC naanị na adreesị loopback nke onye ọbịa ahụ, yabụ igwe ndị ọzọ agaghị erute ya.

```bash
docker run -d --name zebra_local \
  -e ZEBRA_MINING__MINER_ADDRESS="YOUR_ADDRESS" \
  -e ZEBRA_RPC__LISTEN_ADDR=0.0.0.0:8232 \
  -p 8233:8233 \
  -p 127.0.0.1:8232:8232 \
  -v zebrad-cache:/home/zebra/.cache/zebra \
  zfnd/zebra:latest
```

Nke a na-amalite Mainnet node, na-ebipụta ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri P2P (8233) na onye ọbịa Docker, ma na-eme ka ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri RPC (8232) bụrụ nke onye ọbịa ahụ nwere ike iru naanị. Bipụta kuki ahụ na:

```bash
docker exec -it zebra_local cat /home/zebra/.cache/zebra/.cookie
```

- **Debe `0.0.0.0` in `ZEBRA_RPC__LISTEN_ADDR`.** Nke ahụ bụ adreesị Zebra na-ege n'ime akpa ahụ. Docker na-ebuga ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri ebipụtara na njikọ netwọk nke akpa ahụ, yabụ Zebra na-ege ntị na `127.0.0.1` n'ime akpa ahụ agaghị enweta njikọ ahụ, ọbụlagodi site n'aka onye na-anabata ya `127.0.0.1:` n'ihu `-p` bụ ihe na-egbochi ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri ahụ ịbanye na netwọk ahụ.
- **Iwu Akwụkwọ** na-ebipụta ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri RPC na `-p 8232:8232`, nke na-emepe ya na njikọ netwọk ọ bụla nke onye ọbịa ahụ.
- **To practise on Testnet**, add `-e ZEBRA_NETWORK__NETWORK="Testnet"`, set `ZEBRA_RPC__LISTEN_ADDR=0.0.0.0:18232`, publish `-p 18233:18233` and `-p 127.0.0.1:18232:18232`, and give a Testnet address. A Mainnet address stops Zebra from starting on Testnet, and the other way round.

Ịkwụ ndị ọrụ na-egwupụta ihe na ijikwa obere akpa ahụ dị n'èzí ngalaba a. Maka akụkụ obere akpa ahụ, lee [Zallet](/zcash-tech/zallet).
