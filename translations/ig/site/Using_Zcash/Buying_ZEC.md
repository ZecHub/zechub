# Inweta Zcash

## Na nkenke

- Zụta ZEC n’ụlọ exchange na-akwado **mwepụ nke e chebere echebe [shielded]** (Gemini, BitcoinVN, flyp.me) ma ọ bụrụ na o kwe omume
- Ọtụtụ nnukwu centralized exchange (Coinbase, Kraken, Binance) na-akwado naanị **mwepụ Oha [transparent]** — chebe ZEC gị mgbe ị nwetasịrị ya
- decentralized exchange na-enye ohere maka Mgbawe [swap] na-enweghị Onye nlekota [custodial], mana ha na-ewepụkarị ego gaa na Adreesị akpa ego Oha
- Ọ dịghị gị mkpa ịzụta ZEC zuru ezu — obere akụkụ ya na-arụ ọrụ nke ọma

---

Iji Zcash mee Azụmahia [transaction] nke e chebere echebe na nzuzo, ị ga-enwerịrị ZEC. Iji nweta ZEC, ị ga-azụta ya. Ị nwere ike ịzụta ZEC n’ọtụtụ exchange dị iche iche, nke anyị ga-edepụta n’okpuru. A na-atụ aro ka ị jiri exchange na-akwado mwepụ nke e chebere echebe.

_Ị nwekwara ike ịgwụputa [mining] ZEC, mana nke ahụ anaghị ekwekarị ndị na-azụ ahịa nkịtị. More gbasara nke ahụ [ebe a](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/mining-to-shielded-addresses-community-mining-pool/50281) na [ebe a](https://zec.suprnova.cc/StartMining)_.

## Exchange na-akwado mwepụ nke e chebere echebe

**[Gemini](https://www.gemini.com)**

Gemini bụ cryptocurrency exchange a na-achịkwa, e-akpa ego [wallet], na onye nlekota nke na-eme ka ịzụta ZEC, Ether, na cryptocurrency ndị ọzọ dị mfe ma dị nchebe. Ị nwere ike iji ego fiat ma ọ bụ cryptocurrency ndị ọzọ zụta ZEC.

**[BitcoinVN](https://bitcoinvn.io)**

BitcoinVN bụ instant exchange na-enweghị Onye nlekota nke na-akwado itinye ego na iwepụ ego nke e chebere echebe (z-address) maka ZEC — otu n’ime exchange ole na ole n’ụwa niile na-akwado z-address n’uju. Ebe ọ na-arụ ọrụ kemgbe 2014, ị nwere ike ịgbanwe BTC, ETH, USDT, na cryptocurrency ndị ọzọ maka ZEC ozugbo gaa n’e-akpa ego gị nke e chebere echebe.

**[flyp.me](https://flyp.me)**

flyp.me bụ crypto exchange na-achọghị ndebanye aha. Ọ bụ otu n’ime ụzọ kacha nchebe na nke kacha nzuzo iji gbanwee cryptocurrency karịrị 30 ozugbo gaa n’e-akpa ego gị. Ị nwere ike iji cryptocurrency ndị ọzọ zụta ZEC.

## Njehie A Na-ahụkarị Ka A Zere

- **Iwepụ ego gaa na Adreesị akpa ego Oha** mgbe exchange na-akwado mwepụ nke e chebere echebe — jiri z-address gị mgbe niile ma ọ bụrụ na ọ dị
- **Ịwụpụ usoro nchebe** mgbe anabatara ego na Adreesị akpa ego Oha — jiri atụmatụ “Shield Funds” nke e-akpa ego gị ozugbo
- **Ịzụta karịa ihe dị mkpa na mbụ** — malite na obere ego ka ị mụta usoro ahụ tupu ịzụta ego buru ibu
