# Cypherpunk Zero

Cypherpunk Zero bụ usoro akụkọ gbadoro ụkwụ na Zero, onye na-agba mbọ na cypherpunk na onye na-alụ ọgụ maka nnwere onwe. Zero bi ugbu a n'ime ajọ ụwa dystopia, ọ na-ejikwa koodu alụso ndị nche ọnụ ụzọ etiti na-agbụ ọha mmadụ ọgụ. Akụkọ a sitere n'ike mmụọ nsọ nke Zcash na nkà Nzobe ederede [cryptography] nke Halo. Ọrụ okike a bụ mbọ nkwado n'etiti ECC, Stranger World, Might Jaxx, na ụfọdụ ndị mmekọ ecosystem ahọpụtara. Ọrụ okike a lekwasịrị anya karịchaa n'usoro [NFT](https://opensea.io/collection/cypherpunk-zero), e wepụtakwala [ihe egwuregwu a na-anakọta](https://mightyjaxx.com/products/cypherpunk-zero). Obodo NFT, nke na-arụsi ọrụ ike karịsịa na Twitter, emepụtala ọbụna [otu decentralized (DAO)](https://twitter.com/CypherpunkDAO) iji hazie ma mezue ọrụ na-akwado mkpọsa ahụ, yana obodo Zcash sara mbara.

## Roadmap Na-emegide Roadmap

Anyị ahụla ọtụtụ ọrụ na-ekwe nkwa gabigara ókè ma na-eweta ihe na-erughị nkwa na roadmap NFT ha. Ya mere, roadmap iwu adịghị. Tụkwasịnụ obi na anyị nwere ụfọdụ uru pụrụ iche dị ịtụnanya n'usoro maka ndị ji NFT, ụfọdụ a tụrụ anya ha, ụfọdụ a na-atụghị anya ha. Kpachapụ anya ma soro anyị n'ọgụ a.
