# Ọdachi Cypherpunk

Cypherpunk Zero bụ usoro akụkọ gbasara Zero, nwa okorobịa na-achọ ihe n'aka ndị na-alụ ọgụ maka nnwere onwe. Zero bi ugbu a n'ọnọdụ nrọ ọjọọ, ọ na-ejikwa koodu lụso ndị nche ọnụ ụzọ ámá ọgụ. Akụkọ ahụ sitere n'ike mmụọ nsọ Zcash na Halo cryptography. Ọrụ okike bụ mbọ mmekorita dị n'etiti ECC, Stranger World, Might Jaxx na ndị mmekọ gburugburu ebe obibi. Ọrụ okike ahụ lekwasịrị anya nke ọma na [Usoro NFT](https://opensea.io/collection/cypherpunk-zero) na [ihe egwuregwu a na-anakọta](https://mightyjaxx.com/products/cypherpunk-zero) a tọhapụkwara ha. Ndị otu NFT, nke na-arụsi ọrụ ike na Twitter, emeela ọbụna [otu na-agbasaghị ókè (DAO)](https://twitter.com/CypherpunkDAO) ịhazi ma mezue ọrụ ndị na-akwado mkpọsa ahụ, na obodo Zcash sara mbara.
