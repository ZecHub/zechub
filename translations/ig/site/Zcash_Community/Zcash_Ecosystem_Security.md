# Nchekwa Ecosystem Zcash

## Onye Nduzi Nchekwa Ecsystem

E guzobere ọrụ ndu nke nchekwa gburugburu ebe obibi Zcash site na onyinye ZCG iji nye injinia nchekwa raara onwe ya nye maka gburugburu ebe obibi Zcash sara mbara - ọkachasị ndị enyere onyinye ZCG - n'èzí ECC na ZF.

- **2022–2023:** [ibi n'ụwa](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-ecosystem-security-lead/42090) jere ozi dị ka onye ndu nchekwa gburugburu ebe obibi mbụ. Mụtakwuo na [zecsec.com](https://zecsec.com).
- **2024–2025:** Ahọpụtara ZCG [Ike Kachasị Ala](https://leastauthority.com) iji nọgide na-arụ ọrụ ahụ site na nke ọhụrụ [RFP](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/rfp-zcash-ecosystem-security-lead-2023/45723)Enwere ike ịchọta mmelite [Ebe a](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-update-zcash-ecosystem-security-lead/47541).
- **2026:** Ụlọ nyocha nchekwa [Taylor Hornby gbara akwụkwọ](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/shielded-labs-engages-taylor-hornby-as-security-consultant/55421) dịka onye ndụmọdụ nchekwa iji mee ka ikike nchekwa Zcash sikwuo ike.

## Atụmatụ Mkpughe Nchekwa na Nkwarụ ZCG

Ihe [Atụmatụ Mkpughe Nchekwa na Nkwarụ ZCG](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcg-security-vulnerability-disclosure-initiative/55545) na-enye usoro maka ikpughe ihe ndị na-adịghị ike nchekwa n'ofe usoro Zcash.

## Mmelite Nchekwa Ndị Na-adịbeghị Anya (2026)

- **Zebra 4.4.1 (Mee 2026):** [Ndozi nchekwa dị oke mkpa](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zebra-4-4-1-critical-security-fix/55588) ewepụtara. A na-agba ndị ọrụ node niile ume ka ha kwalite ozugbo.
- **Zebra 4.3.1 (Eprel 2026):** [Ndozi nchekwa dị mkpa, igwu ala na-akụnye ihe na ime ka CI sie ike](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zebra-4-3-1-critical-security-fixes-dockerized-mining-and-ci-hardening/55389) ewepụtara.
- **Edoziri Ọtụtụ Nsogbu (Eprel 2026):** [A rụchaala ọtụtụ nsogbu Zcash nke ọma](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/several-zcash-vulnerabilities-successfully-remediated/55388) na-enweghị mmetụta ego onye ọrụ ma ọ bụ nzuzo.
- **Ndụmọdụ zcashd (Eprel 2026):** [Ndụmọdụ iji belata elu mwakpo zcashd](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/advisory-reduce-your-zcashd-attack-surface-by-shielding-it-behind-zebra/55390) site n'ịtụgharị okporo ụzọ site na Zebra.

## Ngosipụta Ọrụ Dị Mkpa

The Electric Coin Company and Zcash Foundation both conform to this Responsible Disclosure [ọkọlọtọ](https://github.com/RD-Crypto-Spec/Responsible-Disclosure/tree/d47a5a3dafa5942c8849a93441745fdd186731e6) na mgbanwe ndị a:

> "Zcash bụ teknụzụ nke na-enye nzuzo siri ike. A na-ezochi ndetu ndị ahụ gaa ebe ha na-aga, wee debe ntọala ego site na ihe akaebe efu nke ezubere ka onye ji Zcash n'ezie mepụta. Ọ bụrụ na nke a ada ada, ma ọ bụrụ na njehie na-apụta, enwere ike iji ahụhụ adịgboroja ahụ mee ihe n'enweghị ụzọ ọ bụla maka ndị nyocha blockchain ịchọpụta onye mere ya ma ọ bụ data dị na blockchain ejiri mee ihe iji rie ahụhụ ahụ. Ya mere, mgbanwe tupu oge ahụ, dịka e mere na ụfọdụ ọrụ ndị ọzọ n'ọnọdụ ndị dị otú ahụ, agaghị ekwe omume. Ụkpụrụ ahụ na-akọwa ndị na-akọ akụkọ banyere adịghị ike gụnyere nkọwa zuru oke nke nsogbu, iji megharịa ya. Nke a dị mkpa dịka ọmụmaatụ n'ihe gbasara onye nyocha mpụga ma na-egosi ma na-egosi na enwere nsogbu nchekwa n'ezie, na nsogbu nchekwa nwere mmetụta nke ha kwuru na o nwere - na-enye ndị otu mmepe ohere itinye ihe n'ọnọdụ ziri ezi ma dozie nsogbu ahụ. Agbanyeghị, n'ihe gbasara ahụhụ adịgboroja, dịka ọ dị na CVE-2019-7167, anyị nwere ike ikpebi ịghara itinye nkọwa ndị ahụ na akụkọ anyị nye ndị mmekọ tupu mwepụta nhazi, ma ọ bụrụhaala na anyị ji n'aka na ha adịghị ike."
