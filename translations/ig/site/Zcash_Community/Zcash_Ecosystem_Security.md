# Zcash Nchekwa nke usoro gburugburu [Ecosystem Security]

## Onye Ndu Nchekwa nke usoro gburugburu

E guzobere ọrụ Onye Ndu Nchekwa nke usoro gburugburu nke Zcash site n'ikike [grant] ZCG iji nye injinia nchekwa pụrụ iche maka usoro gburugburu Zcash sara mbara — karịsịa ndị natara ikike ZCG — na-abụghị ECC na ZF.

- **2022–2023:** [earthrise](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-ecosystem-security-lead/42090) jere ozi dị ka Onye Ndu Nchekwa nke usoro gburugburu mbụ. Mụtakwuo na [zecsec.com](https://zecsec.com).
- **2024–2025:** ZCG họpụtara [Least Authority](https://leastauthority.com) ka ọ gaa n'ihu n'ọrụ ahụ site na [RFP](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/rfp-zcash-ecosystem-security-lead-2023/45723) ọhụrụ. Enwere ike ịhụ mmelite [ebe a](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-update-zcash-ecosystem-security-lead/47541).
- **2026:** Shielded Labs [were Taylor Hornby](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/shielded-labs-engages-taylor-hornby-as-security-consultant/55421) dịka onye ndụmọdụ nchekwa iji kwado ikike nchekwa nke Zcash.

## ZCG Mmemme Nchekwa na Mkpughe Adịghị Ike

Mmemme Nchekwa na Mkpughe Adịghị Ike nke [ZCG](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcg-security-vulnerability-disclosure-initiative/55545) na-enye usoro maka mkpughe a haziri ahazi nke adịghị ike nchekwa n'ofe usoro gburugburu Zcash.

## Mmelite Nchekwa Ndị Na-adịbeghị Anya (2026)

- **Zebra 4.4.1 (Mee 2026):** E wepụtara [ndozi nchekwa dị oke mkpa](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zebra-4-4-1-critical-security-fix/55588). A na-agba ndị ọrụ node niile ume ka ha kwalite ozugbo.
- **Zebra 4.3.1 (Eprel 2026):** E wepụtara [ndozi nchekwa dị oke mkpa, ịgwọpụta [mining] nke ejiri Docker rụọ na ime ka CI sie ike](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zebra-4-3-1-critical-security-fixes-dockerized-mining-and-ci-hardening/55389).
- **Edozila Ọtụtụ Adịghị Ike (Eprel 2026):** E mechiri nke ọma ọtụtụ adịghị ike [Zcash](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/several-zcash-vulnerabilities-successfully-remediated/55388) na-enweghị imetụta ego ndị ọrụ ma ọ bụ Nzuzo [privacy].
- **Ndụmọdụ zcashd (Eprel 2026):** [Ndụmọdụ iji belata ebe mwakpo zcashd](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/advisory-reduce-your-zcashd-attack-surface-by-shielding-it-behind-zebra/55390) site n'ịduzi okporo data gafee Zebra.

## Mkpughe Nwere Ọrụ

Electric Coin Company na Zcash Foundation ha abụọ na-agbaso ụkpụrụ [Mkpughe Nwere Ọrụ](https://github.com/RD-Crypto-Spec/Responsible-Disclosure/tree/d47a5a3dafa5942c8849a93441745fdd186731e6) a, ma e wezụga ihe ndị a:

> "Zcash bụ teknụzụ nke na-enye Nzuzo siri ike. A na-eji Nzobe ederede [encryption] zoo note [note] ruo ebe ha na-aga, ma a na-echekwa ntọala ego ahụ site na proof zero-knowledge [zero-knowledge] e mere ka naanị onye ji Zcash n'ezie nwee ike ịmepụta ha. Ọ bụrụ na nke a ada ada, ma ahụhụ ịkpụ ego adịgboroja apụta, enwere ike irigbu ahụhụ ịkpụ ego adịgboroja ahụ n'enweghị ụzọ ndị na-enyocha blockchain [blockchain] ga-esi mata onye mere ya ma ọ bụ data dị na blockchain ejirila rigbuo ahụhụ ahụ. Ya mere, iweghachi ọnọdụ azụ tupu oge ahụ, dịka e mere n'ọrụ ndị ọzọ ụfọdụ n'ọnọdụ ndị dị otu a, agaghị ekwe omume. Ụkpụrụ ahụ na-akọwa na ndị na-akọ adịghị ike ga-agụnye nkọwa zuru ezu banyere nsogbu, iji mepụtaghachi ya. Nke a dị mkpa, dịka ọmụmaatụ, mgbe onye nyocha nọ n'èzí na-egosi ma na-egosikwa proof na nsogbu nchekwa dị n'ezie, nakwa na nsogbu nchekwa ahụ nwere mmetụta ọ sịrị na o nwere — na-enye ndị otu mmepe ohere ibute nsogbu ahụ ụzọ nke ọma ma dozie ya. Otú ọ dị, n'ihe banyere ahụhụ ịkpụ ego adịgboroja, dịka ọ dị na CVE-2019-7167, anyị nwere ike ikpebi ịghara itinye nkọwa ndị ahụ na akụkọ anyị na-ezigara ndị mmekọ tupu mwepụta a haziri ahazi, ma ọ bụrụhaala na anyị ji n'aka na ha nwere ike imetụta."
