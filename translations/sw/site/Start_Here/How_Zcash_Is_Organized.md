# Jinsi Zcash imeandaliwa

## TL;DR

- Zcash haijajengwa na kampuni moja, imejengwa kwa mashirika mengi huru ambayo kila mmoja anamiliki sehemu tofauti ya kazi hiyo.
- Kwa zaidi ya historia yake mashirika mawili kuongozwa maendeleo, Electric Coin Company na Zcash Foundation
- In January 2026 the entire Electric Coin Company team resigned after a governance dispute, and the ecosystem reorganized into several independent teams
- Leo itifaki, programu node, pochi, utafiti, kuongeza ukubwa na fedha ni kushughulikiwa kwa vikundi tofauti
- Hakuna shirika moja kudhibiti Zcash, mtandao ni wazi chanzo na ruhusa-bure, na kuendelea kufanya kazi kawaida kupitia kila mabadiliko

## Kwa nini hilo ni jambo la maana?

Kuelewa muundo hufanya kila kitu kingine rahisi. Inakuambia ni nani anayesimamia msimbo unaotegemea, ambaye unapaswa kumkaribia kwa ruzuku, na anayewajibika kwa sehemu ya mtandao unayojali. Pia inaonyesha mojawapo nguvu za Zcash: kwa sababu kazi imeenea katika vikundi huru, hakuna hatua moja ya kutofaulu inaweza kukamata au kusimamisha mradi huo.

## Jinsi ilivyokuwa ikifanya kazi zamani

Kwa historia ya Zcash, mashirika mawili yaliongoza.

The Electric Coin Company launched Zcash in 2016 and employed much of the core development team. It was overseen by Bootstrap, a nonprofit board created to support Zcash. The Zcash Foundation worked alongside it as an independent nonprofit, focused on stewardship of the protocol and on building an independent node. Both were funded largely by a portion of the block reward set aside for development.

This two-pillar structure held for years, but it depended on that shared funding and on the two organizations staying aligned. As the original development funding evolved and its long term future became less certain, the question of how to pay for ongoing work grew more pressing. That funding question sits in the background of much of what changed next, and it is part of why some teams now raise outside capital while others rely on grants.

<br/>

## 2026 marekebisho ya shirika la

In January 2026 the structure changed sharply. On January 7, Electric Coin Company chief executive Josh Swihart announced on X that the entire company team had resigned.

Bootstrap was a nonprofit created in 2020 to govern the Electric Coin Company, which had become a wholly owned subsidiary of it. The disagreement between the company team and this board built up over time and touched several issues, including the direction of the organization, how development should be funded, and the future of the Zashi wallet, which the team wanted to move into a private company to raise outside capital. Swihart described the departure as a constructive discharge, a legal term meaning conditions were changed so severely that resignation was effectively forced, and said a majority of the board had moved out of alignment with Zcash's mission.

Wakati huohuo, kampuni ya Zcash ilitoa taarifa rasmi kwa serikali kwamba ilikuwa imefanya kazi na watu wazima katika eneo hilo. Kampuni hiyo ilisema kuwa haikuwa ikichukua hatua yoyote dhidi yao wakati walipokuwa wakiendelea kufanya biashara zao za fedha bila kujali hali halisi ya maisha yao au jinsi walivyojiendesha.

Two things were not in dispute. No party alleged any criminal conduct, so this was a corporate and governance disagreement rather than a legal case. And the Zcash network itself was unaffected, it stayed open source, permissionless, secure, and fully operational throughout, a point both Swihart and Wilcox stressed to users.

What followed was reorganization rather than collapse. The former company team went on to form ZODL later in 2026, and separately three former Bootstrap board members formed Sovright. Development settled into a more distributed shape across several independent teams.

Taarifa zilizoelezwa hapa zilitolewa hadharani kwenye X mnamo Januari 7, 2026, na Josh Swihart (@jswihart) na Zooko Wilcox (@zooko), ambapo machapisho ya asili yanaweza kusomwa kwa ukamilifu.

## Nani hujenga Zcash sasa?

Kazi leo imegawanywa katika mashirika huru, kila mmoja akiwa na sehemu iliyo wazi.

### Mashirika mawili kutoka 2026 kugawanywa

1. ZODL, the Zcash Open Development Lab, was formed by the former Electric Coin Company team and led by Josh Swihart. It raised more than twenty five million dollars from outside investors and works on core protocol development, including the Halo 2 proving system that powers Zcash's newest shielded transactions, and on the ZODL wallet, a shielded by default mobile wallet formerly called Zashi. See [ZODL](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zodl).
2. Sovright is a nonprofit formed by three former Bootstrap board members. It focuses on tools and support for the ecosystem, and built Argos, a tool to help early users recover funds stranded in an old, unmaintained wallet. See [Sovright](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/sovright).

### Usimamizi wa itifaki, utafiti na programu ya node

3. Zcash Foundation inaendelea Zebra, node ya kutu ambayo inakuwa mtandao wa msingi node kama mteja zamani zcashd ni kustaafu. Pia stewards shirika la GitHub za Zcash, tovuti z.cash na akaunti kuu ya Zcash kwenye X, na washirika na ZecHub kusaidia kusimamia baadhi ya mali hizo Tazama. [Zcash Foundation](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zcash-foundation).
4. Shielded Labs is an independent, donation-funded nonprofit based in Switzerland. It focuses on research and long term sustainability, including the network sustainability mechanism that funds future development and the Crosslink work on adding proof of stake finality to Zcash, and it funded the security audit that discovered the Orchard pool vulnerability in 2026. See [Maabara ya Kuhifadhiwa](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/shielded-labs).
5. The Electric Coin Company remains part of the history as the organization that created and launched Zcash in 2016. See [Electric Coin Company](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/electric-coin-company).

### Scaling na cryptography

6. Mradi Tachyon ni jitihada za kuongeza ukubwa ulioongozwa na cryptographer Sean Bowe. Inapendekeza njia mpya kwa pochi ili kuunganisha na blockchain, inayoitwa usawazishaji wa oblivious, ambayo hupunguza shughuli na kama athari ya upande, huhamisha Zcash kuelekea faragha baada ya quantum. Kazi yake imeandikwa katika jarida la Utafiti juu ya Usawa wa Quantum (CQS) mnamo Aprili 2017. [tachyon.z.cash](https://tachyon.z.cash/).
7. The Valar Group is a cryptography research and engineering lab working on the Zcash protocol for private, post-quantum digital cash at scale. It collaborates closely with Project Tachyon on the scaling and quantum work. More on its work is at [valargroup.dev (kiungo cha kiunganishi)](https://valargroup.dev/).

### Mashirika ya kikanda na jamii

8. Obscura Labs ni shirika huru lililosajiliwa nchini Nigeria, linalolenga Afrika na masoko yanayoibuka kwa kujenga miundombinu ya ujenzi wa viwanda vya vifaa. Tazama: [Obscura Labs](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/obscura-labs).

### Elimu ya juu

9. ZecHub is a decentralized education hub for Zcash. Community members work together to create, validate, and promote content that helps people understand the ecosystem and learn how to participate, through tutorials, wiki documentation, a podcast, and a weekly newsletter. The wiki you are reading now is part of ZecHub, and the Zcash Foundation partners with it to help manage some community resources.

### Ufadhili wa kifedha

10. Zcash Community Grants funds independent contributors and community projects from a portion of the block reward, spreading work across many teams beyond the core organizations. See [Zcash Misaada ya Jumuiya](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zcash-community-grants).
11. The Financial Privacy Foundation inasaidia mfumo wa mazingira na miradi ya jamii Zcash. Tazama [Msingi wa Faragha ya Fedha](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/financial-privacy-foundation).

All of these organizations maintain open source repositories, so their work can be read, checked, and built on by anyone. And the organizations are not the whole story. Many significant contributions come from individuals and from contracted companies funded through grants, rather than from the core organizations alone. Alongside them sit wallet teams, regional communities, independent developers, and investors who hold and support ZEC without building the protocol. The list above is the backbone, not the full picture.

## Mahali pa kuanza kama mgeni mpya

Shirika ambalo ni muhimu kwako hutegemea kile unachotaka kufanya.

1. Kutumia Zcash, unahitaji mkoba, hivyo ZODL na mfuko wake ni hatua ya asili kuanzia.
2. Kuendesha node au kuelewa programu ya mtandao, kuangalia kwa Zcash Foundation na Node yake Zebra.
3. Ili kufadhili mradi au kuchangia kazi ya kulipwa, angalia Zcash Community Grants.
4. Kufuatia utafiti na siku zijazo za itifaki, fuata Shielded Labs, Mradi Tachyon, na Kikundi cha Valar.

## Maoni yasiyo sahihi ya kawaida

- Zcash si inayomilikiwa au kudhibitiwa na kampuni yoyote moja, hakuna shirika inaweza kubadilisha au kuacha mtandao peke yake
- 2026 mzozo hakuwa na kuathiri mtandao, fedha au faragha, ilikuwa kutoelewana wa shirika, na itifaki mbio kawaida katika yote ya
- Timu ya kuondoka Electric Coin Company hakuwa mwisho Zcash, kazi wakiongozwa na mashirika mapya huru
- Kuwa na mashirika mengi ni nguvu, si udhaifu, inaondoa pointi moja ya kushindwa na inaweka mradi ujasiri
- Kuweka au kukuza ZEC si sawa na kujenga Zcash, wawekezaji na watangazaji ni sehemu ya jamii lakini tofauti kutoka kwa timu zinazoendeleza itifaki hiyo.
