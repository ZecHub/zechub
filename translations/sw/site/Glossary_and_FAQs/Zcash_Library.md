| PCD (Proof-Carrying Data) | A primitive where data travels alongside a proof of its own correctness, so combining data also combines the proofs. Project Tachyon rebuilds the shielded protocol around PCD, letting each wallet carry a recursive proof that its own balance is correct instead of rescanning the chain. The Zcash implementation is [Ragu](https://github.com/tachyon-zcash/ragu), ambayo inafuata Halo na inahitaji hakuna kuaminika kuweka.
Mitandao ya Peer-to-Peer mitandao P2P ni msingi wa dhana ya utengamano usanifu wa msingi wa teknolojia blockchain.
PIR (Private Information Retrieval) mbinu ambayo kuruhusu kupata rekodi kutoka server bila ya kujifunza seva ambayo kumbukumbu uliomba. Chini utafiti wa kazi kwa Zcash kama njia kwa ajili ya mkoba mwanga kuokoa kile wanachohitaji bila kuvuja nini wao ni kuangalia kwa.
