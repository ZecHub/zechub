# <img src="/content-images/image-2024-02-03-174147713-63a42e536c.webp" alt="Alt Text" width="400"/> Zcash Community Links

Jumuiya ya Zcash ni kundi la watu wanaofanya kazi kwa bidii ili kufanya ZEC kuwa mojawapo ya sarafu za siri zinazotumiwa sana ulimwenguni. Jamii hiyo inaundwa na watu tofauti kutoka kote duniani - watengenezaji, waalimu, watetezi, na wajenzi-waliounganishwa na imani iliyoshirikiwa katika faragha ya kifedha.

----

## Ambapo unaweza kupata wanajamii wa jamii

### Zcash Jamii Forum

Makala ya kwanza. [Zcash Jamii Forum](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/) is the hub for long-form discussions on Zcash - governance, grant proposals, technical debate, and ecosystem announcements. Now stewardship has transferred to the Zcash Foundation following ECC's transition to ZODL.
