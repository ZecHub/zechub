# <img src="/content-images/image-2024-02-03-174147713-63a42e536c.webp" alt="Alt Text" width="400"/> Zcash Community Links

Jumuiya ya Zcash ni kundi lenye nguvu la watu wanaofanya kazi ili kuifanya ZEC kuwa mojawapo ya sarafu za kidijitali zinazohifadhi faragha zinazotumiwa sana duniani. Jumuiya hiyo imeundwa na watu mbalimbali kutoka kote ulimwenguni - watengenezaji, waelimishaji, watetezi, na wajenzi - waliounganishwa na imani ya pamoja katika faragha ya kifedha.

----

### Jukwaa la Jumuiya ya Zcash

Ya [Jukwaa la Jumuiya ya Zcash](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/) is the hub for long-form discussions on Zcash - governance, grant proposals, technical debate, and ecosystem announcements. Now stewardship has transferred to the Zcash Foundation following ECC's transition to ZODL.
