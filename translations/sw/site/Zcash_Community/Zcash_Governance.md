# Zcash Fedha & Utawala Maelezo ya jumla

Zcash ya on-chain fedha mfano, block tuzo utaratibu, na majukumu ya mashirika makubwa

## 1. Jinsi Zcash Block Rewards Kazi

Zcash ni uthibitisho wa kazi cryptocurrency. Kila block kuchimbwa inashiriki yake **block ruzuku** (ZEC wapya kuundwa) pamoja na ada ya manunuzi kulingana na kanuni fasta itifaki kuweka kwa upgrades mtandao.

- **Mfano wa sasa (baada ya NU6 / Novemba 2024 na kuendelea)** 
  Kama ya Aprili 2026 usambazaji ni:

Mpokeaji asilimia fedha/hali.
|--------------------------------|------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
Wachimbaji. 80% Wauzaji wa moja kwa moja malipo ya kuzuia wachimbaji
Zcash Community Grants (ZCG) 8% Msaada wa jamii (inaendelea hadi ~2028)
Lockbox (protocol-controlled) 12% Fedha zinakusanyika; hakuna utaratibu wa matumizi bado, kura ya jamii inayohitajika baadaye.

- ** Pre-NU6 historia ya mfuko wa dev (2020 - Nov 2024)** 
  20% ya kila ruzuku ilienda moja kwa moja kwenye mashirika ya maendeleo:

  - 7% -> Electric Coin Company (ECC) / Bootstrap Mradi wa 
  - 5% -> Zcash Foundation (ZF) 
  - 8% -> Zcash Community Grants (ZCG) - Msaada wa Jumuiya ya Kiuchumi kwa ajili ya Biashara za Kimataifa.

Hii 20% "mali ya maendeleo" ilibadilishwa na 8% ZCG + 12% lockbox mfano kupitia [ZIP 1015](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1015).

### Mapendekezo ya Maendeleo: ZIP 1016 - Jumuiya na Coinholder Funding Model
ZIP 1016 (ilipendekezwa Februari 2025, hali: Ilipendekezwa) inaanzisha mfano wa ufadhili uliodhibitiwa zaidi. Ingekuwa:
- Kuendelea 8% ya mgawo kwa ZCG.
- Kubadilisha 12% lockbox katika "Coinholder-Kidhibitiwa Mfuko" (mbegu na fedha zilizopo lockbox + unaendelea 12% misaada ya kundi).
- Kuamsha mtindo huu mpaka nusu ya tatu (takriban miaka 3).
- Kuwawezesha wamiliki wa sarafu za ZEC kupiga kura kila robo mwaka juu ya misaada kupitia mchakato uliofafanuliwa na jamii (wingi rahisi, kiwango cha chini cha quorum ya 420,000 ZEC).
- Require Key-Holder Organizations (currently including ZF and Shielded Labs, with Bootstrap/ECC referenced in grant contexts) to administer disbursements via multisig, bound by legal agreements and coin-holder decisions.
- Kudumisha ZIP 1015 mahitaji yote juu ya matumizi lockbox (fadhili misaada mazingira).

This proposal aims to shift from organization-controlled to direct coin-holder governance for the 12% allocation. It does not alter the ZIP process or trademark rules.

## 2. Mashirika ya msingi na vyanzo vyao fedha

** Electric Coin Company (ECC) / Bootstrap Mradi** 
- Waumbaji wa awali wa Zcash (2016). 
- Kihistoria kupokea ~ 7% ya mfuko dev hadi Novemba 2024. 
- In January 2026, the core engineering and product team resigned from Bootstrap/ECC due to governance disputes and formed the Zcash Open Development Lab (ZODL).  
- ECC/Bootstrap haipati tena fedha za moja kwa moja na haitumii timu ya msingi ya maendeleo. Inategemea michango, udhamini, na hazina yake mwenyewe. 
- Ana umuhimu wa kihistoria lakini si tena shirika la maendeleo ya itifaki hai. 
-> Angalia maelezo kamili: [Electric Coin Company](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/electric-coin-company)

** Zcash Open Maabara ya maendeleo (ZODL) ** 
- Formed in January 2026 by the original Zcash protocol developers (the core ECC engineering and product team) after they left Bootstrap/ECC.  
- Kupanda zaidi ya $25 milioni katika ufadhili mbegu kutoka wawekezaji kubwa ikiwa ni pamoja na a16z Crypto na Coinbase Ventures. 
- The team, consisting of the original inventors and developers of the Zcash protocol, continues core protocol development, ZIP contributions, and privacy-focused tools including the Zodl mobile wallet (rebranded from Zashi).  
- Hakuna moja kwa moja kwenye mzunguko wa mkataba fedha; kazi kama VC-mkono huru maabara ililenga kuendeleza Zcash faragha miundombinu. 
-> Angalia maelezo kamili: [ZODL](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/ZODL)  
-> Tovuti rasmi: [zodl.com](https://zodl.com/)
  
**Zcash Foundation (ZF)** 
- Independent 501 ((c) ((3) lisilo la faida linalenga miundombinu, programu ya node, utafiti na afya ya mazingira. 
- Kihistoria kupokea 5% ya mfuko dev. 
- No longer receives direct protocol funding post-NU6. Relies on donations and grants.  
- Inamiliki alama ya biashara Zcash (iliyotolewa na ECC mnamo 2019) na ina jukumu muhimu katika utawala. 
- Anaendesha Jopo la Ushauri wa Jumuiya ya Zcash (ZCAP) na husaidia kuwezesha uchaguzi wa jamii. 
- Hufanya kama Shirika-Mmiliki wa Ufunguo chini ya ZIP iliyopendekezwa 1016. 
-> Angalia maelezo kamili: [Zcash Foundation](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zcash-foundation)  
-> Tovuti rasmi: [zfnd.org](https://zfnd.org/)

**Zcash misaada ya Jumuiya (ZCG)** 
- Programu ya Zcash Community Grants inawadhibiti timu na miradi huru ili kufanya maendeleo makubwa yanayoendelea na kazi nyingine kwa manufaa ya umma wa mazingira ya ZCash. 
- Msaada wa kifedha huamuliwa na kamati iliyochaguliwa katika jumuiya. 
- Inaendelea kupokea kamili 8% ya block tuzo (baada NU6), kusimamiwa kupitia Financial Privacy Foundation. 
- Misaada ya kifedha hutolewa kupitia maombi na mchakato wa kupiga kura wazi kwa jamii. 
-> Angalia maelezo kamili: [Zcash Misaada ya Jumuiya](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zcash-community-grants)  
-> Tovuti rasmi: [zcashcommunitygrants.org/ - Ni kweli, ni ukweli!](https://zcashcommunitygrants.org/)

**Fundation ya Faragha Fedha (FPF)** 
- Shirika lisilo la faida lililoingizwa katika Visiwa vya Cayman. 
- Receives the 8% block subsidy allocation directly from the protocol (per ZIP 1015) and handles all legal, financial, and operational administration for the Zcash Community Grants program.  
- Inatoa mfumo wa mwavuli na msaada wa kiutawala kwa shughuli za ZCG, ikiwa ni pamoja na malipo ya fedha, mikataba, na kufuata. 
- ZCG inafanya kazi kama chombo cha kujitegemea kilichochaguliwa na jamii chini ya mwavuli wa FPF. 
-> Angalia maelezo kamili: [Msingi wa Faragha ya Fedha](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/financial-privacy-foundation)  
-> Tovuti rasmi: [financialprivacyfoundation.org/ - Ni nini?](https://www.financialprivacyfoundation.org/)

** Maabara ya Kuhifadhiwa** 
- Independent, mchango unaofadhiliwa Zcash msaada shirika makao yake makuu katika Uswisi. 
- The first organization in the Zcash ecosystem that has never received direct or indirect funding from the Development Fund or block rewards.  
- Inazingatia mipango ambayo inawafaidi wamiliki wa ZEC na inapeana kipaumbele sauti ya mmiliki katika kuunda mwelekeo wa Zcash. 
- Hufanya kama Shirika la Mmiliki-Muhimu chini ya ZIP iliyopendekezwa 1016 kwa usimamizi wa Mfuko unaodhibitiwa na Coinholder. 
- Inachangia maendeleo ya itifaki, mchakato wa ZIP na utawala (wakilishi wa mhariri wa Zip). 
-> Angalia maelezo kamili: [Maabara ya Kuhifadhiwa](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/shielded-labs)  
-> Tovuti rasmi: [shieldedlabs.net](https://shieldedlabs.net/)

## 3. Utawala - Jinsi Maamuzi Yanavyofanywa

Utawala wa Zcash ni mchanganyiko wa "sheria za itifaki ya on-chain" na "makubaliano ya kijamii nje ya mnyororo":

1. ** Mchakato wa ZIP (Mapendekezo ya Uboreshaji wa Zcash) ** 
   - Mtu yeyote anaweza kuwasilisha ZIP. 
   - Mjadala wa umma kwenye vikao, Discord, GitHub. 
   - Wahariri wa ZIP (kwa sasa Jack Grigg, Daira-Emma Hopwood, Kris Nuttycombe katika uwezo binafsi, Arya kutoka ZF, na wawakilishi kutoka Shielded Labs) kupitia upya na kuamua kukubalika. 
   - ZIPs kukubalika ni pamoja na katika kuboresha mtandao ijayo.

2. **Mkataba wa Alama ya Biashara (2019-2024)** 
   - ECC ilitoa alama ya biashara Zcash kwa ZF mnamo 2019. 
   - Mkataba awali required makubaliano ya pande zote mbili kutoka ECC na ZF kwa ajili yoyote kuboresha mtandao kujenga mpya itifaki maelewano. 
   - Mnamo Aprili 2024 ECC ilitangaza nia ya kumaliza; ilani rasmi ya kukomesha ilitolewa Agosti 2024. 
   - As of 2025, ZF is the sole steward of the Zcash trademark and has adopted a new permissive trademark policy reflecting ecosystem decentralization. The trademark no longer functions as a governance veto mechanism.

3. ** Zcash Jopo la Ushauri wa Jumuiya (ZCAP) ** 
   - Kikundi cha wataalamu wa mazingira. 
   - Kutumiwa kwa ajili ya uchaguzi wa jamii yasiyo na wajibu juu ya maamuzi makubwa.

4. ** On-chain Ratification** (Uthibitisho wa Mfululizo) 
   - Mara baada ya kuboresha mtandao ni kupelekwa, wengi wa kasi hash mtandao lazima kukubali yake (hakuna ngumu-fork hatari kama makubaliano imefikiwa).

5. ** Uelekeo wa baadaye - Lockbox & ZIP 1016** 
   - Fedha 12% lockbox ni kukusanya katika itifaki. 
   - ZIP 1016 proposes converting this into a Coinholder-Controlled Fund with quarterly coin-holder voting and multisig administration by Key-Holder Organizations (ZF and Shielded Labs currently noted).
