# NU6.2

> NU6.2 ilianza kutumika kwenye mtandao wa Zcash katika block 3,364,600 (Juni 3, 2026 UTC).

Nini utachukua mbali: nini NU6.2 fasta, kwa Zcash paused ulinzi shughuli kwa karibu siku moja, na jinsi mtandao kufungwa shimo bila kupoteza fedha yoyote.

NU6.2 ni Zcash [kuboresha mtandao](../start-here/network-upgrades), rasmi ilivyoelezwa katika [ZIP 257 (Kifungo cha posta)](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0257), ambayo re-enabled Orchard ulinzi itifaki baada ya pause dharura. Ni kusahihishwa soundness mdudu katika mzunguko sifuri maarifa Orchard na kurejeshwa [iliyohifadhiwa na kioo](../using-zcash/shielded-pools) Fikiria ni kama sura ya dharura-marekebisho ya hadithi Orchard mdudu. kufuatilia upgrading, ambayo inaonekana kuwa na matokeo mazuri sana katika biashara yako kwa ajili ya fedha za mikopo. [Ironwood (NU6.3)](../zcash-tech/ironwood), kisha aliongeza bwawa mpya na ukaguzi wa umma ya zamani.

Why this matters. Orchard is where most private Zcash payments happen. When a flaw was found in the math that keeps that pool honest, the network faced a hard choice: keep shielded transactions running with a known risk, or pause them until the fix was ready. NU6.2 is how Zcash chose safety first and still restored privacy within a day.

![NU6.2 emergency timeline: the Orchard bug was found on May 29, Orchard was paused by an emergency soft fork on June 2, and NU6.2 re-enabled it on a corrected circuit on June 3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-2-timeline.png)

## Ni nini kilichotokea?

Trigger ilikuwa soundness mdudu katika Orchard ya Action mzunguko, hesabu ambayo ni lazima kuthibitisha kila shughuli ulinzi hutii sheria. Independent utafiti Taylor Hornby aligundua wakati wa uchunguzi na Daktari Dr. [Maabara ya Kuhifadhiwa](../zcash-organizations/shielded-labs) Zcash alikuwa ameketi bila kuonekana tangu Orchard ilizinduliwa mwaka 2022, na kinadharia inaweza basi shughuli batili kupita kama halali, ambayo ingeweza kuruhusu fedha kuibiwa au mara mbili alitumia ndani ya bwawa orchard. uhasibu turntile wa zcash bado capped kiasi gani thamani anaweza milele kuacha Orchard, hivyo jumla za usambazaji ZEC yenyewe haingeweza inflated, lakini kasoro katika math kwamba anaendelea akiba ulinzi ni kubwa, kwa hiyo mtandao alitenda ndani ya siku. [Mti wa chuma](../zcash-tech/ironwood) ukurasa anaelezea mdudu na jinsi inaweza kujenga thamani katika kamili.

## Kusitisha dharura

Mnamo Juni 2, 2026, uma laini dharura kwa muda walemavu shughuli zote za Orchard. Iliyotumwa kama hotfix katika zcashd v6.12.5 na zebra v4.5.3.

1. Shielded Orchard hutuma na kupokea walikuwa paused, hivyo hakuna ushahidi mpya wa orchard inaweza kuundwa au kukubalika.
2. Uwazi na shughuli za Sapling ziliendelea kufanya kazi kawaida.
3. Hakuna fedha walikuwa wakiongozwa, waliohifadhiwa au waliopotea. pause tu kusimamishwa shughuli mpya Orchard wakati fix halisi ilikuwa kumaliza.

![What paused and what kept working: transparent and Sapling transactions ran the whole time, while only Orchard paused for about a day between June 2 and June 3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-2-what-paused.png)

## Nini NU6.2 iliyopita

Siku iliyofuata, Juni 3, 2026, NU6.2 hard fork ilianzishwa katika block ya 3.364.600 na kuifanya tena Orchard kwenye mzunguko uliosahihishwa.

![The NU6.2 circuit fix: the flawed circuit had a missing constraint and an old verifying key, and NU6.2 added the constraint to produce a corrected circuit with a new verifying key that rejects bad proofs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-2-the-fix.png)

1. Ni fasta Action mzunguko wa variable-msingi scalar kuzidisha gadget kwa kuongeza vikwazo kukosa.
2. Hiyo ilitokeza ufunguo mpya wa kuthibitisha, hivyo uthibitisho uliofanywa chini ya mzunguko wa zamani usiofaa haukubaliki tena, na kila ushahidi mpya lazima utumie ule uliosahihishwa.
3. Ilifanya urefu sahihi wa uthibitisho kuwa sheria ya makubaliano, ikifunga pengo ambalo halikufanywa kabla.

Kanuni kusahihishwa shipped katika zcashd v6.20.0 na zebra v5.0.0, kujengwa juu ya halo2_gadgets v0.5.0 na bustani crate v0.14.0.

## Nini NU6.2 hakufanya nini?

NU6.2 kupata mzunguko kwa kila shughuli mpya, lakini thamani kuundwa chini ya sheria za zamani bado ameketi katika bwawa Orchard. Ukaguzi kwamba thamani na hoja yake kwa pool safi ni kazi tofauti, na hiyo ndiyo nini [Ironwood (NU6.3)](../zcash-tech/ironwood) kushughulikia na yake mpya pool na turnstile.

## Je, kuna kitu chochote kilichopotea?

Hakuna ushahidi mdudu alikuwa milele kutumika, hakuna uundaji wa thamani ruhusa na hakuna athari kwa fedha za watumiaji. uhasibu turntile Zcash ya alithibitisha jumla ugavi alibakia intact katika tukio zima.

## FAQs

Did I lose access to my ZEC? No. For about a day, shielded Orchard transactions were paused, but no funds were lost or at risk, and transparent and Sapling transactions kept working.

Kwa nini pause badala ya patching kimya? mdudu soundness inaweza basi fedha kuibiwa au mara mbili alitumia ndani ya bwawa Orchard, hivyo hatua salama ilikuwa kuacha mpya shughuli orchard mpaka mzunguko kusahihishwa alikuwa hai.

Je, zamani ulinzi ushahidi bado kazi? uthibitisho kufanywa chini ya mzunguko wa kale tena kuthibitisha baada NU6.2. pochi juu ya kutolewa mkono moja kwa moja kutumia mzungko kusahihishwa.

Je bug milele kutumika? Hakuna ushahidi ilikuwa. Ilikuwa hawakupata na utafiti wa usalama na fasta kabla ya madhara yoyote inayojulikana.

NU6.2 ni tofauti na Ironwood? NU6.2, kusahihishwa mzunguko na re-enabled Orchard. [Ironwood (NU6.3)](../zcash-tech/ironwood) kisha aliongeza bwawa mpya na turntile umma kukagua na kuhamisha thamani ya zamani pool.

## Jaribu uelewevu wako

Kwa nini Zcash kusimamisha shughuli Orchard kwa siku badala ya kuacha yao mbio wakati fix iliandikwa?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Kwa sababu kasoro ilikuwa bug soundness, maana shughuli batili inaweza kupita kama halali. Kuondoka Orchard wazi ingekuwa kushika mlango wazi kwa wizi na matumizi mara mbili ndani ya bwawa. Pausing mpya mikataba orchard kufungwa kwamba mlango mpaka mzunguko kusahihishwa kuanzishwa katika NU6.2.
</details>
