# Jinsi mbali Zcash imefika juu ya kutoa taarifa za kuchagua, na nini kushoto

On August 17 the Zcash Community Grants committee met to consider a proposal called the Shielded Compliance Bridge. Deborah Olaboye, an open-source contributor whose work includes Ethereum's execution-specs repository, had built a proof of concept that ingests a unified full viewing key, scans compact blocks against a testnet indexer, and carries a "compliance reference" in a Zcash memo field linking a shielded transfer to an off-chain Travel Rule record. She asked for a grant to finish it, but the committee declined.

Makala ya kwanza. [sababu ya kuamua mambo](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-community-grants-meeting-minutes-8-17-2026/57119) Mkutano huo umekataa mapendekezo mengine kwa sababu sawa, moja ya "programmatible kuchagua kutoa taarifa" virtual mashine na faragha na imani usanifu mfumo, ambayo ni pamoja ramani ya masuala ya kisheria Marekani.

Of course, these decisions are not a sign that nobody wants this. Considered alongside the rest of the year, it is the opposite. Binance made a protocol-level ask in 2024 and had a ZIP within the year. Gemini has run shielded withdrawals under regulatory supervision since 2020. The ecosystem stood up a Washington policy organization in June and an institutional-integration shop in August. What the committee said, in effect, is that the need has not yet been written down as a requirement anyone can build to.

## Jinsi mfumo hufanya kazi

A shielded Zcash address is controlled by a spending key. Derived from it are viewing keys that can read transaction data without being able to move funds. The full viewing key sees incoming and outgoing transactions for the account, the incoming viewing key sees only received funds, and the outgoing viewing key lets a sender reconstruct what they sent. Since [ZIP 316 - Ujumbe wa posta.](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0316), hizi ni bundled katika kuunganishwa kuangalia funguo, moja kamili na moja ya kuingia, mdogo kwa akaunti, ambapo akaunti ni nafasi katika mfuko wa mti muhimu chini ya [ZIP 32 - Ujumbe wa posta.](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0032). Muungano kamili kuangalia muhimu bundles per-pool vipengele kwa kila kulindwa pool akaunti anatumia, na pia ina transparent kupanuliwa umma ufunguo wa akaunti hiyo, hivyo kugawana moja exposes mnyororo uwazi kama vile historia ya ulinzi.

Kuangalia muhimu ni katika maana hii retroactive. decrypts historia ya akaunti nzima kutoka wakati akaunti iliundwa, na haiwezi kuondolewa mara moja pamoja, short wa kusonga fedha kwa akaunti mpya. Hakuna kila shughuli kuangalia ufunguo na hakuna muda mdogo mmoja. Josh Swihart, mtendaji mkuu wa ZODL, kampuni iliyoanzishwa mwaka huu na itifaki zamani ECC timu uhandisi, alithibitisha hili, pia kutaja kwamba per-transaction malipo kutoa taarifa "si kutekelezwa mahali popote".

zcashd, awali kumbukumbu node, kusafirishwa njia mbili RPC, `z_getpaymentdisclosure` na `z_validatepaymentdisclosure`, kwamba basi mtumaji kuthibitisha malipo moja kwa mtu wa tatu. The [Nyaraka za RPC](https://zcash.github.io/rpc/z_getpaymentdisclosure.html) alama yao majaribio, walemavu isipokuwa operator kuweka bendera ya vipengele vya majaripio na kutekeleza "kazi katika maendeleo Malipo Disclosure ZIP". kwamba ZIP, [303](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0303), sasa ni alama kuondolewa, na mrithi wake, [ZIP 311 - Ujumbe wa posta.](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0311), bado rasimu na hakuna utekelezaji zilizoambatanishwa. mbinu kufunikwa tu Sprout bwawa, 2016 awali kulindwa pool, na kamwe walikuwa kupanuliwa kwa Sapling au Orchard, ambayo ni kusema kwa mabwawa kwamba kubeba kila shughuli kisasa ulinzi yenyewe. zcashd mwenyewe [alifikia mwisho wa maisha yake.](https://zcash.github.io/zcash/user/end-of-life.html) Julai 18, 2026, kusimamishwa iliyopangwa kabla ya Ironwood kuboresha. Wala Zebra, Zcash Foundation's makubaliano node, wala Zallet, mrithi wa mfuko wa fedha, [orodha za majina ya watu](https://github.com/zcash/zallet/releases) Kwa ajili ya makundi kwamba jambo, kwa kila shughuli kutoa taarifa bado ni mpango wa kubuni.

Nia bado ni hai, lakini mbali zaidi kuliko ilionekana Julai. [ZIP 231 - Ujumbe wa posta.](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0231), vifurushi memo, ni rasimu ambayo ingekuwa kufanya nafasi kwa ajili yake: Swihart ilivyoelezwa bundles memo kama msingi wa mwisho kwa reply-kwa kuthibitishwa kwamba inaweza kutumika kama ushahidi wa malipo, na kutegemea rasimu, [anwani za jibu zilizohakikishwa](https://zips.z.cash/draft-ecc-authenticated-reply-addrs), mahitaji ya uthibitisho kubwa mno kwa ajili ya sasa memo uwanja. Memo vifurushi ni mabadiliko manunuzi muundo, na mashirika matano kuratibu mchakato wa kuunganisha data kutoka kwenye tovuti yako ili kupata taarifa za kina kuhusu jinsi gani unaweza kufanya hivyo katika hali yoyote ile. [NU7 mtoaji wa kura ya sarafu](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/nu7-coinholder-vote/56912) Agosti kwamba mabadiliko ya muundo ni nje ya wigo kwa ajili NU7 na katika upeo wa juu baada. kura yenyewe, ambayo anaendesha kutoka 25 August hadi 14 Septemba kuweka utoaji, sera ada, block spacing na Sprout deprecation wamiliki; kutoa taarifa haikuwa kwenye ballot. njia wakubwa, ZIP 311, inahitaji hakuna kubadilisha makubaliano lakini bado rasimu bila utekelezaji na hakuna chanjo Orchard.

Hivyo kile meli leo ni akaunti-kuzingatia kuona funguo, kamili au kuja tu, retroactive, irrevocable na pamoja katika mpango wa mmiliki wa akaunti ya, kwa shughuli ngazi ufunuo juu ya ramani barabara.

## Kuelewa ufunuo

Ufumbuzi wa kutoa taarifa, katika Zcash au mahali pengine popote, unaweza kupatikana kwa yafuatayo: Ni nini kinachofunuliwa (kiwango). Kwa nani (mtoaji) Wakati ruzuku inapoanza kutumika na ni mbali gani inapofikia (wakati). Ikiwa inaweza kuondolewa (uwezao kurudishwa nyuma). Na ambaye anaanzisha hiyo, mada ya data au mamlaka (kuanzishwa).

Compliance regimes are requirements over those, and architectures are positions on them. Zcash's viewing keys are by design broad scope, holder-chosen grantee, fully retroactive, irrevocable, user-initiated. The requirement side, as it is applied by exchanges and supervisors, tends to be scoped to a relationship or a period, granted to a named institution or its analytics vendor, continuous from onboarding forward, revocable when the relationship ends, and initiated by the institution as a condition of service.

Yote mawili ya mambo haya kupata kuitwa kuchagua kutoa taarifa. Katika ulimwengu wa sifa, uteuzi kutoa habari ina maana kuwasilisha subset ya sifa kutoka hati za kuthibitisha saini bila kufunua wengine, ambayo ni nini [SD-JWT](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc9901/), ISO mdoc na BBS + saini ni kujengwa kufanya. kitengo cha kutoa taarifa ni sifa, wakati wa sasa ni muda kuwasilisha, na mmiliki anaamua kwa mwingiliano.

Katika ulimwengu wa malipo ya ulinzi, kifungu hiki kinamaanisha kufunua data za shughuli kwa mtu aliyechaguliwa, ambapo kitengo ni historia ya akaunti na wakati wowote ufunguo unapewa.

## Nini mahitaji upande ameomba kwa ajili ya

Swihart alisema ombi pekee la kufuata kiwango cha itifaki timu ya uhandisi imewahi kupokea kutoka kwa Binance, ambayo mnamo Januari 2024 ilihitaji dhamana wazi-kutoka kwa amana. Jibu la timu lilikuwa: [ZIP 320 - Kijiji:](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0320), transparent-source-only addresses, and the ZIP text indeed records the origin "as required by Binance." A TEX address is an ephemeral transparent pass-through that guarantees the exchange receives funds from a transparent source. It gives the exchange a return path without giving it the sender's history. The one accommodation the protocol made to an exchange discloses exactly what the exchange asked for and nothing more.

Since the Sapling upgrade, Swihart argues, there has been no technical reason for an exchange not to support shielded transactions, because proving is fast, exchange customers are already identified at onboarding, and enhanced due diligence exists for the cases that need it. On that view the span between the machinery and the compliance workflow is not really a missing primitive, but in fact a missing conversation.

Divij Pandya is working the other end of that conversation. He is executive director of Pretty Good Policy for Zcash, the Washington policy organization stood up in June as a continuation of an earlier ECC-led initiative, and his organization's project is to get viewing-key disclosure recognized as an acceptable compliance mechanism in a document a compliance officer can cite. To his knowledge, he said, no regulator has yet engaged with viewing keys as an instrument. PGPZ's scope, naturally, is recognition rather than builder guidance. A tool cannot be specified against a requirement until someone with authority has ruled on the instrument, so recognition is the upstream step. The organization's filings are signed by Paul Brigner as PGPZ's founder and as ZODL's chief policy and regulatory officer, so the policy and engineering efforts share an officer as well as a thesis.

A July 21 statement for the record to a House subcommittee on FinCEN oversight describes Zcash as having "selective disclosure capabilities, allowing users to voluntarily share transaction details through the use of viewing keys with auditors, tax authorities, or compliance officers," and refers to zero-knowledge proofs "paired with selective viewing keys" for proving specified facts. A comment letter on FinCEN's docket, [FINCEN-2026-0101-0050 - Utoaji wa huduma za afya kwa watoto na vijana.](https://www.regulations.gov/comment/FINCEN-2026-0101-0050), anasema, kwa Pandya, kwamba kuona funguo na ufunuo wa kuchagua lazima iwe kukubalika utaratibu kufuata. swali wazi wao kuweka juu ya mdhibiti ni moja ya granularity, kama akaunti-scoped, wote historia ufichuzi kutosha kwa ajili ya "malengo shughuli habari", au ikiwa jibu anasubiri kwenye ZIP 231.

Hadi kwamba kutatuliwa, timu ya kufuata uso kizuizi kuwa jukwaa bandia bango poster, strahncryptography, [ilivyoelezwa](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-shielded-compliance-bridge/56862) katika majadiliano ya pendekezo Olaboye: wao kufuata sheria kwa barua, na si kuchukua njia unrecognized bila mdhibiti au kampuni ya kisheria uthibitisho wa hata hivyo sauti cryptography. yoyote disclosure design kwamba attaches rejea shughuli inathibitisha tu hop mwisho, na hop mwisho inaweza kuwa amana wenyewe. ufunguo viewing ina kikomo sawa. ni wazi akaunti mmiliki biashara, si ambapo fedha za washirika alikuja kutoka, ambayo ni swali screen vikwazo anauliza.

Kuangalia funguo wazi bar ambapo mahitaji ni ushahidi na data ya somo la kile kilichotokea, kama asili ya amana mtoaji anachagua kueleza, ukaguzi wa hiari, faili kodi, uthibitisho kwamba malipo maalum yalifanywa. Wale ni user-ulianzishwa, retroactive, moja wakati ufunuo. Wao si wazi bar ambako mahitaji ni ufuatiliaji kuendelea kutoka onboarding mbele, vikwazo screening dhidi ya washirika akaunti mmiliki haina kudhibiti, scoping kwa kipindi au uhusiano, au uondoaji katika offboarding.

Gemini imepokea pesa kwa anwani za ulinzi tangu [Septemba 2020](https://www.gemini.com/blog/gemini-adds-support-for-shielded-zcash-transactions), wakati msemaji alitoa mazungumzo yanayoendelea na wasimamizi, ilipanua hilo kwa bwawa la Orchard mnamo Novemba 2025, na kwenye bwawa baada ya Ironwood siku mbili baada ya uanzishaji Julai 2026. Inakubali amana zilizotumwa kutoka anwani zilizohifadhiwa kuwa ulinzi wa uwazi. Kraken's [orodha ya anwani za uondoaji wa fedha](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360001474826-supported-address-formats-for-cryptocurrency-withdrawal) inasaidia wazi ZEC anwani tu, Coinbase ni vivyo hivyo uwazi-tu, na Binance inahitaji TEX. Nini hakuna kubwa umewekwa kubadilishana bado inatoa ni ulinzi wa kuhifadhi, a walinda amana anwani, au workflow ambayo kuangalia muhimu ya mteja kwenda katika.

Uingizaji ni kuwa kujengwa safu chini. Miongoni mwa mapendekezo katika sarafu-mmiliki wa moja kwa moja ruzuku misaada pande zote [chini ya ukaguzi huu Septemba](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/call-for-proposals-coinholder-directed-retroactive-grants-program-q3/56885) are ZecBooks, a Mac bookkeeping tool that imports a view-only key, classifies notes, and exports a scoped package of rows for an accountant without granting lasting access to the wallet; ZecLedger, a read-only cost-basis and reconciliation tool built around a unified full viewing key, tested against a mainnet wallet; and seer-sync, a chain-sync engine that takes a viewing key so a payment processor or accounting system can observe shielded activity without holding spending keys. None is an exchange workflow, and none has a regulator's attention. Each is the accountant-facing half of the same problem, built by one or two people without a partner asking, and ZecBooks' scoped export is selective disclosure operating at the interface where it is most often needed first: between a shielded user and the person keeping their books.

## Darasa, si sarafu ya dhahabu.

Watengenezaji wa Zcash wanafanya kazi kwenye tatizo linaloshirikiwa na kila usanifu wa faragha.

In Zama's confidential-EVM design, an access control list determines which addresses may request decryption of a ciphertext, and a threshold network of key-management operators performs the decryption on request. That moves the grantee axis from a key the user hands over to a permission a contract records, and makes initiation programmable, since a contract can grant an auditor decryption rights as a condition of use. It does not make disclosure revocable in the sense a compliance officer means, because a ciphertext already decrypted stays decrypted, and it trades a user's key custody for operator honesty as the trust base. Asset-level auditor keys, of the kind in Solana's confidential transfer extension and Avalanche's encrypted ERC design, fix a grantee at the token level in that the issuer or a designated auditor can read balances and transfers for that asset.

Inco imechapisha a [mfumo wa msingi](https://www.inco.org/blog/programmable-view-access) kwa ajili ya kutazama predefined, ambapo upatikanaji ni fasta katika nyenzo muhimu wakati wa shughuli, dhidi programu view kupata, ambapo haki za kuona ni hali mkataba ambayo inaweza kupewa, scoped na kuondolewa baadaye.

## Kwa nini ni vigumu sana kufanya hivyo?

Nini hutenganisha ufunguo wa kutazama kutoka kwa mtiririko wa kazi ya kufuata?

Kuangalia muhimu inaonyesha historia ya mmiliki wa akaunti. vikwazo uchunguzi na ufuatiliaji kuendelea kuuliza kuhusu wapinzani, ambapo fedha upande mwingine wa kila shughuli alitoka, na kwamba ni hasa habari kulindwa pool ipo kwa kuzuia. Hakuna mtu bado umeonyesha mpango ambayo majibu swali mpinzani bila ama kitabu uwazi au tatu trusted chama cha tatu.

Kama kumbukumbu vifurushi ardhi katika kuboresha baada ya NU7 na jibu-anwani rasimu ifuatavyo, "makini shughuli habari" katika filings PGPZ inakuwa halisi. Hiyo ni angalau upgrades mbili mbali, na kujibu udhibiti hufika juu ya mratibu wa muda line.

The demand is easily seen by the number of people building toward it. What is missing is a fixed requirement for privacy-preserving disclosure. Zcash is the ecosystem where that decision is being negotiated in the open, by engineers, advocates, builders, an exchange and a grants committee who seem to agree on the destination and are arguing about the order of the steps. It is also being worked by solo developers who started on the accounting layer without waiting for the requirement to be written.
