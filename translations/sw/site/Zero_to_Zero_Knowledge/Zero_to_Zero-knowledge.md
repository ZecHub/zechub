# Zero to Zero Knowledge

Mfululizo mfupi kuhusu mawazo yaliyo nyuma ya pesa taslimu ya kidijitali yenye faragha [private digital cash]. Anza kutoka juu.

| # | Mada | Utakachojifunza |
|---|-------|---------------------|
| 1 | [Vitendakazi vya Hash](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/hash-functions) | Ingizo lolote hubadilika kuwa tokeo lisilobadilika. Sifa tatu ambazo hash lazima iwe nazo. |
| 2 | [Maneno ya kurejesha pochi](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/mnemonic-seed-phrases) | Jinsi orodha ya maneno inavyokuwa funguo zinazohifadhi fedha zako. |
| 3 | [Anwani za Zcash](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/zcash-addresses) | Miamala ya uwazi [transparent] na miamala ya faragha [shielded], na Anwani Zilizounganishwa. |
| 4 | [Mazingira Yanayoaminika ya Utekelezaji](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/tees) | Vifaa vinavyoweka ukokotoaji kuwa wa faragha, na maeneo ambayo havitoshelezi. |
| 5 | [Cryptonote](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/cryptonote) | Muundo wa faragha ulio nyuma ya Monero, na jinsi unavyotofautiana na Zcash. |
| 6 | [Lelantus](/zero-to-zero-knowledge/lelantus) | Itifaki iliyo nyuma ya Firo, na jinsi inavyolinganishwa na Zcash. |
