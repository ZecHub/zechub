## Taarifa ya Ujumbe

The mission at ECC is to empower economic freedom. Financial privacy is core to this endeavor, and essential to protect human dignity, security, self-sovereignty, and consent. Obtaining financial privacy should be as easy as closing the curtains in your home. But with the emergence of Web2, power is deeply centralized, and those with control are already in your living room. You cannot close the curtains. We must build something new.
Mfumo huu mpya wa zana za kifedha, kupatikana kwa kila mtu, itakuwa user-kirafiki, binafsi kabisa na chaguzi kwa ajili ya kutoa taarifa, na miundombinu yake kusaidia itasambazwa na imara.

## Zodl, zamani wa Zashi: [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zashi-zcash-wallet/id1672392439) - [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.electriccoin.zcash) - [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/co.electriccoin.zcash.foss/)

Zashi ilikuwa mkoba wa kujilinda uliolindwa na ZEC kwa shughuli za dijiti zilizofichwa, iliyojengwa na ECC na kutolewa mnamo 2024.

It was designed to onboard friends and family into Zcash and private digital payments, with an interface simple enough for people with no prior crypto experience to set up a wallet, get ZEC, and start transacting in minutes.

Mkoba huo ulipewa jina jipya **Zodl** mnamo Februari 2026 baada ya timu kuhamia ZODL. Watumiaji waliopo hawakuhitaji kufanya chochote, kwani programu ilibadilishwa kwenye sasisho lake linalofuata, ndiyo sababu viungo vya duka hapo juu vimefunguliwa na Zodl. Sasa inatunzwa huko badala ya ECC, kwa hivyo angalia [Ukurasa wa ZODL](/zcash-organizations/zodl) kwa habari ya sasa.

Ushirikiano kujengwa wakati wa miaka ECC:
- [Coinbase:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/coinbase-zashi-1-2-release/) crypto newbies wanaweza kununua ZEC moja kwa moja ndani ya mkoba kuruka hassles wa usajili akaunti, KYC, na kuondoa sarafu kutoka kubadilishana.

- [Flexa:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/zashi-flexa-integration-is-here-spend-zec-at-thousands-of-retailers/) inafanya kuwa rahisi kutumia ZEC, kuruhusu watumiaji wapya kuutumia katika maelfu ya maeneo kama vile Barnes & Noble, GameStop na Chipotle.

- [Keystone:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/drumroll-please-introducing-zashi-keystone-hardware-wallet-integration-for-shielded-zec/) hii ni mafanikio makubwa kwa ajili ya jamii Zcash ambayo imekuwa miaka katika kufanya. ushirikiano huu alama mageuzi muhimu katika usability Zcash na kuwezesha kuhifadhi baridi wa walinzi ZEC.

## Utafiti na Maendeleo

Timu wameongoza mafanikio kutambuliwa katika matukio kadhaa, kwanza kuwa [uzinduzi wa Zcash] na kuanzisha Bitcoin.](https://electriccoin.co/blog/zcash-begins/) kama matumizi ya kwanza duniani kibiashara Zero-Ujuzi Ushuhuda.

Baadhi zaidi ya kufuata kama vile [Sapling mtandao kuboresha](https://electriccoin.co/blog/sapling-activation-complete/) allowing for lighter, shielded transactions with a time reduction of 90% for constructing transactions, and a memory reduction of over 97%, enabling for shielded transactions on a mobile device for the first time.

mafanikio makubwa kuwa hivi karibuni. [Uundaji na kupelekwa kwa teknolojia ya kompyuta, ambayo ni muhimu sana katika maendeleo ya sasa](https://electriccoin.co/blog/nu5-activates-on-mainnet-eliminating-trusted-setup-and-launching-a-new-era-for-zcash/) Hii iliondoa kuaminika na kuzindua enzi mpya kwa Zcash. Tangu 2022, hii imekuwa hai kwenye Mainnet ya Zcash na inaruhusu watumiaji kutumia ujenzi salama zaidi & rahisi kuboreshwa kwa itifaki yao ya malipo. 

## Ushirikiano na ushirikiano 

The ECC team supports and contributes to the Zcash ecosystem in many ways, collaborating with both internal & external teams regularly to help maintain and improve the Zcash protocol. 

Partnerships include working with Gemini to enable shielded transactions on their platform, Flexa to enable easy Zcash mobile payments at retal locations, hardware wallet companies like Keystone to enable shielded cold storage of ZEC, Brave for Zcash support in the browser, & other teams in the Zcash ecosystem to bring imrovements to Zcash. 

ECC pia ni mfadhili wa fahari ya [PGP kwa Crypto](https://x.com/pgpforcrypto) a meeting series that brings together professionals, enthusiasts, and experts in the cryptocurrency space to discuss developments, share insights, and foster community engagement. PGPforCrypto is led by Board Member and VP of Strategic Alliances at Electric Coin Co, Paul Brigner.

## Sera na Udhibiti

Maarifa na uongozi wa ECC kuhusu faragha ya kwenye mnyororo na athari zake kwa usalama wa kibinafsi na kitaifa ni muhimu sana kwa watunga sera. Jitihada thabiti za kuongoza vizuri sera kila inapowezekana zimefanywa tangu ECC ilipoanzishwa. 

Examples of outreach include dialogue with Financial Action Task Force (FATF) taking part in meetings which resulted in the organizations recommendations for the cryptocurrency sector. As a result of these efforts Zcash became approved by the New York State Department of Financial Services (NYDFS), the only privacy-protecting cryptocurrency to do so.
