## Taarifa ya Ujumbe

Valar Group is an independent engineering organization focused on scaling Zcash, strengthening coinholder governance, and improving the privacy, performance, and long-term resilience of the protocol.

Kazi yake ni umakini juu ya itifaki ngazi miundombinu: binafsi ishara-mmiliki upigaji kura, high-utendaji full node programu, mkoba-sync teknolojia na upgrades mtandao kwamba kufanya shielded Zcash zaidi usable katika kiwango kikubwa.

Shirika hilo linalenga kuwapa wamiliki wa ZEC njia ya kueleza mapendeleo binafsi, kutoa watendaji node haraka na uwezo zaidi programu, na kutoa pochi zana ambazo kuhifadhi faragha user wakati kupunguza gharama za kushiriki katika mtandao.

## Mambo ya Kujua Kuhusu Mazoezi

Kikundi cha Valar kinaongozwa na Dev Ojha (ValarDragon), mwanzilishi mwenza wa Osmosis na mshiriki wa timu iliyozindua Cosmos. Katika muongo uliopita amefanya kazi katika zk-SNARKs, makubaliano ya BFT, na mifumo ya uzalishaji DeFi .

The group’s public work in Zcash became prominent as the ecosystem shifted toward independent protocol teams after the 2026 reorganization of core development. Valar Group emerged as one of the organizations building the next generation of Zcash infrastructure alongside Project Tachyon, Shielded Labs, ZODL, and the Zcash Foundation.

Makala ya mara kwa mara katika kazi yake ni kwamba mali Zcash's faragha lazima kupanua zaidi malipo. Kama wamiliki wanaulizwa kupiga kura juu ya utoaji, block nyakati, au mtandao-upgrade wigo, wao inapaswa kuwa na uwezo wa kufanya hivyo kutoka mizani ulinzi bila kufunua utambulisho, salio, au kura za mtu binafsi. mahitaji hayo imesababisha Valar Group kubuni na meli maalum sarafu mmiliki uchaguzi mlolongo .

Scaling sawa na cryptography background pia umbo node yake na kazi ya usawazishaji. vitalu kasi, nyepesi mkoba kusawazisha, na uwezo zaidi full Node ni kutibiwa kama prerequisites kwa ajili ya fedha binafsi ambayo inaweza kutumika katika malipo-mtandao wadogo badala tu kama kuhifadhi thamani.

## Maono ya Mbele

Valar Group's vifaa vya umma na kazi ya mradi wa pointi kuelekea mtandao Zcash ambayo inaweza:

- Kusaidia binafsi, auditable coinholder kupiga kura kama mchakato wa utawala kurudia.
- Punguza malipo ya uthibitisho wa kazi bila kuhatarisha faragha iliyohifadhiwa.
- Kupunguza mkoba na node vikwazo kupitia PIR, kupogoa, na kuenea kwa kasi ya kuzuia.
- Kuongeza utekelezaji utofauti kwa meli huru full-node stack.
- Kusaidia baada ya quantum utayari na rasmi kupitia upya upgrades itifaki.

Shirika linafanya kazi kama mchangiaji huru, sio mmiliki wa itifaki. Mabadiliko ya Itifaki bado yanaendelea kupitia ZIPs, utekelezaji, ukaguzi na ishara za jamii. Jukumu la Valar Group ni kubuni, kutekeleza, kuendesha, na kufungua mifumo ambayo hufanya michakato hiyo iwezekane.

## Maeneo ya Kimkakati

Valar Group's kazi makundi kuzunguka maeneo manne.

### Private Coinholder Utawala

Zcash does not use automatic on-chain protocol control. Coinholder polls are advisory signals that feed a broader rough-consensus process. Valar Group built the Tokenholder Voting Chain so those signals can be collected from shielded balances without exposing voter identity or individual vote size.

Muundo wa sasa hutumia:

- Dedicated Cosmos SDK maombi mlolongo kuandaa raundi ya kupiga kura.
- Ushuhuda wa picha dhidi ya noti za Ironwood zinazoweza kutumiwa.
- Homomorphic encryption ya kura kiasi.
- Private Information Retrieval kwa nullifier ushahidi wa uanachama.
- Mratibu multisig na mamlaka ya uchaguzi kusambazwa.

Lengo ni kuchukua nafasi ya awali ishara-mmiliki mchakato wa kupiga kura na reusable, audited, mfuko mfumo integratable kwamba mashirika mengine inaweza kuendesha na kujitegemea tally.

### Node Programu na Mtandao Scaling

Valar Group collaborates with Project Tachyon on Zakura, a Zcash full node built from the Zebra codebase. Zakura is positioned as a high-performance node for operators who need faster initial sync, pruning, snapshot bootstrapping, and a compatibility path for former `zcashd` watumiaji.

Kazi ya kupanua kuhusiana ni pamoja na:

- Haraka zaidi lengo block nyakati, ikiwa ni pamoja na 25-sekunde block majaribio juu ya NU7 testnets.
- Kuboreshwa peer-to-peer kuzuia kuenea.
- Full-node makala lengo la kuweka Zcash usable kama shughuli shielded kukua.

### Mkoba na Sync Miundombinu

Wallet Shielded kihistoria kuwa na scan kiasi kikubwa cha data mnyororo. Valar Group yanaendelea PIR mifumo hivyo pochi wanaweza kupata ushahidi wanaohitaji bila kushusha seti kamili nullifier au kufunua ambayo maelezo wao huduma kuhusu.

Kazi hii inaonekana wote katika kura ya kuongoza na kwa ujumla utafiti mkoba-sync. Kundi pia imechangia mfuko wa upande uaminifu kazi, ikiwa ni pamoja na kuwasilisha shughuli mbalimbali server na kuboresha seva uteuzi kutumika katika ZODL's simu stack .

### Upgrades ya Itifaki na Ecosystem Uratibu

Valar Group was one of the organizations that publicly committed to the Ironwood response after the Orchard circuit vulnerability. Ironwood introduced a new shielded pool, sealed the original Orchard pool behind a turnstile, and restored a path to independently verifying circulating supply. Valar Group worked with Project Tachyon, Shielded Labs, ZODL, and the Zcash Foundation on architecture, consensus-rule implementation, and ecosystem coordination.

Kikundi pia inashiriki katika NU7 scoping, operesheni ya testnet na ZIP editing. Dev Ojha ni waliotajwa kama mhariri wa ZIP .

## Mipango ya Sasa

### Tokenholder Chain kupiga kura / Shielded Vote

Shielded Vote ni Valar Group's private governance protocol for Zcash. Wamiliki kura na mizani shielded bila kufunua kiasi mtu binafsi au kuunganisha kura kwa utambulisho.

Mali muhimu ni pamoja na:

- Mkutano mmoja wa mtandaoni ili kupiga kura, badala ya siku nyingi za kuidhinisha / kufungua mchakato.
- A Keystone-ambatana snapshot saini kwamba inachukua haki za kupiga kura kwa hotkey bila kuweka fedha katika hatari.
- Encrypted kura kiasi kutumia homomorphic ElGamal.
- PIR maswali hivyo nullifiers si kuvuja wakati wa uthibitisho snapshot.
- Kura kugawanywa na kuchelewa kuwasilisha relay kupunguza uwiano wa muda.
- Taarifa za umma auditable.

Mnamo Agosti 2026, Valar Group na Mradi Tachyon walitumia stack hii kwa kura ya mmiliki wa sarafu NU7. Haki inahitajika ZEC iliyohifadhiwa katika Ironwood kwenye urefu kuu 3,459,350. Kura iliendelea kutoka 25 August hadi 14 Septemba, 2026, na kizingiti cha ushiriki 1,000,000 ZEC kwa matokeo kutibiwa kama mwakilishi. Maswali yalifunika NSM kutolewa laini, wakati wa kutoa tena, Sprout / v4 kupunguzwa thamani, nyakati za kuzuia sekunde 25, na upeo/utayari wa NU7 .

Default-chain coordination uses a 2-of-5 multisig among Project Tachyon, Valar Group, the Zcash Foundation, ZODL, and Shielded Labs. A separate validator set holds per-round decryption-key shares. No single validator can recover individual votes; a threshold of validators is required to produce the final tally.

### Zakura

Zakura ni Zcash full node maendeleo kama ushirikiano kati ya Valar Group na Mradi Tachyon. Ni inayotokana kutoka Zebra na anaongeza kasi sync, asili kupogoa, snapshot bootstrapping, `zcashd` njia utangamano, na majaribio ya juu-utendaji P2P kazi.

Zcash Foundation hadharani kukaribishwa mradi, na kueleza kuwa Zebra ilitolewa chini ya leseni permissive hivyo timu huru inaweza kugawanyika na kuboresha yake, na kwamba wachangiaji kadhaa Zakura alikuwa tayari kuchangia upstream kwa Zebra.

### Kupata Habari za Kibinafsi

Valar Group inaendelea huduma PIR na maktaba kwa ajili ya matatizo mawili yanayohusiana:

- Kuthibitisha kwamba noti ilikuwa unspent katika urefu snapshot bila kufunua nullfier yake.
- Kupunguza data pochi lazima kuleta ili kulandanisha au kupiga kura.

Hii ni msingi utegemezi wa Shielded Vote na block jengo kwa ajili ya haraka mkoba binafsi UX.

### Ironwood na NU7 Engineering

Valar Group was part of the June 2026 joint commitment to Ironwood and contributed to consensus-rule implementation and client work around the new pool. It also operated NU7 testnet infrastructure, including join scripts and public nodes hosted under `nu7.valargroup.dev`.

### Maktaba ya Open-Source Protocols

Makala ya kwanza. `valargroup` Shirika la GitHub linachapisha kura na node stack kama hazina za umma, pamoja na:

- [`vote-sdk`](https://github.com/valargroup/vote-sdk)  mchakato maalum wa maombi kwa ajili ya binafsi kwenye mlolongo kupiga kura
- [`zcash_voting`](https://github.com/valargroup/zcash_voting)  mteja-upande kulindwa maktaba ya kupiga kura, uthibitisho, kuhifadhi na FFI
- [`voting-circuits`](https://github.com/valargroup/voting-circuits)  Halo2 ujumbe na mzunguko wa kupiga kura
- [`vote-nullifier-pir`](https://github.com/valargroup/vote-nullifier-pir)  PIR kwa proofs invalidating yasiyo ya ushirika
- [`token-holder-voting-config`](https://github.com/valargroup/token-holder-voting-config)  mkoba huduma-ugunduzi Configuration
- [`zebra`](https://github.com/valargroup/zebra)  Valar Group's Zebra/Zakura maendeleo fork

- ** Roman Akhtariev**  Mhandisi mkuu. Kabla ya mhandisi kuu katika Osmosis; kazi ni pamoja na PIR mfuko wa fedha kulandanisha, ishara-mmiliki kupiga kura, na Zakura utendaji sync.
- **Evan Forbes**  Mhandisi Mkuu. zamani Celestia makubaliano kuongoza na mwanzilishi mhandisi; kazi ni pamoja na haraka zaidi ya muda tayari block-na QUIC P2P stack.

## Muundo wa Shirika

Valar Group inafanya kazi kama shirika huru la uhandisi. Sio sehemu ya Zcash Foundation, ZODL, Shielded Labs au Misaada ya Jumuiya ya Z Cash .

Katika kubuni kura-mnyororo, Valar Group ni moja ya mashirika tano mratibu. jukumu hilo ni parameter wa mfumo wa kupiga kura, si madai ya udhibiti pekee juu Zcash utawala. timu nyingine unaweza kukimbia validators, kusimama nje minyororo mbadala za uchaguzi, au ukaguzi kuchapishwa tallies kutoka tooling umma.

Taarifa za ziada kuhusu aina ya chombo kisheria, muundo wa bodi na utawala wa ndani haijachapishwa kwa undani kama mashirika ya zamani ya Zcash.

## Ufadhili wa kifedha

Public forum statements from mid-2026 describe Valar Group and Project Tachyon as funded through private donations. Unlike ZODL’s disclosed venture round or Shielded Labs’ public donation announcements, Valar Group has not published a detailed donor list or grant schedule.

Mfano huo wa ufadhili unafanya timu iwe huru na mfuko wa kihistoria wa maendeleo / njia ya tuzo za jumla, lakini pia inamaanisha kujulikana kwa umma kidogo katika saizi ya bajeti na vyanzo vya fedha.

## Jukumu katika Zcash Ecosystem

Valar Group ni moja ya mashirika huru itifaki kwamba sumu kuzunguka Zcash 2026 maendeleo mazingira. Katika mazingira hayo:

- **Zcash Foundation** inaendelea usimamizi wa jamii na Zebra.
- **ZODL** inalenga katika bidhaa mkoba na kuendelea itifaki baada ya ECC kugawanywa.
- **Shielded Labs** inalenga katika uendelevu, usalama na utafiti wa makubaliano.
- ** Mradi Tachyon** unazingatia kurudia, uthibitisho rasmi na uwezo wa kupanuka kwa muda mrefu.
- ** Valar Group** inalenga katika binafsi coinholder kupiga kura, node utendaji, PIR, na uhandisi required kuendesha mifumo hiyo katika uzalishaji.

mchango wake wa kipekee ni kufanya ulinzi utawala kazi. NU7 kura ni matumizi ya kwanza kubwa ya kwamba stack: wamiliki kuthibitisha Ironwood mizani, pochi kama vile Zodl na Vizor unaweza kuunganisha mtiririko, na mtu yeyote anaweza kukagua tally bila kujifunza jinsi mmiliki fulani alipiga kura.

Timu hiyohiyo ya node na kazi sync ni nia ya kusaidia nusu nyingine ya picha kwamba. binafsi kupiga kura ni chini muhimu kama pochi hawezi kulandanisha, nodes hawawezi kuendelea juu, au upgrades haiwezi kutekelezwa haraka. Valar Group kutibu utawala wa serikali, Node programu, na mkoba miundombinu kama tatizo moja: kufanya Zcash kibinafsi usable katika kiwango bila kuelekeza nguvu za uendeshaji katika shirika moja.
