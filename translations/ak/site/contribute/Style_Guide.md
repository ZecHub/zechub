# ZecHub Style Akwankyerɛ

ZecHub kwan no yɛ mmerɛw na wotumi bɛn. Yɛma obiara akwaaba na yɛde yɛn adwene si Zcash mpɔtam hɔfo so.

## Nnyinasosɛm ahorow

1. **Ready to assist** - Kyerɛkyerɛ Zcash dwumadiefoɔ sɛ yɛwɔ wɔn afã.

2. **Tiatiaa na ɛkɔ asɛm no mu:** Fa nkrasɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛw sen biara ma. Fa asɛm a ɛho hia sen biara no fi ase.

3. **User-centered:** Yɛkyerɛw ma users kan, developers nea ɛto so abien, na obiara a aka no yɛ nea ɛto so abiɛsa.

## Mfitiasesɛm

- **Fa kɔ asɛm no so**. Fa asɛm a ɛho hia sen biara no fi ase. Front-load keywords a wɔde yɛ skimming. Ma nea ɔde di dwuma no options ne anammɔn a edi hɔ no mu nna hɔ.

- **Twerɛ sɛnea wokasa**. Yɛn a yɛde di dwuma no yɛ nnipa ankasa. Wo ne wɔn nkasa wɔ krataa so.

- **Yɛ tiawa**. Ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no nsɛm a ɛdɔɔso a ɛbɛma wɔadi ɔhaw bi ho dwuma anaasɛ wɔbɛpaw.

- **Skip periods wɔ asɛmti ahorow mu**. Yi awiei nkyerɛwde a ɛwɔ asɛmti, nsɛmti, nsɛmti nketewa, UI asɛmti, na kyerɛw nneɛma a nsɛmfua abiɛsa anaa nea ennu saa wom. Wɔde bere ahorow di dwuma wɔ nkyekyem ne nipadua no mfonini mu.

  - Nhwɛsoɔ: Mia Send button no so->Mia Send button no so

- **Sɛ w’adwene mu yɛ wo naa a, kwati sɛ wobɛkyerɛw nkyerɛwde akɛse**. Fa nkyerɛwde akɛse kyerɛw edin ankasa anaa edin te sɛ Zcash nkutoo, ne asɛmfua a edi kan wɔ asɛmti anaa kasasin bi mu.

  - Examples: How to Send ZEC with Unified Address. → How to send ZEC with unified address

- **San kyerɛw nkyerɛwee a ɛyɛ mmerɛw**. Mpɛn pii no, fa adeyɛ asɛm bi fi asɛm biara ase. Wubetumi na na ɛwɔ hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsan hwɛ mu.

  - Nhwɛsoɔ: Wubetumi de ZEC amena na woagye afiri wo fon a wokura kyin so->Fa wo fon so de ZEC amena na wogye

- **Nnyɛ spacey**. Fa baabi biako pɛ di dwuma wɔ periods ne colons akyi.

- **Kwati nsɛmfua a ɛnyɛ Borɔfo kasa** te sɛ de facto anaa ad hoc. Kwati latin kasa mu nsɛmfua ntiantiaa a wɔde kyerɛw Engiresi kasa mu nsɛmfua a wɔtaa de di dwuma no.

    | Fa eyi di dwuma | Sɛ́ anka eyi bɛba no |
    |-------------|-----------------|
    | sɛ nhwɛso no | s.e., s.e.            |
    | ɛno ne | i.e. |

## Tweets a wɔde kyerɛw nsɛm

> Saa akwankyerɛ a ɛfa ɔkwan a wɔfa so yɛ ade nyinaa ho yi yɛ nea wɔde kyerɛw nsɛm anaa akwankyerɛ a wɔde di dwuma ma ZecHub. Ɛfa tweets ne nsɛm ntiantiaa afoforo nso ho: ma ɛnyɛ tiawa, fa asɛm no hyɛ anim, na twa awiei nkyerɛwde agyiraehyɛde wɔ nkyerɛwde ntiantiaa so.
