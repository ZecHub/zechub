# Ɔreboa ZecHub

ZecHub boa nkurɔfo ma wosua Zcash ho ade. Sɛ worekenkan kratafa yi a, yɛn ani agye ankasa sɛ woresusuw ho sɛ wobɛboa! Ntoboa biara a wode bɛma no, wobesusuw ho [zechub.wiki na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://www.zechub.wiki/) ne ZecHub sohyial media afoforo.

### Wɔn a wɔde mmoa foforo ma

Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wunya ZecHub ho nsɛm a, kenkan... [KENKAN NKYERƐKYERƐMU](https://github.com/ZecHub/zechub/blob/main/README.md).


### Sɛnea wofi ase

ZecHub de GitHub di dwuma de hwɛ mpɔtam hɔfo ntoboa so. Sɛ woyɛ foforo wɔ GitHub mu a, ɛnsɛ sɛ wohaw wo ho! Yɛrebɛkyekyɛ sɛdeɛ wobɛtumi de wo ho ahyɛ mu sɛ mpɔtam hɔfoɔ a wɔboa ZecHub. Yɛtua tips wɔ ZEC mu ma ntoboa a wɔagye atom. Wɔ saa akwankyerɛ yi mu no wobɛnya ntoboa adwumayɛ kwan no ho nsusuiɛ afiri asɛm bi a wobɛbue, twetwe abisadeɛ (PR a wobɛbɔ), PR no a wobɛsan ahwɛ mu, na woaka abom.

### Fa wo ho hyɛ nkɔmmɔbɔ no mu

Nea edi kan no, fa wo ho hyɛ nkɔmmɔbɔ no mu wɔ yɛn [mpɔtam hɔfo nkitahodi](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-community/community-links).

### Style Akwankyerɛfo

Ɛsɛ sɛ ntoboa biara a wɔde bɛma ZecHub no di akyi [ZecHub kwankyerɛfo kwankyerɛfo](https://zechub.wiki/contribute/style-guide). Eyi ka wiki, docs ne social media mu nsɛm ho.

### Akwan a wubetumi afa so aboa

ZecHub yɛ adwuma a mpɔtam hɔfoɔ di anim a ne botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛma mmoa ne nneɛma ama Zcash dwumadiefoɔ ne wɔn a wɔyɛ no. Akwan pii wɔ hɔ a wobɛfa so de wo ho ahyɛ ZecHub mu, a nea ɛka ho ne sɛ wobɛkyerɛw ama yɛn dapɛn dapɛn nsɛmma nhoma, wobɛboa yɛn nimdeɛ, anaasɛ wobɛboa wɔ nkɔso adwuma mu.

Eyinom ne ntoboa ahorow a ZecHub gye tom mprempren:

#### Dev Adwuma - 0.12 kɔsi 0.5 ZEC wɔ PR biara a wɔapene so

Dev adwuma biara a wɔapene so a ɛboa ma wɔkyekye Zcash ecoystem no. Eyi betumi ayɛ yɛn wiki, sika kotoku foforo, anaa application biara a wubetumi asusuw ho.

#### Zcash Nkyerɛkyerɛ (video) - ɛkɔ 0.15 ZEC wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ biara mu

Nhwɛsoɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ bi a ɛwɔ aseɛ ha nie:

Yɛ na kyɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ wɔ Zcash apps so na nya akatua. Fa PR kɔ zechub/tutorials anaa fa video kɔ #video-content channel wɔ Discord mu. Sɛ video no du yɛn gyinapɛn ho a, yɛde bɛto hɔ na yɛde afotu ama wo.

#### ZecHub Wiki - ɛkɔ 0.08 ZEC wɔ wiki krataafa foforo biara a wɔatintim mu

Yɛn wiki wɛbsaet no de Zcash nkyerɛkyerɛ nneɛma ma wɔ ɔkwan a ɛnyɛ den na ɛyɛ mmerɛw so. Zcash yɛ mfiridwuma a ɛkɔ anim paa a ɛwɔ mpɔtam a ɛyɛ hyew, enti nkrataa pii da so wɔ hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkyekye. Yɛn botae ne sɛ yɛbɛkyekye nkrataa wɔ:

```
- Zcash and its related technologies
- ZEC (Zcash currency) Use cases
- New User Guides
- Zcash Community and Ecosystem
- Privacy Ecosystem & Tools
```

Eyinom yɛ mmeae a ɛtrɛw yiye, enti nneɛma pii wɔ hɔ a wobetumi afi mu ayɛ adwuma. Sɛ wopɛ nhyɛsoɔ bi a, hwɛ yɛn mprempren [wiki-docs wɛbsaet no](https://zechub.wiki/) na hwɛ nea ɛyera. Sɛ wohu deɛ wopɛ sɛ wokyerɛw wie a, hyɛ aseɛ yɛ wo nsakraeɛ na sua sɛdeɛ wode PR bɛkɔ ZecHub repo no mu. Wɔabɔ yɛn docs nyinaa na wɔahwɛ so wɔ saa repo yi mu. Di akyi [ZecHub kwankyerɛfo kwankyerɛfo](https://zechub.wiki/contribute/style-guide) bere a worekyerɛw wiki krataafa, na fa krataafa bi a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw wɔ ɔfa koro no ara mu sɛ nhyehyɛe mu nkyerɛkyerɛmu. Sɛ wode PR kɔ wie a, yɛsrɛ wo, fa nkra @dismad, @squirrel, anaa @vito wɔ #zechub fã a ɛwɔ discord no mu, na wɔbɛhwɛ wo PR no mu na wɔaka abom sɛ ɛyɛ krado sɛ wɔde bɛka sait no ho a. Sɛ wɔka bom a, wɔde doc no bɛka ZecHub wɛbsaet no ho. Sɛ doc no nsiesiee ne ho a, wɔbɛhyɛ nyansa sɛ wɔmfa edits mma wo wɔ PR no mu.

#### ZecHub Wiki - 0.015 ZEC wɔ nsakraeɛ biara a wɔgye tom kɔ docs so

Ɛtɔ da bi a yɛn nsɛm a ɛwɔ docs no mu no nyɛ spot on. Ɛno yɛ okay. Ɛno nti na yɛbue-source wɔn! Sɛ wohu biribi a ɛhia nsakraeɛ wɔ wiki-doc mu a, yɛsrɛ wo kɔ doc no ase (a ɛkɔ ne Github krataafa no so) na fa PR so hyɛ nyansa sɛ wɔnyɛ nsakraeɛ.

#### ZecHub Wiki - 0.005 ZEC wɔ link a abubu biara mu a wɔasiesie

Sɛ wuhu sɛ link bi asɛe, anaasɛ wɔakyerɛw biribi a ɛho hia wɔ ɔkwan a ɛnteɛ so a, yɛsrɛ wo kɔ doc no ase (a ɛkɔ ne Github krataafa no so) na fa PR so hyɛ nsakrae no ho nyansa.

#### Nsɛmma nhoma - 0.05 ZEC wɔ nea wɔatintim biara mu

Yɛyɛ abɔde a nkwa wom ho nsɛmma nhoma dapɛn biara. Eyi yɛ super low lift / ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a wobɛfa so de wo ho ahyɛ mu! Nsɛmma nhoma no pue Fida anaa Kwasida biara. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wokyerɛw nsɛmma nhoma a, message @squirrel wɔ #zecweekly fã a ɛwɔ Discord no mu na ma wɔnhu.

Sɛ woyɛ saa wie a, wubetumi akɔ [nsɛmma nhoma fã a ɛwɔ saa adekorabea yi mu](/newsletter/newsletterbasics.md) na fa twe adesrɛ bɛmena sɛ wɔmfa nyɛ nsɛmma nhoma no foforo. Yɛsrɛ sɛ di ɔkwan a wɔfa so de di dwuma wɔ eyi mu no akyi [nsusuwso](/newsletter/newslettertemplate.md).

Sɛ woyɛ eyi wie a @squirrel anaa (wɔ Discord mu) bɛhunu sɛ wo nsɛmma nwoma foforɔ no wɔ hɔ, na wɔbɛhwɛ mu na afei wɔaka abom akɔ akoraeɛ no mu. Sɛ wɔka bom wie a, wɔbɛfa emu nsɛm no na wɔde afa Substack so akɔ.


#### Podcast - .25 ZEC wɔ episode biara a wɔde too ZecHub socials so

So wowɔ adwene bi wɔ nsɛm ho amanneɛbɔ dwumadi, podcast, Twitter kasa, anaa video/audio ade foforo ho? Ka kyerɛ yɛn wɔ Discord #video-content mu na yɛbɛkasa.

Akatua a ɛwɔ saa nsɛm yi ho no sõ kakra, enti ɛho behia sɛ wɔde nsusuwii bi kɔma ZecHub DAO ansa na wɔapene sika a wɔsɛe no so.


#### Adwenkyerɛ afoforo? Ma yɛnhu!

Wowɔ nyansahyɛ foforo? Ka kyerɛ yɛn wɔ #general mu wɔ Discord so. Yebetumi asusuw ho na yɛahwɛ sɛ ZecHub DAO bɛboa anaa.

### Sɛ Wobɛwie

Yɛsrɛ sɛ ntwentwɛn wo nan ase sɛ wubefi ase aboa nnwuma no protocol ahorow a wobu no kɛse no biako. Eyi yɛ ɔkwan pa a wobɛfa so de wo ho ahyɛ Zcash mu. Sɛ wowɔ nsɛmmisa bi fa ntoboa ho a, yɛsrɛ sɛ ma yɛnhu wɔ so [Akasakasa](#join-the-conversation).

Aseda!
