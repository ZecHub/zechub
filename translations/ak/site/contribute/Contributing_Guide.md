# Ɔreboa ZecHub

ZecHub boa nkurɔfo ma wosua Zcash. Sɛ worekenkan kratafa yi a, yɛn ani agye ankasa sɛ woresusuw ho sɛ wobɛboa! Ntoboa biara a wode bɛma no, wobesusuw ho [zechub.wiki na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://www.zechub.wiki/) ne ZecHub sohyial media afoforo.

### Wɔn a wɔde mmoa foforo ma

Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wunya ZecHub, kenkan.. [KENKAN NKYERƐKYERƐMU](https://github.com/ZecHub/zechub/blob/main/README.md).


### Sɛnea wofi ase

ZecHub de GitHub di dwuma de hwɛ mpɔtam hɔfo ntoboa so. Sɛ woyɛ foforo wɔ GitHub, ɛnsɛ sɛ wohaw wo ho! Yɛrebɛkyekyɛ sɛdeɛ wobɛtumi de wo ho ahyɛ mu sɛ mpɔtam hɔfoɔ a wɔboa ZecHub. Yɛtua tips wɔ shielded ZEC mu ma ntoboa a wɔagye atom. Wɔnhyɛɛ akatua dodoɔ wɔ ZEC mu — hwɛ [Sɛnea wɔde akatua ahorow si hɔ](#how-rewards-are-set). Wɔ saa akwankyerɛ yi mu no wobɛnya ntoboa adwumayɛ kwan no ho nsusuiɛ afiri asɛm bi a wobɛbue, twetwe abisadeɛ (PR a wobɛbɔ), PR no a wobɛsan ahwɛ mu, na woaka abom.

### Fa wo ho hyɛ nkɔmmɔbɔ no mu

Nea edi kan no, fa wo ho hyɛ nkɔmmɔbɔ no mu wɔ yɛn [mpɔtam hɔfo nkitahodi](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-community/community-links).

### Style Akwankyerɛfo

Ɛsɛ sɛ ntoboa biara a wɔde bɛma ZecHub di akyi [ZecHub style akwankyerɛ](https://zechub.wiki/contribute/style-guide). Eyi ka wiki, docs ne social media mu nsɛm ho.

### Akwan a wubetumi afa so aboa

ZecHub yɛ adwuma a mpɔtam hɔfoɔ di anim a ne botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛma mmoa ne nneɛma ama Zcash dwumadiefoɔ ne wɔn a wɔyɛ no. Akwan pii wɔ hɔ a wobɛfa so de wo ho ahyɛ ZecHub, a nea ɛka ho ne sɛ wobɛkyerɛw ama yɛn dapɛn dapɛn nsɛmma nhoma, wobɛboa yɛn nimdeɛ, anaasɛ wobɛboa wɔ nkɔso adwuma mu.

Eyinom ne ntoboa ahorow a ZecHub gye tom mprempren:

### Sɛnea wɔde akatua ahorow si hɔ

Wɔtua tips wɔ shielded ZEC. ZEC nɔma a na ɛtra nsɛmti a ɛwɔ ase ha no mu no yɛ abakɔsɛm mu mfonini ahorow a ɛwɔ ZEC/USD bo dedaw so. Mfa wɔn sɛ mprempren rates.

Sɛnea wɔpaw sika dodow bi:

1. Fa adwuma no hyia USD ntamgyinafo wɔ [bounty sika a wɔde ma ho nhyehyɛe](https://bounties.zechub.wiki/docs/bounty-amounts).
2. Paw botae bi wɔ saa band no mu — ɛnyɛ automatically top.
3. Dane wɔ ɔmanfo ZEC/USD beae na hyɛ ZEC wɔ bounty no so:

```
zec_to_enter = usd_target / zec_usd_spot
```

Kurukuruwa kɔ 4 decimal mmeae. Policy fael no ne nokware fibea biako pɛ. Sɛ krataafa yi ne saa fael no nhyia a, nhyehyɛe no di nkonim.

Wɔakyerɛw adwuma a wotua ho ka wɔ [ZEC Bounties](https://bounties.zechub.wiki/).

Aman abiɛsa a ɛnyɛ pɛ:

1. **Merged** — wɔgye PR no tom wɔ adekorabea no mu.
2. **Wɔapene akatua so** — sponsor anaa DAO pene so sɛ akatua bi ka, ne kɛseɛ bɛn.
3. **Paid** — ZEC du wo Shielded Unified Address.

Ntoboa a wɔaka abom no ankasa mpene akatua bi so. Akatua a wɔapene so nyɛ sika a wɔawie.

#### ZecHub Wiki - wiki krataafa foforo a wɔatintim

Yɛn wiki wɛbsaet no de Zcash nkyerɛkyerɛ nneɛma ma wɔ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw na ɛyɛ mmerɛw so. Zcash yɛ mfiridwuma a ɛkɔ anim paa a ɛwɔ mpɔtam a ɛyɛ hyew, enti nkrataa pii da so ara wɔ hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkyekye. Yɛn botae ne sɛ yɛbɛkyekye nkrataa wɔ:

```
- Zcash and its related technologies
- ZEC (Zcash currency) Use cases
- New User Guides
- Zcash Community and Ecosystem
- Privacy Ecosystem & Tools
```

Eyinom yɛ mmeae a ɛtrɛw yiye, enti nneɛma pii wɔ hɔ a wobetumi afi mu ayɛ adwuma. Sɛ wopɛ nhyɛsoɔ bi a, hwɛ yɛn mprempren [wiki-docs wɛbsaet no](https://zechub.wiki/) na hwɛ nea ɛyera. Sɛ wohu deɛ wopɛ sɛ wokyerɛw wie a, hyɛ aseɛ yɛ wo nsakraeɛ na sua sɛdeɛ wode PR bɛkɔ ZecHub repo no mu. Wɔabɔ yɛn docs nyinaa na wɔahwɛ so wɔ saa repo yi mu. Di akyi [ZecHub style akwankyerɛ](https://zechub.wiki/contribute/style-guide) bere a worekyerɛw wiki krataafa, na fa krataafa a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw wɔ ɔfã koro no ara mu di dwuma sɛ nhyehyɛe mu nkyerɛkyerɛmu. Sɛ wode PR kɔ wie a, yɛsrɛ wo, fa nkra @dismad, @squirrel, anaa @vito wɔ #zechub fã a ɛwɔ discord no mu, na wɔbɛhwɛ wo PR no mu na wɔaka abom sɛ ɛyɛ krado sɛ wɔde bɛka sait no ho a. Sɛ wɔka bom a, wɔde doc no bɛka ZecHub wɛbsaet no ho. Sɛ doc no nsiesiee ne ho a, wɔbɛhyɛ nyansa sɛ wɔmfa edits mma wo wɔ PR no mu.

#### ZecHub Wiki - a wɔakyerɛ ase wiki krataafa

ZecHub's botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛma wɔanya nkyerɛkyerɛ beaeɛ a wɔabue ano a obiara a ɔwɔ Zcash mpɔtam hɔ bɛtumi aboa. Hub no nkonimdie kɛseɛ baako ne sɛ wɔbɛhunu sɛ mpɔtam hɔfoɔ akyerɛ ZecHub nneɛma ase akɔ wɔn mpɔtam hɔ kasa mu.

Hyɛ no nsow: ZecHub Global krataafa nkyerɛase dodow anohyeto yɛ nkratafa 10 dapɛn biara.

Curated locale nkratafa a ɛwɔ ase `translations/<locale>/site/` wɔde source-hash manifest di akyi de gyina wɔn Engiresi fibea so. Hwɛ [nkyerɛase/README-sync.md](https://github.com/ZecHub/zechub/blob/main/translation/README-sync.md) ama staleness detection, sync adwumayɛ kwan, ne protected-terms validation.

#### ZecHub Wiki - sesa kɔ doc a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw mu

Ɛtɔ da bi a yɛn nsɛm a ɛwɔ docs no mu no nyɛ spot on. Ɛno yɛ okay. Ɛno nti na yɛbue-source wɔn! Sɛ wohu biribi a ɛhia nsakraeɛ wɔ wiki-doc mu a, yɛsrɛ wo kɔ doc no ase (a ɛkɔ ne GitHub krataafa no so) na fa PR ho nyansahyɛ ma nsakraeɛ.

#### ZecHub Wiki - wɔasiesie link a abubu

Sɛ wuhu sɛ link bi asɛe, anaasɛ wɔakyerɛw biribi a ɛho hia wɔ ɔkwan a ɛnteɛ so a, yɛsrɛ wo kɔ doc no ase (a ɛkɔ ne GitHub krataafa no so) na fa PR so hyɛ nsakrae no ho nyansa.

#### Newsletter - a woagye no foforo

Yɛyɛ abɔde a nkwa wom ho nsɛmma nhoma dapɛn biara. Eyi yɛ super low lift / ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a wobɛfa so de wo ho ahyɛ mu! Nsɛmma nhoma no pue Fida anaa Kwasida biara. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wokyerɛw nsɛmma nhoma a, message @squirrel wɔ #zecweekly fã a ɛwɔ Discord no mu na ma wɔnhu.

Sɛ woyɛ saa wie a, wubetumi akɔ [nsɛmma nhoma fã a ɛwɔ saa adekorabea yi mu](https://github.com/ZecHub/zechub/blob/main/newsletter/newsletterbasics.md) na fa twe adesrɛ bɛmena sɛ wɔmfa nyɛ nsɛmma nhoma no foforo. Yɛsrɛ sɛ di ɔkwan a wɔfa so yɛ adwuma wɔ eyi mu no akyi [nsusuwso](https://github.com/ZecHub/zechub/blob/main/newsletter/newslettertemplate.md).

Sɛ woyɛ eyi wie a @squirrel anaa (wɔ Discord) bɛhunu sɛ wo nsɛmma nwoma foforɔ no wɔ hɔ, na wɔbɛhwɛ mu na afei wɔaka abom akɔ akoraeɛ no mu. Sɛ wɔka bom wie a, wɔbɛfa emu nsɛm no na wɔde afa Substack so akɔ.

#### Nsɛmma nhoma - nkyerɛase

Mprempren yɛwɔ nkyerɛase ahorow a ɛwɔ Spania, Portugal ne Russia kasa mu. Wɔde nkyerɛase ahorow a wɔakyerɛ ase no to wɔn socials so, na yɛyɛ nea yebetumi biara sɛ yɛbɛma ayɛ kɛse denam ZecHub socials no so.

Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wokyerɛ nsɛmma nhoma no ase kɔ wo mpɔtam hɔ kasa mu a, ma yɛnhu ɔkwan a wobɛfa so akyɛ ne kasa a wobɛfa so atintim nsɛmma nhoma no, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a yebetumi ayɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛbɛma yɛayi no adi.

#### Podcast - episode a wɔde too ZecHub socials so

So wowɔ adwene bi wɔ nsɛm ho amanneɛbɔ dwumadi, podcast, Twitter kasa, anaa video/audio ade foforo ho? Ka kyerɛ yɛn wɔ Discord #video-content mu na yɛbɛkasa.

Akatua a ɛwɔ saa nsɛm yi ho no sõ kakra, enti ɛho behia sɛ wɔde nsusuwii bi kɔma ZecHub's DAO ansa na wɔapene sika a wɔsɛe no so.

#### Adebɔ mu nsɛm a wɔde gu sohyial media so

Yɛpɛ nneɛma foforo a ɛyɛ anigye ma yɛn social media. Wogye video ntiantiaa, GIF, memes, ne adebɔ ho nsɛm afoforo bere a ɛne.. [ZecHub style akwankyerɛ](https://zechub.wiki/contribute/style-guide). Akatua kɛse di akyi [bounty sika a wɔde ma ho nhyehyɛe](https://bounties.zechub.wiki/docs/bounty-amounts).

Wubetumi nso ayɛ mfonini nketewa ama yɛn nsɛmma nhoma ne podcast. Sɛ wowɔ design talent a, message yɛn wɔ #design wɔ Discord.

#### Adwenkyerɛ afoforo? Ma yɛnhu!

Wowɔ nyansahyɛ foforo? Ka kyerɛ yɛn wɔ #general mu wɔ Discord. Yebetumi asusuw ho na yɛahwɛ sɛ ZecHub's DAO bɛboa anaa.

### Sɛ Wobɛwie

Yɛsrɛ sɛ ntwentwɛn wo nan ase sɛ wubefi ase aboa nnwuma no protocol ahorow a wobu no kɛse no biako. Eyi yɛ ɔkwan pa a wobɛfa so de wo ho ahyɛ Zcash. Sɛ wowɔ nsɛmmisa bi fa ntoboa a wode bɛma ho a, yɛsrɛ sɛ ma yɛnhu wɔ [Discord](#join-the-conversation).

Aseda!
