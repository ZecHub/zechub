# ZecHub hackathon ɔdansifoɔ akwankyerɛ

## TL;DR

- Hu nea enti a worekyekye ansa na woakyerɛw code, mfaso di complexity so
- Ma ɛnyɛ mmerɛw, adwene ketewaa bi a wɔayɛ no yiye boro adwene kɛse bi a wonwiei
- Sua Zcash infrastructure stack no ntɛm, ɛyɛ ɔfã a ɛso yɛ toro sen biara wɔ foro no mu
- Sɛ wo app no ​​tu sika a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ mainnet so, si testnet so, afei ɛkyerɛ sɛ ɛyɛ nokware wɔ mainnet so
- Documentation ne demo a emu da hɔ betumi ayɛ nea ɛho hia sen ade no ankasa
- Nkonimdi yɛ mfiase, ɛkyekye wo din na ebue apon wɔ mpɔtam hɔ

<br/>

## Hena na eyi yɛ ma no

- Wɔn a wɔresi adan nea edi kan a wɔrehyɛn ZecHub anaa Zcash hackathon mu
- Developers a wofi ecosystems afoforo a wɔyɛ foforo wɔ Zcash mu
- Obiara a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔdan hackathon adwuma bi ma ɛbɛyɛ biribi a ɛtra hɔ daa

<br/>

## Fi ase fa nea enti a ɛte saa no so

Ansa na wubebue wo editor no, hu ɔhaw a woredi ho dwuma ne nea enti a obiara bedwen ho. Sɔhwɛ pa yɛ tiawa: sɛ ade a woresi no nni hɔ a, so obi bɛpa ho kyɛw? Si biribi a w’ankasa wode bedi dwuma. Kokoamsɛm ne nea enti a Zcash wɔ hɔ, enti te nea enti a kokoamsɛm ho hia ma nnipa a worekyekye ama wɔn no ase, afei ma ɛno nsiesie adwuma no nyinaa.

<br/>

## Di kan sua stack no

Ahodwiriw a ɛtaa ma adansifo a wofi nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn afoforo mu ne adesua curve ma Zcash infrastructure, ɛnyɛ coding. Ma wo ho bere fa te sɛnea asinasin no hyia ase ansa na woayɛ wo app no. Fi ase de core stack a wɔtaa frɛ no Z kɔ nea ɛto so abiɛsa no so: zebrad, light server, ne wallet. Afei hu developer nnwinnade no yiye:

1. Kenkan developer krataafa a ɛwɔ wiki no so wɔ [zechub.wiki/wɔn a wɔyɛ no](https://zechub.wiki/developers), ɛyɛ beae a edi kan a wɔkamfo kyerɛ sɛ wobegyina
2. Hwehwɛ zingolib ne zingo-cli, a ne frɛ kata nea adwuma bi hia no mu dodow no ara wɔ akwan horow so
3. Hwɛ librustzcash ne ZODL nkyerɛkyerɛmu sika kotoku no ma adansi nneɛma a ɛwɔ fam
4. Sɛ wopɛ FROST adwuma a, fa Zcash Foundation no frostd ne frost-core a efi crates.io no di dwuma, na fa wo ho to AI so de boa nkyerɛase ahorow, ɛwom sɛ FROST a wode bedi dwuma ahobammɔ mu no da so ara gye mmɔdenbɔ ne bere ankasa de

<br/>

## Te nea mainnet kyerɛ ase

Tracks pii hwehwɛ sɛ wo app no ​​ne Zcash mainnet di nkitaho. Wɔ nnwuma mu no eyi kyerɛ sɛ wo dwumadie no, anaa obi a ɔde di dwuma, a AI agent ka ho, de sika ankasa bɛmena anaa ɔgye wɔ mainnet so, anaasɛ ɛkyekyere na ɛma nnwinnadeɛ a ɛma yei tumi yɛ yie no tu mpɔn. Sɛ wo app no ​​yɛ nnwuma a, ɛsɛ sɛ woda no adi wɔ mainnet so wɔ wo submission no mu.

Si testnet so bere a woreyɛ nkɔso. Mainnet dwumadie no gye ZEC ankasa na ɛbɛnya ne boɔ kɛseɛ nko ara wɔ berɛ akyi, enti testnet ne beaeɛ a wɔkamfo kyerɛ sɛ wɔbɛsan ayɛ bio. Dan kɔ mainnet so na woanya adanse a etwa to. Ma asɛm biako ntra w’adwenem bere a woreyɛ wo nsu a ɛsen no: sɛ sika du address a wɔabɔ ho ban so a, ɛsɛ sɛ wo sika kotoku no scan na ɛhwehwɛ ansa na wɔasɛe no, na saa scan no gye bere kakra. Fa saa twɛn tiawa no hyɛ wo app no ​​mu sen sɛ wobɛfa no sɛ sika a ɛba no ayɛ krado sɛ wode bedi dwuma ntɛm ara.

<br/>

## Ma ɛnyɛ mmerɛw

Adwene a ɛnyɛ den a wɔadi ho dwuma yiye adi adwene a ɛyɛ den so nkonim mpɛn pii. Atemmufo ahwɛ sɛ adwene titiriw bi adi nkonim wɔ adwuma bi a ɛyɛ mfiridwuma mu aniberesɛm kɛse so wɔ adeyɛ koro no ara mu, efisɛ edii ɔhaw ankasa ho dwuma na na ɛnyɛ den sɛ wɔbɛte ase. Fa nea ennu nea wususuw sɛ wubetumi awie. Nsɛm nketenkete a wobu ani gu so, scoping kɛse dodo, ne nhwehwɛmu a wohuruw ne mfomso ahorow a ɛma adansifo hwere nkonimbo. Ma wo dwumadie no nyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛte aseɛ na ɛnyɛ den sɛ wobɛtu mmirika, ɛfiri adwene titire no so kɔsi ahyɛdeɛ a ɛdi kan no so.

<br/>

## Di nkonim wɔ sikani 30 a edi kan no mu

Nhwehwɛmufo nya adwene a emu yɛ den ntɛmntɛm, enti nsɛm a wɔka kyerɛ, asɛmti, ne mfonini ahorow no kura mu duru ankasa, ka sɛnea w’ano aduru no yɛ foforo no ho. Documentation ne demo a emu da hɔ nyɛ afterthoughts. Ɛtɔ da bi a w’adwene a wobɛka ho asɛm no ho hia sen adwene no ankasa, efisɛ sɛ obiara nte nea wokyekyee no ase a, entumi nyɛ yiye. Atemmu taa kari pɛ wɔ mfiridwuma mu bun, osuahu a ɔde di dwuma, mfitiasede, ne mfaso a mfaso wɔ so, na nkrataa a emu yɛ den ma ne nyinaa so.

<br/>

## Hwɛ akwan a ɛyɛ den na ɛyɛ tratraa no

Sɛ wopɛ akansi a nnipa pii nni mu a, mpɛn pii no, nnwom a ɛyɛ den no sua esiane sɛ nnipa kakraa bi na wɔsɔ hwɛ ara kwa nti. Akontaabu track no yɛ ɔkwan pa ma wɔn a wɔrefi ase a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔkwati on-chain transaction adwuma. FROST wɔ tumi na wɔmfa nni dwuma yiye, na ɛyɛ fapem a ɛyɛ den ma adwuma bi. Mpɔtam hɔfo nkyerɛ nea ɛsɛ sɛ wosi, enti adwinnade a etumi yɛ adwuma a abɔde a nkwa wom no wɔ dedaw so a wɔbɛto, sen sɛ wobefi ase afi baabiara no yɛ adeyɛ a nyansa wom.

<br/>

## Wɔ hackathon no akyi

Nkonimdi nyɛ ɔkwan no awiei. Nkonimdi ma wo portfolio ne din pa, bue apon wɔ mpɔtam hɔ, na ebetumi ama woanya sika denam nsusuwii ahorow so.

1. Fa adwuma a emu yɛ den kɔ akyiri sɛ nyansahyɛ ma ZecHub DAO anaa Zcash Community Grants, a ɛwɔ ɔkwankyerɛ, nsɛntitiriw, ne sikasɛm ho ntease
2. Kɔ so yɛ nnam wɔ mpɔtam hɔ wɔ forum, Discord, ne X
3. Kɔka Arborist R ne D nhyiamu ho, fa adwene to hɔ, na bisa mmuaeɛ
4. Kɔ so si sɛ woantumi mpo a, na hwɛ hackathon a edi hɔ no
