# ZGo Payment Processor: Gye Zcash a Wonnye Nhwɛso

ZGo yɛ sikatua ho dwumadie a ɛnyɛ sikatua ho dwumadie ma Zcash. Adetɔfoɔ bi tua ka wɔ ZEC mu firi wɔn ankasa sika kotokuo mu, ZGo hwɛ Zcash blockchain no so ma asɛm no, na sika no kɔ aguadifoɔ no sika kotokuo mu tẽẽ denam shielded transfer so. ZGo nkura sika no wɔ ntam da.

Saa akwankyerɛ yi kyerɛkyerɛ sɛnea sikatua kwan no yɛ adwuma, sɛnea wɔhyehyɛ akontaabu, ne sɛnea wɔde ZGo ne Xero ne WooCommerce bɛka abom. Ɛsan nso ka mfomso abien a ɛde ɔhaw dodow no ara a ɛba bere a edi kan wɔ nhyehyɛe mu ba no so.

## Dɛn nti na wode ZGo bedi dwuma

Cryptocurrency sikatua processors dodow no ara yɛ custodial. Sika di kan kɔ fam wɔ nea ɔyɛ adwuma no akontaabu mu na wɔde kɔma aguadifo no akyiri yi, a ɛkyerɛ sɛ obi a ɔto so abiɛsa di sika no so bere tiaa bi na obetumi de asie, akyɛ, anaasɛ wabɔ ho amanneɛ.

ZGo fa ɔkwan a ɛne no bɔ abira so. Katua fi adetɔfo no sika kotoku mu kɔ aguadifo no sika kotoku mu tẽẽ denam Zcash shielded asɛmdi so. Processor no nkutoo na ɛyɛ invoice no na ɔhwɛ blockchain no ma ɛyɛ nokware. Ntamgyinafo biara nni hɔ a ɛkari pɛ, sika a woyi fi mu nni hɔ, na obi a ɔto so abiɛsa biara nni hɔ a obetumi akura sika a wobesiesie no mu.

Wɔ aguadifo fam no, eyi kyerɛ nneɛma abiɛsa a mfaso wɔ so: ZEC a ɛba no a wɔbɛhwɛ so koraa, asɛm no ho kokoamsɛm a wɔbɛbɔ ho ban denam default so, ne ahotoso a wonni wɔ centralized provider a ɔbɛtra intanɛt so anaasɛ solvent so.

## Sɛnea ZGo yɛ adwuma

Katua a ɛkɔ so no yɛ pɛ ɛmfa ho sɛ wɔde ZGo bedi dwuma a egyina hɔ ma ne ho, ɛnam Xero so, anaasɛ ɛnam WooCommerce so:

1. Oguadifoɔ no yɛ sikatua abisadeɛ wɔ ZGo mu, a ɛkyerɛ sɛ QR koodu a sika dodoɔ, invoice ID, ne Zcash gye address.
2. Adetɔfoɔ no de Zcash sika kotokuo scan QR no (Orchard, Sapling, ne Transparent address ahodoɔ no nyinaa boa wɔ WordPress plugin no so) na ɔpene sikatua no so.
3. Wɔde asɛm no kɔ Zcash network no so sɛ shielded transfer fi adetɔfo no sika kotoku mu kɔ aguadifo no sika kotoku mu.
4. ZGo hwɛ Zcash blockchain no so ma asɛm no.
5. Wɔ si so dua anum akyi no, ZGo hyɛ sikatua no agyirae sɛ nea etwa to na ɛbɔ nkabom biara a ɛka bom (Xero, WooCommerce, anaa wɛbhook) amanneɛ.

Anum-si so dua aboboano no ne nɔma titiriw no. Biribiara a edi kan no yɛ sika a wɔretua, na ɛnyɛ sika a wɔagye. Ɛsɛ sɛ nneɛma a wɔkra no mmamu, nneɛma a wɔakora so no foforo, ne adeyɛ biara a wontumi nsakra wɔ aguadifo afã no twɛn anammɔn 5.

ZGo yɛ adwuma wɔ nnɛyi browser biara mu wɔ desktop anaa mobile so, a enni instɔlehyɛn biara wɔ afã abien no nyinaa. Adetɔfo no hia Zcash sika kotoku; aguadifoɔ no hia Zcash sika kotokuo ne ZGo akonta.

## Akontaabu a wobɛhyehyɛ

Sɛ wobɛbɔ ZGo akonta a, Zcash sika kotoku a ZEC kakraa bi na ɛwɔ mu ho hia. ZEC sika ketewa no tua on-chain fee a wɔbɔ wɔ akontaabu-fiase asɛm no ho. Zcash sika kotoku titiriw biara yɛ adwuma ma eyi; hwɛ [ZecHub Sikakorabea](https://zechub.wiki/wallets) ma mprempren akwan horow a wobetumi apaw.

Nhyehyɛe titiriw no:

1. Bue [zgo.cash no ho](https://zgo.cash/) wɔ browser bi mu.
2. Fa Zcash sika kotoku a ɛwɔ aguadifo no tumi ase yɛ akontaabu. Ɛsɛ sɛ saa sika kotoku yi kura nsafe no. Address a wɔde sie a wɔde sesa nneɛma rentumi nyɛ adwuma (hwɛ [Mfomso a wɔtaa yɛ](#common-mistakes)).
3. Hwɛ sɛ sika kotoku no yɛ nokware denam mfiase asɛm ketewa no a wode bɛmena no so.
4. Hyehyɛ address a wogye no. Sikatua a wɔfa saa akontaabu yi so di ho dwuma nyinaa bɛkɔ fam wɔ sika kotoku yi mu.

Sɛ akontaabu no yɛ adwuma wie a, aguadifo koro no ara betumi de ZGo adi dwuma de atua sika pɛnkoro (QR koodu biako wɔ pop-up adeyɛ bi mu) anaasɛ ɔde wire akɔ nhyehyɛe a ɛtra hɔ daa mu denam Xero anaa WooCommerce so.

## ZKɔ ne Xero

[Xero, na ɔde ne nsa kyerɛɛ ne so](https://www.xero.com/) yɛ cloud accounting platform a nnwuma nketewa ne akɛse pii de di dwuma. ZGo–Xero nkabom no ma aguadifoɔ bi de sika a wɔde tua ho ka ma wɔ Xero mu, ma adetɔfoɔ no tua wɔ ZEC mu, na ɛma Xero hyɛ sika a wɔde tua ho ka no agyiraeɛ berɛ a asɛm no asi so dua pɛn no.

Sɛnea ɛyɛ adwuma:

1. Oguadifo no yɛ invoice wɔ Xero mu sɛnea ɔtaa yɛ no.
2. ZGo de Zcash sikatua kwan bi bata invoice no ho.
3. Adetɔfoɔ no nam wɔn sika kotokuo so tua ka wɔ ZEC mu.
4. ZGo hwɛ [Zcash blockchain no so](https://z.cash/) ama asɛm no.
5. Sɛ wɔasi so dua mpɛn anum akyi no, ZGo san bɔ sika a wɔatua no ho amanneɛ kyerɛ Xero, na ɛhyɛ invoice no agyirae sɛ wɔasiesie.

ZEC no si aguadifo no sika kotoku mu, ɛnyɛ akontaabu biara a ZGo di so anaa Xero di so mu. Akontaabu kyerɛwtohɔ a ɛwɔ Xero no ne on-chain settlement no kɔ so yɛ pɛ.

Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wohyehyɛ nea edi kan a, di nantew a wɔatu ho ama no akyi: [Xero Integration Configuration](https://hedgedoc.vergara.tech/s/4iXC67fmb).

## ZKɔ ne WooCommerce

Wɔ intanɛt so sotɔɔ ahorow a ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ [WooCommerce](https://woocommerce.com/) ne [WordPress](https://wordpress.org/), ZGo de plugin a wɔatu ho ama ma. Plugin no de Zcash ka ho sɛ ɔkwan a wɔfa so tua ka wɔ checkout mu na ɛdi order tebea no ho dwuma ankasa bere a sikatua no si so dua no.

<img width="672" height="378" alt="ZGo WooCommerce plugin checkout and order flow" src="/content-images/55a791bb-1947-4f55-b5b9-55083be8ed49-2bc8d2571e.webp" />

Nsu a ɛsen fi awiei kosi awiei wɔ WooCommerce sotɔɔ bi mu:

1. Adetɔfoɔ no du checkout na ɔpaw Zcash sɛ ɔkwan a wɔbɛfa so atua.
2. Plugin no ma wonya sikatua adesrɛ na ɛkyerɛ QR code no wɔ checkout krataafa no so.
3. Adetɔfo no fi wɔn sika kotoku mu tua ka.
4. Adwuma no bɔ amanneɛ kɔ Zcash ntwamutam no so na ZGo fi ase hwɛ so.
5. Wɔ si so dua anum akyi no, ZGo bɔ sikatua no ho amanneɛ sɛ ɛyɛ nea etwa to ma plugin no.
6. Plugin no hyɛ WooCommerce ahyɛdeɛ no agyiraeɛ sɛ wɔatua na ɛyɛ ahyɛdeɛ database no foforɔ.

Wotua ahyɛde no bere a anammɔn 6 no awie nkutoo. Wobetumi akyerɛ aman a edi kan (broadcast, confirmations a edi kan) akyerɛ adetɔfo no sɛ "akatua a wɔanya, a wɔretwɛn sɛ wobesi so dua," nanso ɛsɛ sɛ inventory, mmamu, ne downstream automation biara twɛn tebea a etwa to no.

Plugin no nso de administrative dashboard bi hyɛ WordPress mu, baabi a aguadifoɔ no bɛtumi ahwɛ ahyɛdeɛ ne ZEC sikatua a ɛba no wɔ WooCommerce ahyɛdeɛ hwɛbea a ɛyɛ daa no nkyɛn. Plugin no boa Zcash address ahorow a ɛwɔ hɔ mprempren nyinaa: Orchard, Sapling, ne Transparent. Adetɔfo a wotua sika fi sika kotoku biara a ɛne mmara hyia mu no betumi awie asɛm no.

## Su

**Non-custodial.** Katua no fi adetɔfo no sika kotoku mu tẽẽ kɔ aguadifo no sika kotoku mu denam nnwuma a wɔabɔ ho ban so. ZGo nkura sika no wɔ ntam da, na aguadifo no kura tumidi nyinaa mu wɔ ne nyinaa mu.

**Flexible deployment.** ZGo betumi adi dwuma ama awia biako wɔ pop-up gua so, ama daa point-of-sale nhyehyɛe, anaasɛ sɛ backend ma intanɛt so sotɔɔ denam Xero anaa WooCommerce nkabom so.

**Browser-based.** Install biara nni hɔ wɔ adetɔfo anaa aguadifo no afã. ZGo yɛ adwuma wɔ nnɛyi browser biara mu wɔ desktop anaa mobile so.

**Wallet compatibility.** Zcash wallet akɛseɛ, a deɛ ɛboa Orchard, Sapling, ne Transparent address ahodoɔ ka ho, tumi tua ZGo invoice a nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ biara nni adetɔfoɔ no afã.

**Nkabom.** Nkitahodi tẽẽ a ɛne Xero (akontaabu) ne WooCommerce (e-commerce) kata aguadifo adwumayɛ nhyehyɛe abien a ɛtaa ba no so fi adaka no mu.

## Mfomso ahorow a wɔtaa di

**Ahyɛdeɛ no a wɔbɛfa no sɛ wɔatua ansa na wɔahyɛ no den anum.** Broadcast transaction ne sikatua a wɔasi so dua nyɛ pɛ. Aguadi no da so ara tumi ntumi nsi so dua anaasɛ wɔmfa nsi ananmu. Sɛ wɔasi so dua anum akyi nkutoo na ZGo bɔ sika a wɔatua no ho amanneɛ sɛ ɛyɛ nea etwa to, na ɛno nkutoo na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔhyɛ ahyɛde no agyirae sɛ wɔatua wɔ nsu no ase. Sɛ aguadifo bi hyehyɛ nneɛma a wɔakora so anaasɛ mmamu ma ɛkanyan wɔ broadcast event no so a, nnaadaa anaasɛ entumi ntua sika no bɛma wɔahwere ade ankasa.

**ZGo a wode bɛkyerɛ exchange deposit address.** Ɛte sɛ Zcash address, nanso exchange deposit address ahorow no, exchange no na ɛhwɛ so, ɛnyɛ aguadifo no. Exchange no kura nsafe no, a ɛkyerɛ sɛ exchange no kura sika no, a ɛdi nea enti a wɔde processor a ɛnyɛ custodial processor di dwuma no so nkonim. Ɛsɛ sɛ sika kotoku address a wɔahyehyɛ wɔ ZGo mu no yɛ sika kotoku a ne aba kasasin no aguadifo no di so tẽẽ.

**ZGo a wobɛfa no sɛ sika kotoku.** ZGo yɛ sikatua dwumadie, ɛnyɛ sika kotokuo. Ɛnkora safe so, ɛnkura sika a aka, anaasɛ ɛmma oguadini no nsɛe sika. Zcash sika kotoku a ɛyɛ soronko a ɛwɔ aguadifo no tumi ase ho hia na ama wɔanya sika a ZGo de fa kwan so no.

## Awie

ZGo ma aguadifoɔ kwan a wɔbɛfa so agye Zcash sikatua a wɔmfa wɔn ho nhyɛ ntamgyinafoɔ so, na wɔmfa wɔn ho nhyɛ ntamgyinafoɔ bi so, na wɔmfa nkitahodie ho nsɛm nkɔda adi wɔ ɔmanfoɔ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn bi so. Nkabom mmienu no (Xero ne WooCommerce) kata aguadifoɔ adwumayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛtaa ba no so; biribiara a aka no, wobetumi de ZGo adi dwuma a egyina hɔ ma ne ho fi browser biara so.

Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wohyehyɛ a, ɔkwan no yɛ tiawa: nya Zcash sika kotoku, yɛ akontaabu wɔ [zgo.cash](https://zgo.cash/), na fi ase yɛ sikatua ho abisade ahorow tẽẽ anaasɛ fa nkabom a ɛfa ho no hyɛ mu.
