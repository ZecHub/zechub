## Zcash na ɛyɛ 

* Ɛlektrɔnik sika a wɔabɔ no kokoam. Cryptocurrency a edi kan a ɛyɛ zero-knowledge encryption a wɔde tua kokoam atipɛnfo ne atipɛnfo sikatua.

hyɛ no nsow: Sɛ wopɛ data a ɛyɛ nokware a wowɔ WO HO TO a, wɔkamfo kyerɛ sɛ fa w’ankasa node [zebrad] a edi mũ no di dwuma. Wubetumi asiesie no
z3 infrastructure [ zebrad + zainod/lightwalletd + "sika kotoku a wɔpaw wɔ ha" ] sɛ wopɛ ano aduru a edi mũ na ɛyɛ den a. Wo kwan a wobɛfa so akɔ hɔ
data a wɔde RPC's(Remote Procedure Calls) di dwuma no .

Sɛ wopɛ sɛnea eyi yɛ adwuma no ho ɔyɛkyerɛ ntɛm a, hwɛ video yi:

## Adwumayɛbea Demo

na Zcash blockchain no na ɛyɛ adwuma.
