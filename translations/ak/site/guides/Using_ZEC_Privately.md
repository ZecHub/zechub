# ZEC a wode bedi dwuma, wɔ kokoam

#### Shielded (Private) vs. Nea ɛda adi pefee

Sɛnea ɛte mprempren no, address ne nkitahodi ahorow abien na ɛwɔ Zcash mu, wɔabɔ ho ban na ɛda adi pefee. Nsonsonoe a ɛda ZEC a wɔabɔ ho ban ne nea ɛda adi ntam no yɛ mmerɛw yiye. Shielded ZEC ma wo sika ne wo nnwuma yɛ kokoam, sɛ baabi a transparent ZEC yɛ adwuma te sɛ Bitcoin, transparent koraa. Eyi kyerɛ sɛ obi betumi ahwɛ wo sika a aka ne wo nnwuma nyinaa sɛ onim wo address a.

Sɛ nkurɔfo di kan fi ase de ZEC di dwuma a, ebia wɔrenhu address ko a wɔde redi dwuma. Eyi te saa efisɛ ɛnyɛ exchanges nyinaa na ɛboa shielded ZEC ne/anaa shielded ZEC withdrawals. 

Enti, sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, sɛ obi de Coinbase di dwuma na wɔtɔ ZEC a, anka ɔbɛtɔ ZEC a ɛda adi pefee na ɔbɛtumi atwe saa ZEC no akɔ address a ɛda adi wɔ sika kotokuo mu nko ara. Walets te sɛ [Zodl](https://zodl.com/) betumi abɔ sika a wɔde kɔ address a ɛda adi pefee so de adi eyi ho dwuma no ho ban, nanso ɛnyɛ obiara na onim saa. Nnipa pii, sɛ yɛbɛka no tiawa a, wɔde ZEC di dwuma wɔ ɔkwan a wɔn exchange anaa primary wallet ma wɔn kwan so.

#### Hwɛ sɛ wo ZEC no abɔ ho ban

Yɛhyɛ nyansa sɛ obiara mfa ne ho nhwɛ ne ZEC so. Nea ɛkyerɛ ne sɛ, fa wo ZEC no fi exchange so kɔ sika kotoku mu. Ɔkwan pa a wobɛfa so ahu sɛ wode shielded, a.k.a private, ZEC redi dwuma ne sɛ wobɛhwɛ address a balance no te mu Sɛ address no fi ase wɔ "z" anaa "u1" , ɛnde wo balance no yɛ shielded. Sɛ address no fi ase de "t" a, ɛnde balance no yɛ transparent.

Mpɛn pii no, akwan abien na ɛwɔ hɔ a wobɛfa so akɔ ZEC a wɔabɔ ho ban no so.

Efi exchange a ɛboa **shielded** withdrawals:

  1. Kɔtɔ ZEC wɔ exchange mu
  2. Fi ase yi sika a woyi no fi ase wɔ nsakrae no mu
  3. Bue wo ZEC sika kotoku a wɔabɔ ho ban no na hwɛ hu sɛ address a wogye no fi ase de "u1" anaa "z".
  4. Run withdrawal no fi wo exchange no mu

Efi exchange a ɛboa **transparent** a wɔayi afi mu:


  1. Kɔtɔ ZEC wɔ exchange mu
  2. Fi ase yi sika a woyi no fi ase wɔ nsakrae no mu
  3. Bue wo autoshielding ZEC sika kotoku no na fa address a ɛda adi pefee a wogye no di dwuma
  4. Run withdrawal no fi wo exchange no mu
  5. Twɛn si so dua du, na afei bɔ ZEC no ho ban fi wo address a ɛda adi pefee no so kɔ address a wɔabɔ ho ban no so


Nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛkyerɛ sɛnea wobɛtwe ZEC afi exchange mu ni. Hyɛ no nsow sɛ eyi yɛ sika a wɔtwe fi mu a wɔabɔ ho ban.

#### Nkitahodi ahorow

Bere a woahwɛ ahu sɛ wo ZEC no wɔ sika kotoku a wɔabɔ ho ban a ɛboa address ahorow a wɔabɔ ho ban mu akyi no, afei wubetumi asi gyinae sɛ wopɛ sɛ wo ne saa ZEC no di nkitaho anaa. Transacting ne ZEC yɛ super mmerɛw. Wubetumi de ZEC akɔ address ahorow a wɔabɔ ho ban anaasɛ ɛda adi pefee so a egyina onipa no pɛ so. Sɛnea ɛte wɔ sikasɛm mu aguadi biara ho no, hokwan nketenkete wɔ hɔ sɛ nkurɔfo betumi de data akɔ. ZEC yɛ nea eye sen biara wɔ ko a wɔko tia data leakage, nanso ɛno nkyerɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wode di dwuma a ahwɛyiye biara nni ho. Nneɛma bi a wobɛpɛ sɛ wokwati bere a wo ne ZEC redi nkitaho ni.

- Wo address a wɔabɔ ho ban no a wobɛda no adi
- Address a wɔabɔ ho ban a wɔde di dwuma sɛ pass through ma t-addresses (a.k.a "mixing") .
- Mmirikatu, na woda wo mmirikatu adi, dodow a ɛkorɔn a wɔabɔ ho ban ama nnwuma a ɛda adi pefee
- Daa a wobɛma nkurɔfo ahu baabi a wosɛe sika shielded ZEC


Ne titiriw no, ade a eye sen biara a wode wo ZEC bɛyɛ ne sɛ wode bɛhyɛ sika kotoku a wɔabɔ ho ban mu, adi nkitaho wɔ address ahorow a wɔabɔ ho ban ntam, na woahwɛ yiye wɔ sɛnea wode ZEC bedi dwuma wɔ baguam (i.e. kɔfetɔnbea). Kokoam nsɛm a wobɛhwɛ ahu no de asɛyɛde bi ba.
