## 4. Multisig Nkitahodi a Wɔyɛ: Setting Up Distributed Key Generation (Multisig) .

Multisig a ɛwɔ Zkool mu no de **Distributed Key Generation (DKG)** di dwuma de hwɛ sɛ wɔn a wɔde wɔn ho hyɛ mu pii di akonta a wɔkyɛ so.



### Anamɔn 1: Fi ase DKG
Paw **Edin** ma sika kotoku a wɔakyekyɛ (sɛ nhwɛso no Anabelle).

Set **Nnipa dodow a wɔde wɔn ho bɛhyɛ mu**.
  
Paw wo **Participant ID** no.
  
Kyerɛkyerɛ **Nnipa dodow a wɔde wɔn nsa hyɛ ase a wɔhwehwɛ (Threshold)**.
    
Paw **Sika a Wɔde Ma Akontaabu**.
  

![img7](/content-images/8a90ca85-5439-4937-b16d-a570e69d55f0-1477202a57.webp)



### Anamɔn 2: Fa Ɔdefo a Wɔde Wɔn Ho Hyehyɛ Mu Addresses Ka Ho
- Hyehyɛ obiara a ɔde ne ho hyɛɛ mu no **Unified Address** (wɔkamfo kyerɛ).


**Hyɛ no nsow:** Sɛ wode Orchard nkutoo anaa Sapling nkutoo address di dwuma a, multisig no bɛto saa pool no nkutoo so (Orchard anaa Sapling). 
Wei kyerɛ sɛ sika kotoku a wɔkyɛ no ntumi nnya sika mfi pool afoforo hɔ. 
Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wo ne no hyia na wotumi sesa a, fa **Unified Addresses** di dwuma bere nyinaa. 


### Anamɔn 3: Tu mmirika DKG Rounds
Twɛn ma wɔn a wɔbɛkɔ bi no nyinaa bɛsesa **round 1** ne **round 2** packages.

## Awie

Sɛ wode Zkool di dwuma a, wobɛtumi: ayɛ akonta, de sika amena na woagye, na wode Distributed Key Generation asiesie **multisig wallet**. Wei hwɛ ma **ahobanbɔ a ɛkɔ anim** ne **adwumayɛ ne ankorankoro sikakorabea sohwɛ**.
