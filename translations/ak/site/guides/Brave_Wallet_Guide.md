# Sɛnea Wɔde Brave Wallet Kɔma na Wogye Shielded Zcash (ZEC): Anamɔn biara a Wɔde Siesie Nsɛm Ho Akwankyerɛ

Brave Wallet yɛ ahobanbɔ, native crypto wallet a wɔasi no tẽẽ wɔ Brave browser no mu - enhia sɛ wɔtrɛw mu.


## Nea Enti a Shielded ZEC Mmoa Ho Hia


Zcash yɛ cryptocurrencies nko ara a ɛde kokoamsɛm ma default a ɛwɔ shielded transactions no mu biako.

Mprempren Brave Wallet boa Orchard shielded ZEC, na ɛma wotumi yɛ kokoam nkitahodi a wonni softwea a ɛto so abiɛsa.

Afei deɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no bɛtumi de ZEC amena/agye a wɔrenna balances anaa metadata adi - ne nyinaa wɔ Brave browser no mu.

## Ɔfã 1: Brave Wallet a Wobɛhyehyɛ

**Anamɔn a wɔakata so:**

1. Bue Brave Browser (desktop nkutoo mprempren) .

2. Klik Wallet icon no so anaa kɔ brave://wallet

3. Hyehyɛ wo sika kotoku (bɔ anaa fa ba) .

4. Kɔ crypto dashboard no so


![img1](/content-images/f54cd1a1-8569-4925-ba1c-7597d030593e-8d83734cd5.webp)


## Ɔfa 2: Zcash (ZEC) a wɔde bɛka Brave Wallet ho

**Anamɔn a wɔakata so:**

1. Wɔ Wallet dashboard no mu no, klik "Manage Assets" so.

2. Hwehwɛ ZEC na ma ɛnyɛ adwuma

3. Afei ZEC bɛda adi wɔ wo sika kotoku mu


![img2](/content-images/6f2b2190-cf55-4394-9d5f-29ff9b5bb525-13d37cb455.webp)


## Part 3: Receiving Shielded ZEC (Unified Address)

**Anamɔn a wɔakata so:**

1. Klik ZEC wɔ w’agyapade mu

2. Paw "Gye".

3. Brave yɛ Unified Address (UA) - address baako a ɛboa nnwuma a wɔabɔ ho ban ne nea ɛda adi pefee

4. Kɔpi anaa scan QR code no

5. Kyɛ wo UA no ma nea ɔde kɔmaa no



![img3](/content-images/53c940b6-1a03-4fa7-aefa-d3478f678a88-23f68a3855.webp)



## Ɔfa 4: Shielded ZEC a wɔde bɛmena wɔ kokoam

**Anamɔn a wɔakata so:**

1. Paw ZEC -> Send

2. Fa nea woregye no Unified Address anaa address a wɔabɔ ho ban no hyɛ mu

3. Hyehyɛ sika dodow

4. Si asɛm a ɛfa asɛm no ho no so dua

5. Klik Send - seesei wɔde sika no kɔ kokoam, de Orchard pool no di dwuma


![img4 na ɛwɔ hɔ](/content-images/Internet_20250808_172118_4-e6df0f7fe2.webp)




## Address Ahorow Ahorow

Nkyerɛkyerɛmu a ɛfa Zcash address type biara ho ni:

**t-addr (t1...)** - Address a ɛda adi pefee yɛ adwuma te sɛ Bitcoin address, a ɛkyerɛ sɛ nkitahodi yɛ baguam wɔ blockchain no so. Ɛnyɛ den sɛ wode bedi dwuma wɔ sika kotoku a ɛnyɛ Zcash mu nanso ɛmma kokoam nsɛm.

**z-addr (zs...)** - Address a wɔabɔ ho ban de Zcash kokoam nsɛm (Sprout/Sapling) di dwuma de sie nea ɔde kɔmaa, nea ogye, ne sika dodow. View keys anaa owura no nkutoo na obetumi ada nsɛm no adi.

**UA (u... anaa QR)** - Address a Wɔaka abom no ka address ahorow pii (transparent, shielded, Orchard) bom yɛ biako. Eyi ma kwan ma ɛne ne ho hyia kɛse bere a wɔpɛ kokoam nsɛm wɔ baabi a ɛbɛyɛ yiye no.

## Awiei: Wɔayɛ Kokoam Nsɛm a Wɔde Di Dwuma

Brave Wallet ne ZEC a wɔabɔ ho ban no, seesei obiara tumi nya kokoam nsɛm - software soronko anaa nimdeɛ a ɛkɔ anim biara nhia.

Ɛyɛ papa ma nsɛm ho amanneɛbɔfo, wɔn a wɔyɛ adwumaden, anaa ankorankoro a wɔpɛ kokoam nsɛm.

Sɔ wo shielded transaction a edi kan hwɛ nnɛ wɔ Brave mu!


**Tena kokoam. Tra hɔ kwa. Fa ZEC kɔma kokoam ne Brave Wallet.**
