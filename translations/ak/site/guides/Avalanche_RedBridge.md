# Zcash Avalanche RedBridge a ɛwɔ hɔ no

Zcash Avalanche RedBridge yɛ decentralized bridge a ɛma wotumi yɛ adwuma wɔ Zcash (ZEC) ne Avalanche (AVAX) blockchains ntam. Wɔayɛ saa bridge yi sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɛbɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wɔde ZEC bɛkɔ Avalanche blockchain no so a ɛnyɛ den, de Avalanche a ɛyɛ adwuma a ɛkɔ soro, ɛka a ɛba fam, ne ecofriendly consensus mechanisms no adi dwuma bere a wɔkora Zcash no kokoamsɛm ho nneɛma so.

RedBridge no boa nsɛm ahodoɔ a wɔde di dwuma, a crosschain decentralized finance (DeFi), ankorankoro nkitahodiɛ, ne sika a wɔkyɛ ka ho, na ɛma Zcash wuranom tumi ma wɔtrɛ kwan a wɔfa so kɔ Avalanche abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no mu. Wɔnam decentralized nodes ne oracle, a wɔfrɛ no **ZavaX**, a ɛhwɛ ma wɔde data kɔ baabi foforo a wotumi de ho to so ne bo a wɔagye atom wɔ Zcash ne Avalanche ntam so na ɛyɛ adwuma.

### Nneɛma Titiriw a Ɛwɔ Hɔ

Privacy Preserving Interoperability: Ɛma Zcash dwumadiefoɔ kwan ma wɔhwɛ kokoamsɛm so berɛ a wɔde DeFi applications redi dwuma wɔ Avalanche so.
Decentralized Oracle ZavaX: Ɛka oracle nhyehyɛe bi bom de hwɛ hu sɛ ZEC/AVAX bo ho nsɛm a ɛyɛ nokware, na ɛma kwan ma wɔyɛ crosschain adwumayɛ a ahotoso nnim.
Scalable ne Eco Friendly: Ɛde Avalanches consensus model di dwuma, na ɛma nkitahodi a ɛkɔ ntɛmntɛm a enni nneɛma a atwa yɛn ho ahyia so nkɛntɛnso ketewaa bi.
Mmoa a wɔde ma DeFi ne DApps: Mprempren Zcash wuranom betumi de wɔn ho ahyɛ DeFi platform ahorow mu wɔ Avalanche a wɔrensɛe kokoam nsɛm.

### Mfiridwuma mu Nneɛma a Wɔde Yɛ Adwuma

**ZavaX Oracle a wɔde ahyɛ aman nsa**
Nkyerɛkyerɛmu: ZavaX oracle no ho hia kɛse ma bridge no, ɛma crosschain bo feeds na ɛma ZEC kɔ AVAX nsakrae a wonni ahotoso tumi.
[Nkitahodi a ɛkɔ Oracle so](https://zavax-oracle.red.dev)

**Cross Chain Bridge Apam**
Nkyerɛkyerɛmu: Smart contract architecture a ɛboa Zcash Avalanche bridge, a ɛdi sika a wɔde asie, nsakraeɛ, ne sika a wɔayi afiri ZEC mu ho dwuma.

**Kokoamsɛm Layer Nkabom**
Nkyerɛkyerɛmu: Ɛhwɛ sɛ wɔakora Zcash kokoam nsɛm so wɔ bridging nhyehyɛe no nyinaa mu, na ɛma kwan ma wɔyɛ kokoam crosschain nkitahodi.

* Nneɛma a wɔde bɛma: 
Wɔ Q1 2025 mu no, kuw no de too gua sɛ wɔde [red·bridge demo website no bɛba](https://redbridge-demo.red.dev/index.html), baabi a obiara betumi asɔ osuahu a ɔde di dwuma no ahwɛ, de nsɛm ama, na wahyɛ nkɔso ho nyansa. Ɛsan nso yɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a wɔfa so de nnipa a wɔnyɛ mfiridwuma ho nimdeɛ bɛba adwuma no mu.

* Kuw no de Zebra dii dwuma maa red·bridge a etwa to no. Sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛsɔ ahwɛ no, wɔyɛɛ node abiɛsa a ɛwɔ wɔn sɔhwɛ blockchain, ZavaX Oracle, a ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ Avalanche Fuji testnet so no mu abien foforo. Wɔyɛɛ node a etwa to no foforo yiye, mprempren [Zavax Oracle](https://zavax-oracle.red.dev/) mprempren ɛtu mmirika wɔ ZEBRA so!

* Wɔ Q1 mu wɔ afe 2025 mu no, wɔde code hyɛɛ red.bridge wɛbsaet no so sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɛbɛma wotumi ahu nneɛma anan afi kɔkɔɔ, Esum, Hann, ne Zebra so a ɛne nea edi kan no bɔ abira, a na ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ.

* Asɛm foforo ne sɛ kuw no bɛma red·bridge L1 no ayɛ adwuma wɔ Avalanche mainnet no so wɔ December 2025. Mfiase no, ɛbɛyɛ oracle ama Zcash blockchain no na afei, ɛno akyi bere tiaa bi no, ama Bitcoin nso. Ɛno mu no, abisadeɛ biara bɛbɔ ka 0.001 AVAX wɔ gas token mu. Saa ɔdan yi bɛma L1 anaa smart contract biara a ɛwɔ Avalanche no atumi abisa data a ne boɔ nyɛ den afiri Zcash ne Bitcoin wɔ decentralized kwan so.

* Wɔ Q2 mu no, kuw no de ACP-77 a ɛyɛ ade titiriw (a wɔfrɛ no Avalanche9000) kɔmaa Avalanche Foundation sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a red.bridge guardian mmirikatu no ntɛm na obiara betumi atɔ. Mfiase no, na ɛsɛ sɛ validators de sika bɛyɛ 2000 AVAX; nanso, ɛnam Avalanche9000costs no nti, validators hia AVAX 1 (ɔsram) pɛ. Bio nso, saa ade titiriw yi nso wie nhyehyɛe a wɔayɛ sɛ wɔde ZF FROST dwumadie no bedi dwuma, a ɛma Guardian biara nya kyɛfa a ɔde ne nsa hyɛ aseɛ ma ahobanbɔ, a wɔakyekyɛ wɔ bridge wallet no so tumi.

* Bra Q1 ne Q2 wɔ 2026 mu, red.bridge bɛgye ne RBR token (kan no na ɛyɛ ZAX) airdrop ama Zcash ne Avalanche mpɔtam hɔfoɔ. Sɛnea nea ɔhyehyɛɛ red.dev kyerɛ no, wɔbɛgye testnet a ɛkanyan wɔn a wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no benya hokwan anya RBR bere a wɔboa ma wɔsɔ bridge no hwɛ no.
