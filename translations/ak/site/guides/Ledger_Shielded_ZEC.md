# Ledger Shielded ZEC Dwumadie Nkyerɛkyerɛmu

Wɔ 23 September 2026 Ledger de native shielded ZEC kaa Ledger Wallet Desktop ho. Mprempren Zcash akontaabu biako kura sika a ɛda adi pefee ne kokoam sika a aka wɔ Zcash's Ironwood pool mu. Wo kokoam safe no tra wo Ledger, na wɔhwɛ asɛm biara a wodi mu na wɔde wɔn nsa hyɛ ase wɔ afiri no so.

**Desktop nkutoo.** Ledger's dawurubɔ no ka sɛ shielded ZEC no "wɔ Ledger Wallet Desktop nkutoo so. Mobile mmoa nni hɔ saa bere yi, nanso ɛreba nnansa yi ara." Desktop app no nso kyerɛ amanneɛbɔ koro no ara. Wɔhwɛɛ mu wɔ 15 Ɔkɔtɔberɛ 2026.

## Ahoboa

- Ledger mfiri a ɛde Zcash app no di dwuma: Nano S Plus, Nano X, Stax anaa Flex. Wɔankyekyere mprempren Zcash app no amma mfitiase Nano S.
- Ledger Wallet Desktop 4.21 anaa nea ɛba akyiri yi, a wɔtwe fi hɔ nkutoo [ledger.com so na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://www.ledger.com). Saa akwankyerɛ yi de version 4.24.0 dii dwuma.
- Zcash app a aba foforo wɔ wo Ledger so (3.9.4 bere a wɔkyerɛw akwankyerɛ yi).
- ZEC kakraa bi a wode bɛsɔ ahwɛ. Wɔyɛɛ anammɔn biara a ɛwɔ aseɛ ha no wɔ mainnet so a 0.001 ZEC. Adwuma biara gye network fee ketewaa bi (0.00015 ZEC wɔ yɛn sɔhwɛ no mu).
- Wo kasasin a ɛwɔ nsɛmfua 24 a wode bɛsan agye no tra krataa so. Mfa nkyerɛw nkɔ kɔmputa so da anaasɛ nkyɛ.

## Anamɔn biara akwankyerɛ (Ledger Wallet Desktop)

**1.1. Install anaa update Zcash app no**
- Bue Ledger Wallet, fa bata na bue wo Ledger, na bue **Me Ledger**. Ma kwan ma nkitahodi no nkɔ afiri no so.
- Hwehwɛ **Zcash** wɔ app catalog no mu, afei klik **Install**, anaa **Update** sɛ wɔahyɛ no dedaw a.
- Bue Zcash app no wɔ wo Ledger. Screen no kenkan sɛ "Zcash app no ayɛ krado". Scroll nifa kɔ **App info** na hwɛ version no.

**2.1. Fa Zcash akontaabu bi ka ho**
- Wɔ Ledger Wallet mu no, paw **Fa akontaabu ka ho**. Wɔ **Paw agyapade** mu no, hwehwɛ Zcash na paw **Zcash (ZEC)**, ɛnyɛ token a wɔabɔ ho ban te sɛ Binance-Peg Zcash Token.
- Sɛ Zcash app no abue wɔ wo Ledger, Ledger Wallet hwehwɛ wo akonta. Tick no wɔ **Paw akontaabu a wopɛ sɛ wode ka ho** ase na klik **Confirm**.
- Akontaabu krataafa no kyerɛ **Kari a ɛwɔ hɔ**, **Nka a ɛda adi pefee** ne **Private balance**, ne **Enable shielded** bɔtn.

![Zcash account in Ledger Wallet Desktop showing the desktop-only notice, transparent and private balances, and the Enable shielded button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-01-account-desktop-only.png)

**3.1. Ma private balance no nyɛ adwuma**
- Klik **Ma shielded** nyɛ adwuma. **Enable Zcash private balance** mfɛnsere no wɔ anammɔn anan: Awoda, UFVK, Device ne Confirmation.
- **Awoda:** sɛ wopɛ akontaabu foforo a, fa awoda no sorokɔ sie nnɛ da. Tick "This account received shielded ZEC in the past" nko ara sɛ wunim sɛ ɛyɛɛ saa a, efisɛ scanning fi block 0 betumi agye bere tenten.
- **UFVK ne Mfiri:** Ledger Wallet bisa wo Ledger sɛ ɔmfa wo Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK) nkɔ amannɔne. Kenkan screens a ɛwɔ device no so na pene so. Sɛ wohu sɛ "Device connection ayera" a, hwɛ sɛ Zcash app no abue, pene so ntɛm ara na klik **Retry**.
- Sɛ wohu sɛ **UFVK a wɔde aba no yiye** a, klik **Fi ase yɛ sync**. Sɛ syncing wie a, button no sesa kɔ **Update balance** a "Last sync" bere.
- Safoa a wode hwɛ ade no tra wo kɔmputa no so. Ebetumi ahu wo sika a wɔabɔ ho ban no nanso entumi mfa nni dwuma.

![Enable Zcash private balance window showing UFVK successfully imported and the Start sync button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-02-ufvk-imported.png)

**4.1. Gye ZEC na hwɛ sɛ address abien no nyinaa yɛ nokware wɔ wo Ledger**
- Klik **Gye** na paw wo Zcash akontaabu. Ledger Wallet kyerɛ **Address ma Zcash 1** (a ɛda adi pefee, ɛfiri aseɛ wɔ t1) ne **Private address ma Zcash 1** (ɛfiri aseɛ wɔ u1). Address ahorow a edi kan no kɔ so yɛ adwuma.
- Wo Ledger kyerɛ "Verify Zcash addresses", **Private address** wɔ nkratafa abien so, afei **Public Address**. Fa nkyerɛwde biara toto screen no ho ansa na woakyɛ address no mu biara, afei si so dua wɔ afiri no so.
- Pɛ kokoam address no; fa nea ɛda adi pefee no di dwuma bere a nea ɔde kɔmaa no no mmoa biribi foforo biara nkutoo.

![Ledger Wallet Receive window showing the transparent address and the private address for Zcash 1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-03-receive-addresses.png)

![Ledger device screens: Verify Zcash addresses, Private address in two pages, then Public Address](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-04-device-verify-addresses.png)

**5.1. Fa ZEC a ɛda adi pefee no kɔ wo private balance mu**
- Klik **Send** so na hwɛ. Wɔ **Self transfer** ase no, paw **Send to private balance**.
- Hyehyɛ sika bi anaa fa 25%, 50%, 75% anaa **Max** di dwuma. Hwɛ network fee no, afei klik **Review** so.
- Wɔ wo Ledger, fa anammɔn fa: "Hwɛ asɛm no mu na fa ZEC", "Transfer from public to private address", sika dodow, output address wɔ nkratafa abien mu, sika a wɔbɔ ne bere a etwa to no sorokɔ. Hwɛ sɛ output address no ne wo private address no hyia fi anammɔn 4, afei mia button abien no nyinaa wɔ **Sign transaction** so.
- Ledger Wallet kyerɛ **Asɛm a wɔde akɔma**. Wɔ asɛm no ho nsɛm mu no, type no kenkan **Private (Ironwood)**.
- Ledger Wallet hyɛ no nsow sɛ self transfer de wo sika a wɔabɔ ho ban no bata wo baguam address ho. Sɛ wogye kɔ wo kokoam address so tẽẽ a, ɛma wɔne wɔn ho wɔn ho nni nkitaho.

![Send ZEC window in Ledger Wallet with the Self transfer option Send to private balance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-05-send-to-private-balance.png)

![Ledger device review screens for moving ZEC from the transparent balance to the private address, ending with Sign transaction and Transaction signed](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-06-device-review-shielding.png)

- Sɛ asɛm no si so dua wie a, transparent balance no kɔ fam na private balance no kɔ soro. Sika foforo a wɔabɔ ho ban no kyerɛ sɛ "ɛrenyin" kosi sɛ wobenya adanse a ɛdɔɔso a wɔde bedi dwuma.

![Zcash account after shielding: transparent balance reduced, private balance increased and shown as maturing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-07-balances-after-shielding.png)

**6. Fa ZEC fi wo private balance so mena**
- Sɛ wotumi sɛe kokoam sika no wie a, klik **Send** na hyehyɛ **Transfer from** kɔ **Private balance**.
- Fa nea woregye no address no hyɛ mu na kyerɛw sika dodow no. Wubetumi de encrypted **Memo (optional)** a ɛkɔ 512 bytes aka ho, a nea ogye no nkutoo na obetumi akenkan.
- Hwɛ nea wogye no, sika dodow ne sika a wotua wɔ wo Ledger, afei mia bɔtn abien no nyinaa so wɔ **Sign transaction** so.

![Sent transaction from the private balance in Ledger Wallet transaction details](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-08-private-send.png)

## Eye sɛ Wubehu

- Shielded ZEC a ɛwɔ Ledger so no yɛ desktop nko ara seesei (wɔahwɛɛ 15 Ɔkɔtɔberɛ 2026).
- Ledger Wallet kokoam sika a ɛwɔ hɔ no de Ironwood ɔtare no di dwuma.
- Ledger se wobɛtumi ne toɔ ho ɔfotufoɔ akyɛ wo nkitahodiɛ abakɔsɛm a wobɛtumi akenkan nko ara. Obiara a ɔwɔ wo viewing key no betumi ahu wo shielded history, enti hyɛ da nkutoo kyɛ.
- Wɔde Ledger's Speculos emulator (Nano S Plus) a ɛde Zcash app 3.9.4, a ɛka Ledger Wallet Desktop 4.24.0 wɔ Zcash mainnet so na ɛkyeree mfiri screens a ɛwɔ akwankyerɛ yi mu. Honam fam Ledger de app koro no ara di dwuma na ɛkyerɛ screen koro no ara.
