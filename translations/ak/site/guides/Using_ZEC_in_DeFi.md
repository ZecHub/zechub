# Zcash a wode bedi dwuma wɔ DeFi mu


## Nea Ɛbɛn Intents 

Wɔaka Zcash ne NEAR Intents abom, na ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi sesa Zcash (ZEC) ne altcoins afoforo a ɛwɔ soro, a Bitcoin, Solana, NEAR, ne XRP ka ho, a wontua hwee. Saa nkabom yi yɛ NEAR Protocol mmɔdenbɔ a ɛde bɛbɔ AI bots a ɛyɛ ne ho na wotumi di ho adanseɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no fã, a ɛno nso de mfasoɔ brɛ Zcash denam AI-powered payment rails a ɛma ɛyɛ adwuma no so. Mprempren Zcash dwumadiefoɔ tumi nya smart contracts ne [DeFi applications a ɛtrɛ](https://nym.com/blog/what-is-defi) bere a wɔkora wɔn kokoam nsɛm so denam [Near Intents](https://app.near-intents.org).

## Nneɛma foforo a atwa Zcash DeFi ho ahyia 

**Layer 1 Ano aduru**

Mprempren wɔrehwehwɛ akwan horow a wɔbɛfa so ama DeFi applications atumi ayɛ adwuma wɔ Zcash ecosystem no mu denam mprempren Layer 1. Eyi betumi ayɛ yiye denam perofrming dodow no ara apam adwumayɛ off-chain ne sequencer & ma wɔyɛ saa nneyɛe no validation wɔ chain so. Wɔyɛɛ eyi fã bi wɔ fekubɔ mu ne JP Morgan wɔ wɔn adwumayɛbea blockchain so. Sɛnea ɛte wɔ NU5 no, adwinnade bi wɔ hɔ (TZE) a ɛde saa ntrɛwmu yi bɛka Zcash ho. 

**zkEVM** na ɛyɛ adwuma.

Wei de native programmability bɛba Zcash a ɛwɔ EVM-compatible virtual machine a ɛboa zero-nimdeɛ adanseɛ akontabuo. Wei bɛma Zcash anya nkɔsoɔ denam developer community a ɛwɔ ahodoɔ pii so na ama ayɛ ecosystem a ɛkora kokoamsɛm so application ne tokens. Eyi bɛma wɔde atoto L2 kokoam nsɛm ano aduru afoforo a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no ho. 

Nhwehwɛmu a wɔtoa so wɔ Proof-of-Stake & the Cosmos Interblockchain Communication Protocol no, ECC na ɛdi anim. Wɔresusuw anammɔn a edi hɔ no ho a ɛka Ethereum Merge to PoS no nkonimdi ho a nsɛm biara a ebetumi asɔre. 

**ZSA/UDA deɛ**

Zcash Shielded Assets / User Defined Assets ayɛ nkɔsoɔ denam kuw bi a wɔatu wɔn ho ama mmoa so. NU5 protocol upgrade no akyi no wɔabɛn abasobɔde kɛse. Akwan a wɔfa so yɛ trustless & private cross-chain bridging of these assets a ɛma wotumi yɛ adwuma bom no wɔ adwuma mu mprempren. Link a ɛkɔ Zcon3 nkyerɛkyerɛmu a ɛfa eyi ho wɔ ase ha.
