## Kamino

[Kamino](https://kamino.com/earn/lend) yɛ DeFi nhyehyeɛ a ɛdi kan wɔ Solana abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ mu no mu baako, ɛde nnwuma te sɛ boseabɔ, boseabɔ, sika a wɔde sie, ne sika a wɔde di dwuma wɔ ɔkwan a ɛnyɛ adwuma so ma. Ɛnam sɛ wɔne Solana abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no bɛka abom nti, Zcash (ZEC) dwumadiefoɔ bɛtumi de Solana-based Wrapped ZEC agyapadeɛ adi dwuma de ne DeFi dwumadie ahodoɔ adi nkitaho.

Sɛ wɔde Solana Wrapped ZEC, wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no betumi anya Kamino's Earn ne Lending features no, na ama wɔatumi de ZEC adi dwuma sɛ agyapade a ɛsow aba denam decentralized liquidity provision ne boseabɔ akwan so. Wei trɛw Zcash's dwumadie mu firi ankorankoro sikatua agyapadeɛ so kɔ deɛ ɛbɛtumi de ne ho ahyɛ sikasɛm dwumadiɛ a wɔde ahyɛ aman nsa mu.

Nkabom a wɔne Kamino no bue hokwan ma Zcash sɛ ɛbɛhyɛn Solana DeFi abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ mu, a ɛkyerɛ nnwuma a wɔyɛ ntɛmntɛm, ɛka a ɛba fam, ne sikasɛm mu dwumadie ahodoɔ a ɛyɛ nyansa a egyina apam so.

## Solana Wrapped ZEC Abɔde a Nkwa Wom (Meteora, Orca, ne Raydium)

Solana Wrapped ZEC ma wotumi de Zcash di dwuma wɔ Solana abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no mu denam agyapadeɛ ananmusifoɔ a ɛne Solana ntwamutam gyinapɛn no hyia so.

Wrapped ZEC, wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no betumi ne DeFi protocol ahorow te sɛ:

- Nsuo a ɛwɔ nsuo mu
- Nneɛma a wɔde sesa nneɛma a wɔde ahyɛ aman nsa (DEX)
- Nnɔbae a wɔde yɛ kua
- Guadidan a wɔyɛ no afiri so

Protocol ahorow te sɛ [Meteora](https://www.meteora.ag/), [Orca](https://www.orca.so/), ne [Raydium](https://raydium.io/swap/?inputMint=sol&outputMint=4k3Dyjzvzp8eMZWUXbBCjEvwSkkk59S5iCNLY3QrkX6R) de sika a wɔde di dwuma ho nhyehyɛe a ɛma ZEC tumi de ne ho hyɛ aguadi ne sika a wɔde ma ho dwumadi ahorow mu bɛma.

Saa nkabom yi ka Zcash's kokoamsɛm mu mfasoɔ ne Solana ntwamutam no ahoɔhare ne adwumayɛ yie bom, na ɛma nsɛm foforɔ a wɔde di dwuma ma ankorankoro agyapadeɛ wɔ nnɛyi DeFi abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no mu.

Sua Pii Fa Ho: <br /> 1. [Meteora Sua ade](https://docs.meteora.ag/get-started). <br />
               2. [Orca Sua ade](https://docs.orca.so/developers/overview). <br />

## THORChain / THORSwap

[THORChain](https://thorchain.org/) yɛ cross-chain liquidity protocol a ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi sesa native agyapade fi blockchains ahorow mu a wɔmfa wɔn ho nto atetesɛm wrapped tokens so.

Fam [THORChain](https://thorchain.org/) ne [THORSwap](https://thorswap.finance/), wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no betumi asesa agyapade wɔ blockchains nyinaa so, a Zcash (ZEC). Saa nkabom yi ma Zcash nya kwan tẽẽ kɔ decentralized liquidity denam THORChain network so, na ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi kura kurom hɔ agyapadeɛ su a ɛho nhia sɛ wɔde centralized intermediaries di dwuma.

Bere a wɔde ZEC pool no aba no [THORChain](https://thorchain.org/), no [THORChain](https://thorchain.org/) mprempren network boa Zcash liquidity dwumadi ahorow a wɔmma ho kwan. [THORChain](https://thorchain.org/) nodes betumi ahwɛ Zcash blockchain no so na ama infrastructure a wɔde bɛyɛ cross-chain aguadi wɔ ZEC ne blockchain agyapade afoforo ntam.

Saa nkabom yi de mfasoɔ pii ma Zcash abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no:

- Akwan a wɔfa so nya cross-blockchain liquidity;
- ZEC aguadi a wɔmfa wɔn ho nto centralized exchanges so;
- Nkitahodi a ɛkɔ anim ne blockchain agyapade afoforo;
- ZEC a wɔde di dwuma a wɔatrɛw mu wɔ multichain DeFi abɔde a nkwa wom nhyehyɛe no mu.

Sua Pii fa: <br /> 1. [THORChain Anisoadehu](https://thorchain.org/vision) <br />
               2. [THORSwap Sua ade](https://docs.thorswap.finance/thorswap)

## Sɛ wode cbZEC di dwuma wɔ DeFi Base so

cbZEC de Zcash sika a ɛwɔ Base ecosystem no mu denam ma a ɛma ZEC dwumadiefoɔ kwan ma wɔne decentralized finance (DeFi) application ahodoɔ di nkitaho a ɛnhia sɛ wɔgyae wɔn ho a wɔde bɛto Zcash ecosystem no mu.

Ɛnam sɛ [Base ntam nkitahodi](https://www.base.org/), cbZEC yɛ adwuma sɛ ZEC gyinabea a wobetumi de adi dwuma wɔ Ethereum Layer-2 tebea no mu. Wei ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi sesa agyapadeɛ, de sika ma, kɔ boseabɔ gua so, na wɔde DeFi dwumadie ahodoɔ a ɛho ka sua na wɔsi so dua ntɛmntɛm sɛ wɔde toto Ethereum ntam nkitahodiɛ titire no ho a.

[Nkabom no](https://x.com/base/status/2094848959986409953?s=20) of cbZEC kɔ Base DeFi platform ahodoɔ mu trɛw Zcash's mfasoɔ mu firi sikatua agyapadeɛ ara kwa kɔ deɛ wɔbɛtumi de adi dwuma wɔ sikasɛm dwumadiɛ ahodoɔ pii a wɔde ahyɛ aman nsa mu.

### Aerodrome Slipstream

[Aerodrome Slipstream](https://aerodrome.finance/) yɛ liquidity pools akɛseɛ a ɛwɔ Base network no so no mu baako. Aerodrome de ve(3,3) model a ɛka escrow voting akwan ne liquidity incentives bom de yɛ gua a ɛyɛ adwuma yie.

Aerodrome Slipstream de adwene a ɛfa sika a wɔde di dwuma a wɔde ahyɛ mu, te sɛ Uniswap V3, a ɛma wɔn a wɔde sika ma no kwan ma wɔkyerɛ bo pɔtee bi sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wobetumi de sika kɛse adi dwuma yiye.

Sɛ cbZEC boa a, wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no betumi:

- Sesa cbZEC ma agyapadeɛ foforɔ a ɛwɔ Base network no so.
- Fa cbZEC sika ma na ama woanya nkitahodi ho ka.
- Fa wo ho hyɛ Base's sikasɛm nhyehyɛe titiriw no mu.

Saa nkabom yi ma Aerodrome yɛ akwan titiriw a Zcash dwumadiefoɔ fa so kɔ Base DeFi gua so no mu baako.

### Uniswap V3 (Base)

[Uniswap V3](https://app.uniswap.org/?intro=true) yɛ decentralized exchange protocols akɛseɛ a ɛde automated market maker (AMM) model di dwuma no mu baako.

Wɔ Base ntwamutam no so no, Uniswap V3 de sika a wɔde tua ho nhyehyɛe a wɔaboaboa ano a ɛma wɔn a wɔde sika ma no tumi kyekyɛ sika kɛse wɔ bo pɔtee bi mu ma.

Mmoa a wɔde ma cbZEC wɔ Uniswap V3 Base no ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no kwan ma:

- Di gua cbZEC a wonni ho kwan.
- Ma liquidity ma cbZEC mmienu mmienu.
- Nya liquidity infrastructure a DeFi application pii de di dwuma no.

Concentrated liquidity model no ma capital efficiency tu mpɔn sɛ wɔde toto atetesɛm AMM models ho a.

Sua pii : [Uniswap V3](https://app.uniswap.org/swap)

### Uniswap V4 (Base)

[Uniswap V4 na ɛyɛ adwuma](https://app.uniswap.org/?intro=true) yɛ Uniswap protocol no awo ntoatoaso a etwa to, a ɛde “hooks”-based architecture aba.

Hooks ma developers ma wɔyɛ dwumadie foforɔ wɔ liquidity pools mu, te sɛ automated aguadi akwan, custom fee mechanisms, ne customizable pool logic.

Wɔ cbZEC, Uniswap V4 a wɔde bɛka abom no bue hokwan ahorow ma:

- Liquidity pools a ɛwɔ mfiri a ɛyɛ mmerɛw kɛse.
- DeFi akwan horow a ɛyɛ den kɛse.
- Nkitahodi a ɛne awo ntoatoaso a edi hɔ no sikasɛm nhyehyɛe a wɔde ahyɛ aman nsa no.

Uniswap V4 ma fapem a wotumi sesa no yie ma agyapadeɛ te sɛ cbZEC ma ɛdi yie wɔ Base ecosystem no mu.

## Nneɛma foforo a atwa Zcash DeFi ho ahyia 

**Layer 1 Ano aduru**

Mprempren wɔrehwehwɛ akwan horow a wɔbɛfa so ama DeFi applications atumi ayɛ adwuma wɔ Zcash ecosystem no mu denam mprempren Layer 1. Eyi betumi ayɛ yiye denam perofrming dodow no ara apam adwumayɛ off-chain ne sequencer & ma wɔyɛ saa nneyɛe no validation wɔ chain so. Wɔyɛɛ eyi fã bi wɔ fekubɔ mu ne JP Morgan wɔ wɔn adwumayɛbea blockchain so. Sɛnea ɛte wɔ NU5, adwinnade bi wɔ hɔ (TZE) a ɛde saa ntrɛwmu yi bɛka Zcash. 

**zkEVM** na ɛyɛ adwuma

Wei de native programmability bɛba Zcash a ɛwɔ EVM-compatible virtual machine a ɛboa zero-nimdeɛ adanseɛ akontabuo. Wei bɛma Zcash anya nkɔsoɔ denam developer community a ɛwɔ ahodoɔ pii so na ama ayɛ ecosystem a ɛkora kokoamsɛm so applications ne tokens. Eyi bɛma wɔde atoto L2 kokoam nsɛm ano aduru afoforo a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no ho. 

Nhwehwɛmu a wɔtoa so wɔ Proof-of-Stake & the Cosmos Interblockchain Communication Protocol no ECC. Wɔresusuw anammɔn a edi hɔ no ho a ɛka Ethereum Merge to PoS no nkonimdi ho a nsɛm biara a ebetumi asɔre. 

**ZSA/UDA deɛ**

Zcash Shielded Assets / User Defined Assets ayɛ nkɔsoɔ denam kuw bi a wɔatu wɔn ho ama mmoa so. NU5 protocol upgrade no akyi no wɔabɛn abasobɔde kɛse. Akwan a wɔfa so yɛ trustless & private cross-chain bridging of these assets a ɛma wotumi yɛ adwuma bom no wɔ adwuma mu mprempren. Link a ɛkɔ Zcon3 nkyerɛkyerɛmu a ɛfa eyi ho wɔ ase ha.
