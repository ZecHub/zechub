# Blockchain Nhwehwɛmufo

## Nnianimu

Wɔ atetesɛm aguadi wiase no mu no, nea ɛka asɛm biara ho ne krataa a wɔde gye sika a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔatɔ ade. Saa ara nso na wɔ blockchain wiase no mu no, obi a ɔde di dwuma no nya dijitaal krataa a ɛkyerɛ sɛ ɔyɛ adwuma id wɔ asɛm biara a wɔawie ho. Sika kotoku dodow no ara bɛma eyi ama wo. Blockchain explorers yɛ nnwinnade a ɛma obi tumi yɛ nea asi dedaw wɔ blockchain so ho mfonini wɔ n’adwenem ara kwa. Wɔfa ma inputs: transaction id's, addresses, anaa block hashes, na wɔde aniwa kyerɛ nea ɛkɔɔ so.

## Blockchain Asase Mfonini ahorow

Enti yɛwɔ saa nkyerɛwde tenten yi sɛ dijitaal krataa, afei dɛn? Eyi ne baabi a yɛde [blockchain explorer](https://nym.com/blog/using-blockchain-privately), anaa asase mfonini, na aboa yɛn ma yɛayɛ nea esii wɔ blockchain no so. Hyɛ sɛnea nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn biara wɔ n’ankasa nkyerɛase a ɛne [blockchain explorer](https://nym.com/blog/using-blockchain-privately) boro so. Ɛho hia sɛ yɛte aseɛ sɛ saa blockchain nnwuma yi nyinaa yɛ nhwɛsoɔ a ɛfa open source software ho. Ɛne sɛ, obiara betumi aboa na anaasɛ ɔde fork ahyɛ mmara no mu sɛnea n’ani gye ho. Saa nteaseɛ no, adwuma biara yɛ specializes wɔ mmeaeɛ ahodoɔ na customizes blockchain explorer no sɛdeɛ ɛne adwuma a wɔaka ho asɛm no ahiadeɛ hyia.

### Nneɛma a wɔde siw ano
Wɔde nkitahodi ahorow no gu *blocks* mu. Sɛ wɔ mined/validated block bi a, wɔasi asɛm biara a ɛwɔ saa block no mu no so dua na wɔyɛ block hash. Hash biara a wɔayɛ no, wobetumi de ahyɛ block explorer mu. Ebia woahu sɛ CEX hia *confirmations* dodow bi ansa na wɔayi wo sika adi, eyi ne metric a wɔde redi dwuma de ahwɛ ahu sɛ wo transaction no te saa 
a wɔawie no sɛnea ɛsɛ. Ɔkwan bɛn so na blockchain no kyerɛ nnwuma a ɛkɔ block a edi hɔ no mu? Nhwehwɛmu asɛmti a ɛyɛ den, nanso nnɛyi nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn dodow no ara de adwene a ɛne sɛ *akatua* di dwuma de kyerɛ onii a ɔbɛkɔ anim wɔ nkyerɛwde no mu. Dodow a sika no dɔɔso no, dodow no ara na hokwan a ɛwɔ hɔ sɛ wobɛforo akɔ ntonto no anim no yɛ kɛse.

### Address ahorow

Ɔkwan a ɛyɛ anigye a wɔfa so sua ade wɔ aniwa so [blockchain explorers](https://nym.com/blog/using-blockchain-privately) ne sɛ wode address a ɛwɔ asɛm biara a wɔde di dwuma kwa no bɛhyɛ mu. Afei wubetumi akɔ w’akyi wɔ bere mu na woahu baabi a sika no fi! Adwuma biara wɔ address a wɔde hyɛ mu ne nea wɔde fi mu nyinaa.  Bere a obi de saa nsɛm yi ayɛ akode no, obetumi akɔ n’anim ne n’akyi ntɛm ara afi asɛm biara a wɔasɛe no mu. Wɔn a wɔpɛ ahodwiriwde no, eyi ne dijitaal a ɛne sikasɛm mu ahodwiriwde kɛse bi yɛ pɛ, na wobetumi de adi dwuma de ayɛ nea ɛda adi pefee. Blockchain explorer a wode bedi dwuma no mma ɛnyɛ sɛ ɛyɛ mmerɛw kɛse sɛ wobɛhwɛ eyi wɔ w’adwenem nko, na mmom *ɛsan nso si* hia a ehia sɛ wonya nkitahodi kokoamsɛm so dua. Gye sɛ wode Zcash a wɔabɔ ho ban redi dwuma, wubetumi de *biara * blockchain a ɛda adi pefee ayɛ eyi: BTC, ETH, ATOM, DOGE, VTC, ne nea ɛkeka ho ... . Saa asɛm yi ho hia ma obiara a ɔde blockchain no di dwuma dwoodwoo a ɔretu akɔ dijitaal nkutoo daakye mu.

### Sika dodow a wɔde ma

Te sɛ address ahorow a ɛwɔ atifi hɔ no, asɛm biara a wɔyɛ wɔ ɔmanfo blockchain so no wɔ sika dodow a ɔmanfo betumi anya wɔ ɔyɛkyerɛ nyinaa mu. Eyi ka sika dodow a ɛwɔ input ne output address nyinaa so ma asɛm biara ho. Adeɛ baako a ɛfiri yei mu ne berɛ a wopaw sɛ wode Shielded Zcash bedi dwuma -- afei sika dodoɔ no nyinaa asie. Wɔ nnwuma nketewa wuranom a ɛho hia sɛ wohia kokoam nsɛm ma *aguadi a ɛfata* no, eyi yɛ mfaso kɛse!

### Nea obi a ɔhwehwɛ nneɛma mu betumi ahu ne nea ontumi nhu wɔ Zcash so

#### TL;DR
- Fann (`t`) address ahorow no da adi koraa wɔ explorer so, te sɛ Bitcoin
- Nkitahodi a wɔabɔ ho ban koraa (z kosi z) de sika dodow, address ahorow, ne memo no sie
- Wɔda so ara da sika a wɔbɔ no da adi, wɔ asɛm a wɔabɔ ho ban koraa mpo so
- Shielding (a ɛkɔ baabiara `t` to shielded) ne deshielding (wɔde kyɛm san kɔ `t`) no fã bi da adi, efisɛ ɔfã biako da adi
- Kokoamsɛm kura bere a sika tra atare a wɔabɔ ho ban no mu nkutoo

Zcash wɔ address bɛboro biako, na explorer bi ne wɔn di wɔ ɔkwan soronko so koraa.

Address ahorow a ɛda adi pefee, fi ase `t`, yɛ adwuma te sɛ Bitcoin. Ɔhwehwɛfo bi kyerɛ nea ɔde sika no kɔmaa no, nea ogyee no, sika dodow no, ne ɔkwan a wɔfa so san kɔ baabi a sika no fi bae no.

Address ahorow a wɔabɔ ho ban ne kokoam fã no. Sika a ɛwɔ Sapling anaa Orchard [atare a wɔabɔ ho ban](https://zechub.wiki/using-zcash/shielded-pools#content) wɔde nimdeɛ ho adanse biara na ɛbɔ ho ban. Hwɛ asɛm bi a wɔabɔ ho ban koraa na ɔhwehwɛfoɔ no ntumi nkyerɛ sika dodoɔ, address ahodoɔ, anaa memo no. Ebetumi asi so dua sɛ asɛm bi a ɛfata sii na wɔkyerɛw too hɔ wɔ block bi mu nkutoo. Eyi ne kokoam nhwɛso a ahintaw a wɔakyerɛ wɔ baabi a ɛbɛn kratafa yi atifi.

Ade biako kɔ so da adi ampa wɔ nnwuma a wɔabɔ ho ban koraa mpo ho: ɛka a wɔbɔ. Zcash consensus mmara hwehwɛ sɛ wɔka sika a ɛda adi pefee no pefee, enti explorer betumi akyerɛ bere nyinaa, bere mpo a wɔakata sika dodow no so. Esiane saa nti ɛyɛ adeyɛ pa sɛ wode sika kotoku ho ka a wɔahyɛ da ayɛ no bedi dwuma, enti w’adwuma no nna nsow denam sika a ɛyɛ soronko a wubetua so.

Ɔhwehwɛfo no nso betumi ahu bere a sika no twa afã horow a ɛda adi pefee ne nea wɔabɔ ho ban no ntam no. Tu a wotu kɔ baabi foforo `t` sika a wɔde kɔ ɔtare bi mu no yɛ kyɛm, sɛ wobɛsan de wɔn akɔ akyi a, ɛyɛ deshielding. Saa mmeae a wɔatwa no fã bi da adi efisɛ ɔfã biako da adi. Z to z dwumadi a ɛyɛ kokoam koraa nkutoo, a ɛnka a da `t` address, de biribiara sie gye sika a wotua no.

Nea wɔde kɔ: kokoamsɛm gyina atare a wɔabɔ ho ban no mu a wobɛtra so. Sɛ sika ka a `t` address, sɛ wɔn abakɔsɛm fã no yɛ baguam te sɛ Bitcoin. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ woda w’ankasa dwumadi a wɔabɔ ho ban no adi akyerɛ obi a wopaw, te sɛ akontaabufo a, kyɛ safe a wode hwɛ ade sen sɛ wode bɛma baguam. Hwɛ [Nsafe a Wɔde Hwɛ Nneɛma](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-tech/viewing-keys#content) kratafa.
