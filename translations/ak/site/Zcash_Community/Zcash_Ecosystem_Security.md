# Zcash Abɔde a Nkwa Wom Ahobammɔ

## Abɔde a Nkwa Wom Ahobammɔ Ho Kan

Wɔnam ZCG mmoa so na ɛde Zcash Ecosystem Security Lead dwumadie no sii hɔ sɛ wɔde ahobanbɔ mfiridwuma a wɔatu wɔn ho ama ama Zcash ecosystem a ɛtrɛ — titire no ZCG mmoa — a ɛwɔ ECC ne ZF akyi.

- **2022–2023:** [asase so atrae](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-ecosystem-security-lead/42090) ɔsom sɛ Ecosystem Security Lead a odi kan. Sua pii wɔ [zecsec.com](https://zecsec.com).
- **2024–2025:** ZCG a wɔpaw [Tumi a Ɛsua koraa](https://leastauthority.com) sɛ wɔbɛtoa dwumadie no so denam [RFP](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/rfp-zcash-ecosystem-security-lead-2023/45723). Wobetumi ahu nsɛm foforo a wɔayɛ [ha](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-update-zcash-ecosystem-security-lead/47541).
- **2026:** Shielded Labs [wɔahyɛ Taylor Hornby aware nhyehyɛe](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/shielded-labs-engages-taylor-hornby-as-security-consultant/55421) sɛ ahobammɔ ho ɔfotufo a ɔbɛhyɛ Zcash ahobammɔ tumi mu den.

## ZCG Ahobanbɔ & Nneɛma a Ɛyɛ Fɛ a Wɔda no Adi Ho Dwumadi

[ZCG Ahobanbɔ & Nneɛma a Ɛyɛ Fɛ a Wɔda no Adi Ho Dwumadi](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcg-security-vulnerability-disclosure-initiative/55545) de nhyehyeɛ a wɔde bɛda ahobanbɔ mu mmerɛwyɛ ahorow adi wɔ Zcash abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no nyinaa mu a wɔayɛ no pɛpɛɛpɛ ma.

## Nnansa yi Ahobammɔ Ho Nsɛm (2026) .

- **Zebra 4.4.1 (May 2026):** [Ahobanbɔ ho nsiesie a ɛho hia](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zebra-4-4-1-critical-security-fix/55588) aba. Wɔhyɛ node operators nyinaa nkuran sɛ wɔnyɛ upgrade ntɛm ara.
- **Zebra 4.3.1 (April 2026):** [Ahobanbɔ ho nsiesie a ɛho hia, dockerized mining ne CI hardening](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zebra-4-3-1-critical-security-fixes-dockerized-mining-and-ci-hardening/55389) aba.
- **Wɔasiesie Mfomso pii (April 2026):** [Wɔasiesie Zcash mmerɛwyɛ pii yiye](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/several-zcash-vulnerabilities-successfully-remediated/55388) a ɛnka wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no sika anaa kokoam nsɛm.
- **zcashd Advisory (April 2026):** [Afotuo a wɔde bɛtew zcashd ntua ani so](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/advisory-reduce-your-zcashd-attack-surface-by-shielding-it-behind-zebra/55390) denam kar a wɔde fa Zebra so no so.

## Asɛyɛde a Wɔda no Adi

Electric Coin Company ne Zcash Foundation nyinaa ne saa Asɛdeɛ a Wɔda no Adi [gyinabea](https://github.com/RD-Crypto-Spec/Responsible-Disclosure/tree/d47a5a3dafa5942c8849a93441745fdd186731e6) a ɛyɛ nea ɛtwetwe adwene a edidi so yi:

> "Zcash yɛ mfiridwuma a ɛma kokoamsɛm a emu yɛ den. Wɔde nsɛm a wɔakyerɛw no sie kɔ baabi a wɔrekɔ, na afei wɔde sika nnyinaso no sie denam zero-nimdeɛ adanse a wɔabɔ wɔn tirim sɛ ɛbɛyɛ nea Zcash kurafo ankasa nkutoo na obetumi ayɛ so. Sɛ eyi di nkogu, na atoro bɔne bi fi mu ba a, wobetumi de saa atoro bɔne no adi dwuma a ɔkwan biara nni hɔ a blockchain nhwehwɛmufo bɛfa so ahu nea ɔyɛ ɔbɔnefo no anaasɛ nea data a ɛwɔ blockchain no mu no wɔde adi dwuma de adi dwuma de ayɛ bɔne no Rollbacks ansa na saa bere no reba, te sɛ nea wɔayɛ wɔ nnwuma afoforo bi mu wɔ nsɛm a ɛtete saa mu no, enti entumi nyɛ yiye ka sɛ ɛwɔ — ɛma nkɔsoɔ kuo no kwan ma wɔde di kan pɛpɛɛpɛ na wɔsiesie asɛm no, nanso, sɛdeɛ ɛteɛ wɔ CVE-2019-7167 mu no, yɛbɛtumi asi gyinaeɛ sɛ yɛremfa saa nsɛm no nka yɛn amanneɛbɔ ho nkɔma ahokafoɔ ansa na yɛayi no adi wɔ nhyehyɛeɛ mu, berɛ dodoɔ a yɛwɔ awerɛhyɛmu sɛ wɔyɛ mmerɛw."
