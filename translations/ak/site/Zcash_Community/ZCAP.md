# Zcash Mpɔtam Hɔ Afotufoɔ Kuo 

Zcash Community Advisory Panel, anaa ZCAP, yɛ panel a Zcash Community no mufoɔ ne wɔn hyia, a wɔde asi hɔ sɛ ɔkwan a wɔbɛfa so de mpɔtam hɔfoɔ ahyɛ mu na wɔabisa wɔn afotuo adwene berɛ biara a ɛhia sɛ Zcash Foundation si gyinaeɛ a ɛfa mpɔtam a wɔaka ho asɛm no ho anaa ɛka. Efi bere a wɔbɔɔ no wɔ afe 2018 mu no, panel no aboa ma wɔakyerɛkyerɛ ɔkwan a wɔbɛfa so ama Zcash fapem no wɔ gyinaesi ahorow a ɛho hia pii mu, a ebi ne:

[Zcash Aguadi Agyiraehyɛde no](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/Elections/blob/master/2018-Q2/General-Measures/monetary-policy.md): Na saa abatow yi fa sɛ ebia ɛsɛ sɛ Zcash Foundation di mmara kwan so apam akyi ne Electric Coin Company (ECC) sɛ wɔbɛkyɛ Zcash aguadi agyiraehyɛde no wurayɛ ne ne tumidi

[Sika Ho Nhyehyɛe](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/Elections/blob/master/2018-Q2/General-Measures/monetary-policy.md): Na saa abatow yi fa sɛ ebia ɛsɛ sɛ Zcash Foundation no gyina nsakrae biara a wɔbɛyɛ wɔ Zcash sikasɛm nhyehyɛe mu, te sɛ sika a wɔde ma, nhyehyɛe a wɔde bɛtew so fã, anaasɛ sika bo a ɛkɔ soro no ho

[ASIC Ɔsɔretia a Ɛko Tia](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/Elections/blob/master/2018-Q2/General-Measures/asicresistance_ballot.md): Na saa abatoɔ yi fa sɛ ebia ɛsɛ sɛ Zcash Foundation de ASIC sɔre tia di kan sɛ botaeɛ ne botaeɛ ma Zcash ntam nkitahodiɛ no

[Zcash Community Grants Boayikuw no Abatow](https://zfnd.org/zcg-election-june-2023/): Na saa abatoɔ yi fa Zcash Community Grants (ZCG) boayikuo no mufoɔ mmienu a wɔbɛpaw wɔn ho, a ɛyɛ wɔn asɛdeɛ sɛ wɔhwɛ na wɔtua sika de boa mmoa ho nyansahyɛ ma nnwuma a ɛfa Zcash ho

[Zcash Minor Grants Dwumadi no](https://zfnd.org/launching-the-zcash-minor-grants-program/): Na saa abatow yi fa mmoa akwammisa a wɔpene so anaasɛ wɔpow ma Zcash nnwuma nketewa a wɔsrɛɛ sika fi Zcash Foundation hɔ no ho

[Zcash Shielded Assets a wɔbɛgye atom na wɔayɛ](https://vote.heliosvoting.org/helios/elections/5dd57b92-01ed-11ec-a0a8-ae3066fac55d/view), a ɛma wotumi de DeFi generic agyapade di dwuma wɔ private by default Zcash ecosystem no mu.

[Zcash Foundation Akwankyerɛ Kuw no mufo a wɔbɛpaw wɔn](https://vote.heliosvoting.org/helios/elections/960b8832-60f9-11ed-b96e-ee6991044d9e/view)


Adwene a ɛwɔ ZCAP akyi ne sɛ, mpɔtam hɔfoɔ kuo ahodoɔ a wɔwɔ aman ahodoɔ, dwumadie ne adwene ahodoɔ na wɔahyehyɛ, de ahwɛ sɛ ɛda so ara de ne ho ahyɛ ne botaeɛ a ɛne sɛ ɛbɛgyina hɔ ama Zcash dwumadiefoɔ ne wɔn a wɔdi dwuma no nne ne wɔn anigyeɛ, berɛ a ɛkura nkitahodiɛ a ɛbue na ɛwɔ ahodoɔ mu.

Ɛde besi october afe 2023 no, ZCAP no yɛ [188 asɔremma](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Y-GbVFA1csIgPbvHOR-1ruOlh7YdA-CHvMtd6R8iSdY/edit#gid=0), na wɔtaa trɛw mu. Afei nso, Zcash Foundation "atrɛw fata a wɔfata ma ZCAP asɔremma mu, a gyinapɛn a Zcash mpɔtam hɔfo de too gua ka ho".
