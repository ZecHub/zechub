# Grayscale Zcash ETF (ZCSH) no yɛ nea ɛwɔ hɔ nnɛ

**Zcash anhyɛ ETF ase. Grayscale yɛɛ**, denam sikakorabea bi a ɔwɔ a ɔdanee no so
run since 2017. Zcash yɛ protocol — enni company, sikakorabea anaa board biara
a ebetumi de sikakorabea bi ama. Grayscale yɛ agyapadeɛ sohwɛfoɔ a ɔde ne ho a ɔtɔ
ZEC wɔ open market so te sɛ obiara.

Saa nsonsonoe no ne ade biako pɛ a wɔkenkan no yiye sen biara wɔ ZCSH, na ɛyɛ baabi
krataafa yi fi ase. Wɔ 25 Ɔbɛnem 2026 no sikakorabea no hyɛɛ aseɛ dii gua wɔ NYSE Arca sɛ
* Zcash ETF *, US spot Zcash exchange-traded ade a edi kan, na ɛyɛ
mprempren ɛyɛ ZEC a ɛwɔ hɔ no mu biako.

## Nea obi a ɔwɔ kyɛfa wɔ mu ankasa

Kyɛfa bi yɛ ZEC. Ɛnyɛ ZEC.

Obi a ɔwɔ kyɛfa no ntumi mfa asɛm a wɔabɔ ho ban nkɔma, ontumi mfa memo nka ho, ontumi
kura address a ɛyɛ biako, na ontumi nhwɛ ne ho so. Agyapade ahorow no mu biara nni hɔ a
nsonsonoe Zcash fi agyapade afoforo wɔ hɔ denam wrapper — the
wrapper da bo adi na ɛnyɛ biribi foforo biara. Wɔ investor a ne botae ne sɛ ɔbɛda ne ho adi fam no
wɔ brokerage anaa retirement account mu no, ɛno ne asɛm no pɛpɛɛpɛ. Ma
obiara a n’anigye wɔ Zcash mu ne kokoamsɛm no, ETF no nyɛ nea wɔde besi ananmu
sika kotoku, na abien no nyɛ nneɛma a wɔde besi ananmu ankasa.

## Wɔyɛ ZEC no wɔ petee mu

Aseresɛm foforo bi wɔ nhyehyɛe no mu, na ɛno ne ade a ano yɛ nnam sen biara wɔ so
krataafa yi. Coinbase Custody kura Trust no ZEC wɔ **transparent** mu
address ahorow, na ɛnyɛ nea wɔabɔ ho ban. Nneɛma a sikakorabea no wɔ no da adi wɔ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn so,
wotumi hwehwɛ mu kɔ nea ɔhwɛ so no nkyɛn, na obiara a ɔdwene ho no betumi ahu wɔ bere ankasa mu
hwɛ. Biribiara nni sikakorabea yi mu a ɛde address a wɔabɔ ho ban di dwuma.

Ɛno nyɛ animhwɛ. Wɔkyekye sikakorabea nhyehyɛe a wɔahyɛ ho mmara no twa akontaabu ho hyia
ne adansedi, na sika a wɔde kari pɛ a wɔabɔ ho ban ne nea akontaabufo ntumi nyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ
ahofadi mu hwɛ sɛ ɛyɛ nokware. Coinbase's ankasa mmoa da asymmetry koro no ara adi: ɛno
bɛgye ZEC a wɔde afiri address a wɔabɔ ho ban so amena, nanso ɛnsoma ZEC nkɔ
baako.

Enti ahyehyɛde kɛse a ɔwɔ kokoam agyapade bi no kura wɔ biako no mu
kratasin a enni kokoamsɛm biara. Saa asɛm yi fi amanneɛbɔ mu sen sɛ wɔde bɛba
the filings — S-3 no kyerɛkyerɛ Trust no kokoam safe a wɔhwɛ so nanso ɛyɛ saa
ɛnyɛ characterize address types — na wɔaka wɔ ha ne saa caveat no.

## Sɛnea ZEC de ne ho hyɛ mu kɛse

Wɔ listing wɔ 25 August 2026 sikakorabea no kura bɛyɛ **387,000 ZEC**, ne
bɛyɛ **$313 ɔpepem** wɔ adwumayɛ ase.

Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wohwɛ bere tenten a: ɛde besi 30 September 2025 no, krataa a wɔde kɔe no ka sɛ Trust no kura
bɛyɛ **2.4% wɔ ZEC**. Nnɔmba abien no nyinaa drift — nea edi kan ne
ZEC bo ne ne adebɔ ne agyede, nea ɛto so abien sɛnea wotua sika no
fi holdings no mu — enti fa mprempren akontaabu fi nnansa yi ara filing anaasɛ
sikakorabea no ankasa da biara da nsɛm a wɔda no adi sen sɛ ebefi kratafa yi so.
