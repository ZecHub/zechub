# ZIP 218: Nea 25-Second Blocks Sesa Ankasa

Wɔ NU7 coinholder poll a ɛtwaam wɔ 14 September 2026 mu no, bɛyɛ 2,397,669 ZEC too aba maa ZIP 218 na 141.6 ZEC too aba tiaa no, a ɛyɛ 99.9% aba. Dodow no ara coverage bɔɔ no mua sɛ "Zcash blocks nya ntɛmntɛm." Ɛno yɛ nokware, nanso egyaw nea nyansahyɛ no yɛ no mu dodow no ara ne nea ɛhyɛ da ma ɛtra hɔ saa ara no mu dodow no ara.

Kratafa yi kyerɛkyerɛ ZIP 218 mu fi n’ankasa nkyerɛwee mu: nea ɛsakra, nea ɛnsakra, ne nea ɛho ka.

## Nkyerɛase tiawa no

| | Ɛnnɛ | Wɔ ZIP 218 akyi |
|---|---|---|
| Siw botae no ntam kwan siw kwan | 75 na ɛyɛ sikani | 25 na ɛyɛ sikani |
| Blocks da biara | 1,152 | 3,456 |
| Block subsidy (mprempren bere a wɔtew so fã) | 1.5625 ZEC na ɛwɔ hɔ | 0.52083333 ZEC na ɛwɔ hɔ |
| ZEC foforo da biara | a ɛnsakrae | a ɛnsakrae |
| Halving ntamgyinafo | 1,680,000 na ɛyɛ blocks | 5,040,000 na ɛyɛ blocks |
| Anohyeto ahorow a ɛwɔ nneyɛe a wɔabɔ ho ban wɔ block biara mu | biara nni hɔ (2 MB kɛse anohyeto no nkutoo) | 330 nyinaa, a ɛwɔ ɔtare biara mu kyɛw |
| Orchard nneɛma a ɛkɔ so (2-action transactions) | bɛyɛ 2.9 wɔ sekan biara mu | bɛyɛ 6.6 wɔ sekan biara mu |

Block dodow a ɛboro so mmɔho abiɛsa, na emu biara tua nea ɛboro nkyem abiɛsa mu biako. Nneɛma a wɔde ma ho nhyehyɛe no tra baabi a na ɛwɔ hɔ no.

## Dɛn nti na sesa block bere no

Botae titiriw ne **bere a wɔde twɛn a ɛba fam**. Ɛnnɛ sɛ wɔkyekyem pɛpɛɛpɛ a, sikatua bi twɛn sikani 75 ansa na wɔagye atom nea edi kan, ɛmfa ho sɛnea netɛw no mu adesoa te biara. Wɔ sikɔne 25 mu no ɛno so tew kodu sikɔne 25 sɛ wɔkyekyem pɛpɛɛpɛ a. ZIP no bɔ point-of-sale payments, exchange deposits ne cross-chain bridges din sɛ nneɛma a wɔde di dwuma a ɛte eyi nka kɛse.

Nsɛntitiriw abien a efi ZIP no mu no fata sɛ yɛma ɛtra yɛn adwenem:

- **Ɛnka nkyerɛ obiara sɛ ɔmfa confirmations kakraa bi nni dwuma.** Wɔ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma a wɔkura abodwokyɛre koro no ara ma rollback asiane te sɛ nnɛ no, ZIP no hwɛ kwan sɛ bere a wɔde si so dua no bɛtu mpɔn kakra a ennu mprɛnsa.
- **Ɛnyɛ ade a wɔde besi awiei adwuma ananmu.** ZIP no ka ne ho asɛm sɛ ɛboa awiei akwan te sɛ Crosslink. Base-layer blocks a ɛyɛ ntɛm boa sɛ wɔde finality layer bɛka ho akyiri yi anaasɛ wɔmfa nka ho.

ZIP no nso hyɛ no nsow sɛ anka wobetumi anya throughput a ɛkorɔn nkutoo denam block kɛse a ɛyɛ kɛse so. Latency ne nea enti a wɔpaw blocks ntiantiaa mmom.

## Nea ɛsakra

### Issuance: ZEC koro no ara da biara

Sɛ wɔma block dodow no mmɔho abiɛsa a, sɛ biribi foforo biara ansakra a, ɛbɛbɔ da biara da a wɔde ma no mmɔho abiɛsa. ZIP 218 siw saa kwan denam mmoa a wɔde ma wɔ block biara mu a wɔbɛkyekyɛ mu abiɛsa foforo bere a NU7 ayɛ adwuma pɛn no so.

Wɔ mprempren fã bere yi mu no, ɛno ma block subsidy no fi **1.5625 ZEC kɔ 0.52083333 ZEC** (52,083,333 zatoshi). Esiane sɛ zatoshi 156,250,000 nkyɛ abiɛsa pɛpɛɛpɛ nti, block biara yɛ kurukuruwa kɔ fam zatoshi nkyem abiɛsa mu biako. Wɔ 5,040,000-block fã a ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ a ne nyinaa yɛ bɛyɛ 0.0168 ZEC.

Subsidy no yɛ ZEC foforo a wɔayɛ wɔ block biara mu nyinaa. Wɔda so ara gye kyɛfa a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw a wɔde boa nkɔso no fi mu, enti wɔn a wotu fam no nya nea ennu akontaabu a edi mũ no, te sɛ nnɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ.

> **Nkyerɛwde bi a ɛfa 0.26041666 ZEC akontaabu no ho.** ZIP's nkyerɛkyerɛmu krataa a wɔayɛ no tintim post-NU7 mmoa no sɛ fam(156250000 / 6) = 0.26041666 ZEC, na nsɛm ho amanneɛbɔ binom asan aka bio. Saa nkyerɛwde no yɛ mfomso denam factor abien so: zatoshi 156,250,000 yɛ post-Blossom mmoa dedaw, enti sɛ wokyekyɛ mu asia a, ɛde Blossom factor abien no di dwuma ne mprenu so wɔ NU7 factor a ɛyɛ abiɛsa no so. Normative formula no ma floor(1,250,000,000 / (2 · 3 · 4)) = 52,083,333 zatoshi wɔ mprempren fã index no so. Zebra's dwumadie ho asɛm a ɛfa nsakraeɛ yi ho ([#11463](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zebra/issues/11463)) kyerɛw krataa no sɛ ɛkan Blossom factor no mmɔho abien, ka kyerɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no sɛ "wɔmfa formula no nni dwuma, ɛnyɛ nkyerɛwde no," na ɔka sɛ wɔde nteɛso bi akɔto ZIP. Akontaabu a ɛteɛ wɔ activation mu ne **0.52083333 ZEC**.

### Halvings kɔ so yɛ wɔn bere

Ntam a wɔde twa fã no bu abiɛsa fi 1,680,000 blocks kosi 5,040,000 blocks. Esiane sɛ block ahorow taa ba mmɔho abiɛsa nti, bɛyɛ sɛ bere koro no ara mu sɛnea anka ɛbɛba sɛ nsakrae no nni hɔ no. Nneɛma a wɔde ma nyinaa cap no nnya nkɛntɛnso biara.

Eyi yɛ soronko wɔ asɛmmisa foforo a ɛfa sika a wɔde ma ho wɔ NU7 nhwehwɛmu no mu, faako a wɔn a wɔwɔ sika no too aba sɛ wɔbɛkora sika a wɔakyekyɛ mu fã so sen sɛ wɔde curve a ɛyɛ mmerɛw besi ananmu no. ZIP 218 ne halving model a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no yɛ adwuma na ɛnsesa.

### Anohyeto foforo a ɛfa nneyɛe a wɔabɔ ho ban ho wɔ block biara mu

ZIP 218 de caps ka ho wɔ dwumadi dodow a wɔabɔ ho ban a block biako betumi akura ho:

| To anohyeto | Maximum wɔ block biara mu |
|---|---|
| Atare a wɔabɔ ho ban nyinaa a wɔaka abom | 330 (Sprout JoinSplit biara bu sɛ 2) |
| Orchard nneyɛe | 330 |
| Sapling inputs a ɛka ho ne outputs | 300 |
| Sprout JoinNkyekyɛmu | 25 |

Nkitahodi no afã horow a ɛda adi pefee no nnya nkɛntɛnso, na 2 MB block kɛse anohyeto no da so ara yɛ adwuma.

Anohyeto no wɔ hɔ efisɛ sɛ ɛnte saa a, blocks pii bɛkyerɛ adwuma pii ama wallet ne nodes. Sɛ caps no wɔ hɔ a, asɛm a enye koraa no nya **ye** ankasa sen nnɛ, mpo sɛ blocks no dɔɔso mmɔho abiɛsa:

- **Wallet sync:** data dodow a wobetumi ahyɛ wallet a ɛyɛ hare ma watwe wɔ da koro mu no so tew fi bɛyɛ 271 MB kosi bɛyɛ 169 MB, bɛyɛ 38% a ɛso tew. Nsɛm a wɔde yiyi nsɛm mu wɔ sɔhwɛ mu a enye koraa no so tew fi bɛyɛ ɔpepem 4.8 kosi bɛyɛ ɔpepem 2.3 da biara.
- **Block verification:** ZIP's benchmarks de Orchard block a ɛyɛ bɔne sen biara no to bɛyɛ 432 ms wɔ anohyeto foforo no ase, a ɛne bɛyɛ 770 ms ma nnɛyi asɛm a enye koraa. Wɔ Sapling, drop no yɛ kɛse, efi bɛyɛ 3,175 ms kosi bɛyɛ 272 ms.

Sapling ne Sprout kyɛw no yɛ nea wɔahyɛ da ayɛ den. Ɛde besi May 2026 no, Orchard kura ZEC, Sapling 11.6% ne Sprout 0.5%, enti atare nketewa no nya baabi a ɛdɔɔso ma wɔn dwumadie ankasa berɛ a ɛma ɔtowhfoɔ bi sua sɛ ɔde bedi dwuma ayayadeɛ. Esiane sɛ ZIP 317 ho ka gye ade koro wɔ adeyɛ biara a ntease wom wɔ ɔtare biara mu nti, ɔtowhyɛfo nnya hwee denam ɔtare biako a ɔde spam bɛmena mmom sen sɛ ɔbɛma foforo so.

### Nneɛma a wɔde fa mu

Sɛ wowɔ Orchard nneyɛe 330 wɔ block biara mu a, Orchard asɛm a ɛyɛ 2-action a ɛyɛ gyinapɛn no fata ⌊330 / 2⌋ = mpɛn 165 wɔ block biara mu. Wɔ block biako mu wɔ sikani 25 biara mu a ɛyɛ bɛyɛ **6.6 nkitahodi wɔ sekan biara mu**, ɛboro bɛyɛ 2.9 nnɛ — ZIP frɛ no 2.3× nkɔanim wɔ Orchard throughput a ɛyɛ daa mu. Sapling pue bɛyɛ 3.0 wɔ sekan biara mu, na ɛda so ara boro nea Orchard di ho dwuma nnɛ no so.

### Nsakrae a ɛyɛ den

Nsɛnnennen algorithm no averages wɔ window a nnansa yi blocks. ZIP 218 ma saa mfɛnsere no so fi 17 blocks kɔ 102, enti ɛda so ara kata bɛyɛ 2,550 seconds of real time, span koro no ara a ɛkataa so bere a Zcash de 150-second blocks fii ase no. ZIP no de nteaseɛ mmienu ma: sɛ wɔbɛkwati sɛ wɔbɛma ntua a ɛyɛ den-manipulation ayɛ mmerɛw (ɛfa Litecoin April 2026 MWEB asɛm no ka), ne sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a bere tiaa mu nsakrae a ɛba wɔ block bere mu no ayɛ mmerɛw.

Wɔ activation akyi pɛɛ no, block times begye bere kakra ansa na akɔtra botae foforo no so. Ɛno yɛ nea wɔhwɛ kwan na ɛkyerɛ nea esii wɔ Blossom, bere a Zcash fii sikɔne 150 kɔɔ 75 no.

### Defaults ma nodes ne sika kotoku

Eyinom yɛ nyansahyɛ ahorow a wɔde bedi dwuma mmom sen mmara a wɔpene so:

- **Transaction expiry:** default expiry no kɔ soro fi 40 kosi 120 blocks, ɛkɔ so bɛyɛ simma 50 koro no ara.
- **Maximum reorg depth:** Zebra's anohyeto no kɔ soro fi 99 kosi 600 blocks, bɛyɛ nnɔnhwerew 4.2 wɔ sikani 25 mu, mfɛnsere koro no ara a ɛkataa so bere a wɔde sii hɔ no.
- **Anchor bun ma shielded transactions:** tra 3 blocks, enti delay no so tew fi simma 3.75 kosi simma 1.25. ZIP no di Blossom nhwɛso no akyi wɔ ha.
- **Networking constants dodoɔ bi** a wɔsusuu wɔ blocks mu no, wɔma ɛyɛ mmiɛnsa enti ɛkata berɛ dodoɔ korɔ no ara so.

## Nea ɛkɔ so yɛ nea ɛte saa ara

- ZEC de ma da biara, fã nhyehyɛe ne nneɛma a wɔde ma no cap
- 2 MB block kɛse anohyeto no
- Nkitahodi a ɛda adi pefee, a adeyɛ anohyeto foforo no nka ho
- Coinbase onyin wɔ 100 blocks. Hyɛ no nsow sɛ mprempren eyi kyerɛ bɛyɛ simma 42 sen sɛ ɛbɛyɛ bɛyɛ 125, efisɛ akontaabu no yɛ blocks, ɛnyɛ bere.

## Aguadi-off: blocks a ayɛ dedaw pii

Block ahorow a ɛyɛ ntɛmntɛm no nyɛ nea wontua hwee. Stale block yɛ block a ɛfata a ɛhwere mmirikatu a wɔde bɛka nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no ho efisɛ block foforo dii kan duu network no ho. Dodow a nsonsonoe a ɛda block ahorow ntam no yɛ tiaa no, dodow no ara na eyi ba, na ZIP no de stale rate no bata block propagation, verification time ne mining centralization risk ho.

- **Ɛnnɛ:** bɛyɛ 0.4%, a ZIP no hyɛ no nsow no betumi abɔ rate a ɛwɔ ase no adewa efisɛ hashpower ayɛ kɛse wɔ pools mu.
- **Theoretical wɔ 25 seconds:** bɛyɛ 3.26%, a egyina susuw Zcash trɛw akyɛde so.
- **Devnet sɔhwɛ:** Zebra nodes 99 a wɔakyekyɛ wɔ asasesin mu a ɛyɛ 2 MB blocks a edi mũ wɔ 25-second spacing mu no susuw 4.86% stale rate ne 0.37% fork rate. Tuning biako pɛ a na ehia ne TCP nhyehyɛe. Esiane sɛ na saa devnet no decentralized sen nnɛyi mainnet nti, ZIP no di eyinom ho dwuma sɛ ɛbɛn akontaabu a enye koraa.
- **Nsɛm a wɔde gyina so:** ZIP no de Ethereum abakɔsɛm mu adanse-a-ɛyɛ-adwuma stale rate a ɛyɛ 5.4% di dwuma sɛ ne ahobammɔ aboboano. Devnet akontaabu abien no nyinaa te n’ase.

Ɛka nketewa abien nso wɔ hɔ. Hann sika kotoku twe bɛyɛ 200 KB pii da biara a ɛyɛ compact block headers. Na esiane sɛ block ahorow no dɔɔso mmɔho abiɛsa nti, node a ɛyɛ ma a ayɛ offline no wɔ blocks pii a ɛsɛ sɛ wodi ho dwuma bere a ɛkyere no, ɛwom mpo sɛ block biara bo yɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛhwɛ sɛ ɛyɛ nokware de. ZIP gye abien no nyinaa tom.

## Gyinabea ne bere nhyehyɛe

- **ZIP tebea:** Draft. Wɔn a wɔyɛ wuranom Dev Ojha ne Evan Forbes; wɔbɔɔ no 13 Oforisuo 2026.
- **Coinholder poll:** wɔtoo mu 14 September 2026, a 99.9% na ɛboaa. Nhwehwɛmu no kyerɛ sɛ wɔpɛ; ɛnsakra mmara a wɔpene so no ankasa.
- **Bere nhyehyɛe:** wɔ Zcash Community Forum dawurubɔ bi mu wɔ 17 September mu no, nkɔso ahyehyɛde ahorow no penee so sɛ wɔbɛyɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛbɛma wɔawie mmara no ansa na September 30 adu, NU7 wɔ testnet so wɔ October 6, gyinaesi a etwa to ne mainnet activation sorokɔ wɔ October 20, ne mainnet activation a wɔde wɔn ani asi so sɛ wɔbɛyɛ bɛyɛ 5 November 2026. November 5 yɛ botae, ɛnyɛ da a wɔahyɛ ato hɔ, kosi sɛ wɔde sorokɔ no besi hɔ.
- **Nneɛma a wɔde di dwuma:** wɔadi akyi wɔ Zebra ([#11440](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zebra/issues/11440)) ne Zakura ([PR #1066](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1066)).

## Nea eyi kyerɛ ma wo

- **Holding ZEC:** biribiara nni hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ. Wo kari pɛ ne nhyehyɛe a wode bɛma no nnya nkɛntɛnso biara.
- **Wɔde sika kotoku a wode bedi dwuma:** update bere a wo sika kotoku no de NU7 mmoa amena. Wɔn a wodi kan si so dua no bɛba bɛyɛ mprɛnsa ntɛm.
- **Node, exchange anaa service a wobɛtu mmirika:** yɛ nhyehyɛe sɛ wobɛma wo ho ayɛ yie ansa na woayɛ adwuma, na hwɛ nhyehyeɛ biara a wɔsusuu wɔ blocks mu, ɛfiri sɛ seesei block dodoɔ a wɔahyɛ da ayɛ no kata berɛ a na ɛyɛ kane no nkyem mmiɛnsa mu baako so.
