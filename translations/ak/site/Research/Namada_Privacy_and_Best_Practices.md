# Namada Ahintasɛm Nkyerεkyerεmu a Ɛfata pa ara no

> Akwankyerɛ a mfaso wɔ so, na wotumi de di dwuma ma wonya ahobammɔ kɛse wɔ Namada - ne ntease ankasa a ɛwɔ baabi a n'ahobanbɔ no wie.

*Privacy yɛ hokwan a ɛho hia.** Namada no wɔ botae sɛ wɔbɛbɔ ho ban denam advanced zero-knowledge cryptography so. Saa akwankyerԑ yi de nsunsuansoɔ pa ahorow bi di dwuma ma wɔn a wɔde ahobanbɔ bɔ ne bra na wɔyɛ nhomasua dwumadie mu adwuma no.

---

## Sɛnea Namada Bɔ Wo Ho Ban Wɔ Nneɛma a Ɛmfa Wo ho no So

Namada yɛ ɔman, ahobammɔ-kan blockchain a ɔde nkotoku addresses sie, transaction amounts, ne balances de no di dwuma sɛ zero-knowledge proofs (zk-SNARKs) **.

### Abɔdeɛ mu Nyinaeɛ Ho Nsɛntitiriw

- **Shielded Transactions** - Ɛde obi a ɔde ne nsa aka ade, ɛne sika no sie koraa.
- *Multi-Asset Shielded Pool (MASP) - Private transfers, swaps, and bridging across any asset. **Nneɛma a wobɛtumi de adi dwuma wɔ wo ankasa ho no yɛ nea edi mu na ɛsan nso di akoten wɔ afoforo so.
- ** Cross-Chain Privacy** - Shielded bridging via IBC (Ethereum ne Solana mmoa bɛba ntɛm).
- *Shielded Yield Rewards* - Nya NAM tokens denam nnwumakuo a wobɛnya ho mfasoɔ no so.
- **Twe ka ketewa** - Ahintasɛm a emu yɛ den bere a w'anfa wo ho ammɔ so.

---

## Nneɛma a Ɛho Hia Paa a Wɔamma No Ankɔ So

Sɛ mpo, ahoma mu ahobanbɔ a emu yɛ den paa no betumi asesa esiane dwuma fεfoͻ suban anaa nneɛma bi nti.

<div class="border-l-4 border-yellow-400 bg-yellow-400/10 p-6 my-8 rounded-r-xl text-sm">

** Namada Nnye ntom wɔ nea edidi so yi ho:**

- Wofa wo ho a ɛnni VPN anaa Tor (wo IP address no da adi)
- Wɔsan de address a wɔabɔ ho ban no di dwuma mpɛn pii
- Ɔyɛ a, dwumadie bi wɔ hɔ (a wɔnfa ho nto so) na ɛyɛ aniha.
- Wo Namada address a wode bɛtoto social media anaa wo real-world identity ho no
- Deɛ ɛfa KYC ho a wɔde di dwuma wɔ afidie so ma obi nya sika anaa ɔtwe no firi n'akwan mu.

</div>

## Akwan a eye sen biara wɔ ahobammɔ ho no

### 1.Nsɛm a y'akyerɛmu no wɔ hɔ.
- Default to **shielded transactions** for every action. (Nneɛma a wɔde asie biara wɔ hɔ).
- Mmafa adrɛs a wɔde asie no nni dwuma bio wɔ atirimpɔw afoforo ho.
- Mma nnkɔfa dwumadie a wɔayi no ne nea ɛdaa adi mmɔ ho bere koro mu.

### 2. Nneɛma a wɔde di nsɛ no
- Fa adrɛs a w'ayi no adi akyerɛ wɔn nkutoo di dwuma wɔ bridges a ɛreba so.
- Sɛ wonya hyɛ baabi a, ntɛm ara bɔ nneɛma ho ban.
- Sɛ ɛbɛyɛ yiye a, yɛ nhyehyɛe na woantwe wɔn amfi Namada.

### 3. MASP (Multi-Asset Shielded Pool) - Ɔfese a wɔhwɛ so bɔ nneɛma pii ho ban.
- Fa nneɛma nyinaa hyɛ MASP mu bere a w'ayi no adi.
- Fa wo MASP akatua no yɛ w'ankasa sika kotoku titiriw.

### 4. Hwehwɛ Nsaano nkyerɛwee
- Fa wo key a wode bɛhwɛ nneɛma no kyɛ wɔn nkutoo a w'agye wɔn adi.
- Mma w'ani mmmere anaa mfa wo nsa nka ahwehwɛde no wɔ baguam.

### 5. Adwuma ho ahotew
- Hyɛ bere ne sika a wɔde yɛ adwuma no mu biara ho.
- Sɛ ɛbɛyɛ yiye a, fa nneɛma pii to gua.
- Mma wo mfa sika a emu yɛ duru anaa nea wotumi hu no nkɔma afoforo.

### 6. Dwumadi Ahotɔsoɔ
- Bere biara fa VPN di dwuma (sɛ ɛbɛyɛ yiye a Tor) bere a wo ne wallets anaa dApps redi nkitaho.
- Mmafa screenshots a ɛwɔ address anaa balance no mmɔ obiara.
- Fa sika nkotoku a ɛsono emu biara di dwuma wɔ nneɛma ahorow mu (tɔn, ntoboa, nea wo ankasa de yɛ adwuma).

---

## Ahwehwɛde a wɔabrɛ mu ama ahobammɔ ho nsɛm no

1. **Sɔ daa na w'atumi abɔ wo ho ban** - fa sika kɔ MASP mu ansa na wode ayɛ adwuma.
2. **Sesa adrɛs a w'ayi no asi hɔ** daa ma nsεmfua ahorow.
3. **Fa sika no kɔ address a wɔabɔ ho ban** so tẽẽ fi sikakorabea ahorow hɔ bere biara a ɛbɛyɛ yiye.
4. **Sesa dwumadie bere a** na ama woatumi ahu kwan.
5. **Fa hardware wallets** di dwuma ma nnwumakuo akɛseɛ.
6. **Ma software no ho ntwԑntwetwe** - da biara fa Namada client a' edi kan di dwuma.
7. Fa encryption ne password manager a ano yɛ den bɔ wo afidie ho ban.
8. **Sɔ ahwɛ paa** fa metadata a ɛyera wɔ chat anaa log ahorow mu.
