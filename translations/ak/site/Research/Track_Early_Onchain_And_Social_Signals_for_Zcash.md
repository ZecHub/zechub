# Tracking Mfiase On-nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn & Social Nsɛnkyerɛnne ma Zcash (ZEC) .

### Nnianimu 

Saa nhwehwɛmu asɛm yi hwehwɛ sɛnea nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn mu nsɛnkyerɛnne a edi kan ma Zcash, titiriw whale a wɔde tu ne asetra mu nkate nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛwɔ nhyiam ase te sɛ X so no wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛmu a ɛho hia wɔ ZEC ahoɔden so. Zcash yɛ cryptocurrency a ɛtwe adwene si kokoamsɛm so a wonya fi Bitcoin codebase mu, a ade foforo titiriw ne sɛ wɔde encrypted ledger bɛka ho denam zero-knowledge proofs a wɔde bedi dwuma so. 

Messari ZEC Dashboard ne X nkɔmmɔbɔ mu nhwehwɛmu yɛ nnwinnade a wɔde hwɛ sɛ ebia Zcash tu akɛse no wɔ abusuabɔ ne tweets pii ne anigye a ɛrenya nkɔanim wɔ intanɛt so anaa. Bea foforo a ɛyɛ anigye ne sɛnea Zcash mpɔtam hɔfo dwumadi te sɛ sika kotoku, nsɛm a esisi ne nkɔso nya Zcash a ɛkɔ gua so no so nkɛntɛnso.

Wɔhwɛ ZEC nneɛma a atwam nso so na wɔde toto kokoam sika afoforo ho; sɛnea nnwuma ahorow ne nnwuma ahorow gye wɔn tom na wogye wɔn tom no boa ma wɔde nsɛm a ɛfa ho ma de gyina nkɔso no so. Ɛnyɛ sɛ yɛbɛfa ZEC bo ho nsusuwii a ɛyɛ hu anaasɛ yɛbɛhyɛ nkɔm a ɛnyɛ mmɔdenbɔ na mmom yɛbɛhwehwɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne mu ntɛm de akyerɛ anigye a ɛrenya nkɔanim, nsakrae akɛse anaa mpɔtam hɔ dwumadi ansa na ɔmanfo pii ahu.

Blockchain mfiridwuma yɛ adwuma wɔ ɔkwan foforo so na adwumayɛ wɔ cryptocurrency mu no de osuahu kɛse ba. Efi sɛnea nkitahodi ahorow kɔ so kosi sɛnea ɛde nsakrae kɛse ba gua so nneyɛe ne nkate mu so no, wobetumi asua gua no mu nkɔso a ɛyɛ anigye no ho ade; mfaso ne adehwere no yɛ osuahu ahorow a wodii kan nyae. 

Mfitiaseɛ on-chain metrics wɔ dwumadie mu gyina hɔ sɛ dwumadie a ɛdi kan no mu baako ne metrics a tumi wom a ɛda akwankyerɛ ne nkɔsoɔ a ɛkɔ gua so ne saa ara nso na investor no dwumadiɛ ne nsakraeɛ a ɛbɛtumi aba a ɛbɛba daakye a enni akyiri wɔ crypto wiase no mu. Bio nso, sɛ wohu saa nsɛnkyerɛnne yi wɔ bere ano a, ebetumi aboa sikakorafo ma wɔanya mfaso, titiriw bere a wɔde nneɛma a ɛnyɛ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn te sɛ sohyial media nkate mu nhwehwɛmu ka ho no. 
Sɛnea Messari ZEC Dashboard, CoinMetrics ne Blockchair kyerɛ no, nhwehwɛmu a wɔyɛ wɔ ZEC network dwumadi ho no ma wonya nhumu a wotumi de di dwuma wɔ sɛ ebia whale akɛse a wɔde wɔn ho kɔ baabiara no ne nkɔanim a ɛba wɔ asetra mu dwumadi te sɛ tweet dodow ne anigye a ɛtrɛw wɔ intanɛt so no hyia anaa. Titiriw no, Messari nhyehyɛe ahorow da nkɔanim a ɛda adi pefee wɔ da biara da nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn so aguadi dodow ne sika a wɔde sesa nneɛma mu wɔ bere a efi July 1 kosi 22, 2025 no mu, na ɛkɔɔ soro bɛyɛ July 22; a ɛne asetra mu nkɔmmɔbɔ a ɛkɔ soro ne nneɛma bo a ɛkɔ soro hyia .
Blockchair data kyerɛ sɛ ZEC kyɛfa a ɛwɔ address ahorow a wɔabɔ ho ban mu no kɔɔ soro fi bɛyɛ 18 % koduu 20 % wɔ mfɛnsere koro no ara mu, a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔde nnwuma a ɛkora kokoam nsɛm so no redi dwuma kɛse.  (Hwɛ Mfonini 1)

### Zcash sɛ kokoam sika a wɔde sie

Zcash yɛ cryptocurrencies a wɔayɛ a ɔdefoɔ kokoamsɛm wɔ adwene mu a ɛde nimdeɛ adanseɛ zero (zk-SNARKs) di dwuma no mu baako na wɔnya firi Bitcoin koodu no mu sɛ kyɛnsee; mprempren wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no betumi ayɛ nnwuma a ɛda adi pefee ne nea wɔabɔ ho ban nyinaa. Bere a botae no ne sɛ ɛbɛboa ma wɔabɔ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no kokoam nsɛm ho ban no, sɛ wonya pii a, ɛma ɛyɛ den sɛ wobɛhwɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne a ebetumi ama aguadi ne sika a wɔde bɛto mu no ayɛ mmerɛw kɛse. Nanso ɛnyɛ nea entumi nyɛ yiye. 

Nea ɛnte sɛ Monero ne DASH, a wɔtaa kyekyɛ mu sɛ “kokoam sika” no, DASH atwe ne ho afi kokoam sika nkyekyɛmu ho wɔ aban kwan so wɔ nneɛma mu nsakrae ne mmara nkyerɛase a ɛrekɔ so akyi (CoinTelegraph, 2024) . ZEC da so ara hyia akansi ne nhwehwɛmu, nanso wobetumi asusuw ne nkɔso denam nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn so dwumadi, whale akwantu abusuabɔ, tweet dodow, ne nkitahodibea a wɔde wɔn ho hyɛ mu a wɔmfa wɔn ho nto bo a wɔbɔ nkutoo so.

On-chain dwumadie kyerɛ nkitahodiɛ a ɛkɔ so tẽẽ wɔ blockchain so nyinaa, te sɛ sika kotokuo a wɔde kɔ sika kotokuo mu, token swaps, smart contract nkitahodiɛ, sika a wɔde sie ne sika a wɔyi firi exchanges mu, NFT minting anaa transfers ne staking/unlocking events. Wobetumi asusuw nneyɛe nhyehyɛe a ɛwɔ nnipa atitiriw mu, titiriw whale, aguadidan akuw, wɔn a wɔyɛ, anaa ahyehyɛde sika kotoku mu bere a wɔayɛ saa nsɛm yi mu nhwehwɛmu no na ɛbɛyɛ yiye sɛ wɔbɛte sɛnea ɛne wɔn ho wɔn ho di nkitaho wɔ gua no a wɔkyerɛ mu no ase yiye.

Sɛnea Messari ZEC Dashboard (Messari) kyerɛ no, wɔ July 1 ne July 22, 2025 ntam no, na nnwuma 12 na ɛboroo ZEC 1,000, nea ɛsõ sen biara no ne ZEC 5,200 yɛ pɛ (CoinMetrics, 2025). Saa ZEC dodow a wɔahyɛ no hye yi de sikasesɛw a ɛba no dwumadi a ɛda nsow kaa ho, a ɛkyerɛ sɛ ebetumi aba sɛ wɔbɛtɔn anaasɛ wɔbɛsakra sikakorabea dwumadi.

Bere koro no ara mu no, na shielded transactions, a ɛyɛ Zcash kokoam nsɛm nhyehyɛe no fã titiriw no da so ara rehu sɛ wɔregye atom wɔ ɔkwan a ntease wom so. Eduu July no, na ZEC a wɔde ma nyinaa mu bɛyɛ 20% wɔ address ahorow a wɔabɔ ho ban mu. (Blockchair 2025) (hwɛ Mfonini 4) .

## Ɔmanfo Nkate 

Wɔ July 2025 mu no, sɛ wɔkyekyɛ mu a, Tweet Volume a ɛka ZEC ho asɛm no bɛyɛ 450 posts da biara a ɛboro 1,200 wɔ July 22, da a wɔde whale dii gua kɛseɛ. ZEC mfiridwuma mu nkɔsoɔ ne kokoam nsɛm no twee adwene pa, sɛdeɛ influencer akontaabuo te sɛ @MarcoPoloMaps de nne maeɛ no. Saa nsɛm yi de adanse ma sɛ whale dwumadi betumi akanyan mpɔtam hɔfo nkitahodi wɔ Intanɛt so. 

Kokoam nsɛm ne nnansa yi mfiridwuma mu nkɔsoɔ, a ɛne Zcash 6.2.0 protocol nkɔsoɔ ne Zashi 2.0 sika kotokuo a wɔayi no adi (Zcash Foundation Blog, Electric Coin Co. Release Notes), de adwene pa baa tweet 13,500 a wɔyɛɛ mu nhwehwɛmu no mu 55%. 30% wɔ tweets no mu no na ɛnyɛ afã biara, na ɛtwe adwene si nsɛm ho amanneɛbɔ anaa tech nhwehwɛmu so, bere a 15% yɛ nea enye, a wɔakyekyere no kɛse wɔ nsusuwii hunu mmara tebea te sɛ kokoam sika a wɔahyɛ sɛ wɔbɛbara a EU de ama afe 2027 no ho. 

Esiane sɛ na akyidifo bɛboro 10,000 nti, akontaabu te sɛ @MarcoPoloMaps de tweet dodow no nyinaa mu nkyem anan mu biako mae, na ɛnam so, na ɛho hia wɔ su no mu. Bere a Zcash 6.2.0 a wɔayɛ no foforo ne Zashi 2.0 a wɔayi no adi no nyaa adwene no, wɔn nkɔmmɔbɔ a ɛfa nsɛm a esisi yi ho no maa mpɔtam hɔfo adwempa yɛɛ kɛse. On-chain data foaa nkɔanim a wohui wɔ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no dwumadi mu, na address ahorow a wɔyɛ adwuma da biara da dodow kɔɔ soro koduu 3,200; nkɔanim 15% a ɛne mmere a whale aguadi a ɛkɔ soro no hyia kɛse.

Wɔhwɛɛ platform afoforo te sɛ Discord, Reddit, ne Zcash Community Hub sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wobenya atenka a ɛfa wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no ho ne sɛnea nkurɔfo yɛ nnam no. How merchants are adopting ZEC were checked out , titiriw ne sɛnea wɔde di dwuma wɔ akatua ano aduru te sɛ Brave Wallet ne Zashi. Nea ɛka ho no, wɔhwɛɛ adwuma no nkɔsoɔ nkɔsoɔ denam GitHub ntoboa ne nnansa yi nkɔsoɔ a wɔayɛ wɔ ntwamutam no so, a Zcash v6.2.0 a wɔayi no adi ka ho.

Na abusuabɔ a ɛkɔ fam nso wɔ hɔ a ɛkyerɛ sɛ sɛ asetra mu nkate yɛ papa a, nkitahodi a wɔabɔ ho ban no dodow taa kɔ soro, na ɛkyerɛ sɛ nkɔmmɔbɔ a ɛfa kokoam nsɛm ho betumi ama nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn mu dwumadi ankasa anya nkɔso ankasa.

Nea ɛyɛ anigye no, mpɔtam hɔfo a wɔde wɔn ho hyɛ mu no huruw 12% wɔ Zashi 2.0 a wɔde sii hɔ akyi, a nkɔmmɔbɔ pii twee adwene sii kokoam nsɛm foforo ne sɛnea wobedi mmara mu akwanside ahorow ho dwuma so. ## Cross-Chain Attention: NEAR ne Maya
Nea afi ase retwe adwene akɔ Zcash so kɛse wɔ asram kakraa bi a atwam no mu no nyɛ whale aguadi a ɛrekɔ soro anaasɛ Intanɛt so mpɔtam hɔfo a wɔrebɔ nkɔmmɔ nko, na mmom ɔkwan a ZEC afa so atwa n’ankasa ahye so. Nkabom a ɛne NEAR Intents infrastructure no abue apon ama nsakraeɛ a ɛnyɛ den na wɔmma ho kwan wɔ ZEC ne agyapadeɛ te sɛ Bitcoin, Ethereum, ne NEAR ankasa ntam. Nea edi kan koraa no, na nnwuma a ɛkora kokoam nsɛm so no betumi afa nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn mu a akasakasa a ɛba wɔ nneɛma a wɔde sesa nneɛma mu anaasɛ ɛka kɛse a wɔbɔ no rentwetwe. Wɔ Zcash dwumadiefoɔ fam no, na eyi kyerɛ pii sen sɛ ɛbɛyɛ mmerɛw; na ɛyɛ nsato a wɔde to nsa frɛ no komm sɛ wɔmmɛbɔ nkɔmmɔ a ɛtrɛw a nhyehyɛe ahorow a egyina adwene so ne ankorankoro sika bom. Hackathon a ɛhyɛɛ saa adwumayɛkuw yi agyirae no nyɛ mfiridwuma mu adeyɛ bi kɛkɛ na mmom na ɛyɛ adwene a wɔde kyerɛ. Wɔn a wɔyɛ nneɛma ne wɔn a wɔkamfo kokoam nsɛm kyerɛ no hyiaam sɛ wɔresusuw sɛnea ebia ZEC bɛyɛ wɔ daakye a ɛfa nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn a wɔatwa ho ahyia mu ho. Adwene a na ɛwɔ nhyiam ahorow ase wɔ ɛno akyi no de ahoɔden ho adwene bi bae. Nkurɔfo kaa eyi ho asɛm sɛ ɛyɛ nneɛma foforo a ebetumi aba mfiase ne hokwan a ɛbɛma Zcash agyae sɛ wɔbɛyɛ no sɛ sɔhwɛ a atew ne ho. Na ɛrebɛyɛ sika a ebetumi akɔ baabiara a sika a wɔde di dwuma no te mmom.
Maya Protocol a aba no ama ZEC anya adwene foforo. Ɛha no, na ɛnyɛ AI nkitahodi a ɛyɛ fɛ so dua na mmom na wɔhyɛ bɔ a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛma sika a wɔde bɛkɔ baabiara no. Ɛdenam Zcash a wɔde besi Maya cross-chain AMM no mu so no, na wɔn a wɔwɔ no betumi adi gua wɔ kokoam a wɔremfa ahofadi mma centralized exchanges. Wɔ bere a mmarahyɛfo redi kokoam nsɛm ho sika ho akɔneaba na nneɛma a wɔayi afi wɔn din mu abɛyɛ ade a wɔyɛ daa mu no, na eyi ho hia kɛse. Wɔ mpɔtam hɔfo pii fam no, na Maya nte nka sɛ ɔyɛ obi a wɔaka abom kɛse na mmom na ɔte nka sɛ nkwa nhama. Na ɛyɛ nkaebɔ sɛ ZEC da so ara betumi atra gua so a wɔrenkotow wɔn a wɔhwɛ so no. Ɔkwankyerɛ no twe adwene si ZEC a wɔde bɛhyɛ Maya abɔde a nkwa wom mu kɛse, na wɔn a wɔde di dwuma dedaw no ka ho asɛm sɛ ɔkwan a wɔbɛfa so ama wɔn sikasɛm adwumayɛbea no akɔ so atra hɔ a amammui tebea mfa ho.
Saa nkabom ahorow yi nyinaa abom ayɛ biribi a ɛyɛ anifere nanso emu dɔ. Wɔatwe Zcash akɔ nkɔmmɔbɔ a wɔne mpɔtam afoforo bɔ mu. NEAR developers a ebia na wɔnhwɛ kokoam sika bi mprenu da no mprempren hu no sɛ ɔhokafo wɔ adwini a wɔde adwempa di dwuma mu. Mayafo a wɔde di dwuma a wonim kasa a ɛfa sika a wɔde di dwuma ne nsakrae ho no rehyia address ahorow a wɔabɔ ho ban ne nyansapɛ a ɛne sɛ wonni nimdeɛ biara no nea edi kan. ZEC nkyerɛ n’ankasa agyidifo nkutoo bio. Ɛrefi ase reyɛ nnyigyei wɔ nkuruwankuruwa foforo mu a ɛnyɛ kokoamsɛm yɛ ade bi nko na mmom ɛyɛ bridge a ɛda ntam nkitahodi ahorow, protocol ahorow, ne nnipa a wɔde di dwuma no ntam.

# Awie
Sɛ yɛde ne nyinaa bɛka abom a, nhwehwɛmu yi kyerɛ sɛ Zcash on-chain ne social signals, ɛwom sɛ ɛtɔ mmere bi a ɛyɛ anifere de, nanso ɛda abɔde a nkwa wom nhyehyɛe a ɛyɛ nnam na ɛrenya nkɔso adi.
Anohyeto ahorow wɔ hɔ; din a wɔmmɔ a ɛfa whale sika kotoku ho a ɛma ɛyɛ den sɛ wobehu sɛ ebia na nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn mu nkitahodi atitiriw no yɛ gua so tɔn anaasɛ gua so tu ara kwa ne mmara nhyehyɛe a ɛresakra, te sɛ EU kokoam sika a wɔbarae wɔ afe 2027 mu, a ebia ɛrenya nea wɔde di dwuma no nkate dedaw nea ɛka ho no, ebia ahotoso a wɔde to asetra mu nsɛm te sɛ X so no nka ankorankoro a wɔde wɔn adwene si kokoam nsɛm so, a ebia wɔbɛpɛ sɛ wɔpaw nkitahodi akwan foforo a egyina wɔn din a wɔmmɔ wɔn din so anaasɛ ahobammɔ wom no adwene so.


Ebia Zcash renhoahoa ne ho wɔ mpɔtam kɛse te sɛ Monero anaasɛ aguadifo a ɛtrɛw ho mprempren ara nanso ne mfiridwuma mu nkɔso a ɛkɔ so; te sɛ v6.2.0 protocol upgrade a ɛma shielded transaction efficiency tu mpɔn, Zashi 2.0 wallet a wɔayi no adi a ɛwɔ mobile dwumadie a ɛyɛ papa ne shielded-by-default support, ne cross-chain integrations denam NEAR Intents ne Maya Protocol so de no da nsow sɛ kokoam sika a ɛma ne infrastructure no mu dɔ nkakrankakra na ɛtrɛw ne nsa ka. Ɛfata sɛ yɛhyɛ no nsow sɛ July 2025 maa Zcash san nyaa nkɔsoɔ, na ɛmaa nkɔmmɔdie baa X so a ɛfa whale ho nkitahodiɛ kɛseɛ ho. Bio nso, nkɔanim a ɛkɔ soro bere nyinaa wɔ nnwuma a wɔabɔ ho ban mu no kyerɛ sɛ kokoam nsɛm da so ara wɔ mfaso kɛse ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no. Abusuabɔ soronko bi wɔ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn mu nsɛm a esisi ne asetra mu nsɛm ntam, na ɛda sɛnea ahemman abien no nyinaa nya wɔn ho wɔn ho so nkɛntɛnso adi. Ɛwom sɛ mmara kwan so akwanside ahorow a yɛbɛfa so no da so ara yɛ asɛnnennen de, nanso sɛ yɛde yɛn ani si nsɛnkyerɛnne a edi kan yi so a, ɛbɛma ntease a yɛwɔ wɔ ZEC kwan a ebetumi aba no ho no ayɛ kɛse, a ɛho nhia sɛ yɛhyɛ nneɛma bo ho nkɔm.
