## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Step-by-Step Guide

### Anammɔn 1: Hyehyɛ Adansedie a Wobɛtua ka no

- Yɛ address a w'ayi no asi hɔ (z)
- Fa eyi yɛ adwuma ma wo nsa ka sika a wobɛma no


### Anammɔn 2: Ma wo nsa nka nkatua ho akwankyerɛ no bi.

Wɔ dwumadie biara ho:
- Ma w'adansedie no nna adi.
- Ma wo nkyerɛ sika dodoɔ a wode bɛtua no.
- Wopɛ a, fa nkaeԑbɔ bi ka ho.

### Anammɔn 3: Di akwankyerɛ so

Fa di dwuma:
- Memos (e.g., Order ID) - Nkrataa ahodoɔ a etwa sɛ yɛhyehyɛ wɔ kasa ahodoɔ mu
- anaa off-chain tracking (database, spreadsheet)


Nhwɛso:
- Ahyɛde #4821


### Anammɔn 4: Siesie wo ka no so na di ho dwuma.

- Di nsɛm a w'aka no so na fa di dwuma.
- Nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛfa agyinatufoɔ ho no yɛ adwuma wɔ akwan ahodoɔ so.


### Anammɔn 5: Ma w'ankasa wo ho ahotew ntena hɔ

- Sɛ ɛbɛyɛ yiye a, mma wonnyi addresses no mfi w'adwumam bio.
- Mma nnkyerɛ tratrahyɛnkɔn abakɔsɛm mu
- Sɛ ɛho behia a, akwantuo nkrataa ahodoɔ no nyinaa wɔ ɔfese baako mu.
