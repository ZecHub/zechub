## <img src="/content-images/celebration-spark-svgrepo-com-bc98dec7c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="spark icon"/> Why Zcash?

Zcash ma *nhyehyɛe a ɛkyerɛ sɛ obi di so* ne "ahonyade ho dawuru" mu da nsow:

- Adesamma a wɔn ani da hɔ no hwɛ sɛ sika ne nkontaabu biara nni ho, enti wo nsa ka bi a ɛkyerɛ sɛ w'ahyehyɛ biribiara wɔ so.
- ** Encrypted memos** betumi de one time login code akɔhyɛ adwuma bi mu wɔ kokoam
- ** View keys** ma kwan sɛ wobɛtumi ayi nea wopɛ akyerɛ, enti app no betumi anya deɛ ɛhia na ɛnyɛ hwee bio.

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> How It Works

Akwan mmienu na ada adi. Abɔdeɛ no nyinaa de wo din a ɛda hɔ ma w'adwumam, nanso ɛnni password biara wɔ so.

### Akwankyerԑ 1: Twerԑ krataafa a' ԑka sԑ wo yԑ obi.

1. App no yɛ biribi a wɔde di dwuma prɛko na ɛyɛ basabasa.
2. Wo sika nkontaabu kyerɛ sɛ wo nsa ano ahyɛnsode no wɔ w'adrɛse akyi.
3. App no bɛhwɛ sɛ wo nsa a wode ato so no yɛ nokware na ɛde wo aba mu.

Biribiara nni hɔ a wɔmmɔ, enti ɛnni ka biara na nso wɔntwɛn sɛ wɔbɛbɔ no. Nsɛm pɔtee bi ne: "Ɛyɛ dɛn araa na obi bɛtumi abɔ biribi ho dawuro?" [ZIP 304, Sapling Address Signatures (Ɔkwan a wɔfa so de adansedi krataa di dwuma)](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0304), a da so yɛ nsusuiɛ, enti akwantuo nkotoku mmoa ma message signing no gu ahorow.

### Abɔdeɛ 2: Sukoa kɛzi ɛbanyia ezukoa mɔɔ bɛtua wɔ mekɛ mɔɔ ɛlɛdi gyima la anwo ngyinlazo.

1. App no yɛ one time code na ɛkyerɛ a wobetua sika ho adesrɛde.
2. Wo de, fa saa code no to krataa a wɔde bɔ wo ho ban mu ma wɔn.
3. App no hwehwɛ memo no, di ne code no akyi na ɛde wo ba mu

Eyi yɛ adwuma wɔ sika nkotoku a ɛboa memos nnɛ, ne dodow no ara. N'afɛfɛde ne sɛ ɛka network ka na w'atwɛn agye atom.

### Adesamma a wɔn ho yɛ hu no, ma w'ani nna hɔ.

Ntwerɔdeɛ bi ntumi mfa wo address nsie na ama w'ahunu no. Nhyehyɛeɛ binom de ne ho ka application-specific value, enti baabi biara wɔhunu identifier soronko a ɛgyina hɔ ma nipa koro no ara. Wei mma kwan sɛ baabi bɛtumi atoto wo din mu afa so aka wo account ahorow abɔsoɛ.

Nkyεrεmu a wɔkyerε:

- **Nnipa ntumi nnya account no bio wɔ default mu.** Sɛ wo bɛhwere key no a, ɛkyerɛ sɛ wobɛyera account no bi. Ɛnsɛ app no de ɔkwan foforo ma so gye anaa
- Address reuse can link you.** address koro no ara a wode di dwuma wɔ mmeae pii so ma nsaneɛdi ho haw ba bio, ɛno nti na app pɔtee mu ahwehwɛde hia.
- ** No ratified standard.** Adwuma biara wɔ ne nhyehyɛeɛ, enti login a wɔayɛ ama obi no ntumi nyɛ adwuma mma ɔfoforɔ.
- ** Ɛnyɛ din a wonhu wo ho no ankasa.** Ɛde w'ahonyade sie wɔ app no anim, nanso app no betumi ayɛ nea woyɛ bere a wowɔ mu akyiwadeɛ.

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Ɔhaw code a wobɛsan de adi dwuma. Ɛsɛ sɛ wode koode biara di dwuma prɛko pɛ na ne bere ba ntɛm, anaa wobɛtumi asan ayi bi ato hɔ bio
- Sɛ wo nsa bɛka sika a ɛho hia de akɔ wɛbsaet hɔ. Sika no yɛ adanse, enti ɛsɛ sɛ ne dodow nyɛ hwee
- Agyirae a ɛnnyɛ adwuma no sie bere a application-specific identifier bɛyɛ saa adwuma koro no ara
- Sɛ wogye di sɛ message signing yɛ adwuma wɔ baabiara a, hwɛ wallet ahorow a w'adwumamfoɔ no ankasa wɔ.
- Sɛ wode krataa bi yɛ adwuma a, ɛkyerɛ sɛ nea ɔde too gua no na ɔyɛɛ saa. Ɛnyɛ asɛmfua biara ne "password"

## <img src="/content-images/checked-checkbox-svgrepo-com-7ea19022da.svg" width="28" height="28" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="done icon"/> Projects Exploring This

Wɔyɛɛ saa maa Zcash Login akwan a ɛrekɔ so wɔ Amerika no. [ZecHub Hackathon 3.0 - Abɔdin a wɔde yɛ nhwehwɛmu wɔ nnipa mu.](https://zechub.wiki/hackathon)Wͻyɛ nhwehwεmu sene sɛ w'awie nnoɔma, na ɛkyerɛ sɛnea adwene koro no ara betumi asiesie ne ho akwan horow.

- **ZecAuth** - a wallet connection protocol for Zcash, wɔ honhom mu nea WalletConnect yɛ no baabi foforo. app no kyerɛ QR code anaa `zecauth://` link a ɔsoa challenge plus tumi no bi aa ɔrehwehwɛ, te sɛ login, payment requests anaa viewing access. No transaction, no fee, no chain interaction. Ɔde protocol nkyerɛwee ne code no di dwuma wɔ akwan nyinaa so na ama woatumi ayɛ adwuma yiye bere biara wͻ aberɛ a wonam kwan foforɔ so de adi dwuma no akyi.
- ZShield - ma adansedie a w'ayi no asi hɔ ayɛ W3C DID ne OpenID Connect. Browser bɔ keypair, server de nonce to interface wɔ ZIP 304 kwan so na wallet hyɛ ho nsa, na server san fa JWT ba. Esiane sɛ nea ebefi mu aba bɛyɛ OIDC compatible nti, app ahorow betumi adi dwuma bere a wɔnfa nnipakan bi mma saa dwumadie yi akyi
- **ZecPass** - di dwuma ma wohu sɛ obi na ɔde saa app no adi dwuma, enti ɛntumi nhunu user address. Ɛma wɔde application scope hash yɛ adwuma te sε stable identifier, ɛsan de nsɛmmisa a wɔn nsa ka bere mu ne nea wɔfa so somee to hɔ, afei nso ɛde React button kɔto baabiara fa Node verification library ho
- **Portal** - login denam shielded transaction a wɔde one time code wɔ memo no mu, na edi dwuma mainnet so. Same flow no ara de di dwuma bio sɛ wode bɛbue paid content ne sɛ wobɛsoma anaa woagye sika afi link bi ho
- **ZcashMe** - de sikasɛm ho nkrataa di dwuma sɛ wo nipa a w'agye no atom, na ɛtwe adwene si kɔmputa so dwumadie ne mobile ntam nsonsonoe so ma enti sɛ wode laptop hyɛ din wɔ intanɛt dwumadibea hɔ a ɛnhia browser ahwehwɛde biara.
- **ZBooks** - nwoma a wɔde di dwuma ne nea wɔtua no, na ɛhwɛ Zcash so sɛ ɔfã bi a wobetumi de adi dwuma bio sen sɛ ɛyɛ ade ankasa. Ɛkenkan sika ho nsɛm denam Unified Full Viewing Key (Key) so
