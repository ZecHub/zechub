> Kratafa yi yɛ nsɛm a ɛfa biribiara ho, ɛnyɛ mmara anaa towtua ho afotu. U.S. towtua ho mmara sesa, enti hwɛ anammɔn biara a wobɛtu no wɔ wo akontaabufo anaa towtua ho ɔfotufo nkyɛn. Mmara biara a ɛwɔ aseɛ ha no de nkitahodie kɔ ne IRS fibea, a wɔhwɛɛ mu wɔ October 2026 mu de gyinaa Publication 526 (ma 2025 sanba) ne Form 8283 (December 2025 nsakraeɛ). Wɔkyerɛw IRS virtual currency FAQ no maa nnwuma a wɔyɛe ansa na afe 2025 reba, enti hwɛ sɛ ɛne mprempren akwankyerɛ nhyia.

## TL;DR

- Fa ntoboa gu sika kotoku a wɔabɔ ho ban a w’ahyehyɛde no di so mu, na ɛnyɛ adwumayɛfo bi ankasa sika kotoku
- IRS bu ZEC sɛ agyapade, enti ZEC akyɛde yɛ ntoboa a ɛnyɛ sika
- Sɛ wopɛ akyɛde a ɛyɛ $250 anaa nea ɛboro saa a, fa krataa a wɔakyerɛw a ɛkyerɛkyerɛ ZEC no mu nanso ɛmfa dɔla bo nhyɛ so mena nea ɔde ma no
- Sɛ wopɛ akyɛde a ɛboro $5,000 a, nea ɔde ma no hia nhwehwɛmu a ɛfata, na wode wo nsa hyɛ wɔn Krataa 8283 ase
- Sɛ wotɔn ZEC wɔ mfeɛ mmiɛnsa akyi a wo nsa aka no na wode wo nsa ahyɛ Ɔfa B ase wɔ ɔdemafoɔ no Krataa 8283 mu a, fa Krataa 8282 no kɔ
- Mpɛn pii no, obi a ɔde mmoa ma a ɔmmɔ ne din nkyerɛ wo no ntumi nkyerɛ sɛ wɔtew $250 anaa nea ɛboro saa so

## <img src="/content-images/user-svgrepo-com-21adf62b7c.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="user icon"/> Who is this for?

- US 501(c)(3) adɔeɛ akuo, asɔre ne mpɔtam akuo a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔgye ZEC tom
- Sikakorabea sohwɛfo, nhoma so ahwɛfo ne board mufo hyehyɛ crypto ma
- Wɔn a wɔde mmoa ma a wɔpɛ sɛ wohu nea adwumakuw bi a wɔnyɛ adwuma a mfaso wɔ so hia fi wɔn hɔ

Sɛ wopɛ ankorankoro ne adebɔfo a, hwɛ [Gye Ntoboa Wɔ Kokoam](/zcash-use-cases/receive-donations-privately).

## <img src="/content-images/warning-error-svgrepo-com-b7ea8a50da.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="warning icon"/> The problem

Crypto-donation dwumadie dodoɔ no ara ma U.S. adɔeɛ akuo ma wo address a ɛda adi pefee, anaasɛ ɛdane akyɛdeɛ no ma wo denam baako so. Wɔ address a ɛda adi pefee so no, obiara betumi ahu akyɛde biara, ne dodow ne wo sika a aka. Obi a ɔde mmoa ma a ɔhyɛ da de ma wɔ kokoam no hwere saa kokoamsɛm no bere a akyɛde no bɛba fam no.

Adwumakuw bi a wɔnyɛ adwuma a mfaso wɔ so nso wɔ nkrataa a ankorankoro bi nni: sika a wɔde ma, IRS nkrataa a wɔde kyerɛw akyɛde akɛse, ne afe afe sika a wɔde san de ma. Kokoamsɛm ne saa nkrataa no nyinaa betumi ayɛ adwuma, bere tenten a woayɛ nhyehyɛe ama wɔn ansa na akyɛde a edi kan no aba no.

| Bere a wotɔn nneɛma | Sɛ wo exchange no gye sika a wɔde asie a ɛda adi pefee nkutoo a, sika dodow a wubetu afi shielded pool no mu no bɛyɛ baguam (hwɛ [afiri a wɔde dannan nneɛma](/zcash-tech/the-turnstile)) |

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-toolbox-9bebbb1619.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="toolbox icon"/> What you need

- Akyɛdeɛ a wɔgye ho nhyehyɛeɛ, a wo board apene so, a ɛfa cryptocurrency ho
- A [sikabɔtɔ](/using-zcash/wallets) a address ahorow a wɔabɔ ho ban, a ahyehyɛde no na ɛhwɛ so
- Exchange account a ɛwɔ ahyehyɛde no din mu, sɛ woayɛ nhyehyɛe sɛ wobɛdan ZEC akɔ dɔla mu a
- Akontaabufo a wadi akyɛde ahorow a ɛnyɛ sika ho dwuma

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Step-by-step guide

### Anamɔn 1: Si wo nhyehyɛe no ho gyinae

Kyerɛw to hɔ, na ma wo board no mpene so:
- Hena na obetumi de sika akɔ baabi foforo, ne sɛ ebia ɛsɛ sɛ nnipa baanu pene tu biara so anaa
- Sɛ́ ebia wobɛtɔn akyɛde biara ntɛm ara anaasɛ wokura ZEC bi
- Sɛ́ ebia wugye akyɛde a wɔmmɔ wo din, ne dodow a ɛkɔ soro

### Anamɔn 2: Fa sika kotoku bi si hɔ a ahyehyɛde no di so

- Yɛ sika kotoku no wɔ ahyehyɛde mfiri bi so, na ɛnyɛ w’ankasa telefon so
- Fa aba kasasin no sie offline, a polisifo baanu a wɔn mu biara betumi adu ho
- Sɛ wopɛ tumi a wɔkyɛ a, hwɛ [Di Private Community Sikakorabea bi so](/zcash-use-cases/private-community-treasury)

### Anamɔn 3: Kyerɛw address a wɔabɔ ho ban

- Kyɛ address a wɔabɔ ho ban nkutoo wɔ wo ntoboa krataafa no so. BI [address a wɔaka abom](/using-zcash/unified-addresses) fi ase wɔ `u1`, nanso ebetumi nso kura nea wogye no a ɛda adi pefee, na afei akyɛde bi a efi sika kotoku a ɛyɛ mmerɛw nkutoo mu ba fam wɔ baguam. Fa wo sika kotoku no address a wɔabɔ ho ban nkutoo no di dwuma sɛ ɛde bi ma a.
- Mfa address a ɛda adi pefee ntintim (ɛfi ase `t1` or `t3`)
- Bisa wɔn a wɔde mmoa ma a wɔpɛ sɛ wonya krataa a wɔde gye sika no sɛ wɔmfa wɔn email nhyɛ.. [memo a wɔde kyerɛw nsɛm](/using-zcash/memos). Wɔakora memo no so, enti w’ahyehyɛde no nkutoo na ebetumi akenkan.

### Anamɔn 4: Kyerɛw akyɛde biara

Wɔ akyɛde biara ho no, kyerɛw sɛ:
- Da ne bere a wɔde maa no
- Sika dodow a ɛwɔ ZEC mu
- Bo a ɛsom wɔ U.S. dɔla mu saa bere no, ne bo fibea a wode dii dwuma
- Aguadi ID ne memo

IRS no bu ZEC sɛ agyapade ([Amanneɛbɔ 2014-21](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-14-21.pdf)), enti eyi yɛ ntoboa a ɛnyɛ sika a wɔde ma wɔ mo nhoma ahorow mu. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wo accountant no akenkan nkutoo kwan a, hwɛ [Kyerɛwtohɔ a wɔde ZEC a wɔabɔ ho ban no sie](/zcash-use-cases/keeping-records-with-shielded-zec).

### Anamɔn 5: Gye akyɛde ahorow a ɛyɛ $250 anaa nea ɛboro saa tom

Obi a ɔde mmoa ma no ntumi ntwa akyɛde biako pɛ a ɛyɛ $250 anaa nea ɛboro saa mu a wankyerɛw krataa amfi wo hɔ ([Nhoma a wotintimii wɔ afe 1771 mu](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1771.pdf)). Sɛ wopɛ ZEC akyɛde a, fa ka ho:
- W’ahyehyɛde no din
- Da a wo nsa kaa no
- Akyɛdeɛ no ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu, sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no "1.5 ZEC". Mfa dɔla bo nhyɛ so: akyɛde no bo a wobɛkyerɛ no yɛ nea ɔde ma no adwuma.
- Sɛ́ ebia wode biribi mae de tuaa ka no, sɛ nhwɛso no "Wɔamfa nneɛma anaa nnwuma biara amma de annye saa ntoboa yi."

Ɛsɛ sɛ nea ɔde ma no nya bi ansa na wɔde wɔn towtua ho nkrataa akɔma ([Nhoma a Wɔatintim 526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p526.pdf)). Mpɛn pii no, ahyehyɛde ahorow de mena ansa na afe a edi hɔ no January 31 adu; sɛ wode bɛmena ntɛm ara a, ɛyɛ mmerɛw sen biara.

### Anamɔn 6: Fa wo nsa hyɛ Form 8283 ase ma akyɛde a ɛboro $5,000

Sɛ obi a ɔde mmoa ma no hwehwɛ sɛ wonya bɛboro dɔla 5,000 a, ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ nhwehwɛmu a ɛfata. Crypto nkan sɛ ahobammɔ a wɔtɔn wɔ baguam, enti nhwehwɛmu no fa ho ([Nhoma a Wɔatintim 526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p526.pdf), [CCA 202302012 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-wd/202302012.pdf)). Nea ɔde ma no de wo brɛ [Krataa 8283](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8283.pdf), na wode wo nsa hyɛ Ɔfa B no fã a wɔde ama no ase.Wo nsaano nkyerɛwee no si so dua sɛ wo nsa kaa ZEC no saa da no; ɛnkyerɛ sɛ nea ɔde mae no bo yɛ den ([IRS Nsɛm a Wɔtaa bisa, Q36](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions)).

### Anamɔn 7: Dane kɔ dɔla mu, sɛ ɛho hia a

- Hwɛ exchange ahorow a wogye sika a wɔde asie a wɔabɔ ho ban wɔ so [Nneɛma a Wɔde Sesa Wɔn a Wɔhwɛ Nnipa So](/using-zcash/custodial-exchanges). Pii gye sika a wɔde sie a ɛda adi pefee nkutoo.
- Sɛ wo de no yɛ nea ɛda adi nkutoo a, fa ZEC no fi wo sika kotoku a wɔabɔ ho ban no mu kɔ exchange no mu wɔ anammɔn biako mu, bere a woretɔn no. Saa sika no bɛyɛ baguam, nanso w’akyɛde afoforo ne sika a aka no kɔ so yɛ kokoam.
- Sɛ wotɔn ZEC wɔ mfeɛ mmiɛnsa mu firi berɛ a wo nsa aka no, na wode wo nsa ahyɛ Ɔfa B ase wɔ ɔdemafoɔ no Form 8283 mu a, fa fael [Krataa 8282](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8282.pdf) wɔ nnafua 125 akyi wɔ adetɔn no akyi na fa bi mena nea ɔde mae no ([IRS Nsɛm a Wɔtaa bisa, Q37](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions)).

### Anamɔn 8: Bɔ wo afe afe sankɔhwɛ ho amanneɛ

Bɔ ZEC akyɛde ahorow ho amanneɛ sɛ ntoboa a ɛnyɛ sika wɔ wo Form 990-series sanba no so, ne [Nhyehyɛe M](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-schedule-m-form-990) sɛ ɛfa wo ho a. Bisa wo akontaabufo sɛnea ɔbɛkyerɛw akyɛde ahorow a wɔmmɔ din.

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/user-svgrepo-com-21adf62b7c.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="user icon"/> Anonymous donors: what they can and can't claim

- Kokoamsɛm a efi ɔmanfo hɔ ne wo din a wɔmmɔ no nyɛ ade koro. Obi a ɔde mmoa ma betumi de ama wɔ kokoam na ɔda so ara akyɛ wɔn email wɔ memo no mu de anya krataa a wɔde gye sika.
- Obi a ɔde mmoa ma a ɔmmɔ wo din ntumi nnya agyede krataa, enti wontumi ntwe akyɛde biako pɛ a ɛyɛ $250 anaa nea ɛboro saa mfi mu.
- Bɛboro $5,000 no, nea ɔde mmoa ma no nso hia nhwehwɛmu ne wo nsaano nkyerɛwee wɔ Form 8283 so, a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔbɛkyerɛ wo nnipa ko a wɔyɛ.
- Wɔ nea ɔde mae no fam no, mpɛn pii no, wɔtwe ZEC akyɛde a wɔde bɛboro afe biako no fi gua so bo a ɛfata so, na wontua tow biara wɔ sika kɛse a wonya fi akyɛde no ho ([IRS Nsɛm a Wɔtaa bisa, Q34–Q35](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions)).

Ka eyi wɔ wo ntoboa krataafa so, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔn a wɔde mmoa ma no betumi apaw ansa na wɔde ama.

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Common mistakes to avoid

- Akyɛde ahorow a wɔde hyɛ adwumayɛfo bi ankasa sika kotoku mu
- Address a ɛda adi pefee, anaa address a wode sika bɛsesa, a wobɛtintim sɛ wo ntoboa address
- Dɔla bo a wɔde bɛhyɛ krataa a wɔde gye tom no mu
- Wɔtɔn wɔ mfe abiɛsa ntam na wɔn werɛ fi Form 8282
- Ɔhyɛ bɔ sɛ wɔbɛtew tow a wɔbɛtew so ama wɔn a wɔde mmoa ma a wɔmmɔ wɔn din
