## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Step-by-Step Guide

### Anammɔn 1: Yɛ ka a wobɛtua no address

Yɛ address a wɔayi no afiri mu.

Anhwɛ a:
- Fa adrɛs ahorow di dwuma ma clients ahodoɔ


### Anammɔn 2: Fa Address ne Client no di nkitaho

Fa wo z-address no kɔma w'adwumamfo no tẽẽ.

Mma eyi nnyɛ wo botae:
- Sɛ ɛho nhia a, yɛ no wɔ baguam.

### Anammɔn 3: Fa nkaeԑbɔ di dwuma ma akwankyerԑ no so.

Sɛ woretua sika a:
- Kyerε wo kͻmputa no sεε w'anya bi (Ɛho nhwɛso, ID) a εbԑkyerεw ne nyinaa.

Eyi bɛboa wo:
- Ma wo nsa nka nkrataa a wode di dwuma no bere biara na amma w'adwumam adi ho adanseɛ.


### Anammɔn 4: Ma obiara nhu sɛ wo ne obi foforo na ɛte.

Sɛ ɛho hia a:
- Fa adaka anaa address a ɛsono emu biara di dwuma ma obi.
- Mmafa obiara ho nsie wo yɔnko nnipa mu.

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Common Mistakes to Avoid
