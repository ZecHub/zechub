## <img src="/content-images/icons8-lock-2f8e221321.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="toolbox icon"/> Why Zcash?


Zcash ma kwan sɛ wɔde nneɛma a wɔabɔ ho ban no di dwuma de yɛ "ne nyinaa so" (fully private transfers) na wɔayɛ adwuma.

Eyi yɛ ɔfã bi:
- Address linking a wobɛtumi ayɛ no bi
- Aban a wɔde yɛ adwuma no hwehwɛ mu wɔ baabiara.

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Step-by-Step Guide

### Anammɔn 1: Hwɛ sɛ wɔabɔ sika no ho ban

Sɛ wo sika wɔ address a w'atumi ahu mu:
- Fa wɔn kɔ baabi a wɔabɔ ho ban kan.


### Anammɔn 2: Fa firi Shielded kɔ Shielded so
Daa mepɛ sɛ mebɔ wo din pa ara. `z to z` adwuma no ho nhyehyɛe

Eyi ma wonya ahobammɔ a ɛboro so.


### Nea ɛto so 3: Kwati sɛ wobɛfa obi ho agyigye wo nsɛm mu

Mfa:
- Bɔ trɛsan no ho dawuro wɔ baguam.
- Fa adrɛs koro no ara di dwuma wɔ nsɛm a ɛfa ho nyinaa mu
- Fa adrɛs di dwuma kɔ amanɔne a wonim no mu.

### Nea Ɛto so Abien: Susuw Bere Ho Nhyehyɛe ho

Nkɔsoɔ a edi kan:
- Sɛ wo nsa ka sika pii a, mma w'ani mmmere ho sɛ wobɛsoma bi ntɛm ara.
- Sɛ ɛbɛyɛ yiye a, twa kwan bi so di dwuma.
