## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Step-by-Step Guide

### Anamɔn 1: Hwɛ sɛ Wɔabɔ Sika no Ho Ban

Sɛ wo sika wɔ address a ɛda adi pefee so a:
- Fa wɔn kɔ address a wɔabɔ ho ban mu kan


### Anamɔn 2: Fa fi Shielded so kɔ Shielded so
Bere nyinaa pɛ `z to z` asɛm a wɔde di gua

Eyi ma wonya kokoam nsɛm a ɛsen biara.


### Anamɔn 3: Kwati Identity Linkage

NNYƐ:
- Fa nkitahodi ahorow ho amanneɛbɔ ma wɔ baguam
- San fa address koro no ara di dwuma wɔ nsɛm a ɛfa ho nyinaa mu
- Fa address ahorow bata nnipa a wonim wɔn ho

### Anamɔn 4: Susuw Bere Nhyehyɛe Ho

Afotu a ɛkɔ akyiri:
- Kwati sɛ wode bɛmena ntɛm ara bere a woanya sika pii akyi
- Bu nhyehyɛe ahorow a wotumi hyɛ ho nkɔm wɔ baabi a ɛbɛyɛ yiye
