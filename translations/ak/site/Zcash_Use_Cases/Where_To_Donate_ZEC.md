> Wɔhwɛɛ mu nea etwa to: October 27, 2026, wɔ ahyehyɛde biara ankasa ntoboa kratafa so. Address ahorow sesa, enti krataafa yi de link kɔ nkratafa a wɔde ntoboa ma no so sen sɛ ɛbɛkyerɛw address ahorow no. Kɔpi address no fi ahyehyɛde no ankasa wɛbsaet so bere nyinaa. Towtua ho nkrataa yɛ nea ahyehyɛde biara ka fa ne ho, na ɛnyɛ towtua ho afotu.

## TL;DR

- Nnwumakuw akron a wɔnyɛ adwuma a wɔde hwehwɛ mfaso tintim ZEC address. Wɔn mu baanum de address a wɔabɔ ho ban na edi dwuma, enti w’akyɛde no tra nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn mu kokoam
- Nnwumakuw baanan a wɔagye din a wɔnyɛ adwuma a wɔde hwehwɛ mfaso gye ZEC tom wɔ address a ɛda adi pefee nkutoo so: EFF, Freedom of the Press Foundation, Tor Project ne Internet Archive
- Ntoboa nhyiamu te sɛ The Giving Block ne Every.org gye ZEC ma adɔeɛ akuo mpempem pii, nanso wɔn mu biara nka sɛ ɛboa ZEC a wɔabɔ ho ban
- Sɛ wuhia tow krataa a, hwɛ ahyehyɛde biara krataa a ɛwɔ ase hɔ no ansa na wode ama

## <img src="/content-images/user-svgrepo-com-21adf62b7c.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="user icon"/> Who is this for?

- ZEC holders a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔde biribi ma adwuma bi a ɛho hia wɔn
- Wɔn a wɔde mmoa ma a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔn akyɛde no tra kokoam
- Obiara a ɔrehwehwɛ ahyehyɛde ahorow a wɔakyerɛw wɔn din sɛ ɔne ZEC bɛboa

Sɛ woyɛ adwuma bi a ɛnyɛ nea wɔhwehwɛ mfaso na wopɛ sɛ wugye ZEC, hwɛ [Gye Ankorankoro Ntoboa sɛ U.S. Adwumakuw a Ɛnyɛ Mfaso](/zcash-use-cases/accept-private-donations-as-a-us-nonprofit).

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-lock-2f8e221321.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="lock icon"/> Shielded or transparent: why it matters

Sɛ wode ZEC fi sika kotoku a wɔabɔ ho ban mu kɔ **address a wɔabɔ ho ban** so a, sika dodow no, nea ɔde kɔmaa ne nea ogye no nyinaa tra kokoam.

Sɛ wode kɔ **transparent** address so a, akyɛde no da adi wɔ blockchain no so: obiara betumi ahu sika dodow no, bere a ɛbaa ne ahyehyɛde no sika a aka. Wo sika kotoku a wɔabɔ ho ban no dwumadi foforo no tra kokoam, nanso akyɛde no ankasa yɛ baguam. Hwɛ [Turnstile a ɛwɔ hɔ no](/zcash-tech/the-turnstile) ma nea ɛbɛyɛ baguam bere a ZEC fi ɔtare a wɔabɔ ho ban mu no.

Wɔn baanu nyinaa da so ara boa ahyehyɛde no. Shielded ma akyɛde no yɛ kokoam kɛkɛ.

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> How to give

1. Paw ahyehyɛde bi wɔ atifi hɔ na bue ne ntoboa kratafa
2. Kɔpi ZEC address no fi saa krataafa no so, ɛnyɛ wiki yi anaa screenshot
3. Hwɛ nkyerɛwde kakraa bi a edi kan ne nea etwa to bere a wode ahyɛ mu awie no
4. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wonya krataa a wɔde gye sika a, fa wo email no ka memo no ho (wɔabɔ ho ban nkutoo) anaa di ahyehyɛde no krataa a ɛwɔ atifi hɔ no akyi
5. Send fi a [sika kotoku a wɔabɔ ho ban](/wallets)

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Common mistakes to avoid

- Address bi a wobɛfa afi forum post, social media anaa screenshot dedaw bi mu sen sɛ wobɛfa ahyehyɛde no ankasa krataafa so
- Sending from an exchange, a ebetumi de akyɛde no abata wo akontaabu ho
- Towtua ho krataa a wobɛhwɛ kwan a wunnni kan nhwɛ ahyehyɛde no krataa no mu
- Sɛ wode kɔ Sapling address bi so fi sika kotoku a entumi mfa nkɔ Sapling so

## <img src="/content-images/checked-checkbox-svgrepo-com-7ea19022da.svg" width="28" height="28" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="done icon"/> Result

Wobɛtumi:

Wunim adwumakuw bi a ɛnyɛ nea wɔhwehwɛ mfaso a wogye ZEC na wɔmfa wɔn din nhyɛ mu? Fa twetwe adesrɛ ho nyansahyɛ ma, fa n’ankasa ntoboa krataafa no bata ho.
