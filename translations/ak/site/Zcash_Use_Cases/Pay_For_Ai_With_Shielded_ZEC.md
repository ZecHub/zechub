## <img src="/content-images/warning-error-svgrepo-com-b7ea8a50da.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="warning icon"/> The Problem

AI ho ka a wobɛtua no taa kyerɛ kaad, email, ne akontaabu. Ɛno de nsɛm biara a wɔbɛka akyerɛ wo a wobɛkyerɛw no bata wo mmara kwan so nipasu ho, na nea ɔhwɛ sikatua so no nso hu.

Wɔkyerɛ sɛ Crypto na esiesie eyi, nanso akwankyerɛ dodow no ara ne bere atwam. Nnwuma sesa nea wogye tom, na nantew a wɔkyerɛw afe a atwam ni no bɛma woakɔ ɔkwan bi a ɛnyɛ adwuma bio so.

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-lock-2f8e221321.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="lock icon"/> Why Zcash?

Akatua a wɔabɔ ho ban de nea ɔde kɔmaa, nea ogye ne sika dodow no sie. Ɔsom no nya akatua, na obiara a ɔhwɛ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no nni hɔ a obehu onii a otuaa ka anaasɛ dodow a otuae.

Ɛno yɛ nokware sɛ wotua **fi** sika a wɔabɔ ho ban nkutoo a. Kratafa yi ka bere pɔtee a ɛkura ne bere a ɛnkura mu.

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-toolbox-9bebbb1619.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="toolbox icon"/> What You Need

- ZEC wɔ **shielded** kari pɛ mu
- Sika kotoku a ebetumi de akɔ address a ɛyɛ biako so. Saa nantew yi de **Noir Wallet**, browser ntrɛwmu di dwuma, enti nsuo a ɛsen no nyinaa tra mfɛnsere baako mu. Zkool ne Zodl yɛ adwuma wɔ ɔkwan koro no ara so
- Bɛyɛ $1 a wode bedi akyi

> **Wofiri exchange mu?** Exchange dodoɔ no ara, a Binance ka ho, twe ZEC kɔ **transparent** address nkutoo so, na wɔrennye a `u1...` address sɛ baabi a wɔrekɔ. Di kan twe sika kɔ w’ankasa address a ɛda adi pefee so, bɔ ho ban wɔ wo sika kotoku mu, afei tua fi sika a aka a wɔabɔ ho ban no mu.

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Route 1: Pay NanoGPT directly

[NanoGPT na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://nano-gpt.com/) ma wo 200+ models, a GPT, Claude, Gemini ne mfonini models ka ho, na ɛgye ZEC natively.

### Anamɔn 1: Bue mu. Wɔnkyerɛw wɔn din biara

Kɔ nano-gpt.com na fi ase de di dwuma. Session biara yɛ anonymous by default na app no ​​ankasa ka saa: *"Wode NanoGPT redi dwuma dedaw wɔ kokoam."* Akontaabu biara nni hɔ a wobɛbɔ na email biara nni hɔ a wode bɛma.

### Anamɔn 2: Di kan sie sign-in token

Ansa na wode sika bɛhyɛ mu no, bue **Settings** na yɛ sign-in token, afei fa sie baabi a ahobammɔ wɔ.

> **Saa anammɔn yi bɔ wo sika ho ban.** Anonymous balance tena wo browser no local data mu. Popa wo cookies no a wunni token a wode asie na sika a aka no ayera, a akontaabu biara nni hɔ a wubetumi asan anya afi mu. Yɛ eyi ansa na wode sika asie, na ɛnyɛ bere a woawie no.

### Anamɔn 3: Fa kari pɛ ka ho

Bue **Balance**, paw **Custom**, na kyerɛw sika dodow bi. Nea ɛba fam koraa ne **$0.10** na nea ɛboro so yɛ $5,000. NanoGPT ka nea ɛtɔ kyerɛ wo, bɛyɛ GPT 5.5 prompts 12 anaa mfonini 18 de gye $1.

![NanoGPT add balance screen showing the custom amount and the ten cent minimum](/content-images/nanogpt-add-balance-acc74a4e6d.webp)

### Anamɔn 4: Paw Zcash

Paw **Digital sika**, afei **Zcash** fi grid no mu.

Wobɛnya QR code, address a wobɛtua, ne **transfer minimum** wɔ ZEC mu ama sika dodoɔ a wopaw no. Saa akontaabu no bo yɛ den bere a kratafa no load no.

![NanoGPT Zcash deposit screen with the QR code, unified address and transfer minimum](/content-images/nanogpt-zec-deposit-bd1980d2f7.webp)

### Anamɔn 5: Fa fi wo sika kotoku mu mena

Kɔpi address no kɔ wo sika kotoku mu, kyerɛw sika dodow no, na fa mena. Netwɛk ho ka no bɛyɛ **0.00015 ZEC**.

> **Send slightly over the minimum.** Wɔbɔ quote no bo bere a krataafa no load na ZEC tu ansa na w'adwuma no asi so dua. Sɛ wode nea ɛba fam koraa no kɔe pɛpɛɛpɛ a, ɛbaa fam **$0.99** sen sɛ ɛbɛyɛ $1.00 wɔ sɔhwɛ mu. Sɛ wode kakra bɛboro so a, ɛde $1.17 sii fam maa $1 a wɔfrɛ no $1 koro no ara, efisɛ NanoGPT de nea wode mena ankasa no ma.

![Noir Wallet send screen with the NanoGPT address pasted in and the network fee shown](/content-images/noir-send-6380a5f4ef.webp)

### Anamɔn 6: Twɛn bɛyɛ sikɔne 30

Wo sika kotoku bɛkyerɛ asɛm no a ɛda so ara wɔ hɔ, afei ɛresi so dua. NanoGPT credits sika a aka no wɔ **first confirmation** no so, enti ɛnsɛ sɛ wotwɛn abiɛsa no nyinaa.

![Wallet confirmation showing the amount sent and the transaction hash](/content-images/noir-sent-2d476e94b9.webp)

Kari pɛ no bɛda adi na wubetumi asɛe no ntɛm ara.

![NanoGPT balance page showing the credited amount and deposit history](/content-images/nanogpt-balance-0b0c0c86ba.webp)

## <img src="/content-images/send-svgrepo-com-b62f643de0.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="send icon"/> Route 2: Services that do not take ZEC

AI dwumadie dodoɔ no ara nnye ZEC ntom. **Venice.ai** ne **OpenRouter** nyinaa fa USDC mmom, na OpenRouter ma wo paw nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn a checkout no si so.

Wɔn a, fa **CrossPay** di dwuma wɔ [Zodl](/zcash-organizations/zodl). Wosɛe ZEC a wɔabɔ ho ban na wotua nea wogye no wɔ agyapade a wɔsrɛɛ no ​​mu, wɔde fa NEAR Intents so a enni centralized exchange na enni KYC.

1. Nya ɔsom no sikatua address ne agyapadeɛ ne nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn a ɛhwɛ kwan, sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no USDC wɔ Base
2. Bue Zodl na paw **CrossPay** .
3. Hyehyɛ saa address no, paw agyapade a ɔsom no pɛ, na kyerɛw sika dodow no
4. Soma fi wo sika a aka a wɔabɔ ho ban no mu

Wo ZEC nhaban a wɔabɔ ho ban. Ɔsom no hu USDC sikatua a ɛyɛ ɔkwan biara so sɛ ɛreba na ɛnsua da sɛ efii ase sɛ ZEC.

> Swap nan no da adi wɔ destination chain no so, enti USDC sikatua no ankasa yɛ baguam te sɛ USDC sikatua foforo biara. Nea ɛda so ara yɛ kokoam ne Zcash afã ne abusuabɔ a ɛda abien no ntam.

### Nea enti a ɔtare no ho hia

NanoGPT deposit address no yɛ address a wɔaka abom. Decoding baako a wɔde maeɛ wɔ August 2026 mu no kyerɛ receivers mmienu pɛpɛɛpɛ: **Sapling** ne **Orchard**.

Esiane sɛ na... [Dade dua](/zcash-tech/ironwood) upgrade activated on 28 July 2026, Orchard yɛ spend-only na boɔ foforɔ biara ntumi nkɔ mu. Ɛno gyaw **Sapling sɛ nea ogye sika biako pɛ a obetumi asi fam wɔ mu ankasa**.

Enti sɛ wo ZEC no wɔ Sapling dedaw a, sikatua no yɛ Sapling to Sapling na biribiara nni ho a ɛyɛ baguam. Nanso sɛ woatu akɔ Ironwood a, tua a wotua no tu bo a ɛsom wɔ ɔtare bi hye so, na [afiri a wɔde dannan nneɛma](/zcash-tech/the-turnstile) tintim sika dodow ne ne sorokɔ ɛwom mpo sɛ nea ɔde kɔma ne nea ogye no tra hɔ ahintaw de.

Screens no hwɛ sɛ ɛyɛ pɛ wɔ ɔkwan biara so. Sapling sika ketewaa bi a wobɛkora so ama sikatua no ne ade a ɛyɛ mmerɛw sen biara a wobesiesie.

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Sika a wode bɛto hɔ ansa na wode sign-in token asie, afei wobɛpopa cookies
- Sending pɛpɛɛpɛ transfer minimum na landing cent tiawa
- Mmɔden a wobɛbɔ sɛ wobɛtwe wo ho afi nsakrae bi mu tẽẽ akɔ a `u1...` adrɛse
- Fa no sɛ sikatua no yɛ kokoam de a wonhwɛ pool ko a wosɛee sika fii mu no
- Tua a wotua wɔ nkitahodi a ɛyɛ daa so bere a na wontumi nhu asɛm no nyinaa
