## TL;DR

- Fa **address a wɔabɔ ho ban (z)** di dwuma.
- Mfa address a ɛda adi pefee (t) nkyɛ wɔ baguam da
- Hyɛ wɔn a wɔde mmoa ma no nkuran ma wɔmfa sika kotoku a wɔabɔ ho ban mu nkɔma
- Kwati sɛ wode wo identity bɛbata nnwuma ho

## <img src="/content-images/warning-error-svgrepo-com-b7ea8a50da.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="warning icon"/> The Problem

Cryptocurrencies dodow no ara da biribiara adi:
- Wo sika kotoku mu sika a ɛwɔ wo sika kotoku mu
- Hena na ɔde sika kɔmaa wo
- Wo nkitahodi ho abakɔsɛm nyinaa

Eyi de asiane ahorow a anibere wom ba:
- Sikasɛm mu kokoam nsɛm a wɔhwere
- Targeting anaa surveillance a wɔde wɔn ani si so
- Wɔn a wɔde mmoa ma no a wɔda wɔn ho adi wɔ baguam

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-lock-2f8e221321.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="toolbox icon"/> Why Zcash?

Zcash shielded nnwuma a wɔde sie:
- Address a ɔde kɔmaa no
- Address a nea ogye no
- Sika a wɔde di gua

Wei ma wotumi nya sika **a worenda wo sikasɛm ho mfonini adi**.

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Step-by-Step Guide

### Anamɔn 1: Yɛ Address a Wɔabɔ ho ban
Bue wo sika kotoku na yɛ **z-address**.
Eyi taa fi ase wɔ: `zs...`


### Anamɔn 2: Kyɛ Wo Shielded Address Nkutoo

- Twerɛ wo z-address no wɔ:
  - Wɛbsaet no
  - Social media sohyial media
  - Ntoboa ho nkratafa

**MMFA address a ɛda adi pefee (t) nkyɛ.**

### Anamɔn 3: Kyerɛkyerɛ wɔn a wɔde mmoa ma (Wɔpɛ nanso wɔkamfo kyerɛ) .

Hyɛ wɔn a wɔde mmoa ma no nkuran sɛ:
- Fa sika kotoku a ɛboa nnwuma a wɔabɔ ho ban di dwuma
- Fa fi atare a wɔabɔ ho ban (ɛnyɛ nea ɛda adi pefee) so mena .

### Anamɔn 4: Kura Kokoam Nsɛm Ho Ahotew Mfitiase Mu

- Kwati sɛ wobɛsan de address koro no ara adi dwuma wɔ nnipa a wonni abusuabɔ mu
- Mfa w’address no ntoto w’ankasa wo ho wɔ baguam gye sɛ ɛho hia

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Address a ɛda adi pefee (t) a wɔkyɛ 
- Wo sika kotoku a wode bɛto w’ankasa wo ho 
- Sika a wogye fi mmeae a ɛda adi pefee a wɔmfa kyɛm mma 
- Address koro no ara a wɔbɛsan de adi dwuma wɔ nsɛm pii mu
