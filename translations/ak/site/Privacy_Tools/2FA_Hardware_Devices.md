# 2FA Hardware Mfiri a Wɔde Di Dwuma

Data ahobammɔ ne kokoam nsɛm yɛ ade titiriw a ɛhaw ankorankoro ne nnwumakuw nyinaa. Intanɛt so ntua ne data a wobu so reyɛ kɛse, na ɛho hia sɛ woyɛ nneɛma a edi kan de bɔ data a ɛho hia ho ban na wokora wo kokoam nsɛm so. Ade biako a ɛte saa ne sɛ wode hardware security keys bedi dwuma, a ɛma ahobammɔ foforo de bɔ wo mfiri ne wo data ho ban.

## Dɛn ne hardware ahobammɔ safe?

Hardware ahobanbɔ safoa yɛ honam fam mfiri a wɔde di dwuma ma nneɛma abien ahotoso (2FA) de bɔ wo akontaabu ne mfiri ahorow ho ban. Ɛne wo din ne wo password bom yɛ adwuma de hwɛ hu sɛ wɔn a wɔama wɔn tumi nkutoo na wobetumi anya wo akontaabu ne wo data.

Wɔayɛ saa nsafe yi sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɛne wo din ne wo password bɛyɛ adwuma na ama wɔahwɛ ahu sɛ wɔn a wɔama wɔn tumi nkutoo na wobetumi anya wo akontaabu ne wo data. Wɔba wɔ akwan horow so te sɛ pen drives, NFC cards anaa Bluetooth mfiri.

![Hardware Ahobanbɔ Safoa Nhwɛso](/content-images/1dff66a4-d8e6-483f-9a1e-065d3974d665-a4c6c73e00.webp)

## Sɛnea hardware ahobammɔ safoa yɛ adwuma

Ɛsɛ sɛ wo nam hardware ahobanbɔ safoa so ma 2FA yɛ adwuma wɔ wɛbsaet anaa platform a ɛboa saa mfiridwuma yi, sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no Binance ne Coinbase nsakraeɛ.

Sɛ wode hardware ahobanbɔ safoa te sɛ YubiKey di dwuma a, wode hardware safoa no hyɛ USB anaa Type-C port a ɛwɔ wo desktop/laptop anaa smartphone so no mu kɛkɛ, na safoa no wie nokwaredi no ma wo.

## Mfaso bɛn na ɛwɔ so sɛ wode hardware security key bedi dwuma?

Hardware ahobammɔ safoa ma mfaso pii sen atetesɛm akwan a wɔfa so di adanse a egyina password so. Ɛyɛ den kɛse sɛ wobɛtow ahyɛ wɔn so sen password ahorow, a ɛnyɛ den sɛ wobetumi asɛe denam brute force, social engineering anaa phishing ntua so. Security keys ma code soronko bi ba bere biara a wɔde bedi dwuma, na ɛkame ayɛ sɛ ɛrentumi nyɛ yiye sɛ ntuafo betumi ahyɛ code no ho nkɔm anaasɛ wɔbɛsan ayɛ bi. Ɛnyɛ den nso sɛ wode bedi dwuma na woasiesie na enhia sɛ wode softwea anaa draiver foforo biara bɛhyɛ wo mfiri no so.

## So hardware ahobammɔ safe ne mfiri ne dwumadi nhyehyɛe nyinaa hyia?

Dabi, hardware ahobammɔ safe no wɔ ahorow pii na ebia ɛne mfiri ne dwumadi nhyehyɛe nyinaa nhyia. Ansa na wobɛtɔ safe bi no, hwɛ hu sɛ ɛne wo mfiri ne wo dwumadi nhyehyɛe no hyia.

![Hardware Ahobanbɔ Titiriw Fom Nneɛma](/content-images/a830c435-ad20-4183-b381-475afe1089b3-5537cd7388.webp)

## So wobetumi de hardware ahobammɔ safoa adi dwuma wɔ akontaabu ahorow pii mu?

Yiw, wobetumi de hardware ahobammɔ safoa biako adi dwuma de abɔ akontaabu pii ho ban wɔ wɛbsaet ne nnwuma ahorow so.

## Sɛ mehwere me hardware security key a, dɛn na ɛba?

Sɛ wohwere wo hardware security key a, wobɛkɔ asiane mu sɛ wɔbɛsiw wo accounts no ano. Wɔhyɛ nyansa sɛ wobɛnya backup key anaa authentication kwan foforɔ na woakwati saa tebea yi.

## Nhwɛso ahorow a ɛfa 2FA Hardware ho

2FA Hardware a ɛwɔ gua so ho nhwɛsoɔ bi ni.

**Yubico YubiKey 5 NFC** Ɔde ne nsa kyerɛɛ ne so.

![Yubico YubiKey 5 NFC na ɔkyerɛwee](/content-images/bf1ac95e-7277-4559-b5ef-63117b66971b-3aa542aba7.webp)

Yubico, a ɔne no yɛɛ FIDO U2F open authentication standard a ahobanbɔ safoa pii de di dwuma no na ɔyɛɛ no, YubiKey 5 NFC no wɔ nsa a ahobammɔ wom mu de bɔ wo mfiri ho ban. Sɛnea ne din kyerɛ no, ɛde USB A (anaasɛ USB C a wɔde adapter di dwuma) anaa NFC na ɛyɛ adwuma. 
Ɛde out of the box compatability ne nnwuma pii a Google Chrome, Facebook, Dropbox, LastPass, 1Password ne nea ɛkeka ho ka ho ma. YubiKey 5 no yɛ ketewa, ɛyɛ den na emu yɛ hare - na ɛyɛ nsuo mpo. 
Wubetumi asiesie YubiKey no sɛ U2F factor a ɛto so abien a ɔhaw biara nni ho denam ɔsom akwankyerɛ a wubedi akyi na wode safoa no ahyɛ USB slot no mu bere a wɔabisa wo no so. Sɛ wobɔ anaa womia sika kɔkɔɔ kurukuruwa a ɛwɔ safoa no so no a, ɛbɛkyerɛw wo safoa no, enti bere foforo a wobɛkɔ mu no, kyerɛw password no kɛkɛ, fa safoa no hyɛ mu, na pia.

**Thetis Fido U2F Ahobammɔ Safoa**

![Thetis Fido U2F Ahobammɔ Safoa](/content-images/b83e42e5-bba6-4175-ad6c-d64972ef54ef-a2ef17a10d.webp)

Sɛ worehwehwɛ bang-for-buck a eye sen biara wɔ ahobammɔ safoa mu a, ɛsɛ sɛ saa mfonini a efi Thetis yi wɔ wo din mu. Bluetooth biara nni hyɛn no mu (ɛwɔ nea ne bo yɛ den so), na ɛboa FIDO U2F gyinapɛn no nkutoo enti UAF anaa OTP a ɛne ne ho hyia biara nni hɔ. Nanso, ɛbɛma woanya wɛbsaet a ahobammɔ wom sɛ wode Chrome anaa Opera di dwuma sɛ browser wɔ Windows, macOS, anaa Linux so a. 
Handily, saa ahobammɔ safe a ne bo yɛ den yi sports a compact folding design a ɛkyinkyini aluminum casing a ɛmma connector no nkɔda adi wɔ nea ebetumi asɛe bere a ɛde rekɔ. Ɛwɔ kɔla abiɛsa a ɛyɛ fɛ: Tuntum, Sikakɔkɔɔ, ne Dwetɛ. 
Plastik a wɔde akata so no te nka sɛ ɛyɛ den na ɛyɛ den kakra, nanso wobɛpɛ sɛ wokwati sɛ wode nneɛma a emu yɛ duru bɛka wo bag mu. Tokuru a wɔde abɔ safe no so no nyɛ kɛse sɛnea ɛsɛ, enti wubenya akwan kakraa bi a wobɛfa so de safe anaa nhama abata ho.

**CryptoTrust NkutooSaa**

![CryptoTrust Nkutoo Safoa](/content-images/13be6ecc-227e-498c-8a36-6d52c023cd78-06e3e924dc.webp)

Sɛnea ne din kyerɛ no, OnlyKey wɔ nneɛma bi a ɛyɛ fɛ a ne akansifo nni. Esiane keypad a ɛwɔ hyɛn no mu a ebetumi atwa keyloggers a wonya kwan kɔ kɔmputa so nti, sɛ kɔmputa anaa wɛbsaet bi asɛe a, ebetumi ama intanɛt so akontaabu ahorow ayɛ nea ahobammɔ wom. Ɛboa akwan pii a wɔfa so yɛ 2FA, a FIDO 2 U2F, Yubico OTP, ne TOTP ka ho. 
Bere a ɛnyɛ asɛmfua sohwɛfo a eye sen biara ma atirimpɔw atitiriw no, OnlyKey betumi de OTP akontaabu 24, asɛmfua 24, ne edin/URL 24 asie, wɔ safoa biako so. Saa ade yi yɛ fɛ yiye sɛ w’ani nnye ho sɛ wobɛkyere password ahorow pii agu wo tirim na wopɛ sɛ safoa no di ho dwuma ma wo a. 
Ɛde nneɛma te sɛ encrypted backup, self-destruct (a ɛpopa device no bere a wɔabɔ mmɔden dodow bi a ɛnteɛ akyi), ne tumi a wɔde yɛ firmware no foforo na ama wɔanya nneɛma foforo ma. Nea enye koraa no, nea ɔde di dwuma no nyɛ nea eye koraa.
