Namada Protocol yɛ adwuma sɛ Layer 1 platform a egyina adanseɛ a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔde wɔn ho ahyɛ mu, a wɔayɛ sɛ ɛbɛma interchain agyapadeɛ-agnostic kokoamsɛm ama. Ɛnam Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC) protocol so no, Namada ne nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn a ɛba awiei ntɛmntɛm no bom a ɛnyɛ den, na ɛma wotumi yɛ adwuma yiye. Bio nso, Namada ne Ethereum de akwan abien a wontumi mfa wɔn ho nto so si hɔ, na ɛma nkitahodi a ahobammɔ wom na wotumi de ho to so wɔ ntam nkitahodi abien no ntam.

Namada de kokoamsɛm di kan denam Multi-Asset Shielded Pool (MASP) circuit no iteration a wɔama anya nkɔso a ɔde bedi dwuma no so. Saa nkyerɛaseɛ a wɔayɛ no foforɔ yi ma agyapadeɛ ahodoɔ nyinaa, a token a ɛtumi sesa ne nea ɛntumi sesa nyinaa ka ho, tumi de shared shielded set di dwuma pɛpɛɛpɛ sɛdeɛ Zcash deɛ no teɛ. Nea ɛde ba ne sɛ, adeyɛ a ɛne sɛ wɔde agyapade a wɔaboa no bɛma Namada no bɛyɛ soronko bere a ɛbɛyɛ den sɛ wobehu esiane kokoamsɛm a ɛkorɔn a ɛwɔ mu nti. Afei nso, Multi Asset Shielded Pool circuit no foforɔ a ɛtwa toɔ no ma shielded set rewards a ɛyɛ groundbreaking feature anaa incentive a ɛkyekyɛ nneɛma de hyɛ kokoamsɛm ho nkuran sɛ ɔmanfoɔ yiedie.

## Ethereum Bridge + IBC a ɛne no hyia

Ethereum bridge no a wɔde bɛka Namada ho no ma ɛho nhia sɛ wɔyɛ protocol a ɛyɛ soronko, efisɛ ɛbɛyɛ Namada abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no fã titire. Wɔde ahyɛ Validators a wɔwɔ Namada mu no nsa sɛ wɔmfa bridge no nkɔ Namada protocol titiriw no nkyɛn. Saa validators yi nso yɛ relayers bere a ɛfa agyapadeɛ a wɔde bɛma Namada ho no, na ɛma actors foforɔ a wɔde wɔn ho bɛhyɛ mu no ho nhia. Ɔkwan foforo so no, sɛ wɔde agyapade rekɔma Ethereum a, abɔnten so nnipa (a wonim wɔn sɛ relayers) na wɔde wɔn ho hyɛ mu, ɛwom sɛ wonni asɛyɛde biara sɛ wɔbɛma bridge no ayɛ nokware anaasɛ wɔbɛbɔ ho ban de.

![Ethereum Bridge Mfonini](/content-images/image-0fd8f754ba.webp)

Namada Protocol nso wɔ tumi a ɛde ne nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn biara a ɛba awiei ntɛmntɛm a ɛboa Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC) protocol no di nkitaho a ɛnyɛ den. Sɛ ɛba sɛ wɔne Ethereum bɛyɛ adwuma a, Namada de Ethereum bridge soronko bi a ahobammɔ wom a ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ ɔkwan a wontumi mfa wɔn ho nto so so di dwuma. Wɔayɛ saa bridge yi yie sɛ ɛbɛma ahobanbɔ adi kan denam flow controls a wɔbɛhyɛ mu den ama bridge connections nyinaa na wɔabu Ethereum transfer biara a ɛdi mfomsoɔ sɛ mfomsoɔ kɛseɛ a ɛbɛtumi ama wɔatwa asotweɛ so.

## Shielded Set Akatua a Wɔde Ma

Wɔ [Namada Protocol](https://blog.namada.net/what-is-namada/), wɔhyɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma a wɔwɔ agyapade a wɔabɔ ho ban no nkuran ma wɔde wɔn ho ahyɛ nhyehyɛe a wɔabɔ ho ban a wɔkyɛ no mu denneennen. Eyi nam MASP circuit a wɔayɛ no foforo a wɔde afrafra mu, a mprempren Convert Circuit foforo no ka ho no so na ɛbae. Ɛdenam saa ade foforo yi a wɔde di dwuma so no, Namada hyɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no nkuran sɛ wɔmfa mmoa mma wɔ kyɛfa a wɔabɔ ho ban no mu denam agyapade a wɔabɔ ho ban a wɔbɛkura so.

Wɔ Namada mu no, wobu set a wɔabɔ ho ban no sɛ ɛyɛ ɔmanfo yiyedi a ɛnyɛ nea ɛfa obiara ho na ɛne akansifo nhyia. Eyi kyerɛ sɛ bere a ankorankoro pii de nneɛma a wɔde kɔ baabi foforo a wɔabɔ ho ban di dwuma no, kokoam nsɛm ho bɔhyɛ ahorow no tu mpɔn ma obiara a ɔde ne ho hyɛ mu no. Protocol no gye hia a ɛho hia sɛ wɔbom gye tom na wɔde wɔn ho hyɛ mu wɔ kokoamsɛm a wɔbɛma akɔ anim ama wɔn a wɔde di dwuma nyinaa. Enti, ɛdenam nkuranhyɛ a ɛkanyan wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no ma wokura agyapade a wɔabɔ ho ban na wɔboa ma wɔkyɛ ahobammɔ nhyehyɛe no mu no, Namada hyɛ kokoam nsɛm a ɛyɛ den na ɛyɛ den kɛse ho nkuran.

## Agyapade a Wɔabɔ ho Ban ho Nkitahodi

Sɛ ɛba shielded transfers so a, sɛ́ ɛfa Ethereum non-fungible token (NFT), ATOM, anaa NAM ho no, wontumi nkyerɛ nsonsonoe a ɛda wɔn ho wɔn ho ntam. Wei kyerɛ sɛ nneɛma a ɛkora kokoamsɛm so a MASP (Modified Accumulator Sapling Protocol), Zcash Sapling circuit no fã a wɔama anya nkɔsoɔ de ama no, fa agyapadeɛ ahodoɔ nyinaa ho pɛpɛɛpɛ. MASP amansin no ma agyapadeɛ a ɛwɔ Namada abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no mu nyinaa tumi kyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ korɔ a wɔabɔ ho ban. Saa kwan yi hwɛ hu sɛ wɔrenkyekyɛ kokoam nsɛm ho bɔhyɛ ahorow mu wɔ ankorankoro agyapade ahorow mu. Ɛmfa ho sɛnea aguadi dodow a ɛbata agyapade pɔtee bi ho no, kokoam nsɛm ho banbɔ no da so ara yɛ nea ɛkɔ so daa na ɛde ne ho.

![Agyapadeɛ a Wɔabɔ ho Ban Nkitahodiɛ Mfonini](/content-images/image-1-512c639d56.webp)

Ɛdenam sheelded set a ɛka bom wɔ agyapade ahorow so no, Namada hwɛ hu sɛ wɔbɛdi kokoam nsɛm so pɛpɛɛpɛ, a agyapade pɔtee a ɛka ho wɔ shielded transfer mu mfa ho. Saa kwan yi hyɛ kokoam nsɛm nhyehyɛe a ɛyɛ biako ho nkuran wɔ protocol no mu na ɛma kokoamsɛm a ɛwɔ nkitahodi ahorow a ɛfa Ethereum NFTs, ATOM, NAM, ne agyapade afoforo a wɔboa ho no yɛ kɛse. Namada nso ma kokoam de kɔ baabi foforo a wɔde token a wotumi sesa ne nea ɛnyɛ nea wotumi de di dwuma denam zk-SNARKs a ɛyɛ foforo so, hwɛ hu sɛ kokoamsɛm bɛba ama token a ɛwɔ hɔ ne nea ɛnyɛ kurom hɔ sɛnea wɔyɛ wɔ Zcash so no.

## Fees a ɛba fam ne Nnwuma a Wɔyɛ Ntɛmntɛm

Namada ka nneɛma atitiriw abien bom de nkitahodi ahoɔhare ne nea etwa to ntɛmntɛm ma: awo ntoatoaso a ɛyɛ ntɛm ne nnɛyi Byzantine Fault Tolerant (BFT) adwene a ɛwɔ hɔ. Saa nneɛma mmienu yi ma Namada tumi nya nkitahodiɛ ho dwumadie dodoɔ a wɔde toto Visa, sikatua nhyehyɛeɛ a agye din a wɔgye tom sɛ ɛtumi yɛ adwuma kɛseɛ. Fast-proof awo ntoatoaso kyerɛ cryptographic adanse a wɔyɛ no yiye a ɛma nkitahodi a ɛteɛ na ɛyɛ nokware wɔ Blockchain no so. Ɛdenam akwan a ɛkɔ akyiri ne nneɛma a wɔyɛ no yiye a wɔde di dwuma so no, Namada Protocol ma kɔmputa so ka a ɛho hia na wɔde ayɛ saa adanse ahorow yi no so tew, na ɛde nkitahodi ahorow no ho adansedi ne si so dua ntɛmntɛm ba.

Bio nso, Namada de nnɛyi BFT consensus algorithms di dwuma, a ɛhwɛ ma nkitahodi ahorow a ɛwɔ ntam no nyinaa yɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ na ɛyɛ biako. Saa akwan a wɔfa so yɛ adwene yi ma Namada tumi nya adwene koro wɔ nhyehyɛe ne sɛnea nnwuma no yɛ nokware ho, na ɛma wonya awerɛhyem a emu yɛ den sɛ ebewie. Sɛ ɛba awiei a, wobu nnwuma sɛ wontumi nsakra, na ɛtew asiane a ɛwɔ hɔ sɛ wɔbɛsɛe sika mmɔho abien anaasɛ wɔbɛsan de nneɛma no akɔ akyi. Namada di ɔkwan a ɛte saa ara a wɔfa so yɛ Anoma, protocol foforo a wonim no sɛ ɛyɛ ne scalability solutions akyi. Namada fa fractal instances, a ɛma kwan ma wɔbɔ nested chains wɔ blockchain titiriw no mu. Saa fractal nhyehyeɛ yi ma horizontal scaling tumi denam adesoa no a wɔkyekyɛ wɔ nsɛm pii mu, na ɛma network no tumi ne adwumayɛ nyinaa kɔ anim.

## Namada ne Zcash Akwankyerɛ Apam

Sɛnea nnansa yi nhoma bi a wobetumi ahu kyerɛ no [Namada Protocol Blog](https://blog.namada.net/rfc-proposal-for-a-strategic-alliance-between-namada-and-zcash/), kuw a ɛwɔ Namada Protocol akyi no ani agye sɛ wɔde nsusuiɛ ne abisadeɛ a wɔde bɛka ho (RFC) a ɛfa apam a ɛfa akwankyerɛ ho a ɛbɛda Namada ne Zcash agyapadeɛ, nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn, ne mpɔtam ntam bɛma.

![Namada-Zcash Akwankyerɛ Apam Mfonini](/content-images/image-2-68804c60f3.webp)

Apam a wɔahyɛ ho nyansa no ka nneɛma atitiriw abiɛsa ho. Nea edi kan no, grant pool bi wɔ hɔ a wɔbɛhyehyɛ de sika ama nnwuma a ɛde mfasoɔ bɛba Zcash ne Namada nyinaa so. Nea ɛtɔ so mmienu, wɔde NAM tokens airdrop bɛma wɔn a wɔwɔ ZEC. Nea etwa to no, wɔayɛ nhyehyɛe sɛ wɔbɛhyehyɛ abɔntenban a ahotoso kakraa bi na ɛbɛka Zcash ne Namada abom. Sɛ wɔde di dwuma wie a, saa bridge yi bɛma ZEC wuranom a wɔfrɛ wɔn Zolders no atumi de wɔn ZEC adi dwuma wɔ Namada so. Bio nso, Zolders benya hokwan afa Namada so anya Cosmos ne Ethereum abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom a ɛtrɛ no. Wubetumi asua pii afa strategic alliance no ho wɔ [Zcash Community Forum](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/rfc-proposal-for-a-strategic-alliance-between-namada-and-zcash/44372)
