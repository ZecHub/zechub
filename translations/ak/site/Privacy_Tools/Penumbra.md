# Penumbra na ɔkyerɛwee 

Penumbra wɔ hɔ sɛ ɛbɛyɛ layer-1 ntam nkitahodi a ɛyɛ nwonwa, a wɔabɔ ho ban koraa a ɛresi akan wɔ Cosmos abɔde a nkwa wom nhyehyɛe no mu. Ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi di gua wɔ ahobammɔ mu, de token to gua na wɔsesa, na wɔyɛ gua so a wɔnda wɔn metadata adi wɔ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn so. Sɛ́ blockchain a wɔabɔ ho ban koraa no, ɛsan nso de nnwuma a wɔabɔ ho ban ma wɔ wɛb no so a ɛnsɛe kokoamsɛm anaasɛ decentralization — Penumbra wɔ kokoamsɛm default, a nkitahodi a ɛda adi pefee anaasɛ bo a ɛsom a ɛda adi nni hɔ.

## Mfiridwuma a wɔde di dwuma wɔ Penumbra IBC mu 

IBC Nkabom: Ɛba a ɛyɛ hyew wɔ nea edi kan ne Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC) protocol, mfiridwuma a ɛyɛ foforo na ɛyɛ foforo a ɛma Penumbra’s adwumayɛ yɛ mmerɛw. Ɛho hia ma nkitahodi a ɛkɔ anim wɔ nkitahodi nhyehyɛe no mu. IBC nso yɛ adwuma denam blockchain ntam nkitahodi ahorow a ɛma wotumi di nkitaho na wɔsesa data so. Nea ɛsen saa no, IBC di dwuma titiriw wɔ agyapade a wɔde kɔ baabi foforo a ɛnyɛ den, data a wɔkyɛ, ne nkitahodi a ɛda Penumbra ntam no mu. Saa nkitahodi ne mfiridwuma yi boa wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no ma wɔde token ne agyapade fa blockchains ahorow so a ɔhaw biara nni ho. Ɔkwan foforɔ so no, IBC nso boa ma scalability, ɛma nkitahodiɛ a ɛkɔ ntɛmntɛm, ɛma adwumayɛ yɛ yie, na ɛtew akyɛdeɛ so wɔ Penumbra’s network mu.

Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs): Penumbra de zero-knowledge proof cryptography mfiridwuma no di dwuma de hwɛ sɛ wɔagye nnwuma nyinaa atom na wɔagye atom a ɛnna nsɛm biara te sɛ nea ɔde kɔmaa, nea ogyee, anaa sika dodow a wɔde kɔmaa afoforo no adi.

Decentralized Exchange with Privacy: Panumbra ntam nkitahodi no mu titiriw ne kokoam nsɛm. Netwɛk no betumi de nsakraeɛ kwan a wɔahyɛ da ayɛ a ɛma aguadi ne crypto nkitahodiɛ kokoamsɛm nya nkɔsoɔ berɛ a ɛsan nso bɔ sikasɛm ho nsɛm ho ban na ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no hokwan sɛ wɔdi wɔn aguadi ho akwan a wɔbɛda no adi no so.

## Fa Nsɛm a Wɔde Di Dwuma

Aguadi a ahobammɔ wom: Penumbra ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no kwan ma wɔsɔ nkitahodi a wɔabɔ ho ban hwɛ wɔ crypto agyapade biara mu. Esiane sɛ Penumbra yɛ blockchain a wɔabɔ ho ban koraa nti, ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi di nkitaho wɔ kokoam, na ɛbɔ nea ɔde kɔma, nea ogye, sika dodow, ne agyapade ahorow ho ban bere a wɔrekɔ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn mu no; ɛhwɛ hu sɛ nnwuma no yɛ kokoam na wontumi nhwehwɛ anaasɛ wɔankyerɛkyerɛ mu a obi a ɔde di dwuma no mma ho kwan. Ɛfata sɛ yɛka sɛ Penumbra’s address biara boa viewing tumi - saa build yi ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no kwan ma wɔpaw sɛ wɔbɛda wɔn dwumadi adi akyerɛ nnipa a wɔto so abiɛsa a wɔama wɔn tumi esiane sɛ wodi mmara so anaa nneɛma afoforo nti. Nea ɛyɛ anigye no, Penumbra nkitahodi ahorow no yɛ adwuma wɔ hann ahoɔhare so a nkitahodi ho ka a ɛba fam. Esiane adanse a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔde wɔn ho ahyɛ mu no nti. 

Staking: Sɛ wohwɛ nnomaa ani a, Penumbra pɛ sɛ ɔtra aduan no atifi. Esiane sɛ wɔyɛ wiase no mu nea edi kan a wɔabɔ ho ban a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔde wɔn ho ahyɛ mu nti, wɔn atirimpɔw biako pɛ ne sɛ wɔbɛhwɛ ahu sɛ wɔn a wɔde ahyɛde ma no si kokoam nsɛm na wɔabu akontaa ama wɔn a wɔma ɛyɛ nokware no. Ade foforo a ɛyɛ anigye a Penumbra de ma ne PEN staking token, a ɛbɔ network no ho ban bere a ɛma wɔn a wɔwɔ token no hokwan sɛ wɔde wɔn ho hyɛ adwuma no nniso mu denam on-chain abatow nhyehyɛe so. Wɔma ananmusifoɔ biara a ɔwɔ network no so native token a wɔfrɛ no dPEN. Sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wubehu ara kwa no, wɔkyerɛw dPEN ahorow wɔ shielded pools no mu na wobetumi adi gua te sɛ agyapade foforo biara. Sɛ yɛhwɛ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn so nnisoɔ fã a, nkitahodiɛ no daakye da wɔn a wɔdi dwuma no nsam berɛ a wɔto aba wɔ nsusuiɛ ho na wɔde wɔn ho hyɛ gyinaesie mu berɛ a wɔhwɛ sɛ adwuma no nyɛ adwumakuo a ɛwɔ mfimfini na ɛdi so. 

Token Swap: Token Swap yɛ ade foforo a wɔde di dwuma ma Penumbra. Netwɛk no boa shielded swaps ntam crypto agyapade biara — bere a wɔde akɔma no, wɔasɔ swaps nyinaa ano! Ɛdenam ZSwap a wɔde bedi dwuma so no, wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no betumi asesa crypto agyapade abien biara a wɔrenna nsɛm a ɛho hia te sɛ aguadi abakɔsɛm anaa bere nsɔano a ebetumi aboa ma wɔako atia sikasɛm mu nsisi adi. Bere a Maximal Extractable Value (MEV) yɛ ade koro ne nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn a ɛda adi pefee no, nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn a wɔabɔ ho ban koraa te sɛ Penumbra nni MEV efisɛ ntwamutam nkitahodi no ntumi nkɔ data a ɛfa nea ɔde di dwuma no dwumadi ho.

Guadidan: Nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn a wɔabɔ ho ban koraa — Penumbra, ma gua so yɛfo tumi sɛ wɔbɛda nsɛm a ɛfa wɔn aguadi akwan ne wɔn su ho adi sen sɛ wɔbɛda wɔn dwumadi nyinaa adi akyerɛ wiase.
