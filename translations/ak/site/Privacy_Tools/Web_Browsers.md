# Wɛbsaet Browser ahorow

1. **Brave:** Browser a agye din na ahobammɔ wom sen biara wɔ gua so nnɛ no mu biako. Ɛsiw kokoam nsɛm-invasive ads & trackers. Ɛsiw nnipa foforo data a wɔkora so no kwan. 

2. **Mulvad:** Wɛb brawsa a ɛtwe adwene si kokoamsɛm so a wɔayɛ no wɔ Mullvad VPN ne Tor Project ntam. Wɔayɛ no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛtew tracking ne fingerprinting so

3. **Tor:** Saa browser yi siw plugins te sɛ Flash, RealPlayer, QuickTime, ne afoforo kwan: wobetumi ayɛ nsakrae wɔ mu ma ada wo IP address adi. Yɛnhyɛ nyansa sɛ fa add-ons anaa plugins foforo hyɛ Tor Browser mu


## Dɛn Ne Ahobammɔ Ho Nsusuwii Bere a Worepaw Wɛb Browser?

Sɛ worepaw wɛb brawsa a nneɛma kakraa bi wɔ hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ wode sie w’adwene mu: Wɔtaa hu ahobammɔ ho mmerɛwyɛ ne mfaso a wɔde di dwuma daa. Browser ahorow bi wɔ hɔ a wotumi hwehwɛ mu wɔ ɔkwan bi so, nanso, browser ahorow bi wɔ hɔ a ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi hwehwɛ nneɛma mu a wɔmmɔ wɔn din - [Tor browser](https://download.torproject.org/) Nhwɛsoɔ. 

**Nsateaa**: Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wode nneɛma sie kokoam te sɛ, software ahorow a wɔde ahyɛ wo mfiri no so, hardware nhyehyɛe, ne nea ɛkeka ho a, susuw ho sɛ wobɛhwɛ wɛb browser a ɛboa nsateaa nkyerɛwee resistance feature. 

**HTTPS Sɔhwɛ**: Ahobanbɔ ho adwene foforo a ɛsɛ sɛ wohwɛ yiye bere a worepaw wɛb brawsa ne HTTPS Sɔhwɛ protocol a ɛboa ma wɔde wɛbsaet nkitahodi sie na ɛde kuki, URL ne metadata ahorow a ɛho hia afoforo sie. HTTPS, nkitahodi no yɛ encrypted na nnipa foforo biara nni network no so ntumi nkenkan nsɛm a wɔde rekɔ server ne wo browser ntam. 

**Kuki Ahobanbɔ a Wɔdi Akyi**: Nea ɛsen saa no, wɛb nkratafa a ɛwɔ wiase nyinaa wɛb no fã kɛse bi wɔ nnipa a wɔdi akyire a wɔde asie; saa trackers yi kenkan na wɔkyerɛw cookies wɔ wo browser no mu. Saa cookies yi betumi de aduru bɔne aba efisɛ wobetumi de adi wo browsing akyi wɔ wɛbsaet ahorow so; sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wubesiw saa akyidi yi ano no, paw wɛb brawsa a esiw browsing cookies a wonnim ano.

**Navigation Test**: Bere a worekɔ wɛbsaet krataafa biako so akɔ foforo so no, wubehu link ahorow pii (ebinom yɛ link ahorow a ɛwɔ mu bere a afoforo nso rekɔ wɛbsaet foforo so). Bere a wobɔ saa link ahorow yi so no, browser API ahorow bi ma sait a edi kan no ne sait a ɛto so abien no di nkitaho. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wusiesie eyi a, paw wɛb brawsa a ɛmma wɛbsaet ahorow ntumi nkyɛ tracking data bere a woabɔ link bi so no. Sɛ worepaw saa browser a wote nka sɛ ebia ɛbɛfata w’ahiade no a, hwɛ hu sɛ ɛde kokoam nsɛm a ɛyɛ den ne nneɛma a ɛho hia te sɛ ad blockers a wɔde ahyɛ mu ne nea ɛnte saa bɛma. Bio nso, saa wɛb brawsa yi mu pii boa ntrɛwmu a wɔde ka ho na ɛtɔ da bi a saa ntrɛwmu yi betumi de ahobammɔ ho asiane aba, hwɛ hu sɛ bere nyinaa wobɛhyehyɛ saa ntrɛwmu yi afi abɔde a nkwa wom nhyehyɛe a wɔabɔ ho ban anaa mmeae a wogye di.
 
## Brave Browser Zcash Sikakorabea Nkabom

Brave browser rebue kwan ama web3 ecosystem no. Sɛn? Mprempren wobetumi de domɛn bɛboro ɔpepem 2 a wontumi nsiw ano adi dwuma de akyerɛ wɛbsaet ahorow a wɔde ahyɛ aman no nsa. Brave Browsers ahobanbɔ a ɛyɛ den nti, nkurɔfo betumi ahwehwɛ intanɛt so a wonni wɔn akyi. Brave ne Zcash reyɛ biako de kokoam nsɛm ho nnwinnade foforo aba Web3 ecosysem mu. Sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a mfaso kɛse bɛba no, Brave bɛka abom ntɛmntɛm ne Zcash protocol denam Brave's crypto sika kotoku a wɔasisi mu no so de asakra crypto nkitahodi - Saa nkabom yi bɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no atumi de ahobammɔ asie, de amena , na wɔagye ZEC ntɛm ara ne crypto sika afoforo nso. Nkitahodi a ɛda Brave wɛb brawsa ne Zcash ntam no bɛsesa agoru no ampa efisɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no benya tumi kɛse wɔ wɔn Web3 dwumadi so. 

Nokwasɛm foforo a ɛyɛ anigye wɔ Brave browser ne Zcash nkabom ho ne kokoam ne decentralized nkrasɛm afã no; Brave, ECC, ne Filecoin Foundation kuw no rebue kwan ama kokoamsɛm-gyinasoɔ afã wɔ Brave browser no mu - saa adwumayɛkuo yi botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛde kokoam nkrasɛm ne nsɛm ho amanneɛbɔfoɔ nkrasɛm bɛma denam Zcash protocol so. Ɛha yi, Interplanetary File System (IPFS) bedi dwuma titiriw bi wɔ sɛnea wɔde nneɛma a wɔde asie a ahobammɔ wom a wɔde bɛma no mu.
