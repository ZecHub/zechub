# Shade Protocol no ho nhyehyɛe

![Shade Protocol Ahyɛnsode](/content-images/Af8EzlNiwGr4FTFX7oqdBuE-71316827d0.webp)

## Dɛn ne Shade Protocol?

Shade Protocol yɛ DeFi (Decentralized Finance) abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom a ɛkora kokoamsɛm so a ɛwɔ botaeɛ sɛ ɛde sikasɛm mu nneɛma ahodoɔ bi bɛma berɛ a ɛhwɛ ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no kokoamsɛm ne ahobanbɔ. Wɔasi wɔ Secret Network so, Shade Protocol twe adwene si sikasɛm tebea a egyina kokoamsɛm so, de nnwinnade te sɛ stablecoins, synthetic agyapade, boseabɔ nhyehyɛe, ne nea ɛkeka ho ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no. Shade Protocol ade titiriw ne Silk Stablecoin, a ɛka kokoam nsɛm ne bo a ɛyɛ den bom.

## Mfiridwuma a Wɔde Di Dwuma 

**Ahintasɛm Ntrɛwmu**

Wɔakyekye Shade Protocol wɔ Secret Network, layer 1 blockchain a ɛma kokoamsɛm a ɛkora smart contracts so. Secret Network de encrypted inputs, outputs, ne state di dwuma de hwɛ hu sɛ asɛm no yɛ kokoamsɛm, na ɛbɔ nea ɔde di dwuma no data ho ban na wɔada no adi wɔ ɔmanfo ledger no so.

![Akontaabu Netwɛk Mfonini](/content-images/1-yyZ5hFOw6z8zXX_rgJn5iw-9091067aa1.webp)

Secret Network ma developers tumi kyekye decentralized applications a encrypted data, nyinaa natively wɔ Secret ne blockchains afoforo denam cross chain nkitahodi, bue tumi foforo dwumadie nsɛm ma Web3.

**Cosmos SDK ne Tendermint Core** a ɛyɛ nea ɛwɔ hɔ nnɛ.

Wɔyɛɛ Cosmos sɛ wɔde bɛka heterogeneous blockchains abom denam akwan a eye na ɛyɛ nnɛyi de a wɔbɛkyerɛkyerɛ mu a wɔbɛfa so ayɛ saa blockchains yi denam nnwinnade foforo te sɛ Tendermint ne Cosmos SDK so. 
Protocol no de Cosmos SDK, modular framework a wɔde kyekye blockchain applications a wotumi sesa na ɛyɛ adwuma bom di dwuma. Wei ma Shade Protocol nya mfasoɔ firi Cosmos abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛyɛ den na ɛyɛ adwuma bom no mu.

![Cosmos SDK ne Tendermint Core Mfonini](/content-images/83dfbb311bfd6ce22d1c9468160d8e33b4c5ce8e-ed16bb7658.webp)

Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wonya adwene a ɛwɔ mu ne ntwamutam ahobanbɔ a, Shade Protocol de Tendermint Core di dwuma, a ɛma Byzantine Fault Tolerant (BFT) adwene hyia, na ɛhwɛ ma ahobammɔ a ɛkorɔn ne nea etwa to ntɛmntɛm.

**Kokoamsɛm a Wɔkora Sikasɛm a Wɔde Ma Aban Ase**

Kokoamsɛm a wɔkora so DeFi de cryptographic akwan a ɛkɔ anim te sɛ encrypted transactions, zero nimdeɛ adanse, ne kokoam smart contracts di dwuma de hwɛ user kokoamsɛm ne data kokoamsɛm so wɔ blockchain networks so. Ɛdenam mfiridwuma te sɛ Secret Network kokoam apam ne Cosmos SDK a wɔde di dwuma de yɛ adwuma bom so no, saa ano aduru yi ma wotumi yɛ kokoam nkitahodi, kokoam agyapade ho nhyehyɛe, ne nea wɔpaw sɛ wɔda no adi, na ɛhwɛ hu sɛ sikasɛm dwumadi ahorow da so ara yɛ nea ahobammɔ wom na ɛne kokoam nsɛm ho mmara hyia. Saa kwan yi bɔ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no ho ban fi akyidi ne nsisi ho, na ɛma wonya sikasɛm mu ahofadi a wɔde ahyɛ aman nsa bere a wodi kokoam nsɛm ho gyinapɛn ahorow so no.

## Nnwuma a Wɔde Di Dwuma

**Shade ne Silk Tokens**

Shade Protocol de hwɛ safoa di dwuma ma ne token atitiriw abien: Shade ($SHD) ne Silk ($SILK). Shade ($SHD) di dwuma ahorow pii, ɛyɛ adwuma sɛ sikakorabea, nniso, ne sika a wɔkyɛ ho sɛnkyerɛnne wɔ abɔde a nkwa wom nhyehyɛe no mu. Wɔde di dwuma wɔ staking, nniso ho nyansahyɛ ahorow, sika a wɔde di dwuma ho nhyehyɛe, nkitahodi, ne bonds mu. Silk ($SILK), ɔkwan foforo so no, yɛ protocol’s privacy focused stablecoin, pegged to a diverse basket of wiase nyinaa sika ne aguade te sɛ sika kɔkɔɔ, Bitcoin, USD, Euro, ne Yen.

### Kokoamusɛm

Ade titiriw biako nti a ebia wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no bɛpɛ Shade Protocol ne kokoamsɛm a ɛde ma no. Wɔbɔ nnwuma a wɔyɛ wɔ Shade so ho ban na ɔmanfo anhu, na wɔhwɛ hu sɛ sikasɛm ho nsɛm bɛkɔ so ayɛ kokoamsɛm. Nea ɛnte sɛ ɔmanfo blockchains a ɛyɛ atetesɛm a obiara hu ayɔnkofa ho nsɛm no, Shade Protocol tokens de encrypted inputs, outputs, ne states di dwuma, a ɛkyerɛ sɛ nsɛm a ɛho hia da so ara sie fi prying aniwa. Eyi ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no kokoam nsɛm ne ahobammɔ yɛ kɛse, na ɛma ankorankoro tumi de wɔn ho hyɛ sikasɛm mu dwumadi ahorow mu a wɔmfa wɔn ho nsɛm anaa wɔn aguadi ho abakɔsɛm nhu, na ɛnam so bɔ wɔn ho ban fi asiane ahorow a ebetumi aba ho na wɔkora sikasɛm mu kokoamsɛm so.

### Botae a Ɛgyina Hɔ

Silk Stablecoin no ma ɛboɔ gyina pintinn, a ɛho hia ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔkwati nsakraeɛ a ɛtaa bata cryptocurrencies ho no. Wɔde sirikyi bata agyapade kɛntɛn bi ho, na ɛboa ma bere kɔ so no, ɛboa ma ne bo kɔ so tra hɔ. Stablecoin biara nya ne bo fi baabi pɔtee bi, mpɛn pii no wɔde bata U.S. dɔla ho. Nanso, saa kwan yi de asiane ahorow a ɛho hia a mpɛn pii no wonnim ba. Silk brɛ asiane ahorow yi ase denam sɛnea ɛkɔ so gyina pintinn wɔ wiase nyinaa nsakrae mu a wɔmfa wɔn ho nto bo biako pɛ te sɛ U.S. dɔla so no so. Mmom no, Silk bo a ɛsom no gyina index a wɔakari a wɔfrɛ no Silk Currency Basket so. Saa kɛntɛn yi ka wiase nyinaa sika a wɔapaw (USD, CAD, EUR, JPY) ne nneɛma te sɛ Bitcoin ne sika kɔkɔɔ. Ɛdenam sika ne agyapade pii a wɔde ka ho so no, Silk nya peg stability kɛse sɛ wɔde toto sika ankorankoro ho a. Nhwehwɛmu ne sɔhwɛ a ɛkɔ akyiri ada no adi sɛ sika kɔkɔɔ ne Bitcoin, sɛ adekoradan a wotumi de ho to so a ɛsom bo, yɛ adwuma sen sika a wɔde di dwuma wɔ amanne kwan so no, na ɛma ɛfata sɛ wɔde ka kɛntɛn no ho.

### Decentralization a wɔde bɛma afoforo

Sɛ́ DeFi platform no, Shade Protocol yɛ adwuma a enni mfinimfini tumidi, na ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no nya sikasɛm mu ahofadi. Shade Protocol nnisoɔ a wɔde ahyɛ aman nsa no ma ne mpɔtam hɔfoɔ a wɔwɔ token no kwan ma wɔde wɔn ho hyɛ gyinaesie nhyehyɛeɛ no mu tẽẽ. Wɔn a wɔwɔ token no betumi ahyɛ nsakrae ho nyansa, ato aba wɔ nhyehyɛe ahorow ho, na wɔaboa ma wɔayɛ nhyehyɛe no ne akwankyerɛ. Eyi hwɛ hu sɛ wɔbɛkyekyɛ tumidi mu wɔ nnipa ahorow a wɔde wɔn ho hyɛ mu no mu sen sɛ ɛbɛyɛ nea tumidi titiriw bi na ɛkura mu. Ɛdenam blockchain mfiridwuma a wɔde di dwuma so no, nniso nhyehyɛe no yɛ nea ɛda adi pefee, ahobammɔ wom, na ɛnsakra, na ɛma abɔde a nkwa wom nhyehyɛe a ɛka nnipa nyinaa bom na ɛtumi gyina ano kɛse.

### Nkitahodi a wɔde di dwuma

Sɛ́ ɛyɛ Cosmos abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no fã no kyerɛ sɛ ɛnyɛ den sɛ Shade Protocol bɛtumi ne blockchains foforɔ adi nkitaho, ama sika a wɔde di dwuma no ayɛ kɛseɛ na ama wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no anya hokwan pii sɛ wɔde wɔn agyapadeɛ bɛdi dwuma wɔ nkitahodiɛ ahodoɔ so. Ɛdenam kokoam nsɛm a wɔkora so mfiridwuma a efi Secret Network so a wɔde di dwuma so no, Shade Protocol hwɛ hu sɛ saa cross chain dwumadi ahorow yi kura kokoamsɛm ne nokwaredi mu. Saa adwumayɛkuo yi nnyɛ sɛ ɛma dwumadie a wɔde ahyɛ aman no nsa no tumi yɛ nsakraeɛ ne dwumadie no kɔ anim nko na mmom ɛma blockchain abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom a ɛwɔ nkitahodie na ɛyɛ den kɛseɛ nso nya nkɔsoɔ, ɛtew ahotosoɔ a wɔde to nkitahodiɛ a atew ne ho so na ɛma sikasɛm ano aduru a wɔde ahyɛ aman nsa no gye tom wɔ ɔkwan a ɛtrɛ so.

## Awie

Shade Protocol yɛ akwampae adwuma wɔ DeFi ahunmu, a ɛde sikasɛm ano aduru a ɛtwe adwene si kokoamsɛm so a wɔasi wɔ mfiridwuma a ɛyɛ den so ma. Ɛdenam stablecoin a ɛde kokoam nsɛm a ɛwɔ mu ne sikasɛm nnwinnade a ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ a wɔde bɛma so no, Shade Protocol botae ne sɛ ɛbɛhwɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma a wobu ahobammɔ, ahotɔ, ne decentralization a ɛsom bo no ho dwuma.

Shade Protocol nnyinasosɛm a ɛfa nkabom ne boɔ a wɔkyere ho no ma fapem a ɛyɛ den ma gyinabea ne osuahu a ɛnyɛ den a ɔde di dwuma, de si hɔ sɛ daakye kannifo wɔ DeFi protocols mu. Ɛnam nnisoɔ a ɛtumi sesa, scalable ne ɔkwan a wɔakyerɛkyerɛ mu yie a wɔde bɛba Shade Protocol primitives foforɔ nti, akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ tumi a ɛbɛtumi ama nkɔsoɔ ntɛmntɛm no yɛ kɛseɛ.
