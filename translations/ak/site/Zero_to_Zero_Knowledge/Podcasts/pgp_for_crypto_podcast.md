# PGP* a wɔde yɛ Crypto Podcast
![bannerpgp a wɔde yɛ adwuma](/content-images/221758326-06cea3f5-4c9e-4466-b9ee-738546-01f88aec39.webp)

Yɛma wo akwaaba ba “PGP * (Pretty Good Policy) ma Crypto Podcast," Electric Coin Co. nhyehyɛe bi!

Yɛhwehwɛ blockchain mfiridwuma, ɔmanfoɔ nhyehyɛeɛ, ne wiase nyinaa mmara nsɛmti nhyiamu. Yɛn botaeɛ ne sɛ yɛbɛma yɛn atiefoɔ anya nhumu a emu dɔ ne animdefoɔ adwene wɔ nsɛm titire a ɛreyɛ blockchain ne cryptocurrency daakye ho.

Wobɛte nsɛmbisa a wɔne nnwuma akannifoɔ, aban mpaninfoɔ, ne adesuafoɔ a wɔdi anim wɔ ɔmanfoɔ nhyehyɛeɛ tebea a wɔreyɛ ama blockchain mfiridwuma ho. Yɛn ahɔhoɔ bɛkyɛ wɔn adwene soronko wɔ nsɛnnennen ne hokwan a nnwuma no hyia ne nkɛntɛnsoɔ a mprempren ne mmara ne nhyehyɛeɛ akwan a wɔahyɛ ho nyansa no anya wɔ blockchain abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no nkɔsoɔ ne nkɔsoɔ so.
