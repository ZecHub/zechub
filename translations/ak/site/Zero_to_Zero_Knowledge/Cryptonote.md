# Nimdeɛ a ɛwɔ Zero kosi Zero: CryptoNote Protocol

**Series:** Zero kosi Zero Nimdeɛ

Nea ɛyɛ anigye nnɛ! 
**CryptoNote** protocol no ma on-chain kokoamsɛm a emu yɛ den tumi yɛ adwuma. Ɛnnɛ yesua ne nneɛma atitiriw nyinaa ne sɛnea wɔde kokoam nsɛm ho adwuma atitiriw pii de adi dwuma.

![CryptoNote nnianim asɛm](/content-images/FrXr5P8WIAAvx36-88db0c8250.webp)

---

## Akyi asɛm

Wɔtintim CryptoNote krataa fitaa a edi kan no wɔ din hunu **"Nicolas van Saberhagen"** ase. 

**Bytecoin** ne cryptocurrency a edi kan a ɛde protocol no dii dwuma. Adwuma a wonim no yiye a wɔde di dwuma nnɛ ne **Monero (XMR)**. Wɔde adi dwuma nso wɔ TurtleCoin, Aeon, ne afoforo pii mu.

---

## Nneɛma atitiriw a ɛwɔ CryptoNote mu

CryptoNote Protocol no de nneɛma atitiriw abiɛsa ma:

1. **Untraceability ne Unlinkability** a ɛfa nnwuma ho
2. **Adwuma ho Adanse a Ɛyɛ Pɛ** (ASIC resistant) 
3. **Dynamic emission a ɛyɛ nnam**

---

## 1. Untraceability - Ring Nsɛnkyerɛnne

Untraceability no, wɔde **Ring Signatures** na ɛyɛ titiriw.

Sɛ wode asɛm bi remena a, wo baguam safoa ankasa no frafra decoy safoa pii ("ring") - ne nyinaa kura sika dodow koro. Eyi ma ɛyɛ den yiye sɛ wobehu onii a ɔde sika no kɔe ankasa.

**Ring size** nya anonymity set no so nkɛntɛnso kɛse. Nkawa akɛse ma obi nya kokoam nsɛm a eye.

![Ring Signatures nkyerɛkyerɛmu](/content-images/FrXteGHXgAANE0F-f11593a0d9.webp)

**Nneɛma a wɔde toto Zcash ho**: 
Zcash’s anonymity set yɛ nkitahodi dodow a *pɛn* a wɔayɛ wɔ shielded pool a wɔde ama mu (ɛsõ sen CryptoNote ring akɛse a wɔtaa yɛ no nyinaa).

---

## Ring CT (Akontaabu Nkitahodi) .

**Ring CT** nhwɛsoɔ no maa kokoamsɛm nyaa nkɔsoɔ kɛseɛ wɔ CryptoNote-based coins mu.

Sɛ anka ɛde nea ɔde kɔmaa no nkutoo besie no, Ring CT nso **ɛma asɛm no sika a ɛwɔ hɔ no yɛ basaa** wɔ nea ɔde kɔmaa ne nea ogye no ntam.

![Ring CT mfonini a wɔde ayɛ](/content-images/FrXuivgWYAAze7B-260071c1b3.webp)

Ɛde:
- Elliptic Curve Nsɛm a Wɔde Kyerɛw Nsɛm
- Pedersen Bɔhyɛ ahorow
- Homomorphic Nsɛm a Wɔde Siesie Nsɛm

**Wɔde adanseɛ** di dwuma de kyerɛ sɛ dodoɔ no boro 0 na ɛwɔ ntam a ɛfata mu **a wɔnda botaeɛ ankasa adi**.

**Stealth Addresses** nso de address a wɔde di dwuma pɛnkoro ka ho ma nea ogye no.

![Sawiafo Address + Adanse](/content-images/FrXut5aWAAMhuRb-f3ce8ea3fd.webp)

---

## 2. Adwumayɛ ho Adanse a Ɛma Nnipa Yɛ Pɛ (ePoW) .

CryptoNote botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛbɔ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛfata a wɔde tu fagudeɛ denam ASIC ahodoɔ a ɛbɛko atia no so.

Ɛde **CryptoNight** algorithm (adwuma a ɛyɛ den sɛ wobɛkae) na edi dwuma. Nea ɛnte sɛ Bitcoin’s SHA256 no, wɔayɛ CryptoNight sɛ ɛbɛto nsonsonoe a ɛda CPU, GPU, ne ASIC miners ntam no mu.

**CryptoNight anammɔn:**
1. Fa pseudorandom data hyɛ aseɛ wɔ memory (scratchpad) beaeɛ kɛseɛ bi
2. Yɛ akenkan/kyerɛw adwuma pii wɔ scratchpad no so
3. Hash scratchpad no nyinaa na ama woanya bo a etwa to

![CryptoAnadwo a wotu fagude](/content-images/FrXvNs3XsAA37LG-5779657f5c.webp)

(Hyɛ no nsow: Efi saa bere no, Monero atwe ne ho afi CryptoNight so akɔ algorithms afoforo so.)

---

## 3. Nneɛma a Ɛma Nneɛma a Ɛyɛ Nnam

Sɛ anka ɛbɛtew nsɛm a esisi so mpofirim (te sɛ Bitcoin) no, CryptoNote de **block akatua a ɛso tew yiye** di dwuma.

Eyi ma emission curve a ɛyɛ mmerɛw kɛse bere kɔ so no.

![Dynamic emission curve a ɛyɛ nnam](/content-images/FrXv8wpXoAEjUxW-e2bbaebced.webp)

**Zcash Nkitahodi**: 
Zcash developers asusuw ho sɛ wɔde emission curve a ɛyɛ mmerɛw bedi dwuma daakye, a ebetumi aba sɛ ɛnam "Zcash Posterity Fund" so.

---

## Awie

CryptoNote ada ne ho adi sɛ ɛyɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ den na wɔasɔ ahwɛ wɔ ɔko mu a ɛfa on-chain kokoam nsɛm ho. Ne nneɛma foforo pii anya kokoam sika a wɔde di dwuma wɔ ɔkwan a ɛtrɛw so no so nkɛntɛnso.

Nhwehwɛmufo binom gye di sɛ awiei koraa no wobetumi de CryptoNote nneɛma no aka atare a wɔde nimdeɛ a wonni ho ban a wontumi mfa wɔn ho to so no ho.
