# Memos a wɔde kyerɛw nsɛm

#### Encrypted Memos a Wɔde Kɔma

Sɛ wode Z2Z (shielded-to-shielded) asɛm remena a, wobɛtumi de memo (nkrasɛm) ahyɛ asɛm no mu. Wobetumi de saa memo yi adi dwuma ama nneɛma ahorow bi.

#### Nkitahodi a Wɔde Wɔn Nsa Hyɛ

Wɔde memos di dwuma titiriw de tua nnwom a wotua. Esiane sɛ shielded transactions de encrypt wo data nti, wuntumi nhu onii a ɔde ZEC kɔmaa wo, ne nea ebia na ZEC no yɛ. Wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no betumi de memo field no de wɔn nsa ahyɛ wɔn din anaa wɔn din a wɔde frɛ wɔn ase de ama wɔn yɔnko no ahu onii a na wofi mu. Wobetumi nso akyerɛkyerɛ nea na wɔde di gua no mu.

#### Nkrasɛm a Wɔde Kɔma

Ade foforo a wɔde di dwuma ma encrypted memo no ne sɛ wode nkra bɛmena obi a ɔwɔ z-addr. Saa nkrasɛm yi betumi afa biribiara ho, sɛ́ ɛyɛ [nkaebɔ ma adamfo bi](https://twitter.com/iansagstette/status/1542142468505870336), anaa [nkrasɛm a ɛyɛ nkate a ɛsɛ sɛ ɛkɔ so yɛ kokoam sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ yiye biara](https://twitter.com/InsideZcash/status/1545800146352578560).

#### Ɔdɔ Nsɛm a Wɔakyerɛw wɔ Blockchain no so

Na obi wɔ hɔ a ɔde ɔdɔ krataa kɔmaa wɔn hokafo wɔ block a edi kan wɔ Zcash blockchain no mu biako mu. Obi hui sɛ wɔn hokafo no nam Zcash memo so de fael bi amena wɔn. Na saa fael yi yɛ tekiti a wɔde kɔ adeyɛ titiriw bi, amannɔne, a ɔne ne dɔfo a ɔwɔ akyirikyiri no aka ho asɛm sɛ wɔbɛbom akɔ. Ná memo no yɛ ɔdɔ ho krataa.
