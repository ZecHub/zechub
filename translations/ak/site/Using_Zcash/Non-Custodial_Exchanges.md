# <img src="/content-images/ZEC-USD-a2189a84b9.webp" alt="Alt Text" width="50"/>   Non-Custodial Exchanges

[Zcash Nsesaeɛ a Ɛnyɛ Custodial](/site/Using_Zcash/DEX_List)

Wɔ wiase a ɛrenya nkɔso bere nyinaa a ɛfa cryptocurrency aguadi ho no, nsakrae a ɛnyɛ nea wɔde asie a wɔsan frɛ no Decentralized Exchanges anaa DEXs a ɛrekɔ soro no resan akyerɛkyerɛ sɛnea wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no ne dijitaal agyapade di nkitaho. Saa platform ahorow yi de ɔkwan foforo a wɔfa so di gua ma denam hia a ɛho nhia sɛ wɔyɛ ntamgyinafo anaa nnipa foforo a woyi fi hɔ na wɔsan de tumidi ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no so.

Wɔabobɔ din wɔ atifi hɔ ne nsakraeɛ a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea a ɛma wotumi nya Zcash na wodi gua a wommɔ mmɔden biara, a enhia sɛ wonya ntamgyinafoɔ wɔ asɛm no mu.

### **Nteaseɛ a ɛfa Nneɛma a Ɛnyɛ Custodial Exchanges ho**

Nsesaeɛ a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea, a wɔsan frɛ no Decentralized Exchanges (DEXs) yɛ nhyiamu a ɛma cryptocurrency aguadi yɛ mmerɛ a ɛnhia sɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no de wɔn sika gu exchange no ankasa mu. Mmom no, wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no kura wɔn kokoam safe so tumi na wodi gua tẽẽ fi wɔn sika kotoku mu a enhia sɛ wɔyɛ nnipa foforo.

Saa kwan yi ma ahobanbɔ ne kokoamsɛm kɔ anim, ɛfiri sɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no mfa wɔn ho nto exchange no so sɛ wɔbɛkura wɔn agyapadeɛ a ɛno nso brɛ asiane a ɛwɔ hacks anaa dwumadie a ɛntene mu no ase. Nkitahodi a wɔyɛ wɔ aguadidan ahorow a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea ahorow so no taa de apam ahorow a nyansa wom di dwuma de hwɛ hu sɛ wɔbɛyɛ aguadi a wontumi mfa wɔn ho nto so na ɛda adi pefee.

Mfaso titiriw a ɛwɔ cryptocurrency exchanges a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea so ne tumi a ɛkɔ soro a wɔde ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma wɔ wɔn agyapade so no. Esiane sɛ saa nneɛma a wɔde sesa nneɛma yi nkora agyapade no so nti, wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no nya wɔn digyital sika no wurayɛ ne tumidi koraa.

### **Nne Sikakorabea Nsesa Vs Ahwɛfo Nsesa**

**#1 Ahobanbɔ**: Nsesa a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea no yi hia a ɛho hia sɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no de wɔn sika anaa wɔn agyapade di adwumakuw titiriw bi mu ahotoso no fi hɔ. Wei ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi hwɛ wɔn kokoam safe so na wonya so tumi, na ɛtew asiane a ɛwɔ hacks, insider attacks ne platform mmerɛwyɛ ahorow a ebia custodial exchanges betumi anya no so.

**#2 Kokoamsɛm**: Nneɛma a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea a wɔde sesa nneɛma taa ma kokoamsɛm kɛse denam ma a ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi di gua tẽẽ fi wɔn sika kotoku mu a enhia sɛ wɔyɛ ntamgyinafo biara. Wobetumi ayɛ nnwuma a wɔmmɔ wɔn din kɛse, efisɛ wɔmfa nsɛm a ɛho hia nsie a ɛnte sɛ Centralized Exchanges

**#3 Decentralization**: Nsesa a ɛnyɛ custodial ne decentralized ethos a ɛwɔ cryptocurrencies mu no hyia kɛse. Wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no wɔ ahofadi kɛse ne tumi kɛse wɔ wɔn aguadi dwumadi ahorow so, a ɛne nnyinasosɛm ahorow a ɛtrɛw a ɛfa blockchain mfiridwuma ho no hyia.

Sɛ ɛba Custodial Exchanges so a, Decentralization level no taa yɛ ketewa koraa wɔ centralized exchanges dodow no ara a ɛma exchange kuw anaa mpanyimfo a wɔhwɛ user data anaa nsɛm a ɛwɔ exchange no so no ba.

**#4 Adaptation to Changing Regulations**: Nsesa a ɛnyɛ afiase de no taa yɛ nsakraeɛ kɛseɛ wɔ mmara tebea a ɛresakra mu. Esiane sɛ wonni sika a wɔde di dwuma no nti, ebia na wonni nsɛnnennen kakraa bi wɔ mmara sodi ho bere a wɔde toto sika a wɔde sesa wɔn a wɔhwɛ wɔn so ho no.

**#5 Nneɛma foforo ne Sɔhwɛ**: Nsesa a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea taa ma nneɛma foforo ba wɔ crypto ahunmu. Wɔhyɛ mfiridwuma a wɔde ahyɛ aman nsa no nkɔsoɔ ho nkuran, te sɛ automated market makers (AMMs) ne decentralized finance (DeFi) applications.

**#6 Wiase Nyinaa Nkɔsoɔ**: Nneɛma a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea a wɔde sesa nneɛma taa ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma wɔ wiase nyinaa nya kwan kɔ cryptocurrencies so, a mpɔtam a mmara mu akwansideɛ bɛtumi asiw sika a wɔde sesa nneɛma a wɔde asie no ano hyeɛ ka ho.

**#7 KYC Ahwehwɛdeɛ biara nni hɔ**: Nsesaeɛ pii a ɛnyɛ ahwɛfoɔ nhwehwɛ sɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no fa nim-wo-adetɔfoɔ (KYC) akwan a ɛtrɛ mu, a ɛde kokoamsɛm ne nkabom a ɛwɔ hɔ a ɛnni sikakorabea nhyiamu bi mu ma.

Afei, momma yɛnhwehwɛ nsakraeɛ a ɛnyɛ custodial exchanges a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a ɛma Zcash aguadi yɛ mmerɛw no mu kakraa bi. Saa platforms yi a wode bedi dwuma no bɛma woanya ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a wobɛfa so anya Zcash sika pii.

### **Tɔfabɔ**

Nsesaeɛ a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea, anaa DEXs, yɛ decentralized platforms a ɛma wotumi di cryptocurrency aguadi tẽẽ fi wɔn a wɔde di dwuma sika kotoku mu. Wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no kura wɔn kokoam safe so tumi, na ɛma ahobammɔ ne kokoamsɛm yɛ kɛse.

Bere a nsakrae a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea de mfaso horow a ɛyɛ den ma no, ɛho hia sɛ yegye tom sɛ ebia ɛde sintɔ ahorow bɛba, te sɛ sikasɛm mu nsɛm a ebetumi aba ne adesua a ɛkɔ akyiri ama wɔn a wonni osuahu pii.

Sɛnea ɛte wɔ sikasɛm ho gyinaesi biara ho no, ɛsɛ sɛ aguadifo susuw nneɛma a wɔde di kan, asiane a wotumi gyina ano, ne mfiridwuma ho nimdeɛ a wonim no yiye ansa na wɔapaw akwan horow a ɛnyɛ nea wɔde bɛsesa nneɛma ne nea wɔde bɛsesa.
