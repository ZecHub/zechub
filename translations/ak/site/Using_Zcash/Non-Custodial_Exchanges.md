### *Ntease a enni asie ho nsesae*

Non-custodial exchanges, also known as Decentralized Exchanges (DEXs) are platforms that facilitate cryptocurrency trading without requiring users to deposit their funds into the exchange itself. Instead, users retain control of their private keys and trade directly from their wallets without the need for third parties.

Saa kwan yi ma ahobanbɔ ne kokoam nsɛm mu trɛ, efisԑ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no nnyina asesae so mfa wɔn agyapade nsie na eyi nso tumi sɛee asiane biara wɔ hackers anaa ammanehunu ho. Asese nnwuma ahorow a ɛnnyɛ nnwumakuo de gyinabea sԑnea wobedi dwa taa fa smart contracts so nya ahotosoɔ ɛne anibuei wɔ adwuma mu.

Nea ɛma cryptocurrency exchange a wonnya nkura no yɛ papa ne sɛ, wotumi di wɔn ahode so yiye. Esiane sɛ saa nneɛma yi ntumi mfa sika nsie nti, nnipa nya tumi wɔ ho ma wɔde wɔn de hyɛ wɔn din mu koraa.

### * Non-Custodial Exchanges Vs Custodial Exchange* - Nkrataa ahodoɔ a etwa sɛ yɛhyehyɛ wɔ kasa foforo mu

Security: Non-custodial exchanges ma no kwan sɛ wɔn a wɔdi dwuma de ne sika anaa agyapadeɛ bɛhyɛ aban bi nsa. Eyi bɛma wɔn tumi akura wɔn private keys so na ama wɔatumi adi ho dwuma, eyi nti asiane biara nni hɔ sɛ wɔbɛbɔ hacks, insider attacks ɛne platform vulnerabilities ato gua ahorow a wɔde nneɛma hyɛ ban mu.

Ɛtumi yɛ adwuma bere biara sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a obiara renhu nea ɛyɛe ntia wo din efisɛ wɔmfa nsɛm ho nimdeɛ nto hɔ te sε Central Exchange (Ɛnkorabea) nkoara

Sɛ ɛfa custodial exchanges ho a, Decentralization level no taa yɛ ketewa wɔ central exchange mu ma enti na Exchange kuo anaa adwumayɛfo di dwuma de hwɛ user data or information so.

### ** Nkrataa a ɛka ho no**

Non-custodial exchanges, anaa DEXs yɛ decentralized platform a ɛma kwan ma cryptocurrency trading firi user wallets. Users kura wɔn private keys so tumi mu, na ɛboa no security ne privacy.

While non-custodial exchanges offer compelling advantages, it is important to acknowledge that they might come with drawbacks, such as potential liquidity issues and a steeper learning curve for less experienced users.

Sɛdeɛ ɛte wɔ sikasɛm ho gyinaesi biara mu no, ɛsɛ sɛ aguadifoɔ dwene wɔn ahwehwɛde ahorow a ɛhia paa ne asiane ano kwan so di dwuma na wonya nimdeɛ fa mfiri yi ho ansa na wɔatumi apaw nsesaeɛ akwan bi a ɛnyɛ nea wɔde sie anaa deɛ wɔde sie.
