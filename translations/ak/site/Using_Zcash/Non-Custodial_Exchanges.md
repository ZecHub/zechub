# <img src="/content-images/ZEC-USD-a2189a84b9.webp" alt="Alt Text" width="50"/>   Non-Custodial Exchanges

[Zcash Nneɛma a Ɛnyɛ Custodial Exchanges](/dex)

Wɔ wiase a ɛrenya nkɔso bere nyinaa a ɛfa cryptocurrency aguadi ho no, nsakrae a ɛnyɛ nea wɔde asie, a wɔsan frɛ no Decentralized Exchanges anaa DEXs, ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no di gua a wɔmfa wɔn sika nkɔma nsakrae akontaabu. Wode w’ankasa wo nsafe sie, nanso ɛno nkyerɛ sɛ obi foforo biara mfa ne ho nhyɛ mu. Ɛgyina ɔkwan no so no, swap betumi afa wɛbsaet anaa wallet app, routing service, smart contracts, solvers ne bridges so.

Exchanges a wɔabobɔ din wɔ atifi hɔ no ma wunya na wodi Zcash fi w’ankasa sika kotoku mu. Sɛnea swap yɛ kokoam no gyina ɔsom no so, ntwamutam a wutua fi mu, ne sɛ ebia wo ZEC bɛkɔ akowie address a wɔabɔ ho ban mu anaa. Afã horow a ɛwɔ ase hɔ no kyerɛkyerɛ nsonsonoe a ɛwɔ mu no mu.

### **Nteaseɛ a ɛfa Nneɛma a Ɛnyɛ Custodial Exchanges ho**

Nsesaeɛ a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea, a wɔsan frɛ no Decentralized Exchanges (DEXs) yɛ nhyiamu a ɛma cryptocurrency aguadi yɛ mmerɛ a ɛnhia sɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no de wɔn sika gu exchange no ankasa mu. Mmom no, wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no di wɔn kokoam safe so na wofi wɔn ankasa sika kotoku mu di gua. Cross-chain swaps da so ara de wɔn ho to afã afoforo so sɛ wɔbɛfa asɛm, afa kwan so na wɔasiesie aguadi no (hwɛ ase hɔ).

Eyi betumi ama ahobammɔ atu mpɔn, efisɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no mfa wɔn ho nto exchange no so mfa nkura wɔn agyapade, na ɛtew asiane a ɛwɔ hɔ sɛ wɔbɛtow ahyɛ so anaasɛ wɔbɛhwɛ so yiye no so. Ɛmma swap nyɛ kokoam wɔ n’ankasa so. Nkitahodi a wɔyɛ wɔ exchanges a ɛnyɛ custodial exchanges so no taa de smart contracts di dwuma, a ɛyɛ ɔmanfo de, na ɔsom a wode di dwuma no da so ara tumi hu w’address ne connection ho nsɛm.

Mfaso titiriw a ɛwɔ cryptocurrency exchanges a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea so ne tumi a ɛkɔ soro a wɔde ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma wɔ wɔn agyapade so no. Esiane sɛ saa nneɛma a wɔde sesa nneɛma yi nkora agyapade no so nti, wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no nya wɔn digyital sika no wurayɛ ne tumidi koraa.

### **Nne Sikakorabea Nsesa Vs Ahwɛfo Nsesa**

**#1 Ahobanbɔ**: Nsesaeɛ a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea yi hia a ɛhia sɛ wɔde sika sie wɔ sikakorabea a ɛwɔ mfimfini mu. Wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no di wɔn kokoam safe so, na ɛtew asiane a ɛwɔ hacks, insider ntua ne platform huammɔdi a ebia custodial exchanges behyia no so. Cross-chain swaps da so ara tumi kura sika bere tiaa bi wɔ deposit address anaa bridge bere a aguadi no resiesie no.

**#2 Privacy**: Nneɛma a ɛnyɛ custodial swaps taa nhia exchange account, enti wotaa huruw sɛ wode email anaa ID bɛkyerɛw wo din. Ɛno ne din a wɔmmɔ no nyɛ ade koro. Sika a wode bɛmena wɔ source network (sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no Solana anaa Ethereum) no yɛ ɔmanfoɔ wɔ saa nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no so, na ɔsom no da so ara tumi hunu wo sika kotokuo address, IP address ne swap ho nsɛm. Kokoamsɛm a ɛwɔ Zcash afã no gyina baabi a wo ZEC si fam (hwɛ ase hɔ).

**#3 Decentralization**: Nsesa a ɛnyɛ custodial no ne decentralized ethos a ɛwɔ cryptocurrencies mu no hyia kɛse. Wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no wɔ ahofadi kɛse ne wɔn aguadi dwumadi ahorow so tumi, a ɛne nnyinasosɛm ahorow a ɛtrɛw a ɛfa blockchain mfiridwuma ho no hyia.

Sɛ ɛba Custodial Exchanges so a, Decentralization level no taa yɛ ketewa koraa wɔ centralized exchanges dodow no ara a ɛma exchange kuw anaa mpanyimfo a wɔhwɛ user data anaa nsɛm a ɛwɔ exchange no so no ba.

**#4 Adaptation to Changing Regulations**: Nsesa a ɛnyɛ afiase de no taa yɛ nsakraeɛ kɛseɛ wɔ mmara tebea a ɛresakra mu. Esiane sɛ wonni sika a wɔde di dwuma no nti, ebia na wonni nsɛnnennen kakraa bi wɔ mmara sodi ho bere a wɔde toto sika a wɔde sesa wɔn a wɔhwɛ wɔn so ho no.

**#5 Nneɛma foforo ne Sɔhwɛ**: Nsesa a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea taa ma nneɛma foforo ba wɔ crypto ahunmu. Wɔhyɛ mfiridwuma a wɔde ahyɛ aman nsa no nkɔsoɔ ho nkuran, te sɛ automated market makers (AMMs) ne decentralized finance (DeFi) applications.

**#6 Wiase Nyinaa Nkɔsoɔ**: Nneɛma a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea a wɔde sesa nneɛma taa ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma wɔ wiase nyinaa nya kwan kɔ cryptocurrencies so, a mpɔtam a mmara mu akwansideɛ bɛtumi asiw sika a wɔde sesa nneɛma a wɔde asie no ano hyeɛ ka ho.

**#7 No KYC Requirements**: Nnipa pii a wɔnyɛ afiase a wɔde sesa wɔn ho wɔn ho no nbisa nkrataa a ɛkyerɛ sɛ obi yɛ onipa no ansa. Dodow no ara da so ara hwehwɛ sika kotoku address ahorow mu wɔ mmara sodi database ahorow ho, na sɛ wɔde frankaa ahyɛ biribi so a, wobetumi akyɛ, asiw ano anaasɛ wɔapow swap. Hwɛ ɔsom no mu nsɛm ansa na wode wo ho ato so.

### **Nea Zcash Bɔ Ho Ban ne Nea Ɛnyɛ Ho Ban**

Zcash kokoamsɛm fi address ahorow a wɔabɔ ho ban mu. Sɛ ZEC kɔ address ahorow a wɔabɔ ho ban ntam a, wɔde nea ɔde kɔma, nea ogye, sika ne memo no ahyɛ Zcash nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no so. Hwɛ [Atare a Wɔabɔ Ho Ban](/using-zcash/shielded-pools) sɛnea eyi yɛ adwuma no ho.

Swap wɔ afã horow a Zcash ntumi nsie:

- **The source network.** Sika a wode firi Solana, Ethereum anaa ɔmanfoɔ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn foforɔ so mena no, wɔda adi wɔ saa nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no so, a wo address ne sika dodoɔ ka ho.
- **Address a ɛgye.** Swap akwan bi de ZEC kɔ address a ɛda adi pefee so. Sɛ nhwɛso no, Near Intents kyerɛw ZEC sɛnea wɔboa [address ahorow a ɛda adi pefee nkutoo](https://docs.near-intents.org/resources/chain-support). ZEC a wɔde kɔ address a ɛda adi pefee (t1 anaa t3) so no yɛ ɔmanfo de, te sɛ Bitcoin ara pɛ. Sɛ wobɔ ho ban wɔ ɛno akyi a, ɛbɔ nea wobɛyɛ akyi no ho ban, nanso transfer a ɛba ne shielding asɛm no kɔ so da adi.
- **Ɔsom no.** App no ne routing service biara hunu address ne sika dodoɔ a wode ma wɔn, ne connection data te sɛ wo IP address.

Fa ZEC kɔ sika kotoku a wohwɛ so na bɔ ho ban ansa na woasɛe sika. [ZEC a Wɔde Di Dwuma wɔ Kokoam](/guides/using-zec-privately) ka anammɔn a edi hɔ no ho asɛm.

### **Hena na Ɔde Ne Ho Hyehyɛ Swap Mu**

Fa swap a wɔde fa Near Intents 1Click dwumadie no so sɛ nhwɛsoɔ. Ɛyɛ [API nsɛmfua](https://docs.near-intents.org/security-compliance/terms-of-service) fa eyinom di dwuma sɛ afã horow a ɛsono emu biara:

- **Interface**: wɛbsaet anaa sika kotoku a wode di dwuma. Intents Technology anaasɛ obi foforo a ɔwɔ n’ankasa nsɛm betumi adi so.
- **1Klik**: ɔkwan a wɔfa so ne nneɛma a wɔde siesie adwuma a Intents Technology Limited na ɛhwɛ so. Wode sika kɔ address a wɔde sie a wɔayɛ ama wo quote no so. Docs no se 1Click nnye custody, nanso nsɛm no hyɛ no nsow sɛ wobetumi de agyapade asie anaasɛ wɔatoto mu wɔ bridge infrastructure mu bere a wɔreyɛ transfer.
- **Protocol no**: Near Intents nyansa apam ahorow no.
- **Solvers**: nnipa a wɔto so abiɛsa a wɔde wɔn ho a wɔhyɛ quote no ma.
- **Bridges**: native ZEC tu fa PoA Bridge, a Intents Technology na ɛyɛ adwuma.

Near Intents nso [screens a wɔaka abom quote flows](https://docs.near-intents.org/security-compliance/risk-and-compliance) atia AML database ahorow pii, na ɔka sɛ coverage gu ahorow sɛnea flow ne integration te. Wɔ ne nsɛm ase no, wobetumi atwentwɛn flagged swap ase, asiw ano, ahyɛ no nwini anaasɛ wɔapow.

### **Nea Wokyɛ Wɔ Swap Bere Mu**

- ZEC address a ɛgye swap no, ne refund address a ɛwɔ source network no so.
- Agyapadeɛ ne sika dodoɔ, ne sika a wode asie a wode bɛmena, a ɛyɛ baguam wɔ source chain no so.
- Nkitahodi data. 1Click nsɛm no ka sɛ Intents Technology betumi aboaboa abisade metadata, IP address ne sika kotoku address ano, na kokoam nsɛm ho nhyehyɛe a ɛwɔ near.com no kyerɛ IP address, beae, browser ne mfiri ho nsɛm.
- Biribiara a app no de ka ho wɔ soro, te sɛ wallet address afoforo a ɛka ho. Apps nso betumi afa wɔn ankasa mmara sodi nhwehwɛmu so ayɛ wo sika kotoku no.

### **Baabi a wobɛhwɛ Mmara ne Mmoa**

Nsɛmfua sesa, enti kenkan mprempren nkyerɛase ahorow no ansa na woasesa kɛse.

- **Fi ase fa app a wode di dwuma no so.** Ɛyɛ wo beae titiriw a wo ne no di nkitaho. 1Click API nsɛmfua no ka sɛ Intents Technology nni abusuabɔ biara tẽẽ ne wɔn a wɔde app ahorow a wɔasi wɔ so di dwuma no ntam.
- **Near Intents:** nsɛm ne kokoam nsɛm ho nhyehyɛe a ɛwɔ near.com/terms ne near.com/privacy, ne.. [1Klik API nsɛmfua so](https://docs.near-intents.org/security-compliance/terms-of-service) ne [asiane ne nea wodi so](https://docs.near-intents.org/security-compliance/risk-and-compliance).
- **Tracking ne mmoa:** hwehwɛ swap bi wɔ [Nea Ɛbɛn Intents Explorer](https://explorer.near-intents.org) anaa bisa wɔ [Nea Ɛbɛn Adwene Telegram](https://t.me/near_intents).
- **Refunds:** wobetumi de swap a entumi ayɛ adwuma asan akɔ refund address a wode mae no so, nanso near.com nsɛm ka sɛ wɔmfa sika a wɔde bɛsan ama no nni guaranteed. 1Click nsɛm no nso ka sɛ wɔmfa recovery requests a ɛfa user mfomsoɔ a ɛwɔ USD 300 ase no ho.

Afei, momma yɛnhwehwɛ nsakraeɛ a ɛnyɛ custodial exchanges a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a ɛma Zcash aguadi yɛ mmerɛw no mu kakraa bi. Saa platforms yi a wode bedi dwuma no bɛma woanya ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a wobɛfa so anya Zcash sika pii.

### **Tɔfabɔ**

Nsesa a ɛnyɛ custodial exchanges, anaa DEXs, ma wutumi fi w’ankasa sika kotoku mu di gua bere a wokura wo kokoam safe so tumi no. Ɛno boa ahobanbɔ, nanso kokoamsɛm gyina ɔkwan no so: fibea nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no yɛ ɔmanfo de, ɔsom no hu wo address ne nkitahodi data, na wo ZEC yɛ kokoam bere a ɛtra address a wɔabɔ ho ban mu pɛnkoro pɛ.

Bere a nsakrae a ɛnyɛ sikakorabea de mfaso horow a ɛyɛ den ma no, ɛho hia sɛ yegye tom sɛ ebia ɛde sintɔ ahorow bɛba, te sɛ sikasɛm mu nsɛm a ebetumi aba ne adesua a ɛkɔ akyiri ama wɔn a wonni osuahu pii.

Sɛnea ɛte wɔ sikasɛm ho gyinaesi biara ho no, ɛsɛ sɛ aguadifo susuw nneɛma a wɔde di kan, asiane a wotumi gyina ano, ne mfiridwuma no a wonim no yiye ansa na wɔapaw akwan horow a ɛnyɛ nea wɔde bɛsesa nneɛma ne nea wɔde bɛsesa.
