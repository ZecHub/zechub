# Mobile Top-up a Wɔde Zcash Di Dwuma

Wɔ saa akwankyerɛ yi mu no, wobɛsua sɛdeɛ wobɛtɔ airtime ne ZEC.

## Di anammɔn yi akyi:

### 1. Yɛ akontaabu
Sra [Coinsbee.com na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://www.coinsbee.com/en/shop/mobile-top-ups/) na yɛ akontaabu.

### 2. Paw wo man ne wo network
Twe kɔ benkum so atifi na paw wo man; afei paw mobile phone credit na paw network a wopɛ sɛ wode hyɛ mu (nhwɛso: MTN).

![Step 2 - Selecting country and network](/content-images/0-qvB7ZCWaVWa-C2d8-db0d4ea372.webp)

### 3. Hyɛ nsɛm a ɛkɔ akyiri no ma na tɔ
Hyɛ nsɛm a ɛfata no ma na bɔ "Tɔ Seesei" bɔtn no. Bue teaseɛnam no na hwɛ wo krataa no, klik toa so, na fa $ZEC si adetɔ no so dua.

![Step 3 - Purchase screen](/content-images/0-ikF-ooBc9W7olfNt-7a4584edda.webp)

### 4. Katua ho nsɛm
Wɔde sika a ɛne no sɛ wɔ $ZEC mu bɛma, ne address a ɛda adi pefee a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde sika no kɔ.

![Step 4 - ZEC payment details](/content-images/0-6_YhAhQnsLbrMvHN-c9646f3604.webp)

### 5. Fa sika a wotua no mena
Copy na paste dodow ne address no yiye. Wɔ memo field no mu no, fa w’ankasa address a wɔabɔ ho ban ka ho sɛ ebia wɔbɛsan de sika no ama wo a.

![Step 5 - Sending ZEC](/content-images/0-DNnpyemsKcW-k-u-bcaaa51d3a.webp)

### 6. Nneɛma a wɔyɛ
Sɛ wɔde sika no mena wie a, wobedi w’abisade no ho dwuma wɔ simma kakraa bi mu.

![Step 6 - Processing screen](/content-images/0-LgXuUVLVecqrUW5G-a894c7b2dd.webp)

### 7. Nsɛm a wɔde si so dua
Hey... Me line no na wɔde credit ama! Wubetumi nso asi wo email no so dua bere a woretua sika no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wobɛfa email so abɔ wo amanneɛ bere a wɔawie asɛm no.

![Step 7 - Successful top-up](/content-images/0-TJKIcqznQ4Y8_jgo-e725aed571.webp)

### Mmeae Afoforo a Wɔde Di Dwuma ZEC

Nnansa yi ara, Zcash ahu mmeae foforo pii a wɔde di dwuma, na ɛma ɛyɛ nea mfaso wɔ so kɛse mpo ma da biara da dwumadie. Intanɛt so sotɔɔ ne aguadifo pii gye ZEC tom sɛ ɔkwan a wɔfa so tua nneɛma ne nnwuma ho ka, titiriw wɔn a wɔde nnwuma a ɛnyɛ kokoam nsɛm di kan. 

Sɛ wowɔ adwuma bi na wopɛ sɛ wufi ase gye ZEC sikatua? Ne [Tua ne Zash](https://paywithz.cash/), wobetumi de w’adwuma no aka nnwuma a wogye ZEC tom wɔ nnwuma a wɔde ma ho no ho.
