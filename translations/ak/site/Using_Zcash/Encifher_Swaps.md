###  Nea Ɛsɛ sɛ Wuhu Ansa na Wobɛsesa 
- **Solana afã.** Wrapping hides amounts, nanso wo wallet address ne sɛnea wɔde app no di dwuma no yɛ baguam. Ɛyɛ [nneyɛe a eye sen biara](https://docs.encifher.io/docs/best-practices) bɔ kɔkɔ sɛ wrap, swap ne unwrap a ɛnyɛ den ma wo transaction no yɛ linkable.
- **Encryption.** Wɔyɛ encrypted balances ho adwuma off-chain wɔ hardware enclave (TEE) mu. Wɔn a wɔyɛɛ no no [krataa](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1504) ka sɛ eyi gyina TEE nokwaredi, nokwaredi threshold key management ne cloud attestation root so, ɛnyɛ cryptography nkutoo so.
- **Cross-chain step.** Wɔde swap a ɛkɔ ZEC no fa Near Intents so, baabi a independent solvers hyɛ order no ma.
- **Zcash afã.** Near Intents kyerɛw ZEC sɛnea wɔboa [address ahorow a ɛda adi pefee nkutoo](https://docs.near-intents.org/resources/chain-support), na ZEC field a ɛwɔ encrypt.trade so no gyee address a ɛda adi (t1 anaa t3) nkutoo bere a wɔhwɛɛ akwankyerɛ yi mu wɔ September 2026. Address a ɛda adi pefee kyerɛ ne balance ne incoming transfers wɔ baguam kosi sɛ wobɛbɔ ho ban.
- **Screening.** App no hwɛ connecting wallets atia databases te sɛ TRM ne Chainalysis, ne ne [kratafa a ɛfa mmara sodi ho](https://docs.encifher.io/docs/compliance) ka sɛ wobetumi ahwɛ kyerɛwtohɔ ahorow a wɔabɔ no kokoam mu sɛ mmara kwan so ntease bi wɔ hɔ a. Near Intents na ɛyɛ n’ankasa de [nhwehwɛmu a wɔyɛ](https://docs.near-intents.org/security-compliance/risk-and-compliance) nso.

###  Anamɔn 5: Bɔ Wo ZEC ho ban 
Sɛ ZEC no ba pɛ a, fa ZODL's **Shield** option no di dwuma de kɔ [ɔtare a wɔabɔ ho ban](/using-zcash/shielded-pools). Enkosi saa bere no, ɛtra address bi a ɛda adi pefee a obiara betumi ahu nea ɛkari pɛ no so. Shielding bɔ nea wobɛyɛ akyi no ho ban, nanso transfer a ɛba ne shielding asɛm no kɔ so da adi wɔ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn so. Bere nyinaa hwɛ sɛ link ahorow no yɛ nokware, kwati sɛ wobɛsan de address ahorow adi dwuma bio, na di kan sɔ nneɛma nketenkete hwɛ.
