## TL;DR

* Zcash nnwinnade abien boa adebɔfo ma wotua wɔn ka wɔ kokoam. Zcash.me ma wo profile page a wo shielded address wom, na TIPZ ma fans de afotu mena wo.
* Zcash.me, wo kyɛ link biako, `zcash.me/[username]`, a ɛkyerɛ wo address a wɔaka abom ne wo social accounts.
* TIPZ, fans betumi tip wɔ cryptocurrencies afoforo mu, na tip biara du wo nkyɛn sɛ shielded ZEC.
* Nnwinnade abien no nyinaa bisa wo address a wɔaka abom (`u1...`).
* Address a wɔabɔ ho ban ho afotu mma wo sika a wonya ne sika a ɛkari pɛ no nyɛ nea ɔmanfo nhu.

5. Kyɛ wo link no: `zcash.me/[yourusername]`

## Aniwa so / Nsɛso

Tip jar a ɛwɔ café counter so yɛ ahwehwɛ. Obiara a ɔwɔ dan no mu hu onii a ɔtow sika bi guu mu ne sɛnea toa no ayɛ ma.

Address a wɔabɔ ho ban yɛ adwuma te sɛ nkrataa adaka a wɔatoto mu a ɛwɔ baabi a wɔde nneɛma gu. Fans de envelope a sika ne krataa bi wom gu mu, na ɔbɔadeɛ no nkutoo na obue adaka no. TIPZ de sika sesa ka ho wɔ ɔpon no ano: fan bi betumi aba a ETH anaa SOL ka ho, na nea ɛba fam wɔ mailbox no mu ne ZEC.

```
TIPZ Payment Flow

Fan -> tipz.cash Protocol -> Creator's Wallet (Shielded ZEC)

Fan selects amount + message -> Protocol auto-converts -> Creator receives private shielded ZEC.
```
