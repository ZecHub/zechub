### Nneɛma Titiriw a Ɛwɔ Hɔ

#### Mprempren Ɛwɔ Hɔ
- **Custom Profile Pages** - Kratafa a wɔayɛ ama wo ankasa a ɛwɔ din, bio, avatar, ne beae
- **Shielded Address Display** - Klik pɛnkoro copy button ma wo address a wɔaka abom no
- **Social Link Integration** - X, LinkedIn, Telegram, ne wɛbsaet ahorow a wɔahyɛ da ayɛ
- **Verification System** - Badge a wɔagye atom ama mpɔtam hɔfoɔ ahotosoɔ
- **Referral Leaderboard** - Di referrers a wɔkorɔn ne mpɔtam hɔ akannifoɔ akyi
- **Activity Status** - Kyerɛ bere a etwa to a ɛyɛ adwuma

### Sɛnea Wofi Ase
1. Sra [https://zcash.me/](https://zcash.me/) na klik **Join** so.
2. Paw edin soronko a wode di dwuma, fa bio ne profile mfonini ka ho
3. Fa wo Zcash unified address (`u1...`)
4. Fa wo social account ahorow no bata ho
5. Kyɛ wo link no: `zcash.me/[yourusername]`

### Nea Enti a Kokoam Nsɛm Ho Hia Ma Adebɔfo

#### Sikasɛm mu Ahofadi
- 0% a wɔde tua platform ho ka
- Non-custodial & obi ankasa hwɛ
- Ntamgyinafo biara nni hɔ

#### Kokoamsɛm-Nsusuwii a Edi Kan
- Tip biara ba sɛ shielded ZEC
- Private sika kotoku mu sika a ɛwɔ hɔ
- Nkrasɛm a wɔabɔ no kokoam a afotu wom
- Ɔbɔadeɛ a wɔmmɔ din nyinaa a wɔpaw

#### Cross-Chain Katua a Wɔde Ma
| Sika a Wɔde Di Dwuma | Nkitahodi |
|----------|---------|
| ETH | Ethereum a ɛyɛ adwuma |
| USDC | Nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn pii |
| SOL | Solana |

#### Ɔbɔadeɛ Ahobammɔ
- Asiane biara nni hɔ a ɛbɛma deplatforming aba
- Censorship a ɛko tia
- KYC biara nhia
