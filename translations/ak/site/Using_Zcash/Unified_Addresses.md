# Address ahorow a Wɔaka abom

> Address biako ma Zcash. Kyɛ, na nea ɔde kɔmaa no sika kotoku no nya ɔkwan a ɛyɛ kokoam sen biara a ɛbɛfa so atua wo ka.

Address a wɔaka abom (UA) yɛ Zcash address biako a ɛka receivers pii bom yɛ biako. Wɔn a wogye sika yɛ address bi afã horow a wogye sika wɔ bo pɔtee bi mu: Ironwood, Sapling, ne nea ɛda adi pefee. UA bi fi ase wɔ `u1` wɔ mainnet so na wɔkyerɛkyerɛɛ mu wɔ [ZIP 316 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0316), a wɔde NU5 ntwamutam a wɔayɛ no foforo no bae wɔ May 31, 2022.

Ansa na address ahorow a wɔaka abom no reba no, na ZEC a wobenya no kyerɛ sɛ wobesusuw address ahorow ho. BI `zs` address gyee Sapling sika, a `t` address gyee sika a ɛda adi pefee, na sɛ wɔde mena wɔ ahorow ntam no faa anammɔn foforo. UA bi de saa asɛm no ba awiei. Wokyɛ address biako, na sika kotoku no yɛ nea aka no ho adwuma.

## Sɛnea ɛyɛ adwuma

Sɛ obi de kɔ wo UA a, wɔn sika kotoku kenkan agyefo a ɛwɔ mu no na ɛpaw kokoam pool a ɛsen biara a sika kotoku abien no nyinaa boa:

1. Sɛ sika kotoku abien no nyinaa boa mprempren shielded pool no a, sikatua no du shielded. Biribiara nni baguam a ɛka nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no.
2. Sɛ nea ɔde amena no tumi de fi ɔtare dedaw bi mu nkutoo soma a, sika kotoku no san hwe ase kɔ agyefo a eye sen biara a mo baanu nyinaa kyɛ no so, te sɛ Sapling.
3. Sɛ nea ɔde kɔmaa no tumi de sika a ɛda adi pefee nkutoo mena a, sika a wotua no si wo UA no a ɛda adi pefee a ogye no mu, na wo sika kotoku no betumi abɔ ho ban afi hɔ.

W’ankasa wuntumi nni eyi ho dwuma da. Address no kɔ so yɛ nea ɛte saa ara; routing no si wɔ ne mu.

## Nea enti a ɛho hia mprempren

Zcash kɔ so yɛ upgrade. Bere a Ironwood pool no de NU6.3 yɛɛ adwuma wɔ July 28, 2026 no, na wɔn a wɔwɔ UA no nhia address foforo: sika kotoku no fii ase paw Ironwood receiver no ara kwa wɔ address ahorow a nkurɔfo wɔ dedaw no mu. Na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma a wokura address dedaw a egyina hɔ ma wɔn ho no tu kɔtra baabi foforo. Wɔn a wɔwɔ UA no anyɛ hwee.

Eyi ne botae a wɔde yɛ adwini no. Pool foforo ba a daakye network upgrades ka ho, na address a wɔaka abom twetwe wɔn a obiara nsan nkyɛ address anaasɛ ɔde ne nsa mfa sika nkɔ baabi foforo. Hwɛ [Zcash bo a ɛsom no pools](/using-zcash/shielded-pools) ma mprempren pool lineup no.

## Address foforo a wɔde ma obiara a ɔde nneɛma bɛmena

Walets betumi ayɛ address ahorow pii a wɔaka abom ama akontaabu biako. Wɔn mu biara tua sika wɔ sika koro mu, nanso wontumi mfa wɔn mu abien biara nka nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn mu. Fa UA soronko ma onipa anaa ɔsom biara a ɛtua wo ka, na wɔn mu baanu biara ntumi mfa address ntoto ho mfa nhu sɛ wotua sika kotoku koro.

## Nnwinnade a ɛfa ho

- **Viewing keys.** Akontaabu biara wɔ viewing keys a wɔaka abom a ɛkyɛ nea UA nya (anaa biribiara a ɛde mena) a ɛnkyɛ sika a wɔsɛe no tumi. Nsɛmfua a wɔde di kan `uview` ne `uivk`. Hwɛ [safe a wɔde hwɛ nneɛma](/zcash-tech/viewing-keys).
- **TEX addresses.** Exchanges binom, mprempren Binance, hwehwɛ sɛ sika a wɔde asie fi baabi a ɛda adi pefee ba. TEX address bi ([ZIP 320 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0320)) yɛ transparent-only hokafo ma wo UA ma saa asɛm no pɛpɛɛpɛ. Hwɛ [address ahorow a wɔde sesa nneɛma a ɛda adi pefee](/using-zcash/transparent-exchange-addresses).
- **Payment requests.** Wobetumi de UA ahyɛ payment request URI mu a wɔde sika ne memo adi kan ahyɛ mu ma. Hwɛ [sikatua adesrɛ URI ahorow](/using-zcash/payment-request-uris).

## Nokwasɛm ahorow a wɔyɛ no ntɛmntɛm

| | |
|---|---|
| Nsɛmfua a wɔde di kan | `u1` (mainnet) |
| Encoding a wɔde kyerɛw nsɛm | bech32m na ɛwɔ hɔ |
| Wɔakyerɛkyerɛ mu wɔ | [ZIP 316 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0316) |
| Wɔde too gua | NU5, Ɔpɛpɔn 31, 2022 |
| Wɔ | Ironwood, Sapling, ne afiri a wɔde gye nneɛma a ɛda adi pefee |
| Sika a wɔsɛe no titiriw | Baako wɔ akontaabu biara mu, a ne UA ahorow nyinaa kyɛ |

Hwɛ sika kotoku bɛn na ɛboa address ahorow a wɔaka abom wɔ [sika kotoku](/wallets) kratafa.
