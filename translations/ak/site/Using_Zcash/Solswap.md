## **Native ZEC anaasɛ ZEC token bi?**

"ZEC" wɔ Phantom mu no betumi akyerɛ agyapade ahorow abien, enti hu nea woretua ho ka.

- **Phantom's built-in Swap button** ma wo token gyinabea a ɛkyerɛ ZEC wɔ Solana (anaasɛ network foforo a Phantom boa). Ɛnyɛ ZEC. Ɛte wo Phantom address so, enni Zcash shielded dwumadie biara, na Zcash sika kotokuo ntumi nhunu anaasɛ ɛbɔ ho ban.
- **Native ZEC** wɔ Zcash blockchain no so nko ara na wɔde kɔ Zcash address bi so. Sɛ wobɛnya no a, ɛhia service a ɛbisa wo Zcash address, te sɛ swap a ɛwɔ mu [ZODL](https://zodl.com), akwan horow a ɛwɔ so no mu biako [DEX kratafa no](/dex), anaa solswap.org a ɛno akyi no, wobɛtwe sika akɔ wo Zcash sika kotoku mu (Anamɔn 8).

### Hwɛ ansa na woatua

- **Network:** ɛsɛ sɛ ZEC a wo nsa bɛka no wɔ **Zcash** network no so. Sɛ ɛka sɛ Solana, Ethereum anaa Base a, ɛyɛ token.
- **Agyapadeɛ:** native ZEC nni token contract anaa mint address biara. Sɛ wo de no kyerɛ biako a, ɛyɛ token. Afei nso, "ZEC" tokens a ɛte sɛ nea ɛdɔɔso wɔ Solana so, enti nkɔ edin no nkutoo so.
- **Address:** native ZEC kɔ Zcash address bi so, a ɛfiri aseɛ `t1`, `u1` or `zs`. Sɛ wɔde ZEC no rekɔ wo Phantom address so a, na worenya token.

##  **Anamɔn 1: Bue Swap Interface no** 
Fi ase **Phantom app** no na kɔ **[solswap.org so na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://solswap.org/)** fi Phantom browser no so. Sait no yɛ adwuma wɔ Near Intents so na ebetumi de ZEC akɔ Zcash address bi so. 

Phantom ankasa **Swap** button nso kyerɛw ZEC, nanso ɛno ma wunya token a yɛaka ho asɛm wɔ atifi hɔ no, ɛnyɛ native ZEC.

##  **Anamɔn 2: Paw Networks ne Tokens a wode bɛto hɔ** 
- Paw wo **source network** (sɛ nhwɛso no, *Ethereum* anaa *Solana*) afei fa to hɔ ma sesa. 


![img3](/content-images/S1SaGYZ9xx-2a27ccdd47.webp)

- Paw base token te sɛ **SOL, USDT, anaa USDC**. 
- Paw **ZEC** sɛ wo **destination token**. 
- Hwɛ sɛ Zcash nam swap interface no so wɔ hɔ. 



![img4](/content-images/ry4QQF-5gx-f3805528ea.webp)

##  **Anamɔn 3: Hyehyɛ Sika dodow & Nhwehwɛmu Quote** 
- Hyehyɛ sika dodow a wopɛ sɛ wosesa. 
- Phantom bɛda **sika a wɔabu akontaa sɛ wobenya** adi wɔ sika a wɔatua akyi. 


![img5](/content-images/B1U1NYW5xe-58cf150668.webp)

---

##  **Anamɔn 4: Hwɛ Gas & Fees** 
- Sɛ wopɛ **same-chain swaps** a, hwɛ hu sɛ wowɔ native gas token no a ɛdɔɔso (*ETH ma Ethereum, SOL ma Solana*). 
- **Cross-chain swaps** hwehwɛ gas wɔ source ne destination chains nyinaa so. 
- Hwɛ sɛnea wɔakyekyɛ sika a wɔbɔ no mu: 
  - Phantom Ka: **0.85%** na ɛyɛ adwuma 
  - Network Gas a Wɔde Di Dwuma 
  - Bridging Ɔdemafoɔ Ka (~**0.3%**) 
  
  
---

##  **Anamɔn 5: Sesa Nsiesiei (Wɔpɛ)** 
Kɔ **Sesa Nsiesiei** so kɔ: 
- Siesie **slippage** (default **0.3%**, wotumi sesa kosi 30%). 
- Ma **priority fees** nkɔ soro wɔ congested networks so.

##  **Anamɔn 6: Si Swap so dua** 
- Hwɛ swap ho nsɛm nyinaa mu. 
- Klik **Swap Now** na fi ase yɛ adwuma no.

## **Anamɔn 7: Monitor Gyinabea** 
- Di wo swap no akyi wɔ **Nnansa yi Dwumadi** tab no mu. 
- Sɛ wopɛ cross-chain swaps a, fa wo **transaction ID** ne **Li.Fi Scanner** di dwuma ma bere ankasa mu nsɛm foforo. 


![img7](/content-images/S1NBwKbcxe-5b7d11f5c1.webp)

## **Anamɔn 8: Twe Native ZEC kɔ Wo Zcash Wallet mu** 
Swap no akyi no, wo ZEC bɛda adi wɔ wo solswap.org **Account** balance no mu. Ɛnnya mmaa Zcash network no so, na ɛnni Phantom nso mu. Sɛ wobɛtu akɔ baabi foforɔ a: 
- Bue Zcash sika kotoku te sɛ [ZODL](https://zodl.com) na kɔpi wo address a wode gye sika no. Krataa a wɔde yi sika no gye (`t1`) anaasɛ wɔaka abom (`u1`) adrɛse. 
- Wɔ solswap.org so no, kɔ **Account** na pia **Withdraw**. 
- Paw **ZEC**, hyehyɛ network no to **Zcash**, fa wo address no hyɛ mu na hwɛ no mprenu ansa na woasi so dua. 

---

## **Anamɔn a Edi Hɔ** 
Sɛ native ZEC wɔ wo Zcash sika kotoku mu pɛ a, wubetumi de abɔ ho ban [akwankyerɛ yi](/guides/using-zec-privately).  

ZEC token a wɔde Phantom Swap button atɔ no, wontumi mmɔ ho ban saa kwan yi so, efisɛ enni Zcash network no so. Anka ɛho behia sɛ wudi kan sesa no ma native ZEC a wɔde akɔ Zcash address bi so.
