# Decentralized Exchanges (Nkrataa ahodoɔ a etwa sɛ yɛhyehyɛ wɔ kasa ahodoɔ mu)

ZecHub nnye Decentralized Exchange dwumadie biara nni, yɛsrɛ sɛ yɛ w'ankasa nhwehwɛmu.

Obiara ne no `###` N'asɛmti a ɛwɔ ase ha no yɛ krataa biako wɔ so. https://zechub.wiki/dex.
Twe, twe anaa yi ɔfã bi firi ha; wiki no bɛgye afiri saa fael yi mu.

<img src="/nearintents.png" alt="Near-intents" width="200" height="100"/>

### Nativeswap

<img src="/nativeswap.png" alt="Nativeswap" width="200" height="100"/>

- Intanɛt so: https://nativeswap.io/
