# Zcash a wobenya

## TL;DR

- Kɔtɔ ZEC wɔ exchange a ɛboa **shielded withdrawals** (Gemini, BitcoinVN, Flyp.me) bere a ɛbɛyɛ yiye
- Dodow no ara titiriw centralized exchanges (Coinbase, Kraken, Binance) boa **transparent withdrawals** nkutoo — kyɛm wo ZEC bere a woanya no akyi
- Decentralized exchanges ma kwan ma ɛnyɛ custodial swaps nanso mpɛn pii no wɔtwe wɔn ho kɔ address ahorow a ɛda adi pefee so
- Wo **ɛnyɛ** sɛ wobɛtɔ ZEC a edi mũ — fractions yɛ adwuma yiye

---

Sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wode Zcash bedi dwuma wɔ kokoam (shielded) nnwuma mu no, ɛsɛ sɛ wunya ZEC. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wunya ZEC a, ɛho behia sɛ wotɔ. Wubetumi atɔ ZEC afi exchange ahorow dodow bi mu, a yɛbɛbobɔ din wɔ ase ha. Wɔhyɛ nyansa sɛ fa exchange a ɛboa shielded withdrawals di dwuma.

_Wo nso wobɛtumi atu ZEC, nanso ɛno ntaa nyɛ yie mma average consumers. Ɛno ho nsɛm pii [ha](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/mining-to-shielded-addresses-community-mining-pool/50281) na [ha](https://zec.suprnova.cc/StartMining)_.

## Nsesa a ɛboa sika a wɔde yi sika a wɔabɔ ho ban

**[Gemini na ɔyɛ ɔbarima](https://www.gemini.com)**

Gemini yɛ cryptocurrency exchange, sika kotoku, ne custodian a wɔahyɛ ho mmara a ɛma ɛyɛ mmerɛw na ahobammɔ wom sɛ wobɛtɔ ZEC, Ether, ne cryptocurrencies afoforo. Wubetumi de fiat anaa cryptocurrencies afoforo atɔ ZEC.

**[BitcoinVN na ɔkyerɛwee](https://bitcoinvn.io)**

BitcoinVN yɛ a ɛnyɛ custodial instant exchange a ɛboa shielded (z-address) deposits ne withdrawals ma ZEC — exchange kakraa bi pɛ wɔ wiase nyinaa a ɛwɔ z-address mmoa a edi mũ no mu biako. Ɛyɛ adwuma firi afe 2014, wobɛtumi asesa BTC, ETH, USDT, ne cryptocurrencies foforɔ ama ZEC tẽẽ akɔ wo sika kotokuo a wɔabɔ ho ban no so.

**[Flyp.me na ɛyɛ adwuma](https://flyp.me)**

Flyp.me yɛ no sign-up crypto exchange. Na ɛyɛ akwan a ahobammɔ wom na ɛyɛ kokoam sen biara a wobɛfa so asesa cryptocurrencies 30+ tẽẽ akɔ wo sika kotoku mu no mu biako. Wubetumi de cryptocurrencies afoforo atɔ ZEC.

## Mfomso a Wɔtaa Tu a Ɛsɛ sɛ Wɔkwati

- **Wɔtwe wo ho kɔ address a ɛda adi so** bere a exchange no boa shielded withdrawals — bere nyinaa fa wo z-address di dwuma sɛ ɛwɔ hɔ a
- **Skipping the shielding step** bere a woagye akɔ address a ɛda adi so akyi — fa wo sika kotoku no "Shield Funds" feature no di dwuma ntɛm ara
- **Tɔtɔ pii sen sɛnea wohia mfiase no** — fi ase ketewaa bi na sua nsu a ɛsen no ansa na woatɔ akɛse
