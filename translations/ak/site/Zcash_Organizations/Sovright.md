# Sovright na ɔkyerɛwee
![Sovright na ɔkyerɛwee](/content-images/sovright-16a27c0d24.webp)

**Sovright** yɛ US 501(c3) adwumakuw a ɛnyɛ nea wɔhwehwɛ mfaso a wɔatu wɔn ho ama sɛ wɔbɛboa nnipa ahofadi. Sɛ yɛde yɛn adwene si Zcash nhyehyɛe, atuo, protocol nkɔso, ne abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom mmoa so a, adwuma no kyekye nnwinnadeɛ ne nnwuma a wɔabɔ wɔn tirim sɛ wɔbɛhyɛ ankorankoro digyital sika nhyehyɛeɛ mu den na wɔaboa Zcash ntam nkitahodiɛ no a ɛbɛtumi agyina ano bere tenten.

Kan no na wɔfrɛ Sovright sɛ **Bootstrap**. Mfitiase din no daa ahyehyɛde ahorow no mfitiase botae a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛbɔ Zcash infrastructure company bi afi mfiase no adi.

[Kɔ wɛbsaet hɔ](https://sovright.com/)

## Din

Edin **Sovright** yɛ **sovereign** ne **right** a wɔabom ayɛ. Ɛda no adi sɛ ahyehyɛde ahorow no de wɔn adwene si ankorankoro tumidi, kokoam nsɛm, ne hokwan a wɔwɔ sɛ wɔpaw sɛnea wɔhwɛ ankorankoro data, sika, nkitahodi, kɔmputa, ne intanɛt so dwumadi so.

Sovrights adwuma no fii ase wɔ Zcash, cryptocurrency a ɛkora kokoam nsɛm so a ɛde zero-knowledge cryptography di dwuma de boa nnwuma a wɔabɔ ho ban. Bere kɔɔ so no, ahyehyɛde ahorow no de wɔn adwene trɛwee sen ankorankoro sika de kaa nnwuma ho haw ahorow a ɛtrɛw ho, a nkitahodi a ahobammɔ wom, nkitahodi a ɛyɛ den, ankorankoro kɔmputa, ne AI nkitahodi a ahobammɔ wom ka ho.

## Mining Pool

Sovright yɛ Zcash a wotu fagude ho adwuma wɔ:

[tuo.sovright.com](https://mining.sovright.com/)

Wɔayɛ Sovright Mining Pool no sɛ ɛbɛboa wɔn a wotu fagude no tumidi na ɛbɛtew mining pool no dodow so. Mining pool concentration yɛ ade a ɛhaw adwene mpɛn pii wɔ proof-of-work networks mu efisɛ pools kakraa bi betumi awie sɛ wɔadi network hashrate kyɛfa kɛse so. Eyi betumi ama decentralization ayɛ mmerɛw na ama ahotoso a wɔde to pool adwumayɛfo atitiriw nneyɛe so no ayɛ kɛse.

Sovright Mining Pool no yɛ nea wɔayɛ ama wɔn a wɔtu fagudeɛ a wɔn nkutoo, adwumayɛfoɔ nketewa, ne afoforɔ a wɔde wɔn ho hyɛ mu a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔboa Zcash mining ecosystem a wɔde ahyɛ aman nyinaa nsa. Pool no si ntease a ɛda adi pefee a ɛfa sikatua ho, akontaabu a wobetumi asusuw ho, ne adwumayɛ ho ka a ɛba fam koraa so dua.

Mprempren testnet version no mma payouts na wɔayɛ sɛ wɔde bɛsɔ ahwɛ nkutoo.

## (Reba seesei ara)

### Network Resilience a Wɔde Yɛ Adwuma

Network resilience kyerɛ ahotoso ne ahoɔden a ɛwɔ infrastructure a ɛde Zcash nnwuma kɔ no mu. Ankorankoro nkitahodi nnyina cryptography nko so, na mmom egyina sɛnea network nhyehyɛe a ɛwɔ wɔn ase no wɔ hɔ ne ahotoso a ɛwɔ so nso so.

Sovright kyerɛ sɛ network resilience yɛ adwuma titiriw a wɔde hwɛ Zcash ahobammɔ a mfaso wɔ so ne sɛnea wɔde di dwuma so.

### Sikasɛm mu Tumidi

Sikasɛm mu tumidi kyerɛ tumi a ankorankoro tumi di wɔn ankasa sika so na wonya bi a wɔmfa wɔn ho nto kwan a efi ntamgyinafo hɔ so. Wɔ Zcash nsɛm mu no, eyi ka ankorankoro sikatua, wɔn ankasa hwɛ, ne nhyehyɛe a ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi di nkitaho wɔ wɔn ankasa nsɛm so.

Sovright bu sikasɛm mu kwan a wobenya ne sika a wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no di so sɛ nneɛma atitiriw a ɛwɔ ankorankoro tumidi mu.
