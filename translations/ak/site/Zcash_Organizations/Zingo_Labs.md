[Ɔmanfoɔ Wɛbsaet](https://zingolabs.org/) - [Github a woagye atom](https://github.com/zingolabs) - [X/Twitter so na woagye atom](https://x.com/ZingoLabs) - [Instagram so na woagye atom](https://www.instagram.com/zingolabesp/)

ZingoLabs yɛ anisoadehunufoɔ kuo a wɔatu wɔn ho ama sɛ wɔbɛma nnipa suahunu akɔ anim. Yegye di sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mfiridwuma so ba adesamma mfaso na yɛnam nkitahodi a yɛpene so so na edi yiye. Yɛrekyerɛ nhwɛso ahorow a ɛma eyi tumi yɛ yiye.

Zingo Lab Cyan yɛ adwuma sɛ Shielded DAO. Yɛkora yɛn sika so wɔ sikakorabea bi a asɔremma biara wɔ view key. Wɔsɛe sika fi sikakorabea hɔ bere a asɔremma tow aba de gyina nyansahyɛ bi akyi no.

## Nnwuma a wɔyɛ

### Zingo! Sikakorabea ([Github](https://github.com/zingolabs/zingo-mobile))
Zingo Wallet yɛ Zcash sika kotoku a ɛwɔ nneɛma nyinaa a wɔayɛ ama wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no adamfofa su, ɛwom sɛ ɛde nneɛma bi a ɛkɔ akyiri ka ho ma wɔn a wɔakɔ anim kɛse de. Ɛboa transparent, Sapling ne Orchard pools, ɛwɔ address book a wɔde tua sika mpɛn pii na ɛwɔ kasa ahodoɔ mu. Ɛyɛ sika kotoku a edi kan a ɛboaa Orchard na ɛde NU5 format ahorow dii dwuma.

Zingo no mu nneɛma atitiriw no mu biako! ne tumi a ɛwɔ sɛ ɛde Memo field no di dwuma de nhumu a ɛsom bo ma wɔ wo nnwuma ho.

Zingo! no wɔ hɔ ma mobile mfiri ne PC ahorow. Wubehu nea wɔatwe nyinaa [ha](https://zingolabs.org/)

### Zingolib ([Github.) no](https://github.com/zingolabs/zingolib))
API ne test-app a ɛda zcash dwumadie adi ma app a wɔde di dwuma. Zingolib de nwomakorabea ma zingo-mobile, ne cli aplikeshɔn a ɛka ho a ɛne zcashd di nkitaho denam lightwalletd so a wɔfrɛ no Zingo-cli, ahyɛdeɛ kwan lightwalletd-proxy client.

### Zaino Indexer ([Github](https://github.com/zingolabs/zaino))
Zaino yɛ Indexer a Zingo kuw no ayɛ wɔ Rust, a ne botae ne sɛ ɛbɛsesa lightwalletd na ɛbɛpia zcashd deprecation adwuma no akɔ anim.

Zaino de nneɛma a ɛho hia ma akraman a wɔn ho yɛ hare nyinaa, te sɛ sika kotoku ne aplikeshɔn a enhia blockchain abakɔsɛm a edi mũ, ne akraman anaa sika kotoku a edi mũ. Ɛsan nso boa block explorers, ma kwan ma wɔkɔ blockchain a wɔawie ne nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn ne mempool a eye sen biara a wɔanwie a Zebra anaa Zcashd full validator di so no nyinaa so.

###  ZLN (zcash-mpɔtam-asau) ([Github](https://github.com/zingolabs/zcash-local-net))
A set of utilities a ɛde Zcash nhyehyɛe ahorow no fi ase na ɛhwɛ so. Wɔde eyi di dwuma ma nkabom sɔhwɛ wɔ nkɔso a ɛfa:
- lightclients a wɔyɛ adwuma wɔ hɔ
- indexers a wɔde kyerɛw nsɛm
- validators a wɔyɛ saa

Ne botae ne sɛ ɛbɛma sɔhwɛ tebea a ɛyɛ mmerɛw kɛse na ɛyɛ den ama core nodes (validators) te sɛ zcash ne zebra, indexers te sɛ lightwallet ne zaino, kakraa bi, zingo-cli sɛ light client wallet.

Wɔayɛ saa akoraeɛ yi sɛ wɔde bɛtoto validator ahodoɔ (te sɛ Zcashd ne Zebrad) ne indexers (te sɛ Lightwalletd ne Zaino) dwumadie ho na ama akwantuo no ayɛ mmerɛ wɔ Zcashd deprecation nhyehyɛeɛ no mu.

Wɔ nnwinnadeɛ a wɔde hyɛ aseɛ, de sie, na wɔde bɛhyɛ Zcash nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn data (ma mainnet, testnet, ne regtest) akyi no, zcash-zocal-net de sɔhwɛ ahodoɔ a wɔde bɛtoto Lightwalletd ne Zaino tumi ho wɔ Lightwallet RPC dwumadie nyinaa mu ka ho. Wobetumi ayɛ saa sɔhwɛ ahorow yi tẽẽ afi Zaino (hwɛ [https://github.com/zingolabs/zaino/blob/dev/docs/testing.md](https://github.com/zingolabs/zaino/blob/dev/docs/testing.md)]) sɛ wɔbɛsɔ Lightwallet RPC dwumadie a wɔagye wɔ Zaino no ahwɛ.
