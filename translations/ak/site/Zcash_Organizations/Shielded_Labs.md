# <img src="/content-images/Shielded-labs-shield-white-2-27aef199ae.webp" alt="Shielded Labs logo" width="50"/> Shielded Labs

[Shielded Labs](https://shieldedlabs.net/) yɛ Zcash mmoa ahyehyɛde a ɛde ne ho, a wɔde ntoboa na ɛyɛ adwuma a ɛwɔ Switzerland. N’adwuma ne sɛ ɛbɛkyekye ankorankoro sika a wontumi nsiw ano a ɛma nnipa ahofadi ne wɔn ankasa tumidi tumi, a wɔde wɔn adwene besi adwuma a ɛma Zcash ntam nkitahodi no a ɛbɛkɔ so atra hɔ, ahobammɔ, ne nea ɛtumi sesa so.

Shielded Labs de mfiridwuma tumi a ɛde ne ho ka Zcash abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no ho. Ɛnyɛ Electric Coin Company, Zcash Foundation, anaa Zcash Community Grants, na ɛka ne ho asɛm sɛ Zcash abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom ahyehyɛdeɛ a ɛdi kan a ɛnnyaa sika tẽẽ anaa ɛnnya sika mfii Development Fund anaasɛ block akatua.

## Asɛmpatrɛw ne Dwuma

Shielded Labs de n’adwene si protocol ne infrastructure adwuma a ebetumi ama Zcash atumi agyina ano kɛse wɔ bere tenten mu so. Ne baguam nneɛma si so dua sɛ:

- Nnipa ahofadi ne sikasɛm mu kokoamsɛm
- Sika a wɔde ma a ɛde ne ho a ɛwɔ Nkɔso Foto no akyi
- Obu a wɔde ma ZEC kurafo ne coinholder input
- Wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no ahofadi ne wɔn ankasa tumidi
- Mfiridwuma mu nnoɔma foforɔ a wɔde bɛyɛ adwuma a ɛbɛtena hɔ daa, ahobanbɔ, ne scalability
- Adwuma a wɔne Zcash mmoa afoforo yɛ

Ahyehyɛde no nam mfiridwuma tẽẽ, ɔmanfo nhwehwɛmu, nnwuma a wɔde boa, ne abɔde a nkwa wom a wɔyɛ no biako so na ɛboa. Ɛsan nso hwɛ ɔmanfoɔ ahodeɛ so ma nnisoɔ abatoɔ ne sika a wɔde sika a wɔde di dwuma no kyerɛ kwan, na ɛboa Zcash mpɔtam hɔfoɔ ma wɔte berɛ ne sɛdeɛ wɔbɛtumi de wɔn ho ahyɛ nnisoɔ nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ mu ase.

## Mprempren Nnwuma a Wɔyɛ

Shielded Labs bobɔ adwuma no mmeae pii a ɛyɛ nnam din wɔ baguam:

- [Network Sustainability Nneɛma a Wɔde Yɛ Adwuma](https://shieldedlabs.net/nsm/) - nsakraeɛ a wɔahyɛ ho nyansa a wɔabɔ wɔn tirim sɛ wɔbɛboa Zcash's ahobanbɔ sikasɛm nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛbɛkyɛ berɛ a wɔkora ZEC nneɛma a wɔde ma ɔpepem 21 no so. NSM adwuma no bi ne ZEC hyew a wofi wɔn pɛ mu yɛ, sika a wɔde ma no smoothing, ne sika a wɔbɛhyew ho nyansahyɛ ahorow a wɔaka ho asɛm wɔ ZIP 233, 234, ne 235 mu.
- [Crosslink](https://shieldedlabs.net/crosslink-faq/) - Hybrid consensus upgrade a wɔahyɛ ho nyansa a ɛbɛka Zcash's proof-of-work block production ne proof-of-stake finality abom, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ZEC wuranom bɛtumi aboa ama wɔabɔ network no ho ban berɛ a miners kɔ so yɛ blocks.
- [Dynamic Fees a Wɔbɔ](https://shieldedlabs.net/fees/) - nhwehwɛmu ne nnwinnade a ɛbɛma wɔatumi ahyɛ nkɔm, ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wɔde bedi dwuma, na ɛyɛ den sɛ wobetua Zcash asɛmdi ho ka bere a ntwamutam no renya nkɔso no.
- [Ohunu](https://shieldedlabs.net/zero/) - a woboa suite a open-source Zcash infrastructure software ma exchanges, mining pools, wallet providers, ne ahyehyɛdeɛ foforɔ a wɔde wɔn ho to Zcash infrastructure so.
- [Ironwood](https://shieldedlabs.net/ironwood/) - Zcash network upgrade ne shielded pool a wɔayɛ de abua Orchard atoro a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, a wɔayɛ sɛ wɔde bɛsan de ahofadi a wobetumi agye atom wɔ Zcash's circulating supply no mu.
- [Epoch](https://shieldedlabs.net/epoch-future-proof-cryptography-for-zcash/) - Nhwehwɛmu ne mfiridwuma adwuma bi a wɔde too gua wɔ October 2026 mu sɛ wɔbɛyɛ production-ready, high-assurance post-quantum cryptography ama Zcash na wɔatrɛw formal verification a ɛfa cryptographic components a ɛho hia ho. Dwumadie no de n’ani asi so sɛ wɔde bedi dwuma a ɛyɛ krado sɛ wɔbɛyɛ adwuma wɔ afe 2027 awieeɛ a ɛbɛtumi ayɛ nnyinasoɔ ama daakye ntwamutam nkɔsoɔ. Ɛboa Project Tachyon ne mmɔdenbɔ afoforo a wɔbɔ wɔ quantum akyi wɔ abɔde a nkwa wom no nyinaa mu.

## Epoch ne cryptography kuw no

Wɔ Ɔkɔtɔberɛ 2026 mu no Shielded Labs hyɛɛ Epoch, mmɔdenbɔ a wɔatu wɔn ho ama sɛ wɔbɛdi Zcash daakye adanseɛ atia quantum kɔmputa, ntua a AI boa, ne ahunahuna foforɔ a ɛkɔ anim. Dwumadie no botae ne sɛ ɛbɛsesa quantum-vulnerable cryptographic components na wɔde formal verification adi dwuma wɔ protocol no afã horow a ɛho hia no mu.

Epoch, nnipa baasa a wɔyɛ cryptography kuw bi na wodi anim:

- **Ulrich Haböck** – Cryptographer panyin (kan no na ɔwɔ StarkWare; kan no na ɔwɔ Polygon Zero). Ɔbenfo wɔ nimdeɛ a enni adanse ne post-quantum cryptography mu; ɔkyerɛwfo a ɔkyerɛw logUp lookup argument no na ɔne no kyerɛw Circle STARKs krataa no.
- **Luke Edwards** – Cryptography Engineer (kan no na ɔyɛ mfiridwuma ho ɔkannifo wɔ cryptography mfiridwuma kuw no mu wɔ Aztec). Ɔwɔ PhD wɔ Nkontaabu a Wɔde Di Dwuma mu wɔ Arizona Sukuupɔn mu.
- **Suyash Bagad** – Cryptography Engineer (kan no na ɔwɔ Aztec, ɔyɛ adwuma wɔ Barretenberg nwomakorabea ne PLONK abusua a ɛyɛ adansedie nhyehyɛeɛ). Ɔwɔ BTech ne MTech abodin krataa wɔ IIT Bombay; nhwehwɛmu a wɔyɛe wɔ adesuafo a wɔawie no twee adwene sii adanse a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔakora nneɛma a wɔde asie so a ɛkora kokoam nsɛm so.

Ɔfa 1 no twe adwene si nhwehwɛmu a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no mu nhwehwɛmu, ahwehwɛ adansi a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔyɛ no mu de atoto Zcash ahobammɔ ne adwumayɛ ho ahwehwɛde ahorow ho, na wɔayɛ quantum akyi nsusuwii a ɛyɛ nokware. Wɔde wɔn ani asi so sɛ wɔde bedi dwuma a ɛyɛ krado sɛ wɔbɛyɛ nneɛma wɔ afe 2027 awiei.

## Ironwood Ntoboa a Wɔde Ma

Wɔ afe 2026 mu no, Shielded Labs dii dwuma titire wɔ abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom a wɔyɛɛ wɔn ade wɔ Orchard atoro a ɛyɛ mmerɛw no ho. Shielded Labs ahobanbɔ nhwehwɛmufoɔ Taylor Hornby na ɔhunuu mmerɛwyɛ no na ɔdaa no adi kyerɛɛ Zcash Open Development Lab (ZODL), a ɛne Zcash mmoa foforɔ yɛɛ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛfa ntɛmpɛ a wɔbɛsiesie ho.

Wɔ ntɛmpɛ a wosiesiee no akyi no, na mpɔtam hɔfo da so ara hia ɔkwan bi a wɔbɛfa so asan de ahotoso a ɛde ne ho aba Zcash's nneɛma a ɛredi akɔneaba no mu. Ironwood dii saa asɛm no ho dwuma denam ɔtare foforo a wɔabɔ ho ban a wɔde Orchard ɔmansin a wɔateɛteɛ no bedi dwuma ne Orchard ɔtare a edi kan no a wɔbɛtoto mu sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a sika betumi afi hɔ afa turnstile no nkutoo so. Shielded Labs ne Project Tachyon, Valar Group, ZODL, ne Zcash Foundation yɛɛ adwuma wɔ nhyehyɛɛ, nhwehwɛmu, ne abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom ahosiesie a wɔbɛyɛ ama Ironwood.

Saa adwuma yi daa Shielded Labs' dwumadie titire baako adi wɔ Zcash: ɛde ahofadiɛ protocol-nkɔsoɔ ne ahobanbɔ tumi kaa ho wɔ mfitiaseɛ Zcash nkɔsoɔ ahyehyɛdeɛ no akyi.

## Aban Adwuma

Shielded Labs nso tintim nnisoɔ ho nsɛm ma Zcash mpɔtam hɔfoɔ. Ne nniso krataafa no di protocol nniso abatow, ZCAP mu kyɛfa, sika a sika a wɔde di dwuma no kyerɛ kwan, ne abatow sika kotoku a wɔboa akyi. Eyi boa ma Zcash gyinaesi yɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobedi akyi ama wɔn a wɔwɔ sika, ZCAP mufo, ne mpɔtam hɔfo a wɔtrɛw mu.

Zcash mfa automatic on-chain governance nni dwuma mma protocol nsakrae. Abatoɔ ne mpɔtam hɔfoɔ adwene yɛ afotuo nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ a ɛma aduane kɔ nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛtrɛ a ɛfa adwene a ɛyɛ den ho no mu. Shielded Labs' nniso adwuma no boa ma wotumi nya saa nsɛnkyerɛnne no yiye na ɛnyɛ den sɛ wobegye atom.
