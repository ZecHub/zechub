# <img src="/content-images/198608b2-9059-4cb7-aeb8-9354971376fd-d67c2d46f5.webp" alt="ZODL Logo" width="50"/> ZODL (Zcash Open Development Lab)

[Wɛbsaet no](https://zodl.com/) - [GitHub, na woayɛ](https://github.com/AgoraCyber) - [X/Twitter so na woagye atom](https://x.com/zodl_app) - [Akasakasa](https://discord.gg/zodl)

## Asɛmpatrɛw Asɛm

ZODL yɛ wiase a wɔrenhwɛ sikasɛm so kɛse, baabi a nnipa a wodi mmara so betumi adi nkitaho wɔ ahofadi mu ne kokoam ho mfonini wɔ n’adwenem. Ahyehyɛde no gye di sɛ tumidi biara nni hɔ a kokoam nsɛm nni hɔ, na ɛsɛ sɛ sikasɛm mu kokoamsɛm yɛ nea wɔde di dwuma na gua so kwan a wɔfa so nya hokwan yɛ onipa.

## Akyigyina & Abakɔsɛm

ZODL (Zcash Open Development Lab) no, Josh Swihart, a na ɔyɛ Electric Coin Company (ECC) no kwankyerɛfoɔ na ɔde sii hɔ wɔ afe 2026 mfitiaseɛ. Wɔ Ɔpɛpɔn 2026 mu no, ECC mfiridwuma ne nneɛma a wɔyɛ no nyinaa gyaee adwuma wɔ nnisoɔ ho akasakasa bi a wɔne Bootstrap, 501(c)(3) adwumayɛkuo a wɔnyɛ adwuma a mfasoɔ wɔ so a ɛhwɛ ECC so no akyi. Kuw titiriw a mfiase no na wɔayɛ Zcash nhyehyɛe ahorow a ɛho hia sen biara no, de adi dwuma, na wɔahwɛ so no dan kɔɔ ZODL so sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔbɛtoa wɔn adwuma so wɔ wɔn ankasa mu.

Wɔsan de Zashi sika kotoku a kan no na wɔyɛe na wɔhwɛɛ so wɔ ECC ase no yɛɛ no ​​Zodl wɔ ahyehyɛde foforo no ase. Na ɛho nhia sɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no yɛ biribiara, efisɛ app no ​​san de ne din too so ankasa bere a wɔde update a edi hɔ no bae no.

## Sika a wɔde ma

Wɔ Oforisuo 2026 mu no, ZODL nyaa bɛboro dɔla ɔpepem 25 wɔ aba ho sika mu firii sikakorafoɔ akuo bi a wɔagye din hɔ, a nea ɛka ho ne:

- **Paradigm**
- **a16z crypto ** Ɔde ne nsa kyerɛɛ ne so.
- **Winklevoss Capital**
- **Coinbase Ventures**
- **Cypherpunk Technologies** ($5M sika a wɔde asie)
- **Chapter One**
- **David Friedberg** Ɔde ne nsa kyerɛɛ ne so.
- **Balaji Srinivasan** Ɔde ne nsa kyerɛɛ ne so.
- Abɔfo foforo a wɔde wɔn sika hyɛ mu a wofi crypto ne mfiridwuma mu

Wɔde ahenkuro no redi dwuma de atrɛw Zcash protocol ne Zodl sika kotoku no nyinaa nkɔso mu.

## Akannifoɔ

- **Josh Swihart** - CEO & Founder (kan CEO a ɔyɛ Electric Coin Company)

Kuw a ɛtrɛw no yɛ mfitiase Zcash protocol abɔfo ne wɔn a wɔyɛe a kan no na wɔkyekyee na wɔhwɛɛ Zcash so wɔ ECC no. Nea ɛka eyi ho ne nimdeɛ a emu dɔ wɔ cryptography mu, adanse a wonni nimdeɛ biara, ne protocol engineering.

## Nneɛma a wɔyɛ

### Zodl Sikakorabea (kan no na wɔfrɛ no Zashi) .

Zodl yɛ mobile sika kotoku a wɔde wɔn ankasa hwɛ a wɔayɛ ama kokoam Zcash nkitahodi. Ɛka nimdeɛ a emu dɔ a ɛwɔ protocol-level ne design a ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wɔde bedi dwuma bom, de nsɛm a ɛyɛ den sie bere a ɛkura ahobammɔ mu.

**Nneɛma a wɔde di dwuma:**
- [iOS (App Store) .](https://apps.apple.com/app/zodl/id6444974742)
- [Android (Agorudibea a Wɔde Di Dwuma)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.electriccoin.zcash)
- [F-Droid, na ɔde ne nsa kyerɛɛ ne so](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/co.electriccoin.zcash.foss/)
- [GitHub, na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://github.com/AgoraCyber)

**Nneɛma Titiriw:**

- **Shielded by Default:** Nkitahodi nyinaa de Zcash shielded pools di dwuma, de sender, receiver, ne transaction sika sie denam zero-knowledge cryptography so
- **One-Tap Shielding:** Dane ZEC a ɛda adi pefee kɔ ankorankoro sika mu denam tap biako so
- **CrossPay:** Send shielded ZEC bere a wɔn a wogye no nya wɔn agyapade a wɔpɛ (BTC, stablecoins, ne nea ɛkeka ho)
- **Private Swaps:** Built-in swaps a NEAR Intents na ɛma ahoɔden, kwati centralized exchanges
- **Keystone Integration:** Hardware sika kotoku mmoa ma awɔw mu akorae a shielded ZEC denam mframa-gapped asɛmdi nsaano nkyerɛwee so
- **Flexa Katua:** Fa ZEC di dwuma wɔ aguadidan akɛse a GameStop, Chipotle, Sheetz, ne Barnes & Noble ka ho
- **Coinbase Integration:** Kɔtɔ ZEC tẽẽ wɔ sika kotoku no mu a wonni nsakrae din a ɛyɛ soronko
- **Encrypted Messaging:** Zcash-level kokoamsɛm ma shielded memos a ɛka ho anaasɛ ɛde ne ho fi nkitahodi ho
- **Address a Wɔaka abom:** Address sohwɛ a wɔayɛ no mmerɛw wɔ Transparent, Sapling, ne Orchard pools nyinaa mu
- **Tor Support:** Wɔama network-level kokoamsɛm a ɛkɔ anim ama nkitahodi ho amanneɛbɔ

**Kokoamsɛm Ho Bɔhyɛ ahorow:**
- Wɔnni sika kotoku dwumadi, asɛm a ɛfa asɛm no ho abakɔsɛm, anaa nea ɔde di dwuma no nneyɛe akyi
- Wɔboaboa amanneɛbɔ a ɛfa akwanhyia ho a wɔmmɔ din nkutoo ano, a wontumi nkyekyere data mma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma pɔtee bi

### Adwumayɛ & Nkɛntɛnso

Efi bere a wɔde sii hɔ mfiase sɛ Zashi wɔ afe 2024 mu no, sika kotoku no ama nkɔso kɛse aba Zcash abɔde a nkwa wom nhyehyɛe no mu:

- Nyini Orchard shielded pool no firii bɛyɛ 1M ZEC kɔɔ 4M ZEC wɔ afe 2025 mu (bɛboro 400% nkɔanim)
- Wɔadi bɛboro dɔla ɔpepem 600 ho dwuma wɔ ZEC swaps mu fi October 2025

## Dwuma a edi wɔ Zcash Ecosystem no mu

ZODL di dwuma abien wɔ Zcash abɔde a nkwa wom nhyehyɛe mu:

1. **Protocol Development:** Kuo no kɔ so ma core Zcash protocol software ne nnwinnadeɛ nya nkɔsoɔ, na wɔne ecosystem akuo foforɔ te sɛ Zcash Foundation ne Zingo Labs yɛ adwuma
2. **Nneɛma a Wɔde Di Dwuma:** Wɔkyekye na wɔhwɛ so sɛ wɔbɛkyekyere adetɔfo sika kotoku titiriw ama Zcash, a wɔde wɔn adwene sii so sɛ wɔbɛma ankorankoro dijitaal sikatua ayɛ nea wɔn a wɔde di dwuma titiriw no betumi anya bi
3. **Shielded Pool Growth:** Wɔaka ZODL sika kotoku mu nneɛma foforo ho asɛm sɛ "ade kɛse biako pɛ a ɛde Zcash san ba asase mfonini no so".
4. **Open Source:** Adwuma nyinaa yɛ open-source, ɛma obiara tumi hwɛ protocol code, audit wallet no, na ɔhwɛ sɛ implementations no yɛ nokware

## Nyansapɛ: Ɔhene no Hwɛfo

ZODL nyansapɛ a ɛkyerɛ kwan no gyina adwene a ɛne "Ɔhene no Awɛmfo" so -- gyidi a ɛne sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ankorankoro kura wɔn sikasɛm mu kokoamsɛm ne wɔn tumidi so tumi koraa. Ahyehyɛde no hyɛ da de wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumidi di kan sen platform so tumidi, na ɛkyekye nnwinnade a ɛma ankorankoro tumi sen sɛ ɛbɛyɛ ntamgyinafo.
