# <img src="/content-images/ZCG-Circle-Logo-10-f41b8229ca.webp" alt="Alt Text" width="50"/>  Zcash Community Grants

## Asɛmpatrɛw Asɛm

Zcash Community Grants nhyehyeɛ no de sika ma akuo a wɔde wɔn ho a wɔhyɛn Zcash abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no mu, de yɛ nkɔsoɔ titire a ɛkɔ so (anaa adwuma foforɔ) ma ɔmanfoɔ yiedie wɔ Zcash abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no mu. 

## Nnipa ne Akuw ahorow

Boayikuw bi na ɛhwɛ Zcash Community Grants no so, a mprempren nnipa baanum na wɔwom. Saa mpɔtam yi hwɛ na wɔpene mmoa akwammisa a ɛfiri akuo a ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔkyekyere Zcash protocol no so. Nsɛm pii wɔ [Team](https://zcashcommunitygrants.org/committee/)

Nnansa yi nnwuma a ZCG apene so ne Zcash Shielded Assets (ZSA) a QEDIT kuw no di anim. DeFi a wode bɛba ZEC ne Zcash a ɛwɔ sikatua protocol foforo a ɛde nneɛma foforo bɛka Zcash mainnet no ho. 

Zcash Media's Short Documentary a ɛkyerɛ Zcashers a wɔda nsow te sɛ Edward Snowden, Zooko & Deirdre Connolly a atirimpɔw ne sɛ wɔbɛyɛ video ahorow a ɛfata, ɛyɛ anigye na ɛkyerɛkyerɛ a wɔbɛkyɛ kɛse. 

Hardware wallets integration of Ledger to fully support Sapling shielded transactions, eyi ma kwan ma wɔde Zcash sie kokoam & ahobammɔ. Zondax Kuo no na wɔayɛ eyi. 

An Easy one-click shielded payment ne Point-of-sale nhyehyɛe ma birikisi ne atɛkyɛ sotɔɔ SDK a ZGo reyɛ. 

Wiase Nyinaa Ɔmanpanyin nhyehyɛe no boa Zcash ma wonya nnipa pii wɔ amanaman ntam ne mpɔtam hɔ nhyiam ahorow a wɔyɛ no ankasa wɔ wiase nyinaa. 

Zcash Grants Committee no ahwɛ nnwuma foforɔ pii mu, hwɛ [Full, Updated List](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1FQ28rDCyRW0TiNxrm3rgD8ai2KGUsXAjPieQmI1kKKg/edit?gid=135980745#gid=135980745) a ɛfa nnwuma a wɔde akɔma ho.

## Ahyehyɛde Nhyehyɛe

Zcash Community Grants yɛ ahyehyɛdeɛ a ɛde ne ho a ɛfiri Electric Coin Company ne Zcash Foundation. 

## Sika a wɔde ma

Electric Coin Company no nya 8% wɔ [Zcash nkɔsoɔ sikakorabea](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1014). Wɔde saa sika yi boa nkɔsoɔ akuo a ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa na wɔde yɛ emu adwumayɛ nso.

## Akadeɛ

[Zcash nkɔsoɔ ne nnisoɔ](https://z.cash/zcash-development-and-governance/)

### Anamɔn 1: Siesie Wo Nsusuwii
Yɛ nyansahyɛ a emu da hɔ a nea ɛka ho ne:
- Dwumadie no asɛmti ne nea ɔrehwehwɛ adwuma no ho nsɛm
- Ɔhaw ho asɛm ne ano aduru a wɔahyɛ ho nyansa
- Nneɛma a wɔde bɛma, bere nhyehyɛe, ne nsɛntitiriw
- Budget ne sika a wɔde bɛma
- Osuahu ne asiane ahorow a ɛfata

---

### Anamɔn 2: Fa kɔ GitHub so
1. Kɔ **Zcash Community Grants GitHub akoraeɛ** .
2. Bue **Issues** tab no na klik **New Issue** so.
3. Paw **grant application template** a ɛfata.
4. Hyehyɛ mfuw a wɔhwehwɛ nyinaa na fa mena

GitHub asɛm no yɛ **official application record**.

---

### Anamɔn 3: Yɛ Community Forum Post
1. Kɔ **Zcash Community Forum** no so.
2. Yɛ thread foforo wɔ **Zcash Community Grants** category no mu
3. Fa asɛmti koro no ara a ɛwɔ GitHub nsɛmma nhoma no mu no di dwuma
4. Fa nsɛm tiawa bi ka ho na fa **link a ɛkɔ GitHub asɛm no so** ka ho.

---

### Anamɔn 4: Fa wo ho hyɛ Nhwehwɛmu mu
- Hwɛ GitHub asɛm no ne forum thread no so
- Bua nsɛmmisa ne nsɛm a wɔka fi ZCG Boayikuw ne mpɔtam hɔfo hɔ

---

### Anamɔn 5: Nea ebefi mu aba
- Nsusuwii ahorow a wɔapene so no kɔ so de sika ma ne amanneɛbɔ
- Nsusuwii a wɔmpene so **EBIA** wɔbɛsan ahwɛ mu na wɔasan de akɔma

---

**Hyɛ no nsow:** GitHub yɛ kyerɛwtohɔ no fibea; nhyiam no yɛ nea ɔmanfo bɛbɔ ho nkɔmmɔ.
