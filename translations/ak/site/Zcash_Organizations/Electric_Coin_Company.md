[Wɛbsaet no](https://electriccoin.co) - [Github a wɔde di dwuma](https://github.com/zodl-inc) - [X/Twitter na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://x.com/ElectricCoinCo)

> **Ɛfa saa GitHub link no ho.** ECC adekorabea ahorow no wɔ `zodl-inc`. Ɛno ne ahyehyɛde koro no ara, a wɔsesaa ne din bere a kuw no tu kɔɔ hɔ no [ZODL](/zcash-organizations/zodl). Dedaw no `github.com/Electric-Coin-Company` din no, obi a ɔne no nni abusuabɔ na ɔkyerɛw too hɔ wɔ May 2026 mu na ɛne ECC anaa ZODL nni hwee yɛ, enti nnye mmara a wɔatintim wɔ hɔ no nni.

## Asɛmpatrɛw Asɛm

Adwuma a ɛwɔ ECC ne sɛ wɔbɛma sikasɛm mu ahofadi anya tumi. Sikasɛm mu kokoamsɛm yɛ ade titiriw wɔ saa mmɔdenbɔ yi mu, na ɛho hia na wɔabɔ nnipa nidi, ahobammɔ, wɔn ankasa tumidi, ne pene a wɔpene so ho ban. Ɛsɛ sɛ sikasɛm mu kokoamsɛm a wubenya no yɛ mmerɛw te sɛ wo fie ntama a wobɛto mu no. Nanso bere a Web2 aba no, tumi no akɔ mfinimfini kɛse, na wɔn a wɔwɔ tumi no wɔ wo dan mu dedaw. Worentumi ntoto ntama no mu. Ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkyekye biribi foforo.
Saa sikasɛm mu nnwinnadeɛ nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ yi, a obiara bɛtumi anya no, bɛyɛ nea ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wɔde bedi dwuma, ɛyɛ kokoam de koraa a ɛwɔ akwan a wɔbɛfa so ada no adi, na wɔbɛkyekyɛ ne nnwuma a ɛboa no na ayɛ den.

## Zodl, a kan no na wɔfrɛ no Zashi: [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zashi-zcash-wallet/id1672392439) - [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.electriccoin.zcash) - [F-Droid a wɔde yɛ adwuma](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/co.electriccoin.zcash.foss/)

Wɔasi ama Ankorankoro Katua. Zashi yɛ ZEC sika kotokuo a wɔde wɔn ho abɔ ho ban a wɔde yɛ digyital nkitahodiɛ a wɔabɔ no kokoam, ECC na ɛsiiɛ na wɔyii no adi wɔ afe 2024 mu.

Wɔyɛɛ no ​​sɛ ɛbɛhyɛn nnamfo ne abusuafo akɔ Zcash ne kokoam dijitaal sikatua mu, a ɛwɔ interface a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a ɛbɛma nnipa a wonni kan crypto osuahu biara atumi asiesie sika kotoku, anya ZEC, na wɔafi ase adi nkitaho wɔ simma kakraa bi mu.

Wɔsesaa sika kotoku no din **Zodl** wɔ Ɔpɛpɔn 2026 mu bere a kuw no tu kɔɔ ZODL akyi. Na ɛho nhia sɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma dedaw no yɛ biribiara, sɛnea app no ​​rebranded wɔ ne update a edi hɔ no so, ɛno nti na store links a ɛwɔ soro no bue Zodl no. Mprempren wɔhwɛ so wɔ hɔ sen sɛ ECC bɛhwɛ so, enti hwɛ... [ZODL krataafa no](/zcash-organizations/zodl) sɛ wopɛ nsɛm a ɛwɔ hɔ mprempren.

Nkabom ahorow a wɔkyekyee wɔ ECC mfe no mu:
- [Coinbase a wɔde yɛ adwuma:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/coinbase-zashi-1-2-release/) crypto newbies betumi atɔ ZEC tẽẽ wɔ sika kotoku no mu skipping hassles of account registration, KYC, ne sika a woyi fi exchanges.

- [Flexa: 1 .](https://electriccoin.co/blog/zashi-flexa-integration-is-here-spend-zec-at-thousands-of-retailers/) ma ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wode ZEC bedi dwuma ankasa, na ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma foforo no tumi sɛe wɔ mmeae mpempem pii a wɔtɔn nneɛma te sɛ Barnes & Noble, GameStop, ne Chipotle.

- [Keystone:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/drumroll-please-introducing-zashi-keystone-hardware-wallet-integration-for-shielded-zec/) nkɔso kɛse ma Zcash mpɔtam hɔfo a wɔde mfe pii reyɛ. Saa nkabom yi hyɛ nkɔsoɔ kɛseɛ agyiraeɛ wɔ Zcash dwumadie mu denam ZEC a wɔabɔ ho ban no a ɛma wɔtumi de sie awɔw mu no so.

## Nhwehwɛmu ne Nkɔso

Kuw no adi nkɔso ahorow a wogye tom anim mpɛn pii, na nea edi kan ne... [Zcash a wɔde sii hɔ](https://electriccoin.co/blog/zcash-begins/) sɛ wiase no mu nea edi kan a wɔde Zero-Knowledge Proofs dii dwuma wɔ aguadi mu.

Nnipa pii a wodi akyi te sɛ nea [Sapling ntwamutam a wɔayɛ no foforo](https://electriccoin.co/blog/sapling-activation-complete/) a ɛma kwan ma wɔyɛ nkitahodi a emu yɛ hare, a wɔabɔ ho ban a bere a wɔtew so 90% ma nkitahodi a wɔkyekye, ne nkae a wɔtew so bɛboro 97%, a ɛma wotumi yɛ nkitahodi a wɔabɔ ho ban wɔ mobile device so nea edi kan.

Nkɔso a ɛho hia sen biara ne nea ɛbae nnansa yi ara. [Adebɔ ne nea wɔde di dwuma](https://electriccoin.co/blog/nu5-activates-on-mainnet-eliminating-trusted-setup-and-launching-a-new-era-for-zcash/) no ho halo2 ahotoso a enni adanse nhyehyɛe wɔ Network Upgrade 5 ma Zcash. Eyi yii nhyehyɛe a wogye di no fii hɔ na ɛde bere foforo sii hɔ maa Zcash. Efi afe 2022 no, eyi ayɛ adwuma wɔ Zcash Mainnet no so na ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi de adansi a ahobammɔ wom & ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wɔbɛkɔ so ayɛ no di dwuma ma wɔn sikatua nhyehyɛe. 

## Nkɔmmɔbɔ & Nkɔmmɔbɔ 

ECC kuw no boa na wɔboa Zcash abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no wɔ akwan pii so, wɔne emu & abɔnten akuo mmienu no nyinaa yɛ adwuma daa de boa ma wɔhwɛ na Zcash protocol no tu mpɔn. 

Nkɔmmɔbɔ no bi ne sɛ wɔne Gemini bɛyɛ adwuma de atumi ayɛ shielded transactions wɔ wɔn platform so, Flexa ama Zcash mobile payments a ɛnyɛ den wɔ retal mmeae, hardware wallet nnwumakuw te sɛ Keystone a ɛbɛma shielded cold storage of ZEC, Brave for Zcash support wɔ browser no mu, & akuw afoforo a wɔwɔ Zcash ecosystem no mu de nkɔso aba Zcash. 

ECC nso yɛ ahantan sponsor a [PGPforCrypto a wɔde kyerɛw nsɛm](https://x.com/pgpforcrypto) nhyiamu a ɛtoatoa so a ɛka adwumayɛfoɔ, wɔn a wɔn ani gye ho, ne abenfoɔ a wɔwɔ cryptocurrency ahunmu bom de susuw nkɔsoɔ ho, kyɛ nhumu, na ɛma mpɔtam hɔfoɔ de wɔn ho hyɛ mu. PGPforCrypto yɛ Board Member ne VP of Strategic Alliances wɔ Electric Coin Co, Paul Brigner na odi anim.

## Nhyehyɛe & Mmarahyɛ

ECCs nhumu ne akanni a ɛfa nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn so kokoamsɛm ne nea ɛkyerɛ ma ankorankoro ne ɔman ahobammɔ ho no som bo yiye ma nhyehyɛeyɛfo. Wɔabɔ mmɔden daa sɛ wɔbɛkyerɛ nhyehyɛe kwan sɛnea ɛfata wɔ baabiara a ɛbɛyɛ yiye fi bere a wɔde ECC sii hɔ no. 

Nhwɛsoɔ a ɛfa nkɔmmɔdie ho ne nkɔmmɔdie a wɔne Sikasɛm Dwumadie Adwumakuo (FATF) de wɔn ho hyɛɛ nhyiamu ahodoɔ mu a ɛde ahyehyɛdeɛ no nyansahyɛ maeɛ maa cryptocurrency adwumayɛkuo no. Esiane saa mmɔdenbɔ ahorow yi nti, New York Mantam Sikasɛm Dwumadibea (NYDFS) a ɛyɛ cryptocurrency biako pɛ a ɛbɔ kokoam nsɛm ho ban a ɛyɛɛ saa no penee Zcash so. 

## Ahyehyɛde Nhyehyɛe

Electric Coin Company no nyinaa yɛ Bootstrap Project no dea. Bootstrap Project yɛ 501(c)(3) a wɔde ama adesamma nkɔsoɔ denam mfiridwuma, nyansahu mu nnoɔma a wɔhunu, nwomasua ne nnipa nhyehyɛeɛ so. Bootstrap ne Electric Coin Company nyinaa wɔ akwankyerɛfo bagua, na Electric Coin Company nso wɔ Nyansahu mu Afotu Kuw.
