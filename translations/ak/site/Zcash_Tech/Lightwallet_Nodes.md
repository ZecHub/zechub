# Zcash Kanea a Wɔde Yɛ Adwuma

## Nnianimu

Zcash, cryptocurrency a ɛtwe adwene si kokoamsɛm so, boa ade bi a wɔfrɛ no "lightwallet nodes" a ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi ne Zcash blockchain no di nkitaho a wɔntwe blockchain abakɔsɛm nyinaa. Saa wiki krataafa yi ma lightwallet nodes ho nsusuiɛ, dwumadie a "lightwalletd" dwumadie no di wɔ Zcash ecosystem mu, lightwallet node servers a wɔahyehyɛ mprempren, ne akwankyerɛ a ɛfa sɛdeɛ wɔbɛsesa servers wɔ wallets a agye din te sɛ Ywallet ne Zingo mu.

## Lightwalletd Ɔsom a Wɔde Di Dwuma

"lightwalletd" dwumadie no, a ɛyɛ "lightwallet daemon" tiawa, di dwuma titire wɔ Zcash lightwallet node ecosystem mu. Ɛyɛ adwuma sɛ ntamgyinafo a ɔma afɛfo a wɔn mu yɛ hare (lightwallets) nsɛm a wohia na ama wɔatumi ayɛ adwuma yiye. Lightwalletd dwumadie no ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu tiawa nie:

__Data Aggregator__: Lightwalletd boaboa data ano firi Zcash blockchain, te sɛ atɔ ho nsɛm, block data, ne shielded pool ho nsɛm.

__Simplified Verification__: Lightwalletd yɛ simplified verification of this data, ma lightwallets tumi nya nsɛm a ɛho hia a enhia sɛ wɔgye blockchain no nyinaa di dwuma.

__Privacy Preservation__: Ɔsom no hwɛ Zcash dwumadiefoɔ kokoamsɛm so denam sɛ ɛnhwehwɛ sɛ wɔda wɔn hwɛbea anaa wɔn ankasa nkitahodi ho nsɛm adi.

__Efficient Synchronization__: Lightwalletd ma synchronization a etu mpɔn ma lightwallets tumi, ɛtew bere ne nneɛma a ɛho hia na ama woanya Zcash blockchain no foforo so kɛse.

## Servers a Wɔsesa wɔ Mobile Walets mu

Lightwallet node server no a wobɛsesa no yɛ mmerɛw koraa. Hwehwɛ na kɔ nhyehyɛe a ɛkɔ akyiri a ɛwɔ application no mu no so.

__Bue Ywallet/Zingo/Zashi/eZcash__: Fi ase wo sika kotoku a wopɛ wɔ wo mfiri no so.

## Awie

Zcash lightwallet nodes ne lightwalletd service no ma ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw na ɛkora kokoamsɛm so ma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no sɛ wɔne blockchain no di nkitaho. Tumi a wotumi sesa server ahorow no ma wotumi yɛ nsakrae wɔ node a ɛfata w’ahiade yiye a wobɛpaw mu.
