# Zcash Shielded Agyapadeɛ

Zcash Shielded Assets (ZSA) yɛ nkɔsoɔ a wɔahyɛ ho nyansa wɔ Zcash protocol no mu a ɛbɛma wɔatumi abɔ, de akɔ baabi foforɔ, na wɔahyew amanne kwan so agyapadeɛ wɔ Zcash nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no so.

Sɛ wunim [ERC-20](https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/standards/tokens/erc-20/) token gyinapɛn wɔ Ethereum blockchain no so, ZSAs yɛ Zcash sɛnea ERC-20 token yɛ Ethereum.

Zcash Shielded Assets bɛma wɔatumi ayɛ amanne token wɔ Zcash blockchain no so, na ɛnam so ama token a ɛnyɛ [ZEC](https://wiki.zechub.xyz/using-zec-privately) sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wobenya mfaso afi nsɛm a wɔmmɔ ne kokoam nsɛm a wɔabɔ ho ban wɔ Zcash blockchain no so no mu.

Ade titiriw a ebetumi de ZSA ahorow adi dwuma bɛyɛ stablecoins a wɔde bɛma wɔ Zcash protocol no so. Stablecoins yɛ cryptocurrencies a ɛde wɔn boɔ to fiat sika so, te sɛ US Dollar anaa Euro. Mprempren, stablecoins a wɔkyekyɛ no kɛse no bi ne ERC-20 tokens te sɛ [USDC](https://www.circle.com/en/usdc) ne [Dai](https://docs.makerdao.com/).

Ade foforo a wobetumi de ZSA ahorow adi dwuma ne sɛ wɔde nniso token bɛma. Sɛ nhwɛsoɔ no, Zechub (wiki yi tintimfoɔ) yɛ Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO) na ɔbɛtumi ayɛ na ɔde ZSA ama ne mufoɔ a atirimpɔw ne sɛ wɔbɛto aba wɔ nsusuiɛ ne nnisoɔ gyinaesie ho.

ZSA ahorow no, [QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/), wɔ mmoa kɛse bi a efi [Zcash Foundation](https://wiki.zechub.xyz/zcash-foundation) a wɔne [Electric Coin Company](https://wiki.zechub.xyz/electric-coin-company). Bere a wɔda so ara reyɛ saa adwuma yi denneennen no, wɔde nsɛm foforo gu [saa thread yi so](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-update-zcash-shielded-assets-monthly-updates/41153) a ɛwɔ Zcash forum no mu. [ZSA mmoa akwammisa krataa no](https://zcashgrants.org/gallery/25215916-53ea-4041-a3b2-6d00c487917d/33106640/) denam QEDIT so no, wobetumi anya afi Zcash Foundation mmoa wɛbsaet hɔ.

## ZSA Mmoa Ho Nsusuwii

ZSA nyansahyɛ a ɛfa Shielded Assets (ZSA/UDA) ho no, [QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/) kuw a wɔbɛkyekyere generic shielded agyapade wɔ Zcash blockchain no so. Wɔtaa frɛ eyinom sɛ User Defined Assets (UDA) anaa Zcash Shielded Assets (ZSA).

Saa nyansahyɛ yi nti, kuw a wɔwɔ [QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/) nhyehyeɛ sɛ wɔde DeFi bɛba Zcash abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ no mu, na berɛ korɔ no ara mu no, wɔama wɔatumi de kokoamsɛm mfiridwuma a ɛyɛ papa adi dwuma wɔ DeFi abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ dada no mu wɔ pool nhwehwɛmu bi ho, baabi a ekuo no bisae, na mpɔtam hɔfoɔ buaa sɛ: [generic shielded assets (ZSA/UDA) ne adeɛ a wɔbisa paa seesei](https://twitter.com/BenarrochDaniel/status/1428327864034791429)

Saa nsusuwii ahorow yi di [Zcash Improvement Proposal (ZIP) .](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0000) nkyerɛkyerɛmu na wɔakyerɛkyerɛ mu wɔ ZIP 226 & ZIP 227 mu.

1. [ZIP 226 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://qed-it.github.io/zips/zip-0226): Zcash Shielded Assets a wɔde kɔ baabi foforo ne nea wɔhyew
2. [ZIP 227 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://qed-it.github.io/zips/zip-0227): Zcash Shielded Assets a wɔde ma
