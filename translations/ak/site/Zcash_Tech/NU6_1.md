# NU6.1

> NU6.1 kɔɔ so traa ase wɔ Zcash mainnet so wɔ block 3,146,400 (November 24, 2025 UTC).

Nea wobɛfa: sɛnea Zcash tua n’ankasa nkɔso ho ka a adwumakuw biara nni so, ne sɛnea NU6.1 maa mpɔtam hɔfo ne wɔn a wɔwɔ sika no nyaa asɛm tẽẽ wɔ saa sika no mu.

NU6.1 yɛ Zcash [network upgrade a wɔde yɛ adwuma](../start-here/network-upgrades), a wɔakyerɛkyerɛ mu wɔ [ZIP 255 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0255), a ɛde sikasɛm ne nniso nhyehyɛe foforo sii hɔ maa Zcash nkɔso. Ɛsi baabi a block akatua no fã bi kɔ ne nea ɔhwɛ so ho gyinae, na ɛsan nso de mfiridwuma mu nsiesie nketenkete kakraa bi kɔe. NU6.1 no, Electric Coin Company ne Zcash Foundation nyinaa na wɔpenee so.

Nea enti a eyi ho hia. Software dodow no ara yɛ nea adwumakuw bi tua ho ka. Zcash yɛ decentralized network a enni owura baako, enti ɛhyia asɛmmisa a ɛyɛ den: ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na ɛde sika ama nnipa a wɔkɔ so si no, wɔ ɔkwan bi so a mpɔtam hɔfoɔ de wɔn ho to so? NU6.1 yɛ Zcash mmuaeɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ mprempren. Ɛde block biara akatua no fã bi kɔ atare abien mu, biako ma mpɔtam hɔ mmoa ne biako a sika wuranom ankasa di so.

![The NU6.1 funding split: most of each block reward goes to miners, 8 percent to Zcash Community Grants, and 12 percent to a coin-holder-controlled fund](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-1-funding-split.png)

## Sika a wɔde ma ho nhwɛso no

NU6.1 kyekyɛ block akatua biara mu abiɛsa.

1. Kyɛfa kɛse, bɛyɛ ɔha biara mu nkyem 80, kɔ wɔn a wotu fam a wɔbɔ nkitahodi nhyehyɛe no ho ban no nkyɛn.
2. Ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 8 kɔ Zcash Community Grants (ZCG), a sika a wɔde ma mpɔtam hɔfoɔ na wɔyɛ adwuma ma wɔn.
3. ɔha biara mu nkyem 12 kɔ sikakorabea foforo a sika wuranom di so, a Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox na ɛde aba.

Wɔakyerɛkyerɛ saa nhwɛsoɔ yi mmara mu wɔ [ZIP 1016 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1016), na wɔtena hɔ kɔsi Zcash fã a ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa, a ɛma mpɔtam hɔfoɔ nya berɛ hwɛ sɛdeɛ ɛyɛ adwuma ansa na wɔayɛ no daa anaa wɔasesa.

## Hena na odi sika-kura sikakorabea no so

Asuten a ɛyɛ ɔha biara mu nkyem 12 no ne adwene foforo no. Sɛ́ anka adwumakuw anaa fapem bi besi sɛnea wɔde bedi dwuma ho gyinae no, wɔn a wɔwɔ sika no bom si gyinae sɛ wobeyi saa sika no adi anaasɛ wobekura mu.

1. Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox a wɔyɛɛ no ​​wɔ NU6 nkɔsoɔ a atwam no mu no, na ɛgu saa sikakorabea yi.
2. Wɔn a wɔwɔ sika no bom si gyinae sɛ wɔbɛkyekyɛ sika no ama abɔde a nkwa wom ho adwuma anaasɛ wɔbɛto mu.
3. Eyi ma nnipa a wokura ZEC no nya nne tẽẽ wɔ sɛnea Zcash tua ne daakye ho ka.

![The NU6.1 governance flow: the Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox seeds the coin-holder fund, and coin holders decide whether to distribute grants or hold the funds](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-1-governance.png)

## Nsiesiei nketewa no

NU6.1 nso tew mfiridwuma mu nneɛma abien ho.

1. Ansa na NU6.1 reba no, na RPC akwan bi nhu sika a wɔayiyi afiri Orchard pool no mu yie, enti sika a aka ne sika a wɔtua no betumi ayɛ sɛ ɛnteɛ wɔ sika kotokuo ne nhwehwɛmufoɔ mu. NU6.1 siesiee saa asɛm no.
2. Ɛsesaa zcashd sika kotoku default no sɛ ɛbɛkyekyere version 5 nkitahodi, format a wodii kan de bae wɔ NU5 mu, sen sɛ ɛbɛyɛ version 4. Version 4 nkitahodi no tra hɔ.

## Sɛnea Zcash sika a wɔde ma no nyaa nkɔso

NU6.1 yɛ anammɔn baako wɔ asɛm tenten bi a ɛfa sɛdeɛ Zcash tua n’ankasa nkɔsoɔ ho ka.

1. Canopy (2020) de mfitiaseɛ founders akatua no baa awieeɛ na ɔbɔɔ a [nkɔso sikakorabea](../start-here/development-fund).
2. NU6 (2024) hyehyɛɛ Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox, de kyɛfa a wɔde bɛma no siee maa daakye mmoa a mpɔtam hɔfoɔ asi ho gyinaeɛ.
3. NU6.1 (2025) buaa asɛmmisa a NU6 gyaw no, hena na ɔsi gyinae ne ɔkwan a ɔfa so yɛ, denam block akatua no ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 8 a wɔtoa so kɔ mpɔtam hɔ mmoa so na ɔkyerɛ ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu 12 kwan kɔ sikakorabea a sikakurafoɔ di so a saa sika a wɔde asie no seeded mu.

![How Zcash funding evolved: Canopy created the development fund, NU6 set up the lockbox, and NU6.1 set the rules for who controls it](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-1-funding-history.png)

## FAQ

So NU6.1 sesa me ZEC anaa me kokoam nsɛm? Dabi NU6.1 fa sɛnea wɔde sika boa nkɔso ho, na ɛnyɛ nkitahodi anaa kokoam nsɛm ho. Wo sika ne nnwuma a wɔabɔ ho ban no nnya nkɛntɛnso biara.

Ɛhe na sika a wɔde boa no fi? Efi block akatua so no, ZEC foforo a wɔde ma sɛnea wɔtu blocks no. Wɔde kyɛfa bi kɔ grant ne coin-holder sika mu sen sɛ ne nyinaa bɛkɔ akɔma wɔn a wotu fagude.

Dɛn na sika a wɔde kura sika no yɛ? Ecosystem grants sɛ sikakorafoɔ bom paw sɛ wɔde sika bɛto mu, anaasɛ sɛ wɔpɛ sɛ wɔtwɛn a, wɔbɛkura.

So eyi yɛ nea ɛtra hɔ daa? Dabi, nhwɛso no kɔ so kosi fã a ɛto so abiɛsa no so, na afei mpɔtam hɔfo betumi de asie, asesa, anaasɛ wɔde asi ananmu.

Ɔkwan bɛn so na ɛsono NU6.1 wɔ NU6 ho? NU6 na ɔyɛɛ lockbox no. NU6.1 de mmara a ɛfa deɛ ɔdi so ne sɛdeɛ wɔkyekyɛ sika no mu.

## Sɔ wo ntease hwɛ

NU6 de sika pii siee lockbox mu nanso ogyaw asɛmmisa kɛse biako a wɔamma ho mmuae. Na ɛyɛ dɛn, na ɔkwan bɛn so na NU6.1 buaa no?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Na asɛmmisa a wɔabue ano ne hena na osi sɛnea wɔde lockbox sika no bedi dwuma no ho gyinae. NU6.1 buaa no denam sika no a wɔkyekyɛɛ mu no so: ɔha biara mu nkyem 8 de kɔmaa Zcash Community Grants, na ɔha biara mu nkyem 12 de kɔmaa sikakorabea bi a sika wuranom ankasa di so, a wɔpaw sɛ wɔbɛkyekyɛ anaasɛ wɔbɛkura mu.
</details>
