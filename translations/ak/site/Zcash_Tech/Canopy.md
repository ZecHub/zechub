# Canopy a wɔde yɛ adwuma

> Canopy kɔɔ live wɔ Zcash mainnet so wɔ block 1,046,400 (November 18, 2020 UTC).

Nea wobɛfa: sɛnea Zcash kɔɔ so de sika boaa n’ankasa nkɔso bere a founders akatua no baa awiei, ne sɛnea Canopy hyehyɛɛ sikasɛm mu mpaapaemu a akyiri yi nkɔso da so ara si so.

Canopy yɛ Zcash network upgrade a ɛtɔ so anum, a wɔsan frɛ no Network Upgrade 4 (NU4). Wɔde di dwuma denam [ZIP 251 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0251), na ɛyɛɛ adwuma wɔ mainnet block 1,046,400 wɔ November 18, 2020 (UTC), bere koro no ara a Zcash block akatua a edi kan no yɛɛ fã. Ná Canopy yɛ nniso ne sikasɛm mu nkɔso titiriw. Ɛde mfitiaseɛ a wɔhyehyɛɛ no ​​akatua no baa awieeɛ na ɛhyɛɛ Zcash Development Fund foforɔ a ɛtua Electric Coin Company, Zcash Foundation, ne wɔn a wɔgye mmoa a wɔde wɔn ho no ka. Nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛwɔ saa sikakorabea no akyi no firii mpɔtam nnisoɔ nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔatrɛ mu wɔ afe 2019 mu.

Nea enti a eyi ho hia. Zcash de sika ma n’ankasa nkɔso fi block akatua mu, efisɛ enni adwumakuw biara wɔ n’akyi. Ná wɔahyɛ sɛ wɔn a wɔhyehyɛɛ no ​​akatua a wotuae wɔ ne mfiase mfe no ho no bɛba awiei wɔ fã a edi kan no mu. Canopy na wɔde sii ananmu: ɛde block akatua biara mu kyɛfa pɔtee bi kɔɔ Nkɔso Foto mu na ɛkyerɛɛ nea obenya. Wɔnam nkɔso a wɔyɛe akyiri yi so yɛɛ saa mfonini no yiye, kosii sɛ [NU6.1](../zcash-tech/nu6-1).

![Before Canopy the founders reward funded development and was set to end at the first halving. After Canopy the Development Fund takes 20 percent of each block reward and runs to the second halving in 2024](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/canopy-founders-to-devfund.png)

## Nkɔso sikakorabea no

Canopy de mfitiaseɛ founders akatua no baa awieeɛ na ɔde Zcash Development Fund sii ananmu. Nsakraeɛ no sii fam wɔ block korɔ no ara so sɛ Zcash fã a ɛdi kan no, berɛ a block akatua no kɔɔ fam firii 6.25 ZEC kɔɔ 3.125 ZEC. Enti wɔn a wotu fam huu sɛ wɔatwa wɔn akatua no mu abien da koro no ara a saa akatua ketewaa no fã foforo fii ase sen kɔɔ nkɔso mu no.

Na wɔahyɛ sɛ sikakorabea no bɛyɛ adwuma mfeɛ nnan, ɛfiri saa fã a ɛdi kan yi wɔ November 2020 mu kɔsi fã a ɛtɔ so mmienu wɔ afe 2024. Wɔkyerɛw nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔpenee so no sɛ [ZIP 1014 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1014). Mfiri a wɔpene so a ɛde sika no kɔ ankasa ne sika a wɔde ma no kwan: [ZIP 207 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0207) de ɔkwan a wɔfa so de block subsidy no fã bi kyerɛ wɔn a wɔakyerɛkyerɛ mu sɛ wobenya no bae, na [ZIP 214 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0214) hyehyɛ mmara pɔtee ne wɔn a wɔbɛgye no address ama Nkɔsoɔ Fotoɔ no.

## Sɛnea wɔkyekyɛ sika no mu

Development Fund no gye block biara akatua ɔha biara mu nkyem 20. Wɔn a wotu fam no sie ɔha biara mu nkyem 80 a aka no. Afei wɔkyekyɛ saa ɔha biara mu nkyem 20 no mu akwan abiɛsa, di ZIP 1014 akyi.

1. ɔha biara mu 35 de maa Bootstrap Project, Electric Coin Company no awofo ahyehyɛde no.
2. ɔha biara mu nkyem 25 de bɛma Zcash Foundation.
3. ɔha biara mu nkyem 40 ma Major Grants a ɛma wonya sika de yɛ adwuma a wɔde wɔn ho yɛ na Zcash Foundation na ɛhwɛ so. Akyiri yi Mmoa Kɛseɛ bɛyɛɛ Zcash Community Grants (ZCG).

Sɛ wɔde toto block akatua no nyinaa ho sen sɛ wɔde sikakorabea no nkutoo susuw ho a, saa kyɛfa no yɛ adwuma kodu ɔha biara mu nkyem 7 ma Electric Coin Company, ɔha biara mu nkyem 5 ma Zcash Foundation, ne ɔha biara mu nkyem 8 ma Major Grants. Akwan abien no nyinaa a wɔfa so kyerɛkyerɛ mu no yɛ akontaahyɛde koro.

![The Development Fund is 20 percent of each block reward, split 35 percent to Bootstrap and the Electric Coin Company, 25 percent to the Zcash Foundation, and 40 percent to Major Grants](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/canopy-dev-fund-split.png)

## Sprout pool no sesa

Canopy nso fii ase gyaee ɔtare a akyɛ sen biara a wɔabɔ ho ban no. Sprout ne Zcash ɔtare a edi kan a wɔabɔ ho ban, na Canopy fii ase twitwaa mu faa mu [ZIP 211 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0211).

Efi bere a Canopy yɛɛ adwuma no, wontumi mfa botae foforo biara nka Sprout pool no ho. Wɔ mfiridwuma mu no, ɛsɛ sɛ vpub_old field a ɛwɔ JoinSplit biara mu no yɛ zero. Sika a ɛwɔ Sprout dedaw no da so ara tumi yi, enti wɔmfa obiara nkɔ, nanso ɔtare no betumi atew afi ha nkutoo. Eyi yɛ anammɔn a edi kan a ɛbɛma awiei koraa no wɔbɛpoo Sprout ɔtare a ɛyɛ agyapade no de agye atare foforo a wɔabɔ ho ban no atom.

![Before Canopy, value could both enter and leave the Sprout pool. After Canopy, no new value can enter but withdrawals are still allowed](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/canopy-sprout-pool.png)

## Mfiridwuma mu nneɛma a ɛka ho no

Wɔ sikasɛm mu nsakrae no akyi no, Canopy de mfiridwuma mu ZIP nketewa abien kuraa. [ZIP 212 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0212) sesaa sɛnea obi a ogye no nya Sapling bere tiaa mu ahintasɛm no, na onya fii nsɛm a wɔakyerɛw no pefee mu. [ZIP 215 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0215) kyerɛw mmara a ɛda adi pefee a ɛbɛma Ed25519 nsaano nkyerɛwee no ayɛ nokware, enti node biara pene so wɔ nsaano nkyerɛwee a wɔkan no sɛ ɛyɛ nokware no ho.

## FAQ

So Canopy sesa me ZEC anaa me kokoam nsɛm? Dabi, Canopy fa sɛnea wɔde sika boa nkɔso ho, ne mfiridwuma ho mmara kakraa bi. Wo sika a aka ne wo sika a wɔabɔ ho ban no nnya nkɛntɛnso biara.

So Canopy twaa block akatua no mu? Canopy yɛɛ adwuma wɔ block koro no ara so sɛ Zcash fã a edi kan no, a ɛtew akatua no so fii 6.25 ZEC kɔɔ 3.125 ZEC. Nneɛma a wɔatew so no yɛ Zcash sikasɛm nhyehyɛe no fã. Na Canopy adwuma ne sɛ obesi sɛnea wɔde saa akatua ketewaa no mu kyɛfa bedi dwuma no ho gyinae.

Dɛn na Nkɔso Foto no yɛ? Ɛma nnipa a wɔresi Zcash no sika. Sika no kɔ Electric Coin Company (ɛnam Bootstrap Project so), Zcash Foundation, ne Major Grants, a ɛboa adwuma a ɛde ne ho.

So metumi da so ara de sika adi dwuma wɔ Sprout pool no mu? Aane. Woda so ara betumi ayi sika a ɛwɔ Sprout dedaw no. Worentumi mfa mfaso foforo nka ho kɛkɛ wɔ Canopy akyi.

So Nkɔso Foto no yɛ nea ɛtra hɔ daa? Dabi, na wɔahyɛ sɛ ɛbɛkɔ so mfeɛ nnan, ɛfiri fã a ɛdi kan wɔ November 2020 mu kɔsi fã a ɛtɔ so mmienu wɔ afe 2024 mu, na ɛbɛma mpɔtam hɔfoɔ anya berɛ ahwɛ sɛdeɛ ɛyɛ adwuma ansa na wɔasan akɔsra.

Ɔkwan bɛn so na Canopy ne NU6 ne NU6.1 wɔ abusuabɔ? Canopy hyehyɛɛ sikasɛm mu mpaapaemu a ɛfa akwan abiɛsa so ne sika a wɔde ma no mfiri. Akyiri yi nkɔsoɔ a NU6 ne NU6.1 ka ho no san kɔɔ Nkɔsoɔ Fotoɔ a wɔsii wɔ saa fapem no atifi no so na wɔsan yɛɛ no ​​foforɔ.

## Sɔ wo ntease hwɛ

Canopy yɛɛ adwuma wɔ block koro no ara pɛpɛɛpɛ sɛ Zcash fã a edi kan no. Dɛn nti na wɔpaw saa bere no, na sɛ ɛnyɛ Canopy a, anka ɛbɛba nkɔso ho sikasɛm so?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Ná wɔayɛ nhyehyɛe sɛ mfitiasefo a wɔhyehyɛɛ no ​​akatua no bɛba awiei wɔ fã a edi kan no mu. Sɛ ɛnyɛ Canopy a, anka akatua nketewa a wɔde bɛma wɔ block akyi fã akyi no nyinaa bɛkɔ ama wɔn a wotu fagude, na ɛremma sika biara nni hɔ a wɔde bɛma wɔ protocol-level a wɔde bɛyɛ nkɔso. Canopy de Development Fund no sii wɔn a wɔhyehyɛɛ no ​​no akatua ananmu wɔ saa block no pɛpɛɛpɛ, enti sika a wɔde mae no kɔɔ so a na nsonsonoe biara nni mu.
</details>
