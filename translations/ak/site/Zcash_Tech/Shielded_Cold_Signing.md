# Nsɛnkyerɛnne a Ɛyɛ Nwini a Wɔabɔ Ho Ban

> **Adwene titiriw:** Ɔhokafo sika kotoku no hu biribiara a ehia na wɔde akyekye na wɔahwɛ asɛm a wɔabɔ ho ban so. Hardware sika kotoku no nkutoo na ɛwɔ tumi a ɛma kwan ma wɔsɛe sika. Saa mpaapaemu no ne ade titiriw a ɛwɔ shielded cold signing mu.

Shielding wɔ Zcash mu no hwehwɛ sɛ wode sika fi ɔmanfo address a ɛda adi pefee so kɔ kokoam address a wɔabɔ ho ban mu, de atɔ ho nsɛm sie denam Zcash's kokoam nsɛm so.

### Dɛn na ɛsakra bere a wobɔ ZEC?

Sɛ wowɔ Zcash asɛm a ɛda adi pefee a, nsɛm te sɛ:

- address a ɔde kɔmaa no
- address a nea wogye no
- sika dodow a wɔde kɔ baabi foforo

wobetumi ahu wɔ blockchain no so.

Sɛ wobɔ ZEC, wo sika no kɔ ankorankoro ɔtare bi mu (te sɛ Ironwood). Blockchain no betumi ahwɛ sɛ asɛm bi yɛ nokware a ɛnda wo kokoam asɛm no ho nsɛm adi. Efi bere a wɔyɛɛ NU6.3 no foforo wɔ 28 July 2026 no, sika a wɔabɔ ho ban foforo no kɔ Ironwood pool no mu efisɛ Orchard pool dedaw no nnye sika foforo a wɔde asie bio nanso ɛda so ara ma kwan ma woyi sika.

### Components Of A Shielded Aguadi

### 1. Nsɛm a Wɔakyerɛw (The Shielded Balance Record)

Nkyerɛwde yɛ kokoam, cryptographic kyerɛwtohɔ a egyina hɔ ma ZEC sika a wɔansɛe no a obi a ɔde di dwuma wɔ.

Nea ɛnte sɛ ɔmanfo blockchain, baabi a wɔakyerɛw sika a aka no wɔ nsɛm a emu da hɔ mu no, krataa a wɔabɔ ho ban de sie bo ne ne wurayɛ ho nsɛm sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔn a wɔhwɛ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn so no rentumi nhu onii a ɔwɔ sika no.

Sɛ worefi ase ayɛ asɛm bi a, sika kotoku a ɛka ho no hwehwɛ ledger no mu, hu nea ɔde di dwuma no nsɛm a wansɛee no, na ɛpaw nkrataa a ɔde bedi dwuma.

### 2. Nullifier (Afiri a Wɔde Hu Nneɛma a Wɔde Di Dwuma Mmienu)

Esiane sɛ wɔde krataa bi ho nsɛm asie ɔmanfo nhomakorabea nti, nkitahodi nhyehyɛe no hia adwinnade bi a ɛbɛma wɔahu sika a wɔsɛe no mmɔho abien a wɔrenkyerɛ krataa ko a wɔresɛe no.

Nullifier yɛ cryptographic marker soronko a wonya fi nkyerɛwde pɔtee bi mu. Sɛ wɔsɛe note bi a, wɔtintim ne nullifier no kɔ blockchain no so.

Netwɛk no hwɛ sɛ wɔankyerɛw nullifier no kan. Sɛ ɛnyɛɛ saa a, wobetumi agye sika a wɔsɛee no atom. Nea ɛho hia titiriw no, obi a ɔhwɛ abɔnten so ntumi mfa nullifier no nni dwuma mfa nhu nkyerɛwde pɔtee a efi mu bae.

Ɔhokafoɔ sika kotokuo no nya na ɛyɛ nullifier a ɛfata sɛ ayɔnkofa data a wɔboaboa ano no fã.

## Safoa ahorow

Keys yɛ nsɛm a ɛyɛ cryptographic a ɛhwɛ kwan a wobɛfa so akɔ wo sika so na ɛma wo sika kotoku no yɛ adwuma te sɛ ZEC.

Sika kotoku kura safoa ahorow pii, na emu biara di atirimpɔw soronko bi ho dwuma, te sɛ sɛnea akontaabu biako betumi anya adansedi nkrataa a ɛsono emu biara ama akenkan kwan a wɔfa so kenkan kwan ne akyerɛw kwan.

Safe yɛ ade titiriw wɔ abusuabɔ a ɛda ɔhokafo sika kotoku ne hardware sika kotoku ntam, efisɛ safe ahorow di atirimpɔw ahorow ho dwuma, na emu biako pɛ na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔto mu.

Ɛho hia sɛ wuhu sɛ ɔtare biara a wɔabɔ ho ban no si ne nsafe sɛ dua a efi mu ba, baabi a agyan kyerɛ fi ade bi so kɔ biribiara a wobetumi anya afi mu so. Ɛsɛ sɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma a wɔpɛ sɛ wonya sikatua a wɔabɔ ho ban no nya sikatua address a wɔabɔ ho ban, a wonya fi sika a wɔsɛe no safe mu.

### Nneɛma titiriw a wɔde di dwuma wɔ ɔkwan a ɛkɔ soro so

**1.1. Sika a wɔsɛe no key**

Eyi ne safe a ɛfa ahobammɔ ho sen biara. Ɛma cryptographic tumi a ɛma kwan ma wɔsɛe sika wɔ ZEC.

Eyi ne safoa a wɔka ho asɛm bere biara a "hardware sika kotoku no kura safoa no" bɛba baabi foforo wɔ amanneɛbɔ yi mu.

Wɔ awɔw mu signing architecture mu no, wɔde sika a wɔsɛe no safe no sie hardware sika kotoku no mu na wɔmfa nkyerɛ ɔhokafo sika kotoku no da. Ɛnsɛ sɛ ɛho hia sɛ ɔhokafo sika kotoku no nya saa safe yi koraa.

Wɔ Sapling ne Orchard, nhyehyɛe atitiriw no nya tumi a wɔde di dwuma ne tumi a wɔde hwɛ sɛ nkorabata abien a ɛsono emu biara fi sika a wɔsɛe no ntini mu:

- **Sapling:** Safoa a wɔatrɛw mu a wɔde di dwuma wɔ sika a wɔsɛe no mu (nx,nx,nx) nya safoa a ɛma adanseɛ tumi krataa (n,nn) ne safe a wɔde hwɛ ade a edi mũ (n, θθ, θθ), a ɛsan ma wonya safe a wɔde hwɛ nneɛma a ɛba (nn) ne address ahorow a ɛsono emu biara.
- **Orchard:** Ntini a wɔde di dwuma ɔkwammɔne so safoa (nx) no nkorabata tẽẽ kɔ safoa a ɛma sika a wɔde di dwuma ho tumi krataa (nx) ne safoa a wɔde hwɛ ade a edi mũ (nx,nx,nx) mu.

Saa nhyehyeɛ mu mpaapaemu yi ma cryptographic fapem a ɛyɛ mmara kwan so ma hardware safoa isolation a ɛfa sika kotokuo no a ɛde viewing branch (UFVK) kɔ host kɔmputa no so ma balance synchronization ne transaction building, berɛ a ɛma spend-authorizing branch no kɔ so to device so daa.

Key derivation yɛ ɔkwan biako so katee. Eyi kyerɛ sɛ wontumi nsakra safe a wɔde hwɛ ade no mfa nsan nkyekye tumi a wɔde di dwuma wɔ sika a wɔsɛe no mu. Cryptographic a wontumi nsakra no ma Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK) a wɔde kɔ amannɔne no yɛ ahobammɔ efisɛ ɛma wotumi hu sika kotoku no koraa a ɛnda ahintasɛm a ɛfa sika a wɔsɛe no ho adi wɔ mfiri so.

**2.1. Nsafoa a wɔhwɛ**

Zcash tetew tumi a wɔde hu dwumadi a wɔabɔ ho ban no fi tumi a wɔde di dwuma no ho. Safoa a wɔde hwɛ nneɛma ma sika kotoku anaa obi foforo a wɔama no tumi hwɛ nsɛm a ɛfa nnwuma a wɔabɔ ho ban ho a ɛmma tumi sɛ wɔmfa sika nni dwuma.

- **Full Viewing Key (FVK), a wɔabɔ no sɛ Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK):** wɔahyɛ da anya afi sika a wɔsɛe no safoa no mu, ɛma software hu na ɛkyerɛkyerɛ sika kotoku no dwumadi a wɔabɔ ho ban no mu, na ɛnam so ahu nnwuma a wɔayɛ. Ɛma ɛsan nso bu sika a aka a entumi mma kwan sɛ ɔsɛe sika. Wɔka ho asɛm yiye sɛ ɛnyɛ kokoam ade a wonya fi sika a wɔsɛe no safe no mu, na ɛnyɛ aba no bi a ɛyɛ mmerɛw.

Full Viewing Key (FVK) no ma wotumi hu akenkan nkutoo bere a wokura ahobammɔ mu mpaapaemu a emu yɛ den mu. Ɛdenam decrypting incoming ne outgoing note ciphertexts (ɛnam ne outgoing-viewing-key component so) so no, FVK-only companion wallet betumi akyerɛ asɛmti abakɔsɛm a edi mũ, a wɔakyerɛw so sen sɛ ɛbɛyɛ running balance kɛkɛ.

Address ahorow pii a wɔde tua ka a ɛsono emu biara a wontumi nka ho no kyɛ FVK ne incoming viewing key (IVK) koro, na ɛma kwan ma wɔyɛ address awo ntoatoaso a anohyeto nni mu a ɛnma blockchain scanning overhead nkɔ soro.

Saa dwumadie mu mpaepaemu yi na ɛyɛ adwuma a wɔde wɔn nsa hyɛ aseɛ a ɛyɛ nwini no fapem. Wɔde nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn scanning a ɛkɔ so, abakɔsɛm akyi di, ne address sohwɛ nyinaa gu host software so, bere a tumi a wɔde di dwuma no da so ara tew ne ho wɔ hardware device no so.

### Nsonsonoe a ɛho hia: UFVK vs. sika a wɔsɛe no titiriw

> **UFVK no betumi akyɛ. Ɛsɛ sɛ sika a wɔsɛe no safe no tra hardware sika kotoku no so.**
>
> Ɔhokafo sika kotoku no de safe a wɔde hwɛ nneɛma no di dwuma de hwɛ sika kotoku no so, hu sika a ɛreba, hu nsɛm a wɔakyerɛw, na ɛboa ma wosiesie nnwuma. Enni kokoam safe a ehia na ama wɔatumi asɛe sika.

Sɛnea yɛadi kan aka no, safe a wɔpaapae mu no ne fapem a wɔde yɛ nwini a wɔde kyerɛw nsɛnkyerɛnne nhyehyɛe no.

Ɔhokafo sika kotoku no betumi ayɛ adwuma dodow no ara a ehia na wɔde asiesie asɛm bi a enni sika no so tumi da. Hardware sika kotoku no hia sɛ egye asɛm a wɔasiesie no nkutoo na ɛyɛ nsaano nkyerɛwee adwuma a etwa to no.

Ɛho nhia sɛ wɔn a wɔyɛ hardware sika kotoku no ankasa yɛ saa mpaapaemu yi. Zcash's nhyehyɛe titiriw no tetew tumi a wɔde hwɛ nneɛma fi tumi a wɔde di dwuma wɔ protocol gyinabea no mu dedaw, na wɔn a wɔyɛ no kyekye wɔn nsaano nkyerɛwee ne nneɛma a wɔde kɔ amannɔne dwumadi ahorow no ara kwa wɔ saa nhyehyɛe a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no so. Protocol no ma kwan ma wonya nsɛm te sɛ address a wɔabɔ ho ban anaasɛ safoa a wɔde hwɛ ade mũ no nyinaa fi sika a wɔsɛe no safe mu, bere a sika a wɔsɛe no safe no ankasa da so ara yɛ kokoam, na ɛma ɛyɛ yiye sɛ hardware sika kotoku te sɛ Ledger, Keystone, ne Hito ma ɔhokafo softwea nya hwɛ ho nsɛm a ehia a ɛnda kokoam safe a ebetumi de sika no adi dwuma no adi da.

### Nea ɔhokafo sika kotoku no de nsɛm atitiriw yɛ ankasa

Sɛ wode nneɛma a wɔde hwɛ ade titiriw no di dwuma a, sika kotoku a ɛka ho no betumi:

- kyerɛ wo sika ne nsɛm a woakyerɛw
- kyerɛ nsɛm a wɔakyerɛw a wobetumi de adi dwuma
- nya address ahorow a ɛfata anaa kyerɛ
- kyekye asɛm no
- generate anaa hyehyɛ adanse a wɔhwehwɛ no
- siesie asɛm no ma wɔde wɔn nsa bɛhyɛ ase

Ɛho hia sɛ wuhu sɛ hia a ehia sɛ wɔyɛ asɛm bi ne sɛ wuhia kokoam sika a wɔsɛe no safe no nyɛ ade koro. Saa decoupling no yɛ ahobammɔ ho adwene titiriw a ɛwɔ hardware wallet nkabom akyi no mu biako.

## Shielded Transaction Adansi ne Tumi a Wɔde Ma

Eyinom ne akwan a wɔfa so yɛ asɛm no na wɔma ho kwan. Sɛ yɛbɛka no tiawa a, saa akwan yi ne nea sika kotoku no de saa data no yɛ ankasa de mena sika:

### Transaction Construction (Aguadi a Wɔabɔ ho Ban no a Wɔboaboa Ano)

Nkitahodi adansi yɛ ɔkwan a wɔfa so boaboa nsɛm a ɛho hia ano na wɔde ayɛ Zcash asɛm a ɛfata afi sika a ɔde di dwuma no abɔ ne tirim sɛ obetua no mu.

Aguadiɛ adansiɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no ka nsɛm a wɔapaw, wɔn a wɔgye, sika dodoɔ, sika a wɔtua, nullifiers, ne atɔfoɔ ho nsɛm foforɔ a wɔhwehwɛ bom yɛ ayɔnkofa nhyehyɛeɛ. Wɔ nnwuma a wɔabɔ ho ban ho no, ɛsan nso siesie nsɛm a ɛhia ma awoɔ ntoatoasoɔ a ɛho hia a ɛyɛ nimdeɛ a ɛnyɛ hwee adanseɛ ne nsaano nkyerɛwee.

Mpɛn pii no, ɔhokafo sika kotoku no yɛ saa fã yi. Ɛno

- Paw nsɛm a wɔakyerɛw a ɛsɛ sɛ wode bedi dwuma
- Kyerɛ asɛm no mu nsɛm a wɔde hyɛ mu ne nea wɔde fi mu ba
- Bu akontaa wɔ gyinapɛn ahorow a ɛho hia no ho
- Siesie asɛm no ho nsɛm a akyiri yi wɔbɛda no adi na wɔde wɔn nsa ahyɛ ase.

Wɔ awɔw mu nsaano nkyerɛwee nhyehyɛe mu no, wobetumi de asɛm no ho nsɛm a efi mu aba no ahyɛ PCZT mu na wɔde akɔ hardware sika kotoku no mu ama ahobammɔ-a ɛho hia signing anammɔn no.

### Proving (Ɔkyerɛ sɛ Protocol no Mudi Mu)

Proving kyerɛ awoɔ ntoatoasoɔ a ɛyɛ zero nimdeɛ adanseɛ (te sɛ Halo 2 anaa Groth16) a ɛkyerɛ sɛ asɛm bi di Zcash protocol mmara akyi.

Adanse no boa network no ma ɛma awerɛhyem sɛ:

- Wɔama nea ɔde di dwuma a ɔsɛe sika no tumi sɛ ɔmfa nsɛm a wɔakyerɛw no nni dwuma
- Nsɛm a wɔakyerɛw no wɔ hɔ ankasa
- Nullifiers a ɛne no hyia no yɛ soronko
- Nneɛma a wɔde ba nyinaa yɛ pɛ ne nneɛma a wɔde ba nyinaa a wɔde ka ho.

Ɛyɛ eyinom nyinaa a ɛnna kokoam nsɛm biara a ɛhyɛ ase adi.

Adanse awo ntoatoaso yɛ akontaabu mu den. Sɛ wode saa asɛyɛde yi gu afiri a ɛka ho no so a, ɛmma hardware sika kotoku no ntumi nsiw anaasɛ ɛnsa.

### Signing (Akwan a Wɔma Wɔsɛe Ka)

Sɛ wɔde wɔn nsa hyɛ ase no yɛ soronko titiriw wɔ adanse a wɔde kyerɛ ho. Digitals nsaano nkyerɛwee yɛ adwuma sɛ tumi krataa stamp a ɛda adi pefee a kokoam safe kurafo no yɛ.

Bere a nimdeɛ a wonni ho adanse di adanse sɛ asɛm no yɛ nokware no, dijitaal nsaano nkyerɛwee kyerɛ sɛ ɛwɔ tumi a wɔde di dwuma wɔ sika a wɔsɛe no mu. Nsaano nkyerɛwee no di adanse sɛ owura ankasa no pene transfer parameters pɔtee no so.

Sɛ wode nsa hyɛ ase a, ɛhwehwɛ sɛ wode kɔmputa ahoɔden kakraa bi na ɛyɛ adwuma, na ɛma ɛyɛ nea eye ma hardware sika kotoku.

### Dɛn ne shielded cold signing?

Shielded cold signing yɛ ɔkwan a wɔfa so ma kwan ma Zcash shielded transaction bere a wɔma kokoam sika a wɔsɛe no safe no atew ne ho fi intanɛt-connected companion wallet no ho. Wobetumi ayɛ asɛm no na wɔasiesie wɔ intanɛt so kɔmputa so, bere a hardware sika kotoku a ɛyɛ soronko, a wɔde asie wɔ intanɛt so anaasɛ atew ne ho wɔ ɔkwan foforo so no de ne nsafe a wɔabɔ ho ban no di dwuma de ma asɛm no ho kwan.

## Ɔhokafo Ne Hardware Sikakorabea

Nkitahodi a wɔabɔ ho ban betumi ayɛ ɔhokafo sika kotoku ne hardware sika kotoku nyinaa, a emu biara di adwuma soronko bi ho dwuma.

### Ɔhokafo Sikakorabea

Dɛn ne ɔhokafo sika kotoku?

Ɔhokafo sika kotoku no yɛ adwuma wɔ kɔmputa anaa telefon a wokura kyin so na ɛyɛ nnwuma dodow no ara a ɛho hia wɔ asɛm no a wɔyɛ mu, te sɛ:

- Nsɛm a wɔakyerɛw a ɛsɛ sɛ wode bedi dwuma no a wobɛpaw
- Nkitahodi no a wɔbɛkyekye
- Akontaabu a ɛho hia a ɛfa asɛmdi ho data ho
- Awo ntoatoaso a wɔhwehwɛ sɛ wɔyɛ adanse a wonni nimdeɛ biara no ho nhyehyɛe.

### Dɛn nti na mfaso wɔ ɔhokafo sika kotoku mu?

Ɔhokafo sika kotoku no di asɛm a wɔde yɛ adwuma no ho dwuma, nanso sɛ kɔmputa no nya malware a, ebia ɔtowhyɛfo bi betumi:

- hwɛ nea woreyɛ,
- sesa asɛm bi a wɔmfa wɔn nsa nhyɛ ase,
- bɔ mmɔden sɛ wobɛdaadaa wo ma woapene biribi so.

Ɛho nhia sɛ ɔhokafoɔ sika kotokuo no nya kwan kɔ kokoam safoa a wɔde asie wɔ hardware sika kotokuo no so, enti ɛtumi di ayɔnkofa adansiɛ ne kɔmputa nnwuma foforɔ ho dwuma berɛ a hardware sika kotokuo no de ahobanbɔ hyeɛ ma kokoam safoa no.

### Hardware Sikakorabea

Hardware wallet yɛ offline honam fam mfiri a ɛtetew wo kokoam safe fi fon anaa kɔmputa a ɛka ho, na ɛyɛ adwuma te sɛ sikakorabea a ahobammɔ wom ma wo crypto agyapade.

### Dɛn nti na mfaso wɔ hardware sika kotoku so?

Sɛ wo kɔmputa no nya malware a, ebia obi a ɔtow hyɛ wo so betumi:

- hwɛ nea woreyɛ,
- sesa asɛm bi a wɔmfa wɔn nsa nhyɛ ase,
- bɔ mmɔden sɛ wobɛdaadaa wo ma woapene biribi so.

Wɔrentumi nyi kokoam safoa no mfi hardware sika kotoku no mu kɛkɛ enti afiri no betumi ayɛ adwuma sɛ ahobammɔ hye.

Mfonini hokafo vs hardware tumi ahorow

Nkyekyɛmu a ɛda saa sika kotokuo mmienu yi ntam no ho hia titire ma nnwuma a wɔabɔ ho ban ɛfiri sɛ ɛyɛ den sene nnwuma a ɛda adi pefee na ɛfa nneɛma te sɛ nkrataa a wɔabɔ ho ban, nneɛma a ɛma hunu, bɔhyɛ, nimdeɛ a ɛnyɛ hwee ho adanseɛ, ne nkitahodiɛ nsaano nkyerɛwee ho.

Ɛbɛyɛ nea mfaso nni so sɛ hardware sika kotoku no bɛyɛ kɔmputa dwumadi biara. Mmom no, ɔhokafo sika kotoku no siesie asɛm no na ɛde nsɛm a ɛho hia ma hardware sika kotoku no ma wɔde wɔn nsa hyɛ ase, bere a kokoam sika a wɔsɛe no safe no da so ara tew ne ho wɔ hardware mfiri no mu.

## Key Tew a Wɔtew Wɔn Ho

> Key isolation kyerɛ sɛ kokoam keys a ebetumi ama kwan sɛ wɔsɛe sika no mfi hardware sika kotoku no mu da. Ɔhokafo no betumi asiesie asɛm no; entumi mfa ne nsa nhyɛ ase.

Key isolation kyerɛ sɛ wobɛma kokoam safe a ɛho hia na ama wɔama asɛm bi ho kwan no atew ne ho afi ɔhokafo sika kotoku ne afiri a ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ so no ho. Wɔ shielded cold signing nhyehyɛe mu no, ɔhokafo sika kotoku no betumi ayɛ asɛm no na ayɛ adanse a wɔhwehwɛ no ho nhyehyɛe, bere a hardware sika kotoku no ma kokoam sika a wɔsɛe no safe a ɛho hia no tew wɔn ho.

Sɛ kɔmputa anaa fon a ɛka ho no asɛe a, ebia ɔtowhyɛfo bi betumi ne asɛm no adi nkitaho ansa na ɔde ne nsa ahyɛ ase, nanso sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ yiye no, ontumi nyi kokoam safe no mfi hardware sika kotoku no mu. Enti safoa no da so ara wɔ hardware afiri no ahobammɔ hye no mu.

Saa mpaepaemu yi ma ɔhokafoɔ sika kotokuo no tumi yɛ ayɔnkofa adansiɛ ne akontabuo nnwuma a ɛmma no kwan tẽẽ kɔ nneɛma titire a ɛhia na ama wɔatumi ama asɛm no ho kwan.

![Key isolation](/content-images/key-isolation.webp)

Nsonsonoe titiriw a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyɛ ne sɛ "viewing key" nkyerɛ sɛ app no nya aba no fã a ɛyɛ mmerɛw.

Wɔ Zcash, wɔhyɛ da nya Full Viewing Key (FVK/UFVK) fi sika a wɔsɛe no safoa no mu na ɛma nea okura no tumi hu na ɔkyerɛkyerɛ dwumadi a wɔabɔ ho ban a ɛfata mu, nanso ɛnyɛ sɛ ɔmma sika a wɔsɛe no ho kwan. FVK yɛ ade a ɛnyɛ kokoam ade a wonya fi sika a wɔsɛe no mu.

**Kokoamsɛm ho kɔkɔbɔ biako a ɛho hia:**

Enti ɛsɛ sɛ wohu sɛ app no ntumi nwia sika no ne UFVK nkutoo nanso ebetumi ahu sika kotoku no kokoam shielded dwumadi.

Ɛno nti na ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ sɛ yɛbɛkyerɛkyerɛ ɔhokafoɔ application no mu sɛ ɛwɔ *hwɛ tumi*, sene sɛ yɛbɛka kɛkɛ sɛ ɛwɔ "ɔmanfoɔ nsɛm."

Ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a wobɛfa so aka adwene no nyinaa ne sɛ ɔhokafo sika kotoku no nya nneɛma atitiriw a ɛdɔɔso a ɛbɛma wɔahu nea ɛrekɔ so wɔ sika kotoku no so, nanso ɛnyɛ nneɛma atitiriw a ɛdɔɔso a ɛbɛma nea ɛto sika kotoku no sika ho kwan. Nanso hardware sika kotoku no ma sika a wɔsɛe no no tew ne ho na ɛde di dwuma bere a nea ɔde di dwuma no apene asɛm bi so nkutoo.

Ɛno ne core key isolation model a ɛwɔ saa shielded Zcash hardware wallet designs yi akyi na ɛyɛ model koro no ara a ɛda adi, implementation by implementation, wɔ Ledger, Keystone, ne Hito a wɔbɛkata so nnansa yi ara.

## PCZT

PCZT gyina hɔ ma Partially Created Zcash Transaction. Ɛyɛ package a ɛde Zcash asɛm ne nsɛm a ɛhia na wɔde awie firi stage baako kɔ foforɔ.

Ɛyɛ standardized format ma Zcash asɛm bere a wɔda so ara rekyekye asɛm no. Ɛma wɔn a wɔde wɔn ho hyɛ mu anaa mfiri ahodoɔ tumi yɛ ayɔnkofa adebɔ nhyehyɛeɛ no afã ahodoɔ na wɔde asɛm a wɔawie no fã bi no fa wɔn ntam.

Aguadi a wɔbɛkyekye no yɛ nea ɛyɛ den kɛse sen sɛ wode wo nsa bɛhyɛ ɔmanfo nsa a wɔde bɛkɔ baabi foforo a ɛnyɛ den wɔ nimdeɛ zero cryptocurrency protocols te sɛ Zcash. Nkitahodi a wɔabɔ ho ban hwehwɛ sɛ wɔde akontaabu a emu yɛ den di adanse a wonni nimdeɛ biara, te sɛ nea wɔde Halo 2 anaa Groth16 na ɛyɛe. Nea afi mu aba ne sɛ, ebia mfiri a ahoɔden nnim te sɛ hardware sika kotoku nni memory ne processing tumi a ɛbɛma wɔadi asɛm no nyinaa ho dwuma.

PCZT a wɔde aba no di nsɛnnennen atitiriw pii a ɛwɔ shielded crypto architecture mu ho dwuma. PCZT yɛ mfinimfini data nhyehyɛe a wɔahyɛ da ayɛ a ɛpaapae nkitahodi adebɔ mu kɔ modular, dwumadi ahorow a ɛde ne ho mu. Ɛma kɔmputa a ɛyɛ ɔhokafo a ahoɔden wom tumi di asɛmdi nhyiam ne adanse awo ntoatoaso ho dwuma bere a ɛto offline hardware sika kotoku ano hye katee wɔ tumi krataa ne nsaano nkyerɛwee ho.

### Hardware ne Cold Storage Anohyeto Ahorow

Bere a hardware sika kotoku ma tebea a ahobammɔ wom a wɔde bɔ kokoam sika a wɔsɛe no safe ho ban no, mpɛn pii no, ɛwɔ RAM, kɔmputa ahoɔden, ne data a wɔde fa kɔmputa so a ɛyɛ kakraa bi bere a wɔde toto kɔmputa ahorow a ɛka ho no ho no. Saa anohyeto ahorow yi ma ɛnyɛ nea mfaso wɔ so sɛ wɔbɛyɛ akontaabu a emu yɛ den zero nimdeɛ adanse awo ntoatoaso anaasɛ wɔbɛkɔ so akura atɔ tebea a edi mũ a wɔhwehwɛ wɔ asɛm no adansi nhyehyɛe no mu.

Nea afi mu aba ne sɛ, ɔhokafo sika kotoku no tumi di nnwuma a ɛyɛ den wɔ kɔmputa so no ho dwuma, bere a hardware sika kotoku no da so ara de n’adwene si ahobammɔ dwumadi a ɛho hia a ɛne sɛ wɔbɛma kwan ma wɔde ne sika a wɔsɛe no safe a wɔabɔ ho ban no ama asɛm no so.

Ɛma wo dwene sɛ, sɛ afiri no yɛ saa constrained a, ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na ɛda so ara yɛ *enough* verification sɛ ɛbɛyɛ nea wotumi de ho to so?

Wiɛ sɛ yɛbɛka no tiawa a, hardware sika kotoku ntumi nnye bo a wɔpɛ mfi kɔmputa a ɛka ho no hɔ na ɔde ne nsa bɛhyɛ ase. Ɛhia nsɛm a ɛdɔɔso a ɛbɛkyerɛ sɛ boɔ a wɔde wɔn nsa ahyɛ aseɛ no ne asɛm a ɔde di dwuma no abɔ ne tirim sɛ ɔbɛma ho kwan no hyia. Wei ma ɛkari pɛ a ɛyɛ den ba efisɛ ɛsɛ sɛ afiri no yɛ parsing ne verification a ɛdɔɔso de bɔ signing nhyehyɛe no ho ban, bere a ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ anohyeto a emu yɛ den mu wɔ memory, computation, firmware size, ne implementation complexity ho.

Ledger's Orchard PCZT dwumadie yɛ saa asɛnnennen yi nhwɛsoɔ, a ɛkyerɛ sɛ parsing titire a egyina amansin so no bɛtumi ama dwumadie no ayɛ yie wɔ mfiri a wɔahyɛ no den so nanso ɛbɛtumi nso ahwehwɛ sɛ wɔyɛ nsakraeɛ wɔ firmware mu berɛ a nkitahodiɛ nhyehyɛeɛ anaa amansin a wɔboa no renya nkɔsoɔ.

Mmɔdenbɔ abien a ɛkɔ so no kyerɛ sɛnea wɔyɛ wɔn ade wɔ anohyeto koro yi ara ho.

Wɔde nhwehwɛmu nyansahyɛ bi a ɛfa bere a ɛkɔ so daa koraa a ɛkyerɛ sɛ pipeline fata ma open-source hardware wallet architectures ne kɔmputa a ɛnyɛ den no too gua wɔ Grant #423 mu. Na botae no ne sɛ wɔbɛhwehwɛ cryptographic infrastructure a wobetumi de adi dwuma ahobammɔ mu wɔ mmeae te sɛ C anaa Rust bere a ɛtew nsɛnnennen ne nsiesie adesoa a ɛwɔ shielded Zcash mmoa so no so.

Nanso ɔkwan foforo a wɔfa so yɛ no gyina hɔ ma denam **Zcash SeedSigner** a wɔahyɛ ho nyansa a ɛyɛ signer a ɛyɛ open-source, air-gapped signer a wɔde nneɛma a ne bo nyɛ den a wotumi nya ntɛm mu. N’adwumayɛ nhyehyɛe a wɔahyɛ ho nyansa no ma kɔmputa a ɛka ho no tetew ne ho fi afiri a wɔde nsa hyɛ ase no ho, na ɛde PCZT bi fa mframa mu kwan bi so de hwɛ sɛnea mfiri no so yɛ nokware na wɔde wɔn nsa hyɛ ase.

https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-research-on-a-fully-constant-time-proving-pipeline-for-open-source-hardware-wallet-architectures-reusable-for-standard-computers/57555

Sɛ wɔka saa akwan yi bom a, ɛkyerɛ mfiridwuma mu asɛnnennen a ɛtrɛw a ɛwɔ hardware sika kotoku a wɔabɔ ho ban akyi: asɛm a wɔde sie ne adanse betumi aba wɔ afiri a ɛho hia ahobammɔ no akyi, nanso ɛsɛ sɛ nea ɔde ne nsa hyɛ ase no da so ara nya nsɛm ne tumi a ɔde di adanse a ɛdɔɔso na ama wama asɛm a efi mu ba no ho kwan dwoodwoo.

### PCZT Ma Split Architecture no tumi yɛ adwuma

Partially Created Zcash Transaction (PCZT) gyinapɛn no yɛ data nhyehyeɛ a ɛwɔ mmara mu a ɛka ɔhokafoɔ sika kotokuo ne hardware sika kotokuo no bom.

Ɔhokafo sika kotoku tumi di nkyerɛwde a wɔpaw ho dwuma, yɛ nullifiers no ho adwuma, na ɛyɛ zero nimdeɛ adanse no nyinaa wɔ n’ankasa so. Eyi betumi aba efisɛ nsɛm a ehia ma anammɔn ahorow yi, a safe a wɔde nya nullifiers ka ho no fi safe a wɔde hwɛ ade no mu sen biribiara a ɛbata tumi a wɔde di dwuma wɔ sika a wɔsɛe no ho. Afei ɔhokafo sika kotoku no hyehyɛ saa nneɛma yi gu PCZT ade mu.

Wɔde PCZT no ma hardware sika kotoku no, a ɛkenkan asɛm no ho nsɛm (te sɛ sika dodow ne nea ogye no address), de nsaano nkyerɛwee no di dwuma denam ne sika a wɔsɛe no safe a wɔabɔ ho ban no so, na ɛsan de PCZT a wɔayɛ no foforo no ma ɔhokafo mfiri no.

Saa nhyehyeɛ yi de ahobanbɔ hyeɛ a ɛyɛ katee si hɔ: host hardware a ahoɔden wom tumi yɛ nimdeɛ a ɛyɛ zero akontabuo a ɛnhia sɛ wɔnya kwan kɔ nsafe a wɔsɛe no so, berɛ a mfiri a wɔde sie nwini a ahoɔden sua no tumi hyɛ sikasɛm mu ahobanbɔ a enhia sɛ wɔyɛ kɔmputa a emu yɛ duru.

### Prover Kokoam Nsɛm Ho Nsiesiei

Bere a nimdeɛ adanse awo ntoatoaso a ɛyɛ zero a wɔde bɛhyɛ ɔhokafo sika kotoku anaa ɔbofo foforo nsa no ma sika a wɔsɛe no safe no tew ne ho no, ɛda so ara hwehwɛ sɛ ɔbenfo no nya nkitahodi ho nsɛm a ɛho hia, te sɛ nkyerɛwde bo, nea ogye no address, ne bɔhyɛ ahorow.

PCZT no hwɛ adansiɛ mu mpaepaemu no so denam hwɛ a ɛhwɛ sɛ wɔbɛtumi de saa nsɛm yi ama de adi adanseɛ a wɔremfa kokoam sika a wɔsɛe no safe a ɛhia ma tumi krataa no nkɔ baabi foforɔ.

Eyi ma awerɛhyem sɛ wɔatew tumidi a wɔda no adi ne tumi a wɔde di dwuma wɔ sika a wɔsɛe no ho. Prover no betumi ayɛ computationally intensive proof generation a onnya cryptographic tumi a ɔde bɛsɛe nea ɔde di dwuma no sika.

### PCZT Nkitahodi a Wɔde Di Dwuma

Bere a PCZT bɛyɛ Zcash hardware sika kotoku, multisignature, ne mframa-gapped signing adwumayɛ nhyehyɛe no fã titiriw no, nkitahodi a ɛda ahofadi a wɔde di dwuma ntam no bɛyɛ nea ɛho hia kɛse.

June 2026 nsusuiɛ bi a AngryDavee de maeɛ no hyɛɛ no nsow sɛ PCZT dwumadie no wɔ hɔ dada wɔ nnwuma ahodoɔ a Ledger, Keystone, Hito, OneKey, ne nkyerɛkyerɛmu dwumadie a ɛwɔ librustzcash. Nanso, nsusuiɛ no kyerɛɛ sɛ sɔhwɛ ahodoɔ a wɔkyɛ nni hɔ a wɔde bɛsi so dua sɛ saa dwumadie yi kyerɛ PCZTs ase daa.

Ano aduru a wɔahyɛ ho nyansa no yɛ open-source interoperability test suite a canonical PCZT test vectors ne cross-implementation tests a ɛkata create → sign → finalize adwumayɛ nhyehyɛe a edi mũ no so. Ɛsan nso hyɛɛ nyansa sɛ wɔbɛsɔ dwumadie ahyeɛ te sɛ Combiner no ahwɛ, baabi a wɔbɛtumi de nsɛm a wɔde wɔn nsa ahyɛ aseɛ fã bi afiri wɔn a wɔde wɔn nsa ahyɛ aseɛ ahodoɔ hɔ abom ansa na wɔawie asɛm bi.

Eyi fa hardware sika kotoku ho titiriw efisɛ PCZT ma kwan ma wɔtetew nkitahodi adansi ne nsaano nkyerɛwee mu: ɔhokafo sika kotoku betumi ayɛ atɔ na asiesie PCZT, bere a hardware mfiri nya nsaano nkyerɛwee ho nsɛm a wɔhwehwɛ na ɛyɛ ahobammɔ a ɛho hia a wɔde wɔn nsa hyɛ ase adwuma no.

Wɔamfa nsusuiɛ no anni dwuma ɛfiri sɛ Zcash Community Grants sii gyinaeɛ sɛ wɔrenkɔ wɔn anim wɔ mmoa no mu berɛ a wɔasan ahwɛ nsusuiɛ no mu na wɔasusu mpɔtam hɔfoɔ mmuaeɛ ho akyi. Nea ɛde bae ne sɛ, wɔamfa saa mmoa yi so anhyehyɛ cross-implementation test suite a wɔahyɛ ho nyansa no. Enti ɛyɛ mmuaeɛ a wɔahyɛ ho nyansa wɔ PCZT nkitahodiɛ asɛnnennen a wɔakyerɛ ho, ɛnyɛ gyinapɛn a ɛwɔ hɔ mprempren wɔ Zcash.

### Ledger sɛ Hardware Nsɛnkyerɛnne Atwa Yɛn Ho Ahyia

Ledger yɛ adwumakuw bi a wɔyɛ hardware sika kotoku. Wɔasisi ne mfiri ahorow no atwa Secure Element a ɛyɛ chip a wɔatu ho ama a wɔayɛ sɛ ɛbɛkura kokoam safoa nneɛma na ama ayɛ honam fam atew ne ho afi mfiri no nkae no ho, ne biribiara a ɛka ho no ho. Saa Secure Element no ne ade nyinaa nti a Ledger mfiri bi yɛ nea wotumi de ho to so ma nneɛma a wɔde sie awɔw mu. Wei kyerɛ sɛ sɛ kɔmputa anaa fon a wɔde ahyɛ mu no ayɛ basaa mpo a, wɔrentumi nyi nsafe a ɛwɔ Secure Element no mu no mfi mu.

Nanso, wɔ n’ankasa mu no, Ledger mfiri bi yɛ blank wɔ cryptocurrency pɔtee biara ho. Ɛhia sɛ wɔde application bi ahyɛ so a enim sɛnea wodi saa sika no cryptography pɔtee bi ho dwuma.

Ledger mfiri bi yɛ blank wɔ obuo mu ma cryptocurrency pɔtee biara enti ɛhia application a ɛnim saa sika no cryptography. Zcash deɛ, awoɔ ntoatoasoɔ mmienu na ɛwɔ hɔ:

- Mfitiaseɛ Zondax-built “Zcash Shielded” app, a wɔde dii dwuma ne ahokafoɔ sika kotokuo te sɛ Zkool na wɔretwe wɔ 5 November 2026 ne,
- Ledger's Zcash app foforo (3.9.4+), a Ledger Wallet Desktop 4.21 + de di dwuma ankasa ma Ironwood shielded ZEC.

Nhwɛso titiriw a ɛma wɔtew wɔn ho fi afoforo ho a ɛwɔ ase ha no fa abien no nyinaa ho.

Zkool yɛ companion wallet a ɛwɔ wo fon anaa kɔmputa so a ɛne saa app no kasa. Ɛyɛ ayɔnkofa no, de kɔ Ledger, Zondax app a ɛwɔ afiri no so afei ɛde ne nsa hyɛ ase na ɛde nsaano nkyerɛwee no san kɔ.

Enti Zondax yɛ software a ɛreyɛ adwuma wɔ hardware no so, Zkool yɛ software a ɛreyɛ adwuma wɔ n’akyi a ɛde Zondax's app no di dwuma de ne afiri no di nkitaho.

### Dɛn na efi Ledger?

Ledger's Zcash software no wɔ dwumadie pɔtee bi a wɔde bɛkɔ amannɔne sika kotokuo no hwɛ safoa, a ɛyɛ safoa a ɛma softwea hunu sika a aka ne nnwuma a ɛntumi nsɛe hwee.

Saa dwumadie yi betumi asan de nneɛma mmienu no mu baako aba:

- Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK), a ɛyɛ viewing key a edi mũ a ɛkata Zcash's shielded pools nyinaa so prɛko pɛ, anaa
- Nnuadewa Orchard Full Viewing Key (FVK)

Ɔkwan biara so no, nea efi mu ba no yɛ safe a wɔde hwɛ ade bere nyinaa. Ɛmfa ho sɛ wobɛbisa no, saa dwumadie yi nni ɔkwan biara a ɛbɛfa so asan de nsɛm baako a anka ɛbɛma obi atu sika ankasa, sika a wɔsɛe no safe no.

Ɛno ne asɛm no nyinaa a ɛfa sɛnea wɔasi no ho deɛ, wɔayɛ no enti adeɛ baako pɛ a ɛtumi de kɔ amannɔne ne biribi a ahotɔ wɔ sɛ wode bɛhyɛ ɔhokafoɔ app nsa.

Wɔahyehyɛ Zcash's key hierarchy no sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wɔ shielded pool biara ho no, spending key no nya nneɛma abien a ɛsono emu biara:

- Safoa a ɛma kwan ma wɔsɛe sika, na
- Safoa a wɔde hwɛ ade a edi mũ

Afei FVK betumi anya n’ankasa tumi foforo a ɛfa hwɛ ho, nanso bere biara nni hɔ a saa kwan a wɔfa so nya no no nsan nkɔ sikasɛm mu tumidi mu. Sɛ safe bi nya nkorabata kɔ ɔfã a wɔhwɛ wɔ nhyehyɛe no mu wie a, ɛtra hɔ na wontumi mfa nsan nsiesie sika a wɔsɛe no tumi safe a efi mu bae no.

Ironwood di saa nhyehyɛe koro yi ara akyi, efisɛ ɛsan de Orchard's safe ne nkyerɛwde a ɛwɔ ase no di dwuma. Nanso, ɛyɛ ɔtare soronko wɔ adwene a ɛwɔ mu no gyinabea, a n’ankasa ahofama dua ne nullifier a wɔahyehyɛ. Ɛno kyerɛ sɛ UFVK yɛ safe a wɔde hwɛ nneɛma a ɛwɔ ɔtare biara mu (a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, Sapling, Orchard, ne mprempren Ironwood), ɛnyɛ safe biako a ɛyɛ tratraa.

UFVK a wɔde akɔ amannɔne ansa na Ironwood reyɛ adwuma no renka Ironwood hwɛ safoa ho, efisɛ na saa ɔtare no nni hɔ da. Eyi nti pɛpɛɛpɛ na na ɛsɛ sɛ sika kotoku yɛ foforo na ama wɔahu na wɔanya UFVK ahorow Ironwood-capable wɔ NU6.3 nkɔso akyi.

### Dɛn na ɛtra Ledger?

Tumi a ɛma wɔsɛe sika no da so ara wɔ afiri no so.

Ledger Signer no nya transaction/PCZT firi host no hɔ na ɔyɛ spend authorization signature no gyina pool ko a asɛm no bɔ sika fi mu (Ironwood pool no ɛfiri sɛ Ledger's mprempren nkabom no boa Ironwood nko ara). Afei wɔyɛ nsaano nkyerɛwee no wɔ afiri no so, na nea ɔgye no no nsa ka nsaano nkyerɛwee no nkutoo, na ɛnyɛ kokoam sika a wɔsɛe no safe no.

Eyi nso ne Ledger's ahobammɔ nhyehyɛe a ɛfa nneɛma nyinaa ho no hyia. Ne Secure Element no yi kokoam safoa nneɛma fi hɔ, na Ledger's application ahobanbɔ ahwehwɛde ahorow no bara application ahorow pɔtee sɛ ɛnda saa ahintasɛm no adi nkyerɛ host no.

Ledger's signer betumi de FVK/UFVK no akɔ amannɔne bere a ɔde ne ho reyɛ atɔfoɔ nsaano nkyerɛwee. Wɔnam dwumadi ahorow a ɛsono emu biara so na ɛde tumi abien no ma, na ɛnyɛ nea wɔaboaboa ano ayɛ no biako.

### Dɛn na ɛno kyerɛ wɔ nneyɛe mu?

Fa no sɛ wo Ledger kura ZEC.

Ɔhokafoɔ sika kotokuo no tumi nya UFVK na ɛno nti ɛkyerɛ: "Saa sika kotokuo yi anya 10 ZEC." Etumi scan na ɛkyerɛ nnwuma ne sika a aka.

Nanso sɛ obi de ɔhokafo sika kotoku no to asiane mu a, UFVK nkutoo mma wɔn tumi a wɔde bɛyɛ sika a wɔde di dwuma ho tumi krataa nsaano nkyerɛwee no.

Botae a ɛwɔ sika a wɔsɛe no ho tumi krataa a ɛyɛ soronko no mu ne sɛ ɛbɛma mfiri a wɔahyɛ no den te sɛ hardware sika kotoku atumi ama sika a wɔsɛe no a wɔabɔ ho ban ho kwan a ɛho nhia sɛ wɔn ankasa yɛ zk-SNARK adansedi mũ no nyinaa.

### Zondax Ledger a wɔde di dwuma; hardware sika kotoku no fã

#### Dɛn ne Zondax?

Zondax yɛ adwumayɛkuo a ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa a wɔyɛɛ Ledger Zcash app no. Ɛyɛ firmware application ankasa a ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ Ledger hardware so na ɛma device no tumi te ase na ɛyɛ Zcash nkitahodi wɔ nea edi kan no mu.

### Mfitiaseɛ Zcash Ledger dwumadie

Zondax's Ledger Zcash app akoraeɛ yɛ software a ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ Ledger mfiri so. Mfiase no na app no twe adwene si Sapling shielded transactions so, ansa na Orchard reba sɛ Zcash's shielded pool titiriw, enti na Sapling mmoa no yɛ baabi a nea Ledger mfiri betumi de shielded ZEC.

Ne nhyehyeɛ no kyerɛ mpaepaemu no, a ahɔhoɔ/ɔhokafoɔ fã no siesie asɛmdi ho nsɛm berɛ a Ledger di dwumadie titire a ɛho hia ne nsaano nkyerɛwee ho dwuma.

### Adannandie a ɛkɔ Orchard so

Ledger's adwuma no kɔɔ Orchard + PCZT. Na eyi yɛ nsakrae a ntease wom sen sɛ ɛbɛyɛ foforo ketewaa bi a wɔde ama sɛ Orchard de adansedi nhyehyɛe soronko di dwuma (Halo 2, Sapling's Groth16) ne nkyerɛwde ne safoa adansi soronko, enti Sapling-only firmware a wɔbɛtrɛw mu de adi Orchard nsaano nkyerɛwee ho dwuma no hwehwɛɛ cryptographic fapem foforo wɔ mfiri no fã, ɛnyɛ firmware patch kɛkɛ.

Nanso, berɛ a Zcash kɔɔ Ironwood, Zondax ne Ledger danee wɔn nkɔsoɔ adwene kɔɔ Ironwood a wɔbɛboa no so tẽẽ, sene sɛ wɔbɛwie Orchard dwumadie bi a akyiri yi wɔbɛsan ayɛ no foforɔ ama Ironwood. Eyi gyina hɔ ma ɔfã foforo wɔ Ledger dwumadie no nkɔsoɔ mu, berɛ a Ironwood si Orchard cryptographic architecture so berɛ a ɔde shielded pool a ɛyɛ soronko ne protocol nsakraeɛ foforɔ reba.

### Nea ɛkɔ so wɔ Ledger app yi mu

2. Host no de atɔ ho nsɛm a ɛfa ho no kɔ Ledger afiri no so.

4. Host no di biribiara a ɛwɔ ase no ho dwuma a binding signature ne final transaction assembly ka ho.

App a ɛretu mmirika wɔ Ledger no mu no nhu anaasɛ ɛnnyɛ biribiara da a ɛboro sika a wɔde di dwuma ho tumi krataa nsaano nkyerɛwee no ankasa so, na adwuma a ɛyɛ duru wɔ kɔmputa so anaasɛ ɛyɛ data-kɔm (a ɛkyerɛ, ɛkyekyere, nhyiam) nyinaa tra host no so, baabi a enhia sɛ wɔde nneɛma atitiriw di dwuma.

### Ledger's kurom hɔ shielded nkabom; Ledger Sikakorabea Desktop so

Wɔsan nso sii so dua sɛ Keystone hardware sika kotoku biara nni mu.

Ɛnnya nsii so dua sɛ sika no yerae ankasa. Mmom no, wosusuw sɛ ɛyɛ sika kotoku a wohu anaasɛ atutra ho asɛm, baabi a na wɔankyerɛ sika a kan no na wotumi hu bio wɔ nsakrae a wɔyɛe kɔɔ Ironwood. Ɛnsɛ sɛ wɔka sika a wɔanhu anaa wɔankyerɛ no ho asɛm ara kwa sɛ sika a ayera daa na saa nsonsonoeɛ yi ho hia berɛ a wɔrete atutena ho haw ase.

## 2. Keystone

**Dɛn ne Keystone?**

Keystone yɛ hardware sika kotoku a mframa wom. Ne nsaano nkyerɛwee mfiri no nni nhama anaa wireless nkitahodi biara a ɛkɔ ɔhokafo app no so tẽẽ bere a wɔde wɔn nsa hyɛ ase no ankasa, na ɛnam QR koodu ahorow so di nkitaho mmom. Ɛyɛ paired ha ne Zashi (seesei rebranded Zodl), Zcash native companion wallet, na saa pairing no ma key isolation nnyinasosɛm no yɛ soronko pefee na ɛnyɛ den sɛ wobɛhwehwɛ.

**Keystone/Zashi nsuo a ɛsen**

Keystone's Zcash nkabom ne Zashi ma saa mpaepaemu yi da adi pefee. Sɛ wobɔ Keystone kɔ Zashi, wɔde Unified Full Viewing Key fi Keystone kɔ Zashi.

Enti nsu a ɛsen ne sɛ:

![Keystone and Zashi viewing flow](/content-images/keystone-zashi-viewing-flow.webp)

**Dɛn na Zashi betumi ayɛ wɔ UFVK no ho?**

Zashi betumi de adi dwuma de:

- kyerɛ nnwuma a wɔabɔ ho ban a ɛba
- kyerɛ sɛnea wɔbɛkari pɛ
- nya nsɛm a ɛfa dwumadi a wɔabɔ ho ban ho

Nanso entumi mfa sika no nni dwuma esiane sɛ ɛwɔ UFVK no ara kwa nti.

UFVK yɛ "hwɛ tumi" a ɛma kwan ma adesua kari pɛ ne nkitahodi nanso ɛmma kwan sɛ wɔbɛsɛe sika.

### Dɛn na ɛtra Keystone?

Aba/sika a wɔsɛe no ho tumidi no tra Keystone. Zashi nnya aba no, sika a wɔsɛe no safe, anaa sika a wɔsɛe no ho tumi krataa kokoam safe no. Ɛgye UFVK, address ho nsɛm, ne nsɛm a ɛho hia ma view only dwumadie.

Sɛ wɔka saa fã abien yi bom wie mfonini no. Nea efi afiri no mu, te sɛ UFVK, address ahorow, ne watch nkutoo ho nsɛm no dɔɔso ma Zashi sɛ ɔbɛhwɛ akontaabu no so, nanso biribiara nni hɔ a efi afiri no mu a ɛdɔɔso sɛ ɛbɛma sika afi mu.

Eyi nti na Zashi ne Keystone nkabom no betumi ama wɔanya awɔw mu nneɛma a wɔde sie a wɔabɔ ho ban. Wallet application no betumi ahwɛ akontaabu no so a enni tumi a wɔhwehwɛ sɛ wɔsɛe sika fi mu. Wɔde nkabom no sii hɔ pɔtee sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a wobetumi de ZEC.

## 3. Hito, na ɔyɛ

Hito di key isolation model a ɛte sɛ Keystone, nanso ne dwumadie yɛ foforɔ. Sɛ anka ɛbɛyɛ pairing ne ɔhokafo sika kotoku a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw te sɛ Zashi, Hito de n’ankasa Android sika kotoku mena, na wɔakyerɛw onboarding nhyehyɛe a ɛda hardware afiri no ne saa Android app no ntam wɔ Hito's nkɔso nsɛntitiriw mu.

**Onboarding: nea Hito app no nya**

Android sika kotoku no de:

- a wɔaka Full Viewing Key (UFVK)
- Address a Wɔaka abom
- ZIP-32 aba nsateaa nkyerɛwee
- network ho nsɛm

bere a woreyɛ onboarding hardware akontaabu no.

Hyɛ no nsow sɛ Android application no nnya hardware sika kotoku no aba anaa kokoam sika a wɔsɛe no safe da.

![Hito onboarding](/content-images/hito-onboarding.webp)

**Dɛn na Hito app no nya?**

Onboarding ho nsɛm a wɔakyerɛw ato hɔ no bi ne:

- UFVK na ɛyɛ
- Address a Wɔaka abom
- ZIP-32 aba nsateaa nkyerɛwee
- Nɛtwɛke

UFVK no ma Android sika kotoku no nsɛm a ehia na ama wɔatumi de akontaabu no adi dwuma sɛ sika kotoku a wotumi hwɛ a ennya aba no. Aba nsateaa nkyerɛwee yɛ tenten a wɔahyɛ ato hɔ a wɔde kyerɛ a wonya fi aba no mu, a wɔde hu akontaabu a ɛyɛ nea ɛwɔ he, ɛnyɛ aba no ankasa na ɛmma sika a wɔde di dwuma biara, ɛno nti na ne ba a ɛwɔ hɔ no ne "ennya aba no da" a wɔka wɔ atifi hɔ no nhyia.

**Dɛn na ɛtra Hito?**

Aba ankasa, kokoam sika a wɔsɛe no nsafe, ne tumi a wɔde hyɛ ase no da so ara wɔ hardware mfiri no so. Afei Hito yɛ signing offline.

Mprempren Hito ade titiriw no ka sɛ adebɔ, export, offline signing, ne import adwumayɛ nyinaa yɛ adwuma, a ɛkyerɛ BLE akwantu ne BBQr QR export a ɛwɔ PCZT.

Saa adwumayɛ kwan no fata sɛ wɔbɔ din pefee, efisɛ ɛyɛ kyinhyia a edi mũ a wɔadi kan aka ho asɛm wɔ amanneɛbɔ yi mu no ho nhwɛso pɔtee: Android sika kotoku no yɛ PCZT, de kɔ Hito, Hito de ne nsa hyɛ ase offline ne ne nneɛma titiriw a atew ne ho, na wɔde PCZT a wɔde wɔn nsa ahyɛ ase no san ba Android sika kotoku no mu.

Bere biara nni hɔ a aba anaa sika a wɔsɛe no safe no ntwa saa hye no nkɔ ɔkwan biara so.

## Orchard to Ironwood: Nkɔso a Ɛfa Hardware Wallet Mmoa Ho

Orchard yɛ shielded pool wɔ Zcash protocol mu a ɛma wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no tumi de kokoamsɛm a ɛkɔ anim sie na wɔde di ZEC ho dwuma. Wɔde baa Network Upgrade 5 (NU5), Orchard de Halo 2 adansedie nhyehyɛeɛ ne cryptographic architecture a wɔayɛ no foforɔ di dwuma de boa nkitahodiɛ a wɔabɔ ho ban a ɛnda nkitahodiɛ ho nsɛm a ɛho hia te sɛ nea ɔde kɔmaa, nea ɔgye, anaa sika dodoɔ adi.

Ironwood yɛ shielded pool a wɔde baa Zcash's NU6.3 network upgrade mu sɛ nea ɛbɛdi Orchard pool no akyi. Ɛde Orchard cryptographic protocol di dwuma bere a ɛde nsakrae a wɔayɛ sɛ ɛbɛma sika a wɔabɔ ho ban no ahobammɔ a ɛkɔ so bere tenten no atu mpɔn, a quantum recoverability ne awerɛhyem a emu yɛ den a ɛfa nneɛma a wɔde ma no mudi mu kura ho ka ho.

Mfiase no, ɛho hia sɛ yɛma emu da hɔ sɛ Ironwood amfa cryptographic nhyehyɛe foforo koraa anhyɛ Orchard protocol no ananmu. Wɔde Ironwood bae sɛ ɔtare foforo a wɔabɔ ho ban a wɔde Orchard protocol a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no asi. Wei kyerɛ sɛ wɔwɔ Action nhyehyɛe koro, Halo 2 proving system koro, nkyerɛwde koro ne key constructions.

Ironwood wɔ n’ankasa note commitment dua, nullifier set, anchor, ne value pool, enti wɔ consensus level no ɛyɛ pool a ɛyɛ soronko ankasa. Nanso cryptographically, ɛyɛ Orchard ntrɛwmu mmom sen sɛ wɔbɛsan akyekye.

Wɔyɛɛ no saa kwan yi so sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a:

1. **Supply integrity.** Wɔ May 29, 2026 no, ahobanbɔ ho nhwehwɛmufoɔ Taylor Hornby daa soundness sintɔ kɛseɛ bi adi wɔ kokoam wɔ Orchard's zero knowledge proof system no mu, deɛ anka ɛbɛtumi ama kwan ma wɔayɛ ZEC atoro a wɔrennya on-chain trace. Esiane sɛ Orchard's asɛm no ho nsɛm yɛ kokoam nti, obiara ntumi mfa ne ho nhwɛ sɛ wɔammɔ ZEC atoro biara ansa na wɔresiesie no. Sɛ wosɔ ɔtare dedaw no ano na wofi ase yɛ foforo wɔ zero no di saa ɔhaw no ho dwuma wɔ ɔkwan bi so sɛ patching Orchard wɔ beae no antumi.
2. **Hwɛ sɛ Quantum recoverability.** Wɔyɛɛ Ironwood sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ne sika bɛtumi de ne ho ahyɛ daakye recovery mechanism mu sɛ wɔbubu discrete log assumptions a ɛwɔ aseɛ no da bi a.
3. **Twe nsakraeɛ a ɛwɔ dwumadie mu no so tew.** Sɛ anka wɔbɛhyehyɛ shielded protocol foforɔ koraa no, wɔsan de Orchard's architecture a wɔada no adi no dii dwuma. Na eyi kyerɛ sɛ na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔkyerɛw asɛm foforo a wɔde di gua, proving, sika kotoku, ne hardware sika kotoku ho mmara fi mfiase koraa.

### Ɔmanfo bere nhyehyɛe

- May 29: Wɔdaa Orchard mmerɛwyɛ no adi.
- June: Wɔabara Orchard nkitahodi bere tiaa bi bere a nea edi kan a wosiesie no fa NU6.2. Wɔ June 12 no, developer titiriw bi de n’ani asi July 21 so sɛ wɔbɛyɛ upgrade no nyinaa; akyiri yi ɛhwe ase dapɛn biako. Wɔkaa mpɔtam hɔfo haw sɛ Keystone wuranom bɛyɛ nea etwa to a wɔatu akɔtra baabi foforo, afei core developer bi bua sɛ Keystone bɛboa Ironwood fi da a edi kan, a firmware de wɔn ani asi so sɛ July 3, retwɛn sɛ wɔawie signing constants ne Orchard API nsakrae a efi Zcash core.
- July mfiase: NU6.3 du testnet so; Zebra 6.0.0 po so ahyɛn July 10.
- July 27–28: Ledger's Ironwood PCZT v2 nsaano nkyerɛwee adwuma no ka bom yɛ nkabom baa dwumadibea. Ironwood (NU6.3) yɛ adwuma July 28 wɔ block 3,428,143, na ɛkata Orchard pool a edi kan no mu wɔ turnstile akyi a ɛkata dodow a ZEC betumi agyaw Orchard wɔ sika dodow a wɔde guu mu wɔ mmara kwan so no so, na ɛde asɛm foforo a wɔde di dwuma no ba.

Zebra bɛyɛ node a wɔhwehwɛ, ɛfiri sɛ zcashd ntumi mmoa mmara foforɔ a ɛfa adwene a wɔhyia ho no. Address ahorow nsakra efisɛ Ironwood san de Orchard's receiver nhyehyɛe no di dwuma, enti sika kotoku de address ahorow a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no kɔ pool foforo no mu ankasa. Nea sika kotoku hia ne atutra dwumadi, efisɛ awiei koraa no ɛsɛ sɛ Orchard sika no wuranom de kɔ Ironwood, na mprempren wɔde adwene a ɛwɔ hɔ siw nneɛma foforo a wonya a wonya Orchard kwan koraa.

### Nea ɛsakrae wɔ mfiridwuma mu

Nkitahodi nhyehyɛe ahorow no fii version 5 kɔɔ version 6, enti asɛm biako betumi de Ironwood fã bi aka Sapling/Orchard/transparent afã horow a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no ho. Wɔtrɛw PCZT mu wɔ anammɔn:

- Encoding fii PCZT v1 kɔɔ v2.
- Mprempren Ironwood bundle a ɛyɛ soronko te Sapling ne Orchard de no nkyɛn.
- v6 nkitahodi ne v6 sighash foforo na wɔboa.
- Hyɛ no nsow sɛ nkyerɛwee a emu da hɔ nyaa nkyerɛase foforo (V3).

Sighash nsakrae no ho hia kɛse ma hardware sika kotoku pɔtee. Wɔ v6 mu no, wɔayi Orchard ne Ironwood anchors no afi signature hash no mu na ɛma data digest a ɛma tumi krataa no nkutoo aduan. Ɛno yɛ firmware nsakrae ankasa, ɛnyɛ version bump kɛkɛ. Ɛsɛ sɛ afiri bi de sighash foforo no di dwuma yiye na ɛsan bu n’ankasa ho akontaa sen sɛ ɛbɛgye nea ɔhokafo app no kyerɛ sɛ ɛpene so no di.

Isolation contract no ankasa ansesa: device no da so ara san de spend authorization signature no nkutoo ba, da `alpha`, na ɛnnyɛ signature a ɛkyekyere no da, host no nso da so ara di proving ne biribiara a aka ho dwuma.

Nanso, signer mmara biako sesae wɔ v6 mu ampa: dummy padding spends no, IO Finalizer na ɛde ne nsa hyɛ ase sen sɛ wɔde bɛhyɛ afiri no ase, efisɛ afiri no nni ɔkwan biara a ɛbɛfa so ahwɛ sɛ ɛyɛ nokware wɔ note data ankasa ho. Device no Signer dwumadie no skips wɔn sene sɛ wɔbɛka akyerɛ wɔn sɛ wɔmfa wɔn nsa nhyɛ biribi a entumi nhwɛ mu. Mprempren sika a wɔada no adi nso bom Orchard ne Ironwood bo a aka no nyinaa bom, efisɛ v6 asɛm bi betumi asɛe sika afi pool biara mu wɔ asɛm koro no ara mu.

Saa pool akontaabu no a wobenya no yiye no ho hia wɔ nneyɛe mu, ɛnyɛ nsusuwii mu nkutoo. Nnansa yi bɔne bi a ɛwɔ Brave's ankasa Zcash sika kotoku mu yɛ mfatoho a mfaso wɔ so: ne ka ho akontaabu no faa no sɛ nneɛma a wɔde ahyɛ mu a wɔabɔ ho ban nyinaa yɛ Orchard pool a ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw no dea, na ɛde nneyɛe a wɔayɛ no abien ne sikatua ho akontaabu a ɛnteɛ ba bere biara a asɛm bi fa Ironwood. Ɛno ne mfomso kuw a "recompute it yourself, dont trust the host" nnyinasosɛm a ɛwɔ hardware wallet signing akyi no wɔ hɔ sɛ wɔbɛkyere no pɛpɛɛpɛ. Ɔhokafo sika kotoku bi nyaa pool akontaabu a ɛnteɛ na mfiri bi a ɛde ne ho hwɛ n’ankasa akontaabu nkutoo na ɛbɛhyɛ saa frankaa ansa na ɔde ne nsa ahyɛ ase.

### Baabi a obiara a ɔde ne nsa ahyɛ ase no gyina

**Ledger sesaa mfonini no nyinaa kɛkɛ.** Wɔ September 23, 2026 no, Ledger de native shielded Zcash mmoa kɔmaa tẽẽ wɔ Ledger Wallet Desktop mu, na ɛho nhia sɛ ɔyɛ ɔhokafo app a ɛyɛ soronko bio. Wo spending key no tra device no so, kɔmputa no yɛ transaction akontaabu, na wopene biribiara a ɛwɔ screen so ansa na wode wo nsa ahyɛ ase. Ɛboa Ironwood, ɛnyɛ Orchard anaa Sapling atare dedaw no. Nea enye wɔ ho ne sɛ wɔregyae "Zcash Shielded" app a ɛyɛ agyapade a Zondax, yɛe a na ɛhwehwɛ sɛ wɔde ɔhokafo sika kotoku te sɛ Zkool yɛ adwuma no. Ledger retwe saa app dedaw no afi nea ɛbɛba mu wɔ November 5, enti obiara a ɔda so ara de di dwuma no hia sɛ ɔde ne sika kɔ baabi foforo ansa na saa bere no adu.

**Keystone wɔ tebea pa mu.** Firmware 3.0.2 de Ironwood mmoa a edi mũ kaa ho wɔ July mu, na ɛno da so ara yɛ mprempren, ahwehwɛde a ɛyɛ den. Nanso saa nkyerɛase no anaa foforo no ho hia na wode wo nsa ahyɛ biribiara ase.

**Hito anya nkɔsoɔ atwam wɔ testnet a ɛdi kan no mu.** Ɛdii nkonim de ne nsa hyɛɛ aseɛ na ɛde ne Orchard-to-Ironwood asɛm ankasa a ɛdi kan wɔ honam fam hardware (mfitiaseɛ no wɔ testnet so) kɔmaa. Nsakraeɛ a ɛdi akyire no sii mainnet flows (Ironwood→Ironwood, Ironwood→Transparent, ne afoforɔ) so dua wɔ production hardware so. Firmware a ɛyɛ den ne nea wɔayi no adi a ɛtrɛw da so ara rekɔ so.

Zondax ne Zkool reyɛ nsakrae mprempren. Zondax's app no ne nea wɔrekɔ pɛnhyen wɔ Ledger. Zkool, a ɛde ne ho too saa app no so sɛ ɔne Ledger mfiri ahorow bɛkasa no nkyerɛɛ nea ɛbɛba saa dwumadi no so bere a app dedaw no ayera no. Ɛnyɛ nea ɛda adi sɛ ɛkɔ so yɛ nea ɛfata ma Ledger dwumadiefoɔ kɔ n’anim, anaasɛ ne daakye yɛ mfiri foforɔ titire.

Atutra no ankasa ne asorɔkye kɛse a ɛsen biara. Esiane sɛ sika a ɛtwa pools no yɛ ɔmanfoɔ nti, sika kotoku trɛw transfers mu wɔ standard denominations mu wɔ bere mu sen sɛ ɛbɛtu balance wɔ chunk biako a wotumi hu a ɛkyerɛ sɛ nnwuma pii de wɔn nsa hyɛ ase wɔ migration biara mu, na ɛno nti pɛpɛɛpɛ na batch PCZT signing ho hia kɛse ma hardware wallets.

Ledger's firmware adwuma no nso tuu mmirika kɔɔ hardware anohyeto ahorow mu: sika a wɔsɛe no ankasa wɔ nkyerɛwde mu no betumi adu cryptographic syscalls mpempem du du wɔ ahyɛde biako mu, na abubu mfiri a ɔhwɛ so no. Fix no de field arithmetic kɔɔ software so na na ɛhwehwɛ sɛ wɔkyɛ scratch tebea na ɛbɛfata Nano X's stack anohyeto ahorow no mu.
