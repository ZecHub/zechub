# Sɛnea wɔahyehyɛ Zcash

## TL;DR

- Zcash nyɛ adwumakuw biako na ɛkyekyee, ahyehyɛde ahorow pii a wɔde wɔn ho a wɔn mu biara wɔ adwuma no fã soronko na ɛkyekyee
- Wɔ n’abakɔsɛm fã kɛse no ara mu no ahyehyɛde abien na edii nkɔso anim, Electric Coin Company ne Zcash Foundation
- Wɔ Ɔpɛpɔn 2026 mu no, Electric Coin Company kuw no nyinaa gyaee adwuma wɔ nniso ho akasakasa bi akyi, na wɔsan hyehyɛɛ abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom no yɛɛ no ​​akuo ahodoɔ a wɔde wɔn ho
- Ɛnnɛ akuw ahorow na wodi protocol, node software, sika kotoku, nhwehwɛmu, scaling, ne sikasɛm ho dwuma
- Ahyehyɛdeɛ baako biara nni hɔ a ɛdi Zcash so, network no yɛ open source na enni kwan, na ɛkɔɔ so yɛɛ adwuma sɛdeɛ ɛteɛ wɔ nsakraeɛ biara mu

<br/>

## Hena na eyi yɛ ma no

- Wɔn a wɔaba foforo a wɔrebɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛte nea ɔkyekye Zcash na ɔhwɛ so ankasa ase
- Obiara a ahyehyɛde din pii a ɛwɔ abɔde a nkwa wom mu no ama n’adwene atu afra
- Wɔn a wɔde mmoa ma a wɔresi onii a wɔne wɔn bɛyɛ adwuma anaa baabi a wɔde nyansahyɛ bi bɛmena ho gyinae

<br/>

## Nea enti a eyi ho hia

Sɛ yɛte sɛnea wɔahyehyɛ no ase a, ɛma biribiara a aka no yɛ mmerɛw. Ɛkyerɛ wo onii a ɔhwɛ mmara a wode wo ho to so no so, onii a wobɛkɔ ne nkyɛn akɔhwehwɛ mmoa, ne nea ɔhwɛ netɛw no fã a wodwen ho no so. Ɛsan nso da Zcash ahoɔden a ɛyɛ komm no mu biako adi: esiane sɛ adwuma no atrɛw wɔ akuw a wɔde wɔn ho mu nti, huammɔdi biako biara nni hɔ a entumi nkyere adwuma no anaasɛ ennyae.

Kratafa yi yɛ asase mfonini. Wɔ ahyehyɛde biara a ɛwɔ kratafa mũ dedaw wɔ wiki yi so no, wubehu nsɛm tiawa bi a wɔakyerɛw ne link a wode bɛkenkan pii, sen sɛ wobɛsan ayɛ nea wɔakyerɛw wɔ hɔ no bio.

<br/>

## Sɛnea na ɛyɛ adwuma kan no

Zcash abakɔsɛm fã kɛse no ara mu no, ahyehyɛde abien na edii anim.

Electric Coin Company no hyɛɛ Zcash ase wɔ afe 2016 mu na ɛde nkɔsoɔ kuo titire no mu dodoɔ no ara yɛɛ adwuma. Bootstrap, board bi a wɔnyɛ adwuma a wɔde hwehwɛ mfaso a wɔhyehyɛɛ sɛ wɔmfa mmoa Zcash na ɛhwɛɛ so. Zcash Foundation yɛɛ adwuma kaa ne ho sɛ adwumakuw a ɛde ne ho a ɛnyɛ nea wɔhwehwɛ mfaso, a ɛde n’adwene sii protocol no sohwɛ ne node a ɛde ne ho a wɔkyekyee so. Wɔde block akatua a wɔde sii hɔ maa nkɔso no fã bi na ɛboaa abien no nyinaa.

Saa adum abien nhyehyɛe yi kuraa mu mfe pii, nanso na egyina saa sika a wɔkyɛ no ne ahyehyɛde abien no a wɔbɛkɔ so ayɛ biako no so. Bere a mfitiase nkɔso ho sika no renya nkɔso na ne daakye a ɛbɛkyɛ no ankɔ so ayɛ nea wontumi nsi pi no, asɛmmisa a ɛfa sɛnea wobetua adwuma a ɛkɔ so ho ka ho no yɛɛ nea ɛho hia kɛse. Saa sikasɛm ho asɛmmisa no te nea ɛsakrae akyi no mu dodow no ara akyi, na ɛyɛ nea enti a mprempren akuw binom nya sika kɛse a efi abɔnten bere a afoforo de wɔn ho to mmoa so no fã.

<br/>

## Afe 2026 nhyehyɛe foforo no

Wɔ January 2026 mu no, nhyehyɛe no sesae kɛse. Wɔ January 7 no, Electric Coin Company panyin Josh Swihart de too gua wɔ X so sɛ adwumakuw no kuw no nyinaa agyae adwuma.

Bootstrap yɛ adwumayɛkuo a wɔnyɛ adwuma a wɔde hwehwɛ mfasoɔ a wɔhyehyɛɛ no ​​afe 2020 sɛ wɔmfa nni Electric Coin Company a na abɛyɛ ne fã a ɛyɛ ne dea nyinaa so. Adwene a enhyia a ɛda adwumakuw kuw no ne saa bagua yi ntam no yɛɛ kɛse bere kɔɔ so na ɛkaa nsɛm pii, a nea ɛka ho ne ahyehyɛde no akwankyerɛ, sɛnea ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde sika boa nkɔso, ne Zashi sika kotoku a kuw no pɛ sɛ wotu kɔ ankorankoro adwumakuw bi mu kɔboaboa abɔnten sika kɛse ano no daakye. Swihart kaa kɔ a wɔfirii no ho asɛm sɛ ɛyɛ adwuma a ɛma nkɔsoɔ, mmara mu asɛmfua a ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔsesaa tebea ahodoɔ no kɛseɛ araa ma wɔhyɛɛ sɛ wɔgyae adwuma no wɔ ɔkwan a etu mpɔn so, na ɔkaa sɛ adwumakuo no mu dodoɔ no ara atu akɔ baabi foforɔ a ɛne Zcash asɛmpatrɛ adwuma no nhyia.

Akontaabu no fã foforo no ho hia ma atɛntrenee. Bootstrap hyehyɛɛ ntawntawdi no sɛ ɛyɛ nniso ne mmara a wɔdi so a wɔnyɛ adwuma a mfaso wɔ so ho asɛm. Zcash a ɔhyehyɛɛ no, Zooko Wilcox, yii board mufo a wɔabobɔ wɔn din wɔ akasakasa no mu no ano wɔ baguam, na ɔkae sɛ ɔne wɔn ayɛ adwuma mfe pii na obu wɔn sɛ nnipa a wodi nokware kɛse, bere a ɔdaa no adi pefee sɛ onni afã biara wɔ adwene a enhyia no ankasa ho.

Ná nneɛma abien a akyinnyegye nni ho. Ɔfã biara ammɔ nsɛmmɔnedi biara ho asɛm, enti na eyi yɛ adwumakuw ne nniso mu adwene a enhyia mmom sen sɛ ɛbɛyɛ mmara kwan so asɛm. Na Zcash network no ankasa annya nkɛntɛnso biara, ɛkɔɔ so yɛɛ open source, na enni kwan, ahobammɔ wom, na ɛyɛ adwuma koraa wɔ ne nyinaa mu, asɛm a Swihart ne Wilcox nyinaa sii so dua kyerɛɛ wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no.

Nea edii akyi bae ne nhyehyɛe foforo mmom sen sɛ ɛbɛhwe ase. Kan adwumakuw kuw no kɔɔ so hyehyɛɛ ZODL akyiri yi wɔ afe 2026 mu, na wɔtew wɔn ho kan Bootstrap board mufo baasa hyehyɛɛ Sovright. Nkɔsoɔ kɔɔ so yɛɛ adwuma wɔ ɔkwan a wɔakyekyɛ mu kɛseɛ wɔ akuo ahodoɔ pii a wɔde wɔn ho mu.

Nsɛm a wɔaka ho asɛm wɔ ha no, wɔkaa no baguam wɔ X so wɔ Ɔpɛpɔn 7, 2026, a Josh Swihart (@jswihart) ne Zooko Wilcox (@zooko) na wɔkaa no, baabi a wobetumi akenkan mfitiaseɛ nsɛm no nyinaa.

<br/>

## Hena na ɔkyekye Zcash mprempren

Adwuma a wɔyɛ no nnɛ no atrɛw wɔ ahyehyɛde ahorow a wɔde wɔn ho mu, na emu biara wɔ ɔfã bi a emu da hɔ.

### Ahyehyɛde abien a efi afe 2026 mu no mu paapaee

1. ZODL, Zcash Open Development Lab, no, kan Electric Coin Company kuw no na wɔhyehyɛɛ no ​​na Josh Swihart na odii anim. Ɛboaboaa bɛboro dɔla ɔpepem aduonu nnum ano fii abɔnten sikakorafoɔ hɔ na ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ core protocol nkɔsoɔ ho, a Halo 2 proving system a ɛma Zcash shielded transactions foforɔ koraa no tumi ka ho, ne ZODL wallet so, shielded by default mobile wallet a kan no na wɔfrɛ no Zashi. Hwɛ [ZODL](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zodl).
2. Sovright yɛ adwumakuw a ɛnyɛ nea wɔhwehwɛ mfaso a kan Bootstrap board mufo baasa na wɔhyehyɛe. Ɛtwe adwene si nnwinnade ne mmoa a wɔde ma abɔde a nkwa wom so, na ɛkyekyee Argos, adwinnade a ɛbɛboa wɔn a wodi kan de di dwuma no ma wɔasan anya sika a akɔda sika kotoku dedaw bi a wɔanhwɛ so yiye mu. Hwɛ [Sovright na ɔkyerɛwee](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/sovright).

### Protocol sohwɛ, nhwehwɛmu, ne node software

3. Zcash Foundation no hwɛ Zebra, Rust node a ɛbɛyɛ network no node titiriw bere a zcashd afɛfo dedaw no akɔ pɛnhyen. Ɛsan nso hwɛ Zcash GitHub ahyehyɛdeɛ, z.cash wɛbsaet, ne Zcash akonta titire a ɛwɔ X so, na ɛne ZecHub yɛ adwuma bom de boa ma wɔhwɛ saa agyapadeɛ no bi so. Hwɛ [Zcash Foundation](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zcash-foundation).
4. Shielded Labs yɛ adwumakuw a ɛde ne ho, a wɔde ntoboa na ɛyɛ adwuma a ɛnyɛ nea wɔhwehwɛ mfaso a ɛwɔ Switzerland. Ɛtwe adwene si nhwehwɛmu ne bere tenten mu nkɔsoɔ so, a network sustainability mechanism a ɛma daakye nkɔsoɔ ho ka ne Crosslink adwuma a ɛfa adanseɛ a ɛkyerɛ sɛ stake finality bɛka Zcash ho, na ɛboaa ahobanbɔ akontabuo a ɛhunuu Orchard pool mmerɛwyɛ wɔ afe 2026. Hwɛ [Labs a Wɔabɔ ho Ban](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/shielded-labs).
5. Electric Coin Company no da so ara yɛ abakɔsɛm no fã sɛ ahyehyɛdeɛ a ɛbɔɔ na ɛde Zcash sii hɔ wɔ afe 2016. Hwɛ [Electric Coin Company](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/electric-coin-company).

### Scaling ne cryptography a wɔde kyerɛw nsɛm

6. Project Tachyon yɛ scaling mmɔdenbɔ a cryptographer Sean Bowe di anim. Ɛhyɛ ɔkwan foforo a sika kotoku bɛfa so ne blockchain no ayɛ biako, a wɔfrɛ no oblivious synchronization, a ɛtew nkitahodi so na, sɛ nea efi mu ba no, ɛde Zcash kɔ post-quantum kokoamsɛm so ho nyansahyɛ ma. Wɔakyerɛw n’adwuma no wɔ [tachyon.z.sika a wɔde yɛ adwuma](https://tachyon.z.cash/).
7. Valar Kuo no yɛ cryptography nhwehwɛmu ne engineering lab a ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ Zcash protocol a ɛfa kokoam, post-quantum digital cash wɔ scale mu. Ɛne Project Tachyon yɛ adwuma bom yiye wɔ scaling ne quantum adwuma no ho. N’adwuma ho nsɛm pii wɔ [valargroup.dev](https://valargroup.dev/).

### Ɔmantam ne mpɔtam hɔ ahyehyɛde ahorow

8. Obscura Labs yɛ ahyehyɛdeɛ a ɛde ne ho a wɔakyerɛw ne din wɔ Nigeria, a ɛde n’adwene si Afrika ne gua a ɛrenya nkɔsoɔ so, ɛkyekyere nnwuma ne akwan a wɔfa so gye wɔn tom. Hwɛ [Obscura Labs a ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/obscura-labs).

### Nwomasua

9. ZecHub yɛ nwomasua beaeɛ a wɔde ama Zcash. Mpɔtam hɔfoɔ bom yɛ adwuma de yɛ, gye tom, na wɔhyɛ nneɛma a ɛboa nkurɔfoɔ ma wɔte abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom ase na wɔsua sɛdeɛ wɔde wɔn ho bɛhyɛ mu, denam nkyerɛkyerɛ, wiki nkrataa, podcast, ne dapɛn dapɛn nsɛmma nwoma so. Wiki a worekenkan seesei no yɛ ZecHub fã, na Zcash Foundation ne no yɛ adwuma boa de boa ma wɔhwɛ mpɔtam hɔ nneɛma bi so.

### Sika a wɔde ma

10. Zcash Community Grants de sika ma wɔn a wɔde wɔn ho ntoboa ne mpɔtam hɔ nnwuma fi block akatua no fã bi mu, na ɛtrɛw adwuma mu wɔ akuw pii mu kɔ akyiri sen ahyehyɛde atitiriw no. Hwɛ [Zcash Mpɔtam Hɔ Mmoa a Wɔde Ma](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zcash-community-grants).
11. Sikasɛm mu Kokoamsɛm Fapem no boa Zcash abɔdeɛ a nkwa wom ne mpɔtam hɔ nnwuma. Hwɛ [Sikasɛm mu Kokoam Nsɛm Fapem](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/financial-privacy-foundation).

Saa ahyehyɛdeɛ yi nyinaa hwɛ open source repositories so, enti obiara bɛtumi akenkan wɔn adwuma, ahwɛ, na ɔde asi so. Na ahyehyɛde ahorow no nyɛ asɛm no nyinaa. Ntoboa atitiriw pii fi ankorankoro ne nnwumakuw a wɔayɛ apam a wɔnam mmoa so de sika ma, sen sɛ ebefi ahyehyɛde atitiriw no nkutoo hɔ. Wɔn nkyɛn no, sika kotokuo akuo, mpɔtam hɔfoɔ, wɔn a wɔde wɔn ho, ne sikasɛm mu asisifoɔ a wɔkura na wɔboa ZEC a wɔnsi protocol no. Nea wɔahyehyɛ wɔ atifi hɔ no ne akyi dompe, na ɛnyɛ mfonini no nyinaa.

<br/>

## Baabi a wubefi ase sɛ obi a waba foforo

Ahyehyɛde bɛn na ɛho hia wo no gyina nea wopɛ sɛ woyɛ so.

1. Sɛ wode Zcash bedi dwuma a, wopɛ sika kotoku, enti ZODL ne ne sika kotoku yɛ abɔde mu mfiase.
2. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ woyɛ node bi anaa wobɛte network software no ase a, hwɛ Zcash Foundation ne ne Zebra node no.
3. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wode sika ma adwuma bi anaasɛ wode sika bɛboa adwuma a wotua ho ka a, hwɛ Zcash Community Grants.
4. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wodi nhwehwɛmu ne daakye a ɛbɛba wɔ protocol no ho akyi a, di Shielded Labs, Project Tachyon, ne Valar Kuw no akyi.

## Adwene a ɛnteɛ a wɔtaa nya

- Zcash nyɛ adwumakuw biako biara dea anaasɛ ɛhwɛ so, ahyehyɛde biako biara nni hɔ a n’ankasa ntumi nsakra anaasɛ ennyae ntwamutam no
- Afe 2026 akasakasa no anhaw network, sika, anaa kokoam nsɛm, na ɛyɛ ahyehyɛde no adwene a enhyia, na protocol no kɔɔ so sɛnea ɛte wɔ ne nyinaa mu
- Kuw a wofii Electric Coin Company no anwie Zcash, adwuma no kɔɔ ahyehyɛde foforo a wɔde wɔn ho mu
- Ahyehyɛde pii a wowɔ no yɛ ahoɔden, ɛnyɛ mmerɛwyɛ, eyi huammɔdi nsɛntitiriw biako na ɛma adwuma no kɔ so gyina ano
- ZEC a wɔbɛkura anaa wɔbɛhyɛ ho nkuran no ne Zcash a wɔbɛkyekyere no nyɛ pɛ, sikakorafoɔ ne asɛmpakafoɔ yɛ mpɔtam hɔfoɔ no fã nanso wɔyɛ soronko wɔ akuo a wɔyɛ protocol no ho
