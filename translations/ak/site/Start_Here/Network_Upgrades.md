# Zcash Network Nkɔsoɔ a Wɔayɛ

Zcash nam network upgrades so tu mpɔn: nsakraeɛ a wɔayɛ no pɛpɛɛpɛ wɔ mmara a node biara pene so, emu biara yɛ adwuma wɔ set block height. Upgrade biara a ɛwɔ aseɛ ha no wɔ n’ankasa krataafa a ɛkyerɛkyerɛ mu, wɔ kasa a ɛnyɛ den mu, deɛ ɛsesaeɛ ne deɛ enti a ɛsesaeɛ. Ɛyɛ foforo wɔ Zcash? Kenkan wɔn nnidiso nnidiso, efi Sprout kosi Ironwood.

Sɛ wopɛ asɛm a wɔde aniwa hu a ɛfa sɛnea Zcash's kokoamsɛm akɔ so wɔ saa nkɔsoɔ yi nyinaa mu a, hwɛ [Nkɔso a Ɛba wɔ Kokoam Nsɛm Ho](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-evolution). Kratafa yi ne index no. Ɛno ne bere nhyehyɛe.

Wɔakyerɛ nna no wɔ UTC mu. Dashboard ahorow bi kyerɛ wɔn wɔ mpɔtam hɔ bere mu, a ɛyɛ block koro no ara ne bere koro no ara. Fixed trigger ma upgrade biara ne ne activation block sorokɔ, ɛnyɛ kalenda da.

### NU7 tebea

NU7 wɔ ɔmanfo Testnet so wɔ ne sorokɔ **4,465,026** (wɔyɛɛ adwuma 4 Ɔkɔtɔberɛ 2026). Mainnet dwumadie no sorokɔ yɛ **3,543,405** ([ZIP 259 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0259)), a wɔde asi wɔn ani so sɛ **5 November 2026**.

Nneɛma a Ɛwɔ Hɔ (ZIP 259): .

- **25-second blocks** (ZIP 218) — nea edi kan a wosi so dua ~3× ntɛmntɛm; da biara da ZEC a wɔde ma no nsakrae.
- **Network Sustainability Mechanism** (ZIP 235 + ZIP 237) — 60% a wɔayi afiri a wɔde di gua no mu akɔ reserve, a wɔsan de maa wɔn a wɔtu fagudeɛ wɔ nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔde bɛkora so fã a ɛfiri ~February 2031.
- **Sprout retirement** (ZIP 2003) — version 4 nkitahodi ahorow no bɛyɛ nea enni mu; Sprout sika a aka no bɛyɛ nea wontumi mfa nni dwuma.
- Adeyɛ anohyeto a wɔabɔ ho ban ne mfɛnsere a ɛtrɛw a ɛyɛ den sɛ wɔbɛkyekyere (17 → 102 blocks).

Wɔmfa nkitahodi nhyehyɛe foforo biara mma. Baa dwumadibea ID a wɔpene so: `0x77190ad9`.

Hwɛ sɛnea [Nkɔso a Ɛba wɔ Kokoam Nsɛm Ho](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-evolution) bere nhyehyɛe ne [ZIP 259 na ɛwɔ hɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0259) sɛ wopɛ nsɛm a edi mũ a. Forum bere nhyehyɛe: [NU7 Bere nhyehyɛe](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/nu7-timeline/57655).
