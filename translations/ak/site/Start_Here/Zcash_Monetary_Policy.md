# Zcash Mfitiasesɛm

## Dɛn ne Zcash?

Zcash's sika nnyinaso no yɛ pɛ ne Bitcoin's fixed supply a ɛyɛ ɔpepem 21 ZEC sika units. Sikɔne 75 biara, foforo [si kwan](https://zcash.readthedocs.io/en/latest/rtd_pages/glossary.html#:~:text=Block,mempool%20in%20an%20unconfirmed%20state.) yɛ mined kɔ Zcash blockchain na block akatua a ɛyɛ 1.5625 ZEC ba circulation. Wɔkyekyɛ saa block akatua yi ma wɔn a wotu fagude ne.. [Zcash nkɔso sikakorabea](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1014).

Block akatua dodow no so tew fã bɛyɛ mfe anan biara kosi sɛ ZEC ɔpepem 21 no nyinaa bɛkɔ so. Ɛkame ayɛ sɛ Zcash inflation suasua Bitcoin. Ɛho hia sɛ yɛhyɛ no nsow sɛ bere a wɔbɔ sika foforo no, nneɛma bo kɔ fam, na bere biara a ɛso tew fã no, dodow no so tew kɛse.
