# Livestream Nhyehyɛe

**Nea wobɛhia:**

- Video camera a HDMI output a enni overlays
- Tripod a wɔde di dwuma
- HDMI-to-USB video a wɔkyere (2x) .
- HDMI a wɔde kyere nkyerɛkyerɛmu slides/demo (sɛ eye sɛ wireless a) .
- Audio feed fi mixer (ɛyɛ papa sɛ wireless a) .
- Laptop a etumi de OBS anaa software a ɛte saa di dwuma (eye sen biara wɔ ethernet mu) .

*Botae a ɛwɔ saa kit yi mu ne sɛ ɛyɛ papa na wotumi fa so.*

Akwan bi wɔ hɔ a wobɛfa so de ano aduru a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a wobetumi anya (te sɛ telefon a wode bedi dwuma sen sɛ wode mfoninitwa afiri bedi dwuma) a wɔbɛka ho asɛm wɔ wiki post foforo mu besi saa nnwinnade yi bi ananmu.

Nhwɛsoɔ nhyehyɛeɛ sɛdeɛ wɔbɛka mfiri nyinaa abɔ mu ama streaming. Ɔkyerɛwfo: [decentralistdan](https://twitter.com/decentralistdan)
