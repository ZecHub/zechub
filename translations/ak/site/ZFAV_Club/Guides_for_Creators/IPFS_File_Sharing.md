# P2P Fael Kyɛfa ne IPFS ne Brave Browser

Yebetumi de IPFS adi dwuma de akyɛ media fael akɛse wɔ yɛn ntam wɔ Peer to-Peer kwan so.

Enti dɛn ne ɔhaw no: IPFS apon no nyɛ adwuma mma fael akɛse, wɔbɛtwa mu.

**Ahwehwɛde ahorow**: [IPFS Desktop](https://docs.ipfs.tech/install/ipfs-desktop/) + [Browser a Brave](https://brave.com/)

tldr, Video no wɔ n’awiei.

Yebetumi de eyi adi dwuma wɔ yɛn ntam wɔ ZF AV Club no mu ama transferring media fael akɛse na yɛakyerɛw ama mo nyinaa efisɛ ne anigye ne super cool!!

*PLUS*, yɛne IPFS kuw a ɛwɔ Protocol Labs no bɛyɛ adwuma abom tẽẽ de ayɛ saa dwumadie asɛm yi.
Mfaso foforo a efi nkitahodi ahorow a wɔyɛe wɔ dWeb Camp mu bae!

Sɛ wɔmfa IPFS nhyɛ afiri a ɛgye no so NA wode browser a ɛnyɛ Brave redi dwuma a: Ɛbɛhia sɛ wotwɛn ma aponkɛseɛ no bɛhyɛ fael no. *Eyi bɛsan de 504 mfomso aba fael akɛse so.*

Next: Sɛnea wobɛ pin peoples afoforo fael wɔ wo mfiri so sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a yɛrennya point of failure problem biako bere a mfitiase node no kɔ offline.
