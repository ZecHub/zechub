# AI nnwinnade: Nsɛm, Mfonini, Video, Ɔdio a wɔyɛ (intanɛt so) .

Nsɛm a wɔahyehyɛ a ɛbɛma asetra ayɛ mmerɛw ni. Sɛnea wɔde bedi dwuma no yɛ ade foforo.

Nsɛm a ɛkanyan adwene a eye sen biara a wɔkyerɛw no wɔ adesua kwan. Ɛyɛ yiye sɛ wubetumi ayɛ mfitiasede ahorow no yiye ntɛmntɛm.

## Text generating/ Aboafoɔ

[Nhwehwɛmufo a wɔyɛ ntɛm](https://novelai.net/tokenizer) (tokenizer) de sua sɛnea prompts yɛ adwuma ne token dodow a wɔde di dwuma.

Wubetumi abisa Chatbot aboafo bere nyinaa sɛ wɔmmoa mma wɔmfa nkɔmmɔ pa mma wo.

### Nnwinnade a wontua hwee:

- [Anthropic nhoma Claude na ɔkyerɛwee](https://claude.ai/) - betumi ahia VPN na woakyerɛw wo din (US nkutoo) .
- [NkɔmmɔbɔGPT 3.5](https://chat.openai.com/) - bisa telefon nɔma
- [Bing Nkɔmmɔbɔ (GPT4)](https://www.bing.com/search?q=Bing+AI&showconv=1&FORM=hpcodx) - a woahyehye no anohyeto da biara
- [Llama 2 - 70B. Ɔde ne nsa kyerɛɛ ne so](https://www.llama2.ai/) - a wontua hwee wo wɛbsaet so

### Nnwinnade a wotua ho ka:

- [Free2Z Nkɔmmɔbɔ2Z](https://free2z.com/ai) - tua ZEC / 2Zs ma GPT-3.5 ne GPT-4 cents/prompt
- [Nkɔmmɔbɔ GPT-4](https://chat.openai.com/auth/login) - $20/ɔsram biara.
