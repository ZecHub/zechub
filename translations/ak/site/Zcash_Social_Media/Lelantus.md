# Nimdeɛ a Ɛwɔ Zero kosi Zero: Lelantus Protocol

**Series:** Zero kosi Zero Nimdeɛ

Ɛnnɛ yɛhwɛ **Lelantus**!

Wɔyii no adi wɔ afe 2019 mu, saa protocol yi si Zerocoin so. Wɔde di dwuma wɔ **Firo** sika (kan no na wɔfrɛ no Zcoin) mu de ma kokoam on-chain nkitahodi tumi yɛ adwuma. Ɛte sɛ Zcash wɔ akwan bi so nanso ɛsono no pefee wɔ afã dodow no ara mu.

![Lelantus nnianim asɛm](/content-images/Fsk18DgXsAEc0Ob-a8cd9a85d1.webp)

---

## Zcash vs Firo Protocol Fapem ahorow

- **Zcash** - Ɛkyekyere **Zerocash** protocol no so 
- **Firo (Zcoin)** - Ɛkyekyere **Zerocoin** protocol no so

![Zerocash vs Zerocoin ntotoho](/content-images/Fsk2Fk7WcAA81ty-92158edf6b.webp)

---

## Firo Privacy Protocols no nkɔso

Te sɛ Zcash no, Firo de address ahorow a wɔabɔ ho ban di dwuma de tua sika a wɔmmɔ din.

**Bere nhyehyɛe:**
- **Zerocoin** - Nnyigyei a abubu
- **Sigma** - Asɔre nhyehyɛe a wɔahyɛ da ayɛ
- **Lelantus 1.0** - Enni ahobammɔ ho adanse a ɛteɛ

![Protocol adannandi](/content-images/Fsk2NdaWAAAKVgH-f84ae27c48.webp)

---

## Anohyeto ahorow a ɛwɔ Sigma Protocol no mu

Na Σ (Sigma) protocol a wɔde dii dwuma wɔ Zcoin/Firo nkyerɛase ahorow a atwam no mu no wɔ anohyeto kɛse: na wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no betumi ayɛ mint fixed denominations nkutoo.

Eyi maa anonymity sets nketewa bae na ebuee ɔpon maa bere ntua wɔ mint ne redeem adwumayɛ ntam (ne "tainted change" haw no).

![Sigma asɔre ahorow](/content-images/Fsk2fxfWcAMUBDo-333a8f9df3.webp)

---

## Sɛnea Lelantus Ma Kokoam Nsɛm Tu mpɔn

**Lelantus** di fixed denomination asɛm no ho dwuma denam mints a wɔma ho kwan fi set kɛse biako mu no so.

Mfaso atitiriw a ɛwɔ so:
- Yi fixed denomination anonymity nhyehyɛe ahorow fi hɔ
- Ɛtew bere ntua a ɛda ɔhyew/gye ntam no so
- Yi nsakrae ho haw a ɛho agu fĩ no fi hɔ

**Anohyeto**: Set size mprempren wɔahyɛ no **65,000 sika**.

![Lelantus mfaso horow](/content-images/Fsk2wK3X0AA6MEe-06f29b3621.webp)

---

## Sika a Wɔde Yɛ Adwuma

**sika bɔhyɛ** yɛ bɔhyɛ a wɔde anifurae abien ahyɛ mu a ɛkyerɛw sika no serial nɔma ne sika no bo.

Eyinom yɛ adwuma te sɛ **Notes** wɔ Zcash mu.

Wɔtintim sika no bɔhyɛ no na wɔkora so wɔ ledger no so bere a wɔabɔ sika no (ɛnam Mint anaa Spend nkitahodi so).

![Coin ahofama ho mfonini](/content-images/Fsk3AWNX0AIHya8-0ed01a73c1.webp)

---

## Basecoin a wɔde yɛ adwuma < - > Zerocoin Nhwɛsode

Lelantus de **basecoin a wɔagye din no di dwuma < - > zerocoin** nhwɛsoɔ.

**Adeɛ a ɛho hia**: Afei de agyede fã bi ayɛ yie berɛ a wɔde nea aka ne sika dodoɔ no sie.

Te sɛ Zcash no, ɛsɛ sɛ nea ɔde di dwuma no paw nkitahodi a ɛda adi pefee.

![Lelantus nsuo a ɛsen](/content-images/Fsk3HrjXgAMgqmX-4d727febf5.webp)

## Adanse Pii Mu Baako

Lelantus de **One-of-Many Proofs** di dwuma de yi input values ​​a ɛho hia ma adanse a ɛkyerɛ sɛ ɛkari pɛ a ɛnna input no mfiase adi - na enhia sɛ wɔyɛ nhyehyɛe a wotumi de ho to so.

Wɔde saa adanseɛ yi nso di dwuma wɔ **Triptych** (wɔaka ho asɛm wɔ yɛn CryptoNote thread no mu).

![Adanse pii mu Baako](/content-images/Fsk3Z0nWIAAPD4k-b76f087018.webp)

## Network Layer Nsɛm a Wɔde Sie: Dandelion++

Firo nodes de Network Magic koro no ara di dwuma sɛ Zcash Magicbean.

Te sɛ Monero no, Firo de **Dandelion++** dii dwuma de kokoamsɛm kaa ho denam IP address a ɔde bɛkata asɛm no ho amanneɛbɔfo no so.

**Dandelion ++ fa ahorow:**
- **Stem phase** - Wɔde nkitahodi no kɔ random node biako so sen sɛ wɔde bɛkɔ atipɛnfo nyinaa so
- **Fluff phase** - Randomly initiated, afei ɛdan kɔ normal gossip mode so

Eyi ma ɛyɛ den kɛse sɛ wobɛhwehwɛ baabi a asɛm bi fi bae denam nkitahodi nhyehyɛe mu nhwehwɛmu so.

![Dandelion++ nkyerɛkyerɛmu](/content-images/Fsk4A8VWcAU84MR-538b054cab.webp)

## Daakye: Lelantus-Spark

**Lelantus-Spark** (wɔayɛ nhyehyɛɛ ama akyiri yi 2023) de akwan mmienu a wɔfa so hu opt-in a wɔde **ZIP-32 style derivation** ne address ahodoɔ di dwuma ba.

Ɛbɛsan nso de mmoa aka ho ama:
- Multisig
- Ahintasɛm Agyapade a Ɔdefo Akyerɛkyerɛ Mu

Saa nneɛma yi ne Zcash Shielded Assets di nsɛ.
