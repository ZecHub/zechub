# Zcash Podcast le Digital Cash Network ŋu
![Screenshot_2023-01-22_21-50-14](/content-images/213973803-b0629c92-08d4-459b-99f1-594854-6272d02af6.webp)

Zcash Podcast naa emetsonu siwo ƒe nyawo woɖe yeyee, nyati ƒe nyadzɔdzɔ kɔkɔe siwo me woɖo ɖe eme tsitotsito, kple bubuwo, na Zcash ƒe nuto xoxo la be woakpɔ eye woama. Enaa mɔ hã na Digital Cash Network channel ƒe amedzro siwo le mɔ xexlẽ la be woanya Zcash nyuie wu. 

Le nyateƒe me la, channel sia ɖo ŋku edzi be:

* Wòanɔ agbe me abe ame si me bank mele o le cryptocurrency dzi eye nàma wò agbe blibo katã naɖu eɖokui dzi.
* Wòaɖe nuto si le be woakpɔ dzidzɔ le Zcash ƒe gɔmeɖoɖowo kple mɔ̃ɖaŋu la ŋu wu crypto ƒe amesiamina ƒe nuto la.

Tsɔ kpe ɖe eŋu la, live ƒe mɔnu la nana ƒeɖekawɔwɔ nava le audience la kple channel la dome, eye woate ŋu anɔ eme eye woabia biabiawo le live me na host la kple amedzrowo. Esia dze be woatsɔ aɖo fan yeyewo gbɔ, ke hã ena mɔ be woakpɔ emetsonu didi la megbe.
