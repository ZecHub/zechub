# PGP* na Crypto Podcast
![bannerpgp](/content-images/221758326-06cea3f5-4c9e-4466-b9ee-738546-01f88aec39.webp)

Woezɔ na “PGP* (Pretty Good Policy) na Crypto Podcast," si nye Electric Coin Co ƒe dɔwɔwɔ aɖe.!

Miele gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu [blockchain] ƒe mɔ̃nu, dukɔmevi ƒe politika, kple xexeame sewo ƒe nyawo ƒe nuto. Míaƒe taɖodzinu nye be míana nunya sesẽ kple amedzrowo ƒe nyagblɔɖiwo míaƒe toviwo tso nya vevi siwo le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu kple cryptocurrency ƒe etsɔme ŋgɔ ƒe mɔnukpɔkpɔme.

Àse dɔwɔƒe la ƒe dzikpɔlawo, dukɔƒe dɔwɔlawo, kple sukɔlawo ƒe nyamedzodzro siwo le ŋgɔ na dukɔmevi ƒe politika ƒe nɔnɔme si le blockchain technology ŋu wɔwɔ me. Míaƒe amedzroawo ama woƒe ŋutilã ƒe nukpɔkpɔ vovovo tso kuxiwo kple mɔnuwo siwo le dɔwɔƒe la ŋgɔ, eye tso ale si fifia sewo, se siwo wole bɔbɔm, kple politika ƒe mɔnuwo wɔa dɔ ɖe blockchain ƒe nɔnɔme ƒe tsitsi kple ŋgɔyiyi ŋu.
