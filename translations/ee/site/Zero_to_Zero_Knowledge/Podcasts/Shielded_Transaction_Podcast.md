# Gaɖoɖo ɣaɣla [Shielded Transactions] Podcast tso Forsight Institute

Foresight Institute nye numekuku kple non-profit ƒe ha si doa alɔ ɖe mɔ nyui dzi be teknoloji siwo ƒe ŋusẽ gã nɔa eme naɖo ŋgɔ.
