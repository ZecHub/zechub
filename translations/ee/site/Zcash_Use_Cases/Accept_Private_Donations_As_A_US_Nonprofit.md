> Axa sia nye nyatakaka siwo ku ɖe nusianu ŋu, ke menye senyawo alo adzɔxexe ŋuti aɖaŋuɖoɖowo o. United States ƒe adzɔxexe ŋuti sewo trɔna, eyata lé ŋku ɖe afɔɖeɖe ɖesiaɖe ŋu le wò akɔntanyala alo adzɔxenyawo ŋuti aɖaŋuɖola gbɔ. Se ɖesiaɖe si le ete la do ƒome kple eƒe IRS dzɔtsoƒe, si wokpɔ le October 2026 me tsɔ sɔ kple Agbalẽ 526 (hena ƒe 2025 ƒe tɔtrɔgbɔ) kple Agbalẽvi 8283 (December 2025 ƒe tɔtrɔ). Woŋlɔ IRS virtual currency FAQ na asitsatsa do ŋgɔ na ƒe 2025, eyata lé ŋku ɖe eŋu ne wotsɔe sɔ kple mɔfiame siwo li fifia.

## TL;DR

- Dzra nudzɔdzɔwo ɖe gakotoku si dzi wokpɔ ta na si dzi miaƒe habɔbɔa kpɔ ŋusẽ ɖo me, ke menye dɔwɔla aɖe ŋutɔ ƒe gakotoku o
- IRS bua ZEC abe nunɔamesi ene, eyata ZEC nunana nye nudzɔdzɔ si menye ga o
- Ne èdi nunana si ƒe home nye dɔlar 250 alo esi wu nenema la, ŋlɔ agbalẽ si me woŋlɔ ZEC la ɖo gake mède dɔlar ƒe asixɔxɔ ɖe edzi o la ɖe nunala la
- Le nunana siwo wu dɔlar 5,000 gome la, nunala la hiã na asixɔxɔ dede eŋu si dze, eye nàde asi woƒe Agbalẽvi 8283 te
- Ne èdzra ZEC le ƒe etɔ̃ me tso esime nèxɔe eye nède asi nunala la ƒe Agbalẽvi 8283 ƒe Akpa B te la, ke tsɔ Agbalẽvi 8282 de eme
- Zi geɖe la, nunala si ƒe ŋkɔ womeyɔ o la mate ŋu abia be woaɖe ga home si nye dɔlar 250 alo esi wu nenema le eme o

## <img src="/content-images/warning-error-svgrepo-com-b7ea8a50da.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="warning icon"/> The problem

Crypto-nunana dɔwɔƒe akpa gãtɔ na United States dɔmenyohabɔbɔwo naa adrɛs si me kɔ la wò, alo trɔa nunana la na wò to ɖeka dzi. Le adrɛs si me kɔ dzi la, amesiame ate ŋu akpɔ nunana ɖesiaɖe, eƒe ga home kple wò ga si susɔ. Nunala si ɖoe koŋ na nu le eɖokui si la bu le adzame ma le ɣeyiɣi si me nunana la va dze anyi.

Agbalẽ siwo ame ɖeka mewɔna o hã le dɔwɔƒe si mekpɔa viɖe aɖeke tso eme o si: nunala ƒe gaxɔgbalẽviwo, IRS nunana gãwo ƒe agbalẽviwo, kple ƒe sia ƒe ƒe gagbugbɔɖoɖo. Ame ŋutɔ ƒe nyawo tsɔtsɔ aɣla kple agbalẽ ma siaa ate ŋu awɔ dɔ, ne èwɔ ɖoɖo ɖe wo ŋu hafi nunana gbãtɔa nava ko.

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Step-by-step guide

### Afɔɖeɖe 1: Ðo nya me le wò ɖoɖoa ŋu

Ŋlɔ agbalẽ, eye nàna wò habɔbɔa nada asi ɖe edzi:
- Amekae ate ŋu aʋu ga, kple nenye be ele be ame eve nada asi ɖe ʋuʋu ɖesiaɖe dzi
- Eɖanye be èdzra nunana ɖesiaɖe enumake alo nèlé ZEC aɖewo ɖe asi o
- Nenye be èxɔa nunana siwo mènya o, kple ga home si nèxɔ

### Afɔɖeɖe 2: Ðo gakotoku aɖe si dzi habɔbɔa kpɔ ŋusẽ ɖo

- Wɔ gakotokua le habɔbɔ ƒe mɔ̃ aɖe dzi, ke menye le ame ŋutɔ ƒe telefon dzi o
- Dzra nuku ƒe nyagbea ɖo le Internet dzi, kple kpovitɔ eve siwo dometɔ ɖesiaɖe ate ŋu aɖo egbɔ
- Ne èdi dziɖuɖu si woama la, kpɔ [Dzra Nutoa me Gaxɔdzikpɔƒe si nye Ame ŋutɔ tɔ dzi](/zcash-use-cases/private-community-treasury)

### Afɔɖeɖe 3: Ta adrɛs si wokpɔ ta na

- Ma adrɛs si ŋu woxe mɔ ɖo ɖeɖeko le wò nudzɔdzɔ ƒe axa dzi. A. A [adrɛs si wowɔ ɖekae](/using-zcash/unified-addresses) dze egɔme kple `u1`, gake ate ŋu anye nusi xɔa ga si me kɔ hã, eye nunana aɖe si tso gakotoku si me kɔ ko me la va dzena le dutoƒo emegbe. Zã wò gakotokua ƒe adrɛs si ŋu wokpɔa akpoxɔnu le ko ne etsɔ ɖeka na wò.
- Mègata adrɛs si me kɔ o (edzea egɔme kple `t1` or `t3`)
- Bia nunala siwo di be yewoaxɔ gaxɔgbalẽvi la be woatsɔ yewoƒe e-mail ade.. [memo](/using-zcash/memos). Wotsɔ nya ɣaɣlawo de nuŋlɔɖia me, eyata miaƒe habɔbɔa koe ate ŋu axlẽe.

### Afɔɖeɖe 4: Ŋlɔ nunana ɖesiaɖe ɖi

Le nunana ɖesiaɖe gome la, ŋlɔe ɖi be:
- Ɣletiŋkeke kple gaƒoƒo si woxɔ
- Ga home si le ZEC
- Asixɔxɔ le United States dɔlar me ɣemaɣi, kple asixɔxɔtsoƒe si nèzã
- Asitsatsa ƒe ID kple nuŋlɔɖi

IRS bua ZEC abe nunɔamesi ene ([Nyatakaka tso ƒe 2014-21 me](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-14-21.pdf)), eyata esia nye nudzɔdzɔ si menye ga o le wò agbalẽwo me. Be nàna wò akɔntanyala nate ŋu axlẽ nu ko la, kpɔ [Nuŋlɔɖiwo wɔwɔ kple ZEC si wotsɔ akpoxɔnu wɔe](/zcash-use-cases/keeping-records-with-shielded-zec).

### Afɔɖeɖe 5: Lɔ̃ ɖe nunana siwo ƒe home nye dɔlar 250 alo esi wu nenema dzi

Nunala mate ŋu aɖe nunana ɖeka si ƒe home nye dɔlar 250 alo esi wu nenema le eme ne meŋlɔ agbalẽ tso gbɔwò o ([Agbalẽ si wota le ƒe 1771 me](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1771.pdf)). Ne èdi ZEC nunana la, tsɔ:
- Wò habɔbɔa ƒe ŋkɔ
- Ɣletiŋkeke si dzi nèxɔe
- Nunana la ŋuti numeɖeɖe, le kpɔɖeŋu me "1.5 ZEC". Mègatsɔ dɔlar ɖeka ƒe asixɔxɔ de edzi o: asixɔxɔ dede nunana la ŋu nye nunala la ƒe dɔ.
- Eɖanye be èna nane tsɔ ɖo eteƒe o, le kpɔɖeŋu me "Wometsɔ adzɔnu alo dɔwɔna aɖeke na ɖe nudzɔdzɔ sia teƒe o."

Ele be wòanɔ nunala la si hafi woatsɔ woƒe adzɔxegbalẽvi ([Agbalẽ si wota 526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p526.pdf)). Zi geɖe la, habɔbɔwo ɖonɛ ɖa hafi ƒe si kplɔe ɖo ƒe January 31 naɖo; eɖoɖo ɖa enumake le bɔbɔe wu.

### Afɔɖeɖe 6: De asi agbalẽvi 8283 te hena nunana siwo wu dɔlar 5,000

Ne nunala aɖe bia be yexɔ ga si wu dɔlar 5,000 la, ehiã be woawɔ asixɔxɔ dede wo ŋu si dze. Crypto mebua dedienɔnɔ si wodzrana le dutoƒo o, eyata asixɔxɔdedea ku ɖe eŋu ([Agbalẽ si wota 526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p526.pdf), [CCA 202302012 ƒe xexlẽdzesi](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-wd/202302012.pdf)). Nunala la tsɔ wò vɛ [Agbalẽvi 8283](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8283.pdf), eye nède asi Akpa B ƒe akpa si wonae la te. Wò asidede agbalẽ te ɖo kpe edzi be yexɔ ZEC la le ŋkeke ma dzi; meɖo kpe nunala la ƒe asixɔxɔ dzi o ([IRS Nyabiasewo, Q36](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions)).

### Afɔɖeɖe 7: Trɔe wòazu dɔlar, ne ehiã

- Kpɔe ɖa be gaɖɔliƒe kawoe xɔa ga si wotsɔ dea gaxɔ me le [Vidzikpɔkpɔ ƒe Asitɔtrɔ](/using-zcash/custodial-exchanges). Ame geɖe xɔa ga si woda ɖe gadzraɖoƒe si me gaglãa le ko.
- Ne tɔwò nye nusi me kɔ ɖeɖeko la, tsɔ ZEC la tso wò gakotoku si ŋu wokpɔ akpoxɔnu le me yi gaɖɔliƒea le afɔɖeɖe ɖeka me, le ɣeyiɣi si me nèdzrae. Ga home ma va zua dutoƒo, gake wò nunana bubuawo kple wò ga si susɔ la nɔa ame ŋutɔ si.
- Ne èdzra ZEC le ƒe etɔ̃ me tso esime nèxɔe, eye nède asi nunala la ƒe Agbalẽvi 8283 ƒe Akpa B te la, tsɔe de agbalẽ me [Agbalẽvi 8282](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8282.pdf) le ŋkeke 125 me tso esime wodzrae eye nàɖo eƒe kɔpi ɖe nunala la ([IRS Nyabiasewo, Q37](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions)).

### Afɔɖeɖe 8: Ka nya ta tso wò ƒe sia ƒe ƒe gagbugbɔɖoɖo ŋu

Gblɔ ZEC nunanawo be wonye nudzɔdzɔ siwo menye ga o le wò Form 990-series return me, kple [Ðoɖowɔɖi M](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-schedule-m-form-990) ne eku ɖe ŋuwò. Bia wò akɔntanyala alesi nàŋlɔ nunana siwo ƒe ŋkɔ womeyɔ o.

## <img src="/content-images/user-svgrepo-com-21adf62b7c.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="user icon"/> Anonymous donors: what they can and can't claim

- Ame ŋutɔ ƒe nyawo tsɔtsɔ aɣla tso amewo gbɔ mesɔ kple ŋkɔmaɖemaɖe tso gbɔwò o. Nunala ate ŋu ana nu le eɖokui si eye wòagama woƒe e-mail ɖe nuŋlɔɖia me kokoko be wòaxɔ gaxɔgbalẽvi.
- Nunala si ƒe ŋkɔ womeyɔ o na wò mate ŋu axɔ akpedada ƒe agbalẽvi o, eyata womate ŋu aɖe nunana ɖeka pɛ si nye dɔlar 250 alo esi wu nenema le eme o.
- Esi wòxɔ dɔlar 5,000 kple edzivɔ la, ehiã hã be woawɔ asixɔxɔ dede eŋu kple wò asidede agbalẽ te le Agbalẽvi 8283 dzi, si fia be woagblɔ amesiwo wonye na wò.
- Le nunala gome la, zi geɖe la, woɖea ZEC nunana si le esi wu ƒe ɖeka le asi si sɔ nu, eye womexea adzɔ ɖe ga si wokpɔ tso nunana la ta o ([IRS Nyabiasewo, Q34–Q35](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions)).

Gblɔ nya sia le wò nudzɔdzɔ ƒe axa dzi, ale be nunalaawo nate ŋu atiae hafi woana nu.
