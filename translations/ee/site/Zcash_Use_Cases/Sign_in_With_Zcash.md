- Ge ɖe eme to ɖaseɖiɖe be nèkpɔ ɖe Zcash gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi [address] dzi, le nyagbeɖeɖe [password] zãzã teƒe
- Ɖoɖo eve le zãm: **kafukafu ƒe dzesi asidede** loo **ɣaɣla [shielded] fexexe [payment] ɖeka si me kɔd le nya kpui [memo] me**
- Esi ɣaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo ɣla balans kple ŋutinya, ɖaseɖiɖe be nèkpɔ ɖe wo dzi maaɖe wo ƒe gaɖoɖowo fia o
- Nuɖoɖo sia nye yɔyɔ. Mɔɖoɖo si woɖo kple ame blibo hã menɔ o, eye dɔwɔɖoɖowo mate ŋu wɔ dɔ kple wo nɔewo o

## <img src="/content-images/warning-error-svgrepo-com-b7ea8a50da.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="warning icon"/> Kuxi La

Ge ɖe eme ƒe mɔ geɖewo ɖea nu aɖe fia:

- **Nyagbeɖeɖe kple email** wɔa akɔnt si wòakɔ kple wòame nyenye, eye wo evea kata dzua le nyatakaka fɔfɔ ƒe xɔlɔwo me
- **Hadome geɖeɖe** gblɔa na ame si naa wòame nyenye ƒe dɔwɔla be afi kae nègea ɖe eme kple ɣe ka
- **Gakotoku [wallet] geɖeɖe le ɖoɖonu siwo ƒe nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi [transparent] me** vɔ̃ wu ale si wòɖe le. Gakotoku kadodo ate ŋu ana nyatakakadzraƒea nakpɔ wòƒe balans blibo kple gaɖoɖo [transaction] ƒe ŋutinya, le tegbee

Zi geɖe la, nèle tiatia wɔm le bɔbɔe kple nyatakaka ɖe fia dome.

## <img src="/content-images/celebration-spark-svgrepo-com-bc98dec7c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="spark icon"/> Nu ka ta Zcash?

Zcash maɖe *ɖaseɖiɖe be nèkpɔ ɖe nu dzi* tso *wòƒe gaɖoɖowo ƒe ɖe fia* gbɔ:

- **Ɣaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo** ɣla balanswo kple gaɖoɖo ƒe ŋutinya, ale be ɖaseɖiɖe be nèkpɔ ɖe ɖeka dzi megblɔ naneke tso nu si nèkpɔ ɖe e dzi o
- **Ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ [encryption] me nya kpuiwo** ate ŋu atsɔ geɖeɖe ƒe kɔd si woazã zi ɖeka koe ayi le gaɖoɖo me le ɣaɣla me
- **Nukpɔsafui [viewing key]wo** naa mɔ na nyatakaka tɔxɛ ɣaɣla [selective disclosure], ale be app aɖe nakpɔ nu si wòhia koe, eye wòagakpɔ nu bubu aɖeke o

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Ale Si Wòwɔ Dɔe

Mɔ eve do go. Wo evea kata wɔe be app la nakpɔ wò ƒe dzesi si le te ɖe mɔɖa dzi, eye nyagbeɖeɖe manɔ e si o.

### Mɔ 1: De kafukafu ƒe dzesi asi

1. App la wɔ kafukafu nyuitɔ si woazã zi ɖeka koe
2. Wò gakotoku de asidede agbalẽ te [signature] na kafukafu ma kple safui si le wò gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi megbe
3. App la kpɔ asidede agbalẽ te la ɖa, eye wòna nège ɖe eme

Wonyaa naneke ɖe go o, eyata adzɔ [fee] aɖeke menɔ o eye mele naxɔ gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe [block] aɖeke ŋɔŋlɔm o. Ɖoɖoɖo si ku ɖe eŋu la nye [ZIP 304, Sapling Gaɖoƒe ƒe Dzesi ƒe Asidede Agbalẽ Te](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0304), si gale nuŋlɔɖi koe, eyata gakotoku ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu na mesaj ƒe asidede agbalẽ te vovoa.

### Mɔ 2: Ɖe eɖe fia kple ɣaɣla fexexe

1. App la wɔ kɔd si woazã zi ɖeka koe eye wòfia fexexe ƒe biabia
2. Nèdo ɣaɣla gaɖoɖo sue aɖe kple kɔd la le nya kpui me
3. App la léa ŋku ɖe nya kpui la ŋu, etsɔa kɔd la sɔa nu, eye wòna nège ɖe eme

Esia wɔ dɔ kple gakotokuwo siwo tše nya kpuiwo kpɔ egbe xoxo, si nye woƒe akpa gãtɔ. Nu si wòaxɔ ɖe eteƒe lae nye be network ƒe adzɔ le eŋu eye nèle kpekpem ɖe dzesiɖeɖe ŋu.

### Ale si woaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi

App aɖe mele be wòadzra wò gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖo be wòate ŋu ade dzesi wò o. Dɔwɔɖoɖo aɖewo tsɔa eƒe hash ƒoa nu kple nusi woɖo ɖe app ma tɔxɛ, ale be nyatakakadzraƒe sia kple sia nakpɔ dzesi vovo, si le te ɖe mɔɖa dzi, na zãla ɖeka. Esia kpɔa mɔ na nyatakakadzraƒewo be woasɔ woƒe nyatakakawo ɖe wo nɔewo gbɔ be woakɔ wòƒe akɔntwo o.

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-toolbox-9bebbb1619.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="toolbox icon"/> Nu siwo Wòele Be Nàtsɔ Atsɔ

Ewɔ be nàse egɔme hafi nàtu nu ɖe eŋu alo nàɖo ŋku edzi.

| | Kafukafu si wode asi | Ɣaɣla fexexe |
|---|---|---|
| Xexlẽ | Bliboa | Network ƒe adzɔ le geɖeɖe sia kple sia ŋu |
| Kabakaba | Tete | Ele kpekpem ɖe dzesiɖeɖe ŋu |
| Gakotoku ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu | Suesue, ZIP 304 nye nuŋlɔɖi | Gã, nya kpuiwo koe wòhia |
| Eɖoa nu ɖe ɖoɖonu me | Ao | Ɛ̃, gaɖoɖo aɖe li |

Ƒomevi ƒe nuƒoƒowo:

- **Akɔnt gbugbɔxɔxɔ mele mɔ gbãtɔ me o.** Safui ƒe bu-bubu fia be akɔnt la bu, negbe app la ɖo mɔ aɖe na gbugbɔxɔxɔ
- **Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi zãzã atsɔ akɔ wò.** Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka zãzã le nyatakakadzraƒe geɖe me agbugbɔ wɔ ŋkuɖiɖi ƒe kuxi la, si tae app tɔxɛ ƒe dzesiwo le vevie
- **Mɔɖoɖo si woɖo kple ame blibo mele o.** Dɔwɔa sia kple sia ƒe eya tɔ ƒe ɖoɖo li, eyata geɖeɖe si wotu na ɖeka mate ŋu awɔ dɔ kple bubu o
- **Menye ame manya ƒe nɔnɔ le eɖokui me o.** Eɣla wòƒe gaɖoɖowo na app la, gake app la ate ŋu akpɔ wò ƒe nɔnɔ me nyuie to nu si nèwɔ ne nège eme vɔ la dzi

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Vodada Siwo Wòele Be Nànɔ Gbɔ Na Wo

- Mègazã kafukafu ƒe kɔd ɖeka zi eve o. Kɔd sia kple sia nale be woazã zi ɖeka koe eye wòawɔ dɔ va seƒoƒo me kaba, ne menye nenema o, woate ŋu agbugbɔ azã kɔd si woɖe le mɔ me
- Mèbia be zãlawo nado ga gã aɖe be woage ɖe eme o. Fexexe la nye ɖaseɖigbalɛ [proof], eyata gaƒoƒo la nale be wòanye sue ŋutɔ
- Mègadzra gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi gɔmeɖeɖe la ɖo ne app tɔxɛ ƒe dzesi ate ŋu awɔ dɔ ma kee
- Mèbu be mesaj ƒe asidede agbalẽ te wɔ dɔ le afi sia kple sia o. Kpɔ gakotoku siwo wò zãlawo le vavã la ɖa
- Mègabu nya kpui be enye nusiawo ɣla le eme vɔ megbe o. Eɖea fia be dɔwɔla la wɔ nu, menye nyagbeɖeɖe o

## <img src="/content-images/checked-checkbox-svgrepo-com-7ea19022da.svg" width="28" height="28" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="done icon"/> Dɔwɔɖoɖo Siwo Le Esia Ɖom Kpɔ

Wotu esiawo na **Zcash Geɖeɖe** ƒe mɔkplɔkplɔ le [ZecHub Hackathon 3.0](https://zechub.wiki/hackathon) me. Wo nye dodokpɔwo le nuwɔwɔ siwo wòwu enu teƒe, eye woɖea fia ale si woate ŋu atu susu ɖeka ke le mɔ vovovowo nu.

- **ZecAuth** - gakotoku kadodo ƒe mɔɖoɖo na Zcash, le nusi WalletConnect wɔ le afi bubuwo me ƒe mɔ nu. App la fia QR kɔd loo `zecauth://` kadodo si tsɔ kafukafu kple nusiwo katã wòle biam, abe geɖeɖe, fexexe ƒe biabiawo, loo nukpɔ ƒe mɔ ene. Gaɖoɖo aɖeke mele o, adzɔ aɖeke mele o, ɖoɖonu me dɔwɔwɔ aɖeke mele o. Wòtsɔ mɔɖoɖo ƒe agbalẽŋlɔŋlɔ ɖeka kpe ɖe kɔd la ŋu
- **ZShield** - ewɔ ɣaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi be wòanye W3C DID kple OpenID Connect ame ƒe dzesi. Browser la wɔ safui eve, server la naa nonce to ZIP 304 ƒe mɔ me, gakotoku la de asidede agbalẽ te nɛ, eye server la trɔa JWT la na. Esi nu si do go la sɔ kple OIDC, app siwo li xoxo ate ŋu azãe manɔ mɔkadodo tɔxɛ aɖe wɔ o
- **ZecPass** - eɖea gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ƒe nɔnyi ɖe fia to nya kpui si wode asi me, eye wotu e be app la maganya zãla la ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi kpɔ o. Ewɔa hash si wòakɔ ɖe app ma tɔxɛ, be wòanye dzesi si le te ɖe mɔɖa dzi, ewɔa kafukafuwo be woazã wo zi ɖeka koe eye woanɔ ɣeyiɣi ƒe se me, eye wòtsɔ React button si woate ŋu aɖo ɖe eme kple Node ƒe ɖaseɖiɖe ƒe library ɖe asi
- **Portal** - geɖeɖe to ɣaɣla gaɖoɖo ɖoɖe kple kɔd si woazã zi ɖeka koe le nya kpui me, esi le mainnet dzi. Woazã akpɔɖeŋu ma ke hã be woaʋu kɔntɛnt si wòhiã fexexe, kple be woaɖo loo woaxɔ ga tso kadodo aɖe me
- **ZcashMe** - ezãa ɣaɣla fexexe abe ame ƒe dzesi ƒe ɖaseɖigbalɛ ene, eye wòtsɔ ŋku dea desktop kple mobile ƒe vovototo ŋu, ale be geɖeɖe le laptop dzi magblɔ be browser ƒe extension wòhiã o
- **ZBooks** - akɔnt xexlẽ kple gaɖoɖo dɔwɔnu si kpɔa geɖeɖe kple Zcash abe auth ƒe mɔnu si woate ŋu azã zi geɖe ene, menye dɔwɔnu la ŋutɔ ene o, eye wòxlẽa gadzraɖoƒe [treasury] ƒe nyatakakawo to Unified Full Viewing Key me
