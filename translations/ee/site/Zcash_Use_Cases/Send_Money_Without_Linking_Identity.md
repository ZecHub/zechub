## Nyatakakadzra

- Ɖo ga ɣe sia ɣeyiɣi sia ɣeyiɣi tso **ɣaɣla [shielded] gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi [address]** me
- Mègawɔ t yi t gaɖoɖowo [transactions] kple keke o
- Ɖe ame ƒe nudzɔdzɔwo ƒe kadodo teƒe
- Nàɖo ŋku ɖe ɣeyiɣi kple nɔnɔme siwo woƒe nɔnɔme wogblɔna dzi

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/user-svgrepo-com-21adf62b7c.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="user icon"/> Ame kae wòŋlɔe na?

- Ame sia ame si le fexexe [payment] nyawo siwo le vevie wɔm
- Zãlawo siwo ƒe ɣaɣla [privacy] le vevie na wo
- Ame siwo le kpɔkpɔ ƒe afɔku te

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/warning-error-svgrepo-com-b7ea8a50da.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="warning icon"/> Kuxi la

Crypto gaɖoɖo hãgblɔna:
- Wò gakotoku [wallet] ƒe nudzɔdzɔ
- Wò gaɖoɖo ƒe ŋutinya
- Ame siwo kplii nèle kadodo me

Esia wɔna **fexexe ƒe kadodo si wòate ŋu ade afɔ le**.

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-lock-2f8e221321.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="toolbox icon"/> Nu ka ta Zcash?


Zcash wɔa **fexexe siwo le ɣaɣla bliboa me** zi.

Esia ƒe nu siwo wògbãnae nye:
- Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo ƒe kadodo
- Dukɔme gaɖoɖo ƒe afɔdedo

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-toolbox-9bebbb1619.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="toolbox icon"/> Nu Si Wòhia

- Gakotoku si doa ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo dzi
- Z-gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo ƒe gɔmesese gã aɖe

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Fiafia Ƒe Mɔfiame

### Afɔ 1: Kpɔ Egbɔ Be Ga La Le Ɣaɣla Me

Ne wò ga le nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi [transparent] gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi me la:
- Tsɔe de ɣaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi me gbã


### Afɔ 2: Ɖo Ga Tso Ɣaɣla Me Yi Ɣaɣla Me
Ɖo ŋku ɖe `z to z` gaɖoɖo dzi ɣe sia ɣeyiɣi sia ɣeyiɣi

Esia nana ɣaɣla le teƒe si wòanyo wu.

### Afɔ 3: Mègana Nudzɔdzɔ Ƒe Kadodo Nadze

Mègawɔ esiawo o:
- Mègblɔ gaɖoɖowo ɖe dukɔme o
- Mègazã gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ma ke le nɔnɔme vovovowo me o
- Mègakɔ gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo kple ame siwo wònya ƒe nudzɔdzɔwo o

### Afɔ 4: Bu Ɣeyiɣi Ƒe Nɔnɔmewo Ŋu

Aɖaŋu si le ŋgɔ:
- Mègana nàɖo ga enumake le ga gã xexlẽ megbe o
- Ɖe nɔnɔme siwo wòate ŋu akpɔ be woagblɔ ɖi la teƒe ne ète ŋu

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Vodadawo Si Wòle Be Nàƒo Asa

- Ɖo ga tso nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo me kple keke  
- Zã gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ma ke na ame geɖewo  
- Ɖo kpee be gaɖoɖowo dzɔ le dukɔme  
- Mètsɔa ŋku ɖe nyatakaka bubuwo (ɣeyiɣi, zi si wòdzɔna) o
