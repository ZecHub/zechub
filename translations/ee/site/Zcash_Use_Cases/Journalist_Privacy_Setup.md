### Afɔɖeɖe 2: Zã Adrɛs Siwo Wokpɔ Akpɔtɔ Ko

- Zã z-adrɛswo nàtsɔ awɔ asitsatsa ɣesiaɣi

### Afɔɖeɖe 3: Ta Dzɔtsoƒe Fexexewo Ta

Ne fexexetsoƒewo:

- Mègazã adrɛswo ake o

### Afɔɖeɖe 5: Lé Dɔwɔƒea ƒe Dedienɔnɔ me ɖe asi
