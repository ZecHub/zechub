## TL;DR

- Zã ɣaɣla [shielded] gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo na gaɖoɖowo katã
- Mègana gakotoku [wallet] ƒe ameŋkɔ nava go le dukɔme o
- Kpɔ ŋutifafa na dzɔdzɔƒewo to fexexe siwo womate ŋu awɔe ɖe wò ŋu o dzi
- Zã nya kpui [memo]wo kple dzodzro na nyadzɔdzɔ si le dedie

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Fiafia ƒe Mɔ

### Afɔ 1: Wɔ Gakotoku Suesue aɖe

- Zã gakotoku ɖeka ko na dɔ siwo le dzodzro me
- Mègatsɔe akpa kple wò ŋutɔ ƒe gaɖoɖowo o

### Afɔ 2: Zã Ɣaɣla Gaɖoƒe Ƒe Dzesiwo Ko

- Wɔ gaɖoɖo ɣesiaɣi kple z-addresses
- Ɖe asi le nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi gaɖoɖowo katã ŋu

### Afɔ 3: Kpɔ Dzɔdzɔƒe Ƒe Fexexewo Ŋu

Ne nèle fexexe wɔm na dzɔdzɔƒewo:
- Zã ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo (z → z)
- Ɖe asi le ɣeyiɣi ƒe ɖoɖo siwo womate ŋu aka ɖo ŋu ŋutɔ ŋu
- Mègazã gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka zi eve o

### Afɔ 4: Nànya Ŋutɔ Kple Nya Kpuiwo

- MÈGATSɔ nyadzɔdzɔ si le dzodzro me ade nya kpuiwo me o
- Zã nu si mekpɔa ŋku na ame o alo dzesi siwo woɖoe be woawɔe ne ehiã

### Afɔ 5: Lé Dɔwɔwɔ Ƒe Dedie Mewɔ

- Ɖe asi le gakotoku ƒe kakaka ɖe wò ŋutɔ ƒe ameŋkɔ ŋu
- Mègaxɔ se le dukɔme be nèwɔ gaɖoɖowo o
- Zã mɔ vovovo na nyadzɔdzɔ
