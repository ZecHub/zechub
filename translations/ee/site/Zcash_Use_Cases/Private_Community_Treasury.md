- Ma dɔwɔwɔ ƒe mɔewo tso dukɔme ƒe ameŋkɔ gbã

## <img src="/content-images/warning-error-svgrepo-com-b7ea8a50da.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="warning icon"/> Kuxi La

Hame ƒe ga siwo le dukɔme gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonuwo dzi doa esiawo fia:
- Gadzraɖoƒe ƒe ga ƒomekuku bliboa
- Ga siwo woɖo ɖa ƒe ŋutinya
- Ame siwo kpe ɖe eŋu ƒe amedzesiwo

Eya doa afɔkuwo:
- Ga ƒe didime
- Me ƒe dzrewɔwɔwo elabena nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi ƒe teƒe le
- Amegbetɔ ƒe ɣaɣla ƒe tsɔtsɔ ƒe vuvɔ

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-lock-2f8e221321.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="toolbox icon"/> Nu ka tae Zcash?

- Woatsɔ ga aɖe ɣaɣla
- Woawɔ gaɖoɖowo manafia numeƒeawo o
- Woakpɔ me ƒe akɔntaɖeɖe gbɔ manafiae na dukɔme o

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-toolbox-9bebbb1619.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="toolbox icon"/> Nu Siwo Hiã Wò

- Zcash gakotoku [wallet] (alo gakotoku geɖewo hena dɔwɔwɔ ƒe mama)
- Me ƒe mɔnu klaro siwo na woakplɔ ga dzi
- Ne wòdi: hame ƒe nuŋlɔɖi hena wɔwɔ ƒe yometoto

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Fiafia Ƒe Mɔfiame

### Afɔ 1: Wɔ ɣaɣla Gadzraɖoƒe ƒe Gaɖoƒe ƒe Dzesi

- Wɔ **z-address** na gadzraɖoƒe la
- Esiae nye gaƒoƒe vevi


### Afɔ 2: Ðo Dɔwɔwɔ Mɔewo

Ma dɔwɔwɔwo:
- Gadzikpɔla (eƒe asi le ga la dzi)
- Ame siwo kpe ɖe eŋu (woabia be woatsɔ ga aɖo ɖa)
- Ame siwo kpɔe ɖa (woana asi le eŋu le eme)

Mègatsɔ dɔwɔwɔ mɔawo akɔ kple dukɔme ameŋkɔwo o.



### Afɔ 3: Xɔ Ga Le ɣaɣla Me

- Xɔ kpekpeɖeŋuwo to ɣaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi la dzi
- Ðe dzi ɖe ame siwo kpe ɖe eŋu la ŋu be woaɖo ga tso ɣaɣla gakotokuwɔwo me



### Afɔ 4: Lé Gaɖoɖowo Ƒe Yometoto Le Eme

Zã:
- Gakotoku ƒe nya kpuiwo
- Nuŋlɔɖi siwo mele ɖoɖonu dzi o (le kpɔɖeŋu me, hame ƒe docs)

Nya kpui ƒe kpɔɖeŋu: 
 - Ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu #12 - Mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖoŋlɔla fexexe



### Afɔ 5: Wɔ Fexexewo Le ɣaɣla Me

- Ðo ga ɖa to ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo (z → z) dzi
- Mègana be woafia ame si xɔ ga la ƒe amedzesi le dukɔme o

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Vodadawo Siwo Wòle Be Nàwɔ O

- Zã nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo na gadzraɖoƒe  
- Do gakotoku ƒe ga ƒomekukuwo fia le dukɔme

- Tsɔ gadzraɖoƒe ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi la kɔ kple amewo siwo woate ŋu ade dzesi
