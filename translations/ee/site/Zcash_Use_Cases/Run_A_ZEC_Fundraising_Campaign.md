## TL;DR

- Na amedzroa nanɔ gakotoku [wallet] ƒe akɔnta tɔxɛ kple ɣaɣla [shielded] gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi, ale be wòmegaɖe asi kple ga bubuwo o
- Ma fexexe [payment] ƒe biabia ƒe kadodo alo QR code si me ga si woato ɖo be woadzra le kple nya kpui [memo] si woƒo xoxo le ŋgɔ la na amewo
- Fia ŋgɔdzedze la menye be nàɖe ame siwo dona ga la ƒe nyawo ɖe go o: Ŋlɔ ga blibo la ɖe go wòŋutɔ, alo na nukpɔsafui [viewing key] na ame ɖeka si nèle dziwò
- Nukpɔsafui ɖeka kpɔ akɔnta bliboa katã, menye gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka ko o, eye eya ta amedzroa xɔ akɔnta tɔxɛ
- Ƒo amedzroa nu le ŋkeke si nègblɔ do ŋgɔ la dzi, eye nàɖe ale si woza ga la fia

- Hamea ƒe ƒuƒowo, dɔwɔwɔ siwo ƒe teƒe ɖeŋu kple nuwɔlawo siwo le gaƒom na taɖodzinu ɖeka
- Dukɔmenyawo siwo menye gaƒoƒe ƒuƒowo o, siwo wɔ ƒe ƒe nuwuwu alo xɔxɔna ƒe biabia le ZEC
- Ame sia ame si di be amesiwo dona ga la natsɔ dzidzɔ akpɔ amedzroa dzi, ke womeɖe nunana sia sia ƒe nyawo ɖe go o

Ne ènye US dukɔmenya si menye gaƒoƒe ƒuƒo o la, xlẽ [Xɔ ɣaɣla ƒe nunana le US Dukɔmenya si menye gaƒoƒe ƒuƒo o me](/zcash-use-cases/accept-private-donations-as-a-us-nonprofit) gbã na dzesiɖeŋlɔwo kple IRS ƒe agbalẽwo ta.

## <img src="/content-images/warning-error-svgrepo-com-b7ea8a50da.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="warning icon"/> Kuxi la

Crypto gaƒoƒe ƒe amedzrowo geɖe zã nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi [transparent] gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi be amesiwo dona ga la nakpɔ ale si ga blibo la le dzi yim. Esia hã fia nunana sia sia, eƒe ga home, ɣeyiɣi si wòva kple afi si ga la yi le emegbe. Amesiwo dona ga ɣaɣla ta la ƒe ɣaɣla [privacy] bu le wo ŋutɔ, eye ame sia ame ate ŋu adze gaawo yome le amedzroa megbe.

Amedzro si le ɣaɣla bliboa me la ƒe kuxi le vovo: Amesiwo dona ga mate ŋu akpɔ be woɖo taɖodzinu la dzi alo ale si woza ga la o.

<br/>

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-lock-2f8e221321.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="lock icon"/> Nu ka ta Zcash?

Kple ɣaɣla ZEC, nunana sia sia nɔ ɣaɣla le ɖoɖonu [chain] dzi, eye wòe ƒo nu si nàfia: ga blibo, ŋgɔdzedze ƒe mɔkpɔkpɔ, alo mɔ na amesi le dɔkpɔm be wòaxlẽ ko. Amesiwo dona ga ate ŋu atsɔ gbedeasi ade [nya kpui](/using-zcash/memos) la me, eye àte ŋu akafu wo, ke ame bubu mate ŋu akpɔ ame si na ga o.

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> Mɔfiafia le afɔ ɖe afɔ dzi

### Afɔ 1: Ɖo taɖodzinu kple sewo

Ŋlɔ esiawo ɖe anyi hafi nàdze egɔme:
- Ga si nèle ƒom kple nu si wòaxɔe na
- Nuwuwu ƒe ŋkeke
- Nu si ava dzɔ ne nèxɔ ga geɖe wu alo sue wu taɖodzinu la
- Ame si kpɔa gaawo dzi, kple be ame geɖe wu ɖeka ƒe asixɔxɔ hiã hafi woazã ga (kpɔ [Wɔ Hamea ƒe Gad zraɖoƒe ɣaɣla](/zcash-use-cases/private-community-treasury))

Ɖe esiawo fia le amedzroa ƒe axa dzi. Amesiwo dona ga doa dziɖuɖu le amedzro si gblɔa nu si wòawɔ kpuie le gɔmedzedzea me la ŋu.

### Afɔ 2: Wɔ akɔnta tɔxɛ na amedzroa

Wɔ akɔnta yeye le wò gakotoku me, zãe na amedzro sia ko, eye nàtsɔ ɣaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka tsoe. Ne nàde amedzroa ɖe aga la, ga blibo la xexeame kpɔe bɔbɔe, eye esia hiã le emegbe ne nàna nukpɔsafui, elabena nukpɔsafui fia akɔnta bliboa ƒe nudzɔdzɔwo ([Nukpɔsafuiwo](/zcash-tech/viewing-keys)).

### Afɔ 3: Wɔ fexexe ƒe biabia ƒe kadodo kple QR code

[Fexexe ƒe biabia URI](/using-zcash/payment-request-uris) nye `zcash:` ƒe kadodo si dea gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi, ga si woato ɖo be woadzra le kple nya kpui la eme na amesi dona ga. Zã ZecHub ƒe [fexexe ƒe biabia wɔla](/tools) na nàwɔ ɖeka, eye nàmae abe kadodo kple QR code ene.

Nya kpui si le abe "Amedzroa ƒe ŋkɔ, ɖoɖoɖo: wò email alo handle" ene doa asi kple amesiwo di be woaxɔ akpeɖeŋu alo dzesiɖeŋlɔ. Gblɔ le axa la dzi be nya kpui la menye nu si wòele be woatsɔ adoe o.

### Afɔ 4: Ɖe amedzroa ƒe axa ɖe go

De esiawo ɖe axa la dzi:
- Taɖodzinu, nuwuwu ƒe ŋkeke kple sewo tso Afɔ 1 me
- Ɣaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi, QR code kple kadodo la
- Ale si woana ga tso ɣaɣla gakotoku me, kple afi si woakpɔ ɖeka
- Ale si nàgblɔ ŋgɔdzedze ƒe nya (Afɔ 5)

Tsɔ gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi la ɖe teƒe ɖeka, eye nàgblɔ na amesiwo dona ga be woakɔpie tso afi ma ko.

### Afɔ 5: Gblɔ ŋgɔdzedze ƒe nya ne mèɖe amesiwo dona ga la ƒe nyawo fia o

Tia ɖeka eye nàgblɔ ka wòtiae le axa la dzi:
- **Ŋlɔ ga blibo la ɖe go wòŋutɔ**, abe zi ɖeka le kɔsiɖa sia kɔsiɖa ene, tso wò gakotoku ƒe ga si le amedzroa ƒe akɔnta me
- **Na nukpɔsafui na ame ɖeka si nèle dziwò**, abe board ƒe membre alo hamea ƒe membre si amesiwo dona ga la nya ene, si akafu ga blibo la le amewo katã ŋkume. Nukpɔsafui si kpɔa ga si va la fia nunana siwo woxɔ; nukpɔsafui blibo hã fia ga zãzã ([Nukpɔsafuiwo](/zcash-tech/viewing-keys))

Mègblɔ nukpɔsafui ɖe go le amedzroa ƒe axa dzi o. Ame sia ame si xɔe ate ŋu akpɔ nunana sia sia ƒe ga home kple nya kpui, ame siwo dona ga la ƒe kadodo ƒe nyawo hã.

### Afɔ 6: Da akpe na amesiwo dona ga

Ɖo eŋu na amesiwo dona ga siwo de kadodo ɖe nya kpui la me. Mègaɖe amesi dona ga ƒe ŋkɔ alo ga home fia mekpɔe gbã o.

### Afɔ 7: Ƒo nu eye nàgblɔ nya

Le nuwuwu ƒe ŋkeke la:
- Gblɔ be amedzroa ƒo nu, eye nàɖe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi kple QR code la ɖa alo nàtrɔ wo
- Ŋlɔ ga blibo mamlɛtɔ kple nu si ga la xɔ na ɖe go
- Ne nèma nukpɔsafui la, bia be dɔkpɔla la nakafu ga blibo mamlɛtɔ kple ga zãzã

[Fexexe ƒe nyafɔɖeɖewo](/zcash-tech/payment-disclosures) ate ŋu ana nàɖe fexexe ɖeka ƒe nyateƒe afia gbe ɖeka manaa nukpɔsafui o le ŋkeke geɖe me, gake se la nɔa draft me ha, eyata mètsɔe akɔ ɖo fifia o.

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Vodada siwo nàle srɔ̃m be nàvɔ̃

- Wɔ amedzroa tso wò gakotoku ƒe akɔnta si nèzãa gbe sia gbe me
- Ɖe nukpɔsafui ɖe go, si ɖea ame sia ame si dona ga ƒe ga home kple nya kpui fia
- Zã nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi alo gatrɔƒe [exchange] ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi, elabena eŋɔ be woafia ga blibo la
- Ɖe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi la ɖe go to amedzroa ƒe nuwuwu megbe, ale be nunanawo gale vava me ne ɖoɖo aɖeke mele wo ta o
- Megblɔ ale si woza ga la ƒe nya o ɣeaɣi aɖeke
