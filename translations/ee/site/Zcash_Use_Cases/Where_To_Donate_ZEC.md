> Wokpɔe zi mamlɛtɔ: October 27, 2026, le habɔbɔ ɖesiaɖe ŋutɔ ƒe nudzɔdzɔ ƒe axa dzi. Adrɛswo trɔna, eyata axa sia doa ka kple nunana ƒe axawo le esi teƒe be wòawɔ adrɛswo ƒe kɔpi. Kɔpi adrɛs la tso habɔbɔa ŋutɔ ƒe nyatakakadzraɖoƒea ɣesiaɣi. Adzɔxexe ŋuti nuŋlɔɖiwo nye nusi habɔbɔ ɖesiaɖe gblɔna tso eɖokui ŋu, ke menye adzɔxexe ŋuti aɖaŋuɖoɖowo o.

## TL;DR

- Habɔbɔ enyi siwo mekpɔa viɖe aɖeke tso eme o ta ZEC adrɛs. Wo dometɔ atɔ̃ zãa adrɛs si wotsɔ akpoxɔnu ɖo, eyata wò nunana la nɔa ame ŋutɔ si le kɔsɔkɔsɔ me
- Dɔwɔha xɔŋkɔ ene siwo mekpɔa viɖe aɖeke tso eme o xɔa ZEC le adrɛs si me kɔ ko dzi: EFF, Freedom of the Press Foundation, Tor Project kple Internet Archive
- Nudzɔdzɔ ƒe mɔnuwo abe The Giving Block kple Every.org xɔa ZEC na dɔmenyohabɔbɔ akpe geɖe, gake wo dometɔ aɖeke megblɔ be yedoa alɔ ZEC si wokpɔ ta na o
- Ne èhiã adzɔxegbalẽvi la, ke lé ŋku ɖe habɔbɔ ɖesiaɖe ƒe nuŋlɔɖi si le ete ŋu hafi nàna

## <img src="/content-images/user-svgrepo-com-21adf62b7c.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="user icon"/> Who is this for?

- ZEC xɔla siwo di be yewoana nu dɔ aɖe si ŋu yewotsɔ ɖe le
- Nunala siwo di be yewoƒe nunana nanɔ yewo ɖokui si
- Ame sia ame si le habɔbɔ siwo ƒe ŋkɔwo dim be yeado alɔe kple ZEC

Ne èle habɔbɔ aɖe si mekpɔa viɖe aɖeke tso eme o dzi kpɔm eye nèdi be yeaxɔ ZEC, kpɔe ɖa [Xɔ Ame ŋutɔ ƒe Nudzɔdzɔwo Abe United States ƒe Habɔbɔ si Mekpɔa Viɖe aɖeke O ene](/zcash-use-cases/accept-private-donations-as-a-us-nonprofit).

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-lock-2f8e221321.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="lock icon"/> Shielded or transparent: why it matters

Ne èɖo ZEC tso gakotoku si ŋu woxe mɔ ɖo me yi adrɛs **si wotsɔ akpoxɔnu ɖo** dzi la, ga homea, amesi ɖoe ɖa kple amesi xɔe la katã nɔa ame ŋutɔ ƒe nya me.

Ne èɖoe ɖe **transparent** adrɛs la, nunana la dzena le blockchain la dzi: amesiame ateŋu akpɔ ga homea, ɣeyiɣi si wòva ɖo kple habɔbɔa ƒe ga si susɔ. Wò gakotoku si ŋu akpoxɔnu le ƒe dɔwɔna bubu nɔa ame ŋutɔ si, gake nunana la ŋutɔ ya nyea dutoƒo. Kpɔ [Turnstile ƒe ʋuƒoa](/zcash-tech/the-turnstile) na nusi va zua dutoƒo ne ZEC dzo le ta si ŋu wokpɔ akpoxɔnu le me.

Wo ame evea siaa gakpɔtɔ le megbe na habɔbɔa. Ðeko Shielded nana nunana la nɔa ɣaɣla.

## <img src="/content-images/ladder-svgrepo-com-7232bf46ed.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="step icon"/> How to give

1. Tia habɔbɔ aɖe si le etame eye nàʋu eƒe nudzɔdzɔ ƒe axa
2. Kpɔ ZEC adrɛs la tso axa ma dzi, ke menye tso wiki sia alo screenshot me o
3. Kpɔ ŋɔŋlɔdzesi gbãtɔ kple mamlɛawo ɖa ne ètsɔe de eme vɔ
4. Ne èdi be yeaxɔ gaxɔgbalẽvi la, tsɔ wò e-mail kpe ɖe nuŋlɔɖia me (si ŋu wokpɔ ta na ɖeɖeko) alo wɔ ɖe habɔbɔa ƒe nuŋlɔɖi si le etame dzi
5. Ðo ɖa tso a [gakotoku si ŋu wokpɔa akpoxɔnu le](/wallets)

## <img src="/content-images/icons8-cancel-7f786be3c1.svg" width="24" height="24" className="inline-block align-middle mr-1 p-[2px]" alt="cancel icon"/> Common mistakes to avoid

- Adrɛs aɖe ƒe kɔpi tso nyamedzroƒe ƒe nyatakaka aɖe, hadomenyatakakadzraɖoƒe alo screenshot xoxo aɖe me ɖe habɔbɔa ŋutɔ ƒe axa teƒe
- Nudɔdɔ tso asitɔtrɔ aɖe me, si ate ŋu atsɔ nunana la asɔ kple wò akɔnta
- Mɔkpɔkpɔ na adzɔxexe ƒe gaxɔgbalẽvi evɔ màlé ŋku ɖe habɔbɔa ƒe nuŋlɔɖi ŋu gbã o
