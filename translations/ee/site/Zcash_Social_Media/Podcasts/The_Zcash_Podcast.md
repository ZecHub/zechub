# Zcash Podcast si le Digital Cash Network dzi
![Nukpɔkpɔ ƒe nɔnɔmetata_2023-01-22_21-50-14](/content-images/213973803-b0629c92-08d4-459b-99f1-594854-6272d02af6.webp)

Zcash Podcast la xɔa nyatakakawo abe yeyewo, nyati tɔxɛwo ƒe tsi me nɔnɔ goglo, kple bubuawo na Zcash habɔbɔ si li fifia be woaɖu eye woama, eye wònaa mɔnukpɔkpɔ Digital Cash Network channel ƒe nudɔla siwo li fifia be woanya Zcash nyuie wu. 

Etɔxɛe la, mɔ̃ sia ƒo nu tso:

* nukpɔsusu si nye be nànɔ agbe le gadzraɖoƒe manɔmee tso cryptocurrency gbɔ eye nàtsɔ wò agbe bliboa ade asi na amewo.
* curating a community si ate ŋu atsɔ ɖe le Zcash ƒe gɔmeɖosewo kple mɔ̃ɖaŋununya me wu ne wotsɔe sɔ kple crypto nyasela siwo le mama dedie nu.

Tsɔ kpe ɖe eŋu la, alesi wowɔa nu le agbe me la naa kadodo ƒe seselelãme nɔa wo kple nyaselawo dome, amesiwo te ŋu kpɔa gome le eme hebiaa nya amedzroxɔla kple amedzroawo le agbe, si sɔ nyuie na eƒe alɔdzedɔwɔƒe yeyewo gbɔ ɖoɖo, evɔ wògaɖea mɔ kokoko be woaɖu nyatakaka siwo le agbalẽ didi me la emegbe.
