# PGP* na Crypto Podcast
![bannerpgp ƒe ƒuƒoƒo](/content-images/221758326-06cea3f5-4c9e-4466-b9ee-738546-01f88aec39.webp)

Míexɔ mi nyuie ɖe “PGP * (Pretty Good Policy) na Crypto Podcast,” si nye Electric Coin Co. ƒe ɖoɖo aɖe!

Míedzroa blockchain mɔ̃ɖaŋununya, dukɔa ƒe ɖoɖowo, kple xexeame katã ƒe ɖoɖowɔwɔ ƒe nyatiwo ƒe tsatsam me. Míaƒe taɖodzinue nye be míana míaƒe nyaselawo nakpɔ gɔmesese deto kple eŋutinunyalawo ƒe nukpɔsusuwo le nya vevi siwo le tɔtrɔm le blockchain kple cryptocurrency ƒe etsɔme ŋu.

Àse gbebiame siwo wowɔ kple dɔwɔƒea ƒe ŋgɔnɔlawo, dziɖuɖumegãwo, kple agbalẽnyala siwo le ŋgɔgbe le dukɔa ƒe ɖoɖowo ƒe nɔnɔme wɔwɔ na blockchain mɔ̃ɖaŋununya me. Míaƒe amedzrowo agblɔ woƒe nukpɔsusu tɔxɛwo le kuxi kple mɔnukpɔkpɔ siwo dze ŋgɔ dɔwɔƒea kple ŋusẽ si sedede siwo li fifia kple esiwo wodo ɖa kple ɖoɖowɔɖi ƒe mɔnuwo kpɔ ɖe blockchain lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe dzidziɖedzi kple ŋgɔyiyi dzi.
