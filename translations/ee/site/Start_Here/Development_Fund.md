# Zcash ƒe Ŋgɔyiyi Gadzraɖoƒe

## Kpuie ko (TL;DR)

- Zcash xea fe na eƒe ŋutɔ ƒe ŋgɔyiyi tso gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetuwo me, eya ta eƒe tulawo mekpɔa ŋu ɖe gadolala siwo tso gotagome la ŋu o
- Egbea la, gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu ɖesiaɖe ƒe 8% yina na Zcash Community Grants, eye 12% yina na gadzraɖoƒe si ga tɔwo (coin holders) kpɔa dzi. Nukulawo (miners) xɔa susɔea
- Gakpekpeɖeŋu sia trɔ le fetu ƒe afãmama (halving) ɖesiaɖe me: gɔmeɖolawo ƒe fetu (founders' reward) le gɔmedzedzea me, Ŋgɔyiyi Gadzraɖoƒe (Development Fund) tso 2020, gadaka si wotu (lockbox) tso 2024, eye ga tɔwo ƒe dzikpɔkpɔ tso November 2025
- Ga tɔwo daa akɔ tso ale si woazã 12% gadzraɖoƒea ŋu. Zcash Community Grants ŋutɔ tsoa nya me le eƒe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋuwo (grants) ŋu
- Èdi gakpekpeɖeŋua? Bia tso Zcash Community Grants si na dɔ yeye, alo tso ga tɔwo ƒe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu siwo wona ɖe dɔ siwo wowu enu xoxo ta (retroactive grants) si na dɔ si nèwɔ xoxo

## Ame kawo ŋuti wòle

- Zcash zãla yeye siwo di be yewoanya ame si xea fe na Zcash
- Tulawo, ŋlɔlawo kple gɔmeɖeɖelawo siwo le gakpekpeɖeŋu dim
- Ga tɔ siwo di be yewoase nusi ŋu yewodaa akɔ le gɔme

## Nusita esia le vevie

Dɔwɔƒe aɖeke mele Zcash tɔ o. Ke hã ele be ame aɖe naxe fe na gakotokuwo (wallets), ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ (node) ƒe kɔmpiutadɔwɔnuwo, numekuku kple nufiafia. Le esi teƒe be Zcash naƒo ga tso gadolalawo gbɔ la, eɖea gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu yeye ɖesiaɖe ƒe akpa aɖe ɖe vovo na ŋgɔyiyi. Esia nana ame siwo le Zcash tum la ƒe susu nɔa eƒe zãlawo kple eƒe ɣaɣla (privacy) ƒe taɖodzinu dzi.

## Ale si wòtrɔ

| Ɣeyiɣi | Nusi dzɔ | Ale si womaa gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu ɖesiaɖe |
|---|---|---|
| 2016 va ɖo 2020 | **Gɔmeɖolawo ƒe fetu.** Wòxe fe na Zcash ƒe gɔmeɖolawo, gadola gbãtɔwo, dɔwɔlawo kple Zcash Foundation, va se ɖe fetu ƒe afãmama gbãtɔ | 20% na gɔmeɖolawo ƒe fetu |
| Nov 2020 va ɖo Nov 2024 | **Ŋgɔyiyi Gadzraɖoƒe**, si [Canopy](/zcash-tech/canopy) wɔ ([ZIP 1014](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1014)) | 7% Electric Coin Company, 5% Zcash Foundation, 8% Ga ƒe Kpekpeɖeŋu Gãwo (emegbe Zcash Community Grants) |
| Nov 2024 va ɖo Nov 2025 | **Gadaka si wotu**, si [NU6](/zcash-tech/nu6) wɔ ([ZIP 1015](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1015)) | 8% Zcash Community Grants, 12% yi Ŋgɔyiyi Gadzraɖoƒe si Wotsɔ Ðo Ðe Megbe ƒe Gadaka si Wotu me, eye wodzra ɖo va se ɖe esime nutoa me tɔwo tso nya me le ale si woazãe ŋu |
| Nov 2025 va ɖo fifia | **Ga tɔwo ƒe dzikpɔkpɔ**, si [NU6.1](/zcash-tech/nu6-1) ɖo anyi ([ZIP 1016](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1016)) | 8% Zcash Community Grants, 12% na Gadzraɖoƒe si Ga Tɔwo Kpɔa Dzi, si wodze egɔme kple gadaka si wotu me ga |

Le ɣeyiɣi ɖesiaɖe me la, nukulawo xɔa 80% susɔea.

## Ale si wòwɔa dɔe egbea

Tso esime NU6.1 dze dɔwɔwɔ gɔme le November 24, 2025 dzi la, gakpekpeɖeŋu ƒe mɔ eve doa go tso gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu ɖesiaɖe me:

- **8% na [Zcash Community Grants](/zcash-organizations/zcash-community-grants)** (ZCG). Dziɖuha aɖe si le ɖokuisi naa ga na dɔ yeye le Zcash ƒe ƒuƒoƒo me: gakotokuwo, dɔwɔnuwo (tools), numekuku, nufiafia kple kpekpewo.
- **12% na Gadzraɖoƒe si Ga Tɔwo Kpɔa Dzi.** Edze egɔme kple ga siwo wodzra ɖo ɖe gadaka si wotu me. Ga tɔwo daa akɔ tso alesi be woaxe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋuwo tso eme alo woagblee wòanɔ anyi. Safuitɔ Habɔbɔwo (Key-Holder Organizations) lé gaa ɖe asi, eye se bla wo be woazãe ɖe ale si ga tɔwo tso nya me ko nu ([ZIP 1016](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1016)).

Ɖoɖo sia anɔ dɔ wɔm va se ɖe Zcash ƒe fetu ƒe afãmama etɔ̃lia. ZIP 1016 ɖe mɔ be ŋkekea ate ŋu atrɔ, elabena NU7 trɔa gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe ɣeyiɣiɖoɖo. Emegbe nutoa me tɔwo atso nya me be yewoalé ɖe eŋu, atrɔe alo awu enu.

## Ame si tsoa nya me

- **Zcash Community Grants** dzraa biabiawo me kpɔna eye wodaa akɔ le ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu ɖesiaɖe ŋu.
- **Ga tɔwo** tsɔa woƒe ZEC daa akɔ le 12% gadzraɖoƒea ƒe susuɖoɖowo ŋu. Womebua woƒe gawo ɖe ga me hafi daa akɔ o.
- **Tɔtrɔ le gakpekpeɖeŋua ŋutɔ me** hiã ZIP yeye kple mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ (network upgrade), le nutoa me tɔwo ƒe nyaƒoƒo le [Zcash Nutoa me tɔwo ƒe Nyaƒoƒoƒe](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com) megbe.

Be nàkpɔ nuwo katã ƒe nɔnɔme la, kpɔ [Zcash ƒe Dziɖuɖu](/zcash-community/zcash-governance) kple [Ale si wowɔ ɖoɖo ɖe Zcash ŋu](/start-here/how-zcash-is-organized).

## Ale si nàxɔ gakpekpeɖeŋu

- **Dɔ yeye si nèdi be yeawɔ:** bia tso [Zcash Community Grants](/zcash-organizations/zcash-community-grants) gbɔ.
- **Dɔ si nèwu enu xoxo:** bia tso [Ga Tɔwo ƒe Ga ƒe Kpekpeɖeŋu siwo Wona ɖe Dɔ si Wowɔ Xoxo Ta](/zcash-organizations/coinholder-directed-retroactive-grants) gbɔ, siwo wowɔna le ɣleti etɔ̃ ɖesiaɖe me.
- **Kpekpeɖeŋu suewo na wiki sia:** ZecHub naa nunana kpekpeɖeŋunalawo le ZEC me. Kpɔ [Kpe asi ɖe ZecHub tutu ŋu](/contribute/help-build-zechub).
