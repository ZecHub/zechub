# Zcash ƒe Ŋgɔyiyi ƒe Gaxɔ

Zcash nye blockchain protocol tɔxɛ aɖe elabena eya ŋutɔe dzɔa ga. Esia fia be mehiã be ƒuƒoƒo siwo le dɔ wɔm le Zcash ŋu naxɔ ga tso egodo gadelawo gbɔ o, eye woate ŋu ayi edzi alé fɔ ɖe ʋukuku ɖe vixɔxɔnyi ŋu esime wole ɖekawɔwɔ me kple Zcash ƒe ethos kple dɔdasi si keke ta wu hã.

## Block Teƒeɖoɖowo

Le blockchains me la, nane li si woyɔna be [block](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/b/block-bitcoin-block.asp), si wowɔna tsɔ ŋlɔa asitsatsa siwo ŋu womeɖo kpee haɖe le network la dzi o la ɖi. Ne wonya da asi ɖe asitsatsa dzi ko la, wotua mɔxenu la.

Wode dɔ asi na block wɔlawo (si woyɔna be tomenukulawo) be woada asi ɖe asitsatsa siwo le network me dzi eye woawɔ block yeyewo. Ne wole block siawo wɔm la, wotsɔa block reward ɖoa eteƒe na block wɔlawo. Le Zcash me la, le sɛkɛnd 75 ɖesiaɖe kloe me la, wowɔa block yeye aɖe, eye block ƒe fetu si nye 3.125 ZEC va nɔa tsatsam. Block rewards le Zcash me awɔ gaku yeyewo vaseɖe esime Zcash naɖo eƒe 21 million max supply gbɔ. Ne woɖo max supply gbɔ vɔ la, woaxe block rewards to transaction fees dzi.

## Zcash ƒe gadodo ƒe mɔnu (do ŋgɔ na NU6) .

Le Bitcoin kple cryptocurrencies bubuwo me la, block ƒe teƒeɖoɖowo katã yia block producers gbɔ. Zcash le etɔxɛ le nyateƒe si wònye be wotsɔa block ƒe fetu ƒe 20% dea [Zcash ŋgɔyiyi gaxɔ me](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1014). Ga sia naa ga ƒuƒoƒo siwo le dɔ wɔm tso Zcash ƒe ɖoɖowɔɖia ŋu.

Fifia la, [Zcash Nutome Gakpekpeɖeŋunana](https://zcashcommunitygrants.org/) xɔa 8% le block ƒe teƒeɖoɖowo me, Electric Coin Co. xɔa 7%, eye Zcash Foundation xɔa 5%. Esia wɔa dɔ va ɖoa ŋgɔyiyi ƒe ga ƒe 40%, 35%, kple 25%.
![Dev Gaxɔlawo ](/content-images/212411570-4858a3d6-f7a1-465a-bf0c-d2ef72-1efe3a104e.webp)

Le ƒe 2020 me la, Zcash nutoa me tɔwo da asi ɖe ŋgɔyiyi ƒe gaxɔa kple ƒuƒoƒo siwo wòdo alɔe dzi. Le ƒe 2024 me la, nutoa me tɔwo agada akɔ ake be woakpɔ amesi axɔ ga tso ŋgɔyiyi ƒe gaxɔa me.


# Zcash Hybrid Deferred Dev Gaxɔ ƒe Aɖaka (NU6) .

Hybrid Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox nye gadodo ƒe mɔnu si wotia na Zcash ŋgɔyiyi ga ƒe dzidzime si gbɔna. Tiatia sia ma Zcash block ƒe teƒeɖoɖowo katã ƒe 20%, 60% na Zcash Grants Committee eye 40% na protocol lockbox, si naa kakaɖedzi be nunɔamesiwo li be woado alɔ Zcash ƒe lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe etsɔme esime wole ŋgɔyiyi kple edzikpɔkpɔ dzi yim.

## Nyametsotsowɔwɔ ƒe Ðoɖo

Wotso nya me be woawɔ 20% Dev Fund Lockbox la ŋudɔ to ɖoɖo si me nutoa me tɔwo kplɔna bliboe me. Esia lɔ Zcash Community Advisory Panel (ZCAP) ƒe numekuku eve, kpakple numekuku geɖe siwo wowɔ le lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ŋuti ɖoɖowo katã me ɖe eme. Numekuku siawo na be ame geɖe siwo kpɔ gome le eme la gblɔ woƒe susuwo eye wokpe asi ɖe nyametsotsowɔwɔ ŋu.

![](/content-images/fdb9fcfc723fbfdc57c1ee276e7d4a57cd40fbbd-8f378b3e3d.webp)

Esi wole dev fund lockbox la hem ɖe megbe (si fia be woaʋu ga to network upgrade dzi emegbe) la, eteƒe madidi o Zcash Community atso nya me le ga mama ƒe mɔnu aɖe ŋu. Tiatia ɖeka si wodo ɖa enye ‘Zbloc’ dziɖuɖu ƒe ɖoɖo si woɖe ɖe vovo.
