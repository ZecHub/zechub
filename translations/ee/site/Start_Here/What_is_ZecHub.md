# Nu ka nye ZecHub?
---

ZecHub nye ha nuɖo agni [decentralized] srɔ̃ƒe na Zcash ƒe nusrɔ̃. [ZecHub](http://zechub.xyz) ƒe taɖodzinu nye be wòana nusrɔ̃ƒe aɖe, afi si habɔbɔa ƒe amewo ate ŋu awɔ dɔ ɖekae le em be woawɔ nyawo, akpɔe ɖa, eye woado ŋgɔ na nyawo si doa alɔ Zcash ƒe habɔbɔa. Míewɔ esia to mɔ aɖewo dzi.

1. [Nufiafiawo](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6_epn0lASLFF28ePj7P5TvtMG_WE7p3O)
2. [Wiki-dokumentewo](https://zechub.wiki/)
3. [Podcast si ƒoa nyamedzidzia kple habɔbɔa ƒe amewo](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6_epn0lASLHlNCMtUErX8UfaJK6N9K5O).
4. [Nyadzɔdzɔgbalẽ [newsletter] si doa le kwasiɖa sia kple kwasiɖa sia me Zcash.](https://zechub.substack.com/)

Nufiafiawo, blog ŋkekeviwo, kple nyadzɔdzɔgbalẽ la nye open source, eye woɖoe ɖe GitHub dzi, si fia be habɔbɔa ƒe amesi ke hã ate ŋu akpe ɖe eŋu. Esia ana be nyawo nanɔ nyateƒe, eye be kana la ŋutɔ magblẽ o le nuɖoɖo ɖeka ƒe megbe o.

Ne èye yeye le Zcash me la, zã [mɔfia sia](/start-here/new-user-guide).****
