# Alesi wowɔ ɖoɖo ɖe Zcash ŋui

## TL;DR

- Menye dɔwɔƒe ɖeka koe tu Zcash o, habɔbɔ geɖe siwo le wo ɖokui si siwo dometɔ ɖesiaɖe si dɔa ƒe akpa vovovowo le ye tue
- Le eƒe ŋutinya ƒe akpa gãtɔ me la, habɔbɔ eve xɔ ŋgɔ na ŋgɔyiyi, Electric Coin Company kple Zcash Foundation
- Le January 2026 me la, Electric Coin Company ƒe ƒuƒoƒo bliboa ɖe asi le dɔ ŋu le dziɖuɖu ŋuti nyaʋiʋli aɖe megbe, eye wogbugbɔ ɖo lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ɖe ɖoɖo nu wòzu ƒuƒoƒo geɖe siwo le wo ɖokui si
- Egbea la, ƒuƒoƒo vovovowoe kpɔa protocol, node software, gakotokuwo, numekuku, scaling, kple gadodo gbɔ
- Habɔbɔ ɖeka aɖeke mekpɔa ŋusẽ ɖe Zcash dzi o, network la nye dzɔtsoƒe si le ʋuʋu ɖi eye mɔɖeɖe mele eŋu o, eye wòyi edzi nɔ dɔ wɔm nyuie le tɔtrɔ ɖesiaɖe me

<br/>

## Ameka tae esia nye

- Ame yeye siwo le agbagba dzem be yewoase amesi tututu tua Zcash heléa be nɛ gɔme
- Amesiame si habɔbɔ ƒe ŋkɔ gbogbo siwo le lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe ɖoɖoa me tɔtɔ
- Nudzɔla siwo le nya me tsom le amesiwo woawɔ dɔ kplii alo afisi woaɖo aɖaŋuɖoɖo aɖe ɖa ŋu

<br/>

## Nusitae esia le vevie ɖo

Dɔwɔɖoɖoa gɔmesese nana nu bubu ɖesiaɖe nɔa bɔbɔe. Egblɔa amesi léa se si dzi nèɖoa ŋu ɖo la me ɖe asi, amesi gbɔ nàte ɖe eŋu axɔ gakpekpeɖeŋu, kple amesi ƒe agbanɔamedzie wònye be wòakpɔ network la ƒe akpa si ŋu nètsɔ ɖe le la na wò. Eɖe Zcash ƒe ŋusẽ siwo le ɖoɖoezizi me dometɔ ɖeka hã fia: esi dɔa kaka ɖe ƒuƒoƒo siwo le wo ɖokui si me ta la, kpododonu ƒe teƒe ɖeka aɖeke mate ŋu alé dɔa alo atɔ te o.

Axa sia nye anyigbatata. Le habɔbɔ ɖesiaɖe si si axa blibo le xoxo le wiki sia dzi la, àkpɔ nuŋlɔɖi kpui aɖe kple kadodo aɖe si axlẽ nu geɖe, tsɔ wu be nàgbugbɔ nusi woŋlɔ ɖe afima.

<br/>

## Alesi wòwɔa dɔe tsã

Le Zcash ƒe ŋutinya ƒe akpa gãtɔ me la, habɔbɔ evee xɔ ŋgɔ.

Electric Coin Company dze Zcash gɔme le ƒe 2016 me eye wòxɔ ŋgɔyiyi ƒe ƒuƒoƒo vevitɔa ƒe akpa gãtɔ ɖe dɔ me. Bootstrap, si nye habɔbɔ si mekpɔa viɖe aɖeke tso eme o si woɖo be woado alɔ Zcash ye kpɔ edzi. Zcash Foundation wɔ dɔ kplii abe habɔbɔ si mekpɔa viɖe aɖeke tso eme o si le eɖokui si ene, eƒe susu nɔ ɖoɖowɔɖia dzikpɔkpɔ ŋu eye wòtu node si le eɖokui si. Wokpɔ ga na wo ame evea siaa ƒe akpa gãtɔ tso block ƒe fetu si woɖo ɖi na ŋgɔyiyi ƒe akpa aɖe me.

Sɔti eve ƒe ɖoɖo sia nɔ anyi ƒe geɖe, gake enɔ te ɖe ga ma si woama kple habɔbɔ eveawo ƒe ɖekawɔwɔ dzi. Esi ga si wozã ɖe ŋgɔyiyidɔwo ŋu le gɔmedzedzea me nɔ tɔtrɔm eye kakaɖedzi meganɔ eƒe etsɔme si anɔ anyi didi ŋu o la, alesi woaxe fe ɖe dɔ siwo yia edzi ta ƒe nyabiasea va nɔ sesẽm ɖe edzi. Gazazã ŋuti nyabiase ma bɔbɔ nɔ nusiwo trɔ emegbe ƒe akpa gãtɔ me, eye wònye nusita ƒuƒoƒo aɖewo ƒoa ga tso egodo fifia evɔ bubuwo ɖoa ŋu ɖe gakpekpeɖeŋunana ŋu la ƒe akpa aɖe.

<br/>

## Ƒe 2026 ƒe ɖoɖo yeye si wowɔ

Le January 2026 me la, xɔa trɔ ŋutɔ. Le January 7 lia dzi la, Electric Coin Company ƒe amegã Josh Swihart ɖe gbeƒãe le X dzi be dɔwɔƒea ƒe ƒuƒoƒo bliboa ɖe asi le dɔ ŋu.

Bootstrap nye habɔbɔ si mekpɔa viɖe aɖeke tso eme o si woɖo le ƒe 2020 me be wòakpɔ Electric Coin Company, si va zu eƒe dɔwɔƒe blibo la dzi. Masɔmasɔ si nɔ dɔwɔƒea ƒe ƒuƒoƒoa kple habɔbɔ sia dome la va nɔ dzidzim ɖe edzi le ɣeyiɣi aɖe megbe eye wòka nya geɖe, siwo dometɔ aɖewoe nye habɔbɔa ƒe mɔfiame, alesi wòle be woakpɔ ga atsɔ awɔ ŋgɔyiyie, kple Zashi gakotokua ƒe etsɔme, si ƒuƒoƒoa di be yeaʋu ayi dɔwɔƒe si menye ame ŋutɔ tɔ o me be yeakpɔ ga tso egodo. Swihart ɖɔ dzodzoa be enye asiɖeɖe le dɔ ŋu tuameɖo, senya si gɔmee nye be wotrɔ nɔnɔmewo vevie ale gbegbe be wozi asiɖeɖe le dɔ ŋu dzi ŋutɔŋutɔ, eye wògblɔ be habɔbɔa me tɔ akpa gãtɔ ʋu le ɖekawɔwɔ me kple Zcash ƒe dɔdasi.

Nuŋlɔɖia ƒe akpa kemɛ le vevie na dzɔdzɔenyenye. Bootstrap gblɔ be masɔmasɔa nye dziɖuɖu kple sedziwɔwɔ si mekpɔa viɖe aɖeke tso eme o. Zcash ɖola, Zooko Wilcox, ʋli habɔbɔa me tɔ siwo ƒe ŋkɔ woyɔ le nyaʋiʋlia me ta le dutoƒo, hegblɔ be yewɔ dɔ kpli wo ƒe geɖe eye yebu wo be wonye ame siwo ƒe fɔmaɖimaɖi deŋgɔ, esime wòna eme kɔ ƒã be yemele akpa aɖeke wɔm le masɔmasɔa ŋutɔ me o.

Nu eve menɔ nya hem o. Akpa aɖeke megblɔ be ewɔ nuvlo aɖeke o, eyata esia nye adzɔhawo kple dziɖuɖuwo ƒe masɔmasɔ tsɔ wu be wòanye senya. Eye Zcash network ŋutɔ mekpɔ ŋusẽ ɖe edzi o, enɔ ʋuʋu ɖi, mɔɖeɖe aɖeke mele eŋu o, enɔ dedie, eye wòwɔ dɔ bliboe le ɣeyiɣi bliboa me, nya si dzi Swihart kple Wilcox siaa te gbe ɖo na ezãlawo.

Nusi kplɔe ɖoe nye ɖoɖo yeye wɔwɔ tsɔ wu be woagbã. Dɔwɔƒea ƒe ƒuƒoƒo si nɔ anyi tsã la yi edzi ɖo ZODL emegbe le ƒe 2026 me, eye Bootstrap ƒe habɔbɔa me tɔ etɔ̃ tsãtɔwo ɖo Sovright ɖe vovo. Ŋgɔyiyi va nɔ nɔnɔme si woma wu me le ƒuƒoƒo geɖe siwo le wo ɖokui si me.

Nya siwo ŋu woƒo nu tsoe le afisia la, wogblɔ le dutoƒo le X dzi le January 7, 2026 dzi, to Josh Swihart (@jswihart) kple Zooko Wilcox (@zooko) dzi, afisi woate ŋu axlẽ nya gbãtɔ siwo woŋlɔ la bliboe le.

<br/>

## Amekae tua Zcash fifia

Dɔa kaka ɖe habɔbɔ siwo le wo ɖokui si me egbea, eye akpa si me kɔ le wo dometɔ ɖesiaɖe si.

### Habɔbɔ eve siwo tso ƒe 2026 me la ma

1. ZODL, Zcash Open Development Lab, nye esi Electric Coin Company ƒe ƒuƒoƒo si nɔ anyi tsã la ɖo eye Josh Swihart ye nɔ ŋgɔ na wo. Efɔ ga si wu dɔlar miliɔn blaeve vɔ atɔ̃ tso egodo gadelawo gbɔ eye wòwɔa dɔ tso ɖoɖowɔɖi veviwo wɔwɔ ŋu, si me Halo 2 kpeɖodziɖoɖo si naa ŋusẽ Zcash ƒe asitsatsa yeyetɔ siwo ŋu wokpɔa ta ɖo, kple le ZODL gakotokua dzi, si nye asitelefon dzi gakotoku si wokpɔ ta na le gɔmedzedzea me si woyɔna tsã be Zashi. Kpɔ [ZODL](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zodl).
2. Sovright nye habɔbɔ si mekpɔa viɖe aɖeke tso eme o si Bootstrap ƒe habɔbɔa me tɔ etɔ̃ siwo nɔ anyi tsã ɖo. Eléa fɔ ɖe dɔwɔnuwo kple kpekpeɖeŋunana lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ŋu, eye wòtu Argos, si nye dɔwɔnu si akpe ɖe ezãla gbãtɔwo ŋu woaxɔ ga siwo tsi gakotoku xoxo aɖe si womelé be na o me. Kpɔ [Sovright ƒe nyawo](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/sovright).

### Protocol dzikpɔkpɔ, numekuku, kple node software

3. Zcash Foundation léa Zebra, Rust node si va zua network ƒe node vevitɔ esime zcashd asisi xoxoa xɔ dzudzɔ le dɔme. Ekpɔa Zcash GitHub habɔbɔa, z.cash nyatakakadzraɖoƒe, kple Zcash ƒe akɔnta vevitɔ si le X hã dzi, eye wòwɔa dɔ kple ZecHub be wòakpe asi ɖe nunɔamesi mawo dometɔ aɖewo dzi kpɔkpɔ ŋu. Kpɔ [Zcash Foundation](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zcash-foundation).
4. Shielded Labs nye dɔwɔƒe si le eɖokui si, si mekpɔa viɖe aɖeke tso eme o, si dzɔa ga tso nudzɔdzɔwo me, si le Switzerland. Eléa fɔ ɖe numekuku kple ɣeyiɣi didi ƒe ŋgɔyiyi ŋu, si me network sustainability mechanism si naa ga etsɔme ŋgɔyiyi kple Crosslink ƒe dɔwɔwɔ le kpeɖodzi siwo fia be stake finality tsɔtsɔ kpe ɖe Zcash ŋu hã le, eye wòna ga dedienɔnɔ ƒe agbalẽdzikpɔkpɔ si ke ɖe Orchard pool ƒe afɔku ŋu le ƒe 2026. Kpɔ [Labs Siwo Wotsɔ Akpoxɔnu Wɔe](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/shielded-labs).
5. Electric Coin Company gakpɔtɔ nye ŋutinya ƒe akpa aɖe abe habɔbɔ si wɔ Zcash eye wòdze egɔme le ƒe 2016. Kpɔ [Electric Coin Company](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/electric-coin-company).

### Scaling kple nya ɣaɣlawo zazã

6. Project Tachyon nye agbagbadzedze be woadzidze nu si nya ɣaɣlawo ŋuti nunyala Sean Bowe kplɔ. Edo mɔnu yeye aɖe si dzi gakotokuwo ato awɔ ɖeka kple blockchain, si woyɔna be oblivious synchronization, si ɖea asitsatsa dzi kpɔtɔna eye abe eƒe akpa aɖe ene la, eʋãa Zcash yia adzamenyawo gbɔ kpɔkpɔ le quantum megbe. Woŋlɔ eƒe dɔa ɖe agbalẽ me le [tachyon.z.ga si wotsɔna xɔa gae](https://tachyon.z.cash/).
7. Valar Ƒuƒoƒoa nye nya ɣaɣlawo ŋuti numekuku kple mɔ̃ɖaŋudɔwɔƒe si le dɔ wɔm tso Zcash ƒe ɖoɖowɔɖi ŋu na ame ŋutɔ ƒe, post-quantum digital ga le agbɔsɔsɔ me. Ewɔa dɔ aduadu kple Project Tachyon kplikplikpli le scaling kple quantum dɔwɔwɔ me. Nu geɖe tso eƒe dɔa ŋu le [valargroup.dev. ƒe ƒuƒoƒo](https://valargroup.dev/).

### Nutome kple nutoa me habɔbɔwo

8. Obscura Labs nye habɔbɔ si le eɖokui si si woŋlɔ ŋkɔ na le Nigeria, si ƒe susu le Afrika kple asi siwo le ŋgɔ yim ŋu, tu xɔtuɖaŋuwo kple mɔ siwo dzi woato axɔ wo. Kpɔ [Obscura Labs](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/obscura-labs).

### Susᴐsrɔ̃

9. ZecHub nye sukudede ƒe dɔwɔƒe si woɖe ɖe vovo na Zcash. Nutoa me tɔwo wɔa dɔ ɖekae tsɔ wɔa nyatakaka siwo kpena ɖe amewo ŋu be woase lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ gɔme eye wosrɔ̃a alesi woakpɔ gome le eme, to nufiamewo, wiki nuŋlɔɖiwo, podcast, kple kwasiɖa sia kwasiɖa ƒe nyadzɔdzɔgbalẽ me. Wiki si xlẽm nèle fifia la nye ZecHub ƒe akpa aɖe, eye Zcash Foundation wɔa ɖeka kplii tsɔ kpena ɖe nutoa me ƒe nunɔamesi aɖewo dzi kpɔkpɔ ŋu.

### Gakpekpeɖeŋunana

10. Zcash Community Grants naa ga na nudzɔla siwo le wo ɖokui si kple nutoa me dɔwo tso block ƒe fetu ƒe akpa aɖe me, si kakaa dɔwɔwɔ ɖe ƒuƒoƒo geɖewo me yia ŋgɔ wu habɔbɔ veviawo. Kpɔ [Zcash Nutome ƒe Gakpekpeɖeŋunana](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zcash-community-grants).
11. Ganyawo ƒe Adzamenyawo Gbɔkpɔha doa alɔ Zcash ƒe lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ kple nutoa me dɔwo. Kpɔ [Ganyawo Ŋuti Nyatakakawo Ŋuti Ðoɖowɔha](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/financial-privacy-foundation).

Habɔbɔ siawo katã léa be na nudzraɖoƒe siwo woate ŋu azã faa, eyata ame sia ame ate ŋu axlẽ woƒe dɔwo, alé ŋku ɖe wo ŋu, ahatu wo ɖe wo dzi. Eye menye habɔbɔawoe nye ŋutinya bliboa o. Nudzɔdzɔ vevi geɖewo tsoa ame ɖekaɖekawo kple dɔwɔƒe siwo wowɔ nubabla kplii siwo wokpɔa ga to kpekpeɖeŋunana me gbɔ, ke menye tso habɔbɔ veviwo ɖeɖeko gbɔ o. Gakotoku ƒe ƒuƒoƒowo, nutome nutoawo, dɔwɔƒe siwo le wo ɖokui si, kple gadelawo siwo léa ZEC ɖe asi hedoa alɔe evɔ wometu ɖoɖowɔɖia ɖo o la nɔa wo xa. Nusiwo woŋlɔ ɖe etame lae nye megbeƒu, ke menye nɔnɔmetata bliboa o.

<br/>

## Afisi nàdze egɔme tsoe abe ame yeye ene

Habɔbɔ si le vevie na wò la nɔ te ɖe nusi nèdi be yeawɔ dzi.

1. Be nàzã Zcash la, èdi be yeakpɔ gakotoku, eyata ZODL kple eƒe gakotoku nye dzɔdzɔme gɔmedzedze.
2. Be nàwɔ node alo ase network software gɔme la, kpɔ Zcash Foundation kple eƒe Zebra node.
3. Be nàkpɔ ga atsɔ awɔ dɔ aɖe alo adzɔ nu si woxea fe na la, kpɔ Zcash Community Grants.
4. Be nàkplɔ numekuku kple ɖoɖowɔɖia ƒe etsɔme ɖo la, zɔ ɖe Shielded Labs, Project Tachyon, kple Valar Group dzi.

## Nukpɔsusu totro siwo bɔ

- Zcash menye dɔwɔƒe ɖeka aɖeke tɔ alo kpɔ ŋusẽ ɖe edzi o, habɔbɔ ɖeka aɖeke mate ŋu atrɔ alo atɔ te network la le eɖokui si o
- Ƒe 2026 ƒe nyaʋiʋlia mekpɔ ŋusẽ ɖe network, ga, alo ame ŋutɔ ƒe nyatakakawo dzi o, enye habɔbɔa ƒe masɔmasɔ, eye ɖoɖowɔɖia nɔ edzi yim abe alesi wòle le ɣeyiɣi bliboa me ene
- Ƒuƒoƒo si dzo le Electric Coin Company mewu Zcash nu o, dɔa ʋu yi habɔbɔ yeye siwo le wo ɖokui si gbɔ
- Habɔbɔ geɖe ƒe amesinɔnɔ nye ŋusẽ, ke menye gbɔdzɔgbɔdzɔ o, eɖea kpododonu ɖekaɖekawo ɖa eye wònana dɔa nɔa te ɖe nɔnɔme sesẽwo nu
- ZEC léle ɖe asi alo dodo ɖe ŋgɔ mesɔ kple Zcash tutu o, gadelawo kple nyanyuigblɔlawo nye nutoa me tɔwo ƒe akpa aɖe gake woto vovo tso ƒuƒoƒo siwo wɔa ɖoɖowɔɖia gbɔ
