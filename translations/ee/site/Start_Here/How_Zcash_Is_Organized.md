# Ale si Zcash wɔa ɖoɖo le

## TL;DR

- Ƒomevi ɖeka menye wɔ Zcash o, ke boŋ hame siwo nye ɖe ɖe le wɔe, eye wòwɔ ɖe sia ɖe ƒe akpa wɔn
- Eƒe ŋutinya ƒe akpa gã me la, hame eve kplɔ ŋgɔ le mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖo me: Electric Coin Company kple Zcash Foundation
- Le 2026 ƒe dzove me la, Electric Coin Company ƒe ƒuƒo blibo la dze ɖe edzi le dziɖuɖu [governance] ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu ta, eye nutome la woɖo ɖe ƒuƒo siwo le ɖe ɖe me
- Egbe la, ƒuƒoawo vovovo wɔn protocol, ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ [node] ƒe software, gakotoku [wallet], nunyala ƒe dɔ, nu ƒe dziyiyi [scaling], kple ga ƒe nana
- Hame aɖeke mekpɔa Zcash ɖekaɖeka o, network la nye open source eye zãzã faa [permissionless], eye wòyi edzi wɔa dɔ nyuie le trɔtrɔ siawo katã me

<br/>

## Ame kae wònye na

- Ame yeye siwo di be yewoase ame siwo wɔa eye wɔnɔa Zcash dzi gɔme
- Ame sia ame si tɔtɔe le hame ƒe ŋkɔ geɖe siwo le nutome la me ta
- Ame siwo do kpɔ ɖe dɔwɔwɔ ŋu alo le afɔfɔ ɖe afia aɖe ŋu be woawɔ dɔ kplii alo woaɖo ɖoɖo aɖe ɖa

<br/>

## Nu ka ta esia le vevie

Ɖoɖo la ƒe gɔmesese wɔa nu bubuawo katã bɔbɔe. Efia wò ame si wɔnɔa code si wòtsɔa wɔ dɔ dzi, ame si nàte ŋu ayi gbɔ be nàbia ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu [grant], kple ame si wɔ akpa si le network la me si nèle dzidzɔe ŋu. Eɖea Zcash ƒe ŋusẽ si menoa tsi o aɖe hia: elabena wo ma dɔ la ɖe ƒuƒo siwo le ɖe ɖe dome, dɔƒe ɖeka si ŋu nu katã dze mɔ o ate ŋu akpɔ project la ɖeka alo akpɔe ɖe te.

Axa sia nye anyigbatata. Na hame sia hame si katã exɔ axa blibo le wiki sia dzi la, àkpɔ dzesi kpui aɖe kple link be nàxlẽ nu geɖe, le nu si woŋlɔ xoxo le afi ma ƒe gbugbɔɖi ƒe nɔnɔ teƒe.

<br/>

## Ale si wònɔna tsã

Le Zcash ƒe ŋutinya ƒe akpa gã me la, hame eve kplɔ mɔ la ŋgɔ.

Electric Coin Company wɔ Zcash le 2016 me eye wòɖo core development ƒe ƒuƒo la ƒe amewo geɖe dɔ. Bootstrap, nonprofit ƒe board si wowɔ be wòakpe ɖe Zcash ŋu, kpɔe dzi. Zcash Foundation wɔ dɔ kplii abe nonprofit si le ɖe eɖokui ŋu ene, eye wòtsɔ eɖokui de protocol la ƒe dzikpɔkpɔ kple ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ si le ɖe eɖokui ŋu ƒe tutu me. Woɖea ga na wo evea zi geɖe tso gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe [block] ƒe fetu ƒe akpa si woda ɖi na mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖo ta me.

Pillar eve sia ƒe ɖoɖo la li hena ƒe geɖe, gake wòde asi le gaƒe mama sia kple hame eveawo ƒe nɔnɔ ɖekae. Esi mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖo ƒe ga si nègbɔna la trɔ eye eƒe ŋkeke didi ƒe future mekpɔa edzi o la, nusiatsɔ awɔ ga na dɔ si le edzi yina la zu kuxi si va kpɔ ŋusẽ geɖe. Ga ƒe kuxi sia le nu geɖe si trɔ emegbe la ƒe megbe, eye wònye nusi ta ƒuƒo aɖewo ɖea asi le ga si tso afima va, esi bubuawo le ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu dzi.

<br/>

## 2026 ƒe ɖoɖo yeye

Le 2026 ƒe dzove me la, ɖoɖo la trɔ ŋutɔ. Le dzove ƒe ŋkeke 7 lia dzi la, Electric Coin Company ƒe chief executive, Josh Swihart, gblɔ le X dzi be company la ƒe ƒuƒo blibo la dze ɖe edzi.

Bootstrap nye nonprofit si wowɔ le 2020 me be wòakplɔ Electric Coin Company dzi, si zu eƒe wholly owned subsidiary. Ƒomevi la kple board sia dome kpekpeɖeŋu la tso dzi ɖo dzi eye wòku ɖe nu vovovo aɖewo ŋu, abe hame la ƒe afi si wòle yi ge, ale si woaxɔ ga na mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖo, kple Zashi gakotoku la ƒe future ene; ƒuƒo la di be woatsɔe aɖe private company me be woate ŋu axɔ ga tso afima. Swihart ƒo nu tso dzoɖe la ŋu abe constructive discharge ene, si nye se ƒe nyagblɔɖi si fia be wotrɔ nɔnɔawo vevie ale gbegbe be dzoɖe zu nu si wɔm wòle vevie, eye wògblɔ be board la ƒe akpa gã la va le afi si menye ɖeka kple Zcash ƒe mission o.

Nya la ƒe akpa bubu hia be nu nanɔ dzɔdzɔe. Bootstrap de kpekpeɖeŋu la fia abe dziɖuɖu kple nonprofit ƒe se ƒe wɔwɔ nyuie ƒe nya ene. Zcash ƒe founder, Zooko Wilcox, ɖe asi kpe board ƒe amewo siwo wɔ ŋkɔ le kpekpeɖeŋu la me ŋu le dutoƒo me, eye wògblɔ be ewo dɔ kplii hena ƒe geɖe eye wòkpɔ wo abe amewo siwo ƒe nɔnɔ nyo ŋutɔ ene, esi wòɖe eƒe nya me be menɔ akpa aɖeke dzi le kpekpeɖeŋu la me o.

Nu eve li si mewɔ kpekpeɖeŋu o. Ƒomevi aɖeke meɖe nya be ame aɖe wɔ nu vɔ̃ si se bla o, eyata esia nye company kple dziɖuɖu ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu, menye se ƒe nya o. Eye Zcash network la ŋutɔ mekpɔ nɔnɔ aɖeke o; enɔ open source, zãzã faa, dedie, eye wòle dɔ wɔm blibo le ɣeyiɣi blibo la me, nya si Swihart kple Wilcox evea veve na zãlawo.

Nu si va yina emegbe nye ɖoɖo yeye, menye vuvumu o. Company ƒe ƒuƒo xoxo la va wɔ ZODL emegbe le 2026 me, eye le akpa bubu me la, Bootstrap ƒe board ƒe amewo etɔ va wɔ Sovright. Mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖo la va nɔ afi geɖe le ƒuƒo siwo le ɖe ɖe dome.

Nyagblɔɖi siwo woƒo fiã le afisia la, woƒo wo le dutoƒo me le X dzi le 2026 ƒe dzove ƒe ŋkeke 7 lia, tso Josh Swihart (@jswihart) kple Zooko Wilcox (@zooko) gbɔ, afi si wòate ŋu axlẽ post gɔmedzedzeawo blibo.

<br/>

## Ame kae wɔnɔa Zcash tutum egbe

Dɔ la le hame siwo le ɖe ɖe me, eye hame sia hame xɔ dɔ ƒe akpa kɔkɔe aɖe.

### Hame eve siwo tso 2026 ƒe mama me

1. ZODL, Zcash Open Development Lab, tso Electric Coin Company ƒe ƒuƒo xoxo la me eye Josh Swihart kplɔa wo ŋgɔ. Exɔ dollar million bla eve kple atɔ̃ wu tso investors siwo le afima, eye wòwɔa core protocol ƒe mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖo, si me Halo 2 proving system si nana ŋusẽ Zcash ƒe ɣaɣla [shielded] gaɖoɖo [transaction] yeyeawo le, kple ZODL gakotoku, mobile gakotoku si le ɣaɣla me tso gɔmedzedze, si ŋkɔ xoxo nye Zashi. Kpɔ [ZODL](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zodl).
2. Sovright nye nonprofit si Bootstrap ƒe board ƒe amewo etɔ xoxo wɔ. Ewɔa dɔ le dɔwɔnu [tools] kple kpekpeɖeŋu na nutome la ŋu, eye wotu Argos, dɔwɔnu si kpea ɖe zãla gbãtɔawo ŋu be woagbugbɔ axɔ ga si wogblẽ le gakotoku xoxo si meganɔa dzikpɔkpɔ te o me. Kpɔ [Sovright](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/sovright).

### Protocol ƒe dzikpɔkpɔ, nunyala ƒe dɔ, kple ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ ƒe software

3. Zcash Foundation wɔnɔa Zebra, Rust ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ si zu network la ƒe ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ vevi esi woɖe zcashd client xoxo la ɖa. Ewɔa dzikpɔkpɔ na Zcash GitHub hame, z.cash website, kple Zcash account vevi le X dzi, eye wòwɔa dɔ kple ZecHub be woakpe ɖe assets siawo ƒe akpa aɖewo ƒe dzikpɔkpɔ ŋu. Kpɔ [Zcash Foundation](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zcash-foundation).
4. Shielded Labs nye nonprofit si le ɖe eɖokui ŋu, si xɔa donation ƒe ga, eye wòle Switzerland. Etsɔ eɖokui dea nunyala ƒe dɔ kple nɔnɔ didi ƒe ɖeɖe ŋu, si me network ƒe nɔnɔɖeɖe ƒe mɔnu si tsɔa ga dea future ƒe mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖo me, kple Crosslink ƒe dɔ le proof of stake finality ƒe tsɔtsɔ kpe Zcash ŋu me, eye wòna ga na security audit si kpɔ Orchard gaƒuƒoƒo [pool] ƒe gbegblẽ le 2026 me. Kpɔ [Shielded Labs](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/shielded-labs).
5. Electric Coin Company gale ŋutinya la ƒe akpa, abe hame si wɔ eye dze Zcash gɔme le 2016 me ene. Kpɔ [Electric Coin Company](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/electric-coin-company).

### Nu ƒe dziyiyi kple cryptography

6. Project Tachyon nye nu ƒe dziyiyi ƒe dɔ si cryptographer Sean Bowe kplɔa ŋgɔ. Eɖo ɖe mɔ yeye aɖe ŋu na gakotokuwo be woasynchronize kple gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu [blockchain], si ŋkɔ nye oblivious synchronization, si tsɔa gaɖoɖowo me eye, abe eƒe nɔnɔ ƒe akpa ene, etsɔa Zcash yi post-quantum ɣaɣla gbɔ. Woŋlɔ eƒe dɔ la le [tachyon.z.cash](https://tachyon.z.cash/).
7. Valar Group nye cryptography ƒe nunyala kple engineering lab si le wɔm le Zcash protocol ŋu na private, post-quantum digital cash si le ɖoƒe gã me. Ewɔa dɔ vevie kple Project Tachyon le nu ƒe dziyiyi kple quantum ƒe dɔ ŋu. Nusiwo tso eƒe dɔ me geɖe le [valargroup.dev](https://valargroup.dev/).

### Hame siwo le nutome kple community me

8. Obscura Labs nye hame si le ɖe eɖokui ŋu, si wogbalẽ le Nigeria, eye wòle Africa kple emerging markets me, le infrastructure kple mɔwo siwo dzi adoption ato ŋu ƒe tutu me. Kpɔ [Obscura Labs](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/obscura-labs).

### Nusrɔ̃

9. ZecHub nye ha nuɖo agni [decentralized] nusrɔ̃ ƒe hub na Zcash. Community ƒe amewo wɔa dɔ ɖekae be woawɔ, aɖe ɖase le, eye woado ŋgɔ na numeɖeɖe si kpea ɖe amewo ŋu be woase nutome la gɔme eye woasrɔ ale si woate ŋu akpɔe me, to tutorials, wiki ƒe nyagbalẽwo, podcast, kple nyadzɔdzɔgbalẽ [newsletter] si doa go le kwasiɖa sia kwasiɖa me. Wiki si nèle xlẽ fifia la nye ZecHub ƒe akpa, eye Zcash Foundation wɔa dɔ kplii be wòakpe ɖe community ƒe resources aɖewo ƒe dzikpɔkpɔ ŋu.

### Ga ƒe nana

10. Zcash Community Grants naa ga na ame siwo wɔa dɔ le ɖe wo ɖokuiwo ŋu kple community ƒe projects tso gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu ƒe akpa me, eye wòmãa dɔ la ɖe ƒuƒo geɖe dome le core ƒe hameawo ƒe xoxo. Kpɔ [Zcash Community Grants](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/zcash-community-grants).
11. Financial Privacy Foundation kpea ɖe Zcash nutome kple community ƒe projects ŋu. Kpɔ [Financial Privacy Foundation](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-organizations/financial-privacy-foundation).

Hame siawo katã ɖea open source repositories, eyata ame sia ame ate ŋu axlẽ, adzro, eye wòatu woƒe dɔ ŋu. Eye hameawo menye nya blibo la o. Dɔ vevi geɖe tsoa ame ɖekaɖekawo kple company siwo wowɔ contract kple wo eye woƒe ga tsoa ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu me, le core ƒe hameawo ko ƒe nɔnɔ teƒe. Gakotoku ƒe ƒuƒoawo, nutome siwo le afi vovovo, mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖoŋlɔla [developer] siwo le ɖe wo ɖokuiwo ŋu, kple investors siwo le ZEC eye wodea asi kpee, ganye woanɔ wo dome, gake wometa protocol la o. Nya si le etame la nye ƒeƒedzikpɔkpɔ, menye nɔnɔ blibo la o.

<br/>

## Afi si nàdze egɔme abe ame yeye ene

Hame si le vevie na wò la dze egɔme le nu si nèdi be nàwɔ ŋu.

1. Ne èdi be nàzã Zcash la, gakotoku hia wò, eyata ZODL kple eƒe gakotoku nye afi nyuie be nàdze egɔme.
2. Ne èdi be nàwɔ ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ alo nàse network ƒe software gɔme la, kpɔ Zcash Foundation kple eƒe Zebra ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ.
3. Ne èdi be nàna ga na project aɖe alo nàwɔ dɔ si woaxɔ fetu na la, kpɔ Zcash Community Grants.
4. Ne èdi be nàkpɔ nunyala ƒe dɔ kple protocol la ƒe future la, ɖo to Shielded Labs, Project Tachyon, kple Valar Group ŋu.

## Nunya siwo ame geɖe bua be wonye nyateƒe gake menye nenema o

- Zcash menye company ɖeka ƒe tɔ alo nɔnɔ te o, hame ɖeka mate ŋu atrɔ alo atsɔ network la atsi ɖe eɖokui ŋu o
- 2026 ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu la mekpɔ network la, gaawo, alo ɣaɣla la o; hame ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu koe nye, eye protocol la wɔ dɔ nyuie le ɣeyiɣi blibo la me
- Ƒuƒo si dzo tso Electric Coin Company me la mewu Zcash o; dɔ la yi hame yeye siwo le ɖe wo ɖokuiwo ŋu gbɔ
- Hame geɖe ƒe nɔnɔ nye ŋusẽ, menye gbɔgbɔmeƒoƒo o; etsɔa dɔƒe ɖeka si ŋu nu katã dze mɔ ɖa eye wòna project la ƒe ŋusẽ nɔ anyi
- ZEC ƒe nɔnɔ alo eƒe ɖoɖo ŋgɔ menye Zcash ƒe tutu o; investors kple evangelists nye community la ƒe akpa, gake wonye vovototo tso ƒuƒo siwo ɖoa protocol la ŋu
