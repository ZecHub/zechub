# Zcash Mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ [Network Upgrades]

Zcash Dzea nyuie to mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ me: tɔtrɔ siwo woɖoa ɖeka le sewo si dzi ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ [node] ɖe sia ɖe ɖoa ƒo, eye woɖea woƒe dɔwɔwɔ gɔme le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe [block] ƒe kɔkɔe si woɖo la dzi. Tɔtrɔ ɖe sia ɖe si le ete la ƒe axa tɔxɛ le eɖokui si ɖe nu si wotrɔ kple ta si ta wotrɔe la me kple gbe bɔbɔe. Èye ame yeye le Zcash me oa? Xlẽ wo le woƒe woƒe ɖoɖo nu, tso Sprout yi Ironwood.

Ne wodi be nàkpɔ ale si Zcash ƒe ɣaɣla [privacy] trɔ le mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ siawo me la, kpɔ [ɣaɣla ƒe Nunyametsotso](https://zechub.wiki/zcash-evolution). Axa sia nye index. Ema nye ɣeyiɣi ƒe mɔdzraɖoƒe.

Woɖea ɣeyiɣiwo fia le UTC me. Ɖoɖowo ƒuƒopé [dashboard] aɖewo fia wo le ƒeƒe me ɣeyiɣi me, ke nye gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ma ke kple ɣeyiɣi ma ke. Tɔtrɔ ɖe sia ɖe ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ƒe dzedze ƒe se si mele trɔm o nye eƒe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe kɔkɔe, menye kalenda ƒe ŋkeke o.

### NU7 ƒe nɔnɔme

NU7 le dɔ wɔm le Testnet si duto ate ŋu akpɔ la dzi le kɔkɔe **4,465,026** (wodze egɔme le October 4, 2026). Mainnet ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ƒe kɔkɔe nye **3,543,405** ([ZIP 259](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0259)), eye woɖo be woadze egɔme le **November 5, 2026**.

Eƒe dɔƒe (ZIP 259):

- **Sekondi 25 gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒewo** (ZIP 218) — kpekpeɖeŋu gbãtɔ naa ɣeyiɣi kpui le teƒe 3; ZEC ƒe gaɖeɖe le ŋkeke sia ŋkeke me metrɔ o.
- **Mɔ̃ ƒe Dziɖuɖu ƒe Mɔnu** (ZIP 235 + ZIP 237) — woaɖe gaɖoɖo ƒe adzɔwo ƒe 60% le gaƒeƒu me vaɖo dzadzraƒe aɖe me, eye woagbugbɔa woade na nukulawo le ɣeyiɣi ƒe ɖoɖo si mekaka fetu ƒe afãmama o me, si adze egɔme le February 2031 ƒe ƒome.
- **Sprout ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ƒe nudidi** (ZIP 2003) — version 4 gaɖoɖowo meganye seawo nu o; ga siwo gaƒo le Sprout me la meganyo avu o.
- Ɣaɣla ƒe nudzɔdzɔwo ƒe seɖoƒe kple xexlẽ ƒe kuxi ƒe ɣeyiɣi ƒe abɔ gã wu (17 → 102 gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒewo).

Womeɖoa gaɖoɖo ƒe nɔnɔme yeye aɖeke o. Gbegbɔgblɔ ɖi [consensus] ƒe alɔ̃fɔ ƒe dzesi: `0x77190ad9`.
