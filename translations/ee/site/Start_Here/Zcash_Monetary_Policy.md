# Zcash Gɔmeɖosewo

## Nukae nye Zcash?

Zcash's ga ƒe gɔmeɖoanyi sɔ kple Bitcoin's fixed supply si nye 21 million ZEC ga units. Le sɛkɛnd 75 ɖesiaɖe me la, yeye aɖe [xe mɔ](https://zcash.readthedocs.io/en/latest/rtd_pages/glossary.html#:~:text=Block,mempool%20in%20an%20unconfirmed%20state.) woɖea tome na Zcash blockchain eye block ƒe fetu si nye 1.5625 ZEC va nɔa tsatsam. Womaa block reward sia na tomenukulawo kple.. [Zcash ŋgɔyiyi ƒe gaxɔ](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1014).

Block ƒe teƒeɖoɖoa ƒe home dzi ɖena kpɔtɔna ɖe afã me abe ƒe ene ɖesiaɖe ene vaseɖe esime ZEC miliɔn 21 la katã nanɔ asitsatsa me. Zcash ga ƒe asixɔxɔ sɔ kple Bitcoin. Ele vevie be míade dzesii be ne wole gaku yeyewo wɔm la, ga ƒe asixɔxɔ dzi ɖena kpɔtɔna, eye le afã ɖesiaɖe me la, ga ƒe asixɔxɔ dzi ɖena kpɔtɔna ŋutɔ.
