# Zcash Gɔmedzedze

## Nu kae nye Zcash ƒe gaɖoɖo ƒe agbenɔnɔ?

Zcash ƒe ga ƒe gɔmeɖoɖo sɔ kple Bitcoin ƒe ga ƒe akpa 21 million ZEC si wode ɖo. Le sekond 75 siaa megbe la, wɔa nukuku na yeye [gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe](https://zcash.readthedocs.io/en/latest/rtd_pages/glossary.html#:~:text=Block,mempool%20in%20an%20unconfirmed%20state.) le Zcash gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu me, eye gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu 1.5625 ZEC va yɔ le ga ƒe mɔzɔzɔ me. Mema ƒe fetu sia wo maa na nukulawo kple [Zcash ƒe ŋgɔyiyi gaƒoƒo](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1014).

Gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu la ƒe hãhã maa va zu afã le ƒe ene sia ƒe abe, va se ɖe esi ZEC 21 million katã anɔ mɔzɔzɔ me. Zcash ƒe ga ƒe vɔvɔɖiɖi sɔ kplikpaa kple Bitcoin tɔ. Ele vevie be nàde dzesii be, esi wɔa ga yeyeawo la, ga ƒe vɔvɔɖiɖi ɖea dzi, eye le hãhã ɖesiaɖe me la, eƒe kadodo ɖea dzi ŋutɔ.
