# Zcash Ƒe Gɔme

## Nu ka nye Zcash ƒe gaɖoɖonuwo?

Zcash ƒe gaŋusẽɖoɖo la sɔ kple Bitcoin ƒe didiɖoɖo si gbɔna: ZEC gaɖoɖonu miliɔn 21. Le ŋkeke 25 ɣeyiɣiwo me (ŋkeke 75 ɣeyiɣiwo le ŋgɔ na esime NU7 wòva dze egɔme le 2026 ƒe Novemba 6), wòwɔna [gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe](https://zcash.readthedocs.io/en/latest/rtd_pages/glossary.html#:~:text=Block,mempool%20in%20an%20unconfirmed%20state.) yeye aɖe le Zcash blockchain ƒuƒoƒo me, eye gaƒoƒo ƒe fetu si nye 0.52083333 ZEC va ɖoa dɔwɔwɔ me. Hafi NU7, gaƒoƒo ƒe fetu la nye 1.5625 ZEC ɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe sia ɖe sia; [ZIP 218](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0218) ɖe eƒe asi eve me, kple ɣeyiɣi si wòle ɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe sia ɖe sia ŋu, ale be ZEC yeye si wòva ɖo ɖe ŋkeke ɖeka me meŋu trɔ o. Tso NU7, 0.52083333 ZEC la ma 80% na nukula la (0.41666668 ZEC, kple gaɖoɖo adzɔwo), 8% na Zcash Community Grants (0.04166666 ZEC) eye 12% na lockbox la (0.06249999 ZEC), eye wòɖoa [ZIP 1015](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1015) nu.

Gaƒoƒo ƒe fetu ƒe homea ƒe asi eve me le ɣleti 48 siwo ƒe ɣeyiɣi me, va se ɖe esime ZEC gaɖoɖonu miliɔn 21 katã le dɔwɔwɔ me. Zcash ƒe gaŋɔŋlɔnu ƒe dzɔdzɔme le vavã la me sɔ kple Bitcoin tɔ. Ele vevie be wòanya be ne gaɖoɖonu yeyewo wɔa la, gaŋɔŋlɔnu la nɔa ɖe edzi; eye le asi eve me wɔwɔ sia ɖe sia me la, dzɔdzɔme ƒe ɖoɖo la tsia ɖe edzi ŋutɔ.
