# Zcash Ƒe Gaɖoɖo Ƒe Mɔ̃

## TL;DR

* Zcash ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ si wòate ŋu axɔ la nye 21 million ZEC, eye Bitcoin hã ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ ƒe se sia tɔ.
* Tso esime NU7 dze egɔme le 6 November 2026 la, gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe yeye vaa zi 25 seconds ɖesiaɖi eye wòtona 0.52083333 ZEC.
* Esia tsɔa ZEC yeye 1,800 ɖo ŋkume le ŋkeke ɖesiaɖi, eye woƒe agbɔsɔsɔ sɔ kple esi nɔ anyi hafi NU7.
* Gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖesiaɖi ƒe fetu maɖo 80% na nukula la, 8% na Zcash Community Grants, eye 12% na lockbox la.
* Gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu la ƒe afãa vaa le afɔdɔdzesi 4 ɖesiaɖi. Wobu be fetu ƒe afãmama si kplɔe ɖo ava le November 2028 ƒe nuwuwu.

## Nyatakakadzro Ƒe Xexlẽme

### Nu ka nye Zcash ƒe ɖoɖo?

Zcash ƒe ga ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ ƒe mɔ̃ sɔ kple Bitcoin ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ si le 21 million ZEC ƒe gaɖoɖo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe se me. Le seconds 25 ɖesiaɖi (seconds 75 hafi NU7 dze egɔme le 6 November 2026) la, [gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe](https://zcash.readthedocs.io/en/latest/rtd_pages/glossary.html#:~:text=Block,mempool%20in%20an%20unconfirmed%20state.) yeye wònukukua ɖe Zcash blockchain la me, eye gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu 0.52083333 ZEC vaa ɖe amewo siwo ƒe asi me. Hafi NU7 va, fetu la nye 1.5625 ZEC le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖesiaɖi; [ZIP 218](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0218) ɖe eƒe agbɔsɔsɔ ƒe etɔ̃ me, eye wòɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe ɣeyiɣi hã ƒe etɔ̃ me, ale be ZEC yeye siwo vaa le ŋkeke ɖesiaɖi me metrɔ o. Tso NU7, 0.52083333 ZEC la maa ɖe ale: 80% na nukula la (0.41666668 ZEC, kple gaɖoɖo ƒe adzɔwo), 8% na Zcash Community Grants (0.04166666 ZEC), eye 12% na lockbox la (0.06249999 ZEC), le [ZIP 1015](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1015) nu.

Gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu la ƒe afãa vaa le afɔdɔdzesi 4 ɖesiaɖi, va se ɖe esime ZEC 21 million blibo nɔ amewo siwo ƒe asi me. Zcash ƒe ga ƒe asixɔxɔ ƒe tsɔtsɔe sɔ kple Bitcoin ƒe tɔ hafi tɔ. Wòle be nàde dzesi be ne ga yeyewo wɔa la, ga ƒe asixɔxɔ ƒe tsɔtsɔe nana wòɖea ɖe edzi, eye fetu ƒe afãmama ɖesiaɖi ƒe ɖoɖo ɖea asi le afɔdɔdzesi geɖe.

## Nɔnɔmetata / Kpɔɖeŋu

Bu afɔku si doa ga yeye ɖe ameƒe vault si woɖe ƒo la ŋu. Vault la me 21 million ZEC le, eye ame aɖeke mate ŋu atsɔ ga ɖeka akpe eŋu o.

Le seconds 25 ɖesiaɖi, afɔku la doa ga yeye sue aɖe, si nye 0.52083333 ZEC. Nukula si kɔ gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe la ƒe 80% le afɔku la me. Zcash Community Grants xɔa 8%. 12% mamlɛtɔ la yia lockbox la me.

Le afɔdɔdzesi 4 ɖesiaɖi, ga si doa la ƒe akpa ƒe afãa zu. Esiae nye fetu ƒe afãmama.

NU7 wɔe be afɔku la doa ga zi etɔ̃ wu tsã, eye afɔku ɖesiaɖi doa ga ƒe akpa ƒe afãa. Ga yeye siwo doa le ŋkeke ɖesiaɖi me nɔ abe tsã ene.

## Nyatakakadzro Ƒe Dedie

### ZEC Yeye le Ŋkeke ɖesiaɖi, Ɣeyiɣi Ƒe Akpa ɖesiaɖi

Fetu ƒe afãmama ɖea fetu si woxɔna le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖesiaɖi me ƒe afã. Gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe siwo vaa kabakaba (Blossom le 2019, NU7 le 2026) hã ɖea gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖesiaɖi ƒe fetu ƒe afã, eye wotsɔe ɖea gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe ɣeyiɣi hã ƒe afã, ale be woɖea ŋkeke ɖesiaɖi ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ ɖe esi nɔ tsã.

| Ɣeyiɣi | Gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe ɣeyiɣi | Fetu le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖesiaɖi | ZEC Yeye le ŋkeke ɖesiaɖi |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Dɔwɔwɔ ƒe gɔmedzedze, 2016 va 2019 | seconds 150 | 12.5 ZEC | 7,200 |
| [Blossom](/zcash-tech/blossom), December 2019 va November 2020 | seconds 75 | 6.25 ZEC | 7,200 |
| Fetu ƒe afãmama gbãtɔ ([Canopy](/zcash-tech/canopy)), November 2020 va November 2024 | seconds 75 | 3.125 ZEC | 3,600 |
| Fetu ƒe afãmama evelia ([NU6](/zcash-tech/nu6)), November 2024 va November 2026 | seconds 75 | 1.5625 ZEC | 1,800 |
| [NU7](/zcash-tech/nu7), November 2026 va November 2028 ƒe nuwuwu | seconds 25 | 0.52083333 ZEC | 1,800 |
| Fetu ƒe afãmama etɔ̃lia, wobu be ava le November 2028 ƒe nuwuwu | seconds 25 | Fetu si le fifia ƒe afã | abe 900 ene |

Ŋkeke ɖesiaɖi ƒe xexlẽme siawo katã nye fetu le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖesiaɖi me ƒe xexlẽme si woɖo ɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe siwo ava le ŋkeke me (seconds 86,400 ƒe maɖoɖo kple gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe ɣeyiɣi). Gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe ɣeyiɣi ƒoa viɖe, eya ta ga si wodoa le ŋkeke ɖesiaɖi me trɔa viɖe le xexlẽme siawo ŋu.

### Fetu ƒe Afãmama si Kplɔe ɖo Ƒe Ɣeyiɣi

NU7 wɔe be gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe vaa zi etɔ̃ wu tsã, eya ta fetu ƒe afãmama si kplɔe ɖo ƒe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe nɔmba hã trɔ. Fetu ƒe afãmama si kplɔe ɖo le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe nɔmba 6,132,390 dzi, eye wobu be ava le November 2028 ƒe nuwuwu ([ZIP 259](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0259)). Nyatakakadzro xoxoawo ɖea gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe nɔmba 4,406,400. Nɔmba ma nyo hafi NU7 va.

### Adzɔwo kple Network Sustainability Mechanism

Tso NU7, gaɖoɖo ƒe adzɔwo siwo le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖesiaɖi me ƒe 60% wòtsɔna le amewo ƒe asi me, eye wòtsɔna ɖo ga xɔxɔ si le gbɔna na ame sia ame. Tso gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe nɔmba 8,939,664, si wobu be ava le February 2031 me la, gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖesiaɖi de ga si wòna nukulawo tso ga xɔxɔ sia me la ƒe akpa ɖo, eye wòtsɔna kpe ɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu si wòna ɖesiaɖi ŋu. 21 million ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ ƒe se la metrɔ o. [Network Sustainability Mechanism](/zcash-tech/network-sustainability-mechanism) ƒe axa la ɖe alesi wòwɔna ŋu nyatakakadzro.

## Axa Siwo Sɔ Kplii

- [Network Sustainability Mechanism](/zcash-tech/network-sustainability-mechanism): afi si 60% le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖesiaɖi ƒe adzɔwo yina, eye ɣeyiɣi si wògatrɔna.
- [Development Fund](/start-here/development-fund): gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu ƒe akpa si wotsɔna ɖe ŋgɔɖoɖo ŋu ƒe ŋutinya.

- [Zcash Community Grants](/zcash-organizations/zcash-community-grants): ɖoɖo si xɔa 8% ƒe akpa la.
- [Zcash Nukukula Ƒe Gaƒuƒoƒowo](/using-zcash/zcash-mining-pools): afi si nukulawo geɖe xɔa gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu ƒe akpa.
