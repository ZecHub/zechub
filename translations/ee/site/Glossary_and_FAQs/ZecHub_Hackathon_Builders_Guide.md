# ZecHub hackathon ƒe nuwɔla ƒe mɔfia

## Nya veviwo

- Nya si ta nèle nu wɔm hafi nàŋlɔ code, dɔwɔwɔ ƒe vevienyenye wu nɔnɔmetata
- Nàwɔe bɔbɔe, susu sue si wowɔ nyuie wu susu gã si wɔwɔ medze egɔme o
- Srɔ̃ Zcash infrastructure stack la zuɖe, elɛ kekeƒe ŋuɖeƒe si sesẽ wu
- Ne wò app wɔa ga ƒe tsɔtsɔ, ele be wòawɔ dɔ le mainnet dzi, tu wo le testnet dzi, eye nàɖe eƒe dɔwɔwɔ fia le mainnet dzi
- Agbalẽŋlɔɖoɖo kple demo si ƒe gɔme se nyuie ate ŋu anye vevi wu ọjaŋutɔ
- Dzidzedze nye gɔmedzedze ƒe fli, ewɔa wò ŋkɔ nyuie eye wòɖea mɔwo ʋu na wò le hadome

<br/>

## Ame kawoe nye esia na

- Nuwɔla yeye siwo ge ɖe ZecHub alo Zcash hackathon me zi gbãtɔ
- Mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖoŋlɔlawo tso ecosystem bubuwo me siwo le yeye le Zcash ŋu
- Ame sia ame si di be yeatrɔ hackathon ƒe dɔwɔwɔ zu nu si anɔ anyi

<br/>

## Dze egɔme kple nya si ta

Hafi nàʋu wò editor, ka nyateƒe be ka kuxi nèle ɖum kple nya si ta ame aɖe atsɔ vevie. Dodokpɔ nyui aɖe bɔbɔe: Ne nu si nèle wɔm la menɔ anyi o la, ame aɖe atɔ asi le eŋu oa? Wɔ nu si wò ŋutɔ nàtsɔ awɔ dɔ. Ɣaɣla [privacy] nye nya si ta Zcash le anyi, eyata se nya si ta ɣaɣla le vevie na amewo siwo ta nèle nu wɔm, eye nàɖe eƒe mɔ na dɔwɔwɔ bliboa.

<br/>

## Srɔ̃ stack la tsã

Nunya si wɔnɔa be wòava ɖe nuwɔlawo tso ɖoɖonu bubuwo me dzi akpɔ zi geɖe la nye srɔ̃srɔ̃ ƒe mɔ na Zcash infrastructure, menye code ŋlɔŋlɔ o. Na ɣeyiɣi wò ŋutɔ be nàse ale si akpaawo doa ƒu hafi nàɖo wò app ƒe ɖoɖo. Dze egɔme kple stack vevi la, si woyɔa zi geɖe be Z to the third: zebrad, light server aɖe, kple gakotoku [wallet] aɖe. Emegbe, nànya mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖoŋlɔla ƒe dɔwɔnuwo:

1. Xlẽ mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖoŋlɔla ƒe axa le wiki la dzi le [zechub.wiki/developers](https://zechub.wiki/developers), esiae nye afi si wodoa aɖaŋu be nàdzoe egɔme
2. Kpɔ zingolib kple zingo-cli la, si ƒe yɔyɔwo doa nu geɖe si dɔwɔwɔ aɖe hia le trackwo katã me
3. Kpɔ librustzcash kple ZODL reference gakotoku la na ɖoɖoƒe ƒe akpa siwo le emetɔ
4. Na FROST ƒe dɔwɔwɔ, zã Zcash Foundation ƒe frostd kple frost-core tso crates.io, eye nàzã AI be wòakpe ɖe wò ŋu le gɔmeseseɖoɖo me, gake FROST ƒe zazã le dedie me hia dɔ kple ɣeyiɣi vavã

<br/>

## Se nya si mainnet gɔme le

Track geɖewo bia be wò app nàdo ƒome kple Zcash mainnet. Le wɔwɔ me la, esia gɔme be wò dɔwɔwɔ, alo ame aɖe si zãm, si me AI agent hã le, ɖoa alo xɔa ga vavã le mainnet dzi, alo ewɔa eye wòle dɔwɔnuwo si wɔ esia dzɔdzɔe ŋu ɖe edzi. Ne wò app wɔa gaɖoɖowo [transactions], ele be nàɖe wo fia le mainnet dzi le wò submission me.

Tu le testnet dzi le wò dɔwɔwɔ ƒe ɣeyiɣi me. Mainnet ƒe dɔwɔwɔ xɔa ZEC vavã eye anɔ asɔ gbɔ ɣesiaɣi, eyata testnet nye afi si wodoa aɖaŋu be nàtrɔtrɔ nuwo le. Trɔ yi mainnet dzi na ɖaseɖigbalɛ [proof] mamlɛtɔ. Ðo nu sia ɖeka ŋu le wò flow ƒe ɖoɖo me: Ne ga va ɖe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɣaɣla dzi la, ele be wò gakotoku nàkaka eye wòadi wo hafi woate ŋu azã wo, eye kakawɔwɔ sia xɔa ɣeyiɣi sue aɖe. Ðe mɔ na lala kpui sia le wò app me, menye be nàbu be ga siwo va ɖo la ŋutɔe wɔ dɔ be woazã enumake o.

<br/>

## Nàwɔe bɔbɔe

Susu bɔbɔe si wowɔ nyuie ɖua susu sesẽ aɖe dzi zi geɖe. Cɔnuɖolawo kpɔ be susu gɔmeɖaɖa aɖe dze edzi wu dɔwɔwɔ si ƒe mɔ̃ ƒe akpa sesẽ wu le nudzɔdzɔ ɖeka ma ke me, elabena eɖe kuxi vavã aɖe eye eƒe gɔmesese bɔbɔe. Wɔ dɔ sue wu ale si nèbu be àte ŋu awu enu. Nudzodzrogbalẽ, dɔwɔwɔ ƒe fli gã akeke, kple didi mewɔwɔ o nye vodadawo siwo na nuwɔlawo ƒe dzidzɔwo bu. Wɔ wò dɔwɔwɔ be eƒe gɔmesese kple eƒe dɔwɔwɔ nanye bɔbɔe, tso susu vevi la dzi va se ɖe sedede gbãtɔ.

<br/>

## Dzi ɣeyiɣi 30 gbãtɔ la

Nudzodzrolawo wɔa susu sesẽ enumake, eyata ɖeɖefia, tanya, kple nɔnɔmetatawo xɔa teƒe gã, kple ale si wò ŋuɖoɖo la yɛ yeye. Agbalẽŋlɔɖoɖo kple demo si ƒe gɔme se nyuie menye nu si wòbu le emegbe o. Wò susu ƒe ɖeɖefia le vevie zi aɖe wu susu la ŋutɔ, elabena ne ame aɖeke mese nu si nètu o la, mate ŋu adze edzi o. Cɔnuɖoɖo doa nɔnɔmetata ƒe sesẽ, user experience, yeyeɖeɖe, kple dɔwɔwɔ ƒe vevienyenye ɖe nu ɖeka me, eye agbalẽŋlɔɖoɖo nyui doa wo katã ɖe edzi.

<br/>

## Kpɔ track siwo sesẽ eye wo me ame geɖe mele o

Ne wòdi asɔhẽ si me ame geɖe mele o la, track siwo sesẽ la wɔa be entrywo sɔ gbɔ elabena ame sue ko doa wo ŋu. Accounting track la nye mɔ nyui na amedzrowo siwo di be yewoage ɖa le gaɖoɖo ɖoɖonu dzi ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ŋu. FROST le ŋusẽ gã eye wometsɔ zã geɖe o, eye ewɔa gɔmeɖoƒe sesẽ na dɔwɔwɔ aɖe. Hadome la mefia nu si nàtu o, eyata tututu ɖe dɔwɔnu ŋusẽtɔ si ecosystem la le xoxo ŋu, le be nàdzo le zero dzi o, nye afɔɖeɖe nyuitɔ.

<br/>

## Le hackathon la megbe

Dzidzedze menye mɔ la ƒe nuwuwu o. Dzidzedze ɖoa wò portfolio kple wò ŋkɔ nyuie ɖe edzi, eʋua mɔwo ʋu le hadome, eye ate ŋu akplɔ wò yi ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu me to proposals dzi.

1. Kplɔ dɔwɔwɔ sesẽ aɖe yi edzi abe proposal na ZecHub DAO alo Zcash Community Grants ene, kple mɔmapu, dzidzimeƒewo, kple ga ƒe xɔxɔ ƒe gɔmesese
2. Nànɔ dɔ wɔm le hadome le forum la, Discord, kple X dzi
3. Va Arborist R and D ƒe meetingwo, nàtsɔ susuwo ade, eye nàbia numekpɔkpɔ
4. Yi edzi nàtu nu togbɔ be mèdze edzi o, eye nàkpɔ hackathon si kplɔe ɖo
