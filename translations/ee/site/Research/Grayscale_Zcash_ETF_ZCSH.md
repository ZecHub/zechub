# Grayscale Zcash ETF (ZCSH) ƒe ƒuƒoƒoa

**Zcash medze ETF gɔme o. Grayscale wɔe**, to ga aɖe si le esi la tɔtrɔ me
run since 2017. Zcash nye protocol — dɔwɔƒe, gaxɔ alo board aɖeke mele esi o
si ate ŋu ana gaxɔ aɖe. Grayscale nye nunɔamesiwo dzikpɔla si le eɖokui si si ƒlea nu
ZEC le asi si le ʋuʋu ɖi me abe ame bubu ɖesiaɖe ene.

Vovototo ma nye nu ɖeka kolia si woxlẽ vodadatɔe wu le ZCSH, eye eyae nye afisi
axa sia dze egɔme. Le 25 August 2026 dzi la, gaxɔa dze asitsatsa gɔme le NYSE Arca abe
* Zcash ETF*, gbãtɔ si nye United States spot Zcash exchange-traded product, eye wònye
fifia enye ZEC ƒe ame ɖekaɖeka siwo lolo wu siwo li la dometɔ ɖeka.

## Nusiwo le gomenɔla si ŋutɔŋutɔ

Akpa aɖe nye nuɖeɖe ɖe ZEC. Menye ZEC.

Gomenɔla mate ŋu aɖo asitsatsa si ŋu wokpɔ ta na o, mate ŋu atsɔ nuŋlɔɖi aɖe akpe ɖe eŋu o, mate ŋui o
lé adrɛs ɖeka me ɖe asi, eye mate ŋu akpɔ eɖokui dzi o. Nuwo dometɔ aɖeke meli si
vovototo Zcash tso nunɔamesi bubuwo gbɔ li to wrapper — la
wrapper ɖea asixɔxɔ fiana eye menye nu bubu aɖeke o. Le gadelawo si ƒe taɖodzinue nye be yeaɖe ye ɖokui afia
le asitsaƒe alo dzudzɔxɔxɔledɔme ƒe gakɔnta me la, nya la tututue nye ema. Elabena
amesiame si ƒe ɖetsɔleme le Zcash me nye adzamenyawo, ETF menye nusi woatsɔ aɖo eteƒe o
gakotoku, eye le nyateƒe me la, wo ame evea menye esiwo woate ŋu azã ɖe wo nɔewo teƒe o.

## Wowɔa ZEC la le gaglãgbe

Nukokoedonamenya bubu aɖe gali le xɔa me, eye eyae nye nusi ɖa wu ɖesiaɖe le eŋu
axa sia dzi. Coinbase Custody lé Trust ƒe ZEC ɖe **transparent** me
adrɛswo, ke menye esiwo ŋu wokpɔ ta na o. Gaxɔa ƒe ga si le esi la dzena le kɔsɔkɔsɔ dzi,
woate ŋu ake ɖe eŋu vaseɖe edzikpɔla gbɔ, eye amesiame si tsɔ ɖe le eme nate ŋu akpɔe le ɣeyiɣi ŋutɔŋutɔ me
kpᴐ. Naneke mele gaxɔ sia me zãa adrɛs si wokpɔ ta na o.

Ema menye ɖekematsɔleme o. Wotu gaxɔ ƒe xɔtuɖoɖo siwo ŋu wowɔ ɖoɖo ɖo ɖe agbalẽdzikpɔkpɔ ŋu
kple ɖaseɖiɖi, eye ga si woda ɖe akpoxɔnu me nye nusi tututu agbalẽdzikpɔla mate ŋu awɔ o
woɖo kpe edzi le wo ɖokui si. Coinbase's kpekpeɖeŋu ɖea asymmetry ma ke fiana: eya
axɔ ZEC si woɖo ɖa tso adrɛs si wokpɔ ta na, gake meɖo ZEC ɖe
ɖeka.

Eyata dɔwɔƒe gãtɔ kekeake si si ameŋunyatakakawo ƒe nunɔamesi aɖe le la lénɛ ɖe ɖeka me
agbalẽvi si me ame ŋutɔ ƒe nya aɖeke mele o. Nyatakaka nana gbɔe nya sia tso tsɔ wu be woatsoe
the filings — S-3 la ƒo nu tso Trust ƒe ame ŋutɔ ƒe safuiwo dzi kpɔkpɔ ŋu gake eƒo nu tso eŋu
menye dzesi adrɛs ƒomeviwo o — eye wogblɔe le afisia kple nuxlɔ̃ame ma.

## Aleke gbegbee ZEC kpɔ gome le emee

Le ŋkɔ ŋɔŋlɔ me le 25 August 2026 dzi la, gaxɔa xɔ abe **387,000 ZEC**, kple
anɔ **$313 miliɔn** le dɔdzikpɔkpɔ te.

Ne èdi be yeakpɔe didi wu la: tso September 30, 2025 dzi la, agbalẽa gblɔ be Nudzraɖoƒea le esi
anɔ abe **2.4% le ZEC**. Xexlẽdzesi eveawo siaa drift — gbãtɔ kple
ZEC ƒe asi eye kple nuwɔwɔwo kple xɔnamewo, evelia abe fe si woxena ene
tso holdings — eyata xɔ xexlẽme si li fifia tso nyatakaka yeyetɔ kekeake si wotsɔ de agbalẽ me alo
gaxɔa ŋutɔ ƒe gbesiagbe nyatakakawo ɖeɖe ɖe go tsɔ wu be wòatso axa sia dzi.
