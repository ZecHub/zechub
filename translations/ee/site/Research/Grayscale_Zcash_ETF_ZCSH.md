# Grayscale Zcash ETF (ZCSH)

**Zcash mede ETF o. Grayscalee mede**, to fund si wònɔ dzim tso 2017 me trɔe. Zcash nye protocol — company, gadzraɖoƒe [treasury] alo board aɖeke mele eŋu si ate ŋu ato fund aɖe o. Grayscale nye asset manager si le eƒe ŋutɔ dzi, eye wòƒlea ZEC le asime abe ame bubuwo katã ene.

Vovototo siae nye nu si ame geɖe gɔmeɖe medzɔ le ZCSH ŋu wu, eye afimae axa sia dze egɔme. Le 25 August 2026, fund la de asi wɔwɔ me le NYSE Arca dzi abe *The Zcash ETF* ene, si nye US spot Zcash exchange-traded product gbãtɔ, eye fifia enye ZEC ƒe dzɔlawo ƒe ame ɖeka siwo xɔe sɔgbɔ le xexeame.

## Fund ma ke, ƒomevi etɔ̃

| Ɣe ka me | Nu kae trɔ |
| --- | --- |
| October 2017 | Edoe abe private placement ene, accredited investors ko ƒe |
| October 2021 | Woɖe shares la ƒe asi le OTCQX dzi kple ticker ZCSH, eye wònɔ asime le NAV te |
| 25 August 2026 | Wòtrɔ zu ETP si woate ŋu axɔ atrɔ le NYSE Arca dzi, eye wòɖe "Trust" ɖa le ŋkɔa me |

2026 ƒe afɔɖeɖe la menye nu si mekpɔna ko o. Trust si woɖe eƒe asi le OTC me la, redemption mechanism mele eŋu o, eyata eƒe shares ate ŋu adzo ayi le premium alo discount me tso assets siwo le eyome ƒe asixɔxɔ gbɔ, zi aɖewo nenye vevie — esiae va dzɔ le 2021 kple 2026 dome. Exchange-traded product si me creation kple redemption le dɔ wɔm me la, arbitrage mɔ le eyome si naa share ƒe asixɔxɔ lea ɖe net asset value ŋu. Trɔtrɔ tso ɖeka me yi bubu me trɔa nu si nu la wɔ abe security ene.

## Ame si wɔ nu sia nu

| Sponsor | Grayscale Investments Sponsors, LLC |
| Trustee | CSC Delaware Trust Company |
| Administrator kple transfer agent | The Bank of New York Mellon |
| Prime broker | Coinbase, Inc. — ena ZEC na trust la |
| Authorized participants | Jane Street, Virtu — wowɔa shares eye woɖea woaƒe ga na ame |
| Marketing agent | Foreside Fund Services |
| **Custodian** | **Coinbase Custody Trust Company, LLC** — afi ɖeka si ZEC vavã le |

Ƒome siawo dome ɖeka hã menye fund la o. Ɖoɖoɖo [arrangement] si le wo domee nye fund la, eye esiae diagram si le dzi la ɖe fia. Dɔ siawo dome geɖe bu alo woɖe wo gɔme vɔ̃ le nyadzɔdzɔwo me le listing ƒe ɣeyiɣia me; xexlẽsẽ si le afisia ɖoa eɖokui ɖe Grayscale ƒe Form S-3 registration statement si le EDGAR dzi ŋu.

## Ale si wowɔa shares eye woɖea woaƒe ga na ame

Authorized Participants ko — broker-dealers siwo ŋkɔwo le register me, eye wode agreement kple Sponsor la ƒe asi — ate ŋu awɔ alo aɖe shares ƒe ga na ame, eye woate ŋu awɔe le 10,000 ƒe ƒuƒoƒewo me ko, siwo woyɔna be Baskets. Investor si nye ame gbãtɔa mate ŋu awɔ dɔ sia o; ele shares siwo li xoxo la ƒle kple woƒe dzidzi le gatrɔƒe [exchange] la dzi abe listed security bubu ene.

Mechanism la nye **cash** fifia, menye in-kind o. Authorized Participant aɖe ɖoa cash ɖe cash account me, alo wòxɔa cash tsoe me, eye third-party liquidity provider aɖee nye ame si va xɔa alo dze ZEC ɖa vavã. Registration statement la gblɔ in-kind process aɖe hã, si me Authorized Participant aɖe aɖo ZEC tẽ ɖe Trust la gbɔ alo axɔ ZEC tsoe gbɔ — gake egblɔe kpuie be "In-Kind Regulatory Approval has not been obtained," eye womeate ŋu axɔe gbeɖe o.

Eyata le cash process la te, ZEC trɔa asi le liquidity provider kple asime dome, ke menye investor kple Trust la dome o. Ame aɖeke si le retail mɔa dzi mekpea Zcash gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi [address] ŋu o.

## Woxɔa adzɔ [fee] le ZEC me

Sponsor ƒe adzɔ nye **2.5% le ƒe ɖeka me**, abe ale si wònɔ le listing ŋkeke la ene. Woxɔa eƒe akontabu gbesiagbe abe net asset value ƒe percentage ene, eye abe filing la gblɔe ene, "payable in ZEC to the Sponsor daily in arrears."

Eƒe nusi wodze le eme nye ƒeɖoɖo nu, menye nu si dzɔ ko o. Adzɔ la tsoa fund la ƒe ZEC me, eyata ZEC ƒe amount si le share ɖeka ƒe megbea ɖea ɖe edzi vie gbesiagbe. Share menye ZEC ƒe quantity si mele woƒom ɣe sia ɣe o; enye claim si le nu si le viem vie la ŋu. Ame si le ZCSH exposure le ɣeyiɣi didi me sɔsɔ kple ZEC ɖeasi fɔm la ƒe sɔsɔ wɔm la, ele be wòade esia hã ɖe sɔsɔa me.

Ne míatsɔe ade eme la, 2.5% sɔ kple adzɔ si le spot bitcoin ETF tloa me ƒe teƒe ewɔ wui 10. Grayscale gblɔ be sponsor ƒe adzɔ ƒe ga si ava do ŋgɔa woatrɔe ade Zcash ecosystem ƒe ŋgɔyiyi me.

## Nu si shareholder aɖe le asi vavã

Share ɖeka nye ZEC ƒe asixɔxɔ ƒe exposure. Menye ZEC o.

Shareholder aɖe mate ŋu aɖo ɣaɣla [shielded] gaɖoɖo [transaction] aɖe o, mate ŋu atsɔ nya kpui [memo] aɖe akpe ɖe eŋu o, mate ŋu akpɔ unified address o, eye mate ŋu awɔ self-custody o. Nu siwo katã naa Zcash vovototo tso assets bubuwo gbɔ la mele wrapper la me o — wrapper la tsɔa asixɔxɔ ko va, eye menaa nu bubu aɖeke o. Na investor si ƒe taɖodzinu nye be wòakpɔ exposure le brokerage alo retirement account me la, esiae nye ta vevi. Na ame si ƒe dzidzɔ le Zcash ŋu nye eƒe ɣaɣla [privacy] la, ETF menye gakotoku [wallet] ƒe teƒeɖem o, eye wo amevevle o.

## Wole ZEC ɖoɖonu dzi le ame katã ƒe ŋkume

Nu si wòle ɖe eŋu hã le ƒeɖoɖoa me, eye esiae nye nu vevie wu le axa sia dzi. Coinbase Custody le Trust la ƒe ZEC ɖeasi le **nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi** gaɖoƒewo me, menye ɣaɣla tɔwo me o. Fund la ƒe holdings le ɖoɖonu [chain] dzi, ame ate ŋu akpɔ wo, ayi woƒe mɔ dzi va se ɖe custodian la gbɔ, eye ame si di be wòakpɔ wo la ate ŋu akpɔ wo le ɣeyiɣi sia me. Nu aɖeke le fund sia me metsɔ ɣaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi o.

Esia menye be wogblẽ nu aɖe o. Regulated fund ƒe infrastructure woɖoe ɖe audit kple attestation ŋu, eye ɣaɣla balance enye nu si auditor mate ŋu akpɔ eye wòaɖe eƒe nyateƒe fia le eŋutɔ ŋu o. Coinbase ƒe support ŋutɔ ɖea asymmetry ma ke fia: wòaxɔ ZEC si wodo tso ɣaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi me, gake wòmeɖoa ZEC ɖe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ma tɔ me o.

Eyata institutional holder si le ɣaɣla asset aɖe ƒe dzɔlawo ƒe amegã wu la le esi le ƒome si ɣaɣla aɖeke mele me la me. Wotsɔ nyadzɔdzɔwo, ke menye filings o, de ta na gɔmesese sia — S-3 la gblɔ Trust la ƒe private keys ƒe custody, gake wòmeɖe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ƒe ƒomewo ƒe nɔnɔ gɔme o — eye wogblɔe afisia kple dzesi sia.

## ZEC ƒe amount si le eme

Le listing ŋkeke 25 August 2026 la, fund la le ZEC **387,000** kple kloe, eye **$313 million** kple kloe le eƒe dzikpɔkpɔ te.

Na ɣeyiɣi didi ƒe nukpɔkpɔ: le 30 September 2025 la, filing la gblɔ be Trust la le circulating ZEC ƒe **2.4%** kple kloe. Number evea katã ƒe nɔnɔ trɔa — gbãtɔa trɔa kple ZEC ƒe asixɔxɔ kple creations kple redemptions, eye evelia trɔa esi woxɔa adzɔ tso holdings la me — eyata xɔ figure si le fifia tso filing yeyetɔ alo fund la ŋutɔ ƒe daily disclosure me, ke menye tso axa sia dzi o.
