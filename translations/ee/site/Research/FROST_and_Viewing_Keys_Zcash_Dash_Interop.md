# FROST & Nukpɔkpɔ ƒe Safuiwo: Zcash/Dash Interop Numekuku Kpuie

*Wodzrae ɖo na ZecHub · Wogbugbɔ trɔ asi le eŋu le 27 September 2026 · Wokpɔ nyawo katã tso fli me*

## Dɔdzikpɔlawo ƒe nyatakaka kpui aɖe

ZecHub fɔ biabia sia ɖe te esi wòtsɔ shielded DASH kpe ɖe eŋu abe wiki nunana ƒe tiatia ene: ɖe Zcash-style viewing keys, alo FROST threshold signatures, ateŋu aɖe egɔme ɖe Dash?

Numekukua gbugbɔ ɖoe ɖe ɖoɖo nu. Safuiwo kpɔkpɔ menye nyabiase si woʋu o — Dash shipped the [Zcash Orchard ta si wokpɔ ta na](https://www.dash.org/news/shielded-transactions-are-live-on-the-dash-evolution-mainnet/) ɖe eƒe Evolution kɔsɔkɔsɔa me, eye Orchard's safuiwo ƒe ɖoɖowo lɔ safuiwo kpɔkpɔ to xɔtutu me ɖe eme. Dash ŋutɔ tɔ [mɔzɔzɔ ƒe nɔnɔmetata](https://www.dash.org/roadmap/) ɖoa wo ɖe ɖoƒe na agbalẽdzikpɔlawo ƒe nyatakakawo ɖeɖefia kple Mɔzɔzɔ ƒe Sewo dzi wɔwɔ. Wozãa afã ma, ke menye nususugblɔe o.

**FROST nye afisi dometsotso vavãtɔ bɔbɔ nɔ.** Dash ƒua du BLS ƒe dzidzenu ƒe asidede agbalẽ te xoxo to eme [Masternode Quorums siwo nɔa agbe didi](https://docs.dash.org/projects/core/en/stable/docs/guide/dash-features-masternode-quorums.html), gake esiawo subɔa network-level nukpɔsusu ɖeka — ChainLocks kple InstantSend. [ZIP 312 ƒe xexlẽdzesi](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0312) taɖodzinu na nusi to vovo: mɔɖeɖe ɖe gazazã ƒe dzidzenu ŋu le akɔnta ɖeka si wokpɔ ta na si le safuitɔ ɖekaɖekawo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo sue aɖe si dzi. Wo ame evea menye esiwo woate ŋu atrɔ ɖe wo nɔewo ŋu o. Eye esi wònye be ZIP 312 gakpɔtɔ nye **Draft** ta la, nufiame ƒe dɔwɔwɔ aɖeke mele kɔsɔkɔsɔ yi melidzeƒe eveawo dometɔ aɖeke dzi o, eyata esia anye dɔ yeye si akpa ka kee tue o.

## Ɣeyiɣikɔntabubu: nusitae tsɔtsɔ sɔ kple wo nɔewo sia mebɔ fifia o

Nudzɔdzɔ eve siwo dzɔ le tadeaguƒe si wotsɔ akpoxɔnu wɔe la dzɔ le kwasiɖa ʋɛ aɖewo ko me le wo nɔewo gbɔ le ƒe 2026 ƒe domedome.

**Zcash ʋu le Orchard.** Numekula Taylor Hornby ɖe nutome sue aɖe ƒe afɔku aɖe ɖe go le Orchard si woate ŋu azã atsɔ adzi nuzazãwo ɖe dzi le mɔ si womate ŋu ade dzesii o nu. Zcash ɖo eŋu to **Ironwood (NU6.3)** ƒe dɔwɔwɔ le **28 July 2026** dzi, eye wòto ta yeye si ŋu wokpɔ ta na si me turnstile migration mechanism le vɛ.

**Dash ʋu yi Orchard.** Dash ɖe gbeƒã ɖoɖoa le [19 Dzove 2026. Eƒe dukɔ nye …](https://www.dash.org/blog/dash-is-adding-shielded-transactions-to-evolution/) — *"Míele mɔ kpɔm be míate ŋu adze shielded transfers gɔme kpuie, le dzɔdzɔme nu la, dedienɔnɔ ƒe agbalẽdzikpɔkpɔ kple kɔdawo me toto bubuwo lalam."* Dash's [mɔzɔzɔ ƒe nɔnɔmetata](https://www.dash.org/roadmap/) ŋlɔ Shielded Balances be **wowu enu le July 2026** kple Dash Platform **v4.0**, eye Dash ta [*"Adzɔnu siwo wokpɔ ta na la le agbe le Dash Evolution mainnet dzi"*](https://www.dash.org/news/shielded-transactions-are-live-on-the-dash-evolution-mainnet/) le **Amegbetɔ 4, 2026** dzi.

> **Nyatakaka aɖe tso nudɔdɔ ŋu.** Nyatakaka aɖewo tsɔ Dash ƒe mainnet ƒe dɔwɔwɔ da ɖe 17 July 2026 dzi, si atsɔe aɖo Ironwood. Edze abe ŋkeke ma tso gbeƒãɖeɖea ŋuti nyatakaka nana nyadzɔdzɔŋlɔlawo gbɔ tsɔ wu be wòatso gbeƒãɖeɖea ƒe dɔwɔwɔ gbɔ ene. Le Dash ŋutɔ ƒe dzɔtsoƒewo dzi la, nɔnɔmea wu enu le July me eye woɖe gbeƒãe le agbe le August 4 lia dzi — le Ironwood. Kɔsɔkɔsɔ eveawo tso mɔwo le kwasiɖa ʋɛ aɖewo ko me; ɖoɖo si tututu woawɔ la nɔ te ɖe nu vevi si woxlẽ dzi, eye agbalẽ kpui sia megblɔ be ɖeka o.

Vevietɔ la, menye Dash nyi nudzodzoea ƒe dome o. Woƒe gbeƒãɖeɖea me kɔ: *"míewɔ Orchard ƒe tɔtrɔa ŋudɔ ga ƒe asixɔxɔ ƒe vodada aɖeke si wonya manɔmee. Nu gbegblẽ aɖe nɔ tɔtrɔ si do ŋgɔ la me si woate ŋu azã atsɔ ado Zcash's nuzazãwo ɖe dzi le mɔ si womate ŋu ade dzesii o nu."*

Eyata Dash fifia ƒua du patched fork of the cryptography Zcash ŋutɔ ʋu tso le base layer, esime Zcash's dzidzime si gbɔna ƒe ta (Ironwood) kple dzidzime si gbɔna ƒe gazazã-mɔɖeɖe ɖoɖo (FROST) le wo nɔewo yome yeye le agbe eye wogakpɔtɔ le Draft.

## Safuiwo kpɔkpɔ: wozã, ke menye numekuku ƒe dometsotso o

Dash ƒe ta si ŋu wokpɔ akpoxɔnu le lae nye [Orchard](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0224), si wotu ɖe Halo 2 zk-SNARKs dzi si mehiã be woawɔ ɖoɖo si dzi woka ɖo o. Orchard's safui ƒe ɖoɖo la tsɔa Full Viewing Keys kple Incoming Viewing Keys dea eme ɣesiaɣi abe eƒe wɔwɔme ƒe akpa aɖe ene tsɔ wu be wòanye kpeɖeŋutɔ — eyata ŋutetea va ɖo kple code la, ke menye abe melidzeƒe si wòle be kɔsɔkɔsɔ ɖesiaɖe nawɔ ɖoɖo ɖe eŋu ene o.

Dash ƒe mɔfiamegbalẽa gblɔ tameɖoɖoa tẽ:

> *"To vovo na ameŋunyatakakawo takpɔkpɔ ƒe ɖoɖo siwo wòle be woawɔ siwo dze ŋgɔ asitɔtrɔ le ŋkɔwo ƒe xexlẽdzesiwo ɖeɖeɖa kple sewo ƒe masɔmasɔwo ŋu la, Shielded Balances doa alɔ nyatakakawo ɖeɖe ɖe go tiatia aɖe to nukpɔkpɔ ƒe safuiwo dzi — si ɖea mɔ na ezãlawo kple dɔwɔƒewo be woama asitsatsa ŋuti nyatakakawo kple agbalẽdzikpɔlawo alo awɔ ɖe Mɔzɔzɔ Ŋuti Se ƒe nudidiwo dzi ne ehiã, evɔ womagblẽ ameŋunyatakakawo me na gbesiagbe zazã o."*

Nu eve siwo ŋu wòle be woaŋlɔ ɖi:

**Dash le nukpɔkpɔ ƒe safuiwo ɖom ɖe teƒe si ƒo xlã nuwɔwɔ ƒe zazã ƒe nɔnɔme si me kɔ wu alesi Zcash's dɔwɔnuwo ɖo.** Zcash's fexexe-ɖeɖefia dɔwɔnuwo gakpɔtɔ nye dodokpɔ akpa gãtɔ eye wotiae le gakotokuwo katã me. Dash le nukpɔkpɔ safuiwo ɖom ɖa abe sedziwɔwɔ ƒe nɔnɔme kple ŋkɔ zazã ƒe nɔnɔmewo ene, le kɔsɔkɔsɔ si hã naa abe sɛkɛnd ɖeka ƒe nyametsotso ƒe nyametsotso kple abe blaeve vɔ eve ƒe gakotoku ƒe ɖekawɔwɔ le eya ŋutɔ ƒe gbeƒãɖeɖe ɖesiaɖe nu.

**Nu si woʋu la nye compatibility drift, menye ŋutete o.** Ne Dash ƒe viewing-key implementation gakpɔtɔ nye wire-compatible kple Zcash's Orchard viewing-key format esime kɔsɔkɔsɔ eveawo siaa le tɔtrɔm le wo ɖokui si la, edze be woakplɔe ɖo. Enye ŋkuléleɖenuŋu ƒe nyabiase tsɔ wu be wòanye numekukudɔ.

## Nu vevi si woɖe tso eme: Zcash kple Dash tsɔtsɔ sɔ kple wo nɔewo

Akpa sia ƒo nu tso numetola ƒe nyabiasea ŋu tẽ. Ŋuɖoɖo kpuia enye be *shielded* key trees la sɔ kloe elabena code la ma — vovototo siwo ŋu gɔmesese le la le alesi kɔsɔkɔsɔ ɖesiaɖe **ke** ati ma le eƒe gakotoku safui teƒe, kple le nu bubu si xɔa teƒe ma me.

### Zcash

Zcash zãa [ZIP 32, *Gakotoku siwo woɖo tae si woɖo ɖe ɖoɖo nu*](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0032), si ƒe ɖoƒe nye **Final**. Le esi teƒe be ZIP 32 natsɔ safui siwo ŋu wokpɔ ta na la ade BIP 32 ati ɖeka me la, enaa ta ɖesiaɖe si wokpɔ ta na la eya ŋutɔ ƒe safui vevitɔ kple eya ŋutɔ ƒe mɔ:

```
m_Orchard / purpose' / coin_type' / account'
m_Sapling / purpose' / coin_type' / account'
```

`purpose` woɖoe ɖe `32'` (0x80000020) ɖe BIP 43 ɖesiaɖe dzi, eye `coin_type` kplɔ SLIP 44 ɖo, kple testnets katã mamã index `1`.

Le Orchard akɔnta me la, ɖoƒea nye mɔ ɖeka dzi ko — dzidzenu ɖesiaɖe ateŋu akpɔ nusianu si le ete eye naneke mele etame o:

| Asafui | Ate ŋu awɔe | Derives |
|---|---|---|
| Spending key | Zã nuŋlɔɖiwo | `ask`, `nk`, `rivk` |
| Spend authorizing key (`ask`) | Ðe mɔ ɖe gazazãwo ŋu | — |
| Full Viewing Key (`ak`, `nk`, `rivk`) | Kpɔ fexexe siwo va **kple** siwo dona | IVK, OVK, si nye agbalẽ si nye IVK |
| Incoming Viewing Key | Kpɔ fe siwo woxena ɖe eta ɖeɖeko | Adrɛs vovovowo |
| Outgoing Viewing Key | Gbugbɔ fexexe si nèdo goe ŋuti nyatakakawo | — |
| Diversified address | Xᴐ | — |

Orchard na esia le bɔbɔe ne wotsɔe sɔ kple Sapling: per the [Orchard ƒe Agbalẽ](https://zcash.github.io/orchard/design/keys.html), si nye nullifier ƒe ame ŋutɔ ƒe safui `nsk` woɖee ɖa, `nk` va zu agblemenuku tsɔ wu be wòanye teƒe si woƒo xlãe, eye `ovk` woɖee tso nukpɔkpɔ ƒe safui bliboa me fifia tsɔ wu be woalée ɖe vovo.

Esia tame bɔbɔ nɔ anyi [ZIP 316, *Adrɛs Siwo Wowɔ Ðeka Kple Nukpɔkpɔ Safui Siwo Wowɔ Ðeka*](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0316) — Revision 0 Active, Revision 1 Withdrawn, Revision 2 Draft — si ƒoa safui ɖesiaɖe nu ƒu ɖe **Unified Full Viewing Key** ("ƒoa Full Viewing Key… Nuwo") kple **Unified Incoming Viewing Key**. Vovototo si ŋu wòle be gakotokuwɔla nade bubui: UFVK ɖea dɔwɔna si va kple esi dona siaa fiana, UIVK si gena ɖe eme koe.

### Fli

Dash ƒoa ke ɖe to ɖe nusianu me le BIP 32 ati si wozãna ɖaa me, kple SLIP 44 gaku ƒomevi `5'`, eye wòtsɔ eya ŋutɔ ƒe derivation kekeɖenudɔ eve kpee.

[DIP-0009, *Nɔnɔmewo ƒe Dzɔtsoƒe Mɔwo*](https://docs.dash.org/projects/core/en/stable/docs/dips/dip-0009.html) dea **nɔnɔme** ƒe ɖoɖo si ma safui ƒe teƒea to gaku-koŋ ƒe dɔwɔwɔ me:

```
m / purpose' / coin_type' / feature' / *
```

kple `purpose` woɖoe ɖe `9'` (0x80000009) kple `coin_type` at `5'` (0x80000005) ƒe xexlẽme. DIP ƒe ʋiʋli si wogblɔ enye be woaɖe wo ɖokui ɖe aga — *"ate ŋu anyo be woalé ga tsakatsaka me ɖe asi le mɔ si woɖe ɖe aga tso ga siwo metsaka o gbɔ."*

[DIP-0014, *Safui ƒe Dzɔdzɔenyenye si keke ɖe enu to 256-bit Unsigned Integers zazã me*](https://github.com/dashpay/dips/blob/master/dip-0014.md) yi ŋgɔ wu ema, ekɔ BIP 32 ƒe 31-bit index ƒe seɖoƒe dzi ale be mɔ ƒe akpawo nate ŋu atsɔ 256-bit ƒe asixɔxɔ blibowo. Ema ɖe mɔ ɖe mɔ siwo wokpɔ tso amenyenye me abe:

```
m(userA)/9'/5'/15'/0'/(userA's unique id)/(userB's unique id)
```

afisi akpa eve mamlɛawo nye zãla ƒe dzeside hashes. Zcash mekpɔ analogue o: ZIP 32 mekpɔ susu aɖeke be yeakpɔ mɔ vevi aɖe tso akpa bubu ƒe dzedzeme me o.

### Afisi wo ame evea to vovo le ŋutɔŋutɔ

**Ati sue si wokpɔ ta na la sɔ.** Dash ƒe safui siwo wokpɔ ta na la nye Orchard safuiwo, elabena Dash ƒe ta si wokpɔ ta na la nye Orchard. Gakotokuwɔla aɖe si le ʋuʋum le wo ame evea dome le dɔ wɔm kple gazazã-safui-ɖe-nukpɔkpɔ-safui ƒe ɖoɖo ɖeka ma ke.

**Ke la to vovo.** Zcash ɖea ta ɖesiaɖe si wokpɔ ta na la ɖe vovo le eya ŋutɔ ƒe safui vevitɔ te kple taɖodzinu `32'`. Dash tsɔa akpa si wokpɔ ta na la hea ati ɖeka si wɔ ɖeka la ŋu le tameɖoɖo te `9'`, kpe ɖe akpa bubu ɖesiaɖe ŋu. Zcash's mama nye to cryptographic pool dzi; Dash tɔ nye to adzɔnuwo ƒe nɔnɔme nu.

**Dash ƒe safui teƒea me nane si Zcash's mele o: BLS domenyiŋusẽfianu si le vovo.** Masternode dɔwɔla ƒe safuiwo, akɔdada ƒe safuiwo kple quorum safui siwo LLMQwo zãna nye BLS safuiwo, menye Schnorr-ƒome ƒe safuiwo o, eye wonɔa BIP 32 ati si ŋu míeƒo nu tsoe le etame la godo keŋkeŋ. Afi sia tututue Dash ƒe threshold signing si li fifia le — eye nusita tututu meŋlɔa ha kple Orchard gazazã ƒe mɔɖeɖe o, abe alesi akpa si kplɔe ɖo gblɔe ene.

**Dzesidede-kadodo ƒe dzɔtsoƒe nye Dash-ko.** DIP-0014 ƒe 256-bit mɔwo li be woakpɔ safuiwo tso ƒomedodo siwo le dzesideŋkɔwo dome. Ema nye Dash Platform ƒe susu si me Zcash sɔsɔe aɖeke mele o, eye enye nya si me kɔ wu le esi woɖoe koŋ ƒe ɖoɖo eveawo to vovo tsɔ wu be woama le vo me.

*Kpɔ Nɔnɔmetata 1 hena ke ƒe ɖoɖo eve siwo le ƒoƒom ɖe Orchard ati sue si woama dzi.*

## FROST: nyabiase si woʋu vavã

Dash si tsitsi ƒe dzidzenu-asidede ɖoɖo le **BLS-si wotu ɖe LLMQs** (Long-Living Masternode Quorums), wozãna na ChainLocks, InstantSend, kple Dash Platform validator ƒe nukpɔsusu ɖeka.

[ZIP 312, *FROST na Gazazã ƒe Mɔɖeɖe ƒe Asidede Asi Geɖewo*](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0312), nɔnɔme **Draft**, wɔa nu bubu. Eɖoa dzidzenu na Schnorr-si wotu ɖe gazazã ƒe mɔɖeɖe ƒe asidede agbalẽ te siwo Sapling kple Orchard ɖe gɔme xoxo — **RedJubjub** kple **RedPallas** ɖe wo nɔewo yome — ale be, le ZIP's ɖoɖowɔwɔ me la, *"zãlawo kple ame etɔ̃lia ƒe dɔwɔƒe siwo ma gakotoku aɖe dzi kpɔkpɔ, alo amewo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo si kpɔa ga si woama dzi"* ate ŋu abia dzidzenu ƒe mɔɖeɖe abe 2-of-3 do ŋgɔ gazazã aɖe. Wodae ɖe hatsotsowo me abe **Gakotoku** ZIP: ewɔa asidede agbalẽ te siwo sɔ kple gazazã ƒe mɔɖeɖe si li xoxo tsɔ wu be wòatrɔ nukpɔsusu ɖeka. Eléa Ðoɖowɔla ƒe akpa aɖe me ɖe asi, si ZIP gbe tẽ be yemaɖe ɖa o, eye wòƒoa nu tso dzidzi-dealer key generation kple distributed key generation siaa ŋu.

Vovototo si le vevie, kple susu si ta esiawo menye nusiwo woatsɔ aɖo eteƒe o:

| | Dash BLS / LLMQ ƒe ƒuƒoƒo | Zcash FROST (ZIP 312) Ƒe 1999 ƒe ɣleti gbãtɔ me |
|---|---|---|
| Asidede agbalẽ te ƒe ɖoɖo | BLS | Schnorr — DzĩJubjub / DzĩPallas |
| Amekae dea asi ete | Masternodes ƒe ƒuƒoƒo aɖe | Safui ɖekaɖekawo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo sue aɖe |
| Nusi ŋu woɖe mɔ ɖo | Netwɔƒe ƒe nyateƒenya aɖe: block lock, transaction lock | Gazazã tso gakɔnta ɖeka si wokpɔ ta na me |
| Ɖoɖo | Nusiwo dzi woda asi ɖo | Gakotoku |
| Teƒe vevi aɖe | BLS domenyiŋusẽfianu ɖe vovo | Orchard/Sapling gazazã ƒe mɔɖeɖe ƒe safuia |
| Ɖoƒe | Woɖoe ɖe dɔ me | Draft, nufiame ƒe dɔwɔwɔ aɖeke meli o |

Dash si si BLS threshold signatures le **mefia** be FROST. Gake efia be Dash ƒe mɔ̃ɖaŋudɔwɔlawo nya nu tso threshold signing, mama vevi dzidzime kple quorum coordination le aƒeme — nuteƒekpɔkpɔ vavãtɔ si woate ŋu atsɔ ne wotiae be yewoatu esia.

*Kpɔ Nɔnɔmetata 2 lia hena nusi tututu ɖoɖo ɖesiaɖe de asi.*

### Nusi FROST le Dash ƒe Orchard fork dzi abia, le mɔzɔzɔ gbãtɔ me

1. **A FROST DKG kple asidede agbalẽ te ƒe wɔna ɖe RedPallas dzi**, Orchard's gazazã ƒe mɔɖeɖe ƒe ɖoɖo — Schnorr ƒe tɔtrɔ ɖe Pallas ƒe ʋuʋudedi dzi. Esia to vovo tso, eye womate ŋu aɖe edzi akpɔtɔ ɖe, Dash ƒe BLS DKG si li fifia na LLMQwo gbɔ o.
2. **Gakotoku kple UX ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu na akpa geɖewo ƒe asidede akɔnta ɖeka si wokpɔ ta na**, si nye kadodo ƒe ɖoɖo si to vovo tso masternode-quorum dɔwɔnuwo gbɔ eye wòhiã na Ðoɖowɔla si sɔ kplii.
3. **Nyametsotso le layer.** Anɔ eme godoo be gakotoku-dzidzenu koe, elabena ZIP 312 nye gakotoku ƒe ɖoɖo ɖe primitives siwo li dzi tsɔ wu be woaɖo kpe edzi ɖe Dash ƒe Orchard fork ŋu koŋ, menye esi wosusu tso Zcash's scoping me o.

## Kafukafunya

**Safuiwo kpɔkpɔ — nuŋlɔɖi, mègawɔ numekuku o.** Woɖo ŋutetea ɖe kɔsɔkɔsɔ eveawo siaa dzi. Wiki nuŋlɔɖi kpui aɖe si ŋlɔ be Dash ƒe akpoxɔnu ta la me nukpɔkpɔ safuiwo le, eye wòdo ka kple Dash ƒe mɔfiame, xe mɔ na ZecHub's nyaselawo be woagasusu be eganye nususugblɔ kokoko. Track wire-format ƒe ɖekawɔwɔ esime kɔsɔkɔsɔ eveawo le tɔtrɔm.

**FROST — mɔnukpɔkpɔ ŋutɔŋutɔ, si woxe mɔ na le dzigbe.** Enɔ te ɖe ZIP 312 ƒe ɖoɖowɔwɔ ɖe nufiame ƒe dɔwɔwɔ gbɔ, alo Dash ƒe tiatia be yeatu le ɣeyiɣi ɖeka me dzi. ZecHub mate ŋu awɔe kabakaba tẽ o.

**Afɔɖeɖe si kplɔe ɖo si ƒe asixɔxɔ de ŋgɔ wue nye dzeɖoɖo, ke menye kplɔ̃dzi numekuku geɖe wu o.** Ame siwo atu esia la nye amesiwo gbɔ woate ŋu aɖo.
