Le August 17 dzi la, Zcash Community Grants committee kpe be yewoade ŋugble le ɖoɖo aɖe si woyɔ be Shielded Compliance Bridge ŋu. Deborah Olaboye, amesi nye open-source ƒe dɔwɔla eye eƒe dɔa lɔ Ethereum's execution-specs repository ɖe eme la wɔ proof of concept (dzesi) aɖe si me woɖe nuŋɔŋlɔ blibowo katã gɔme ɖo, wòxlẽa blɔki siwo wotsɔ sɔ kple testnet indexer tsɔna dzenae, eye "compliance reference" nɔa edzi le Zcash memo field si do ka na wo dome tso asitelefon ŋuti nyatakaka gbɔ va Travel Rule nuŋlɔɖi bubu gbɔ. Ebia kpekpeɖeŋu be wòawu enu hafi gake kɔmitia gbe nɛ.

Ŋkɔa enye: [susuwo ŋu bubu](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-community-grants-meeting-minutes-8-17-2026/57119) enye be nuƒomɔ alo dɔwɔƒe si azã wo la koŋ ƒe asi mee wowɔa ɖekawɔwɔ ŋuti ɖoɖowo ɖo, eye seɖoƒe aɖeke meli na eɖoɖo ɖe edzi o, eye ne menye kpeɖeŋutɔ aɖe bia mɔ̃ɖaŋunu sia ta ko hafi womate ŋu axɔe dzi ase o. Kpekpe ma ke gbe aɖaŋu bubu siwo le susu mawo tɔgbe tae, esiwo dometɔ ɖekae nye "programmable selective disclosure" virtual machine kple ameŋkumemakpɔmakpɔ kpakple kakaɖedzimɔnu me nɔnɔ, si lɔ Amerika-ʋɔnudrɔ̃nya aɖewo hã ɖe eme.

Of course, these decisions are not a sign that nobody wants this. Considered alongside the rest of the year, it is the opposite. Binance made a protocol-level ask in 2024 and had a ZIP within the year. Gemini has run shielded withdrawals under regulatory supervision since 2020. The ecosystem stood up a Washington policy organization in June and an institutional-integration shop in August. What the committee said, in effect, is that the need has not yet been written down as a requirement anyone can build to.

## Alesi ɖoɖowɔɖia wɔa dɔe

A shielded Zcash address is controlled by a spending key. Derived from it are viewing keys that can read transaction data without being able to move funds. The full viewing key sees incoming and outgoing transactions for the account, the incoming viewing key sees only received funds, and the outgoing viewing key lets a sender reconstruct what they sent. Since [ZIP 316 ƒe ŋkɔwo le afi sia.](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0316), woƒoa esiawo nu ƒu ɖe kpɔdzesi ɖeka me, si nye esi yɔa akpa aɖe kple bubu siwo va yina la dometɔ ɖeka ko le gaƒoɖokui dzi. Gaƒoƒo sia ƒe teƒe enye gaƒletɔawo ƒe agbaɖeƒe gã si wotsɔna kpɔa woƒe asitelefonwo alo kɔmpiutawo ŋu. [ZIP 32 ƒe adrɛswo:](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0032). Kpe ɖeka si wotsɔna kpɔa nu le mɔ blibo dzi la lɔa ƒuƒoƒo ɖesiaɖe ƒe akpa siwo ŋu wotrɔ asi le be woazã ŋuti nyatakakawo na ame bubuwo, eye ekpena ɖe eŋu hã wòtea ŋu ɖea agbalẽawo me nyawo gɔme nyuie. Eya ta ne ètsɔ aɖe emee la, ana amewo nakpɔ nusi dzɔ va yi kple nusiwo katã nɔ edzi yim do ŋgɔ siaa adze sii bɔbɔe.

Aƒlevi si woɖona le gɔmesese sia nu la nyea nusi wowɔna tso ɣeyiɣi aɖe megbe. Eɖea nuŋlɔɖi bliboa me ne woɖo nyatakaka ɖe eŋu, eye womate ŋu atrɔe o nenye be wodzrae zi ɖeka ko alo wotrɔ ga ɖo na akɔnta bubui. Medzɔa asi kple adzɔnu aɖeke ƒe dzesiwo kpɔna loo alo nɔa anyi hena ɣeyiɣiawo katã o. Josh Swihart, amesi nye ZODL-ha gã dziɖula, dɔwɔƒe si ɖoɖowɔɖia ƒe ECC mɔ̃ɖaŋudɔwɔla gbãtɔ wɔ le ƒe ma me la ka nya ta nenema ke hegblɔ hã be "wometsɔnɛ wɔa dɔ le afisiafi" o".

zcashd, si nye nuƒolanɔƒe gbãtɔ la ɖo RPC-mɔ̃ eve ɖe wo gbɔ. `z_getpaymentdisclosure` kple `z_validatepaymentdisclosure`, si naa amesi ɖo agbalẽa ɖa la te ŋu ɖoa kpe edzi be yexe fea zi ɖeka na ame etɔ̃lia. [RPC ƒe agbalẽwo](https://zcash.github.io/rpc/z_getpaymentdisclosure.html) marks them experimental, disabled unless the operator set an experimental-features flag, and implementing a "work-in-progress Payment Disclosure ZIP." That ZIP, [303](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0303), si dzi woŋlɔ ɖo fifia be woagbugbɔe aƒu gbe, eye eƒe dzidzimevi la nye: [ZIP 311 (Afi si woɖo ame ɖo)](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0311), enye ɖoɖo si me womewɔe ɖo o. Mɔ̃awo ku ɖe Sprout pool, ƒe 2016 tɔ ŋu eye womekeke enu yi Sapling alo Orchard dzi kpɔ gbeɖe o, esia fia be woƒo nu tso zcashd ŋutɔ hã ŋu le egbeŋkekea me tsɔ kpe asi ɖe eƒe dɔwɔwɔwo ŋuti. [wòɖo eƒe agbenɔƒe ƒe nuwuwu.](https://zcash.github.io/zcash/user/end-of-life.html) le July 18, 2026 dzi, si nye ɖoɖo be woatɔ te Ironwood ƒe ŋgɔyiyi. Zebra o, Zcash Foundation's consensus node, alo Zallet, ame siwo va xɔ ga la hã meyi edzi wɔ dɔ aɖeke o; [ŋkɔwo ƒe nuŋlɔɖiwo](https://github.com/zcash/zallet/releases) Le ƒuƒoƒo siwo ŋu nya le gome la, nuxexlẽ ɖe ɖoɖo si wowɔna dzi kpɔtɔ nye nusi woɖo be woawɔ.

Edzro wo vevie, gake womele abe alesi wòdze le July me ene o. [ZIP 231 (Afi si woɖo ame ɖo)](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0231)Swihart ɖɔ memoawo be woate ŋu anye gɔmeɖoanyi na nyaŋuɖoɖo si dzi woada asi ɖo atsɔ aɖo kpe edzi be ɖe yewoxe fe la, eye wògblɔ hã be nuŋɔŋlɔ siwo le te nɔm ɖe eŋu ate ŋu akpe ɖe ame bubuwo ŋu. [ame siwo ŋu woxɔ nyatakakawo le la ƒe adrɛs si dzi woŋlɔ ɖo.](https://zips.z.cash/draft-ecc-authenticated-reply-addrs), hia kpeɖodziwo siwo lolo akpa na nuŋlɔɖi ƒe teƒe si li fifia. Nuŋlɔɖiawo nyea asitsatsa ƒe ɖoɖo yeye, eye habɔbɔ atɔ̃ siwo wɔa dɔ le wo ŋu la kpɔa egbɔ be wowɔ tɔtrɔ siawo katã hafi woate ŋu awɔ ɖeka kple ame bubuwo o. [NU7 gaxɔlawo ƒe akɔdadawo](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/nu7-coinholder-vote/56912) agreed in August that format changes are out of scope for NU7 and in scope for the upgrade after. The vote itself, which runs from August 25 to September 14, put issuance, fee policy, block spacing and Sprout deprecation to holders; disclosure was not on the ballot. The older route, ZIP 311, needs no consensus change but remains a draft with no implementation and no Orchard coverage.

Eyata nusi míetsɔna naa amewo egbeae nye nyatakaka siwo le asitelefon dzi, esiwo katã alo esiwo gbɔna ko la ƒe akpa aɖe si ŋu wotrɔ asi le emegbe eye womate ŋu atrɔ o kple nusiwo woama ɖe ame bubuwo gbɔ to eƒe didi nu me. Woana numedzodzro sia nanɔ ɖoɖowɔɖi bliboa me abe alesi wowɔa dɔdzikpɔdɔ ene.

## Nugɔmesese ƒe ɖeɖefia

Nusiwo le Zcash alo teƒe bubu ɖesiaɖe ate ŋu anɔ afisi: Nukawoe woɖena fiana (nusi me wodzɔna ɖo). Amekae wonanaa (amesi wonaa nu) Ɣekaɣie nunana la dzea dɔwɔwɔ gɔme kple alesi wògblẽ ɖe megbei (azãgbe si woɖo ɖi); Ne woateŋu axe mɔ nɛ hã (nuŋɔŋlɔmatrɔ̃), Kple amesi ɖoe anyi, nyatakaka ƒe nyati loo alo ŋusẽtɔ aɖe (ɖoɖoa wɔwɔ).

Zcash ƒe nuŋɔŋlɔwo nye nusiwo wobia tso wo si, amesiwo xɔe le woƒe asitelefon dzi la tɔna ɖe edzi, eye ame siwo zãa kɔmpiuta kple internet tsɔ kpɔa nuwo gbɔ hã nɔa te. Nuŋɔŋlɔawo me nyawo fia be ele vevie ŋutɔ be woaɖo se aɖe koŋ anyi alo aɖo ɖoɖo bubu aɖe ɖi na dɔwɔƒe aɖewo ale be woate ŋu awɔ dɔ sia ne wowɔ tɔtrɔ siawo katã hafi.

Woyɔ nu eve siawo be tiatiawɔwɔ le nyatakakawo ɖeɖe fia me. Le ɖaseɖigbalẽ siwo ŋu wowɔa asi ɖo ƒe xexeame la, tiawɔƒe si woɖea dzesi na amewo dometɔ aɖewo tsoa agbalẽ aɖe si dzi woda ŋkɔ ɖo gbɔ evɔ wometsɔa bubuwo ɖea gbeƒãe o, eye esia nye nusi woyɔna be "elective disclosure" (ɖe ame ŋutɔ tɔ). [SD-JWT](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc9901/), ISO mdoc kple BBS+ ƒe asinuŋɔŋlɔwo le ɖoɖo nu be woawɔ. Numeɖeɖe nye nɔnɔme, ɣeyiɣi enye ɖeɖefiaɣi eye amesi si wo la ɖoe ɖe alesi wòawɔ nui dzi.

Le xexe si me woxea fe na ame le dedie nu la, nyagbɔgblɔ sia fia be woaɖe adzɔnyawo ŋuti nyatakakawo ɖe go afia amesi wolɔ̃ faa axɔ dɔe. Esia ƒe akpa aɖee nye ga siwo amewo zãna tsɔ kpɔa woƒe ganyawo gbɔ eye ɣeyiɣi ma mee wotsɔa agbalẽawo naa woe.

## Nusiwo hiã ƒe akpa si biae la na wo

Swihart said the only protocol-level compliance request the engineering team has ever received came from Binance, which in January 2024 required a transparent-provenance guarantee for deposits. The team's response was [ZIP 320 - Eʋegbe me tɔ:](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0320), transparent-source-only addresses, and the ZIP text indeed records the origin "as required by Binance." A TEX address is an ephemeral transparent pass-through that guarantees the exchange receives funds from a transparent source. It gives the exchange a return path without giving it the sender's history. The one accommodation the protocol made to an exchange discloses exactly what the exchange asked for and nothing more.

Swihart gblɔ be tso esime wowɔ Sapling ƒe ŋgɔyiyi la, susu aɖeke meli si ta asitsatsaƒewo mate ŋu akpe asi ɖe nuɖoanyi siwo dzi woato akpɔ ame ta o elabena enana amewo kpɔa woƒe nyawo gbɔ kaba eye wodea dzesi amesiwo le asitsatsadɔwɔƒea xoxo ne wole dɔ wɔm kpli wo. Le nukpɔsusu sia nu la, ɣeyiɣi didi aɖe va yi hafi dɔwɔƒewo kple ɖoɖowɔwɔ ŋuti dɔa te dɔwɔwɔ nyuie wu ale si dze; menye nya vevi aɖee nye ema megali na amesiame o ke boŋ enye numedzodzro bubu ko.

Divij Pandya is working the other end of that conversation. He is executive director of Pretty Good Policy for Zcash, the Washington policy organization stood up in June as a continuation of an earlier ECC-led initiative, and his organization's project is to get viewing-key disclosure recognized as an acceptable compliance mechanism in a document a compliance officer can cite. To his knowledge, he said, no regulator has yet engaged with viewing keys as an instrument. PGPZ's scope, naturally, is recognition rather than builder guidance. A tool cannot be specified against a requirement until someone with authority has ruled on the instrument, so recognition is the upstream step. The organization's filings are signed by Paul Brigner as PGPZ's founder and as ZODL's chief policy and regulatory officer, so the policy and engineering efforts share an officer as well as a thesis.

July 21 ƒe nya si wogblɔ na House subcommittee le FinCEN dzi kpɔkpɔ ŋu la ɖɔ Zcash be "eɖea ame ɖe go, eye eɖe mɔ na amewo be woagblɔ nu siwo wowɔna to asitelefonwo zazã kple numekulawo alo adzɔxɔlawo me" eye wòƒo nu tso zero-knowledge proofs (dziɖenununya aɖeke manɔ eme o) ŋuti kpeɖodziwo ŋu. Woŋlɔ lɛta aɖe ku ɖe ale si FinCEN wɔa dɔe ŋu gblɔ be: [FINCEN-2026-0101-0050 Woɖo kpe edzi be:](https://www.regulations.gov/comment/FINCEN-2026-0101-0050), gblɔna, le Pandya ƒe nya nu be ele be mɔfianuwo kpɔkpɔ kple wo ɖeɖe ɖe go nanye nusiwo dzi woate ŋu awɔ ɖo. Nya si wobia dzikpɔla lae nye alesi woaɖo nyawo ŋui tsitotsitoe; nenye be nyatakaka siwo katã ku ɖe ga ŋuti la koe hiã na "dzedzemeŋutinya" alo ne ZIP 231 ye akpɔ nyaa gbɔ o.

Va se ɖe esime woadzudzɔ la, agba le dɔdzikpɔlawo dzi be woaɖo aɖaŋu na ame aɖe si nye agbalẽŋlɔla ƒe ŋkɔ manɔmee. [woɖɔe ɖo.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-shielded-compliance-bridge/56862) le Olaboye ƒe aɖaŋuɖoɖo me dzodzro: wozɔna ɖe se la dzi pɛpɛpɛ, eye womalɔ̃ axɔ mɔ si womeɖe dzesi o ne setɔwo alo senyalawo ƒe ɖaseɖigbalẽ meli o, eɖanye aleke ke nyaŋɔŋlɔa de to hã. Numeɖeɖe ɖesiaɖe si dea nuƒleƒe asi na asitsatsa aɖe nye nusi fia be eƒe nuwuwu koe li ko, eye ete ŋu anye amesi da ga ɖo eɖokui mee wòava yi mlɔeba. Seɖoƒe ma kee nɔa ŋkuʋuʋui ɖeka ya ŋu. Eɖea dɔwɔgbalẽvi tɔ ƒe dɔwɔwɔwo fiana, menye afisi amesiwo wɔ nua tso teƒeawo va tsoe o; esiae nye biabia si wobiaa esi wole amebiamɔ ŋuti nyatakaka ɖem ɖa.

Ne ame aɖe di be yeaɖe nu si dzɔ la me na amewo, abe afi si ga le esi wòdi be yeadzra ɖo ɖe eŋu alo ale si woadae ƒe akɔnta ene. Ame siwo wɔa esia nye amesiwo zãa Internet dzi nyatakakawo kple esiwo wowɔna tso ɣeaɖeɣi ke; womate ŋu awɔ ɖoɖo aɖeke o ne ehiã be woanɔ ŋku lém ɖe nuwo ŋu ɣesiaɣi va se ɖe esime woaɖo asi dɔa dzi eye wole to hem nɛ hafi aɖo dɔ gɔme anyi loo alo woaɖe mɔ nɛ wòaxɔ eƒe dɔwɔɖui ɖa le asitsatsaƒe bubu aɖe (off-boarding).

Gemini lɔ̃na ɖe ga siwo woɖea le eƒe adrɛs dzi la ŋu tso esime wòxɔa dɔ sia. [Ƒe 2020 ƒe September me.](https://www.gemini.com/blog/gemini-adds-support-for-shielded-zcash-transactions), esi nyadzɔdzɔgblɔla aɖe gblɔ be wole dze ɖom kple setɔwo la, ena esia va ɖo Orchard pool le November 2025 me eye emegbe Ironwood pool si woɖo ŋkeke eve megbe le July 2026 me. Exɔa ga siwo wodɔ tso adrɛs siwo ŋu wotrɔ asi le na wo dzi ɖena ɖe eŋu nyuie. Kraken ƒe dɔdzikpɔlawo xɔe se be ame aɖeke mate ŋu akpɔ mɔ atsɔ awɔ ɖoɖo sia o. [ame siwo le asi trɔm ɖe ga ŋu ƒe adrɛswo](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360001474826-supported-address-formats-for-cryptocurrency-withdrawal) supports transparent ZEC addresses only, Coinbase is likewise transparent-only, and Binance requires TEX. What no major regulated exchange yet offers is shielded custody, a shielded deposit address, or a workflow into which a customer's viewing key would go.

Wole ɖoɖo wɔm be woana ga amesiwo si gaku le la atsɔ akpɔ woƒe nuwo dzi. [le dzigbɔɖi me September sia](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/call-for-proposals-coinholder-directed-retroactive-grants-program-q3/56885) are ZecBooks, a Mac bookkeeping tool that imports a view-only key, classifies notes, and exports a scoped package of rows for an accountant without granting lasting access to the wallet; ZecLedger, a read-only cost-basis and reconciliation tool built around a unified full viewing key, tested against a mainnet wallet; and seer-sync, a chain-sync engine that takes a viewing key so a payment processor or accounting system can observe shielded activity without holding spending keys. None is an exchange workflow, and none has a regulator's attention. Each is the accountant-facing half of the same problem, built by one or two people without a partner asking, and ZecBooks' scoped export is selective disclosure operating at the interface where it is most often needed first: between a shielded user and the person keeping their books.

## Menye gaku la o, ke boŋ ameƒomeviwoe.

Zcash-dɔwɔlawo le dɔ wɔm ɖe kuxi aɖe si dzi ameƒomevi ɖesiaɖe ƒe dedienɔnɔ ŋuti ɖoɖowo to la ŋu.

In Zama's confidential-EVM design, an access control list determines which addresses may request decryption of a ciphertext, and a threshold network of key-management operators performs the decryption on request. That moves the grantee axis from a key the user hands over to a permission a contract records, and makes initiation programmable, since a contract can grant an auditor decryption rights as a condition of use. It does not make disclosure revocable in the sense a compliance officer means, because a ciphertext already decrypted stays decrypted, and it trades a user's key custody for operator honesty as the trust base. Asset-level auditor keys, of the kind in Solana's confidential transfer extension and Avalanche's encrypted ERC design, fix a grantee at the token level in that the issuer or a designated auditor can read balances and transfers for that asset.

Inco ɖe agbalẽ aɖe si me woƒo nu tso alesi woawɔ dɔe ŋu la ɖe go. [ɖoɖowɔɖi me.](https://www.inco.org/blog/programmable-view-access) for pre-defined viewing, where access is fixed in key material at transaction time, against programmable view access, where viewing rights are contract state that can be granted, scoped and withdrawn afterwards.

## Nu si tae wòsesẽna nenema gbegbe ɖo.

Nukae maa ŋkuɖodzinu kple dɔwɔna ƒe ɖoɖo dome?

Aƒlevi aɖe si le nu kpɔm fiaa ame ƒe nuŋlɔɖi. Nutsotsowo ŋuti numekuku kple ŋkuɖoɖo edzi ɣesiaɣi bia be woanya amesiwo gbɔ ga la tso, afisi nudzɔdzɔ ɖesiaɖe me ganyawo dzɔ tso eye nya sia tututue nye nyatakaka siwo ŋu wotre ɖo na amewo hafi dzraa woe ɖo. Ame aɖeke mekpɔ ɖe ɖoɖo aɖe si ana woaɖo akɔtabiase ŋu ne agbalẽdzraɖoƒe mele eme o alo amesi dzi woka ɖo o la fia haɖe o.

If memo bundles land in the upgrade after NU7 and the reply-address draft follows, "targeted transaction information" in PGPZ's filings becomes literal. That is at least two upgrades away, and the regulatory answer arrives on a regulator's timeline.

Ehiã be woade dzesi didi sia le alesi ame geɖe tsɔa ɖe le emee la me. Nusi mele anyi o enye nudidi si li na nyatakaka siwo ana woakpɔ amewo ƒe agbe ta. Zcash nye afisi wowɔa ɖoɖowo ɖo hena nyametsotso ma, to mɔ̃ɖaŋudɔwɔlawo, amesiwo ʋlia edzi ta, xɔtulawo, asitsaha kple habɔbɔ aɖe si naa kpekpeɖeŋu dɔmenuwo dzi wɔwɔ gbɔ eye wòdze abe wolɔ̃na tso teƒeɖoɖoa ŋu hele nya hem le afɔɖeɖeawo ƒe ɖoɖo nu ene. Wole dɔa wɔm hã to dɔwɔla ɖekaɖekawo dzi amesi dze akɔntabubu gɔme evɔ womele lalam hafi woaŋlɔ nusi hiã nɛ la ɖi o.
