*Nyati sia nye ahuhɔ̃e tso [Kpeɖodzi Mɔdodo](https://www.proofstreet.xyz/), si nye agbalẽ si le eɖokui si si ƒo nu tso akɔntabubu siwo kpɔa ame ŋutɔ ƒe nyatakakawo ta ŋu: ZK, FHE, MPC, TEEs, kple amenyenye si woɖe ɖe vovo. Wotae gbã be ["Nya ɣaɣlawo tsɔtsɔ sɔ kple wo nɔewo: nusi ameŋunyatakakawo ƒe xɔtuɖaŋu ene do ŋugbe".](https://www.proofstreet.xyz/confidentiality-compared-what-four-privacy-architectures-promise/) le August 12, 2026. Stephen Webber ye ŋlɔe.*

Le keke July ƒe nuwuwu lɔƒo la, Zama ʋu a [nya ɣaɣlawo ɖɔliɖɔli teƒe](https://www.zama.org/post/announcing-zama-confidential-rfq-private-beta) le Ethereum mainnet dzi, le ame ŋutɔ ƒe beta me do ŋgɔ na September ƒe dutoƒo dodo ɖe ŋgɔ, si wɔnɛ be asitsala nate ŋu atsɔ tameɖoɖo si wotsɔ nya ɣaɣlawo ŋlɔe si ɣla ga homea, mɔɖeɖe ɖe ʋuʋu ŋu, kple asitsatsa ƒe mɔfiame.

Eɖoa ŋu ɖe akɔdada sue aɖe ŋu hena esia wɔwɔ. Asitsala la tsɔa adzame ga eve dea asi, ɖeka tsɔa nunɔamesi ŋutɔŋutɔwo eye ɖeka tsɔa afɔ si tsi tre ɖe eŋu kple ga home zero, eyata eteƒekpɔla kpɔa mɔ eve dzi ʋuʋu si ƒe lolome ɣla eye mate ŋu anya nuƒle tso nudzadzra me o. Asitsalawo ƒe ŋkɔ ɣiwo meɖea tameɖoɖoa gɔme, wokpɔa eƒe lolome gake womekpɔa mɔfiame si le eme o, eye woyɔa akpa evea siaa ƒe nyawo. Nudzadzra si wotre nu na la nɔa kɔsɔkɔsɔ dzi, eye amesi ɖu dzi koe nyaa mɔ si dzi asitsatsa la yi. Nusi dona tso eme ŋutɔŋutɔe nye asitɔtrɔ si wowɔna le eƒe lolome nu le dutoƒo kɔsɔkɔsɔ aɖe dzi, afisi akpa ɖeka aɖeke mele asitsatsa la godo meƒoa nɔnɔmetata bliboa nu ƒu gbeɖe o.

Zama gblɔ tso adzɔnua ŋu be enye nya ɣaɣla tso nuwuwu vaseɖe nuwuwu, gake nuka tututue nya ɣaɣla ma bia? Nya ɣaɣlawo gbɔgblɔ le afisia bia be woawɔ nya ɣaɣlawo le nuwɔna ƒe kɔsɔkɔsɔ blibo aɖe me, ale be dukɔa nakpɔ nu ɖeka, amesiwo bu la nakpɔ geɖe wu, elabena woɖe asitsatsa la ƒe lolome gɔme be yewoate ŋu ayɔe, eye amesi ɖu dzi la kpɔa nu geɖe wu. Ne míagblɔe kpuie la, afɔɖeɖe ɖesiaɖe si kplɔ wo nɔewo ɖo la le nenema le nyametsotso aɖe si ame aɖe wɔ le amesi wòle be wòanya nusi, kple ɣekaɣi ŋu ta. Anyo be míaƒo nya ɣaɣlawo ta kpuie be "ame aɖeke mekpɔa naneke o," gake naneke mawɔ dɔ le nuwɔna me o. Ele be woaɖo nya ɣaɣlawo ɖe ɖoɖo nu, eye ele be woawɔ ɖe ɖoɖo si nu wole nu.

Nusi dodzidzɔname enye be ƒe ewo kloe do ŋgɔ na gɔmedzedze ma la, Zcash tsɔ nya ɣaɣlawo gbɔ kpɔkpɔ ƒe ƒuƒoƒo gbãtɔ da ɖe dutoƒo blockchain dzi, ezã kpeɖodzi siwo me sidzedze zero le tsɔ ɣla amesi ɖoe ɖa, amesi xɔe, kple ga home si wowɔ le asitsatsa me esime wòle kpe ɖom edzi na amesiame be asitsatsa la sɔ. Le ƒe ewo siwo va yi tso ɣemaɣi me la, "nya ɣaɣla" va zu nɔnɔmefiaŋkɔnya na mɔ̃ɖaŋununya ƒe ƒome vovovo ene ya teti: asitsatsa siwo wokpɔ ta na, nya ɣaɣla siwo ƒe nɔnɔme sɔ bliboe, akɔntabubu si me ame geɖe le, kple amewuwu ƒe nɔnɔme siwo dzi woka ɖo. Fifia wole adzɔnuwo wɔm le ƒome eneawo katã me, eye wo dometɔ geɖe le kɔsɔkɔsɔ ɖeka me. Eyata nukae le gazea me?

Ƒome eneawo naa ŋuɖoɖo vovovowo eye ɣeaɖewoɣi la, wotsia tre ɖe wo nɔewo ŋu na kuxi vovovo ene, eye esi wònye be ŋuɖoɖo ɖesiaɖe va ɖona fifia si wobla ɖe agba me abe adzɔnu ene ta la, dɔ ŋutɔŋutɔe nye be nànya kuxi si le ŋuwò ŋutɔŋutɔ.

## Nukae nya ɣaɣlawo ƒe nya aɖe gblɔ?

Nya ɣaɣla ɖesiaɖe si wogblɔ, ne èɖee vaseɖe eƒe awu te la, ele be wòaɖo nyabiase ene ŋu. Nyatakaka kawoe woɣla? Ameka gbɔe wòtso? Nyatakakaawo ƒe agbenɔƒe ƒe akpa ka mee? Eye nuka dzie wòle be woaka ɖo hafi kakaɖedzia nate ŋu anɔ agbe?

Abe kpɔɖeŋu ene la, bu teƒe si wowɔa asitɔtrɔ le wo nɔewo ŋu le. Ele be wòaɣla agbɔsɔsɔme, mɔfiame, kple alesi wòage ɖe eme. Nukatae menye nunɔamesi eveawo o? Zama gblɔ be etsɔme tɔtrɔ aɖe aɣla ema hã. Ameka gbɔe: dukɔmeviwo kple bot siwo le ŋgɔ yim bliboe, asitsala siwo yɔa nyawo ƒe akpa aɖe, amesi ɖu dzi le wo katã dome. Le akpa si me: to nyayɔyɔ kple nudzadzra me, kple nyatakaka si ŋu wotrɔ asi le le nyaa gbɔ kpɔkpɔ me. Eye amesi dzi wòle be nàka ɖo: kakaɖedzia nɔ te ɖe FHE ŋutɔ dzi, ɖe smart contracts dzi, kple threshold key-management system si mele be eƒe dɔwɔlawo nawɔ ɖeka ato threshold aɖe ŋu o. Per the [litepaper ƒe agbalẽ](https://docs.zama.org/protocol/zama-protocol-litepaper), ame ɖeka aɖeke mate ŋu akpɔ nyatakakadzraɖoƒea ƒe safuiawo gbeɖe o.

Enye aɖaŋu na dɔwɔwɔ ƒe lolome nyadzɔdzɔwo kaka ƒe aɖaŋu manɔmee. Asitsalawo kpɔa lolome elabena womate ŋu axɔ asi na nya si wogblɔ la manɔmee o. Amesi ɖu dzi la srɔ̃a mɔfiame elabena, nyuie, nyaa gbɔ kpɔkpɔ biae.

Nusi kpɔɖeŋua ɖe fia enye be nyagbɔgblɔ si ɖi nya eve, abe ɖe nane nye nya ɣaɣla alo menye nya ɣaɣla o ene la, gbãna ɖe amewo ɖeɖeɖa ƒe ɖoɖowɔɖi aɖe me ŋutɔŋutɔ. Mɔ vovovowo ŋutɔ li siwo dzi woato aɖo nya siwo ɖi wo nɔewo gbɔ.

## Nukae nya ɣaɣlawo ŋuti ƒome ɖesiaɖe naa?

### Ametakpɔnu

Zcash ƒe asitsatsa si wokpɔ ta na la ɣlaa amesi ɖoe ɖa, amesi xɔe, kple ga homea. Nusi gakpɔtɔ le dutoƒo enye kpeɖodzi: sidzedze zero ƒe nyaʋiʋli, si amesiame si le node dɔ wɔm ate ŋu akpɔ, be asitsatsa ɣaɣla la zɔ ɖe seawo dzi. Ga siwo wokpɔ ta na la li abe gagbalẽ siwo wotsɔ nya ɣaɣlawo ŋlɔe ene, wo dometɔ ɖesiaɖe nyea ga home aɖe kple etɔ ŋuti nuŋlɔɖi si dzena le kɔsɔkɔsɔ me abe nya ɣaɣla ƒe adzɔgbeɖeɖe ko ene. Nuŋlɔɖi aɖe zazã ta nullifier, si nye asixɔxɔ si xea mɔ na nuŋlɔɖi ɖeka ma ke be woagazã zi eve gbeɖe o evɔ meɖea nuŋlɔɖi si wònye la fiana o. Esia fia be network la ate ŋu agbe gazazã zi eve evɔ womasrɔ̃ ga siwo ʋu la gbeɖe o, eyata gazazã megblẽa mɔ aɖeke ɖi si atsɔ asitsatsa ɖeka asɔ kple bubu o. Kpeɖodzia ƒe dɔe nye be wòaɖo kpe edzi be gazãla la tɔe nye nuŋlɔɖia, be asitsatsa la ƒe asixɔxɔwo da sɔ, eye be womewɔ ZEC aɖeke alo tsrɔ̃e o, eye esiawo katã meɖe agbɔsɔsɔme siwo le eme la dometɔ aɖeke ɖe go o.

Nusi ɖe dzesi le aɖaŋua ŋu la dzena wu ne wole nusiwo dzi wòle be woaka ɖo ŋu bum. Kɔmiti aɖeke meli si ate ŋu aɖe asitsatsa si wokpɔ ta na la gɔme o, dɔwɔla aɖeke meli si ƒe nugbeɖoɖo ƒe dzidzenu le vevie o, xɔtunudzrala aɖeke meli si ƒe ɖaseɖiɖi dzi wòle be woaxɔ ase o. Nusi gakpɔtɔ le kakaɖedzinɔƒeae nye akɔntabubu kple nutome sue siwo ŋlɔa seawo ƒe nyateƒenyenye. Le amesiame si ƒe ŋɔdzidoname ƒe kpɔɖeŋua lɔ xɔtuɖaŋuwo ŋutɔ ɖe eme, dɔwɔla siwo woate ŋu aƒoe ɖe enu, nudzrala siwo woate ŋu agblẽ nu le ŋu la, ŋkɔ kpui mae nye nusi wowɔ.

Le nyateƒe me la, ɖeko wodo kakaɖedzi ƒe gɔmeɖoanyi ma kpɔ le ewɔwɔ me. Le May 29 lia dzi la, Taylor Hornby, si nye dedienɔnɔ ŋuti numekula si le agbalẽ me dzrom na Shielded Labs, kpɔ gbeɖiɖi ƒe vodada aɖe le Orchard ta la ƒe kpeɖodzi nutome, si nɔ anyi tso esime wodze ta la gɔme le ƒe 2022 me, esi, le Shielded Labs ƒe nya nu la, ate ŋu aɖe mɔ be woawɔ ZEC aʋatso si seɖoƒe meli na o le Orchard me evɔ womakpɔ dzesi aɖeke le kɔsɔkɔsɔa dzi o.

Woɖo nuwo gbɔ kpɔkpɔ kpata le ŋkeke atɔ̃ me: Orchard tɔ vie le June 2 lia dzi, emegbe wogbugbɔe ɖo te le June 3 lia dzi kple nutome sue si woɖɔ ɖo. ZODL ƒe ɖɔɖɔɖodɔa he ŋusẽkpɔɖeamedzia kpuie wu Shielded Labs, si ƒo nu tso afɔku si le gazazã zi eve le ta la me tsɔ wu be woazã aʋatsonuwo ŋu, eye wòka nya ta be ezãlawo ƒe adzamenyawo kple nusiwo woatsɔ ana ƒe seɖoƒe bliboa mekpɔ ŋusẽ ɖe wo dzi o.

Woɖo verifiability gbugbɔgatu la ɖe meli emegbe. [Ironwood ƒe ati](https://shieldedlabs.net/ironwood/), activated as NU6.3 on July 28, seals the Orchard pool permanently, opens a fresh pool on the corrected circuit, backed by independent audits and formal verification work, and routes all exiting funds through a turnstile that caps withdrawals at what [woŋlɔe ɖe eme si ŋu kakaɖedzi le](https://shieldedlabs.net/four-questions-about-the-orchard-vulnerability/), si ana ŋutete si le ezãla ɖesiaɖe si be wòalé ŋku ɖe nusiwo le tsatsam ŋu le eɖokui si evɔ makpɔ ame aɖeke ƒe asitsatsa o. Nudzɔdzɔa nye kakaɖedzi-si wotu ɖe agbalẽ gã si le dɔ dzi. Ne nutome ƒe ɖɔɖɔɖo do kpo nu la, atike la nye akɔntabubu kple ʋuʋu, ke menye kɔmiti aɖe ƒe didi o, eye wotsɔa agbalẽdzikpɔkpɔ si xɔtuɖaŋua tu ɖe ta la ƒe liƒo dzi ƒe aɖewo do ŋgɔ la ɖoa seɖoƒe na wowó ƒe didime.

Nusi akpoxɔnu ƒlena kple kakaɖedzi suetɔ kekeake ma la le kpuie le eɖoɖo koŋ me. Nɔnɔme ɣaɣla la nye fexexe, asixɔxɔ si tso akpa ɖeka yi bubu gbɔ. Nɔnɔme aɖeke meli si wotsɔ nya ɣaɣlawo ƒo ƒui si dzi akpa geɖe bua akɔnta le o, nubabla ƒe susu ɣaɣla aɖeke meli o, eye nudɔdɔgbalẽ aɖeke meli si wotsɔ nya ɣaɣlawo ŋlɔe o.

### Nya ɣaɣla si nye homomorphic bliboe

FHE na akɔntabubu zɔna le ciphertext dzi. Le Zama ƒe ɖoɖowɔɖi me la, tɔtrɔa le agbe le Ethereum dzi fifia, ga si susɔ kple ga si wotsɔ yi teƒe bubu la li le kɔsɔkɔsɔ dzi abe asiléƒe siwo wotsɔ nya ɣaɣlawo ŋlɔe le nuŋɔŋlɔa te ene [ERC-7984 ƒe dzidzenu](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7984), coprocessors wɔa akɔntabubu si wotsɔ nya ɣaɣlawo ŋlɔe la le kɔsɔkɔsɔ me, eye nyatakakawo ɖeɖeɖa bia be safuidzikpɔkpɔ dɔwɔla siwo le wo ɖokui si ƒe ha, habɔbɔ wuietɔ̃ le mainnet ƒe gɔmedzedze, siwo wotia na ŋkɔxɔxɔ kple dɔwɔwɔ ƒe akpawo, eyata akpa ɖeka aɖeke mate ŋu axlẽ nyatakakaawo o. Esiae na be nudɔdɔgbalẽ si wotsɔ nya ɣaɣlawo ŋlɔ alo gadodo ƒe ta si woɣla la te ŋu dzɔna kura: akpa geɖe ƒe nɔnɔme ɣaɣla, si ŋlɔa nubabla ƒe susu si woama me.

Gake dɔdzikpɔkɔmiti vevitɔwo ya ɖe? Ele be ame aɖe nate ŋu aɖe nyatakakaawo me, alo womate ŋu azã nyatakakaawo o. Aɖaŋua ƒe ŋuɖoɖoe nye be wòana ame ma nanye dɔwɔƒe siwo le wo ɖokui si ƒe dzidzenu tsɔ wu be wòanye nudzikpɔla ɖeka, eye wòakpɔ amesi ate ŋu aɖe asixɔxɔ siwo to mɔɖeɖe ɖe nu ŋu dzi kpɔkpɔ si woŋlɔ ɖe nubablaawo ŋutɔ me, si woate ŋu akeke ɖe enu na ame siwo woɖo abe agbalẽdzikpɔlawo ene, si nye amedɔdɔ ƒe mɔnu si ƒe sedziwɔwɔ ƒe gɔmesesewo nye akpa si kplɔe ɖo le nusiwo kplɔ wo nɔewo ɖo sia me. Nyabiase si wòle na amesi le egbɔkpɔnuwo me tom enye nenye be kɔmitia ƒe ɖoɖowɔwɔ, dzideƒonamenuwo, kple dzidzenu sɔ kple dodokpɔwɔla ƒe ŋɔdzidoname ƒe kpɔɖeŋua.

### Akpa geɖe ƒe akɔntabubu

Akpa geɖe ƒe akɔntabubu ma nyatakakawo ɖe gomekpɔkpɔ siwo woma ɖe node siwo le wo ɖokui si me, siwo wɔa ɖeka bua akɔnta le emetsonu aɖe ŋu evɔ node aɖeke megbugbɔa nusiwo wotsɔ de eme la ɖoa anyi o. Avishay Yanai, si nye Soda Labs ƒe gɔmeɖoanyila kple eƒe dɔdzikpɔlagã, si ƒe... [mɔ̃ si wotsɔ wɔa nu ŋutɔŋutɔe si me wowɔa nu vovovowo le](https://www.proofstreet.xyz/defining-privacy-in-mpc-from-the-operators-seat/) ewɔa nubabla ƒe susu si sɔ kple EVM ɖe asixɔxɔ siwo wotsɔ nya ɣaɣlawo ɣla dzi le ewɔwɔ me le COTI network dzi, eɖɔa MPC ƒe ɖoɖo nyui le ewɔwɔ me be ekpɔa egbɔ be akpa ɖeka aɖeke, eɖanye ŋkuléla, node si le akɔntabubua wɔm, alo nudzrala, mekpɔa nuŋɔŋlɔ gbadzaa ƒe nyawo alo domedome nɔnɔme gbeɖe o, eye nusi ɖeɖefia ƒe ɖoɖoa na wo koe amesiame kpɔna. Kakaɖedzia nɔ te ɖe nugbeɖoɖomawɔmawɔ dzi ko, eye ema nye susu si nàte ŋu awɔ mɔ̃ɖaŋudɔ le ahawɔ nubabla kplii, abe kple dɔwɔƒe siwo le ʋɔnudrɔ̃ƒe vovovowo, le alilikpo vovovowo dzi, kple le agbalẽdzikpɔkpɔ siwo le wo ɖokui si te, kple se ƒe nuɖeɖe ɖe nugbeɖoɖo ŋu ene. Cryptography ƒe dɔ le afisiae nye nusi wòyɔ be kakaɖedzi dzi ɖeɖe kpɔtɔ gã aɖe tsɔ wu kakaɖedzi ɖeɖeɖa, be wòaɖo nusi sɔ kple kakaɖedzidɔwɔƒewo dea clearinghouses gbɔ. Nubabla si to dzidzenua ŋu gblẽa ameŋunyatakakawo me, gake menye nyateƒetoto o, elabena le aɖaŋu siwo ŋu dutoƒonukpɔkpɔ le me la, gotagome kpeɖodzinalawo ate ŋu akpɔe ɖa kokoko be emetsonu siwo ŋu woka nya ta le la sɔ evɔ womawɔ ɖeka kple akɔntabubua o.

Nuxlɔ̃amea koe nye be ŋkuléla ma tɔgbe kpɔnɛ be asitsatsa aɖe dzɔ, ɣekaɣie, nubabla si tsi tre ɖe eŋu, amesi tsɔe ɖo ɖa, tsɔ kpe ɖe hatsotsowo ƒe lolome kple fe siwo woxena ŋu. Node si kpɔ gome le eme ate ŋu asrɔ̃ dɔwɔwɔ si ƒu du kple eƒe dɔwɔwɔ ƒe ɖoɖowo. Ɣeyiɣi ƒe kadodo hã nye nu bubu si ŋu woabu, elabena dɔwɔna ƒe agbɔsɔsɔme ate ŋu atso ne agbɔsɔsɔmewo metso o gɔ̃ hã. Eye nuka kee dɔwɔɖoɖoa ɖale nuŋɔŋlɔ si me kɔ o, nya suesuesue siwo woɖena ɖe go enuenu ate ŋu adzi ɖe edzi azu kpɔɖeŋu siwo ate ŋu adzɔ na akpa aɖe si le wo dim. Eɖe eme be: "Enye ŋugbedodoa ƒe liƒo," eyata ele be numekulawo nabia tso nudzralawo si be woatae tẽ. Akɔntabubu ƒe kpɔɖeŋu aɖeke medzraa nuŋɔŋlɔ dzrowo ɖoɖo ɖa to, le kpɔɖeŋu me, asisi si ŋu woɖe asi le la ɖo o. Ðoɖowɔɖia bua akɔnta nuteƒewɔwɔtɔe le nusianu si wonae ŋu. Amekae ƒoa nu na amesi nye ʋuɖoɖo ƒe kuxi, eye sedziwɔwɔ nye ɖoɖo tsɔ wu mɔ̃: "Nya ɣaɣla aɖeke mewɔa ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ma na wò o."

Garbled-circuit designs zɔna le ɣesiaɣi rounds kple heavy cryptographic material precomputed hafi inputs li, si nye nusi na transaction-rate workloads te ŋu dzɔna le afisi secret-sharing protocols xea network round trips le dɔwɔwɔ ɖesiaɖe me, eye afisi, le Yanai ƒe nya nu la, MPC ƒome aɖewo kpɔa asixɔxɔawo ta gake menye nyateƒe si le akɔntabubu ŋu o, garbled-circuit designs ateŋu akeke cover ɖe counterparty adrɛswo kple nunɔamesiwo nu ƒomeviwo. Gake ƒomea ƒe nunɔamesi si do tso eme wu le tsɔtsɔ sɔ kple wo nɔewo sia mee nye be MPC nye xɔtuɖaŋu le eɖokui si, eye wònye nusi wotsɔ wɔa nu le ƒome bubuawo me hã: dzidzenu vevitɔ ƒe kɔmiti si le FHE ƒe dɔwɔwɔ te la ŋutɔ nye akɔntabubu si me ame geɖe le. Nusɔsrɔ̃la ƒe biabia, le Yanai ƒe ɖoɖowɔwɔ me, enye "nukae nye kɔmitia wɔna, eye nukae mexena ɖe edzi?" Ne kɔmitia li le mɔ eveawo siaa nu la, akɔntabubua ŋutɔ wɔwɔ le MPC me ate ŋu aɖe gotagome ƒuƒoƒo xɔasi aɖe ɖa.

Anyo be míana eme nakɔ be woƒoa nu tso adzɔnu eve siwo to vovo kura ŋu to kpukpui ɖeka zazã me. Custody MPC ma asidede agbalẽ te ƒe safui ɖe akpa vovovowo me ale be wo dometɔ ɖeka pɛ hã mate ŋu ade asi ete ɖeɖeko o; ekpɔa nya ɣaɣla ɖeka ta, eye akɔntabubu ɖekae si le eme nye dɔwɔwɔ ɖeka si woɖo ɖi, si wɔnɛ be wodea asi ete. Compute MPC wɔa ɖoɖowɔɖi ƒe susuŋudɔwɔwɔ le mɔ gbadza nu ɖe ​​nyatakaka siwo nɔa ɣaɣla le ɣeyiɣi bliboa me dzi, eye wòlɔa akpa geɖe ƒe nyawo tsɔtsɔ de eme, nubabla ƒe susuŋudɔwɔwɔ le wo ɖokui si, kple dɔwɔwɔ atraɖii ɖe eme. Akɔntabubuae nye nusi sɔ kple wo nɔewo. Gake le dɔwɔwɔ gome la, wozãa ɖeka hena safuiwo dzikpɔkpɔ eye evelia nyea amewuwu ƒe nɔnɔme ɣaɣla.

### Amewuwu ƒe nɔnɔme siwo dzi woka ɖo

TEE nye nuto si woɖe ɖe aga tso xɔtunuwo gbɔ le dɔwɔnu si tsɔa nyatakakawo ɣla ɖe ŋkuɖodzinu me eye wokpɔa wo ta tso dɔwɔɖoɖoa, hypervisor, kple mɔ̃a ƒe dɔwɔla gbɔ, eye ɖaseɖiɖi le didiƒe nana gotagometɔ aɖe ɖoa kpe kɔpi si le dɔ wɔm le eme dzi. Aɖaŋua ƒlea nu eve siwo cryptographic ƒomewo xea fe na vevie, generality kple speed: enclave wɔa ɖoɖo dzrowo le ga si gogo dzro ko me, si tae TEEs anchor akpa gãtɔ nye nya ɣaɣla-kɔmpiuta nunanawo tso alilikpo gãwo kple kɔsɔkɔsɔ xɔtuɖoɖowo ƒe hatsotso si le dzidzim ɖe edzi.

Kakaɖedzi ƒe gɔmeɖoanyie nye asitsatsa, elabena kakaɖedzia sesẽ abe xɔtunudzrala ƒe aɖaŋu kple nuzazãwo ƒe kɔsɔkɔsɔ, ɖaseɖiɖi ƒe xɔtuɖoɖo, kple enclave ƒe tsitretsitsi ɖe axadzimɔwo ɖeɖe ŋu, si nye amedzidzedze ƒe hatsotso aɖe si ƒe ŋutinya wota ɣeyiɣi didi aɖe ene. Tiatia xɔtuɖaŋu fia be woawɔ tsatsa si ŋu wobu be xɔtunuwo ƒe kakaɖedzi kpe ɖe ɖaseɖiɖi ŋu nye asi nyuitɔ na dɔ ŋutɔŋutɔwo wɔwɔ le duƒuƒu ŋutɔŋutɔ me, eye le dɔwɔwɔ geɖe gome la, ele nenema.

## Afikae akpoxɔnuwɔwɔ sɔ le?

Ðe Zcash si wokpɔ ta na ate ŋu asubɔ asitsaƒe ɣaɣla zazã ƒe nya la? Ao, gake ema menye nusi gbɔdzɔ o. RFQ teƒe aɖe nye mamã encrypted nɔnɔme le nubabla ƒe susu te, akpa geɖe ƒe tameɖoɖo ɣaɣlawo ŋlɔa nu le nudzadzra si ŋutɔ ƒua du ŋkuagbãtɔe la me. Ema sɔ wu na nane abe MPC ene, kple ame ŋutɔ ƒe dukɔ si woama le kadodo enuenu te, nudɔdɔ gbalẽ, netting cycle. Akpoxɔnu mekpɔa nusi dzi woanɔ te ɖo awɔ ema o; eƒe nɔnɔme ɣaɣla nye fe si woxena le akpa eve dome. Teƒea ŋutɔ sɔ na FHE alo MPC, eye enyo be míade dzesii be esi Zama tue la, nya ɣaɣla si wòna la nye ɖoɖowɔɖi si me woaɖe nyatakakawo afia asitsala siwo woŋlɔ ŋkɔ na, ​​siwo wode dzesii, si hiã na dɔwɔƒewo ƒe asitsatsa. Gake ema nye nusi to vovo na nusi asitsatsa si wokpɔ ta na, si nye nya ɣaɣla si me ame ɣiwo ƒe ŋkɔwo mele o eye akpa aɖeke meli si ava srɔ̃ wò mɔfiame mlɔeba o.

Ke dɔwɔƒewo ƒe fexexe kple fetu ƒe ɖoɖoa ya ɖe? Esia nye asi si FHE kple MPC nudzrala ɖesiaɖe drɔ̃na fifia, eye eya tututue nye kuxi si wotu takpɔkpɔ na: ga home siwo woɣla, counterparties ɣla, validity public. Le gɔmeɖose nu la, kɔsɔkɔsɔ si wokpɔ ta na ye nye ŋuɖoɖo si ŋu kakaɖedzi le wu si li, kɔmiti aɖeke meli o, nudzrala aɖeke meli o, dɔwɔla aɖeke meli o. Le nuwɔna me la, wole adzɔha-fexexe ƒe asi dzi ɖum le ketekemɔwo dzi, ERC-20 ƒe ɖekawɔwɔ, nudzrala siwo li fifia, sedziwɔwɔ ƒe dɔwɔnu siwo li fifia, stablecoin denominations, kple Zcash si wotsɔ akpoxɔnu wɔe la naa nunɔamesi bubu le kɔsɔkɔsɔ bubu dzi. Gake enye sesẽ si sɔ le kuxi ƒe nɔnɔme ƒe seƒe eye ɖeko wòle lalam be woawɔ ɖeka. Nya ɣaɣlawo ate ŋu awɔe, eyata nusiwo ŋu wotsi dzi ɖo ŋutɔŋutɔ ku ɖe nusi stack si ƒo xlãe la do ƒome kplii ŋu.

Anyo hã be míalé ŋku ɖe nusiwo ƒome etɔ̃ mamlɛawo naa ŋu, eye akpoxɔnu ya mewɔa esia o: kplɔ̃ɖoƒe si woate ŋu azi. ## Kakaɖedzinu kae nàte ŋu anɔ agbe kplii?

Ne míegalé ŋku ɖe nya siwo ŋu míeƒo nu tsoe le etame ŋu ake la, míakpɔ nɔnɔmetata aɖe si tsi tre ɖe wo nɔewo ŋu. FHE ƒe dɔwɔwɔ akpa gãtɔ ƒe threshold MPC le ete abe decryption layer ene.
