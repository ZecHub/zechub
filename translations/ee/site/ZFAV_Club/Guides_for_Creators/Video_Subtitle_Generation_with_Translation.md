# Video ƒe nyagbewo ƒe dzidzedze kple gɔmeɖeɖe

Dɔwɔnu siwo woza le mɔfiafia sia me:

1. CapCut
2. [CapCut SRT Export](https://www.biyaoyun.com/capcutsrt)
3. Nusiŋlɔ ƒe nuŋlɔƒe
4. [Online Subtitle Translator & Editor](https://www.syedgakbar.com/projects/dst)

Dze egɔme kple wò video ƒe tɔtrɔ mamlɛtɔ ƒe kɔkɔ geɖe CapCut me. Le mɔ bubu nu la, àte ŋu azã CapCut ahɔ wò video hafi nàyi edzi kple ɖoɖo sia.

Ne video mamlɛtɔ le CapCut me la, zã [CapCut SRT Export](https://www.biyaoyun.com/capcutsrt) axɔ nyagbe ƒe faɛl la, kple gaƒoƒo ƒe dzesiwo, tso CapCut ƒe dɔa me.

Ʋu SRT faɛl la le nusiŋlɔ ƒe nuŋlɔƒe me eye nàkpɔ video la bliboa, esi nèle exlem le SRT faɛl la me, nàtrɔ SRT faɛl la ne vodadawo dzɔ. Zã didi kple trɔtrɔ be nà**trɔ katã** le vodada ɖe sia ɖe ta.

Aɖaŋu: Kpɔ video la le 2x esi nèle nyagbewo me kpɔm

Ne wokpɔ nyagbe gbãtɔ ƒe ƒeŋu eye wotrɔe vɔ la, ɖo SRT faɛl la ɖe [Online Subtitle Translator & Editor](https://www.syedgakbar.com/projects/dst) be nàɖe SRT faɛl la gɔme ɖe gbe bubu sia tɔ me kple adzɔ sue aɖe.

Ewogɔmeɖe SRT faɛl la dzi nàkpɔe kple video tɔtrɔ ƒe dɔwɔnu, ame si gɔme se gbe si woɖe gɔmee la na.

Ewogɔmeɖe SRT faɛlwo hia gaƒoƒo ƒe dzesiwo ƒe trɔtrɔ ɣeaɣi, eye video tɔtrɔ ƒe dɔwɔnu geɖewo ate ŋu adi wo eye woadzra wo ɖo bɔbɔe.
