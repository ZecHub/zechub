# MetaMask Zcash Snap Ƒoƒo Ƒe Nyawo ƒe Lɛ̃di

> Lɔ̃ eye dɔ ɣaɣla ZEC tso MetaMask browser extension la me, zã ɖoɖo si le ChainSafe ƒe Zcash Snap kple WebZjs web gakotoku la.

## TL;DR

* ChainSafe Zcash Snap ƒe lɔlɔ̃ kɔkɔtɔŋɔ̃a tsɔa ɣaɣla ƒe ZEC kple MetaMask le desktop dzi (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). Zcash software eve sia evehia o.
* Wò Zcash keywo tsoa wò MetaMask gadzraɖoɖi nya me. Nya ɖeka ma azɔ la wɔa wò ETH kple wò ZEC siaa ɣaɣla.
* Àwɔa ZEC dzi dɔ to WebZjs gakotoku si le webzjs.chainsafe.dev la dzi, menye le MetaMask ƒe nuwo ƒe ŋkɔwo ƒe list me o.
* Wò account la xɔ gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka, ale be ame siwo dɔa ga ɖe wò la na wò le gaƒuƒoƒo ɣaɣla siwo woate ŋu zã la me.
* Xɔ̃e ŋkɔ̃e to trɔtrɔ (le kpɔɖeŋu me ETH va ZEC) alo to ZEC ɖeɖe tsoa gatrɔƒe me va wò gaɖoƒe ɣaɣla dzi.
* Gadzraɖoɖi: gbugbɔ ɖo MetaMask kple Snap, eye emegbe tsɔ wò gadzraɖoɖi nya si wòxɔ xoxo la ge.

## Nyatakakadzesi Siwo Wòle Be Nya

MetaMask Snap nye nu sue si woɖo ɖe sandbox me, eye wòfia blockchain yeye aɖe MetaMask. Zcash Snap, si ChainSafe wɔ eye Hacken da asitsatsa le eŋu, na MetaMask ŋu be wòava ɖe Zcash safekɔwo tso wò ŋuɖoɖo nya xoxo la me, eye wòadzra Zcash gaɖoɖowo.

MetaMask la mefia ZEC ƒe gaɖiɖi o. Ke boŋ, WebZjs web gakotoku la kpena ɖe Snap ŋu, ɖoa eƒe nyatakaka ɖo ɖe Zcash ɖoɖonu la dzi le lightwalletd server dzi, eye wòfia wò gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo kple wò gaɖiɖi. Wò browser la wɔa ɣaɣla ɖaseɖigbalɛwo kple WebAssembly, elabena ema ta dɔ gaɖoɖo la ate ŋu adze ɣeyiɣi vie.

Gɔmeɖeɖe siwo le afisia ɖo alɔ̃ŋu Snap install, WebZjs kpena ɖe eŋu, gakotoku la yɔ fũu, dɔ gaɖoɖo kple xɔ ga, eye wòakpɔ wò gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi siwo le wò gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka me.

MetaMask la ɖoɖa fifia **ɣaɣla Zcash (ZEC)** to **ChainSafe-wɔe Zcash Snap**, eye ema na wò ŋu be nàdɔ, nàxɔ, eye nàɖo ga ɣaɣla ZECwo dzi le wò browser gakotoku la me. **Hacken** da asitsatsa le eŋu, eye woŋlɔe ɖe **MetaMask Snaps Directory si nye official la me**. Edi **software Zcash tẽ aɖeke o** - MetaMask kple Snap ko hiã.

## **Nu Siwo Wòle Be Wòaxɔ**

> [**MetaMask Extension**](https://snaps.metamask.io/snap/npm/zcashcommunitygrants/webzjs-zcash-snap/) (desktop ko) - Chrome, Edge, alo Firefox.
> MetaMask Akɔnta - Seed phrase la le dedie; Snap ɖea Zcash safekɔwo tso eme.  
> Internet ƒe kadodo nyuitɔ - Be nyatakaka nayi ɖe Zcash ɖoɖonu la dzi.  
> Ga - ETH si wòatsɔ aɖɔli ɖɔli na ZEC alo ZEC tso gatrɔƒe aɖe me.

> **Adzɔgbe:** Dzra wò MetaMask ƒe ŋuɖoɖo nya la ɣaɣla - wò ɖoa ETH kple ZEC siaa dzi.

---

## **1. Install Zcash Snap**

1. Yi [**MetaMask Snaps Directory**](https://snaps.metamask.io/snap/npm/zcashcommunitygrants/webzjs-zcash-snap/) dzi.  
2. Di [**"Zcash WebZjs Wallet"**](https://snaps.metamask.io/snap/npm/zcashcommunitygrants/webzjs-zcash-snap/) alo [**"WebZjs Zcash Snap"**](https://snaps.metamask.io/snap/npm/zcashcommunitygrants/webzjs-zcash-snap/).  
3. Zi **Install/Add to MetaMask** dzi.
4. Lɔ̃ ɖe mɔnukpɔkpɔwo siawo ŋu:
   ```
      Manage Zcash accounts 
      Store data on your device
   ```

![Zcash-snap-install](/content-images/Hy5MSG2Oex-42d0c5b346.webp)


---

## **2. (Optional) Tsɔ Zcash Ƒe Network Kpe**

Le MetaMask me la, tia **Add Network** eye ɖo eme:

Na **BNB SmartChain**;
```markdown
-  Name: BNB Smart Chain
-  RPC URL: https://bsc-dataseed.binance.org
-  Chain ID: 56
-  Symbol: BNB
-  Block Explorer URL: https://bscscan.com
```
Esia na network ƒe nyatakaka kple explorer kadodo siwo woaɖo.
![Add-a-custom-Net....](/content-images/S1hq7f2Oel-e1ca8b9044.webp)

Na **Zcash Mainnet**;
```markdown
- Name: Zcash Mainnet  
- RPC URL: https://zjs.zec.rocks 
- Symbol: ZEC
```
`https://zjs.zec.rocks` nye WebZjs-kpeɖeŋu (gRPC-web) lightwalletd server si [zec.rocks](https://zec.rocks) (@emersonian) ɖoa dɔ. Na testnet la, zã `https://zjs.zec.rocks/testnet`. Ne wò ŋutɔ ɖoa WebZjs web gakotoku la dɔ la, ele be nàtsɔ esia ɖo ɖe teƒe si nàŋlɔ be `LIGHTWALLETD_PROXY`.

---

## **3. Kpe ɖe ChainSafe WebZjs Gakotoku Ŋu**

1. Yi [webzjs.chainsafe.dev](https://webzjs.chainsafe.dev).  
2. Zi **Connect MetaMask Snap** dzi.  

![Zcash-web-wallet](/content-images/Sk8nSz3dgl-98ce36cc67.webp)

3. Lɔ̃ ɖe kadodo la ŋu.  
4. Kpɔ wò Zcash akɔnta ƒe ŋuɖoɖo, si me nu siawo le:
   - Unified addresswo kple transparent address

![Account-summary-unif....](/content-images/r17c_Mhdel-f4963826d5.webp)


5. Lala va se ɖe esime sync nu la wu.




---

## **4. Yɔ Wò Gakotoku Fũu**

> **Ðɔli ɖɔli ETH -> ZEC** - Zã dɔwɔƒe siwo abe **LeoDex** ene, eye nàɖo ga ɖe wò ɣaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi la.  
> **Gatrɔƒe ƒe Ɖoɖoɖo Gakɔ** - Ɖe ZEC si wòxɔ la tso gatrɔƒe me yi wò WebZjs ɣaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi la dzi.  

![LeoDex-SWAP](/content-images/HyLQ0G2ugg-8d82ef24f6.webp)


> => Zã ɣaɣla (z) gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo na **ɣaɣla blibo**.

---

## **5. Dɔ / Xɔ ZEC**

1. Le **WebZjs** me la, yi **Transfer Balance**.  
2. Ŋlɔ nu siawo:
```
   - Shielded recipient address  
   - Amount
```
   ![Transfer-Balance](/content-images/rkvcFfhdex-bd55d079eb.webp)

4. Lɔ̃ gaɖoɖo la dzi le MetaMask (dzra gaɖoɖo la).  
5. Ne woɖo dɔɖoɖo nu si wòxɔ la, ado le WebZjs me.

---

## **6. Kpɔ / Ɖo Glia Nuwo**

> Kpɔ **WebZjs** be nàkpɔ gaɖiɖi siwo woŋlɔ yeyewo **(MetaMask meŋlɔ ZEC le eɖokui me o)** .  
> Ne kuxiwo dze:
  ```
  - Confirm you have the official ChainSafe Snap.  
  - Check correct network settings.  
  - Ensure correct address format.  
  - Reconnect via **Connect Snap** if needed.
  ``` 

> **Dedienɔnɔ ƒe Adzɔgbe:** Install **ChainSafe Snap** si woɖa asitsatsa le eŋu la ko; kpɔ mɔnukpɔkpɔwo do ŋgɔ na lɔ̃ɖeɖe.

---

## **7. Kpɔ Gaɖoƒe Ƒe Dzesiwo Ƒe Akpaewo**

1. Yi **Receive** ƒe akpa la - Wò Unified Address la afia le afi ma zi geɖe.  
2. Copy Unified Address la eye nàyi [Zcash Block Explorer](https://mainnet.zcashexplorer.app/) dzi.  
3. Paste wò Unified Address la ɖe search bar la me.  
4. Fifia wòakpɔ Unified Address la ƒe akpa siwo katã, siwo me nu siawo le:
``` 
   Orchard Address  
   Sapling Address  
   Transparent Address
``` 

![Address-components](/content-images/SyPR2f2_gg-3907c5bf58.webp)
