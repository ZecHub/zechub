# Nuŋlɔɖiwo

#### Memo Siwo Wotsɔ Nya Ɣla Ðo Ðoɖo Ðe Ame Ŋu

Ne èle Z2Z (shielded-to-shielded) ƒe asitsatsa ɖom ɖa la, àte ŋu ade nuŋlɔɖi (gbedasi) aɖe asitsatsa la me. Woate ŋu azã nuŋlɔɖi sia na nu vovovo geɖe.

#### Asidede Asitsatsa Ŋu

Hadzidzi ƒe fexexe koŋue wozãna na nuŋlɔɖiwo. Esi wònye be shielded transactions tsɔa wò nyatakakawo dea nyatakakadzraɖoƒea ta la, màte ŋu akpɔ amesi ɖo ZEC ɖe wò, kple nusi ZEC ate ŋu anye na wò o. Zãlawo ate ŋu azã nuŋlɔɖi ƒe akpaa atsɔ ade asi woƒe ŋkɔ alo ŋkɔ bubu te be woana wo hati nanya amesi gbɔ asitsatsa la tso. Woate ŋu aɖɔ nusi ta wowɔ asitsatsa la ɖo hã.

#### Gbedasɛ ɖoɖoɖa

Zãzã bubu na nuŋlɔɖi si wotsɔ nya ɣaɣlawo ŋlɔe nye be woaɖo gbedasi ɖe ame aɖe si si z-addr le. Gbedasi siawo ate ŋu aku ɖe nusianu ŋu, eɖanye [ŋkuɖodzinya na xɔlɔ̃ aɖe o](https://twitter.com/iansagstette/status/1542142468505870336), alo [gbedasi vevi aɖe si wòle be wòanɔ ɣaɣla alesi wòanya wɔe](https://twitter.com/InsideZcash/status/1545800146352578560).

#### Lɔlɔ̃ ƒe Nuŋlɔɖiwo le Blockchain la dzi

Ame aɖe nɔ anyi si ɖo lɔlɔ̃gbalẽ ɖe woƒe kpeɖeŋutɔ le Zcash blockchain ƒe block gbãtɔwo dometɔ ɖeka me. Ame aɖe kpɔe be yewoƒe zɔhɛa ɖo faɛl ɖe yewo to Zcash ƒe nuŋlɔɖi aɖe dzi. Fail sia nye tikiti na wɔna tɔxɛ aɖe, le duta, si eya kple eƒe lɔlɔ̃tɔ si le didiƒe ʋĩ nɔ nu ƒom tsoe be yewoade ɖekae. Nuŋlɔɖia nye lɔlɔ̃ ŋuti nuŋlɔɖi.
