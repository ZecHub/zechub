---

###  Gɔmedzedze  
[**encrypt.trade**](https://encrypt.trade/zec) nye Solana app si JMD Labs Inc. ƒe dzi. Eɖe mɔ na wò be nàwɔ ɖɔli ɖɔli **SOL alo USDC** le Solana dzi yi **Zcash (ZEC)** me. Woatsɔ wò tokenwo atrɔ gbã zu wɔnɔƒe siwo woŋlɔ kple ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ [encryption], ale be gaƒuƒowo manɔ ʋuʋuɖi o le Solana dzi, eye emegbe woawɔ ɖɔli ɖɔli kple wo ayi ZEC to NEAR Intents me.

Ɖɔli ɖɔli la le ɣaɣla me le mɔ aɖewo nu, gake menye mɔ katã nu o. App la ŋutɔ ƒe [nuxlɔ̃amewo](https://docs.encifher.io/docs) gblɔ be wò ha kple ɖoɖonu la menye togbɔbe o: amewo ate ŋu akpɔ be wò gakotoku [wallet] zã app la, gake womate ŋu akpɔ ga si nètsɔ va o. ZEC hã va ɖo gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi [address] si le nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi me, ale eanɔ ʋuʋuɖi le Zcash ɖoɖonu la dzi va se ɖe esime nàɣaɣlae.

###  Nu Si Wòle Be Nànya Hafi Nàwɔ Ɖɔli Ɖɔli  
- **Solana ƒe akpa.** Ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ na gaƒuƒowo ɣaɣla, gake wò gakotoku ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi kple eƒe zãzã na app la le ʋuʋuɖi me. Eƒe [nɔnɔme nyuiwo](https://docs.encifher.io/docs/best-practices) de dzesi be nu bɔbɔe si nye aɣaɣla, awɔ ɖɔli ɖɔli, eye aɖe ɣaɣlae na wò gaɖoɖo [transaction] la nava si woate ŋu akplɔe.
- **Ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ.** Woawɔ dɔ kple gaƒuƒo siwo woŋlɔ kple ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ le ɖoɖonu megbe le hardware enclave (TEE) me. Mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖoŋlɔlawo ƒe [agbalẽ](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1504) gblɔ be esia ɖe TEE ƒe blibonyenye, key dzikpɔkpɔ si woawɔ kple ame geɖe kple cloud ƒe attestation root ŋu, menye cryptography ko ŋu o.
- **Ɖoɖonu geɖe dome ƒe akpa.** Woatsɔ ɖɔli ɖɔli la ayi ZEC to NEAR Intents me, afi si solvers siwo le ɖokuiwo te ŋu wɔa order la.
- **Zcash ƒe akpa.** NEAR Intents dea ZEC ɖe eme be wozãe kple [gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi siwo le nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi me ko](https://docs.near-intents.org/resources/chain-support), eye ZEC ƒe afi le encrypt.trade dzi xɔ gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi siwo le nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi me (t1 alo t3) ko esime woɖe mɔfiafia sia kpɔ le September 2026 me. Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi si le nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi me fia eƒe balance kple ga siwo va ɖo eme le ame katã ƒe ŋkume va se ɖe esime nàɣaɣlae.
- **Dɔmekpɔkpɔ.** App la kpɔ gakotoku siwo geɖo ɖe eŋu la ɖe databasewo siwo nenye TRM kple Chainalysis ŋu, eye eƒe [dɔmewɔwɔ ƒe axa](https://docs.encifher.io/docs/compliance) gblɔ be woate ŋu akpɔ record siwo woŋlɔ kple ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ me ne se ƒe tata nyuitɔ le. NEAR Intents hã wɔa eŋutɔ ƒe [dɔmekpɔkpɔ](https://docs.near-intents.org/security-compliance/risk-and-compliance).
