##  **Afɔɖeɖe 1: Ʋu Swap Interface la** 
Dze **Phantom app** la gɔme eye nàzi **Swap** ƒe dzesi dzi, . 
alo yi **[solswap.org](https://solswap.org/)** tso wò Phantom web-browser dzi.

---

##  **Afɔɖeɖe 2: Tia Networks kple Tokens na Depositing** 
- Tia wò **tsoƒe network** (e.g., *Ethereum* alo *Solana*) emegbe nàde ga eme hena asitɔtrɔ. 


![img3](/content-images/S1SaGYZ9xx-2a27ccdd47.webp)

- Tia gɔmeɖoanyi dzesi abe **SOL, USDT, alo USDC**. 
- Tia **ZEC** abe wò **destination token** ene. 
- Kpɔ egbɔ be Zcash li to swap interface dzi. 



![img4](/content-images/ry4QQF-5gx-f3805528ea.webp)

---

##  **Afɔɖeɖe 3: Ŋlɔ Ga home & Totoɖeme Quote** 
- Ŋlɔ ga home si nèdi be yeaɖɔli. 
- Phantom aɖe **ga home si wobu be woaxɔ** afia le fewo megbe. 


![img5](/content-images/B1U1NYW5xe-58cf150668.webp)

---

##  **Afɔɖeɖe 4: Kpɔ Gas & Fewo ɖa** 
- Le **kɔsɔkɔsɔ ɖeka ƒe tɔtrɔwo** gome la, kpɔ egbɔ be gas ƒe dzesi si le dzɔdzɔme nu (*ETH na Ethereum, SOL na Solana*) sɔ gbɔ na ye. 
- **Cross-chain swaps** hiã gas le dzɔtsoƒe kple yiyi ƒe kɔsɔkɔsɔwo siaa dzi. 
- Dzro fexexe ƒe mamaa me: 
  - Phantom Fetu: **0.85%** 
  - Netwɔƒe ƒe Gas 
  - Bridging Dɔwɔƒe ƒe Fewo (~**0.3%)**) 
  
  
---

##  **Afɔɖeɖe 5: Trɔ asi le Ðoɖowo Ŋu (Ne èdi)** 
Zi **Trɔ Ðoɖowo** dzi be: 
- Trɔ asi le **glidzaglidza** (si woɖo ɖi **0.3%**, woate ŋu atrɔ asi le eŋu vaseɖe 30%). 
- Dzi **fe siwo woatsɔ aɖo nɔƒe gbãtɔ** ɖe edzi le network siwo me amewo sɔ gbɔ ɖo dzi. 

---

##  **Afɔɖeɖe 6: Ðo kpe Swap dzi** 
- Dzro swap ŋuti nyatakakawo katã me. 
- Zi **Trɔ Fifia** dzi be nàdze asitsatsa la gɔme.

- Le cross-chain swaps gome la, zã wò **transaction ID** kple **Li.Fi Scanner** hena ɣeyiɣi ŋutɔŋutɔ me yeyewo.
