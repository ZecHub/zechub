### Nu si sɔtiawo fia

**Gbagbadzedze** fia be ɖe nugbugbɔwɔlaa léa wò ZEC la. Ne menye ga gbadzɛ o la, efia be ele yiyi ge le gakotoku si dzi nèléna me.

**Shielded ZEC** enye ne woate ŋu axe fe na wò ɖe tsimɔ si me wodzraa ga ɖo la me. Transparent fia be woƒe home kple adrɛswo nyea dutoƒo tɔ le blockchain dzi ko.

**Self-hosting** enye ne àte ŋu azã kɔmpiutaɖoɖo la ɖokuiwò, eye be dɔwɔha aɖeke manɔ eme o.

**Aƒlelawo ƒe fe** meɖea Zcash-dzidzikɔntafe siwo ame aɖe xea na wo le nɔnɔme ɖesiaɖe me la ɖe eme o.

Ne ame aɖe si naa nyatakakawo mele wo ɖem ɖe go o la, wogblɔna be "megblɔe" alo "ŋɔŋlɔmɔnu aɖeke meli na esia wɔwɔ o", ke menye ɖeko wobua akɔnta ko o. Esia mesɔ kple nyagbe siwo nye "ao".

### Amekae wòatia?

Ne èdi be yeakpɔ ɖokuiwò ta eye yeaɖu edzi la, zã **BTCPay Server** alo self-hosted **CipherPay.* Evea siaa nyea nu siwo ŋu wotrɔ asi le kple mɔ̃ɖaŋunuwo. Wonye open source, eye womekpɔa ga aɖeke ɖe asiwò o.

Ne èdi be yeaxɔ ga le fiase me tsɔ wu Internet dzi la, zã **Flexa**.

Ne èdi be yeazã Internet dzi mɔ si ŋu wotona wɔa nu sia la, zã **NOWPayments** alo **Plisio**.

One caveat worth repeating: a transparent-only processor publishes every payment amount and address on the blockchain. And with any hosted non-custodial processor you hand over your viewing key, so the company can see your payments even though it cannot spend them. Self-hosting is the only way to avoid that.

### Xexlẽdzesi sia ƒe nyateƒenyenye dzi kpɔkpɔ

Privacy coin support moves around, eyata axa sia nyo abe alesi eƒe dzedzeme va yi ene. Ne èle ekpɔm ɖa la:

1. Kpɔ nuƒlela ƒe ga ŋuti nuŋlɔɖi alo API. Ame etɔ̃liawo tɔ me nyawo do xoxo le nugbugbɔgawɔla eve siwo woɖe ɖa la ŋu.
2. Kpɔe ɖa be adrɛs ƒomevi siwo nye Zcash la ƒe ɖewoe le edzi. "Adrɛs si dzi wokpɔna" fiaa nu siwo me woate ŋu akpɔ nyawo ɖo ko zi geɖe.
3. Wɔ ŋkeke si dzi wowɔa numekuku le la ŋuti nyatakakawo ŋu dɔ le taɖodzinu kple nufiala ma ƒe akpa sia.
