# <img src="/content-images/ZEC-USD-a2189a84b9.webp" alt="Alt Text" width="50"/>   Non-Custodial Exchanges

[Zcash Nusiwo Menye Custodial Exchanges O](/dex)

Le cryptocurrency asitsatsa ƒe xexe si le tɔtrɔm ɣesiaɣi me la, asitsaƒe siwo menye ga si wotsɔ dzraa gae o, siwo woyɔna hã be Decentralized Exchanges alo DEXs, naa ezãlawo dzraa nu evɔ wometsɔa woƒe ga dea asi na asitɔtrɔ ƒe akɔnta o. Èdzraa wò ŋutɔ wò safuiwo ɖo, gake mefia be ame bubu aɖeke mele eme o. Le mɔa nu la, swap ateŋu ato nyatakakadzraɖoƒe alo gakotoku app, mɔzɔzɔ ƒe dɔwɔƒe, smart contracts, solvers kple bridges dzi.

Asitɔtrɔ siwo míeyɔ ɖe etame la na nèxɔa Zcash tso wò ŋutɔ wò gakotoku me eye nèdzraae. Alesi swap nye ame ŋutɔ tɔ nɔ te ɖe subɔsubɔdɔa, network si dzi nèxea fe tsoe, kple nenye be wò ZEC va wu enu ɖe adrɛs si wokpɔ ta na dzi. Akpa siwo le ete la ɖe vovototoa me.

### **Nu Siwo Menye Vidzikpɔkpɔ ƒe Nuɖɔliɖɔli Gɔmesese**

Nudzraɖoƒe siwo menye gaxɔgbalẽviwo o, siwo woyɔna hã be Decentralized Exchanges (DEXs) nye mɔnu siwo naa cryptocurrency asitsatsa nɔa bɔbɔe evɔ mehiã be ezãlawo nade woƒe ga asitsaƒea ŋutɔ o. Ke boŋ ezãlawo kpɔa ŋusẽ ɖe woƒe safui siwo le wo ɖokui si dzi eye wodzraa wo tso woawo ŋutɔ ƒe gakotokuwo me. Cross-chain swaps gakpɔtɔ ɖoa ŋu ɖe akpa bubuwo ŋu be woayɔ nya, aɖo mɔ eye woakpɔ asitsatsa la gbɔ (kpɔ ete).

Esia ate ŋu ana dedienɔnɔ nanyo ɖe edzi, elabena ezãlawo mele ŋu ɖom ɖe asitɔtrɔa ŋu be woalé woƒe nunɔamesiwo ɖe asi o, si ɖea afɔku si le hack alo wo dzi kpɔkpɔ le mɔ gbegblẽ nu dzi kpɔtɔna. Mewɔa swap na ame ŋutɔ tɔ le eɖokui si o. Zi geɖe la, asitsatsa le asitsaƒe siwo menye gaxɔgbalẽviwo ɖɔliɖɔli o zãa smart contracts, siwo nye dutoƒonuwɔnawo, eye dɔwɔƒe si nèzãna la ate ŋu akpɔ wò adrɛswo kple kadodo ŋuti nyatakakawo kokoko.

Viɖe vevi aɖe si le cryptocurrency ƒe asitɔtrɔ siwo menye gaxɔgbalẽviwo ɖɔliɖɔli o ŋue nye alesi wokpɔa ŋusẽ ɖe woƒe nunɔamesiwo dzi na ezãlawo geɖe wu. Esi wònye be gaɖɔliƒe siawo meléa nunɔamesiawo ɖe asi o ta la, ezãlawo kpɔa gome le woƒe dijitaal gawo dzi bliboe eye wokpɔa ŋusẽ ɖe wo dzi.

### **Non Custodial Exchanges Vs Custodial ƒe asitɔtrɔ**

**#1 Dedienɔnɔ**: Gaɖɔliɖɔli siwo menye gaxɔgbalẽviwo o ɖea alesi wòhiã be woadzra ga ɖo ɖe gaɖɔliƒe gã aɖe me ɖa. Zãlawo léa woƒe safui ɣaɣlawo dzi kpɔkpɔ, si ɖea afɔku si le hacks, amedzidzedze kple amedzidzedze kple platform ƒe kpododonu si ate ŋu ado mo ɖa le gaxɔmenɔnɔ ƒe asitɔtrɔwo me dzi kpɔtɔna. Cross-chain swaps ate ŋu alé ga ɖe asi ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe kokoko le gadzraɖoƒe ƒe adrɛs alo tɔdzisasrã dzi esime asitsatsa le edzi yim.

**#2 Ameŋunyatakakawo**: Zi geɖe la, nusiwo menye amedzikpɔkpɔ ƒe asitɔtrɔ o mehiãa asitɔtrɔ ƒe akɔnta o, eyata zi geɖe la, èdzona le asidede agbalẽ te kple e-mail alo ID dzi. Ema mesɔ kple ŋkɔmaɖemaɖe o. Ga si nède ɖe dzɔtsoƒe network (le kpɔɖeŋu me Solana alo Ethereum) la nye dutoƒo le kɔsɔkɔsɔ ma dzi, eye dɔwɔƒea ate ŋu akpɔ wò gakotoku ƒe adrɛswo, IP adrɛs kple asitɔtrɔ ŋuti nyatakakawo kokoko. Adzamenyawo gbɔ kpɔkpɔ le Zcash ƒe akpa dzi nɔ te ɖe afisi wò ZEC ɖi le dzi (kpɔ ete).

**#3 Decentralization**: Nusiwo menye decentralization exchanges o sɔ kple decentralized ethos si le cryptocurrencies me wu. Zãlawo ƒe ɖokuisinɔnɔ kple ŋusẽ geɖe wu le woƒe asitsatsadɔwo dzi, le ɖekawɔwɔ me kple gɔmeɖose siwo keke ta wu le blockchain mɔ̃ɖaŋununya me.

Ne míeƒo nu tso Custodial Exchanges ŋu la, Decentralization ƒe seƒe nɔa sue ŋutɔ zi geɖe le centralized exchanges akpa gãtɔ me si naa exchange team alo amegã siwo kpɔa zãlawo ƒe nyatakakawo alo nyatakaka siwo le exchange la dzi la dona.

**#4 Trɔtrɔ ɖe Sedede siwo le Tɔtrɔm Ŋu**: Zi geɖe la, asitɔtrɔ siwo menye gaxɔmenɔnɔ ƒe asitɔtrɔwo o la te ŋu trɔna ɖe sedede ƒe nɔnɔme siwo le tɔtrɔm ŋu wu. Esi wònye be womeléa ga si zãlawo zãna ɖe asi o ta la, ɖewohĩ kuxi ʋɛ aɖewo koe anɔ wo ŋu le sedziwɔwɔ me ne wotsɔe sɔ kple ga si wotsɔ ɖɔa li gae.

**#5 Nu yeyewo dodo ɖe ŋgɔ kple dodokpɔ**: Nusiwo menye gaxɔmenɔnɔ ƒe asitɔtrɔwo ʋãa nu yeyewo wɔwɔ zi geɖe le crypto teƒea. Wodea mɔ̃ɖaŋununya siwo wotsɔ dea asi na amewo ƒe ŋgɔyiyi ƒe dzi ƒo, abe asitsaha siwo wowɔna le wo ɖokui si (AMMs) kple ganyawo ƒe dɔwɔƒewo (DeFi) dɔwɔɖoɖowo ene.

**#6 Xexeame Katã ƒe Mɔnukpɔkpɔ**: Zi geɖe la, gaɖɔliɖɔli siwo menye gaxɔgbalẽviwo ɖɔliɖɔli o naa mɔnukpɔkpɔ ezãlawo le xexeame godoo, eye nuto siwo me sedede ƒe mɔxenuwo ate ŋu axe mɔ ɖe gaxɔgbalẽviwo ɖɔliɖɔli ƒe dɔwɔnawo ƒe anyinɔnɔ nu hã le.

**#7 KYC ƒe Nudidi aɖeke Meli o**: Ame geɖe siwo menye gaxɔmenɔnɔ ƒe asitɔtrɔwo mebiaa amenyenyegbalẽwo do ŋgɔ o. Wo dometɔ akpa gãtɔ gakpɔtɔ léa gakotoku ƒe adrɛswo ɖe sedziwɔwɔ ƒe nyatakakadzraɖoƒewo ŋu, eye woate ŋu ahe asitɔtrɔ ɖe megbe, axe mɔ ɖe enu alo agbe ne wotsɔ aflaga ɖo nane ŋu. Kpɔ subɔsubɔdɔa ƒe ɖoɖowo ɖa hafi nàɖo ŋu ɖe eŋu.

### **Nusi Zcash Takpɔna Kple Nusiwo Mekpɔa Ta O**

Zcash adzamenyawo tsoa adrɛs siwo ŋu wokpɔ ta na gbɔ. Ne ZEC ʋu le adrɛs siwo wokpɔ ta na dome la, wotsɔa nya ɣaɣlawo dea ame si ɖoe ɖa, amesi xɔe, ga home kple nuŋlɔɖi la ɖe Zcash kɔsɔkɔsɔa dzi. Kpɔ [Ta Siwo Wotsɔ Akpoxɔnu Wɔe](/using-zcash/shielded-pools) le alesi esia wɔa dɔe ta.

Akpa aɖewo le swap si Zcash mate ŋu aɣla o:

- **The source network.** Ga siwo nèɖona ɖa tso Solana, Ethereum alo dutoƒo kɔsɔkɔsɔ bubu gbɔ la dzena le kɔsɔkɔsɔ ma dzi, eye wò adrɛs kple ga home hã le eme.
- **Adrɛs si xɔa.** Swap mɔ aɖewo tsɔa ZEC yia adrɛs si me kɔ. Le kpɔɖeŋu me, Near Intents ŋlɔ ZEC be wodo alɔe [adrɛs siwo me kɔ ɖeɖeko](https://docs.near-intents.org/resources/chain-support). ZEC si woɖo ɖe adrɛs si me kɔ (t1 alo t3) dzi nye dutoƒo, abe Bitcoin ene. Etakpɔnu emegbe kpɔa nusi nèwɔna emegbe ta, gake ga si nètsɔna yina kple ametakpɔnu ƒe asitsatsa la nɔa dzedzem.
- **Dɔwɔƒea.** Dɔdamɔnua kple mɔfiame dɔwɔƒe ɖesiaɖe kpɔa adrɛs kple ga home siwo nèna wo, kpakple kadodo ŋuti nyatakakawo abe wò IP adrɛs ene.

Ðo ZEC la ɖe gakotoku si dzi nèkpɔ ŋusẽ ɖo me eye nàkpɔ eta hafi nàzãe. [ZEC Zazã le Adzame](/guides/using-zec-privately) ƒo nu tso afɔɖeɖe siwo kplɔe ɖo ŋu.

### **Totoɖeme**

Nudzraɖoƒe siwo menye gaxɔgbalẽviwo ɖɔliɖɔli o, alo DEX, na nète ŋu dzraa nu tso wò ŋutɔ wò gakotoku me esime nèle wò safui siwo le asiwò dzi kpɔm. Ema kpena ɖe dedienɔnɔ ŋu, gake ame ŋutɔ ƒe nyawo tsɔtsɔ aɣla nɔ te ɖe mɔa dzi: dzɔtsoƒe kɔsɔkɔsɔa nye dutoƒo, subɔsubɔdɔa kpɔa wò adrɛswo kple kadodo ŋuti nyatakakawo, eye wò ZEC nye ame ŋutɔ tɔ ne enya bɔbɔ nɔ adrɛs si wokpɔ ta na me ko.

Togbɔ be viɖe sẽŋuwo le asitɔtrɔ siwo menye gaxɔmenɔnɔ ŋu o hã la, ele vevie be míalɔ̃ ɖe edzi be woate ŋu ahe kuxiwo vɛ, abe gazazã ƒe nya siwo ate ŋu ado mo ɖa kple nusɔsrɔ̃ ƒe mɔnu si me kɔ wu na ezãla siwo si nuteƒekpɔkpɔ boo aɖeke mele o ene.

Abe alesi wòle le ganyawo ŋuti nyametsotso ɖesiaɖe gome ene la, ele be asitsalawo nalé ŋku ɖe nusiwo wotsɔ ɖo nɔƒe gbãtɔ, afɔku dzi ɖuɖu, kple mɔ̃ɖaŋununya si wonya nyuie ŋu nyuie hafi atiae be woaɖɔli nusiwo menye gadzikpɔkpɔ o kple esiwo woatsɔ adzra gae.
