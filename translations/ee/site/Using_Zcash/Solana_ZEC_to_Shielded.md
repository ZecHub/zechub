# Èxɔ ZEC le Solana dzi? Ɖe eƒo ɣaɣla [shielded] Zcash me

Axawo sia nye na wò ne ZEC va dze le wò Solana gakotoku [wallet] me elabena èle ZCAT alo Solana token bubu si xea ga na ame siwo le eŋu le ZEC me. Mèhia be nàdzra naneke o be nàwɔ esia. Àtsɔ ZEC si le asiwò xoxo la afɔ le Solana adzo yi Zcash gakotoku me, eye wòava ɖo ɣaɣla me.

Míewɔ afɔɖeɖe siawo katã kple gaɖoɖo [transaction] vavã aɖe le 27 September 2026, eye míedze egɔme kple 0.00266336 ZEC le Phantom me. Adzɔwo, ɣeyiɣiwo kple screen siwo le axa sia dzi lae míekpɔ.

## Kpuie

- ZEC si le wò Solana gakotoku me nye **Solana token** (mint `A7bd…QXaS`) si wokpɔ ɖe ZEC vavã dzi 1:1, si NEAR OmniBridge lé le Zcash ɖoɖonu [chain] dzi — "ZEC ƒe pepa" si le Solana ƒe ledger si me nu katã ƒe ʋuʋuɖi [transparent] la dzi, eye womate ŋu aɖe eƒo ɣaɣla le afi ma o.
- Mɔ la ƒe akpa eve le: **ɖe token la ɖe eme** le solswap.org (NEAR Intents), eye nà**doe ɖe aƒe** le near.com yi wò gakotoku ƒe ɣaɣla `u1` gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi [address] dzi. Mègazã Phantom ƒe Swap button o — mate ŋu akpe ɖe ŋuwò o.
- Zã gakotoku si [ZecHub ƒe directory](/wallets) de dzesi be **Ironwood: Dzedze**, eye nàlé SOL sue aɖe ɖe Phantom me na Solana adzɔ [fee].
- Míaƒe dodokpɔ vavã (27 September 2026, 0.00266336 ZEC): emedede la ɖo afi le sekondi 9 me, dododo ɖe aƒe la xɔ miniti 8 kple hã, eye fexexe katã ~0.00025 ZEC + 0.0016 SOL.
- Dedie ƒe se: asidede agbalẽ te [signature] na nyaɖaɖa le `near.com`/`solswap.org` ko ne `intents.near` le nyawo me, mèŋlɔ wò gadzraɖoɖi nya [seed phrase] kpɔ o, eye nàpaste `u1`, menye `t1` o.

## Nu si nèle asi vavã

ZEC si le wò Solana gakotoku me nye token le Solana dzi, menye ga le Zcash network dzi o. NEAR OmniBridge ƒoa e, eye wòlé ZEC vavã le Zcash ɖoɖonu dzi be yeakpɔe ɖe eŋu; ga ƒe lẽ [bridge] la nɔ agbe le Solana dzi tso October 2025. Eƒe Solana afɔ le Wormhole messages kple NEAR Chain Signatures dzi, menye le Zcash nyatakaka si hiã ƒe mɔ [light client] dzi o, eyata Solana ƒe akpa la ƒe dedie ɖe system eve siawo ƒe dedie ŋu. Ameawo yɔa e be "ZEC ƒe pepa". ## Ɖe eƒo

Mɔ la ƒe akpa eve le: de wò ZEC ɖe NEAR Intents me tso Phantom, eye nàdoe ɖe aƒe yi wò Zcash gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi dzi. Lé SOL sue aɖe ɖe Phantom me na Solana adzɔ.

### 1. Ɖe wò ZEC ɖe eme le solswap.org

1. Ʋu Phantom, yi browser tab la me, ŋlɔ `solswap.org` wò ŋutɔ eye nàxɔ wò gakotoku ɖe eŋu.
2. Zi **Deposit**. Ɖo **Asset** ɖe **Zcash**, **Network** ɖe **Solana**, eye nàɖo mɔ la ɖe **Wallet**.
3. De home la (alo zi **Max**) eye nàɖe asi na gaɖoɖo la le Phantom me.

![solswap Deposit screen with Zcash as the asset, Solana as the network and Wallet as the method](/content-images/03-solswap-deposit-425691e62f.webp)

Míaƒe emedede la ɖo afi le Solana gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe [block] me le 15:09:08 (UTC+1), eye solswap fiae be **Wo Wu E** sekondi enyia megbe.

![solswap deposit history showing Completed, +0.0026 ZEC](/content-images/04-solswap-deposit-complete-be5feaf758.webp)

Wò ZEC le wò NEAR Intents balance me fifia. Wò Phantom key dea mɔ na afɔɖeɖe sia sia tso eŋu, NEAR Intents solverwo wɔa delivery la, eye NEAR Intents ate ŋu alé balance aɖe na seŋuti nudzrokpɔkpɔ (kpɔ dzideƒo ƒe nuŋlɔɖiwo siwo le ete).

### 2. Dɔe yi wò Zcash gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi dzi le near.com

solswap hã kpɔ **Withdraw** axa aɖe, gake mewɔ dɔ na mí o. **Received amount** kple **Fee** nɔ "–" eye button la mewɔ naneke o, ne míetia Zcash alo Solana abe network ene hã.

![solswap Withdraw form with the received amount and fee stuck at a dash](/content-images/05-solswap-withdraw-blank-92c6e64c65.webp)

Ne esia dzɔ na wò la, wò ZEC menɔ te o. Balance la ɖe wò gakotoku ƒe key ŋu, menye nyatakakadzraƒe la ŋu o, eyata NEAR Intents app sia sia si me ège ɖe eme kple gakotoku ma ate ŋu awɔ dɔ kple e. Míewu le near.com:

1. Yi `near.com` eye nàge ɖe eme kple Phantom gakotoku ma.
2. Wò solswap balance adze le **Move legacy assets** te (near.com yɔa balance siwo tso NEAR Intents app xoxowo me be "legacy"). Zi **Withdraw** le ZEC ƒe fli dzi. Mèhia **Move** o.

![near.com Move legacy assets page listing 0.0026 ZEC with Move and Withdraw buttons](/content-images/06-nearcom-legacy-assets-7ee16c5ac4.webp)

3. Ɖo **Network** ɖe **Zcash**, paste wò gakotoku ƒe `u1` gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi abe **Recipient** eye nàsɔsɔ akpata gbãtɔ adẽ kple mamlɛtɔ adẽ kple wò gakotoku.

![near.com Withdraw legacy asset form with Zcash as the network and a u1 recipient, receive at least 0.00233164 ZEC, about 2 minutes](/content-images/07-nearcom-withdraw-724ef22b38.webp)

4. Zi **Review withdrawal**, xle nuŋlɔɖi la eye nàzi **Send**.

![near.com Review send screen: network Zcash, recipient receives at least 0.00233164 ZEC, fee 0 ZEC, you pay 0.00266336 ZEC](/content-images/08-nearcom-review-b6053f675b.webp)

5. Phantom bia be nà**Sign Message** na near.com. Asidede agbalẽ te siae de mɔ na NEAR Intents be wòaƒo wò balance. SOL adzɔ aɖeke mele eme o, gake esia menye be mekpɔ afɔku o: nyatakakadzraƒe si wotu abe esi vavã ene ate ŋu afia bia ma ke eye wòakɔ wò NEAR Intents balance katã kple e. Hafi nàzi **Confirm**, kpɔ nu siawo katã, eye nàzi **Cancel** ne ɖeka pɛ ko nɔ vɔ̃:
   - Nyatakakadzraƒe si ƒe ŋkɔ le biaɖaɖa la dzi nye `near.com`. (Emedede le afɔɖeɖe 1 nye gaɖoɖo Phantom ƒe biaɖaɖa le `solswap.org` gbɔ; kpɔ ŋkɔ ma hã aleke ke.)

Nàtsɔ asikplɔ-dzralá [custodial] na ame bubu eye zi geɖe nàtsɔ wò ameŋkɔ hã ana, eye gatrɔƒewo geɖe dɔa ZEC yi `t1` gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo ko. [Nu si gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe explorer ate ŋu akpɔ](/zcash-tech/what-a-block-explorer-can-see) ɖe field bubuwo ŋu.

### Ne èpaste `t1` gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi

Míemedo ɖe esia dzi o, gake emegbe ƒe nu ate ŋu anya. ZEC ɖoa afi le wò gakotoku ƒe nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi balance me, eye explorer la fiaa wò `t1` gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi kple home la na ame sia ame, tegbee. Gakotoku si wɔa ɣaɣla le eɖokui si la avaƒo eɖe gaƒuƒoƒo si wɔ ɣaɣla me; ne menye o la, zi **Shield**, si xɔa network ƒe adzɔ sue aɖe. Gaɖoɖo si wɔ ɣaɣla la hã nye nu si me nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi le, elabena wozãa ga tso wò `t1` gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi me. Nu aɖeke mebu o, gake kadodo si le emedede ma kple wò gakotoku dome nɔa le ɖoɖonu la dzi. Paste `u1`.

## Nàle dedie

Ame siwo xɔ token yeyewo nye target. Scam ƒomevi siwo katã nàkpɔ geɖe wu lae nye siawo:

- **Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ƒomevi si mele te o.** Zcash gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi dzea egɔme kple `u1`, `t1`, `zs` alo `tex1`. Solana gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi mekpɔ prefix aɖeke wo dome o. Mègana ZEC dzɔdzɔe nayi Solana gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi dzi o, eye mègana Solana token la nayi Zcash gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi dzi o.
- **Subɔsubɔ siwo zãa nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi ko.** Ga ƒe lẽ aɖewo, ɖɔli ɖɔli ƒe nyatakakadzraƒewo kple gatrɔƒewo ate ŋu adɔ ga yi `t1` gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo ko. Esia ate ŋu awɔ dɔ ne nàƒo ZEC la ɣaɣla enumake ne wòɖo afi. Mègale eŋu afi ma o.
- **Gakotoku mawɔmawo.** Ɖo tso link si le [gakotoku ƒe directory](/wallets) card dzi alo app store ƒe listing vavã si card la fia la ko. Crypto gakotoku ƒe app mawɔmawo doa ɖe app storewo me, eye wonɔa abe vavã la ene tututu.
- **Gadzraɖoɖi nya ƒe phishing.** Gakotoku aɖeke, ga ƒe lẽ, ɖɔli ɖɔli ƒe nyatakakadzraƒe, support agent, moderator alo airdrop mehia wò gadzraɖoɖi nya o. Asidede agbalẽ te mehia be nàŋlɔe o. Ame sia ame si bia e le mɔ dzrom be yeaƒu nu tso asiwò. [Ga ƒe gaxɔxɔ](/using-zcash/recovering-funds) ɖe scam sia ƒe "míagaxɔ wò gakotoku" ƒomevi ŋu.
- **Token mawɔmawo kple "claim" nyatakakadzraƒewo.** Token siwo yɔ be ZEC, Zcash alo nu si sɔ kplii doa ɖe Solana gakotokuwò me ne mèbia o, zi geɖe kple link be nà"claim" bubu. Ne èxɔ wò gakotoku ɖe link ma ŋu la, woate ŋu akɔe. Kpɔ contract ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi tso axa sia ƒe gɔme eye nàtsɔ nu bubuwo katã ɖe aga.
- **Asidede agbalẽ te ƒe biaɖaɖa vɔ̃wo.** "Sign Message" ƒe biaɖaɖa ate ŋu aƒo wò NEAR Intents balance ne SOL adzɔ aɖeke mele o hã. Ɖe asi na nya le `near.com` alo `solswap.org` ko, eye ne `intents.near` ƒe ŋkɔ le nya la me ko (afɔɖeɖe 5 le etame fia nu si nàkpɔ).
- **Nyatakakadzraƒe siwo wotu abe vavã la ene.** Ŋlɔ `solswap.org` kple `near.com` wò ŋutɔ alo zã bookmarkwo. Mègayi ɖe linkwo siwo tso DMwo, replywo alo adwo me o.

## Vodadawo Si Wozãna

- **Zã Phantom ƒe Swap button** ɖe teƒe be nàzã emedede ƒe mɔ la. Èle token la xoxo — ɖɔli ɖɔli tsɔa "ZEC ƒe pepa" aɖe trɔa token bubu, eye meɖe wò ɖe ZEC dzɔdzɔe gbɔ o.
- **Paste `t1` gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi**, alo `u1` si ɣla receiver si me nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi le, ne wò gakotoku ƒea `u1` si wɔ ɣaɣla ko na wò. Emedede la ɖoa afi le ŋkume eye wòkadodoe kple wò gakotoku tegbee.
- **Bu be ga nɔ te** ne solswap ƒe Withdraw axa fia "–". Balance la yia kple wò Phantom key, menye nyatakakadzraƒe la o — wu dododo ɖe aƒe la le near.com.
- **Ɖe asi na nya ne mèkpɔe o.** Nyatakakadzraƒe si wotu abe vavã la ene ate ŋu akɔ wò NEAR Intents balance kple asidede agbalẽ te ɖeka. Nyatakakadzraƒe la ele be wòanye `near.com` eye nya la ele be `"verifying_contract": "intents.near"` nanɔ eme.
- **Ɖe asi le ticker eve siwo yɔ be "ZEC" la ŋu.** Solana token la kple Zcash dzɔdzɔe ZEC kpɔ ŋkɔ ɖeka, gake network ɖeka menye wo tɔ o — ne èdɔ aɖeke wo dome yi ɖoɖonu bubu dzi la, àbu e.
