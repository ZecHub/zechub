# Adrɛs Siwo Wowɔ Ðekae

> Adrɛs ɖeka na Zcash. Mae, eye ame si ɖoe ɖa la ƒe gakotokua akpɔ mɔ si dzi woato axe fe na wò le adzame wu.

Adrɛs ɖeka (UA) nye Zcash adrɛs ɖeka si ƒoa xɔla geɖewo nu ƒu ɖe ɖeka me. Xɔla nye adrɛs ƒe akpa siwo xɔa ga le asixɔxɔ ƒe ƒuƒoƒo aɖe koŋ me: Ironwood, Sapling, kple transparent. UA aɖe dzea egɔme kple `u1` le mainnet dzi eye woɖe egɔme le [ZIP 316 ƒe xexlẽdzesi](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0316), si woto vɛ kple NU5 network ƒe tɔtrɔ le May 31, 2022 dzi.

Do ŋgɔ na adrɛs siwo wowɔ ɖekae la, ZEC xɔxɔ fia be woabu adrɛs ƒomeviwo ŋu. A `zs` adrɛs xɔ Sapling ga, a `t` adrɛs xɔ ga si me kɔ, eye eɖoɖo ɖe ƒomeviwo dome ɖe afɔ bubuwo. UA aɖe wu ema nu. Èma adrɛs ɖeka, eye gakotokuawo wɔa dɔ susɔea.

## Alesi wòwɔa dɔe

Ne ame aɖe ɖoe ɖe wò UA la, woƒe gakotokua xlẽa nuxɔla siwo le eme eye wòtiaa tsita si nye ame ŋutɔ tɔ wu si gakotoku eveawo siaa doa alɔe:

1. Ne gakotoku eveawo siaa doa alɔ tsita si wokpɔ ta na fifia la, fexexea va ɖoa ta si wokpɔ ta na. Naneke mele dutoƒo ka asi kɔsɔkɔsɔa ŋu o.
2. Ne ame si ɖoe ɖa la ate ŋu aɖoe ɖa tso ta xoxo aɖe me ko la, gakotokua gadzea anyi ɖe nuxɔla nyuitɔ kekeake si mi ame evea miewɔna, abe Sapling.
3. Ne ga si me kɔ koe amesi ɖoe ɖa la ate ŋu aɖoe ɖa la, fexexea adze ɖe wò UA ƒe gaxɔla si me kɔ la me, eye wò gakotoku ate ŋu akpɔ eta tso afima.

Wò ŋutɔ mèkpɔa esia gbɔ gbeɖe o. Adrɛs la nɔa nenema ke; mɔzɔzɔa dzɔna le eme.

## Nusita wòle vevie fifia

Zcash yi edzi le tɔtrɔm ɖe edzi. Esi Ironwood ta la wɔ dɔ kple NU6.3 le July 28, 2026 dzi la, UA tɔwo mehiã adrɛs yeye o: ɖeko gakotokuwo dze Ironwood xɔla la tiatia gɔme le adrɛs siwo le amewo si xoxo la me. Ele be zãla siwo si adrɛs xoxo siwo le wo ɖokui si le naʋu. UA xɔlawo mewɔ naneke o.

Esiae nye aɖaŋua ƒe taɖodzinu. Ta yeyewo va ɖona kple etsɔme network ƒe ɖɔɖɔɖowo, eye adrɛs si wɔ ɖeka xɔa wo evɔ ame aɖeke megama adrɛswo alo tsɔa ga tsɔa ga yia teƒe bubuwo o. Kpɔ [Zcash ƒe asixɔxɔ ƒe ƒuƒoƒo](/using-zcash/shielded-pools) na tadeaguƒe ƒe hatsotso si li fifia.

## Adrɛs yeye na amesiame si ɖoe ɖa

Gakotokuwo ate ŋu awɔ adrɛs vovovo geɖe siwo wowɔ ɖekae na akɔnta ɖeka. Wo dometɔ ɖesiaɖe xea fe ɖe ga ɖeka ma ke me, gake womate ŋu atsɔ wo dometɔ eve aƒo ƒu ɖe kɔsɔkɔsɔ me o. Na UA vovovo ame alo dɔwɔƒe ɖesiaɖe si xea fe na wò, eye wo dometɔ eve aɖeke mate ŋu atsɔ adrɛswo asɔ kple wo nɔewo asrɔ̃e be yewoxea gakotoku ɖeka o.

## Dɔwɔnu siwo do ƒome kplii

- **Safuiwo kpɔkpɔ.** Safuiwo kpɔkpɔ ɖekae le akɔnta ɖesiaɖe si siwo ma nusi UA xɔna (alo nusianu si wòɖona ɖa) evɔ womema gazazã ƒe ŋusẽ o. Gɔmedzenyawo `uview` kple `uivk`. Kpɔ [nukpɔkpɔ ƒe safuiwo](/zcash-tech/viewing-keys).
- **TEX adrɛswo.** Asitɔtrɔ aɖewo, siwo Binance, bia be gadede gadzraɖoƒewo natso teƒe si me kɔ. TEX adrɛs aɖe ([ZIP 320 ƒe xexlẽdzesi](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0320)) nye zɔhɛ si me kɔ-ko na wò UA na nya ma tututu. Kpɔ [adrɛs siwo wotsɔna ɖɔa li wo nɔewo siwo me kɔ](/using-zcash/transparent-exchange-addresses).
- **Fexexe ƒe biabiawo.** Woateŋu atsɔ UA ade fexexe ƒe biabiawo ƒe URI me kple ga home kple nuŋlɔɖi si woŋlɔ do ŋgɔ. Kpɔ [fexexe ƒe biabiawo ƒe URIwo](/using-zcash/payment-request-uris).
