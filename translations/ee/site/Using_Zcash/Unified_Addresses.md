# Gaɖoƒe Ƒe Dzesi Si Woɖo Kpe Ɖeka

> Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka na Zcash katã. Maɖe eƒe, eye ametɔ si le ga ɖom na wò la ƒe gakotoku afia mɔ ɣaɣlatɔ kekeake si dzi wòate ŋu axe fe na wò.

## Nya Vevi Kpuie

* Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi si woɖo kpe ɖeka (UA) nye Zcash gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka si me xɔlawo geɖe le: Ironwood, Sapling, kple nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi.
* Mainnet ƒe UAwo doa egɔme kple `u1`. ZIP 316 ƒo wo ƒe sewo, eye woɖe wo gɔme kple NU5 mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ le May 31, 2022.
* Ametɔ si le ga ɖom la ƒe gakotoku tia gaƒuƒoƒo ɣaɣlatɔ kekeake si gakotokuwɔa siawo katã dea alɔ. Mèkpe ɖe gaƒuƒoƒo aɖeke ŋu wò ŋutɔ o.
* Wò UA wɔ dɔ ɖaa esi Ironwood gaƒuƒoƒo dze egɔme le July 2026. Mehia be nàmaɖe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi yeye o.
* Wò gakotoku ate ŋu awɔ UA yeye na ametɔ sia ametɔ si, eye wo katã axe fe ado akɔntɖe ɖeka me.

## Gɔmesese Vevi

Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi si woɖo kpe ɖeka (UA) nye Zcash gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka si ƒo xɔlawo geɖe kpe ɖeka. Xɔlawo nye gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ƒe akpa siwo xɔ ga le asiga ƒe gaƒuƒoƒo aɖe me: Ironwood, Sapling, kple nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi. UA doa egɔme kple `u1` le mainnet dzi, eye woƒo eƒe se le [ZIP 316](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0316) me, esi woɖe egɔme kple NU5 mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ le May 31, 2022.

Hafi gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi si woɖo kpe ɖeka li la, ZEC xɔxɔ gɔme be nàbu gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ƒomeviwo ŋu. `zs` gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi xɔa Sapling ga, `t` gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi xɔa nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi ga, eye dɔdɔ tso ƒomevi aɖe me yi bubu me hia afɔ bubuwo. UA wɔ nu sia nu va yi. Nàmaɖe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka, eye gakotokuwɔawo atrɔ nuwo katã.

### Ale si wòwɔa dɔe

Ne ame aɖe ɖo ga ɖe wò UA dzi la, eƒe gakotoku xlẽa xɔlawo si le eme eye wòtia gaƒuƒoƒo ɣaɣlatɔ kekeake si gakotokuwɔa siawo katã dea alɔ:

1. Ne gakotokuwɔa siawo katã dea alɔ ɣaɣla [shielded] gaƒuƒoƒo si le fifia la, fexexe la ava ɣaɣla. Nusi aɖeke si nye dukɔme kekeake mage ɖoɖonu la dzi o.
2. Ne ametɔ si le ga ɖom la ate ŋu aɖo ga tso gaƒuƒoƒo xoxo aɖe me ko la, gakotoku la atrɔ ayi xɔla nyuitɔ si mia katã miaxɔ, abe Sapling ene.
3. Ne ametɔ si le ga ɖom la ate ŋu aɖo nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi ga ko la, fexexe la adze wò UA ƒe nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi xɔla me, eye wò gakotoku ate ŋu aɣlae tso afi ma.

Mèkpɔa dɔ sia ŋutɔ o. Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi la nɔa nenema; mɔfiafia la dzɔa le eme.

### Nu ka tae esɔ le fifia

Zcash gale mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ ɖaa. Esi Ironwood gaƒuƒoƒo dze egɔme kple NU6.3 le July 28, 2026 la, UA ƒe tɔxɛlawo mehia gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi yeye o: gakotokuwɔawo de asi tɔme be woatia Ironwood xɔla si le gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi siwo woɖe xoxo la me. Ezɔla siwo le gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi xoxo siwo menye ɖeka o la hia be woatrɔ ayi yeyeyɔme. UA ƒe tɔxɛlawo mewɔ naneke o.

Esiae nye tataƒoƒo la ƒe ta. Gaƒuƒoƒo yeyewo ava kple mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ si ava le ŋgɔ, eye gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi si woɖo kpe ɖeka axɔ wo me, be ame aɖeke magaɖe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi maɖe ake o alo nàtsɔ asi atrɔ ga o. Kpɔ [Zcash asiga ƒe gaƒuƒoƒowo](/using-zcash/shielded-pools) be nàkpɔ gaƒuƒoƒo siwo le fifia.

### Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi yeye na ametɔ sia ametɔ si

Gakotokuwɔawo ate ŋu awɔ gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi si woɖo kpe ɖeka vovovowo geɖe na akɔntɖe ɖeka. Ƒe sia ƒe ɖoa fe ga ɖeka ma me, gake womate ŋu akɔ wo eve aɖe ɖoɖonu dzi o. Na UA vovovo ame sia ametɔ si alo subɔsubɔƒe sia ametɔ si si fe ga na wò, eye womate ŋu atsɔ gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo asɔ kple wo nɔewo be woanya be wote fe ɖe gakotoku ɖeka me o.

## Nɔnɔmetata / Ɖeɖeɖegbegbe

Bu ŋutɔ be xɔa ƒe mɔnu ŋgɔ le kposoƒe etɔ. Kposoƒe yeye kekeake la tsɔa agbalẽwo dea dzidzeƒe si wokplɔ ɖe ɖa me (Ironwood). Evelia la tsɔa wo dea akpakpa si wokplɔ ɖe ɖa me (Sapling). Etɔlia la tsɔa wo dea tsiƒe si le ʋuʋuɖi me, si ame sia ametɔ si ate ŋu axlẽ to fenetre me (nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi).

Ame si tsɔa agbalẽ vɛa la nye ametɔ si le ga ɖom la ƒe gakotoku. Wòtsɔa kposoƒe ɣaɣlatɔ kekeake si wòate ŋu age ɖo eme. Nàtsɔ gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka na mɔnu la, eye ne xɔa xɔ dzidzeƒe yeye la, gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi si le mɔnu la dzi anɔ nenema.

## Nusrɔ̃nu Kpɔtɔ

### Dɔwɔnu siwo ƒo kple eŋu

- **Nukpɔsafuiwo.** Akɔntɖe sia akɔntɖe si me UA le la si nukpɔsafui siwo woɖo kpe ɖeka, siwo maa nya si UA xɔ (alo nu katã si wòɖo) fia me, ke womefia mɔƒeɖeɖe be woazã ga o. Gɔmedzeƒeawo nye `uview` kple `uivk`. Kpɔ [nukpɔsafuiwo](/zcash-tech/viewing-keys).
- **TEX gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo.** Gatrɔƒewo aɖewo, si nye Binance le fifia, bia be ga si woɖea ɖe wo me la nava tso nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi ƒe aƒe aɖe me. TEX gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ([ZIP 320](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0320)) nye nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi-ko ƒe kplɔla na wò UA na nyateƒe ma ko. Kpɔ [nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi gatrɔƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo](/using-zcash/transparent-exchange-addresses).
- **Fexexe ƒe biabiawo.** Wòate ŋu akaka UA ɖe fexexe ƒe biabia URI aɖe me kple gaƒe kple nya kpui si woɖo ɖe eme xoxo. Kpɔ [fexexe ƒe biabia URIwo](/using-zcash/payment-request-uris).

### Nya vevi kpuiewo

| | |
|---|---|
| Gɔmedzeƒe | `u1` (mainnet) |
| Nɔnɔme | bech32m |
| Woƒo eƒe se le | [ZIP 316](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0316) |
| Woɖe egɔme | NU5, May 31, 2022 |
| Eme le | Ironwood, Sapling, kple nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi xɔlawo |
| Gazazãsafui | Ɖeka na akɔntɖe sia akɔntɖe, si UAwɔa katã ƒe akɔntɖe ma tɔxɛla zãa |

Kpɔ gakotokuwɔa siwo dea alɔ gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi si woɖo kpe ɖeka le [gakotokuwɔawo](/wallets) axa dzi.

## Vodadawo Si Dzɔa Zi Geɖe

- **Mèmaɖe `zs` alo `t` gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi xoxo o.** Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka ko ƒe ɖeɖe va ɖo gaƒuƒoƒo ɖeka me, eye mexɔ yeyeawo o. Maɖe wò UA.
- **Mèna UA ɖeka ma ke ame sia ametɔ si o.** Ame eve siwo fe ga na wò ate ŋu asɔ didienuwo kple wo nɔewo eye woakpɔ gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖeka. Na ametɔ si le ga ɖom la UA yeye tso wò gakotoku me.
- **Mègabu be fexexe sia fexexe si yi UA dzi anɔ ɣaɣla o.** Ametɔ si ate ŋu aɖo nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi ga ko la axe fe ado wò nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi xɔla me, eye fexexe ma nye dukɔme kekeake. Ɣlae tso wò gakotoku me emegbe.
