# Zcash Nutome Aɖaŋuɖoha 

The Zcash Community Advisory Panel, or ZCAP, is a panel conformed by members of the Zcash Community, established as a means to involve the community and soliciting their advisory input whenever the Zcash Foundation needs to make a decision involving or afecting said community. Since it's creation in 2018, the panel has helped define the way to go for the Zcash foundation in many important decisions, some of which include:

[Zcash ƒe Asitsadzesi](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/Elections/blob/master/2018-Q2/General-Measures/monetary-policy.md): Akɔdada sia ku ɖe nenye be ele be Zcash Foundation nati se ƒe nubabla yome kple Electric Coin Company (ECC) be woama Zcash ƒe asitsadzesi la ƒe amesinɔnɔ kple edzikpɔkpɔ

[Ganyawo Ŋuti Ðoɖo](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/Elections/blob/master/2018-Q2/General-Measures/monetary-policy.md): Akɔdada sia ku ɖe nenye be ele be Zcash Foundation naʋli tɔtrɔ aɖewo ta le Zcash ƒe gaŋutiɖoɖoa me, abe gaɖeɖe ƒe agbɔsɔsɔme, ɖoɖowɔɖi si dzi woaɖe afã kple afã, alo ga ƒe asixɔxɔ ƒe agbɔsɔsɔme ene

[ASIC Tsitretsitsi](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/Elections/blob/master/2018-Q2/General-Measures/asicresistance_ballot.md): Akɔdada sia ku ɖe nenye be ele be Zcash Foundation natsɔ ASIC ƒe tsitretsiɖeŋu aɖo nɔƒe gbãtɔ abe asixɔxɔ kple taɖodzinu na Zcash network ene

[Zcash Nutome Gakpekpeɖeŋunakɔmiti ƒe Tiatia](https://zfnd.org/zcg-election-june-2023/): Akɔdada sia ku ɖe Zcash Community Grants (ZCG) kɔmiti me tɔ eve tiatia ŋu, si ƒe dɔe nye be wòalé ŋku ɖe gakpekpeɖeŋunana ƒe aɖaŋuɖoɖowo ŋu ahakpɔ ga na dɔ siwo ku ɖe Zcash ŋu

[Zcash ƒe Gakpekpeɖeŋu Suewo ƒe Ðoɖowɔɖi](https://zfnd.org/launching-the-zcash-minor-grants-program/): Akɔdada sia ku ɖe kpekpeɖeŋunana ƒe biabiawo dzi dada alo gbegbe na Zcash dɔ sue siwo bia ga tso Zcash Foundation gbɔ ŋu

[Zcash Shielded Assets ƒe xɔxlɔ̃ kple eƒe ŋgɔyiyi](https://vote.heliosvoting.org/helios/elections/5dd57b92-01ed-11ec-a0a8-ae3066fac55d/view), si na be woate ŋu azã DeFi ƒe nunɔamesiwo katã le Zcash ecosystem si nye ame ŋutɔ tɔ me.

[Zcash Foundation ƒe Aɖaŋuɖoha me tɔwo tiatia](https://vote.heliosvoting.org/helios/elections/960b8832-60f9-11ed-b96e-ee6991044d9e/view)


Susu si le megbe na ZCAP enye be nutoa me tɔwo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo vovovo siwo si dukɔ vovovowo, akpa vovovowo kple nukpɔsusuwo le ye ɖoe, be woaka ɖe edzi be eyi edzi le eƒe taɖodzinu si nye be yeanɔ Zcash zãlawo kple amesiwo kpɔ gome le eme ƒe gbe kple ɖetsɔleme teƒe, esime wòle kadodo si le ʋuʋu ɖi eye wòto vovo la dzi kpɔm.

Tso october ƒe 2023 me la, ZCAP nye [hamevi 188](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Y-GbVFA1csIgPbvHOR-1ruOlh7YdA-CHvMtd6R8iSdY/edit#gid=0), eye wokekenɛ ɖe enu enuenu. Azɔ hã, Zcash Foundation "keke dzedze ƒe dzidzenuwo ɖe enu na ZCAP me tɔ, si me dzidzenu siwo Zcash habɔbɔa me tɔwo do ɖa hã le".
