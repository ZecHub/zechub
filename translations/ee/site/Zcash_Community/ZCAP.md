# Zcash Ƒome ƒe Aɖaŋuɖoɖo Hamea 

Zcash Ƒome ƒe Aɖaŋuɖoɖo Hamea, alo ZCAP, nye ha si Zcash Ƒome ƒe membawoe wɔ. Woɖo eɖe anyi be woatsɔ aƒe na ƒomea be woadze eme eye woana woƒe aɖaŋu ne Zcash Foundation hiã be wòawɔ ŋuɖoɖo si ku ɖe ƒomea ŋu alo si ahe to eme na eŋu. Tso 2018 si woɖo eɖe anyi la, hamea kpe ɖe Zcash Foundation ŋu be wòana mɔ na ŋuɖoɖo vevi geɖe, siwo dometɔ aɖewo nye:

[Zcash ƒe Trademark](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/Elections/blob/master/2018-Q2/General-Measures/monetary-policy.md): Akɔtɔɖeɖe sia ku ɖe be Zcash Foundation nadia mɔ be yeawɔ se ƒe ƒomekɔkɔ kple Electric Coin Company (ECC) be woama Zcash trademark ƒe nɔnɔviɖoɖo kple dɔdzikpɔkpɔ

[Ga ƒe Ðoɖoɖo](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/Elections/blob/master/2018-Q2/General-Measures/monetary-policy.md): Akɔtɔɖeɖe sia ku ɖe be Zcash Foundation nadze agbagba be woatrɔ nu aɖe le Zcash ga ƒe ɖoɖoɖo me, abe ga si wodoa ƒe kadodo, fetu ƒe afãmama ƒe ɖoɖoɖo, alo asixɔxɔ ƒe kadodo ene

[ASIC Ƒe Dzɔdzɔeƒe Mɔxexe](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/Elections/blob/master/2018-Q2/General-Measures/asicresistance_ballot.md): Akɔtɔɖeɖe sia ku ɖe be Zcash Foundation nade asi ɖe ASIC ƒe dzɔdzɔeƒe mɔxexe ŋu abe nudzɔdzɔ kple taɖodzinu na Zcash network

[Zcash Community Grants Committee Ƒe Amewo Tia](https://zfnd.org/zcg-election-june-2023/): Akɔtɔɖeɖe sia ku ɖe be woatia ame eve le Zcash Community Grants (ZCG) committee me, si wɔa dɔ be yeakpɔ ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu ƒe ɖeɖeviwo ɖa eye wòana wo ga na Zcash-kukuwo

[Zcash Minor Grants Program](https://zfnd.org/launching-the-zcash-minor-grants-program/): Akɔtɔɖeɖe sia ku ɖe be woaxɔ alo woagbe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu ƒe biabiawo na Zcash kuku suewo, siwo bia ga tso Zcash Foundation gbɔ

[Zcash ɣaɣla ƒe nunɔamesiwo [Shielded Assets] ƒe zazã kple wɔwɔ](https://vote.heliosvoting.org/helios/elections/5dd57b92-01ed-11ec-a0a8-ae3066fac55d/view), si na be woazã DeFi ƒe nunɔamesiwo le Zcash ƒe ɣaɣla ecosystem si nye nɔnɔ gɔmeɖeɖe la me.

[Zcash Foundation Board of Directors ƒe membawo ƒe tiatia](https://vote.heliosvoting.org/helios/elections/960b8832-60f9-11ed-b96e-ee6991044d9e/view)


ZCAP ƒe susu nye be woawɔe kple ƒome ƒe memba vovovowo, siwo tso dukɔ vovovowo me, siwo wɔnɔƒe kple tametoto vovovo le. Esia ana be wòanɔ te ɖe eƒe taɖodzinu, si nye be yeatsɔ Zcash zazãlawo kple stake holders ƒe gbe kple dzidzɔ na, le esime wòele network si ʋuʋu eye wòxɔ ame vovovo wɔm.

Le October 2023 me la, ZCAP ƒe memba [188](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Y-GbVFA1csIgPbvHOR-1ruOlh7YdA-CHvMtd6R8iSdY/edit#gid=0) le, eye woɖea eƒe membawo kpɔ zi geɖe. Gawu la, Zcash Foundation "ɖe eligibility criteria la hãkeke na ZCAP membership, eye criteria siwo Zcash community ƒe membawo tsɔ na hã le eme".
