# Zcash Dukɔwo Katã ƒe Dɔdɔlawo

Dukɔwo Katã ƒe Dɔdɔla Ƒe Dɔwɔwɔ ƒe Kpɔɖeŋu la di hadome ƒe tɔwo si wɔa nu nyui na Zcash ƒe ƒomea me, eye wòna ŋusẽ wo be woazu kplɔlawo. Dɔdɔlawo ɖea Zcash ƒe hadome la dzi, wɔa be zazãlawo nadze eŋu, eye wòɖea Zcash ƒe ɣaɣla-mɔnu ƒe nuteƒe dzi.

## Nu ka Dɔdɔla a wɔa?

- Wòɖoa ƒomeƒe ƒe alo nyatɔ ƒe hadɔwɔwɔwo
- Wòle social media dzi ɣe sia ɣi, eye wòwɔa emetɔ ƒe nyawo ku ɖe Zcash ŋu
- Wòtrɔa Zcash ƒe nusɔsrɔ̃wo ɖe tutowo ƒe gbewo me
- Wòkplɔa zazãla yeyeawo kple mɔ̃ ƒe ɖoɖoŋlɔla [developer] yeyeawo ɖe Zcash ƒe ƒomea me
- Wòte ŋu na Zcash le crypto ƒe takpekpewo kple hadome ƒe nudzɔdzɔwo me

Dɔdɔlawo ƒe ablɔɖe le woƒe ŋutɔ ƒe nudzɔdzɔ si woɖoa ɖeɖo ŋu, si na woate ŋu atrɔa ameɖoɖo la be wòanɔ te ɖe woƒe nutome ƒe nɔnɔme ŋu.
