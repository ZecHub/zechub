# Zcash Nutoa me ƒe Dedienɔnɔ

## Nu gbagbewo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe dedienɔnɔ ƒe Kplɔla

Woɖo Zcash Ecosystem Security Lead ƒe dɔa to ZCG ƒe kpekpeɖeŋunana me be woana dedienɔnɔ ŋuti mɔ̃ɖaŋununya tɔxɛ na Zcash ecosystem si keke ta wu — vevietɔ ZCG ƒe kpekpeɖeŋunalawo — le ECC kple ZF godo.

- **Ƒe 2022–2023:** [anyigbadzidzedze](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-ecosystem-security-lead/42090) subɔ abe Nu gbagbewo ƒe Agbenɔnɔ Ŋuti Dedienɔnɔ Ŋuti Kplɔla gbãtɔ ene. Srɔ̃ nu geɖe le [zecsec.com](https://zecsec.com).
- **2024–2025:** ZCG tia [Ŋusẽtɔ Suetɔ](https://leastauthority.com) be woayi akpaa dzi to [RFP](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/rfp-zcash-ecosystem-security-lead-2023/45723). Àte ŋu akpɔ nyatakaka yeyewo [le afisia](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-update-zcash-ecosystem-security-lead/47541).
- **2026:** Shielded Labs [do ŋugbe na Taylor Hornby](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/shielded-labs-engages-taylor-hornby-as-security-consultant/55421) abe dedienɔnɔ ŋuti aɖaŋuɖola ene be wòado ŋusẽ Zcash ƒe dedienɔnɔ ƒe ŋutetewo.

- **Zebra 4.4.1 (May 2026):** [Dedienɔnɔ ƒe ɖɔɖɔɖo vevi aɖe](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zebra-4-4-1-critical-security-fix/55588) si ŋuti woɖe asi le. Míele dzi dem ƒo na node dɔwɔlawo katã be woawɔ ŋgɔyiyi enumake.
- **Zebra 4.3.1 (April 2026):** [Dedienɔnɔ ƒe ɖɔɖɔɖo veviwo, dockerized mining kple CI hardening](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zebra-4-3-1-critical-security-fixes-dockerized-mining-and-ci-hardening/55389) si ŋuti woɖe asi le.
- **Woɖɔ Afɔku Geɖewo Gbɔ (April 2026):** [Woɖɔ Zcash ƒe afɔku geɖewo ɖo dzidzedzetɔe](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/several-zcash-vulnerabilities-successfully-remediated/55388) evɔ makpɔ ŋusẽ ɖe ezãlawo ƒe ga alo ame ŋutɔ ƒe nyatakakawo dzi o.
- **zcashd ƒe Aɖaŋuɖoɖo (April 2026):** [Aɖaŋuɖoɖo be woaɖe zcashd ƒe amedzidzedze ƒe anyigba dzi akpɔtɔ](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/advisory-reduce-your-zcashd-attack-surface-by-shielding-it-behind-zebra/55390) to ʋuwo ƒe mɔzɔzɔ to Zebra dzi.

## Nyaɖeɖefia si Ŋu Agbanɔamedzi Le

Electric Coin Company kple Zcash Foundation siaa wɔ ɖeka kple Agbanɔamedzi Ŋuti Nyatakaka sia [dzidzenu](https://github.com/RD-Crypto-Spec/Responsible-Disclosure/tree/d47a5a3dafa5942c8849a93441745fdd186731e6) kple teteɖeanyi si gbɔna:

> "Zcash nye mɔ̃ɖaŋununya si naa adzamenyawo sesẽ. Wotsɔa nya ɣaɣlawo ɣlana ɖe afisi woɖo tae, eye emegbe wodzraa ga ƒe gɔmeɖoanyia ɖo to kpeɖodzi siwo me sidzedze zero le si woɖo be Zcash tɔ ŋutɔŋutɔ koe ate ŋu awɔ. Ne esia do kpo nu, eye aʋatsokaka ƒe vodada aɖe do tso eme la, woate ŋu awɔ aʋatso vodada ma ŋudɔ mɔ aɖeke manɔmee be blockchain numekulawo nade dzesi nuvlowɔla alo esi." wozã nyatakaka siwo le blockchain la me tsɔ wɔ vodadaa ŋudɔ Rollbacks do ŋgɔ na nya ma, abe alesi wowɔe le dɔ bubu aɖewo me le nɔnɔme mawo tɔgbe me ene, eyata manya wɔ o gblɔ be ewɔe — mɔɖeɖe na ŋgɔyiyi ƒe ƒuƒoƒoa be woatsɔ nya la aɖo nɔƒe gbãtɔ ahakpɔ egbɔ pɛpɛpɛ, gake, abe alesi wòle le CVE-2019-7167 me ene la, míate ŋu atso nya me be míagatsɔ nyatakaka mawo akpe ɖe míaƒe nyatakakawo ŋu na hadɔwɔlawo do ŋgɔ na wo ɖeɖe ɖe go le ɖoɖo nu o, ne míeka ɖe edzi be woate ŋu adze afɔku bɔbɔe."
