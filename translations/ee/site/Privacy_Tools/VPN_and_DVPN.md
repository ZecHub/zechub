# VPN & dVPN

Le xexlẽme ƒe ƒe sia me la, internet dzi ɣaɣla [privacy] kple dedienyenye ƒe dzadzra ɖe teƒe vevie. Virtual Private Networks (VPNs) kple decentralized [decentralized] VPNs (dVPNs) wɔa dɔ vevie le wò internet dzi ƒe nɔnɔwo kple data ƒe haza me. Wiki axa sia me la, míakpɔ vovototo vevi siwo le VPNs tsã ƒe mɔnu kple dVPNs yeye siwo do go dome, dedienyenye ƒe nu siwo wòle be nàbu ne ètia VPN, kple VPN ƒe dɔwɔƒewo ƒe xexlẽme siwo xɔa ɣaɣla-ƒe cryptocurrency, Zcash.

# Nu Ka Ƒe Vovototo Le Wo Dome?

__Virtual Private Networks (VPNs)__: VPNs tsã ƒe mɔnu wɔa mɔ doame si wo ɣe ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ [encryption] na le wò mɔ nu kple server didi si VPN ƒe dɔwɔla kpɔa dzi dome. Mɔ sia ɣlaa wò internet dzi ƒe nɔnɔwo na ame siwo di be woakpɔe, abe hackers, ISPs, alo dukɔ ƒe dɔwɔƒewo ene. Wozãa VPNs zi geɖe na nu siwo abe browsing si me womeka wò ŋkɔ o, content siwo wode mɔ na le anyigba aɖe ko me ƒe kpɔkpɔ, kple cyber ƒe afɔkuwo ƒe ɖeɖe le ŋgɔ ene.

![image223](/content-images/image-223-399e3015e7.webp)


__Decentralized VPNs (dVPNs)__: Le esime VPNs tsã ƒe mɔnuwo ƒe nɔnɔ vovo la, dVPNs zãa blockchain [blockchain] kple peer-to-peer ƒe mɔnu be woawɔ ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ [nodes] ƒe ha nuɖo agni [decentralized] network. Wotoa zãlawo ƒe traffic le ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔwo siawo me, eye esia na wòsesẽ be entity ɖeka ko nakpɔ alo nakpɔ network blibo la dzi. Zi geɖe la, dVPNs wosẽ na censorship wu, elabena authority aɖeke menɔ afi ma be woakpɔ network la dzi o. Wɔɖe nyuie na zãlawo siwo di ɣaɣla kple dedienyenye si le sesẽ wu.

![dvpn](/content-images/dvpn-89698d4cb9.webp)


# Dedienyenye Ƒe Nusiwo Wòle Be Nàbu Ne Ètia VPN

Ne ètia VPN ƒe dɔwɔla aɖe la, ele be nàbu dedienyenye ƒe nusiwo gbɔ:

1. __Ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ__: Kpɔ be VPN la zãa ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ ƒe protocol sesẽwo abe OpenVPN alo WireGuard ene be woaxɔ wò data tso xɔxɔ me.

2. __No-Logs Policy__: Di dɔwɔlawo siwo ƒe no-logs policy sesẽ le, si fia be womahã record aɖeke tso wò internet dzi ƒe nɔnɔwo ŋu o.

3. __Server Ƒe Teƒewo__: Bu server ƒe teƒewo ƒe mama le anyigba me be nàkpɔ content tso nutome vovovo me eye nàɖe latency ɖe anyi.

4. __Kill Switch__: Kill switch aɖe atso wò internet ƒe kadodo me ne VPN ƒe kadodo la dze, be wò data megado go o.

5. __Ɣaɣla Kple Jurisdiction__: Nadi VPN ƒe dɔwɔla la ƒe ɣaɣla ƒe nɔnɔwo kple jurisdiction si te la me, elabena dukɔ vovovowo ƒe data ƒe dzadzra ƒe sewo sɔsɔ o.

6. __Leak Ƒe Ɖeɖe__: Kpɔ be VPN la ɖea DNS kple WebRTC ƒe leaks ɖa be wò ŋkɔ manya nɔ anyi.

7. __Xexlẽme Kple Nɔnɔwo__: Kpɔ xexlẽme kple nɔnɔ siwo dɔwɔla vovovowo naa be nàkpɔ esi sɔ kple wò hiãhiãwo kple wò ga ƒe kadodo.

# VPNs Siwo Xɔa Zcash

Zcash (ZEC) nye cryptocurrency si wowɔ be ɣaɣla nasẽ wu, eye esia ta ame siwo di be woŋkɔ manya le wo ƒe gaɖoɖowo [transactions] me la dii e. Togbɔ be VPN ƒe dɔwɔƒe katã mexɔa Zcash tẽ o hã la, ɖewohĩ aɖewo axɔ cryptocurrency ƒe fexexe [payment] to dɔwɔƒe bubuwo gbɔ. Ke ele be nàbia VPN ƒe dɔwɔla la tẽ na fexexe ƒe mɔnu yeye akpa. VPN ƒe dɔwɔƒe ɖe aɖewo siwo wogblɔ be woxɔa cryptocurrency ƒe fexexe lae nye esiwo:

1. [__Mullvad VPN__](https://mullvad.net/en)
   
   Mullvad VPN nye VPN ƒe dɔwɔla si ame geɖewo bɔbɔe ŋutɔ, si wotsɔa ɣaɣla kple dedienyenye ƒe zãlawo ƒe dzadzra dea asi sesẽe. Enye VPN ƒe dɔwɔla ʋɛ aɖe siwo xɔa cryptocurrency, siwo me 
   Zcash hã le, abe fexexe ƒe mɔnu ene. Mullvad ƒe nɔnɔ veviwoe nye:

   __No-Logs Policy__: Mullvad ɖoa no-logs policy sesẽ dzi, si fia be meɖea data aɖeke tso wò internet dzi ƒe nɔnɔwo ŋu ɖo o.

   __Ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ Sesẽ__: Dɔwɔla la zãa ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ ƒe protocol sesẽwo, WireGuard hã le eme, be wò data nanɔ dedie.

   __Multi-Hop VPN__: Mullvad na mɔ na multi-hop ƒe kadodowo, siwo toa wò traffic le servers geɖewo me be ɣaɣla kple dedienyenye nasẽ wu.

   __Bridge Mode__: Eƒe bridge mode le, si ate ŋu akpe ɖe censorship ƒe mɔnu aɖewo ŋu woato.

   __Account Ƒe Wɔwɔ Si Me Ŋkɔ Manya__: Wona account ƒe number si wowɔ zi ɖeka hã na zãlawo, eye esia ɖea ameŋkɔ ƒe hiãhiã ɖa le ŋkɔŋlɔɖoɖo me.

   __Zcash Fexexe__: Mullvad xɔa Zcash abe fexexe ƒe mɔnu ene, eye esia na zãlawo ate ŋu axe wo ƒe subscription ƒe fe kple ɣaɣla si sesẽ wu.Mullvad VPN.

3. [__Nym VPN (dVPN)__](https://nym.com/)
   
   Nym VPN nye decentralized VPN (dVPN) si ɖoa asi sesẽe le zãlawo ƒe ɣaɣla kple dedienyenye ƒe sesẽ na to network si ɖoa ɣaɣla ŋu. Nym VPN wɔa dɔ to mɔ bubu dzi tso VPNs tsã ƒe mɔnuwo gbɔ, 
   elabena ezãa mixnet, afi si wotoa zãlawo ƒe traffic le ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔwo ƒe network me. Nym VPN ƒe nɔnɔ veviwoe nye:

   __Ha Nuɖo Agni__: Nym VPN nye ha nuɖo agni eye wòƒe source code le ʋuʋu, si ɖea mɔkpɔkpɔ ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu kple censorship ƒe afɔku ɖe anyi.

   __Ɣaɣla Ƒe Mixnet__: Dɔwɔla la toa zãlawo ƒe traffic le mixnet aɖe me, eye esia na wòsesẽ na entity ɖeka ko be wakpɔ alo woadzro network ƒe traffic me.

   __Ɣaɣla Dzesi Ŋɔŋlɔ Ƒe Dɔwɔwɔ__: Zãlawo ate ŋu awɔ ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔwo be woakpe ɖe network la ŋu eye woaxɔ fetu, si na infrastructure la va zu ha nuɖo agni wu.

   __Ɣaɣla Sesẽ__: Nym VPN wowɔe kple ɣaɣla le susu me, eye mehia be zãlawo nana ameŋkɔ ƒe nyawo le account ƒe wɔwɔ me o.

   Na Zcash gakotoku [wallet]-tɔxɛ ƒe routing la, kpɔ [Route Zcash Wallet Traffic Over the Nym Mixnet](./Nym_Mixnet_Wallet_Setup.md).
