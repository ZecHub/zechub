# Penumbra 

Penumbra li be network layer-1 si le ŋgɔ hã, si woɣla blibo, eye wòle asi hele ɖe Cosmos ƒe ecosystem me. Ewɔna be ezãlawo nate ŋu awɔ gaɖoɖo le dedie, awɔ ga ƒuƒoƒo kple token ɖɔli ɖɔli, eye woawɔ asiwo ƒe asiasi, tsɔe womafia woƒe metadata ɖe ɖoɖonu dzi o. Elé gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu si woɣla blibo la ta, ena ɣaɣla [shielded] gaɖoɖowo le web dzi, tsɔe womeɖena ɣaɣla [privacy] alo ha nuɖo agni [decentralization] ŋu o — Penumbra ƒe ɣaɣla le eƒe nɔnɔme gɔme, eye nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi [transparent] gaɖoɖowo alo nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi xexlẽ gaƒuƒoƒo [pool] aɖeke meli o.

## Teknolodzi si wo zã le Penumbra IBC me 

IBC Ƒe Ɖekpekpeɖeŋu: Nu si va le teƒe gbãtɔe nye Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC) protocol, teknolojia yeye si wɔ tɔtrɔ gã, si na Penumbra ƒe dɔwɔwɔ dzi. Ehiã ŋutɔ be woana network la ƒe nudzre ɖe amewo dome ƒe dɔwɔwɔ nakpɔ dzi. IBC hã wɔa dɔ to blockchain network vovovowo ƒe nane be woate ŋu ado go kple woawɔ nyatakakadzidodo. Wu o, IBC wɔa dɔ vevi le asiwo ƒe tsɔtsɔ va yi me, nyatakakawo ƒe mama kple dɔwɔwɔ le Penumbra dome ɖe bɔbɔe me. Nudzre ɖe amewo dome kple teknologi sia na ezãlawo nate ŋu atsɔ token kple asiwo ava ayi to blockchain vovovowo dzi, tsɔe fukpekpe aɖeke manɔ o. Le akpa bubu me la, IBC hã doa asi le nu ƒe dziyiyi [scaling] me, he gaɖoɖowo si wɔna kaba va, wɔa network la ƒe dɔwɔwɔ nyuie, eye wòɖea mɔkeke le Penumbra ƒe network me.

Zero-Knowledge Ɖaseɖigbalɛwo [Proofs] (ZKPs): Penumbra zãa zero-knowledge ɖaseɖigbalɛ [proof] ƒe cryptography teknologi be wòana woakpɔ gaɖoɖo siawo katã dzi, eye woaɖe woƒe nyateƒe afia, tsɔe womafia nyatakaka aɖeke abe ametɔ, ame si xɔe, alo ga si woɖo la ƒe home ene o.

Ha nuɖo agni [Decentralized] Gatrɔƒe [Exchange] kple Ɣaɣla: Le Panumbra network la ƒe titina la, ɣaɣla le. Network la ate ŋu azã ha nuɖo agni gatrɔƒe ƒe mɔ si wɔnɛ be asitsi kple crypto gaɖoɖowo ƒe ɣaɣla nasɔ, eye wòakpɔ ga ƒe nyatakakawo ta hã, eye wana ezãlawo nakpɔ mɔ be woakpɔ nudzɔdzɔ wɔnɛ ɖe woƒe asitsi ƒe mɔnuwo ƒe nyatakakawo ƒe fiamedede ŋu.

## Zã Nyawo [Use Cases]

Dedie gaɖoɖo: Penumbra na ezãlawo mɔ be woado kpekpe ɖe ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo ŋu le crypto asi ƒe ɖe sia ɖe me. Elé Penumbra nye gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu si woɣla blibo la ta, ena ezãlawo wɔa gaɖoɖo le ɣaɣla me, kpɔa ametɔ, ame si xɔe, ga ƒe home, kple asi ƒe ƒomeviwo ta, esi wotsɔ wo va yi to ɖoɖonuwo dzi; ewɔa be gaɖoɖowo nanɔ ɣaɣla me, eye ame aɖeke mate ŋu adze wo yome alo aɖe woƒe nyagblɔɖi afia tsɔe ezãla la ƒe mɔɖeɖe manɔ o. Ele be woagblɔ be Penumbra ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi [address] ɖe sia ɖe ƒe ŋkuɖodɔwɔwɔ ƒe ŋutete le — tututu sia na ezãlawo mɔ be woatia be woafia woƒe nudzɔdzɔ na ame etɔwo si woɖo mɔ na wo be woawɔ dɔ le sewo ƒe wɔwɔ dzi alo ta bubuwo ta. Nu si wɔnɛ nye be Penumbra ƒe gaɖoɖowo wɔa dɔ le kekeli ƒe kabakaba me kple gaɖoɖo ƒe adzɔ [fee] suewo. Akpe na proof-of-stake ƒe gbegbɔgblɔ ɖi [consensus].

Ga Ƒuƒoƒo [Staking]: Ne míetsɔ ŋku kpɔe le dziŋgɔ la, Penumbra di be yeanɔ nuɖuɖu ƒe ɖoɖonu la ƒe tame. Elé nye xexea me ɣaɣla proof-of-stake network gbãtɔ la ta, woƒe ta vevi ɖeka koe nye be woana ɣaɣla nasɔ na delegatorwo, eye woana validatorwo naɖo ŋu. Nu si dze ŋku hã si Penumbra tsɔnae nye PEN ga ƒuƒoƒo token, si doa network la ɖe dedie me, eye wòna token ƒe nɔlawo mɔ be woakpɔ gome le project la ƒe dziɖuɖu [governance] me to ɖoɖonu dzi [on-chain] ƒe akɔntɔwo ƒe mɔnuwo dzi. Delegate ɖe sia ɖe si le network la me xɔa native token si woyɔna be dPEN. Be nànya la, wodea dPENwo ƒe nyawo ɖe ɣaɣla gaƒuƒoƒowo me, eye woate ŋu awɔ asi ɖe wo ŋu abe asi bubu ɖe sia ɖe ene. Ne míekpɔe le ɖoɖonu dzi ƒe dziɖuɖu ƒe akpa la me la, network la ƒe etsɔme le stakeholderwo ƒe asi me esi wòkɔ ɖe proposalwo ŋu eye wogbɔa nye le nyaɖoɖowo me, esi womena project la manɔ entity si woɖo ɖe teƒe ɖeka ƒe asi me o.

Token Ɖɔli Ɖɔli [Swap]: Token Ɖɔli Ɖɔli nye zã nyawo bubu hã na Penumbra. Network la tše ɖe ɣaɣla ɖɔli ɖɔliwo ŋu le crypto asi ƒe ƒomevi ɖe sia ɖe dome — ne wodee ɖo la, wotsia ɖɔli ɖɔliwo katã! Esi ezãlawo zãa ZSwap la, woate ŋu awɔ crypto asi ƒe pairs ɖe sia ɖe ƒe ɖɔli ɖɔli tsɔe womafia nyatakaka siwo le be woanɔ ɣaɣla me abe asitsi ƒe ŋutinya alo ɣeyiɣi ƒe dzesiwo ene o; esia ate ŋu akpe ɖe ga ƒe aʋafia wɔwɔ ŋu. Ne Maximal Extractable Value (MEV) le ɖoɖonu siwo ƒe nuwo le ʋuʋuɖi me la ƒe nɔnɔme la, ɖoɖonu siwo woɣla blibo abe Penumbra ene la MEV mele o, elabena network la ƒe gaɖoɖowo medoa nyatakaka tso ezãla ƒe nudzɔdzɔ ŋu o.

Asi Ƒe Asiasi [Marketmake]: Ɖoɖonu si woɣla blibo la — Penumbra, na asi ƒe asasiwɔlawo ŋutete be woafia nyatakaka tso woƒe asitsi ƒe mɔnuwo kple woƒe nɔnɔmewo ŋu, ɖe teƒe be woafia woƒe nudzɔdzɔ katã na xexea.
