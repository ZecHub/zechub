# Penumbra 

Penumbra nye ɣaɣla [shielded] bliboe, proof-of-stake layer-1 network kple ha nuɖo agni gatrɔƒe [exchange] le Cosmos ƒe ecosystem me. Eƒe interchain ɣaɣla gaƒuƒoƒo [pool] ate ŋu adzra assets tso IBC-connected ɖoɖonuwɔwo [chains] me. Zãlawo ate ŋu atrɔ ga, aƒo ga, aɖɔli ɖɔli [swap] eye woadze asi na liquidity le Penumbra me, esi womana woƒe account ƒe ga siwo le wo ŋutɔwo me kple woƒe dzesi nadze le ame gbogboƒe ŋkume o. Esia menye be network alo DEX ƒe nyatakakawo katã nye ɣaɣla [privacy] o.

## Teknɔlɔdzi si wo zã le Penumbra IBC me 

IBC ƒe ƒomekpekpe: Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC) protocol la ƒoa Penumbra kple IBC-enabled ɖoɖonuwɔ bubuwo. IBC ƒe gaɖoɖo [transaction] si ge ɖe eme na ga nadzu ɣaɣla esi woge ɖe Penumbra me; gaɖoɖo si doa go la na woado ɣaɣla la ɖa esi wodo go. Dɔwɔwɔ si le nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi [transparent] counterparty ɖoɖonu dzi la nɔa ame gbogboƒe ŋkume, eye esia ƒe wɔnawo ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi [address] hã nɔa eme. [ɣaɣla ƒe liƒo la yɔna IBC ƒe liƒo ɖe vɔ](https://guide.penumbra.zone/overview/interchain-privacy).

Zero-Knowledge Ɖaseɖigbalɛwo [Proofs] (ZKPs): Penumbra zãna client-side zero-knowledge ɖaseɖigbalɛwo be woadze ale si encrypted state updates le eteƒe la fia, esi womana zãla ƒe gaɖoɖo ƒe nyatakaka siwo nye eye tɔ nadze le ame gbogboƒe ŋkume o. Gakotokulawo [wallets] scan-na eye woɖea nyatakaka si wofe mɔ kpɔ le woƒe teƒe; ame gbogboƒe ƒe ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ [node] mate ŋu aɖo zãla ƒe ga si nye eye tɔ la ko na wò o.

Ha Nuɖo Agni Gatrɔƒe kple Ɣaɣla: Penumbra wɔ DEX na assets ƒe asitsɔtsɔ le eƒe ɣaɣla gaƒuƒoƒo me. Ɖɔli ɖɔli ƒe emetsonu siwo ƒe ame ɖeka wɔ la nye ɣaɣla, ke batch execution ƒe emetsonuwo kple anonymous liquidity ƒe nɔƒewo nɔa ame gbogboƒe ŋkume. Vovototo siawo le vevie ne wòle gatrɔƒe la ƒe nu siwo wòɣla dometoto.

## Zã Nyawo [Use Cases]

Dedie gaɖoɖowo: Gaɖoɖowo siwo le Penumbra ƒe ɣaɣla gaƒuƒoƒo me la kpɔa dɔmedzila, ame si woxɔa ga, ga ƒe home kple asset ƒe ƒomea ta. Zãlawo ate ŋu afia woƒe dɔwɔwɔ ƒe akpa aɖe na ame siwo wode mɔ na. Nusi wòma woɖo gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi le eɖokui me maɖea eƒe tɔa ƒe ga siwo le eŋu o. Nɔnɔ siawo metsa ɖe dɔwɔwɔ si nɔa ame gbogboƒe ŋkume le ɖoɖonu bubu dzi ne woɖe ga la o.

Ga Ƒuƒoƒo [Staking]: Penumbra ƒe native ga ƒuƒoƒo token nye **UM**. Ne wòde UM ɖe asi na validator aɖe la, wòaxɔ delegation tokens siwo fia akpa si wòle validator ma ƒe delegation gaƒuƒoƒo me. Ga si woƒo le validator sia kple sia ƒe gaƒuƒoƒo me katã nɔa ame gbogboƒe ŋkume, ke ame ɖeka ƒe delegationwo menya ame o. Undelegation na validator-specific unbonding tokens nadze gɔme, eye wole slashing ƒe afɔku me va se ɖe esime unbonding ƒe mɔmɔ nuwuwu. Kpɔ [ɣaɣla ga ƒuƒoƒo ƒe mɔfiame](https://guide.penumbra.zone/overview/staking).

Token ƒe ɖɔli ɖɔli: Woƒoa swap intents ƒu le trading pair nu eye wowɔna wo ƒe batch le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe [block] sia kple sia ƒe nuwuwu. Esia ɖea swap intents ƒe dododo le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe me ɖa le traders ƒe mɔnuwo dome; eya nye mɔnu aɖe tɔxɛ, menye layiyi be MEV ƒe ƒome aɖeke mate ŋu anɔ anyi o. Woŋlɔna batch ƒe emetsonuwo ɖe ame gbogboƒe ƒe ɖoɖonu ƒe state dzi, eye zãlawo xɔna woƒe emetsonu ƒe akpa ɣaɣlae ɖe gaƒuƒoƒo me. [DEX ƒe mɔfiame](https://guide.penumbra.zone/overview/dex) dometoto batch execution kple protocol la ƒe automatic arbitrage.

Asiɖeɖe le asime: Liquidity providers wɔna anonymous concentrated-liquidity ƒe nɔƒewo. Nɔƒewo ƒe liquidity kple trading ƒe dɔwo nɔa ame gbogboƒe ŋkume, ke womakplɔ wo ɖe provider la ƒe dzesi ŋu o.
