Network resilience fia kakaɖedzi kple ŋusẽ si le xɔtuɖoɖo siwo tsɔa Zcash ƒe asitsatsa ŋu. Menye nya ɣaɣlawo ɖeɖeko dzie ame ŋutɔ ƒe asitsatsa nɔ te ɖo o, ke boŋ ɖe network infrastructure si le wo te ƒe anyinɔnɔ kple kakaɖedzi hã dzi.

Ganyawo ƒe ɖokuisinɔnɔ fia ŋutete si le ame ɖekaɖekawo si be woakpɔ woawo ŋutɔ ƒe ga dzi ahakpɔ wo evɔ womaɖo ŋu ɖe mɔɖeɖe tso domenɔlawo gbɔ ŋu o.
