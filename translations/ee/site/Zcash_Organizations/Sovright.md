# Sovright
![Sovright](/content-images/sovright-16a27c0d24.webp)

**Sovright** nye US 501(c3) dzikpɔkpɔ ƒe hadome si mewɔa dzidzedze o, eye wode wɔnunya na amegbetɔwo ƒe wɔnɔaɖokpe ŋu. Wotɔ dɔa gɔme kple ɖe ŋkuɖoɖo ɖe Zcash ƒe nuteƒewɔwɔ, nukuku [mining], protocol ƒe nututu, kple ecosystem ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu ŋu; dɔa tua dɔwɔnu [tools] kple subɔsubɔwo siwo ƒe ta nye be woakpe ɖe asɔzɔ̃ si nye sutɔ digital ga ƒe nuteƒewɔwɔ ŋu, eye woakpe ɖe Zcash ƒe network ƒe sesẽtɔtɔ le ɣeyiɣi didi me ŋu.

Tsã la, womanya Sovright be **Bootstrap**. Ŋkɔ gbagbe la fia be hadome la ƒe tɔgbui ƒe ta gã nye be woatu Zcash ƒe nuteƒewɔwɔ ƒe company tso gɔmedzeƒe ke ŋu.

[Yi ɖe asaɖa me](https://sovright.com/)

## Ŋkɔ

Ŋkɔ **Sovright** nye **sovereign** kple **right** ƒe ƒoƒo. Eɖe hadome la ƒe ɖeŋkuɖoɖo ɖe ame sia ame ƒe wɔnɔaɖokpe, ɣaɣla [privacy], kple mɔkpɔkpɔ be woatia ale si woakpɔ amedzidzime ƒe data, ga, nyadzɔdzɔwo, compute, kple internet dzi nɔnɔ wɔ ɖo ŋu fia.

Sovright ƒe dɔwɔwɔ dze egɔme kple Zcash, ga si ɣaɣla nɔa eŋu eye wowɔe be wòanɔ te, si zãa zero-knowledge cryptography be wòakpe ɖe ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo [shielded transactions] ŋu. Le ɣeyiɣi ƒe yiɖe edzi me la, hadome la ƒe ɖeŋkuɖoɖo hãe tso asikplɔ-dzralá [private] ga ko gbɔ yi nuteƒewɔwɔ ƒe nuxaxa kekeakewo gbɔ, siwo me nye nyadzɔdzɔ siwo le dedie, network siwo sesẽ, compute si ɣaɣla nɔa eŋu, kple AI ƒe kadodo siwo le dedie.

## Nukuku ƒe Gaƒuƒoƒo

Sovright wɔa Zcash ƒe nukuku gaƒuƒoƒo [mining pool] le:

[mining.sovright.com](https://mining.sovright.com/)

Woɖo Sovright Mining Pool ɖe eŋu be wòakpe ɖe nukula [miner] ƒe wɔnɔaɖokpe ŋu eye wòaɖe nukuku gaƒuƒoƒo ƒe ƒoƒo ɖe esi. Nukuku gaƒuƒoƒo ƒe ƒoƒo nye nuxaxa si gbugbɔna le proof-of-work networkwo me, elabena gaƒuƒoƒo ʋee aɖe ate ŋu akpɔ network ƒe hashrate ƒe akpa gã aɖe wɔ ɖe wo ŋutɔ ƒe asi. Esia ate ŋu aɖe ha nuɖo agni [decentralized] ƒe ŋusẽ ɖe esi eye wòakpe ɖe gaƒuƒoƒo ƒe wɔnɔlawo ƒe nɔnɔ ŋu.

Woɖo Sovright Mining Pool ɖe eŋu na solo nukulawo, wɔnɔla suewo, kple ame bubuwo siwo di be woakpe ɖe Zcash ƒe ha nuɖo agni nukuku ecosystem ŋu. Gaƒuƒoƒo la ɖea ŋku ɖe fexexe [payment] ƒe wɔwɔ ƒe logic si me nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi [transparent] nɔe ŋu, ŋkuɖoɖo ƒe ŋutete, kple dɔwɔwɔ ƒe adzɔ [fee] sue aɖe ko ŋu.

Testnet ƒe version si le fifia la menaa fexexewo o, eye woɖoe ɖe eŋu na dodokpɔ ko.

## (Egbɔna enumake)

### Network Ƒe Sesẽtɔtɔ

Network ƒe sesẽtɔtɔ fia nuteƒewɔwɔ si tsɔa Zcash ƒe gaɖoɖowo ƒe wɔnɔaɖokpe kple sesẽtɔtɔ. Gaɖoɖo siwo ɣaɣla nɔa eŋu mele ɖe cryptography ko ŋu o, ke boŋ woɖea asi ɖe network ƒe nuteƒewɔwɔ si le wo te ƒe nɔnɔ kple wɔnɔaɖokpe ŋu hã.

Sovright bua network ƒe sesẽtɔtɔ be enye dɔwɔwɔ ƒe teƒe vevi na Zcash ƒe dedienɔnɔ kple zãzã ƒe ŋutete si wòate ŋu awɔ le ŋutinya me ƒe dzidzedze.

### Ga Ƒe Wɔnɔaɖokpe

Ga ƒe wɔnɔaɖokpe fia ame sia ame ƒe ŋutete be wòakpɔ eye wòaɖe asi ɖe ye ŋutɔ ƒe ga ŋu, ne wòmeɖe asi ɖe mɔtitina ƒe mɔɖeɖe ŋu o. Le Zcash ƒe nɔnɔ me la, esia wɔ ɣaɣla fexexewo, ŋutɔ ƒe asikplɔ, kple nuteƒewɔwɔ si naa zazãlawo ŋutete be woawɔ gaɖoɖo le wo ŋutɔ ƒe nɔƒoɖoewo nu.

Sovright bua ga ƒe mɔkpɔkpɔ kple ga si zazãla ŋutɔ kpɔa ɖe asi be wo nye ame sia ame ƒe wɔnɔaɖokpe ƒe akpa veviwo.
