# <img src="/content-images/Shielded-labs-shield-white-2-27aef199ae.webp" alt="Shielded Labs logo" width="50"/> Shielded Labs

[Shielded Labs](https://shieldedlabs.net/) nye Zcash kpekpeɖeŋunahabɔbɔ si le eɖokui si, si dzɔa ga tso nudzɔdzɔwo me, si le Switzerland. Eƒe dɔdeasie nye be wòatu ame ŋutɔ ƒe ga si womate ŋu atɔ te o si ana ŋusẽ amegbetɔ ƒe ablɔɖe kple ɖokuidziɖuɖu, eye wòalé fɔ ɖe dɔ si ado Zcash ƒe kadodoa ƒe anyinɔnɔ, dedienɔnɔ, kple eƒe dzidziɖedzi ɖe ŋgɔ ŋu.

Shielded Labs tsɔa mɔ̃ɖaŋudɔwɔwɔ ƒe ŋutete si le eɖokui si kpena ɖe Zcash lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ŋu. Menye Electric Coin Company, Zcash Foundation, alo Zcash Community Grants, eye wòɖɔ eɖokui be yenye Zcash ecosystem habɔbɔ gbãtɔ si mexɔ ga tẽ alo le mɔ si mele tẽ o nu tso Development Fund alo block rewards gbɔ o.

## Dɔdasi Kple Akpa si Wòwɔna

Shielded Labs léa fɔ ɖe ɖoɖowɔɖi kple xɔtuɖaŋudɔwo ŋu si ateŋu ana Zcash nate ŋu anɔ te ɖe nɔnɔme sesẽwo nu le ɣeyiɣi didi me. Eƒe dutoƒonuawo te gbe ɖe edzi be:

- Amegbetɔ ƒe ablɔɖe kple ganyawo ƒe adzamenyawo
- Gakpekpeɖeŋunana le wo ɖokui si le Ŋgɔyidɔwo ƒe Gaxɔa godo
- Bubudede ZEC xɔlawo kple gakuxɔlawo ƒe nyawo ŋu
- Zãla ƒe ɖokuisinɔnɔ kple ɖokuisinɔnɔ
- Mɔ̃ɖaŋununya ƒe nu yeyewo dodo ɖe ŋgɔ hena nusiwo li tegbee, dedienɔnɔ, kple dzidziɖedzi
- Nuwɔwɔ aduadu kple Zcash nudzɔla bubuwo

Habɔbɔa kpea asi ɖe eŋu to mɔ̃ɖaŋudɔwɔwɔ tẽ, dukɔa ƒe numekuku, xɔtuɖaŋuwo ƒe kpekpeɖeŋunana, kple lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe ɖoɖowɔwɔ ɖekae me. Eléa dukɔa ƒe nunɔamesiwo hã ɖe te hena dziɖuɖu ŋuti numekuku kple ga si gakuxɔlawo fiaa mɔe, si kpena ɖe Zcash nutoa me tɔwo ŋu be woase ɣeyiɣi kple alesi woate ŋu akpɔ gome le dziɖuɖu ƒe dzesiwo me kple alesi woawɔe gɔme.

## Dɔ siwo Wowɔ Fifia

Shielded Labs yɔ dɔa ƒe akpa geɖe siwo le dɔ dzi le dutoƒo:

- [Network Sustainability Mɔ̃](https://shieldedlabs.net/nsm/) - Tɔtrɔ siwo wodo ɖa ƒe hatsotso aɖe si woɖo be woado alɔ Zcash's dedienɔnɔ ƒe gazazã si anɔ anyi ɣeyiɣi didi esime wole ZEC ƒe nuzazãwo ƒe seɖoƒe si nye miliɔn 21 la ta kpɔm. NSM dɔa ƒe akpa aɖewoe nye lɔlɔ̃nu faa ZEC dzobibi, asiɖeɖe le eŋu ƒe zɔzɔ nyuie, kple fexexe ƒe aɖaŋuɖoɖo siwo ŋu woƒo nu tsoe le ZIP 233, 234, kple 235 me.
- [Crosslink](https://shieldedlabs.net/crosslink-faq/) - Wodo susua ɖa be woawɔ nuwo ƒe ɖɔɖɔɖo si nye hybrid consensus upgrade si aƒo Zcash's proof-of-work block wɔwɔ nu ƒu kple proof-of-stake finality, ale be ZEC xɔlawo nate ŋu akpe asi ɖe network la ƒe dedienɔnɔ ŋu esime tomenukulawo yi edzi le blocks wɔm.
- [Fetu Siwo Ŋu Ŋusẽ Le](https://shieldedlabs.net/fees/) - Numekuku kple dɔwɔnuwo wɔwɔ na Zcash ƒe asitsatsa ƒe fe siwo woate ŋu agblɔ ɖi wu, siwo zazã le bɔbɔe, eye woate ŋu anɔ te ɖe nɔnɔme sesẽwo nu ne network la le tɔtrɔm.
- [Naneke o](https://shieldedlabs.net/zero/) - Zcash xɔtuɖoɖo ƒe kɔmpiuta dɔwɔɖoɖo siwo le ʋuʋu ɖi ƒe ƒuƒoƒo si wodo alɔe na asitɔtrɔwo, tomenukuƒewo, gakotoku nana dɔwɔƒewo, kple habɔbɔ bubu siwo ɖoa ŋu ɖe Zcash xɔtuɖoɖowo ŋu.
- [Ironwood](https://shieldedlabs.net/ironwood/) - Zcash network ƒe ɖɔɖɔɖo kple shielded pool si wowɔ le Orchard aʋatsokaka ƒe afɔkua ŋuɖoɖo me, si wowɔ be wòagbugbɔ Zcash's nusiwo le tsatsam ƒe gbeɖiɖi si ŋu woate ŋu aɖo kpee le wo ɖokui si la aɖo anyi.
- [Epoch](https://shieldedlabs.net/epoch-future-proof-cryptography-for-zcash/) - Numekuku kple mɔ̃ɖaŋudɔ aɖe si woɖe gbeƒãe le October 2026 me be woawɔ dzadzraɖo ɖe nuwɔwɔ ŋu, kakaɖedzi gã le post-quantum cryptography na Zcash eye woakeke cryptographic ƒe akpa veviwo ƒe kpeɖodzi le se nu ɖe enu. Dɔa ɖoe be woawɔe si sɔ na nuwɔwɔ le ƒe 2027 ƒe nuwuwu si ate ŋu anye gɔmeɖoanyi na etsɔme network ƒe ɖɔɖɔɖo. Ekpena ɖe Project Tachyon kple agbagbadzedze bubu siwo wowɔ le quantum megbe le lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe ɖoɖoa katã me ŋu.

## Epoch kple nya ɣaɣlawo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo

Le October 2026 me la, Shielded Labs dze Epoch, si nye agbagbadzedze vevie be woatsɔ akpɔ Zcash le etsɔme ɖa le quantum kɔmpiuta zazã, amedzidzedze siwo ŋu AI kpena ɖo, kple ŋɔdzidoname deŋgɔ bubuwo ŋu. Dɔa ƒe taɖodzinue nye be yeaɖɔli quantum-vulnerable cryptographic components eye yeawɔ kpeɖodzi le se nu ŋudɔ le ɖoɖowɔɖia ƒe akpa veviwo ŋu.

Ame etɔ̃ ƒe nya ɣaɣlawo ŋuti ƒuƒoƒo aɖee le ŋgɔ na Epoch:

- **Ulrich Haböck** – Cryptographer ƒe amegã (enɔ StarkWare; do ŋgɔ le Polygon Zero). Eŋutinunyala le sidzedze zero-sidzedze ƒe kpeɖodziwo kple post-quantum cryptography me; logUp lookup argument ŋlɔla kple Circle STARKs ƒe agbalẽa ƒe kpeɖeŋutɔ.
- **Luke Edwards** – Cryptography Engineer (tsã la, enye mɔ̃ɖaŋudɔwɔla ƒe ŋgɔnɔla le cryptography mɔ̃ɖaŋudɔwɔlawo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo me le Aztec). Exɔ PhD le Akɔntabubu Ŋuti Nusɔsrɔ̃ me le Arizona Yunivɛsiti.
- **Suyash Bagad** – Cryptography Engineer (enɔ Aztec, ewɔa dɔ le Barretenberg agbalẽdzraɖoƒe kple PLONK ƒomea ƒe kpeɖodziɖoɖowo ŋu). Exɔ BTech kple MTech ƒe ɖaseɖigbalẽwo le IIT Bombay; sukunuwulawo ƒe numekuku ku ɖe nusiwo wodzra ɖo ƒe kpeɖodzi siwo kpɔa ame ŋutɔ ƒe nyawo ta ŋu.

Akpa 1 lia ku ɖe numekuku siwo li fifia me dzodzro ŋu, woada xɔtuɖoɖo siwo di be yewoaxɔ ɖoƒea kpɔ le Zcash dedienɔnɔ kple dɔwɔwɔ ƒe nudidiwo nu, eye woawɔ aɖaŋuɖoɖo ŋutɔŋutɔ si woawɔ le agbɔsɔsɔme megbe. Woɖoe be woawɔ dɔ si sɔ na nuwɔwɔ le ƒe 2027 ƒe nuwuwu.

## Ironwood ƒe Nudzɔdzɔ

Le ƒe 2026 me la, Shielded Labs wɔ akpa vevi aɖe le lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe nuwɔwɔ ɖe Orchard aʋatsokaka ƒe afɔkua ŋu me. Shielded Labs ƒe dedienɔnɔ ŋuti numekula Taylor Hornby ye ke ɖe afɔku sia ŋu eye wòɖee fia Zcash Open Development Lab (ZODL), si wɔ ɖoɖo ɖe nuwo ɖɔɖɔɖo kpata ŋu kple Zcash nudzɔla bubuwo.

Le nuwo ɖɔɖɔɖo kpata megbe la, nutoa me tɔwo gahiã mɔnu aɖe si dzi woato agbugbɔ kakaɖedzi le wo ɖokui si ɖe Zcash's nusiwo le tsatsam ŋu. Ironwood ƒo nu tso ema ŋu to ta yeye si ŋu wokpɔ akpoxɔnu le wɔwɔ me to Orchard nutome sue si woɖɔ ɖo zazã me kple to nutrenu na Orchard ta gbãtɔa ale be ga nate ŋu adzo to ʋuƒoa dzi ko. Shielded Labs wɔ dɔ kple Project Tachyon, Valar Group, ZODL, kple Zcash Foundation le ɖoɖowɔwɔ, ŋkuléle ɖe nu ŋu, kple lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe dzadzraɖo na Ironwood.

Dɔ sia ɖe Shielded Labs' akpa vevi ɖeka fia le Zcash: wotsɔ ɖokuisinɔnɔ ƒe ɖoɖowɔɖi-wɔwɔ kple dedienɔnɔ ƒe ŋutete kpe ɖe eŋu le Zcash ŋgɔyiyi habɔbɔ gbãtɔwo godo.

## Dziɖuɖu Dɔwɔwɔ

Shielded Labs hã taa dziɖuɖu ŋuti nyatakakawo na Zcash nutoa me tɔwo. Eƒe dziɖuɖu ƒe axa kplɔa ɖoɖowɔɖi ƒe dziɖuɖu ƒe numekukuwo, ZCAP gomekpɔkpɔ le eme, ga si gaxɔlawo fiaa mɔe, kple akɔdada ƒe gakotoku siwo wodo alɔe la ɖo. Esia kpena ɖe eŋu be Zcash nyametsotsowɔwɔ dzi wɔwɔ nɔa bɔbɔe na gakuxɔlawo, ZCAP me tɔwo, kple nutoa me tɔwo ƒe gomekpɔla siwo keke ta wu.

Zcash mezãa kɔsɔkɔsɔ dzi ɖuɖu le eɖokui si hena ɖoɖowɔɖi ƒe tɔtrɔwo o. Akɔdadawo kple nutoa me tɔwo ƒe nyawo nye aɖaŋuɖoɖo ƒe dzesi siwo naa nuɖuɖu amewo ƒe ɖoɖo si keke ta wu si dzi woda asi ɖo le mɔ si mesɔ o nu. Shielded Labs' dziɖuɖudɔa kpena ɖe ame ŋu be dzesi mawo nate ŋu akpɔ wo bɔbɔe eye woƒe kpeɖodzi nanɔ bɔbɔe.
