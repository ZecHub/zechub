# <img src="/content-images/image-2024-02-03-165803028-2532c6c3ac.webp" alt="Alt Text" width="50"/>  Zcash Foundation

## Misión ƒe Nyaa

Míaƒe taɖodzinu le ZF ŋu nye be míawɔ dɔwɔnu siwo ana network siwo ƒe mɔ le dɔme nakpɔ dziɖuɖu, eye wòame na ame sia ame nakpɔ eya ŋutɔ ƒe ɣaɣla [privacy] ɖe eya ŋutɔ ƒe mɔ nu. Ɣaɣla ƒe gɔme veviŋutɔe nye be nàte ŋu atia nu si woama alo woamama kple amewo. Ɣaɣla ƒo autonomy kple lɔlɔ̃ ɖe eŋu; ele vevie na ame ƒe bubu kple dukɔviha ƒe agbe nyuitɔ ƒe yi ɖe edzi.

## Ameawo kple Hamea

Zcash Foundation wɔ dɔwɔla 12 ɖo alɔ̃, siwo le xexea me godoo. Woɖo ŋku protocol ƒe ŋgɔyiyi, protocol ƒe dzidzɔkpɔkpɔ, protocol ƒe dziɖuɖu [governance], infrastructure ƒe ŋgɔyiyi, kple Zcash ƒe nutoa ƒe nudzɔdzɔwo ƒe kpɔkpɔ dzi. Zcash Foundation ƒe dɔmedzoe menye be wòawɔ dɔ ɖeka ko na Zcash o, eyata wògblɔna ɣaɣla ƒe mɔnu siwo wɔnɛ be woakpɔ ɣaɣla ɖe edzi na [amegbetɔ ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu](https://zfnd.org/sai/) kple dukɔ blibo ƒe nyuie.

Zcash Foundation ƒe Ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu [grant] na ecosystem la mɔ be wòaxɔ ga na dɔwɔwɔ siwo kpe ɖe ɣaɣla ƒe ecosystem ŋu, abe [Zebra](https://zebra.zfnd.org/) (ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ [node] ƒe dɔwɔɖoɖo si le eɖokui ŋutɔ me na Zcash) & [FROST](https://frost.zfnd.org/) kple dɔwɔwɔ bubu [vovovowo](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2021/06/08/tor-project-receives-670k-from-zcash-open-major-grants-for-coding-language-upgrade/) to [Zcash Community Grants Committee](https://zcashcommunitygrants.org/) dzi. 

Foundation la hã wɔa [Zcon](https://zfnd.org/zcon/), ƒomevi ƒe takpekpe si wɔna ɖe ŋkume le ƒe sia ƒe, si ɖo ŋku Zcash ecosystem la ŋu, eye wòkpena ɖe nutoa ƒe dɔwɔwɔ siwo tso amewo ŋutɔ gbɔ la ŋu, abe [Zcon Vozes](https://zfnd.org/zcon-voices/), nutome tɔxɛ ƒe [takpekpewo](https://zfnd.org/zcash-dev-summits/), Zcash Foundation A/V [Club](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/the-zero-knowledge-audiovisual-club/43733), si nye Zero Knowledge Audio Visual [Club](https://zkav.club/) fifia, kple nutoa ƒe [yɔyɔwo](https://zfnd.org/arborist-calls/) siwo ʋu na ame sia ame, nutoa ƒe [nudzɔdzɔwo](https://zfnd.org/zf-events/#:~:text=Zcomm) kple [AMA's](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/community-call-with-the-zcg-candidates-december-2024/49707) bubu.

Zcash Foundation wɔa [Zcash Trademark policy](https://zfnd.org/new-zcash-trademark-policy/) ɖe eƒe nunana ƒe ɖoɖowo siwo woɖe yeyee tso Electric Coin Co gbɔ la nu. Esia kpena be woa"kpɔ Zcash, eƒe habɔbɔ siwo kplii, kple Zcash nutoa ƒe ŋkɔ nyui dzi" to be woa"de nyatakaka ƒe teƒe siwo nye nyateƒe fia, eye esia nena nyatakaka si nye aʋatso alo si menye nyateƒe o ƒe dzɔdzɔkpɔkpɔ ɖea" kple be woa"de app kple subɔsubɔ siwo nye nyateƒe fia, eye esia nena nukunu ƒe wɔwɔ ƒe dzɔdzɔkpɔkpɔ ɖea." 

## Hame ƒe Nutoɖoɖo

Zcash Foundation nye [501(c)(3)](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/820707962) ƒe tax si me woɖena o, hame si menya ga ɖe eɖokui ŋu o, [nublanuikɔ](https://zfnd.org/#:~:text=Committed%20to%20Transparency). Le woƒe [Hamea](https://zfnd.org/#:~:text=Our%20Team) ƒe ŋutɔ megbe la, Board of [Directors](https://zfnd.org/#:~:text=Board%20of%20Directors) le wo si, eye woana nutoa ƒe dziɖuɖu ƒe tiatiawo awɔ to be woabia Zcash Community Advisory [Panel](https://zfnd.org/zcash-community-advisory-panel/).

## Ga ƒe Kpekpeɖeŋu

Zcash Foundation megaxɔ ga tso Zcash ƒe ŋgɔyiyi [fund](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1014) me tso November 2024 dzi o. Fifia, woɖea ga na The Foundation to nunana [siwo wòate ŋu aɖe tax le woŋu o](https://www.jotform.com/form/251874383638065) ƒe mɔ dzi. Wòate ŋu akpɔ ale si wowɔa ga la kple woƒe ƒe kpuie ƒe [Transparency Reports](https://zfnd.org/category/transparency-reports/).

Ame atɔ̃ sɔŋ siwo woatia tso Zcash Community Advisory Panel me lae tia ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋuwo.
