# Ga ƒe Dziɖulawo Ƒe Ga Ƒe Kpekpeɖeŋu Si Woɖe Le Ŋgɔ

**Ga ƒe Dziɖulawo Ƒe Ga ƒe Kpekpeɖeŋu Si Woɖe Le Ŋgɔ ƒe Dɔɖoɖo** ƒea ga na dɔ si wowu xoxo eye wotsɔ nusi woate ŋu akpɔ kple ŋkuléle nam le Zcash ƒe ecosystem me.

Etsɔa ga tso **Ga ƒe Dziɖulawo Ƒe Gaƒuƒoƒo** me (gaɖoɖowo ƒe fetu ƒe 12% kple Dev Fund si woɖo ɖe megbe ƒe Lockbox ƒe nudzɔdzɔ), si ZIP 1016 de dzidzɔe eye woɖe eƒe dɔwɔwɔ gɔme le NU6.1 (November 2025). 

Dɔɖoɖoa dzea le ɣleti etɔwo me va se ɖe fetu ƒe afãmama [halving] evelia (~2028). Key-Holder Organizations (kpɔe be Shielded Labs, FPF) wɔ gaawo ƒe ɖoɖo le multisig me.

---

Ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋuwo le **dɔ si wowu xoxo ko** wotsɔna - wonoa fetu na amewo ƒe dɔ si wowu xoxo (kpɔe be dɔwɔwɔ, ɖoɖo ɖe eŋu, hamea ƒe tutu). Dɔ si wòle be nàwɔ le ŋgɔ la nàbia Zcash Community Grants (ZCG, fetu ƒe 8%).

---

## Biabia Siwo Dze Ɖe Eŋu

**Taɖodzinu**:

Dɔ si wowu eye amesiame ate ŋu akpɔ kple ŋkuléle nam, si doa vi na Zcash (ɣaɣla [privacy], ame geɖe ƒe wɔwɔe, dɔwɔnuwo, dedienɔnɔ).

**Nudzɔdzɔwo** (ga ƒe dziɖulawo doa ŋkuléle na):

  - Nudzɔdzɔ si fia be eƒe ŋusẽ le eye wotsɔ nusi le asinye va.
  - Wòtsɔa eɖokui ɖe Zcash ƒe nudzɔdzɔwo (ɣaɣla, ha nuɖo agni [decentralized]) ŋu.
  - Ŋkɔkɔɖi si le ʋuʋuɖi me (code, nyatakakawo, metrics).


**Mafɔfɔ aɖeke mele o**: 

Ame sia ame (ame ɖeka, hatsotso, dɔwɔƒewo) ate ŋu abia alo woaɖe eƒe ŋkɔ afia.

**Biabia siwo le dzidzedze**: 

Woaɖo akɔnta na wo ɖeka ɖeka; ZEC ƒe kpeɖeŋu geɖe wu la wɔa dzɔdzɔ.

**Wote ŋu agbe be o**: 

Key-Holders ate ŋu agbe be o le se ƒe fukpekpe alo nu si ana ve na zãlawo/hamea ta (wogbɔna wò akpae, eye wotsɔ taɖodzinu dona).

## Ale Si Nàɖo Biabia Aɖe ɖa

**Dzra Biabia La Ɖo**:

- Ɖe dɔ si wowu, eƒe ŋusẽ, kple ŋkuléle (kadodoƒewo, nusiwo wotsɔ va) me.
- Ɖe ga si wòbia (ZEC/USD ƒe asime) kple ta si tae nèbiae me.
- Tsɔ gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi kple ame si woate ŋu ado go kplii kpee.


**Ɖo Ɖa**:


- Ɖe eɖe ɖa le Zcash Community Forum (forum.zcashcommunity.com) ƒe Grants akpa me.
- Tɔgbi: "[Retroactive Grant] - [Project Name]".
- Zã markdown ƒe ɖoɖo si le ʋuʋuɖi me.


**Ɣeyiɣi ƒe Nuŋɔŋlɔ**:


- Le ɣleti etɔwo me; woɖea gbe le forum/X dzi (kpɔe be Q4 2025: October 12, 2025, 11:59 PM UTC).
- Biabia siwo va le megbe: Ɖo wo ɖa ake le akpa si kplɔe ɖo alo nàbia ZCG.


**Nàwɔ Dɔ Kple Ameawo**:

Ɖo eŋu na hamea ƒe biabiawo le ŋkeke 30 ƒe metsɔmetsɔ me.

#### Ŋlɔɖi: FPF kpɔa dɔɖoɖoa dzi (se ƒe dzeɖoɖo, nuƒoƒo); woaƒo biabiawo katã ɖe GitHub dzi be amesiame nakpɔ.

## Akɔnta Ƒe Dɔwɔwɔ Kple Ɣeyiɣiwo

**Ɣleti Etɔwo Ƒe Ɣeyiɣi** (kpɔe be Q4 2025, Q1 2026):

**Biabia Ƒe Ɣeyiɣi ƒe Nuŋɔŋlɔ**: 

Le ɣleti ƒe titina (woɖea gbe).

**Ŋkeke 30 Ƒe Metsɔmetsɔ**: 

Edzea egɔme le biabia ƒe nuŋɔŋlɔ megbe; wɔnɔa nuƒoƒo kple nyagblɔɖi le forum ƒe threads me (wogbɔna wɔa summary thread zi geɖe).

**Akɔnta Ƒe Ɣeyiɣi**: 

Edzea metsɔmetsɔ la yome (kpɔe be ~keke 1-2 le November me na Q4).

**Nusi Woɖe Fia Kple Ga Ƒe Nana**: 

Le esia megbe enumake; woxea ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu siwo woda ɖa la ɖe woƒe dzeɖoɖo nu (KYC na ga si wu $50k).

**Akɔnta**:

  - Ga ƒe dziɖulawo tsɔa ZEC siwo le asi na wo wɔa akɔnta (matsɔa ɖe teƒe ɖeke o; Orchard gaƒuƒoƒo [pool] dzea eŋu).
  - Mɔnu: Akɔnta si ha nuɖo agni (kpɔe be to voting authorities).
  - **Dada ɖa**: Nàva se ɖe ZEC ƒe 420,000 quorum kple amewo ƒe akpa gã ƒe "ẽ".
  - Akɔnta ɖeka ɖeka le eɖokui ƒe nɔnɔ me; wòate ŋu azã ga ma ke ɖe biabia vovovowo dzi.
  - Nusiwo doa go le ʋuʋuɖi me eye amesiame ate ŋu akpɔ wo.


#### Ŋlɔɖi: **Akpa Si Le Dzi Fifia (le January 5, 2026)**: Q4 2025 wu enu (November akɔnta); woele mɔ kpɔm be Q1 2026 biabiawo ava enumake - kpɔ forum la dzi na nyagblɔɖiwo.
