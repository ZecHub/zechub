# NU6.1

> NU6.1 de dzi le Zcash mainnet dzi le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe 3,146,400 (November 24, 2025 UTC).

Nusi nànyae nye: ale si Zcash xɔa ga na eŋutɔ ƒe ŋgɔyiyi ne habɔbɔ aɖeke mele dzikpɔm o, kple ale si NU6.1 na hamea kple coin ƒe nɔlawo nyaɖoɖo tɔxɛ le ga sia ŋu.

NU6.1 nye Zcash [mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ](../start-here/network-upgrades), si wo ɖe fia le [ZIP 255](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0255) me, eye wòɖo ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu kple dziɖuɖu [governance] ƒe mɔ̃ yeye ɖe Zcash ƒe ŋgɔyiyi ta. Etsɔa gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu ƒe akpa aɖe dea afisi wòayi kple ame siwo ana dziɖuɖu le eŋu, eye wòtsɔ mɔ̃ ƒe dzidzɔsɔŋu sue aɖewo hã va. NU6.1 xɔ kpekpeɖeŋu tso Electric Coin Company kple Zcash Foundation siaa gbɔ.

Nu ka ta esia le vevie. Software geɖe la, habɔbɔ aɖe woe xɔa ga na. Zcash nye ha nuɖo agni [decentralized] network si me tɔxɛ ɖeka aɖeke mele o, eyata biabia sesẽ aɖe le eŋu: Aleke wòaxɔ ga na amewo siwo gale eƒom le mɔ̃ si hamea axɔ dzi se le ŋu? NU6.1 nye Zcash ƒe ŋuɣi sia ƒe ŋuɖoɖo. Wòtsɔ gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe [block] ƒe fetu sia sia ƒe akpa aɖe dea gaƒuƒoƒo [pool] eve me, ɖeka na hamea ƒe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu [grant], eye ɖeka si coin ƒe nɔlawo ŋutɔe wɔa dziɖuɖu le eŋu.

![The NU6.1 funding split: most of each block reward goes to miners, 8 percent to Zcash Community Grants, and 12 percent to a coin-holder-controlled fund](/content-images/nu6-1-funding-split-890dcf6881.webp)

## Kpuie ko (TL;DR)

- NU6.1 dze egɔme le mainnet (Zcash ƒe kɔsɔkɔsɔ vevitɔ) dzi le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe 3,146,400 me (November 24, 2025 UTC), eye woɖe eme le ZIP 255 me.
- Eɖo gakpekpeɖeŋu ƒe ɖoɖo si wozãna egbea: gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖesiaɖe ƒe fetu ƒe abe 80% ene yina na nukulawo, 8% yina na Zcash Community Grants, eye 12% yina na gadzraɖoƒe aɖe si gaɖolawo kpɔa edzi, si me ga gbãtɔ tso Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox me.
- Sewo (ZIP 1016) awɔ dɔ va se ɖe Zcash ƒe fetu ƒe afãmama etɔ̃lia dzi, eye esia na ɣeyiɣi nutoa me tɔwo be woalé ŋku ɖe wo ŋu hafi awɔ wo woanɔ anyi tegbee alo atrɔ wo.
- Gaɖolawo katã ƒo ƒu tsoa nya me be yewoaɖe 12% gadzraɖoƒea ɖe asi na dɔwɔwɔ le ecosystem (dɔwɔha si ƒo xlã Zcash) me alo yewoatu enu ɖe dzi. Esia na be gbe le wo si tẽ le alesi Zcash akpɔ ga na eƒe etsɔme ŋu.
- Woɖe mɔ̃ŋutinunya ƒe ɖɔɖɔɖo eve ɖe go kpe ɖe eŋu: Orchard-unshielding (ga ɖeɖe le ɣaɣla me) dzesiɖeɖe nyuie le RPC ƒe gaxexlẽwo me, eye zcashd zãa gaɖoɖo ƒe ɖoɖo ƒe tɔtrɔ 5lia le gɔmedzedzea me.

## Kpɔɖeŋu si wokpɔna kple ŋku

NU6.1 trɔa ƒome ƒe gakpeɖeƒe si le NU6 ŋutinya me wòzua akɔnta si wokpɔa egbɔ kple se siwo woŋlɔ ɖi. 12% ƒe akpa la megazua "ga si ame aɖeke mate ŋu aka asi o" o, ke boŋ ezua "ga si ŋu gadzraɖolawo [shareholders] dea akɔ le": woaɖee ɖi hena dɔwɔwɔ le ƒuƒoƒo blibo la me alo woanɔ te ɖe edzi wòanɔ ɖaɖi. 8% si yina na hamea ƒe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu [grants] la yia edzi le eɖokui si, abe ɖoɖo si nɔa anyi ɖaa ene — mehiã akɔdada aɖeke o.

## Ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu ƒe mɔ̃

NU6.1 ma gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu sia sia me akpa etɔ.

1. Akpa gãtɔ, alo 80 percent kple vi, yia nukulawo [miners] gbɔ, ame siwo doa network la ɖe anyi.
2. 8 percent yia Zcash Community Grants (ZCG) gbɔ, si xɔa ga na dɔ si hamea wɔ kple dɔ si wo wɔ na hamea.
3. 12 percent yia gaƒuƒoƒo yeye aɖe me si coin ƒe nɔlawo wɔa dziɖuɖu le eŋu, eye Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox naa eƒe gɔmedzedze.

Mɔ̃ sia ƒe sewo wo ɖe fia le [ZIP 1016](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1016) me, eye wòanɔ anyi va se ɖe Zcash ƒe fetu ƒe afãmama [halving] etɔlia me, si naa hamea ɣeyiɣi be woakpɔ ale si wòwɔna hafi woatsɔe aɖo anyi tegbee alo woatrɔe.

## Ame ka wɔa dziɖuɖu le coin ƒe nɔlawo ƒe gaƒuƒoƒo ŋu

12 percent ƒe tɔtsɔe nye susu yeye la. Le habɔbɔ alo foundation aɖe wɔa nya tso ale si woatsɔe wɔ dɔe nu o, coin ƒe nɔlawo wɔa nya ɖekae be woaɖe ga ma tsoe alo woaɖe asi le eŋu.

1. Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox, si wo wɔ le NU6 mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ me, naa gaƒuƒoƒo sia ƒe gɔmedzedze.
2. Coin ƒe nɔlawo katã wɔa nya be woama ga la na ecosystem ƒe dɔwo alo woaɖe asi le eŋu.
3. Esia naa ame siwo le ZEC ƒe asi la nyaɖoɖo tɔxɛ le ale si Zcash axɔ ga na eƒe etsɔme ŋu.

![The NU6.1 governance flow: the Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox seeds the coin-holder fund, and coin holders decide whether to distribute grants or hold the funds](/content-images/nu6-1-governance-21210bcc35.webp)

## Mɔ̃ ƒe dzidzɔsɔŋu suewo

NU6.1 trɔ nu eve hã ɖe mɔ̃ ƒe nyawo ŋu.

1. Hafi NU6.1, RPC ƒe mɔ aɖewo menya ga si woɖe tso Orchard gaƒuƒoƒo me be meɣaɣla o nyuie o, eyata balance kple adzɔwo [fees] ate ŋu adze be wometsɔ o le gakotokuwɔwo [wallets] kple nusiwo kpɔa nuwo me. NU6.1 trɔ esia.
2. Etrɔ zcashd gakotoku ƒe gɔmedzedze ƒe mɔ̃ be wòawɔ version 5 gaɖoɖowo [transactions], si nye format si woɖe fia gbã le NU5 me, le version 4 teƒe. Version 4 gaɖoɖowo gale mɔ me.

## Ale si Zcash ƒe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu trɔ

NU6.1 nye afɔ aɖe le ŋutinya didi aɖe me tso ale si Zcash xɔa ga na eŋutɔ ƒe ŋgɔyiyi ŋu.

1. Canopy (2020) tsɔ founders reward gɔme la yi eye wòwɔ [ŋgɔyiyi ƒe gaƒuƒoƒo](../start-here/development-fund).
2. NU6 (2024) ɖo Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox ɖe anyi, eye wòɖe issuance ƒe akpa aɖe ɖe aside na hamea ƒe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu si woawɔ nya tsoe le etsɔme.
3. NU6.1 (2025) ɖo biabia si NU6 de ɖe asi la ŋu, ame ka ana dziɖuɖu kple aleke, to 8 percent le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu me yina hamea ƒe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu gbɔ kple 12 percent yina gaƒuƒoƒo si coin ƒe nɔlawo wɔa dziɖuɖu le eŋu, si reserve ma naa eƒe gɔmedzedze.

![How Zcash funding evolved: Canopy created the development fund, NU6 set up the lockbox, and NU6.1 set the rules for who controls it](/content-images/nu6-1-funding-history-bcf6a94ad4.webp)

## Vodada Siwo Bɔ

- **Gbɔgblɔ be "NU6.1 wɔ lockbox la".** NU6 ye wɔ lockbox (gaɖaka si wotu ɖe te) la le ƒe 2024 me; NU6.1 wɔ dziɖuɖu (governance) si ɖoa nu tso ale si woazã ga si wodzra ɖo la ŋu.
- **Yɔyɔ 12% la be dɔwɔƒe aɖe ƒe fexexe.** Dɔwɔƒe aɖeke mexɔnɛ o. Coin ƒe nɔlawo katã ɖoa nu tso ga ɖeɖe ɖe go ŋu, eye esiae nye trɔtrɔ la ƒe taɖodzinu blibo.
- **Susu be ɖoɖo la anɔ anyi tegbee.** ZIP 1016 anɔ dɔ wɔm va se ɖe fetu ƒe afãmama (halving) etɔ̃lia dzi; emegbe la, nutoa me tɔwo ate ŋu alé ɖoɖo la ɖe asi, atrɔe, alo aɖoe nu.
- **Mɔkpɔkpɔ be gakotokuwo (wallets) atrɔ nane na zãlawo.** Ga mama la dzɔna le protocol ƒe dzidzenu dzi; ga si le asi kple fexexe (payments) mekpɔ ŋusẽ aɖeke o.
- **Dɔdɔ tɔtrɔ sue eveawo ɖe ga ƒe tɔtrɔ la me.** Orchard RPC dzesidede ƒe tɔtrɔ kple zcashd v5-gaɖoɖo (transaction) ƒe ɖoɖo gbãtɔ nye ŋutete vovovo siwo womeku ɖe ga ŋu o, gake woɖe wo ɖe go le mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ ma ke me.

## Nyagbalẽ

| Nya | Gɔmesese bɔbɔe |
|---|---|
| Block reward | ZEC yeye si wo wɔa eye wonaa ame le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe sia sia nukuku [mining] me |
| Zcash Community Grants (ZCG) | Ɖoɖo aɖe si xɔa ga na dɔ si Zcash hamea wɔ kple dɔ si wo wɔ na hamea |
| Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox | Reserve si woɖo ɖe anyi le NU6 me, si léa issuance ƒe akpa aɖe ɖe asi na hamea ƒe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu si woawɔ nya tsoe le etsɔme |
| Coin-holder fund | NU6.1 ƒe gaƒuƒoƒo si lockbox naa eƒe gɔmedzedze, si ZEC ƒe nɔlawo katã wɔa dziɖuɖu le eŋu |
| Halving | Ɣeyiɣi si woɖo ɖe anyi be gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu ana afã |
| Network upgrade (NU) | Trɔtrɔ si woɖoa ɖekae wɔa le Zcash ƒe gbegbɔgblɔ ɖi [consensus] ƒe sewo me, eye wòde dzi le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe teƒe si woɖo ɖe anyi dzi |

## Biabia si wo bia zi geɖe

Ðe NU6.1 trɔa nye ZEC alo nye ɣaɣla? Ao. NU6.1 nye tso ale si wo xɔa ga na ŋgɔyiyi ŋu, menye gaɖoɖowo alo ɣaɣla ŋu o. Wòga kple wògaɖoɖowo siwo meɣaɣla o mele trɔtrɔ me o.

Afika ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu ƒe ga la tsoa? Etsoa gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetuwo me, ZEC yeye si woɖea ne wo nukua gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe. Woɖoa eƒe akpa aɖe yi ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu kple coin ƒe nɔlawo ƒe gaƒuƒoƒo me le teƒe be ga blibo la nay i nukulawo gbɔ.

Nu ka ta coin ƒe nɔlawo ƒe gaƒuƒoƒo la le? Na ecosystem ƒe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu si coin ƒe nɔlawo katã atia be woaxɔ ga na, alo woate ŋu aɖe asi le eŋu ne woade asi lalaa.

Ðe esia ate ŋu anɔ anyi tegbee? Ao. Mɔ̃ la anɔ anyi va se ɖe fetu ƒe afãmama etɔlia, eye emegbe hamea ate ŋu awɔe yi edzi, atrɔe, alo atsɔ bubu ade eƒe teƒe.

Aleke NU6.1 vovo le NU6 ŋu? NU6 wɔ lockbox la. NU6.1 ɖo sewo ɖe ame siwo wɔa dziɖuɖu le eŋu kple ale si woama ga la me ŋu.

## Kpɔ be èse egɔme

NU6 ɖe gaƒuƒoƒo aɖe ɖe aside le lockbox me, gake wòde biabia gã aɖe ɖe asi. Nu kae nye esia, eye aleke NU6.1 ɖo eŋu?

<summary>Ŋuɖoɖo</summary>

Biabia si nɔ asi la nye ame ka ana nya tso ale si woazã lockbox ƒe ga la nu. NU6.1 ɖo eŋu to ga la mamam: 8 percent na Zcash Community Grants, eye 12 percent na gaƒuƒoƒo si coin ƒe nɔlawo ŋutɔe wɔa dziɖuɖu le eŋu, eye woatia be woamae alo woaɖe asi le eŋu.
