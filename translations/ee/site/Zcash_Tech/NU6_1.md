# NU6.1

> NU6.1 yi dɔwɔwɔ dzi le Zcash mainnet dzi le block 3,146,400 (November 24, 2025 UTC).

Nusi nàtsɔ adzoe: alesi Zcash xea fe ɖe eya ŋutɔ ƒe ŋgɔyiyi ta dɔwɔƒe aɖeke manɔmee, kple alesi NU6.1 na nutoa me tɔwo kple gakuxɔlawo kpɔ nya tẽ le ga ma me.

NU6.1 nye Zcash [network ƒe ŋgɔyiyi](../start-here/network-upgrades), si gɔme woɖe le [ZIP 255 ƒe xexlẽdzesi](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0255), si ɖo gadodo kple dziɖuɖu ƒe kpɔɖeŋu yeye aɖe na Zcash ƒe ŋgɔyiyi. Etsoa nya me le afisi block ƒe teƒeɖoɖowo ƒe akpa aɖe ayi kple amesi akpɔ edzi ŋu, eye wòɖo mɔ̃ɖaŋununya ƒe ɖɔɖɔɖo sue ʋɛ aɖewo hã ɖee. Electric Coin Company kple Zcash Foundation siaa da asi ɖe NU6.1 dzi.

Nusitae esia le vevie ɖo. Dɔwɔƒe aɖee xea fe ɖe kɔmpiutadziɖoɖo akpa gãtɔ ta. Zcash nye decentralized network si me aƒetɔ ɖeka aɖeke mele o, eyata edze ŋgɔ biabia sesẽ aɖe: aleke wòkpɔa ga na ame siwo yia edzi le etum, le mɔ si dzi nutoa me tɔwo ka ɖo nu? NU6.1 nye Zcash ƒe ŋuɖoɖo si li fifia. Eɖoa block ɖesiaɖe ƒe fetu ƒe akpa aɖe ɖe ta eve me, ɖeka nyea nutoa me ƒe gakpekpeɖeŋunana eye ɖeka si dzi gakuxɔlawo ŋutɔ kpɔ ŋusẽ ɖo.

![The NU6.1 funding split: most of each block reward goes to miners, 8 percent to Zcash Community Grants, and 12 percent to a coin-holder-controlled fund](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-1-funding-split.png)

## Gadodo ƒe kpɔɖeŋua

NU6.1 ma block ƒe fetu ɖesiaɖe ɖe akpa etɔ̃ me.

1. Akpa gãtɔ kekeake, si ade 80 le alafa me, yia tomenukulawo si kpɔa kadodoa ta.
2. 8 le alafa me yia Zcash Community Grants (ZCG), si naa gakpekpeɖeŋunana nutoa me tɔwo eye wowɔa dɔ na wo.
3. 12 le alafa me yia gaxɔ yeye aɖe si dzi gakuxɔlawo kpɔ ŋusẽ ɖo, si Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox si woƒãna la me.

Woɖe kpɔɖeŋu sia ƒe sewo gɔme le [ZIP 1016 ƒe xexlẽdzesi](https://zips.z.cash/zip-1016), eye wonɔa teƒea vaseɖe Zcash ƒe afã dzi ɖeɖe kpɔtɔ etɔ̃lia, si naa ɣeyiɣi nutoa me tɔwo be woakpɔ alesi wòwɔa dɔe hafi awɔe wòanɔ anyi ɖaa alo atrɔe.

## Amekae kpɔa gakuxɔ ƒe gaxɔa dzi

Tɔsisi 12 le alafa me nye susu yeyea. Le esi teƒe be dɔwɔƒe alo gɔmeɖoanyi aɖe natso nya me le alesi woazãe ŋu la, gakudzikpɔlawo tsoa nya me ɖekae nenye be woaɖe asi le ga mawo ŋu loo alo woalé wo ɖe asi.

1. Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox, si wowɔ le NU6 ƒe tɔtrɔ si wowɔ do ŋgɔ me la, ƒãa ga sia.
2. Gakunɔlawo katã tsoa nya me nenye be woama ga la hena lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ŋuti dɔwɔwɔ alo woatue.
3. Esia naa ame siwo lé ZEC la ƒe gbe tẽ le alesi Zcash naa ga eƒe etsɔme ŋu.

![The NU6.1 governance flow: the Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox seeds the coin-holder fund, and coin holders decide whether to distribute grants or hold the funds](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-1-governance.png)

## Woɖɔa wo ɖo le sue wu

NU6.1 hã kɔ mɔ̃ɖaŋunu eve ŋu.

1. Do ŋgɔ na NU6.1 la, RPC mɔnu aɖewo medea dzesi ga siwo woɖe le Orchard ta la me nyuie o, eyata ga si susɔ kple fe siwo woxena ate ŋu adze nu gbegblẽ le gakotokuwo kple anyigbayeyedilawo me. NU6.1 ɖɔ ema ɖo.
2. Etrɔ zcashd gakotoku ƒe gɔmedzedze be wòatu tɔtrɔ 5 ƒe asitsatsa, nɔnɔme si woto vɛ zi gbãtɔ le NU5 me, ɖe tɔtrɔ 4 teƒe. Version 4 ƒe asitsatsa nɔa dɔ wɔm.

## Alesi Zcash ƒe gadodo va trɔe

NU6.1 nye afɔɖeɖe ɖeka le ŋutinya didi aɖe me ku ɖe alesi Zcash xea fe ɖe eya ŋutɔ ƒe ŋgɔyiyi ta ŋu.

1. Canopy (2020) wu gɔmeɖolawo ƒe fetu gbãtɔ nu eye wòwɔ a [ŋgɔyiyi ƒe gaxɔ](../start-here/development-fund).
2. NU6 (2024) ɖo Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox, si dzra ga si woaɖe ɖe go ƒe akpa aɖe ɖo na gakpekpeɖeŋu siwo nutoa me tɔwo atso nya me le etsɔme.
3. NU6.1 (2025) ɖo nyabiase si NU6 gblẽ ɖi, amesi atso nya me kple alesi wòwɔna, to block fetu ƒe alafa memama 8 dzi yiyi ɖe nutoa me ƒe kpekpeɖeŋunana ŋu eye wòfia mɔ 12 le alafa me yi gaku si dzi gakuxɔlawo kpɔna si woƒã to gakudzraɖoƒe ma me.

![How Zcash funding evolved: Canopy created the development fund, NU6 set up the lockbox, and NU6.1 set the rules for who controls it](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-1-funding-history.png)

## Nyabiasewo ƒe Nyabiasewo

Ðe NU6.1 trɔa nye ZEC alo nye adzamenyawoa? No. NU6.1 ku ɖe alesi wokpɔa ga na ŋgɔyiyi ŋu, ke menye asitsatsa alo ame ŋutɔ ƒe nyawo gbɔ kpɔkpɔ ŋu o. Wò ga kple asitsatsa siwo ŋu wokpɔ ta na la mekpɔa ŋusẽ ɖe edzi o.

Afikae ga si wodzɔna la tsona? Tso block rewards dzi la, ZEC yeye si woɖe ɖe go abe blocks wole tome kum ene. Woɖoa gome aɖe ɖe gakpekpeɖeŋunana kple gakuxɔlawo ƒe ga tsɔ wu be wo katã nayi na tomenukulawo.

Nukae gaku-xɔlawo ƒe gaxɔa na? Nu gbagbewo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ŋuti ɖoɖo naa gakuxɔlawo katã tiae be yewoakpɔ ga, alo alée ne wodi be yewoalala.

Ðe esia nɔa anyi ɖaaa? Ao, kpɔɖeŋua zɔna vaseɖe afã etɔ̃lia, eye emegbe nutoa me tɔwo ate ŋu alée ɖe asi, atrɔe, alo aɖɔlii.

Aleke NU6.1 to vovo tso NU6 gbɔe? NU6 ye wɔ gaƒoɖokuiɖaka la. NU6.1 ɖo se siwo ku ɖe amesi kpɔ ŋusẽ ɖe edzi kple alesi woama ga lae ŋu.

## Do wò nugɔmesese kpɔ

NU6 dzra ga gbogbo aɖe ɖo ɖe gaxɔ aɖe me gake wogblẽ nyabiase gã ɖeka ɖi si ŋu womeɖo o. Nukae wònye, eye aleke NU6.1 ɖo eŋu?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Nyabiase si woʋu ɖie nye be amekae atso nya me le alesi woazã ga si woatsɔ axe gaxɔa me ŋu. NU6.1 ɖo eŋu to ga la mama me: 8 le alafa me na Zcash Community Grants, eye 12 le alafa me na gaxɔ aɖe si dzi gakuxɔlawo ŋutɔ kpɔ ŋusẽ ɖo, eye wotiae ne woamae alo woalée ɖe asi.
</details>
