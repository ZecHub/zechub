# Ɣaɣla [shielded] ƒe Dzadzra Me Asidede [cold signing]

> **Nya vevi:** Gakotoku [wallet] si doa ɖe eŋu kpɔa nu siawo katã si hiã be woatu ɣaɣla gaɖoɖo [transaction] eye woakpɔe ɖa. Asikplɔwo dzra mɔ [hardware wallet] koe le ŋusẽ be wòana mɔ woazã ga. Mamae nye ɣaɣla ƒe dzadzra me asidede ƒe ta vevi blibo.

Ɣaɣla wɔwɔ le Zcash me fia be woatsɔ ga atso dukɔmenyawo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi [transparent] gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi [address] aɖo ɣaɣla tɔ me, eye woaɣla gaɖoɖo ƒe nyawo kple Zcash ƒe ɣaɣla ƒe nɔnɔwo.

### Nu kae trɔ ne nàwɔ ɣaɣla na ZEC?

Kple Zcash ƒe nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi gaɖoɖo, woate ŋu akpɔ nyawo abe:

- Ame si dɔ ga la ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi
- Ame si xɔ ga la ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi
- Ga si wotsɔ ɖo la ƒe home

Le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu [blockchain] dzi.

Ne nàwɔ ɣaɣla na ZEC, wò ga adzo yi ɣaɣla gaƒuƒoƒo [pool] me, abe Ironwood ene. Gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu ate ŋu akpɔe be gaɖoɖo aɖe le ete, ke menye be wòaɖe fia wò gaɖoɖo ƒe nya ɣaɣlawo o. Tso NU6.3 mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ [network upgrade] si va le 28 July 2026 la me, ga yeyewo si wòwɔ ɣaɣla na la yina Ironwood gaƒuƒoƒo me, elabena Orchard gaƒuƒoƒo xoxo la megaɖe mɔ na ga yeyewo ƒe deɖe me o, ke wòle mɔ na ga ƒe ɖeɖe tso eme.

### Ɣaɣla Gaɖoɖo Ƒe Akpaewo

### 1. Didienu [note] (Ɣaɣla Ƒe Gaƒuƒoƒo Ƒe Nudzraɖoƒe)

Didienu nye ɣaɣla, kryptografi ƒe nudzraɖoƒe si fia ZEC ƒe ga si wometsɔ zã o, eye ame aɖe nye etɔ.

To vovototo na dukɔmenya gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu, afi si woŋlɔ gaƒuƒoƒowo kple nya ʋuʋuɖi, ɣaɣla didienu doa asitsɔtsɔ ɖe ga ƒe home kple ame si nye etɔ la ƒe nyawo ŋu be ame siwo kpɔa ɖoɖonu dzi la mate ŋu akpɔ ame si nye ga la o.

Ne woele gaɖoɖo aɖe gɔme dzem la, gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la le agbalẽ la ŋu kpɔm, dea asi ame la ƒe didienuwo si wometsɔ zã o ŋu, eye wòtia didienu siwo wòazã.

### 2. Gaɣaɣla Ðeɖe Dzesi [nullifier] (Ga Ƒe Zã Gbɔ Eve Ƒe Sedziedzi Mɔ)

Esi didienu ƒe nyawo ɣla le dukɔmenya agbalẽa me ta la, hiã be network la nakpɔ mɔ si wòatsɔ ade asi ga ƒe zã gbɔ eve ŋu, eye menya didienu si wozã o.

Gaɣaɣla ɖeɖe dzesi nye kryptografi ƒe dzesi tɔxɛ si tso didienu aɖe me. Ne wozã didienu aɖe la, woɖea eƒe gaɣaɣla ɖeɖe dzesi fia le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu dzi.

Network la kpɔa be wometsɔ gaɣaɣla ɖeɖe dzesi la ŋlɔ ɖe afi ma kpɔ o. Ne wometsɔe ŋlɔ kpɔ o la, woate ŋu axɔ ga zazã la. Nya si le vevie lae nye be ame si le gota mate ŋu azã gaɣaɣla ɖeɖe dzesi la ade asi didienu si me wòtso la ŋu o.

Gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la nɔa gaɣaɣla ɖeɖe dzesi si dze eye wòtuae le gaɖoɖo ƒe nyawo ƒe ƒoƒo me.

## Safuiwo [keys]

Safuiwo nye kryptografi ƒe nyawo siwo kpɔa mɔ na wò ga, eye wona wò gakotoku mɔ be wòawɔ dɔwo abe ZEC ƒe xɔxɔ, kpɔkpɔ, kple zazã ene.

Gakotoku aɖe kpɔa safui vovovowo, eye ɖe sia ɖe wɔnɛ dɔ vovovo, abe akpaɖeka ƒe akɔnta ate ŋu akpɔ mɔxɔxɔ ƒe nyawo vovovo na nuxexlẽ kple nuŋlɔŋlɔ ene.

Safuiwo le vevie ŋutɔ le gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu kple asikplɔwo dzra mɔ dome, elabena safui vovovowo wɔnɛ dɔ vovovo, eye ɖeka koe hiã be wòanɔ ɣla.

Ele vevie be nànyaa be ɣaɣla gaƒuƒoƒo ɖe sia ɖe tua eƒe safuiwo abe atia ƒe ati ene, afi si adewo fia nusi woate ŋu adze tso akpa aɖe me. Zãlawo siwo di be woaxɔ ɣaɣla fexexe [payment] la hiã be woakpɔ ɣaɣla fexexe ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi, si wòdzea tso gazazãsafui [spending key] me.

### Safui ƒe kɔkɔe

**1. Gazazãsafui**

Esiae nye safui si hiã dedie le dedienɔnɔ ŋu. Ewɔa be ame ate ŋu akpɔ kryptografi ƒe ŋusẽ be wòaɖe mɔ na ZEC ƒe zazã.

Esiae nye safui si woƒoa nu tso ne woƒoa nu tso "asikplɔwo dzra mɔ kpɔ safui la" le nyatakakaa ƒe akpa bubuwo me.

Le dzadzra me asidede ƒe ɖoɖo me la, wole gazazãsafui la dzram ɖe asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la me, eye wometsɔe ƒoa fia gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la o. Megahiã be gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la nakpɔ safui sia kura o.

Le Sapling kple Orchard siaa me la, safui ƒe kɔkɔe dzena ga zazã ƒe mɔxɔxɔ kple nukpɔkpɔ ƒe ŋusẽwo abe atiƒe eve siwo tso gazazãsafui gɔmedzeƒe me ene:

- **Sapling:** Gazazãsafui si woɖe ɖe go (𝑎𝑠𝑘,𝑛𝑠𝑘,𝑜𝑣𝑘) dzea ɖaseɖigbalɛ [proof] ƒe mɔxɔxɔ safuiwo (𝑎𝑘,𝑛𝑠𝑘) kple full viewing keywo (𝑎𝑘,𝑛𝑘,𝑜𝑣𝑘), siwo hã dzea incoming viewing keywo (𝑖𝑣𝑘) kple gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi vovovowo.
- **Orchard:** Gazazãsafui gɔmedzeƒe (𝑠𝑘) dzea yi tẽ ɖe ga zazã ƒe mɔxɔxɔ safui (𝑎𝑠𝑘) kple full viewing key (𝑎𝑘,𝑛𝑘,𝑟𝑖𝑣𝑘) me.

Mãma me vovototo sia naa kryptografi ƒe gɔmesese le asikplɔwo dzra mɔ ƒe safui ƒe mama adzɔdzɔe ŋu. Eme la, gakotoku la doa nukpɔsafui [viewing key] ƒe atiƒe (UFVK) ɖe host computer la ŋu be woawɔ gaƒuƒoƒo ƒe sɔsɔ, eye wòle ga zazã ƒe mɔxɔxɔ ƒe atiƒe la dzram ɖe mɔ siwo katã le device la dzi.

Safui ƒe dzedze yina ɖeka ko ƒe mɔ dzi. Esia fia be womate ŋu atrɔ nukpɔsafuiwo megbe atu gazazã ƒe mɔxɔxɔ o. Kryptografi ƒe matɔtrɔm ateŋu na Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK) ƒe ɖeɖe go dzadzra, elabena ena gakotoku la ƒe nukpɔkpɔ blibo, eye mena ga zazã ƒe ɣaɣla siwo le device la dzi o.

**2. Nukpɔsafuiwo**

Zcash mae ɣaɣla ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ƒe nukpɔkpɔ kple eƒe zazã ŋusẽ. Nukpɔsafui dea mɔ na gakotoku alo ame bubu si kpɔ mɔ be wòakpɔ nyawo tso ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo ŋu, eye mena ga zazã ƒe mɔxɔxɔ o.

- **Full Viewing Key (FVK), si woɖu kple Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK):** Wodzea eƒe ta me tso gazazãsafui me; ena software mɔ be wòade asi gakotoku la ƒe ɣaɣla dɔwɔwɔwo ŋu eye wòakpɔ wo ƒe asitsɔtsɔ, eye esia na wòdea asi gaɖoɖowo ŋu. Hã, ena mɔ be wòaxlẽ gaƒuƒoƒowo, ke mena mɔ be wòaɖe mɔ na ga zazã o. Eŋutɔe nye gazazãsafui ƒe dzedze si me ɣaɣla medze o, menye nɔnɔa ƒe kopi si do go wu o.

Full Viewing Key (FVK) naa nuxexlẽ ƒe nukpɔkpɔ blibo, eye wòle dedienɔnɔ ƒe mama makaka ŋu. To didienu siwo va kple siwo yi ƒe asitsɔtsɔ ƒe ɖeɖe me (to eƒe outgoing-viewing-key ƒe akpa me) la, gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu si le FVK ko ŋu ate ŋu aɖe gaɖoɖo ƒe ŋutinyawo katã fia kple dzesiwo, menye gaƒuƒoƒo si lia yi ɖe edzi ko o.

Fexexe ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi vovovowo siwo ƒe kadodo manɔ anyi [unlinkable] la ƒoa FVK kple incoming viewing key (IVK) ɖeka, eye esia dea mɔ na gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi geɖe ƒe dzedze manɔ se aɖeke te o, eye menaa gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu ƒe leleliŋu kpɔkpɔ ƒe dɔ la medze o.

Dɔ ƒe mama vovototo sia nye dzadzra me asidede ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ƒe gɔmedzeƒe. Woɖe ɖoɖonu ƒe leleliŋu kpɔkpɔ ɣe sia ɣe, ŋutinyawo ƒe dzadzra, kple gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo ƒe kpɔkpɔ katã ɖe host software la ŋu, eye ga zazã ƒe mɔxɔxɔ nɔ ɣe le asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la me.

### Vovototo si le vevie ŋutɔ: UFVK kple gazazãsafui

> **Woate ŋu ama UFVK. Gazazãsafui la ele be wòanɔ asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la me.**
>
> Gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la zãa nukpɔsafui la be wòakpɔ gakotoku la ɖa, wòade asi ga siwo va la ŋu, wòade asi didienuwo ŋu, eye wòakpe ɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒe dzadzra ŋu. Mekpɔa ɣaɣla safui si hiã be wòaɖe mɔ na ga zazã o.

Abe ale si woegblɔe do ŋgɔ ene la, safuiwo ƒe mama vovototo nye dzadzra me asidede ƒe ɖoɖo ƒe gɔmedzeƒe.

Gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la ate ŋu awɔ dɔ geɖe si hiã be woawɔ gaɖoɖo aɖe ɖo, eye mekpɔa ŋusẽ ɖe ga la ŋu o. Asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la hiã ko be wòaxɔ gaɖoɖo si woɖo ɖo la eye wòawɔ asidede ƒe dɔ mamlɛtɔ.

Asikplɔwo dzra mɔ ƒe wɔlawo mehiã be woatu mama vovototo sia wɔnɔewo o. Zcash ƒe safui ƒe ɖoɖo xɔ nukpɔkpɔ ƒe ŋusẽwo ɖe ga zazã ƒe mɔxɔxɔ ŋu le protocol ƒe nɔnɔa me xoxo, eye wɔlawo tua woƒe asidede kple ɖeɖe go ƒe dɔwo ɖe ɖoɖo si le afi ma xoxo la dzi. Protocol la dea mɔ na nyawo abe ɣaɣla gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi alo full viewing key adze tso gazazãsafui me, eye gazazãsafui la ŋutɔ nɔa ɣla. Esia wɔe be asikplɔwo dzra mɔwo abe Ledger, Keystone, kple Hito ate ŋu ana gakotoku ƒe software la nukpɔkpɔ ƒe nyawo si wòhiã, eye wometsɔa ɣaɣla safui si ate ŋu azã ga la ƒoa fia o.

### Nusi gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la wɔa kple safui ƒe nyawo

To nukpɔsafui ƒe nyawo me la, gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la ate ŋu:

- Ade asi wò ga kple didienuwo ŋu
- Ade asi didienu siwo ate ŋu azã ŋu
- Adze alo ade asi gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi siwo dze ŋu
- Atu gaɖoɖo la
- Awu alo akpe ɖe ɖaseɖigbalɛ siwo hiã la ƒe wɔwɔ ŋu
- Aɖo gaɖoɖo la ɖo na asidede

Ele vevie be nànyaa be gaɖoɖo ƒe tutu hiã menye be ɣaɣla gazazãsafui la hã hiã o. Mãma ƒe vovototo sia nye dedienɔnɔ ƒe susu vevi aɖe le asikplɔwo dzra mɔ ƒe kadodo me.

## Ɣaɣla Gaɖoɖo Ƒe Tutu Kple Mɔxɔxɔ

Esiawoe nye dɔwɔwɔ siwo le gaɖoɖo ƒe tutu kple mɔxɔxɔ me. Le gɔmesese bɔbɔe me la, dɔwɔwɔ siawoe nye nusi gakotoku la wɔa kple nyawo siawo be wòadɔ ga:

### Gaɖoɖo Ƒe Tutu (Ɣaɣla Gaɖoɖo Ƒe Ƒoƒo)

Gaɖoɖo ƒe tutu nye nyawo siwo hiã be woatu Zcash gaɖoɖo si le ete tso zãla la ƒe fexexe si wòdi me la ƒe ƒoƒo.

Gaɖoɖo ƒe tutu ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ƒoa didienu siwo wotia, ame siwo axɔ ga, ga ƒe homewo, adzɔ [fee]wo, gaɣaɣla ɖeɖe dzesiwo, kple gaɖoɖo ƒe nyawo bubu siwo hiã la ƒo ɖe gaɖoɖo ƒe ɖoɖo aɖe me. Na ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo la, wòɖoa nya siwo hiã na zero-knowledge ɖaseɖigbalɛwo kple asidede agbalẽ te [signature]wo ƒe wɔwɔ ɖo hã.

Gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu lae wɔa akpa sia zi geɖe. Eya:

- Tia didienu siwo wòazã
- Ðoa gaɖoɖo la ƒe gegeƒewo kple dodoƒewo ɖo
- Xlẽa ga ƒe home siwo hiã
- Ðoa gaɖoɖo ƒe nyawo si woaɖe ɖase na eye woado asi te le emegbe ɖo.

Le dzadzra me asidede ƒe ɖoɖo me la, woate ŋu aɖu gaɖoɖo ƒe nyawo si do go la ɖe PCZT me eye woatsɔe ana asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la na dedienɔnɔ ƒe asidede ƒe dɔ si le vevie.

### Ðaseɖe (Protocol Ƒe Etenɔnɔ Ƒe Fiafia)

Ðaseɖe fia zero-knowledge ɖaseɖigbalɛwo ƒe wɔwɔ, abe Halo 2 alo Groth16 ene, siwo fia be gaɖoɖo aɖe le Zcash protocol ƒe sewo dzi.

Ðaseɖigbalɛ la kpea ɖe network la ŋu be wòana akpɔe ɖe edzi be:

- Zãla si zãa ga la kpɔ mɔ be wòazã didienuwo
- Didienuwo le afi ma vavã
- Gaɣaɣla ɖeɖe dzesi siwo sɔ kple wo la le tɔxɛ
- Gegeƒewo katã ƒe home sɔ kple dodoƒewo katã kpe ɖe adzɔwo ŋu.

Ewɔ esia katã eye meɖea ɣaɣla nyawo aɖeke le eme la fia o.

Ðaseɖigbalɛ ƒe wɔwɔ xɔa kɔmpiuta ƒe ŋusẽ geɖe. Dɔ sia ƒe ɖeɖe ɖe device si doa ɖe eŋu la ŋu doa ɖe asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la ŋu be wòaganɔ te alo eƒe ŋkuɖodzi aga.

### Asidede (Ga Zazã Ƒe Mɔxɔxɔ)

Asidede le vovototo vevi ŋutɔ kple ɖaseɖe. Digital asidede agbalẽ te wɔa abe mɔxɔxɔ ƒe dzesi si ɣaɣla safui la ƒe tɔ wɔ ene.

Ne zero-knowledge ɖaseɖigbalɛ kpɔa gaɖoɖo ƒe etenɔnɔ la, digital asidede agbalẽ te fiaa ga zazã ƒe mɔxɔxɔ. Asidede agbalẽ te la dea ɖase be tɔ vavã la xɔ akpa siwo le gaɖoɖo la me la dzi.

Asidede mehiãa kɔmpiuta ƒe ŋusẽ geɖe o, eyata edze na asikplɔwo dzra mɔ.

### Nu kae nye ɣaɣla ƒe dzadzra me asidede?

Ɣaɣla ƒe dzadzra me asidede nye mɔ si woatsɔ aɖe mɔ na Zcash ɣaɣla gaɖoɖo, eye wole ɣaɣla gazazãsafuiwo mama ɖe internet ƒe kadodo si le gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la ŋu. Woate ŋu atu eye woaɖo gaɖoɖo la ɖo le kɔmpiuta si le internet dzi, eye asikplɔwo dzra mɔ bubu si woɖe ɖe go alo womama le mɔ bubu me zãa eƒe safuiwo siwo wòle dzram ɖa be wòaɖe mɔ na gaɖoɖo la.

## Gakotoku Si Doa Ðe Eŋu Kple Asikplɔwo Dzra Mɔwo

Ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo ate ŋu akɔ gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu kple asikplɔwo dzra mɔ, eye ɖe sia ɖe wɔnɛ dɔ vovovo.

### Gakotoku Si Doa Ðe Eŋu

Nu kae nye gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu?

Gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la wɔa dɔ le kɔmpiuta alo mobile device dzi, eye wòwɔa dɔ geɖe siwo le gaɖoɖo ƒe tutu me, abe:

- Didienu siwo woazã ƒe tiatia
- Gaɖoɖo ƒe tutu
- Gaɖoɖo ƒe nyawo siwo hiã ƒe xexlẽ
- Zero-knowledge ɖaseɖigbalɛ siwo hiã ƒe wɔwɔ ƒe hadede.

### Nu ka ta gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu le vevie?

Gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la wɔa gaɖoɖo ƒe tutu ƒe dɔ, ke ne malware le wò kɔmpiuta dzi la, ame si va aɖu nuwo ate ŋu:

- Akpɔ nusi nèle wɔm,
- Atrɔ gaɖoɖo si womedo asi te o,
- Ado ŋusẽ akpɔ mɔ be wòaxɔ nya aɖe dzi.

Megahiã be gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la nakpɔ ɣaɣla safui si le asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la me o; eyata ate ŋu awɔ gaɖoɖo ƒe tutu kple kɔmpiuta ƒe dɔ bubuwo, eye asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la wɔa dedienɔnɔ ƒe liƒo na ɣaɣla safui la.

### Asikplɔwo Dzra Mɔ

Asikplɔwo dzra mɔ nye ŋutilã ƒe device si mele internet dzi o, si mamaa wò ɣaɣla safuiwo ɖe telefon alo kɔmpiuta siwo kadodo le ŋu, eye wòwɔa abe dzadzraƒe dedie na wò crypto nudzrowo ene.

### Nu ka ta asikplɔwo dzra mɔ le vevie?

Ne malware le wò kɔmpiuta dzi la, ame si va aɖu nuwo ate ŋu:

- Akpɔ nusi nèle wɔm,
- Atrɔ gaɖoɖo si womedo asi te o,
- Ado ŋusẽ akpɔ mɔ be wòaxɔ nya aɖe dzi.

Womate ŋu aɖe ɣaɣla safui la tso asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la me bɔbɔe o; eyata device la ate ŋu awɔ dedienɔnɔ ƒe liƒo.

Mama vovototo si le gakotoku eve siawo dome la le vevie ŋutɔ na ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo, elabena woƒe nɔnɔ kɔ wu nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi gaɖoɖowo, eye woƒe akpaewo kɔ abe ɣaɣla didienuwo, gaɣaɣla ɖeɖe dzesiwo, adzɔgbeɖeɖe [commitment]wo, zero-knowledge ɖaseɖigbalɛwo, kple gaɖoɖo ƒe asidede agbalẽ tewo ene.

Anɔ kɔ na asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la be wòawɔ kɔmpiuta ƒe dɔ ɖe sia ɖe. Le esime la, gakotoku si doa ɖe eŋu la ɖoa gaɖoɖo la ɖo eye wònaa asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la nya siwo hiã na asidede, eye ɣaɣla gazazãsafui la nɔa ɣe le asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la me.

## Kɔkɔe ƒe Ƒeƒeɖeɖe

> Kɔkɔe ƒe ƒeƒeɖeɖe fia be asikplɔwo dzra mɔ me ƒe kɔkɔe siwo ate ŋu aɖe mɔ na fexexe mele dzã o. Kpeɖeŋuwɔla ate ŋu adzra gaɖoɖo la ɖo; ke menɔa asidede agbalẽ te na eo o.

Kɔkɔe ƒe ƒeƒeɖeɖe nye kɔkɔe siwo hiã be woaɖe mɔ na gaɖoɖo la ƒe ƒeƒeɖeɖe tso kpeɖeŋuwɔla gakotoku kple mɔ si dzi wòle dɔ wɔm la ŋu. Le ɣaɣla kɔkɔe siŋlɔlɔ ƒe ɖoɖo me la, kpeɖeŋuwɔla gakotoku ate ŋu atu gaɖoɖo la eye wòaɖo ɖaseɖigbalɛ siwo hiã la ɖo, ke asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la naa gazazãsafui kɔkɔe siwo ŋu hiã la nɔ eya ŋutɔ ƒe ƒeƒe me.

Ne woɖe aɖe kpeɖeŋuwɔla ƒe kɔmpiuta alo telefon la dzi la, ɖɔwɔla aɖe ate ŋu adɔ gbe ɖe gaɖoɖo la ŋu hafi woade asidede agbalẽ te na, gake le nyateƒe me wòmete ŋu ahe kɔkɔe la tso asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la me o. Eya ta kɔkɔe la nɔa asikplɔ ƒe dedie ƒe liƒo me.

Mãma na mɔ kpeɖeŋuwɔla gakotoku la be wòawɔ gaɖoɖo tutu kple xexlẽ ƒe dɔwo, ke wometsɔ mɔ tẽ dea kɔkɔe ƒe nuwo siwo hiã be woaɖe mɔ na gaɖoɖo la ŋu o.

![Key isolation](/content-images/key-isolation.webp)

Nu vevi si woaɖe to eme nye be "nukpɔsafui" menye be app la xɔa nuku ƒe ƒeɖeɖe bɔbɔe aɖe o.

Le Zcash me la, woɖea Full Viewing Key (FVK/UFVK) tso gazazãsafui la me ɖe asi, eye ena mɔ eƒe tɔ la be wòakpɔ eye wòaɖe ɣaɣla ƒe nu siwo ku ɖe eŋu la gɔme, ke mete ŋu aɖe mɔ na fexexewo o. FVK nye gazazãsafui la ƒe dzidzɔ kɔkɔe si menye nyadzɔdzɔ geɖe o.

**Ɣaɣla ƒe nya vevi aɖe le afi sia:**

Eya ta ele be woanya be app la mate ŋu afia ga la o ne UFVK koe le asi na e, gake ate ŋu akpɔ gakotoku la ƒe ɣaɣla gaɖoɖo siwo le eme.

Esia tae wònye nyateƒe wu be woagblɔ be kpeɖeŋuwɔla app la ƒe mɔe nye *nukpɔɖeɖe ƒe mɔ*, le esime menye be woagblɔ ko be "duame ƒe nyatakaka" le asi na e o.

Mɔ bɔbɔe si wòate ŋu agblɔ susu blibo la nye be kpeɖeŋuwɔla gakotoku xɔa kɔkɔe ƒe nu sɔ gbɔ be wòanya nu si le dzɔm na gakotoku la, ke menye kɔkɔe ƒe nu sɔ gbɔ be wòaɖe mɔ na nu si le dzɔm na gakotoku la ƒe ga o. Ke asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la naa fexexe ƒe mɔ nɔ ƒeƒe eye wòzãa e ne zãla la ɖe asi ɖe gaɖoɖo aɖe ŋu ko.

Esi nye kɔkɔe ƒe ƒeƒeɖeɖe ƒe mɔnu vevitɔ si le ɣaɣla Zcash asikplɔwo dzra mɔ ƒe tata siawo megbe, eye wònye mɔnu ma ke si doa go, dɔwɔɖoɖo ɖe dɔwɔɖoɖo dzi, le Ledger, Keystone, kple Hito me, siwo woakpɔ enumake.

## PCZT

PCZT fia be Partially Created Zcash Transaction. Enye pakete si tsɔa Zcash gaɖoɖo kple nyatakaka si hiã be woawɔ nuwuwu na e tso akpa aɖe yi bubu me.

Enye mɔnu si woɖo ɖe se nu na Zcash gaɖoɖo le esime wole tute ge ɖo hafi. Ena mɔ amewo alo mɔwo vovovo be woawɔ gaɖoɖo wɔwɔ ƒe dɔ la ƒe akpa vovovo eye woatsɔ gaɖoɖo si wode asi wɔ ɖe aƒe me la ana wo nɔewo.

Gaɖoɖo tutu le sesĩe wu be woade asidede agbalẽ te na duame ƒe gaɖoɖo bɔbɔe le zero-knowledge crypto ƒe ɖoɖowo me abe Zcash ene. Ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo hia zero-knowledge ɖaseɖigbalɛ siwo hia xexlẽ sesĩe, abe esiwo woana be woazã Halo 2 alo Groth16 wɔ la ene. Eya ta, mɔ siwo ƒe ŋusẽ menɔa geɖe o abe asikplɔwo dzra mɔ ene, ate ŋu aɖe aƒɔ kple dɔwɔŋusẽ si hiã be woawɔ gaɖoɖo ƒe dɔ blibo la katã o.

PCZT ƒe vava wɔa akpa vevi geɖe ƒe kuxiwo le ɣaɣla crypto ƒe tata me. PCZT nye nyatakaka ƒe mɔnu si woɖo ɖe se nu, si ma gaɖoɖo wɔwɔ la ɖe dɔwɔla ƒe akpa vovovo, siwo ɖe asi le nɔewo ŋu, me. Ena mɔ kɔmpiuta ŋutɔ sesĩe aɖe be wòawɔ gaɖoɖo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo kple ɖaseɖigbalɛ wɔwɔ, esime wòde asikplɔwo dzra mɔ si mele kadodo me ƒe dɔ la katã ɖe mɔ ɖeɖe kple asidede agbalẽ teɖeɖe ko ŋu.

### Asikplɔ Kple Fifi ƒe Dzraƒe ƒe Kuxiwo

Togbɔ be asikplɔwo dzra mɔwo naa dedie ƒe nuto na gazazãsafui kɔkɔewo ƒe dzadzra, zi geɖe RAM, xexlẽ ƒe ŋusẽ, kple nyatakaka ƒe ɖoɖo ƒe ŋusẽ wɔnɔa wo si le sue wu kpeɖeŋuwɔla ƒe kɔmpiutawo. Ƒomevi siawo wɔa be masɔ be woawɔ zero-knowledge ɖaseɖigbalɛ wɔwɔ si hia xexlẽ sesĩe alo woanɔ gaɖoɖo ƒe nɔnɔme blibo si hiã le gaɖoɖo tutu ƒe dɔ la me ŋu o.

Eya ta, kpeɖeŋuwɔla gakotoku ate ŋu awɔ dɔ si hia xexlẽ sesĩe lawo, esime asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la ɖoa eƒe susu ɖe dedie ƒe dɔ vevi, si nye gaɖoɖo la mɔ ɖeɖe kple eƒe gazazãsafui siwo woɣla ɖo ŋu.

Ena wòbia be, ne mɔ la ƒe nɔƒe le kekeake alea, aleke wòwɔa *nu si sɔ* ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu be woate ŋu akaka ɖe eŋu?

Nyateƒe la nye be, asikplɔwo dzra mɔ mate ŋu axɔ asixɔxɔ aɖe tso kpeɖeŋuwɔla ƒe kɔmpiuta me eye wòade asidede agbalẽ te na e o. Ehiã nyatakaka sɔ gbɔ be wòaɖe kpe ɖe edzi be asixɔxɔ si woade asidede agbalẽ te na la sɔ kple gaɖoɖo si zãla la di be wòaɖe mɔ na. Esia wɔa nu sesĩe aɖe, elabena ele be mɔ la naxlem eye wòakpɔ nu sɔ gbɔ be wòaɣla asidede agbalẽ teɖeɖe ƒe dɔ la, esime wònɔ aƒɔ, xexlẽ, firmware ƒe lolome, kple dɔwɔɖoɖo ƒe sesĩe ƒe liƒo sesĩe me.

Ledger ƒe Orchard PCZT ƒe wɔwɔ nye kpɔɖeŋu na kuxi sia, eye wòfia be circuit ƒe ƒomevi ŋu nuxlẽ si woɖo ɖe eŋu tẽ ate ŋu awɔ be dɔwɔɖoɖo la nɔ bɔbɔe wu le mɔ siwo wɔnɔa kekeake ŋu, gake ate ŋu ahiã firmware ƒe tɔtrɔ ne gaɖoɖo ƒe tatawo alo circuit siwo woɖea mɔ na wo la trɔ.

Dɔ siwo wole wɔm yi edzi eve fia ɖoɖo vovovo na fifi ƒe ƒomevi sia ke.

Wotsɔ ŋuɖoɖo ƒe nyawo ɖe gbe me ku ɖe proving pipeline si wòwɔa dɔ le ɣeyiɣi ma ke me, eye wòsɔ na open-source asikplɔwo dzra mɔ ƒe tatawo kple kɔmpiutawo ŋu le Grant #423 me. Taɖodzinu la nye be woanɔ crypto ƒe mɔnu si ate ŋu woawɔ ɖe dedie le teƒe siwo abe C alo Rust me la ŋu, esime wole ɣaɣla Zcash ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu ƒe sesĩe kple dɔdzɔ ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu ɖe anyi.

Ke mɔ bubu aɖe nye esi woɖo ɖe **Zcash SeedSigner** ŋu, si nye signer si me kadodo mele o, eye woɖe e tso nu siwo mexɔ asi o, siwo wòle bɔbɔe be woakpɔ, me. Eƒe dɔwɔmɔ si woɖo ɖe eŋu la na kpeɖeŋuwɔla ƒe kɔmpiuta la nɔ ɖe signer ƒe mɔ la ŋu, eye wòna PCZT to yame ƒe teƒe ma me be woakpɔ e ɖe mɔ la dzi eye woade asidede agbalẽ te na e.

### PCZT Na Mɔnu Ƒe Mamã Ƒe Mɔ

Partially Created Zcash Transaction (PCZT) ƒe se nye nyatakaka ƒe tata si wɔa kadodo le kpeɖeŋuwɔla gakotoku kple asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la dome.

Kpeɖeŋuwɔla gakotoku ate ŋu ati didienuwo, axexlẽ gaɣaɣla ɖeɖe dzesiwo, eye wòawɔ zero-knowledge ɖaseɖigbalɛ la katã le eya ŋutɔ ƒe ŋusẽ me. Esia dzea, elabena nyatakaka si hiã na afɔ siawo, si me kɔkɔe si wozaa be woaɖe gaɣaɣla ɖeɖe dzesiwo gɔme hã le, tso nukpɔsafui la me, menye nu aɖe si do ɖe fexexe ƒe mɔ ŋu o. Emegbe kpeɖeŋuwɔla gakotoku la tsɔa nu siawo dea PCZT ƒe nuɖeɖe me.

Woatsɔa PCZT la dea asikplɔwo dzra mɔ la, si xlea gaɖoɖo ƒe nɔnɔme (abe ga ƒe lolomewo kple amesi woanɔa ga na la ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo ene), eye wòde asidede agbalẽ te kple eƒe gazazãsafui siwo woɣla ɖo la, eye wòtsɔ PCZT si woayɔ ɖe eŋu la na kpeɖeŋuwɔla ƒe mɔ la.

Tata sia ɖoa dedie ƒe liƒo sesĩe: host ƒe asikplɔ sesĩe ate ŋu awɔ zero-knowledge ƒe mathematics sesĩe, esime menye be wòasu gazazãsafuiwo ŋu o, eye fifi ƒe dzraƒe ƒe mɔ siwo wɔnɔa ŋusẽ me ɖe asi ate ŋu akpɔ ga ƒe dedie ŋu, esime menye be woaxexlẽ crypto sesĩe o.

### Prover Ƒe Ɣaɣla Ƒe Ƒeɖeɖewo

Togbɔ be zero-knowledge ɖaseɖigbalɛ wɔwɔ ƒe dɔ ƒe nana na kpeɖeŋuwɔla gakotoku alo prover bubu aɖe na gazazãsafuiwo nɔ ƒeƒe, gake ehiã be prover la nakpɔ gaɖoɖo ƒe nyatakaka siwo wɔnɔa kɔkɔe, abe didienu ƒe ga ƒe lolomewo, amesiwo woanɔa ga na wo la ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo, kple adzɔgbeɖeɖewo ene.

PCZT naa tata ƒe mama nɔ anyi to eme be woate ŋu ana nyatakaka siawo na proving, esime womeva tsɔ gazazãsafui kɔkɔe siwo hiã be woaɖe mɔ na gaɖoɖo o.

Esia ɖea kpe ɖe edzi be proving ƒe mɔ ɖe fexexe ƒe mɔ ŋu. Prover la ate ŋu awɔ ɖaseɖigbalɛ wɔwɔ ƒe dɔ si hia xexlẽ sesĩe, esime wòmeɖea crypto ƒe mɔ si ate ŋu wòazã zãla la ƒe ga o.

### PCZT Ƒe Nɔƒeɖekɔkɔe

Ne PCZT zu akpa vevitɔ le Zcash ƒe asikplɔwo dzra mɔ, asidede agbalẽ teɖeɖe geɖe, kple siŋlɔlɔ si me kadodo mele o ƒe dɔwɔmɔwo me la, nɔƒeɖekɔkɔe le dɔwɔɖoɖowo siwo ɖe asi le nɔewo ŋu me zu nu vevitɔ geɖe.

Ŋuɖoɖo aɖe si AngryDavee wɔ le June 2026 me la de dzesi be PCZT ƒe dɔwɔɖoɖowo nɔ xɔ asi le dɔwo me, siwo me Ledger, Keystone, Hito, OneKey, kple dɔwɔɖoɖo si wozãa abe kpɔɖeŋu le librustzcash me le. Ke ŋuɖoɖo la kpɔ be test siwo woama be woakpɔ be dɔwɔɖoɖo siawo gɔme PCZTwo nenɔ mɔ ma ke nu mele o.

Nuɖoɖo si woɖo ɖe eŋu nye open-source nɔƒeɖekɔkɔe ƒe test ƒe ƒuƒoƒo si me PCZT ƒe test ƒe vector siwo wotsɔ ɖo se nu kple test siwo doa to dɔwɔɖoɖo vovovo me, siwo kpɔ create → sign → finalize ƒe dɔ blibo la, le. Wogblɔ be woado test hã ɖe dɔwɔla ƒe liƒo siwo abe Combiner ene ŋu, afi si woate ŋu aƒo nyatakaka si woade asidede agbalẽ te ɖe eŋu kpuie tso signer vovovowo gbɔ hafi woawɔ gaɖoɖo aɖe nuwuwu.

Esia le vevie na asikplɔwo dzra mɔwo elabena PCZT na gaɖoɖo tutu kple asidede agbalẽ teɖeɖe ƒe mama: kpeɖeŋuwɔla gakotoku ate ŋu atu gaɖoɖo eye wòadzra PCZT ɖo, esime asikplɔ ƒe mɔ la xɔa asidede agbalẽ teɖeɖe ƒe nyatakaka si hiã la eye wòwɔa dedie ƒe dɔ vevitɔ si nye asidede agbalẽ teɖeɖe la.

Womewɔ ŋuɖoɖo la o elabena Zcash Community Grants tia be yemanɔ ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu la ŋu o le ŋuɖoɖo la ƒe kpɔkpɔ kple hadome ƒe numekpɔkpɔ megbe. Eya ta womeɖo test ƒe ƒuƒoƒo si woɖo ɖe eŋu be wòato dɔwɔɖoɖo vovovo me la ɖe anyi to ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu sia me o. Eya ta enye ɖoɖo si woɖo ɖe PCZT ƒe nɔƒeɖekɔkɔe ƒe kuxi si woade dzesi la ŋu, menye se si le fifia le Zcash katã me o.

- App si wo tsɔ ɖo eme

### YWallet ƒe cold signing; mɔ si va do ŋgɔ na

Ywallet nye gakotoku si kplɔa eŋu, si le Ledger kple Zondax siaa gbɔ vovo, eye wòdze eŋutɔ ƒe mɔ dzi yi ɣaɣla cold signing gbɔ.

Ywallet le vevie le ŋutinya me, elabena wòkpɔ cold signing na Zcash ɖe, si fia be woate ŋu aɖe mɔ̃ si dea asidede agbalẽ te la ɖa le gakotoku si le internet dzi gbɔ.

Be woawɔ architecture ma le Ledger hardware ŋu la, Ywallet tu eŋutɔ ƒe Ledger app, si nye app si nye wo tɔ ŋutɔ, eye wòvovo na Zondax app si me míeƒo nu tsoe le akpa si va do ŋgɔ me. Esia fia be Ywallet mele Zondax ƒe Sapling wɔwɔ si le xoxo me ŋu ɖoɖo o; eŋutɔ ƒe firmware le asidede agbalẽ te ƒe akpa dzi wɔm.

### Nu si ta haɗomeɖoɖoa le vevie

Cryptography kple asidede agbalẽ te code ƒe nunya ko medze o. Asikplɔwo dzra mɔ ƒe haɗomeɖoɖo menye cryptography ƒe kuxi ko o, ke ele be wòanye distribution kple platform ƒe kuxi hã. Le etɔme la, wòhia:

- App ƒe dzɔdzɔ/mafa — ne hardware ƒe wɔla ƒe app store si le mɔ̃ me la axɔ app la eye wòamae
- Firmware/app ƒe dedienɔnɔ — ne app la de wɔla la ƒe dedienɔnɔ ƒe hiahiãwo dzi na key material ƒe kplɔɖoɖo
- Communication protocols — ale si gakotoku si kplɔa eŋu kple mɔ̃ la nɔ gaɖoɖo ƒe nyawo mɔm na wo nɔewo
- Awɔ ɖeka kple gakotoku — ne asidede agbalẽ te app ƒe data ƒe formats kple mɔ la de nu si gakotoku si kplɔa eŋu la le mɔm ŋu
- Gaƒuƒoƒowo siwo wòdea ɣaɣla kpekpeɖeŋu na wo — Sapling, Orchard, (eye fifia Ironwood) dome kae app la ate ŋu ade asidede agbalẽ te na

Ywallet ƒe eŋutɔ ƒe Ledger app la de cryptography kple architecture ƒe hiahiãwo dzi; ete ŋu dea asidede agbalẽ te na ɣaɣla Zcash gaɖoɖowo, ke Ledger ƒe app store si le mɔ̃ me la mexɔe o.

### Tso eŋutɔ ƒe Ledger app gbɔ yi

Esi wo mexɔ app la le mɔ̃ me o ta la, ele be Ywallet abia tso ezalawo gbɔ be woaɖo firmware si mewo de asidede agbalẽ te la be woazãe. Esia nye dedienɔnɔ ƒe ametsitsi vavã na ezalawo, eye Ywallet metia be yebia esia tso wo gbɔ o. Eya ta Ywallet va de asi le haɗomeɖoɖo ma tɔxɛ ŋu le emegbe le mɔ ma dzi zɔzɔ ƒe teƒe.

Le app si nye wo tɔxɛ ƒe gbugbɔtsɔtsɔ ƒe teƒe la, emegbe ƒe ŋutinunya (to Zkool, si nye Ywallet ƒe successor) yi Zondax si tu Ledger app si le mɔ̃ me gbɔ. Esia trɔ wɔwɔ si wo mexɔ o, si nye wo tɔxɛ, ɖe wɔwɔ si woɖo ɖeka, si wo xɔ va do le Ledger ƒe distribution ƒe nutowo me. Trɔtrɔ siae na Ledger ƒe haɗomeɖoɖo si wɔa dɔ va dzɔ gbɔ ake.

## 2. Keystone

**Nu kae nye Keystone?**

Keystone nye asikplɔwo dzra mɔ si woɖe ɖa tso kadodo me. Eƒe mɔ̃ si dea asidede agbalẽ te la mekpe ɖe wire alo wireless kadodo aɖeke ŋu kple app si kplɔa eŋu la le asidede agbalẽ te ŋutɔ ƒe ɣeyiɣi me o, ke wòdea gbe to QR codes me. Le afisia la, woɖe e kple Zashi (si fifia wonya be Zodl), Zcash ƒe gakotoku si kplɔa eŋu si nye eŋutɔ tɔ, eye woƒe kadodo sia na key ƒe aɖaŋuɖoɖo ƒe gɔmesesea doa go kple be wòle bɔbɔe be woalé woƒe mɔ me.

**Keystone/Zashi ƒe mɔ**

Keystone ƒe Zcash haɗomeɖoɖo kple Zashi na mama sia doa go vevie. Ne nèkpe Keystone ɖe Zashi ŋu la, woaɖe Unified Full Viewing Key la tso Keystone gbɔ ayi Zashi gbɔ.

Eya ta mɔ lae nye:

![Keystone and Zashi viewing flow](/content-images/keystone-zashi-viewing-flow.webp)

**Nu kae Zashi ate ŋu awɔ kple UFVK?**

Zashi ate ŋu azãe be wòawɔ:

- Dze sii ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo siwo gbɔna
- Ɖo balancewo ƒe home
- Kpɔ nyawo tso ɣaɣla ƒe nudzɔdzɔ ŋu

Ke womate ŋu azã gaawo ɖe eya ta pɛ be yexɔ UFVK o.

UFVK nye "nukpɔsafui ƒe ŋusẽ" si na mɔ be woasrɔ balancewo kple gaɖoɖowo ŋu, ke menoa mɔ na gazazã o.

### Nu kae nɔ anyi le Keystone me?

Seed/gazazã ŋusẽ la nɔ anyi le Keystone me. Zashi mexɔ seed la, gazazãsafui la, alo spend authorization private key la o. Wòxɔ UFVK, gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ƒe nyawo, kple nyawo siwo hiã na view only wɔwɔ.

Ne woƒo akpa eve siawo ɖeka la, woana nɔnɔa ƒe nɔnɔ blibo. Nu siwo doa le mɔ̃ la me, abe UFVK, gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo, kple watch only nyawo ene la, de be Zashi nakpɔ account la dzi, ke nu aɖeke si doa le mɔ̃ la me meate ŋu ana woatsɔ ga aɖe adzoe o.

Esia tae Zashi kple Keystone ƒe haɗomeɖoɖo ate ŋu ana ɣaɣla cold storage. Gakotoku ƒe app la ate ŋu akpɔ account la dzi ne mexɔ ŋusẽ si hiã be wòazã ga tsoe o. Woɖe haɗomeɖoɖo sia ɖe tɔxɛ be wòana cold storage na ɣaɣla ZEC.

## 3. Hito

Hito zã key ƒe aɖaŋuɖoɖo ƒe model si sɔ ŋutɔ kple Keystone tɔ, ke eƒe wɔwɔ nye yeye wu. Le gakotoku si kplɔa eŋu xoxo aɖe abe Zashi ene ƒe kadodo teƒe la, Hito va kple eŋutɔ ƒe Android gakotoku, eye onboarding ƒe mɔ le hardware mɔ̃ kple Android app ma dome la woŋlɔ ɖe Hito ƒe development ƒe dzidzimeɣeyiɣiwo me.

**Onboarding: nu si Hito app la xɔ**

Android gakotoku la zã:

- Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK)
- Unified Address
- ZIP-32 seed fingerprint
- Network ƒe nyawo

Ne wole hardware account la tsɔm gbɔ ɖe.

Ŋku ɖe edzi be Android app la mexɔ hardware wallet ƒe seed alo private spending keys o.

![Hito onboarding](/content-images/hito-onboarding.webp)

**Nu kae Hito app la xɔ?**

Onboarding ƒe nyawo si woŋlɔ ɖe agbalẽ me la me:

- UFVK
- Unified Address
- ZIP-32 seed fingerprint
- Network

UFVK na Android gakotoku la nyawo siwo wòhia be wòawɔ account la ƒe dɔ abe gakotoku si ate ŋu akpɔ nu ene, ne mexɔ seed la o. Seed fingerprint nye dzesi si ƒe didime le ɖeka, si woɖe tso seed me, eye wozãna be woanya account kae nye ka. Menye seed la ŋutɔ o, eye menaa gazazã ƒe ŋusẽ aɖeke o, eya tae eƒe nɔnɔ mele "mexɔ seed o" ƒe nyagblɔɖia ŋu o.

**Nu kae nɔ anyi le Hito me?**

Seed vavã la, private spending keys, kple asidede agbalẽ te ƒe ŋusẽ nɔ anyi le hardware mɔ̃ la me. Emegbe Hito wɔa asidede agbalẽ te offline.

Hito ƒe dzidzimeɣeyiɣi si le fifia gblɔ be wɔwɔ ƒe mɔ blibo na wɔwɔ, export, offline asidede agbalẽ te, kple import le dɔ wɔm, eye wòtsɔ BLE transport kple BBQr QR export na PCZT si woɖe asidede agbalẽ te.

Ele be woayɔ mɔ sia ƒe ŋkɔ kpoo, elabena enye cycle blibo si woƒo nu tsoe tsã la ƒe vavã ƒe kpɔɖeŋu: Android gakotoku la wɔa PCZT si mewo de asidede agbalẽ te o, eye wòɖe e ɖa na Hito, Hito dea asidede agbalẽ te offline kple eƒe key material si woɖe ɖa, eye woatsɔ PCZT si woɖe asidede agbalẽ te la agbugbɔɖe Android gakotoku la me.

Le ɣeyiɣi aɖeke me la, seed alo gazazãsafui la metɔa mɔgbɔ sia dzi le afima alo afima o.

## Orchard kple Ironwood: Asikplɔwo dzra mɔ [hardware wallet] ƒe Ŋgɔyiyi

Orchard nye ɣaɣla [shielded] gaƒuƒoƒo le Zcash ƒe mɔnu me, si na mɔ zazãlawo be woaɖo kple awɔ gaɖoɖo kple ZEC kple ɣaɣla si sesẽ wu. Woɖe eɖe gɔme le Mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ [Network Upgrade] 5 (NU5) me, eye Orchard zãa Halo 2 ɖaseɖigbalɛ [proof] ƒe wɔwɔnu kple ameɖeɖe ƒe mɔnu yeye si woɖo ɖe asaɣa ƒe ɣaɣla nuwo dzi, be wòakpe ɖe ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo ŋu, ne womaɖe gaɖoɖo ƒe nyatakakawo fia o, abe ame si dɔ ga la, ame si xɔe, alo gaƒeƒe ene.

Ironwood nye ɣaɣla gaƒuƒoƒo si woɖe gɔme le Zcash ƒe NU6.3 mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ me abe Orchard gaƒuƒoƒo ƒe teƒenɔla ene. Ezãa Orchard ƒe asaɣa ɣaɣla mɔnu, eye wòtsɔ tɔtrɔwo va si woɖo be woatsɔ asɔ ɣaɣla ga ƒe dedie le ɣeyiɣi didi me, si me quantum ƒe gagbɔɖeɖe kple kadodo ƒe nyateƒe ƒe kaɖedzi sesẽ wu hã le.

Le gɔmedzedze la, ele vevie be woanyae be Ironwood metsi Orchard mɔnu la ɖe asaɣa ɣaɣla ƒe wɔwɔnu yeye blibo me o. Woɖe Ironwood gɔme abe ɣaɣla gaƒuƒoƒo yeye si wotu tso Orchard mɔnu si le xoxo la dzi ene. Esia fia be Action ƒe nɔnɔme ma ke wɔnɔ, Halo 2 ɖaseɖigbalɛ ƒe wɔwɔnu ma ke wɔnɔ, eye didienu [note] kple safuiwo ƒe tutuwo hã wɔnɔ nenema.

Ironwood si tɔ ŋutɔ didienu ƒe adzɔgbeɖeɖe [commitment] ti, gaɣaɣla ɖeɖe dzesi [nullifier] ƒe ƒuƒoƒo, anchor, kple asixɔxɔ gaƒuƒoƒo le esi, ale gbegbɔgblɔ ɖi [consensus] ƒe didime la, enye gaƒuƒoƒo bubu vavã. Ke le asaɣa ɣaɣla ƒe nyagblɔme la, enye Orchard ƒe hehe keke, menye wotu eɖe gɔme tso gɔmedzedze o.

Woɖoe nenema be:

1. **Ga ƒe kadodo ƒe nyateƒe.** Le May 29, 2026 dzi la, dedienɔnɔ ƒe nudzɔla Taylor Hornby ɖe nu vɔ̃ aɖe si sesẽ gã le Orchard ƒe zero-knowledge ɖaseɖigbalɛ ƒe wɔwɔnu me fia ɖe ame siwo le dɔa me le ameɖeɖe me. Nu vɔ̃ sia ate ŋu na mɔ be woawɔ ZEC si menye nyateƒe o, ne wometsɔ dzesi aɖeke de ɖoɖonu dzi o. Esi Orchard ƒe gaɖoɖo nyatakakawo nye ameɖeɖe tɔ, ame aɖeke mate ŋu akpɔe ɖe eɖokui ŋutɔ be ZEC si menye nyateƒe o mewɔ o hafi woɖɔ nu vɔ̃a ɖo o. Gaƒuƒoƒo xoxo la ƒe mamam kple yeye aɖe ƒe gɔmedzedze le zero dzi ɖɔa nya sia ɖo le mɔ si me Orchard ƒe ɖɔɖɔ ɖe teƒe ma ke me mate ŋu awɔ o.
2. **Be quantum ƒe gagbɔɖeɖe nanɔ anyi.** Woɖo Ironwood be ga siwo le eme la nate ŋu akpɔ gome le gagbɔɖeɖe ƒe mɔnu aɖe me le etsɔme, ne discrete log ƒe susu siwo wotu eɖe dzi la gbã ɖeɣe aɖe.
3. **Be wɔwɔ ƒe tɔtrɔ nanɔ sue.** Le ɣaɣla mɔnu yeye blibo ƒe ɖoɖo teƒe la, wozã Orchard ƒe nɔnɔme si woɖo kpe ɖe eŋu xoxo. Esia fia be gaɖoɖo, ɖaseɖigbalɛ, gakotoku, kple asikplɔwo dzra mɔ ƒe kɔdidi yeye geɖe menye be woate ŋu aŋlɔ tso gɔmedzedze o.

- July ƒe gɔmedzedze: NU6.3 va se testnet; Zebra 6.0.0 va do go le July 10.
- July 27–28: Ledger ƒe Ironwood PCZT v2 ƒe asidede agbalẽ te ƒe dɔ wɔwɔ va le haɖeɖe ƒe branch aɖe me. Ironwood (NU6.3) dze egɔme le July 28 le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe 3,428,143, eye wòma Orchard gaƒuƒoƒo gbãtɔ la ɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒe xexlẽdzikpɔɖeƒe [turnstile] aɖe megbe si de se na ZEC ƒe agbɔdodo tso Orchard me be wòanɔ ga si woɖo ɖe eme le nyateƒe me ƒe ƒeƒe ko, eye wòtsɔ gaɖoɖo ƒe nɔnɔme yeye va.

Zebra zu ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ [node] si wòle be woazã, elabena zcashd mekpe ɖe gbegbɔgblɔ ɖi ƒe se yeyeawo ŋu o. Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo metrɔ o, elabena Ironwood zãa Orchard ƒe xɔla ƒe nɔnɔme, ale gakotokuwɔlaawo trɔa gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi siwo li xoxo la ɖe gaƒuƒoƒo yeye la me le woawo ŋutɔ. Nu si gakotokuwɔlaawo hiã enye trɔtrɔ ƒe dɔwɔwɔ, elabena ele be owners wɔnɛ woaɖe Orchard ƒe gaawo ɖe Ironwood me le ɣeyiɣi aɖe me, eye gbegbɔgblɔ ɖi ƒe sewo ƒe mɔnu kpu Orchard ƒe xɔxɔ le didienu yeyeawo me le blibo.

### Nu siwo trɔ le mɔ̃ ƒe didime

Gaɖoɖo ƒe nɔnɔmewo trɔ tso version 5 yi version 6, ale gaɖoɖo ɖeka mate ŋu atsɔ Ironwood ƒe akpa aɖe atsɔ kpe ɖe Sapling/Orchard/nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi [transparent] ƒe akpawoe ŋu. Wohe PCZT ɖe ŋgɔ le afɔɖeɖe siawo me:

- Ƒe kodidi tso PCZT v1 yi v2.
- Ironwood ƒe bundle aɖe le eɖokui ŋutɔ me nɔ fifia ɖe Sapling kple Orchard tɔwo ƒe gbɔ.
- Woɖe mɔ na v6 gaɖoɖowo kple v6 sighash yeye.
- Didienu ƒe plaintextwo kpɔ version yeye (V3).

Sighash ƒe tɔtrɔa le vevie wu na asikplɔwo dzra mɔwo. Le v6 me la, woɖe Orchard kple Ironwood ƒe anchorwo ɖe asidede agbalẽ te ƒe hash la me, eye woɖo woƒe nyawo ɖe authorizing data digest ko me. Esia nye firmware ƒe tɔtrɔ vavã, menye version ƒe kekpɔkpɔ aɖe ko o. Ele be mɔ aɖe wɔ sighash yeye la nyuie eye wòaxle eya ŋutɔ, boŋ wòaɖo dzi ɖe nu si companion app la gblɔ be wòle be wòana mɔ na la dzi o.

Mɔ̃ ƒe mama ƒe nubabla la metrɔ o: mɔ la gbugbɔ naa spend authorization signature ko, metsi `alpha` o, eye metsi binding signature hã o; host la gale ɖaseɖigbalɛ wɔwɔ kple nu bubuwo katã wɔm.

Ke asidela ƒe se ɖeka trɔ le v6 kple: IO Finalizer nea asidede agbalẽ te na dummy padding spends ɖe mɔ la teƒe, elabena mɔ la mekpɔ mɔ be wòaɖe wo akpɔ kple didienu ƒe nyatakaka vavãwo o. Mɔ la ƒe Signer ƒe dɔmeleɖeɖe gblẽa wo, le be woabia e be wòana asidede agbalẽ te na nu si mate ŋu akpɔ nyuie o teƒe. Adzɔ siwo woɖea fia la hã xɔa Orchard kple Ironwood ƒe value balance evea ƒe xexlẽƒe ɖekae, elabena v6 gaɖoɖo aɖe ate ŋu awɔ gaɖoɖo tso gaƒuƒoƒo evea me le gaɖoɖo ma ke me.

Gaƒuƒoƒo ƒe xexlẽsia ƒe nyawo ƒe wɔwɔ nyuie le vevie le dɔme, menye le susu me ko o. Nu vɔ̃ aɖe si va yɛe le Brave ƒe Zcash gakotoku ŋutɔ me fiaa nya sia nyuie: woƒe adzɔ ƒe xexlẽɖoɖo la bu be ɣaɣla ƒe nusiwo katã tso Orchard gaƒuƒoƒo xoxo la me, eye esia va wɔnɛ be Actionwo nagbugbɔ anyi kple adzɔ ƒe xexlẽ ƒe nɔnɔme si menyo o ne gaɖoɖo aɖe me Ironwood le vavã. Esiae nye vodada ƒomevi si "nàxlẽe wòawo ŋutɔ, megaɖo dzi ɖe host la ŋu o" ƒe gɔmeɖeɖe si le asikplɔwo dzra mɔ ƒe asidede agbalẽ te megbe la le be wòakpɔ. Companion gakotoku aɖe wɔ gaƒuƒoƒo ƒe xexlẽsia vɔ̃, eye mɔ aɖe si kpɔa eya ŋutɔ ƒe xexlẽ nyuie le woawo ŋutɔ ko ate ŋu afia esia hafi wòana asidede agbalẽ te.

### Teƒe si asidela ɖe sia ɖe le

**Ledger trɔa nɔnɔme blibo la tututu.** Le September 23, 2026 dzi la, Ledger ɖe ɣaɣla Zcash ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu si tso eɖokui me va le Ledger Wallet Desktop me, eye companion app bubu aɖeke meghiã o. Wò gazazãsafui [spending key] nɔa mɔ la dzi, kɔmputa la wɔa gaɖoɖo ƒe xexlẽwo, eye nàɖo mɔ na nuwo katã le screen la dzi hafi woana asidede agbalẽ te. Ekpe ɖe Ironwood ko ŋu, menye Orchard alo Sapling gaƒuƒoƒo xoxoawo o. Ke nu vɔ̃a enye be "Zcash Shielded" app xoxo si Zondax tu, si hiã companion gakotoku abe Zkool be wòawɔ dɔ, le woɖem le dɔ wɔwɔ me. Ledger ale app xoxo sia le November 5, ale ame siwo gale ezãm la katã le be woaɖe woƒe gaawo hafi ɣeyiɣi ma nava.

**Keystone le nɔnɔme nyui me.** Firmware 3.0.2 tsɔ Ironwood ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu blibo va le July me, eye esiae gale nye hiaƒe si le fifia kple si menɔ kokoko o. Ke ele be version ma alo si le eƒe ŋgɔ wu nanɔ anyi be woate ŋu aɖe asidede agbalẽ te na nu aɖeke.

**Hito do ŋgɔ yi be woɖe eƒe testnet ƒe dzizizi gbãtɔ.** Ewɔ asidede agbalẽ te kple dɔmedzo ɖe Orchard-yi-Ironwood gaɖoɖo vavã gbãtɔ la dzi le asikplɔwo dzra mɔ vavã dzi (gbã la le testnet dzi). Tɔtrɔ bubuwo do kpe ɖe mainnet ƒe mɔnuwo (Ironwood→Ironwood, Ironwood→Nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi, kple bubuwo) ŋu le production hardware dzi. Firmware si menɔ kokoko o kple mɔɖeɖe si ɖe asi le ame geɖe ŋu la gale dɔ wɔm ɖe eŋu.

Zondax kple Zkool le tɔtrɔ me fifia. Zondax ƒe app lae woɖele le Ledger dzi. Zkool, si zãa app ma be wòanɔ nu ƒom kple Ledger mɔwo, megblɔ nu aɖeke be nu ka ava dzɔ na dɔmeleɖeɖe ma ne app xoxo la bu o. Meɖe fia o be wòanɔ vevie na Ledger ƒe zazãlawo le etsɔme, alo ne eƒe etsɔme anɔ vevie le mɔ bubuwo kple.

Trɔtrɔ ŋutɔe nye ŋugbegbe gãtɔ. Esi gaƒeƒe si le gaƒuƒoƒoawo dome ƒe tsetse nye nu si ame katã ate ŋu akpɔ, gakotokuwɔlaawo maa gaɖeɖewo ɖe denomination siwo wozãna la me le ɣeyiɣi aɖe me, le be woaɖe balance katã le akpa ɖeka si ame katã ate ŋu akpɔ me teƒe. Esia fia be gaɖoɖo geɖe le be woaɖe asidede agbalẽ te na le trɔtrɔ sia me, eye esiae ta batch PCZT ƒe asidede agbalẽ te le vevie ŋutɔ na asikplɔwo dzra mɔwo.

Ledger ƒe firmware ƒe dɔ hã do go kpe ɖe asikplɔwo dzra mɔ ƒe seƒoƒowo ŋu: didienu ƒe gaɖeɖe vavã aɖe ate ŋu ase asaɣa ƒe system call akpe geɖe wu le sedede ɖeka me, eye esia wɔnɛ be mɔ la ƒe watchdog nanɔ dɔ me. Dɔmedzoe la tsɔ field arithmetic yi software me, eye wòhia scratch state si woama gbɔ be wòanɔ Nano X ƒe stack ƒe seƒoƒowo me.
