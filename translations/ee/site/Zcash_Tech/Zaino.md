# Zaino ƒe Indexer

Zaino nye Indexer, si Zingo ƒuƒoƒoa wɔ le Rust, si ƒe taɖodzinue nye be yeaɖɔli lightwalletd eye yeatsɔ zcashd ƒe asixɔxɔ dzi ɖeɖe kpɔtɔ ƒe dɔa ayi ŋgɔe.

Zaino naa nu veviwo na kekeli ƒe asisiwo siaa, abe gakotokuwo kple dɔwɔɖoɖo siwo mehiã blockchain ŋutinya bliboa o, kple asisiwo alo gakotoku blibowo ene. Edoa alɔ block explorers hã, enaa mɔnukpɔkpɔ be woakpɔ blockchain si wowu enu kple kɔsɔkɔsɔ nyuitɔ kekeake si womewu enu o kple mempool si Zebra alo Zcashd blibo validator kpɔna la siaa.

## Nukatae woawɔ Indexer yeye?

Susu vevitɔe nye dzadzraɖo ɖe etsɔme ŋu. Wotu Zcashd kple lightwalletd le ƒe 2016 me forked tso bitcoind code, zã C plus. Nuƒolanɔƒe kple kɔpi si wozãna tsɔ tua dɔwɔna eveawo la le tsitsim, esesẽ be woadzi wo ɖe edzi, alé be na wo eye woatu egbegbe nɔnɔmewo ɖe wo dzi.

Rust nye egbegbe gbegbɔgblɔ si me ŋusẽ le eye wòle dedie si na Zcash te ŋu dzrana ɖo ɖe etsɔme ŋgɔyiyi ŋu, si kpea dɔwɔla yeyewo be woatu dɔwɔwɔ yeye geɖe ɖe Zcash ƒe lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe ɖoɖoa dzi kple eƒe golɔgoewo.

Ke hã, Zaino ɖoe be yeawɔ ɖeka kple megbe le afisi wòanya wɔ le, Nana APIwo kple ŋgɔdonya siwo kpena ɖe ame ŋu be woaɖe masɔmasɔ dzi akpɔtɔ le wo xɔxɔ me eye woakpɔ egbɔ be Zcash ƒe lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe ɖoɖo si keke ta wu ate ŋu akpɔ viɖe tso Zainos ƒe ŋgɔyiyiwo me gbugbɔgaŋlɔ alo nusɔsrɔ̃ ƒe fli vevi aɖeke manɔmee.

Azɔ hã, Zaino ana woaɖe kekeli ƒe asitsalawo ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ɖa tso node bliboa gbɔ, to RPC ƒe mɔɖeɖe kple asitsalawo ƒe agbalẽdzraɖoƒe blibo dzi, si ana be dɔwɔlawo nate ŋu awɔ Zaino ɖekae eye woakpɔ kɔsɔkɔsɔ ƒe nyatakakawo tẽ tso woƒe kekeli ƒe asitsaha ƒe dɔwɔwɔ me, si ana nyatakaka vevi siwo tso Zebra node la me nanɔ dedie eye woanɔ dedie.
