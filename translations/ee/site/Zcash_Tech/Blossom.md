# Blossom

> Blossom li va dɔwɔe le Zcash mainnet dzi le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe 653,600 dzi (December 11, 2019 UTC).

Nu si nànya tso esia me: aleke Blossom wɔe be Zcash ƒe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe va ɖo zi eve kabakaba, eye wòtrɔ nu siwo ZEC dziwòna le ŋkeke sia ŋkeke me o.

Blossom nye Zcash [mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ](../start-here/network-upgrades). [ZIP 206](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0206)e wòde asi le edzi, eye gbegbɔgblɔ ɖi ƒe trɔtrɔ vevi si le eme la ɖeɖe le [ZIP 208](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0208). Blossom nye nu ƒe dziyiyi ƒe mɔ̃ trɔtrɔ: eɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe eve dome ƒe ɣeyiɣi si wòɖo ta la tso sekend 150 va sekend 75, ale be gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe va ɖo zi eve kabakaba. Electric Coin Companye kplɔ eye wòɖe nya si wòle le Blossom ŋuti.

Nu ka ta esia le vevie. Ne èdɔ ZEC, èlɔ̃ be mɔ̃ la nakpɔe ɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe me. Ne gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe vaa le blewu, èlɔ̃a ɣeyiɣi didi. Hafi Blossom, wòlɔ̃na be gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe yeye nava ɖe sekend 150 ɖesiaɖe. Blossom ɖe ɣeyiɣi sia ta afã, va sekend 75, ale be ɖeɖeɖeawo vaa kaba eye ɖoɖonu la ate ŋu atsɔ gaɖoɖowo bubuwo zi geɖe le ɣeyiɣi ma ke me. Ewɔ esia, eye wòwɔ be ZEC bubuwo mewɔ o, eye wòtrɔ ɣeyiɣi si le fetu ƒe afãmama siwo gbɔna la o. (NU7, si dze egɔme le November 2026, ɖe ɣeyiɣi si wòɖo ta la ta ake va sekend 25 le [ZIP 218](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0218) nu, eye ewɔ ɖe mɔ̃ ma ke nu.)

## TL;DR

- Blossom nye mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ etɔ̃lia na Zcash, si dze egɔme le mainnet ƒe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe 653,600 dzi (December 11, 2019 UTC); ZIP 206e wòde asi le edzi, eye eƒe trɔtrɔ vevi la le ZIP 208.
- Eɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe dome ƒe ɣeyiɣi si wòɖo ta la tso sekend 150 va sekend 75 — gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe va ɖo zi eve kabakaba, ale be ɖeɖeɖe gbãtɔwo vaa kaba, eye nu siwo wòate ŋu akpɔ le ɣeyiɣi ɖeka me la zɔ zi eve.
- Be wòatsi agbalẽɖeɖe ɖi la, eɖe ga si wònana le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖeka me la afã, eye wòɖe fetu ƒe afãmama ƒe ɣeyiɣi la zi eve, tso gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe 840,000 va 1,680,000.
- ZEC si wònye le ɣeyiɣi ɖeka me eye ɣeyiɣi siwo le fetu ƒe afãmama siwo gbɔna la le ŋkeke sia ŋkeke me medzɔ o.
- Esia nye gbegbɔgblɔ ɖi ƒe trɔtrɔ si wòle be woawɔ: ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ ɖesiaɖe le be wòatrɔ eƒe mɔ̃wo ale be wòagaɖo ɖoɖonu la dzi.

## Visual analogy

Blossom nye bɔsikpɔ mɔ̃ si ƒe mɔzɔzɔwo ɖo zi eve. Fifi bɔsikpɔwo va sekend 75 ɖesiaɖe, si teƒe sekend 150, ale be èlɔ̃ ɣeyiɣi siwo wu afã (ɖeɖeɖeawo vaa kaba), eye mɔ̃ la tsɔa nunɔamesiwo zi eve (nu ƒe dziyiyi). Be wòatsi bɔsikpɔlawo ƒe asitsatsa ɖi la, wòɖe bɔsikpɔla ɖesiaɖe ƒe fetu le mɔzɔzɔ ɖeka me la afã — fetu blibo la sɔ, ke wòɖea le mɔzɔzɔ bubuwo ƒe ŋkeke me.

## Gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe siwo vaa kaba

Blossom ƒe trɔtrɔ vevi la bɔbɔe. Zcash ƒe ɣeyiɣi si wòɖo ta be gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe eve dome nanɔ la, ɖeɖe tso gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖeka va bubu, ɖe le sekend 150 va sekend 75 ([ZIP 208](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0208)). Woɖea gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe to dɔwɔwɔ ƒe ɖaseɖigbalɛ dzi, eya ta gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe eve dome ƒe ɣeyiɣi ŋutɔ ŋutɔ trɔa, gake mɔ̃ la fifi wòɖoa ta be gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe nava ɖe sekend 75 ɖesiaɖe, si teƒe sekend 150.

Nya eve dzɔa esia megbe:

1. Gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe va ɖo zi eve kabakaba, ale be ɖoɖonu la ate ŋu atsɔ gaɖoɖowo zi eve le ɣeyiɣi ɖeka me.
2. Wò gaɖoɖo la kpɔ ɖeɖeɖe gbãtɔ kaba, elabena èmelɔ̃na ɣeyiɣi siwo wu be gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe si kplɔe la nava.

![Before Blossom the block target was 150 seconds with slower confirmations and lower throughput. After Blossom the target is 75 seconds with faster confirmations and roughly double the throughput](/content-images/blossom-block-spacing-50b6bfbacc.webp)

## Agbalẽɖeɖe si wòtsi ɖi

Gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe siwo vaa kaba doa biabia aɖe ɖa. Ne Zcash wɔ gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe zi eve, eye gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖesiaɖe ganye fetu ma ke, mɔ̃ la ana ZEC zi eve kaba. Blossom wɔe be esia megadzɔ o. Eɖe ga si wònana le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖeka me la afã, eye wòɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu ƒe afãmama ƒe ɣeyiɣi zi eve, tso 840,000 va 1,680,000 gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ([ZIP 208](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0208)). Gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe zi eve, ke woɖea afã ɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖesiaɖe, nɔ ɖe ZEC ma ke si wòwɔa le ɣeyiɣi ɖeka me. Agbalẽɖeɖe ƒe ɖoɖo blibo kple fetu ƒe afãmama siwo gbɔna ƒe ɣeyiɣi, si woɖoa le ŋkeke sia ŋkeke me, metrɔ o.

![How Blossom keeps issuance steady: 75 second blocks arrive twice as often, the per-block reward is halved, the halving interval is doubled, so total emission over time stays the same](/content-images/blossom-emission-balance-f2443e29ab.webp)

## Mɔ̃ trɔtrɔ si wòle be woawɔ

Blossom nye gbegbɔgblɔ ɖi ƒe trɔtrɔ si wòle be wòwɔ le ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔwo katã dzi, eya ta wòle be ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ ɖesiaɖe natrɔ eƒe mɔ̃wo ale be wòagaɖo ɖoɖonu la dzi ([ZIP 206](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0206)). Ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔwɔla si di be wòagaɖo ɖoɖo dzi mekpɔ mɔ be yeate ŋu agbe mɔ trɔtrɔ sia o. Blossom dze egɔme le mainnet ƒe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe 653,600 dzi, eye eƒe gbegbɔgblɔ ɖi ƒe ɖoɖonu ƒe ID kple eya ŋutɔ; esia nye dzesi aɖe si na ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔwo kple gaɖoɖowo nya be yele Blossom ƒe sewo dzi. Mɔ̃ trɔtrɔ la zã Zcash ƒe mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ ƒe mɔ̃ sɔgbɔ ([ZIP 200](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0200)).

## Teƒe si Blossom le

Blossom nye Zcash ƒe mɔ̃ ƒe sewo trɔtrɔ etɔ̃lia. Eva megbe na Overwinter kple Sapling, eye wòva hafi Heartwood kple Canopy. Le Sapling si wòtrɔ Zcash ƒe ɣaɣla ƒe cryptography la ŋu la teƒe la, Blossom ɖo ŋku nu ƒe dziyiyi kple kabakaba ŋu. Eƒe dɔ vevi nye gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe ɣeyiɣi, menye ɣaɣla ƒe nu yeye o.

## Mɔ̃ siwo dze klalo

- **Susui be gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe siwo vaa kaba wòfia ZEC geɖe.** Ne èɖe fetu si wòna le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖeka me la afã, eye wòwɔ be gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe wɔwɔ zɔ zi eve la, agbalẽɖeɖe dzɔna pɛpɛpɛ le ɣeyiɣi ɖeka me.
- **Susui be fetu ƒe afãmama vaa kaba le ŋkeke sia ŋkeke me.** Fetu ƒe afãmama ƒe ɣeyiɣi la zɔ zi eve, tso 840,000 va 1,680,000 gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe, eya ta fetu ƒe afãmama ƒe ŋkeke le ŋkeke sia ŋkeke me metrɔ o.
- **Se "sekend 75" abe ŋugbedodo aɖe ene.** E nye dɔwɔwɔ ƒe ɖaseɖigbalɛ ƒe ɣeyiɣi si wòɖo ta; gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe dome ƒe ɣeyiɣi ŋutɔ ŋutɔ trɔa.
- **Wɔ be Blossom nye ɣaɣla ƒe mɔ trɔtrɔ.** Eƒe dɔ nye gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe ɣeyiɣi kple nu ƒe dziyiyi — le Sapling teƒe la, metrɔ cryptography aɖeke o.
- **Susui be ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔwɔlawo ate ŋu atso mɔ trɔtrɔ la dzi.** Blossom nye gbegbɔgblɔ ɖi ƒe trɔtrɔ si wòle be wòwɔ; ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ si mewɔ mɔ trɔtrɔ la o kple mɔ̃ la ƒe ɖoɖonu dome.

## FAQ

Blossom trɔa ZEC ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ alo ɣeyiɣi si fetu ƒe afãmama vaa? Ao. Woɖe ga si wòna le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖeka me la afã, eye woɖe fetu ƒe afãmama ƒe ɣeyiɣi la zi eve le ɣeyiɣi ma ke me, eya ta ZEC si wòwɔ le ɣeyiɣi ɖeka me kple fetu ƒe afãmama siwo gbɔna ƒe ɣeyiɣi le ɖeka.

Blossom trɔa nye ZEC alo nye ɣaɣla? Ao. Blossom trɔ gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe ɣeyiɣi kple fetu ƒe xexlẽme. Metu nye gaƒoƒowo alo nye ɣaɣla ƒe gaɖoɖowo o.

Nu ka sekend 75 fia ŋutɔ? E nye ɣeyiɣi si wòɖo ta, menye ŋugbedodo o. Woɖea gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe to dɔwɔwɔ ƒe ɖaseɖigbalɛ dzi, eya ta gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe dome ƒe ɣeyiɣi ŋutɔ ŋutɔ trɔa. Mɔ̃ la wòɖoa ta be gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe nava ɖe sekend 75 ɖesiaɖe, si teƒe sekend 150.

Dze be mawɔ nu aɖeke ne Blossom dze egɔme? Ne èzã ɣaɣla dzesi ŋɔŋlɔ blibo, ele be nàtrɔe, elabena Blossom nye mɔ trɔtrɔ si wòle be wòwɔ. Ne èzã gakotoku, ele be nàzã eƒe vɛsion si kplɔ mɔ̃ yeyeawo ɖo.

Nu ka ta wòɖe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ƒe fetu afã? Elabena gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe vaa fifi zi eve kabakaba. Ne wòɖe ga si wòna le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe ɖeka me la afã, mɔ̃ la mewɔ ZEC zi eve kaba o.

Ɣekaɣi Blossom dze egɔme? Le mainnet ƒe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe 653,600 dzi, le December 11, 2019 UTC.
