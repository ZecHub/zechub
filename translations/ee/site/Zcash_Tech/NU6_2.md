# NU6.2

> NU6.2 yi dɔwɔwɔ dzi le Zcash mainnet dzi le block 3,364,600 (June 3, 2026 UTC).

Nusi nàtsɔ adzoe: nusi NU6.2 ɖɔ ɖo, nusita Zcash tɔ te asitsatsa siwo wotsɔ akpoxɔnu wɔe abe ŋkeke ɖeka ene, kple alesi network la tu doa evɔ ga aɖeke mebu o.

NU6.2 nye Zcash [network ƒe ŋgɔyiyi](../start-here/network-upgrades), si gɔme woɖe le se nu le [ZIP 257 ƒe xexlẽdzesi](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0257), si gawɔ Orchard shielded protocol la ŋudɔ le ɖiɖiɖeme kpata megbe. Eɖɔ gbeɖiɖi ƒe kuxi aɖe ɖo le Orchard ƒe zero-knowledge circuit me eye wògbugbɔe ɖo eteƒe [wotsɔ akpoxɔnu na](../using-zcash/shielded-pools) asitsatsa ƒe ɖoɖowo. Bu eŋu be enye Orchard ƒe nudzodzoewo ŋutinya ƒe ta si ku ɖe nuwo ɖɔɖɔɖo kpata ŋu. Dɔwɔwɔ ɖe edzi si kplɔe ɖo, . [Gayibɔti (NU6.3) .](../zcash-tech/ironwood), emegbe wotsɔ ta yeye aɖe kple xoxoa ƒe dutoƒo agbalẽdzikpɔkpɔ kpee.

Nusitae esia le vevie ɖo. Orchard nye afisi ame ŋutɔ ƒe Zcash fexexe akpa gãtɔ dzɔna le. Esi wokpɔ vodada aɖe le akɔntabubu si na be ta ma nɔa anukware me la, tiatia sesẽ aɖe dze ŋgɔ network la: na asitsatsa siwo ŋu wokpɔ ta na la nayi edzi kple afɔku si wonya, alo woatɔ vie vaseɖe esime woaɖɔ wo ɖo. NU6.2 nye alesi Zcash tia dedienɔnɔ gbã eye wògagbugbɔ adzamenyawo ɖo anyi le ŋkeke ɖeka me.

![NU6.2 emergency timeline: the Orchard bug was found on May 29, Orchard was paused by an emergency soft fork on June 2, and NU6.2 re-enabled it on a corrected circuit on June 3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-2-timeline.png)

## Nu kae dzᴐ

Nusi ʋãe nye gbeɖiɖi ƒe kuxi aɖe le Orchard ƒe Action nutome, akɔntabubu si wosusu be aɖo kpe edzi be asitsatsa ɖesiaɖe si wokpɔ ta na la ɖoa seawo dzi. Numekula si le eɖokui si Taylor Hornby ke ɖe eŋu le ƒe... [Labs Siwo Wotsɔ Akpoxɔnu Wɔe](../zcash-organizations/shielded-labs) audit eye wòɖee ɖe go le agbanɔamedzi me le May 2026 ƒe nuwuwu. Zcash ƒe turnstile akɔntabubu gakpɔtɔ ɖo seɖoƒe na asixɔxɔ agbɔsɔsɔme si ate ŋu adzo le Orchard gbeɖeka, eyata womate ŋu ado ZEC ƒe nuzazã bliboa ŋutɔ ɖe dzi o, gake vodada aɖe si le akɔntabubua me si nana ga siwo wotsɔ akpoxɔnu wɔe la nɔa anukwareɖiɖi me la lolo ŋutɔ, eyata network la wɔ nu le ŋkeke aɖewo megbe. The [Ironwood ƒe ati](../zcash-tech/ironwood) axa ɖe vodadaa me kple alesi wòate ŋu awɔ asixɔxɔ bliboe.

## Tɔtɔ kpata la

Le June 2, 2026 dzi la, fɔkpa falɛfalɛ aɖe si wozãna kpata la na Orchard ƒe asitsatsa katã mewɔa dɔ o hena ɣeyiɣi aɖe. Woɖoe ɖa abe hotfix ene le zcashd v6.12.5 kple zebra v4.5.3 me.

1. Wotɔ te Shielded Orchard ƒe dɔdɔ kple xɔxɔ, eyata womate ŋu awɔ Orchard ƒe kpeɖodzi yeye aɖeke alo axɔe o.
2. Transparent kple Sapling ƒe asitsatsa yi edzi nɔ dɔ wɔm abe alesi wòle ene.
3. Womeʋu ga aɖeke, wotu tsikpe ɖe eŋu, alo bu o. Ðeko ɖiɖiɖemea na Orchard ƒe dɔwɔna yeyewo tɔ te esime wowu ɖɔɖɔɖo ŋutɔŋutɔ nu.

![What paused and what kept working: transparent and Sapling transactions ran the whole time, while only Orchard paused for about a day between June 2 and June 3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-2-what-paused.png)

## Nusi NU6.2 trɔ

Le ŋufɔke, June 3, 2026 dzi la, NU6.2 hard fork la wɔ dɔ le block 3,364,600 dzi eye wògawɔ Orchard ŋudɔ le nutome sue si woɖɔ ɖo me.

![The NU6.2 circuit fix: the flawed circuit had a missing constraint and an old verifying key, and NU6.2 added the constraint to produce a corrected circuit with a new verifying key that rejects bad proofs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/Zcash_Tech/assets/nu6-2-the-fix.png)

1. Eɖɔ Action circuit ƒe variable-base scalar multiplication gadget ɖo to mɔxenu siwo bu la tsɔtsɔ kpee me.
2. Ema na wokpɔ kpeɖodzi safui yeye, eyata kpeɖodzi siwo wowɔ le nutome sue xoxo si me vodada le te megaɖoa kpe edzi o, eye ele be kpeɖodzi yeye ɖesiaɖe nazã esi woɖɔ ɖo.
3. Ena kpeɖodzi ƒe didime tututu va zu se si dzi woda asi ɖo, eye wòxe mɔ ɖe dometsotso aɖe si dzi womewɔ ɖo tsã o la nu.

Woɖo kɔda si woɖɔ ɖo la ɖe zcashd v6.20.0 kple zebra v5.0.0, si wotu ɖe halo2_gadgets v0.5.0 kple atikutsetsebɔ ƒe aɖaka v0.14.0 dzi.

## Nusi NU6.2 mewɔ o

NU6.2 kpɔa nutome suea ta na asitsatsa yeye ɖesiaɖe, gake asixɔxɔ si wowɔ le se xoxoawo te gakpɔtɔ nɔa Orchard ta la me. Asixɔxɔ ma me dzodzro kple eɖeɖe yi ta si me dzadzɛ me nye dɔ si to vovo, eye emae nye nusi [Gayibɔti (NU6.3) .](../zcash-tech/ironwood) asiléƒewo kple eƒe ta yeyea kple turnstile.

## Ðe nane bua

Ao, kpeɖodzi aɖeke meli be wowɔ vodadaa ŋudɔ kpɔ o, womewɔ asixɔxɔ aɖeke si ŋu womeɖe mɔ ɖo o, eye womekpɔ ŋusẽ aɖeke ɖe ezãlawo ƒe ga dzi o. Zcash ƒe turnstile akɔntabubu ɖo kpe edzi be nusiwo katã wotsɔ vɛ la nɔ anyi nenema le wɔna bliboa me.

## Nyabiasewo ƒe Nyabiasewo

Ðe nye ZEC la megate ŋu kpɔa mɔ oa? Ao, abe ŋkeke ɖeka ene la, wotɔ te Orchard ƒe asitsatsa siwo wotsɔ akpoxɔnu wɔe, gake ga aɖeke mebu alo de afɔku me o, eye asitsatsa siwo me kɔ kple Sapling yi edzi nɔ dɔ wɔm.

Nukatae nàtɔ vie tsɔ wu be nànɔ asi trɔm le eŋu kpoo? Gbeɖiɖi ƒe kuxi aɖe ate ŋu ana woafi ga alo azãe zi eve le Orchard-ta la me, eyata afɔɖeɖe si le dediee nye be woatɔ te Orchard ƒe asitsatsa yeyewo vaseɖe esime nutome sue si woɖɔ ɖo la nanɔ agbe.

Ðe kpeɖodzi xoxo siwo ŋu wotsɔ akpoxɔnu ɖo la gakpɔtɔ wɔa dɔa? Kpeɖodzi siwo wowɔ le nutome xoxoa te megaɖoa kpe edzi le NU6.2 megbe o. Gakotoku siwo le asiɖeɖe le eŋu si wodo alɔe dzi zãa nutome sue si woɖɔ ɖo la le wo ɖokui si.

Ðe wozã nudzodzoea kpɔa? Kpeɖodzi aɖeke meli be eyae o. Dedienɔnɔ ŋuti numekuku lée eye woɖɔe ɖo hafi nuvevi aɖeke si wonya.

Aleke NU6.2 to vovo tso Ironwood gbɔe? NU6.2 ɖɔ nutome suea ɖo eye wògawɔ Orchard ŋudɔ ake. [Gayibɔti (NU6.3) .](../zcash-tech/ironwood) emegbe wotsɔ ta yeye kple dutoƒo tɔtrɔƒe kpee be woatsɔ adzro ta xoxoa ƒe asixɔxɔ me ahaʋuʋui.

## Do wò nugɔmesese kpɔ

Nukatae Zcash tɔ te Orchard ƒe asitsatsa ŋkeke ɖeka tsɔ wu be wòagblẽ wo ɖi woanɔ dɔ wɔm esime wole ɖɔɖɔɖoa ŋlɔm?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Elabena vodadaa nye gbeɖiɖi ƒe vodada, si fia be asitsatsa siwo mesɔ o ate ŋu ato abe esiwo sɔ ene. Ne wogblẽ Orchard ɖi wònɔ ʋuʋu ɖi la, ana ʋɔtru aɖe nanɔ ʋuʋu ɖi na fififi kple gazazã zi gbɔ zi eve le ta la me hafi. Orchard ƒe asitsatsa yeyewo tɔ te do ʋɔtru ma vaseɖe esime nutome sue si woɖɔ ɖo la wɔ dɔ le NU6.2.
</details>
