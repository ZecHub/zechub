# Zcash Ɣaɣla ƒe Nudzɔdzɔwo [Shielded Assets]

## Nu Vevi Keke

Zcash Ɣaɣla ƒe Nudzɔdzɔwo (ZSA) nye protocol ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu si wo đề be wòana nudzɔdzɔwo **siwo menye ZEC o** — stablecoinwo, dziɖuɖu tokenwo, alo nudzɔdzɔ si wòate ŋu adze ɖe wò ŋutɔ ŋu — nɔ Zcash ƒe ɣaɣla gaƒuƒoƒo me, eye woaɣla ame si dɔe ɖa, ame si xɔe, kple ga si woɖo.

- **Nu si wònye:** ERC-20 ƒe ƒomevi me nudzɔdzɔ siwo wòate ŋu adze ɖe wò ŋutɔ ŋu, gake woɣaɣla wo tso gɔmedzedze.
- **Ame siwo le eƒe tutu wɔm:** [QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/), le ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu tso Zcash Foundation me ƒe te, eye woƒoa dɔ ɖekae kple Electric Coin Company.
- **Ale si woɖe eƒe sewo le:** [ZIP 226](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0226) (tsɔtsɔ kple tɔtɔ) kple [ZIP 227](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0227) (dɔwɔwɔ).
- **Nɔnɔme:** Menɔ agbe le mainnet dzi o, eye menye Network Upgrade 7 (NU7) ƒe akpa o. [ZIP 259](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0259), si ɖea nu sia nu si NU7 ade dɔme afia la, mekpɔ ZIP 226 alo ZIP 227 o, eye womede ZSA ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ƒe ŋkeke ɖo haɖe o.
- **Adzɔwo:** Woɖea wo ɖe ZEC me ɣe sia ɣi, togbɔ be nudzɔdzɔ kae wole tsɔm o.

---

## Gɔme Nya Vevi

Zcash Ɣaɣla ƒe Nudzɔdzɔwo (ZSA) nye ɖoɖo si wo đề be woatsɔ akpe ɖe Zcash protocol la ŋu, si ana wòaŋu adze nudzɔdzɔ siwo wòate ŋu adze ɖe wò ŋutɔ ŋu, atsɔ wo, eye wòatɔ wo le Zcash ɖoɖonu dzi.

Ne nènya [ERC-20](https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/standards/tokens/erc-20/) token ƒe standard le Ethereum ƒe gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu dzi la, ZSAwo le Zcash ŋu abe ale si ERC-20 tokenwo le Ethereum ŋu ene.

Zcash Ɣaɣla ƒe Nudzɔdzɔwo ana wòate ŋu adze token siwo wòate ŋu adze ɖe wò ŋutɔ ŋu le Zcash gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu dzi, eye woana token siwo menye [ZEC](/guides/using-zec-privately) o nakpɔ ɣaɣla kple ɣaɣla gaɖoɖowo ƒe nyuiwo le Zcash gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu dzi.

ZSA ƒe dɔwɔwɔ si ate ŋu anye vevi ŋutɔ aɖe nye be woado stablecoinwo ɖe Zcash protocol la dzi. Stablecoinwo nye crypto ga siwo doa woƒe asixɔxɔ ɖe fiat ga, abe US Dollar alo Euro ene, ŋu. Egbe la, stablecoin siwo le amewo dome tsɔtsɔ geɖe wu la aɖewo nye ERC-20 tokenwo abe [USDC](https://www.circle.com/en/usdc) kple [Dai](https://docs.makerdao.com/) ene.

ZSA ƒe dɔwɔwɔ bubu si ate ŋu anye nye be woado dziɖuɖu tokenwo. Le kpɔɖeŋu me, ZecHub (wiki sia ƒe tata) nye Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO), eye wòate ŋu adze ZSA aɖe na eƒe membrewo be woatsɔ akpe ɖe ɖoɖowo kple dziɖuɖu ƒe nya me.

[QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/) le ZSAwo ŋutɔ wɔm le ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu gã tso [Zcash Foundation](/zcash-organizations/zcash-foundation) me ƒe te, eye woƒoa dɔ ɖekae kple [Electric Coin Company](/zcash-organizations/electric-coin-company). Esi wole dɔ sia ŋutɔ wɔm ha la, woɖoa yeye nyawo ɖe [kakaka sia](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-update-zcash-shielded-assets-monthly-updates/41153) me le Zcash forum la dzi. [ZSA ƒe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu ƒe biabia](https://zcashgrants.org/gallery/25215916-53ea-4041-a3b2-6d00c487917d/33106640/) si QEDIT wɔ la le Zcash Foundation ƒe ga ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu ƒe website dzi.

## Nɔnɔmetata / Ɖeɖeɖe

### Agbalẽ ƒe agbado si woƀɔ

Bu Zcash ɣaɣla gaɖoɖo aɖe ɖe agbado bɔbɔe si woƀɔ, eye woƒoe ɖe post ƒe agbadonu me le amewo kata ƒe ŋkume. Ame sia ame ate ŋu akpɔ be woƒo agbado aɖe ɖa. Ame aɖeke mate ŋu akpɔ ame si dɔe ɖa, ame si xɔe, alo nu si le eme o — eye agbadowo katã nɔna abe wo nɔewo ene.

Egbe la, agbado aɖe le Zcash network dzi ate ŋu atsɔ nu ɖeka ko: ZEC.

ZSA metrɔ agbado la o. Etrɔ **nu si wòle mɔ na wòanɔ eme**. Le ZSA megbe la, agbado bɔbɔe ma ke ate ŋu atsɔ stablecoin, DAO dziɖuɖu token, alo dɔwɔƒe ƒe nudzɔdzɔ si wotsɔa dea amewo dzi, eye le gota la, anɔ abe agbado bubu sia bubu le network la dzi ene.

Nya ɖeka si wòle be nàɖo ɖe dzi me nye esi: **Woɖea post ƒe adzɔ la ɖe ZEC me ɣe sia ɣi**, togbɔ be nu kae le agbado la me o.

### Nu si ame si le gota ate ŋu akpɔ

| Nu si ame si le gota ate ŋu akpɔ... | ERC-20 le Ethereum dzi | ZSA le Zcash dzi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ame si dɔe ɖa | Dze ɖe amewo ŋkume | Ɣaɣla |
| Ame si xɔe | Dze ɖe amewo ŋkume | Ɣaɣla |
| Ga si woɖo | Dze ɖe amewo ŋkume | Ɣaɣla |
| Ame ɖeka ƒe ga siwo le esi | Dze ɖe amewo ŋkume | Ɣaɣla |
| Nudzɔdzɔ la ƒe xexlẽme katã | Dze ɖe amewo ŋkume | **Dze ɖe amewo ŋkume — le ta aɖe ta** |
| Ga si woɖea adzɔ le eme | ETH | ZEC |

### Nu ka ta xexlẽme ƒe fli menye vodada o

Tebɔ dzi fli eveawo le kplɔlɔ̃ la me nye afi si ZSA ƒe vevie gɔme ɖe.

ZIP 227 ɖoe be **dɔwɔwɔ nɔa nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi**, be woate ŋu akplɔ nudzɔdzɔ sia sia ƒe xexlẽme si le amewo dome ɖe ɖoɖonu dzi. Ame ɖeka ƒe ga siwo le esi kple fexexewo nɔa ɣaɣla me; token siwo le anyigba dzi ƒe xexlẽme katã menɔa ɣaɣla me o.

Na stablecoin ƒe dodola aɖe la, ƒomevi sia nye ta la, menye nyagblɔɖi o. Woate ŋu adzro reservewo akpɔ wo akplɔ ɖe xexlẽme si woate ŋu akpɔ ɖe amewo ŋkume ŋu, esi ame siwo le token la zãm la ƒe ga siwo le wo si kple woƒe fexexewo nɔa wo ŋutɔ ƒe me.

### Nudzɔdzɔ ɖeka, dzesi ɖeka

Nudzɔdzɔ sia sia kpɔ **Nudzɔdzɔ ƒe Dzesi [Asset Identifier]** si meɖe le dodola la ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ƒe kplɔdzesi kple nudzɔdzɔ la ƒe nuŋlɔɖi me. Dodola vovovowo eve mate ŋu awɔ dzesi ma ke o, eye be woado nudzɔdzɔ aɖe alo woatrɔe la, ele be woakpɔ cryptographic mɔɖagbe tso eƒe dodola gbɔ. Le agbado ƒe nɔnɔmetata me la: ame sia ame ate ŋu aƒo agbado aɖe ɖa, gake mint si ƒe nudzɔdzɔ ma le koe ate ŋu ata wo geɖe wu.

### Zcash Ɖeɖeɖe Ƒe Biabiawo [Improvement Proposals] (ZIPs)

- [ZIP 226](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0226): Zcash Ɣaɣla ƒe Nudzɔdzɔwo ƒe Tsɔtsɔ kple Tɔtɔ
- [ZIP 227](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0227): Zcash Ɣaɣla ƒe Nudzɔdzɔwo ƒe Dɔwɔwɔ
- [ZIP 230](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0230): Gaɖoɖo ƒe ƒomevi 6 ƒe Format

> **Didienu tso ZIP 230 ŋu:** Wodo ZIP 230 ɖi tso eƒe mɔ me tso esime, eye womatsɔe ade dɔme o. Gaɖoɖo ƒe ƒomevi 6 ƒe sewo le [ZIP 229](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0229) me fifia. Kpɔ nyadzɔdzɔ si le [ZIP 230](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0230) ƒe axɔta la ƒe dzime.

ZIP 226 ɖea OrchardZSA protocol la ƒe sewo — Orchard protocol la ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu si tsɔa nudzɔdzɔ siwo wòate ŋu adze ɖe wò ŋutɔ ŋu ƒe tsɔtsɔ kple tɔtɔ. ZIP 227 ɖea ale si wodo nudzɔdzɔ mawo le gɔmedzedze me, eye ele be woawɔe kple ZIP 226 ko.

### ZSA Ƒe Ga Ƒe Kpekpeɖeŋu Ƒe Ɖoɖo

Ɣaɣla ƒe Nudzɔdzɔwo (ZSA/UDA) ƒe ɖoɖo la, [QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/) ƒe ha la tsɔe vɛ be woatu ɣaɣla ƒe nudzɔdzɔ siwo wòate ŋu azã le Zcash gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu dzi. Zi geɖe la, woyɔ esiawo be User Defined Assets (UDA) alo Zcash Ɣaɣla ƒe Nudzɔdzɔwo (ZSA).

Le ɖoɖo sia me la, ha si le [QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/) ƒe nye la ɖoe be yeatsɔ DeFi ava Zcash ƒe ecosystem la me, eye le ɣeyiɣi ma ke me la, yeana wòate ŋu azã ɣaɣla ƒe mɔ̃ɖoɖo nyuitɔ le DeFi ƒe ecosystem si le fifia me. Le akɔŋkuɖeŋu aɖe me la, ha la bia, eye habɔbɔ la ŋu nya be [ɣaɣla ƒe nudzɔdzɔ siwo wòate ŋu azã (ZSA/UDA) nye nɔnɔmetata si woabia wu fifia](https://twitter.com/BenarrochDaniel/status/1428327864034791429).

Ɖoɖo siawo le technical ƒe akpa me wɔnɛ ɖe [Zcash ƒe Ɖeɖeɖe Ƒe Biabia (ZIP)](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0000) ƒe sewo nu, eye woɖe wo ƒe sewo le ZIP 226 kple ZIP 227 me.

1. [ZIP 226](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0226): Zcash Ɣaɣla ƒe Nudzɔdzɔwo ƒe Tsɔtsɔ kple Tɔtɔ
2. [ZIP 227](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0227): Zcash Ɣaɣla ƒe Nudzɔdzɔwo ƒe Dɔwɔwɔ

## Dɔwɔwɔ Me Ƒe Ɣometɔwo

**Ne èle ZEC ɖe asi alo èzãe**

- Woɖe ZSAwo be wonye Orchard ("OrchardZSA") ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu, eyata woama ɣaɣla mɔ̃ɖoɖo si ZEC zã xoxo la. Ele be wò gakotoku nakpɔ ZSA ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu tsitotsito hafi wòate ŋu aɖe wo ɖe asi alo aɖo wo ɖa.
- Ele be ZEC aɖe nanɔ wò si ɣe sia ɣi. Woɖea adzɔwo na ZSA ƒe dododo kple tsɔtsɔ le ZEC me, menye nudzɔdzɔ la ŋutɔ me o.
- Nu aɖeke metrɔ le wò ZEC gaɖoɖo siwo le xoxo la ŋu o.

**Ne wònye be wò nye dodola si ate ŋu anye — stablecoin, DAO, dɔwɔƒe**

- Nudzɔdzɔ ƒe dododo hia cryptographic mɔɖagbe si ƒo ɖe dɔwɔwɔ ƒe kplɔdzesi aɖe ŋu, eyata wò koe ate ŋu ado alo atrɔ wò ŋutɔ ƒe nudzɔdzɔ ƒe nɔnɔmewo.
- Wò nudzɔdzɔ ƒe xexlẽme si le amewo dome ate ŋu anɔ nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi me be woakpɔe, esi wò zãlawo ƒe ga siwo le wo si kple tsɔtsɔwo menɔa nenema o. Na dodola si le sewo te la, esia nye ƒomevi si wobia zi geɖe.
- Dɔwɔwɔ gaɖoɖo ɖeka ate ŋu adze nudzɔdzɔ ɖeka ko ta geɖe wu le ɣeyiɣi ɖeka me.

**Na ecosystem la**

- Esi woɖea ZSA ƒe adzɔ sia sia le ZEC me ta la, dɔwɔwɔ le nudzɔdzɔ sia sia si woado le Zcash dzi le ŋkeke siwo gbɔna la me ana amewo adi ZEC ŋutɔ.
