# Zcash ƒe Nunɔamesi Siwo Wokpɔna

Zcash Shielded Assets (ZSA) nye ŋgɔyiyi si wodo ɖa le Zcash ƒe ɖoɖowɔɖia ŋu si ana woate ŋu awɔ, atsɔ wo ayi teƒe bubu, eye woatɔ dzo nunɔamesi siwo wowɔ ɖe ɖoɖo nu le Zcash kɔsɔkɔsɔa dzi.

Ne ènya nu tso [ERC-20](https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/standards/tokens/erc-20/) dzesi dzidzenu le Ethereum blockchain, ZSAwo nye na Zcash abe alesi ERC-20 dzesiwo le na Ethereum ene.

Zcash Shielded Assets ana woateŋu awɔ dzesi tɔxɛwo le Zcash blockchain dzi, si ana woaɖe mɔ na dzesi bubu siwo menye [ZEC o](https://wiki.zechub.xyz/using-zec-privately) be woakpɔ viɖe tso adzɔnu siwo wokpɔ ta na ƒe ŋkɔmaɖemaɖe kple adzamenyawo me le Zcash blockchain la dzi.

ZSAwo zazã vevi aɖe si ate ŋu adzɔ anye na stablecoins ƒe dodo ɖe Zcash ƒe ɖoɖowɔɖia dzi. Stablecoins nye cryptocurrencies siwo tsɔa woƒe asixɔxɔ dea ga fiat, abe United States Dollar alo Euro ene. Fifia la, stablecoins siwo wokakana wu dometɔ aɖewo nye ERC-20 tokens abe [USDC](https://www.circle.com/en/usdc) kple [Dai](https://docs.makerdao.com/).

ZSAwo zazã bubu si ate ŋu anye dziɖuɖu ƒe dzesiwo nana. Le kpɔɖeŋu me, Zechub (wiki sia tala) nye Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO) eye ateŋu awɔ ZSA eye wòana eƒe hameviwo kple susu be woada akɔ ɖe aɖaŋuɖoɖowo kple dziɖuɖu ŋuti nyametsotsowo dzi.

ZSAwo le [QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/), le kpekpeɖeŋu gã aɖe si tso [Zcash Foundation](https://wiki.zechub.xyz/zcash-foundation) in collaboration with the [Electric Coin Company](https://wiki.zechub.xyz/electric-coin-company). Esi wogale dɔ sia wɔm vevie ta la, wodaa nyatakaka yeyewo ɖe [ka sia dzi](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-update-zcash-shielded-assets-monthly-updates/41153) ƒe Zcash ƒe nyamedzroƒea. [ZSA ƒe kpekpeɖeŋunana ƒe biabiaa](https://zcashgrants.org/gallery/25215916-53ea-4041-a3b2-6d00c487917d/33106640/) by QEDIT la le Zcash Foundation ƒe kpekpeɖeŋunana ƒe nyatakakadzraɖoƒe.

## ZSA ƒe Gakpekpeɖeŋunana ƒe Aɖaŋuɖoɖo

ZSA ƒe aɖaŋuɖoɖo si ku ɖe Nusiwo Wokpɔ Ta (ZSA/UDA) ŋu la nye esi [QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/) ƒuƒoƒo be woatu generic shielded nunɔamesiwo ɖe Zcash blockchain dzi. Zi geɖe la, woyɔa esiawo be Zãla ƒe Nunɔamesiwo (UDA) alo be Zcash Shielded Assets (ZSA).

Le aɖaŋuɖoɖo sia ta la, ƒuƒoƒo si le [QEDIT](https://qed-it.com/) ɖoe be yeatsɔ DeFi ava Zcash ƒe lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe ɖoɖoa me, eye le ɣeyiɣi ma ke me la, woana woate ŋu azã ameŋunyatakakawo ŋuti mɔ̃ɖaŋununya nyuitɔ kekeake le DeFi lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe ɖoɖo si li fifia me le numekuku si wowɔ le tadeaguƒe ŋu, afisi ƒuƒoƒoa bia le, eye nutoa me tɔwo ɖo eŋu be: [generic shielded assets (ZSA/UDA) nye nusi wobia wu fifia](https://twitter.com/BenarrochDaniel/status/1428327864034791429)

Aɖaŋuɖoɖo siawo wɔ ɖe [Zcash Improvement Proposal (ZIP) .](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0000) specification eye woɖe wo gɔme le ZIP 226 & ZIP 227 me.

1. [ZIP 226 ƒe xexlẽdzesi](https://qed-it.github.io/zips/zip-0226): Zcash Shielded Assets ƒe Asitɔtrɔ Kple Wo Dzodzo
2. [ZIP 227 ƒe xexlẽdzesi](https://qed-it.github.io/zips/zip-0227): Zcash Shielded Assets ƒe nana
