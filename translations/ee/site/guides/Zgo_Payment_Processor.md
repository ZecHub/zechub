# ZGo Fexexe Dɔwɔƒe: Zcash Xɔxɔ Vidzikpɔkpɔ Manɔmee

ZGo nye fexexe ƒe ɖoɖowɔla si menye gaxɔgbalẽvi o na Zcash. Asisi aɖe xea fe le ZEC me tso woawo ŋutɔ ƒe gakotoku me, ZGo léa ŋku ɖe Zcash blockchain ŋu hena asitsatsa la, eye ga la va ɖoa asitsala ƒe gakotokua me tẽ to ga si wotsɔ dea gaxɔ me si wotsɔ akpoxɔnu wɔe dzi. ZGo meléa ga la ɖe wo dome gbeɖe o.

Mɔfiame sia ɖe alesi fexexe ƒe sisi wɔa dɔe, alesi woaɖo akɔnta, kple alesi woawɔ ZGo awɔ ɖeka kple Xero kple WooCommerce. Eƒo nu tso vodada eve siwo hea ɖoɖowɔwɔ ƒe kuxi akpa gãtɔ vɛ zi gbãtɔ hã ŋu.

## Nukatae nàzã ZGo

Cryptocurrency fexexe ƒe dɔwɔƒe akpa gãtɔ nyea nudzraɖoƒe. Gaawo gena ɖe dɔwɔƒea ƒe gakɔnta me gbã eye woɖoa wo ɖe asitsala la emegbe, si fia be ame etɔ̃lia aɖe kpɔa ga la dzi hena ɣeyiɣi aɖe eye ate ŋu atɔ te ga la, ahee ɖe megbe, alo agblɔe tso eŋu.

ZGo zãa mɔnu si to vovo na ema. Fexexewo ʋuna tso asisi ƒe gakotoku me yia asitsala ƒe gakotoku me tẽ to Zcash ƒe asitsatsa si wokpɔ ta na me. Ðeko processor la wɔa invoice la eye wòléa ŋku ɖe blockchain la ŋu hena kpeɖodzi. Domenɔla aɖeke meli si susɔ o, gaɖeɖe ƒe sisi aɖeke meli o, eye ame etɔ̃lia aɖeke meli si ate ŋu alé nyaa gbɔ kpɔkpɔ ɖe te o.

Le asitsala gome la, esia fia nu etɔ̃ siwo ŋudɔ woate ŋu awɔ: ZEC si gbɔna la dzi kpɔkpɔ bliboe, asitsatsa ƒe adzamenyawo takpɔkpɔ le gɔmedzedzea me, kple ŋuɖoɖo ɖe dɔwɔƒe si le titina si anɔ Internet dzi alo gaxɔla ŋu o.

## Alesi ZGo wɔa dɔe

Fexexe ƒe sisi le ɖeka eɖanye be wozã ZGo le eɖokui si, to Xero dzi, alo to WooCommerce dzi o:

1. Asitsala la wɔa fexexe ƒe biabiawo le ZGo me, si wɔa dɔ abe QR-kɔda ene kple ga home, adzɔxegbalẽvi ƒe ID, kple Zcash xɔxɔ ƒe adrɛs.
2. Asisi la tsɔa Zcash gakotoku (Orchard, Sapling, kple Transparent adrɛs ƒomeviwo katã doa alɔe le WordPress plugin la dzi) skan QR la eye wòda asi ɖe fexexe dzi.
3. Woɖea asitsatsa la ɖe go ɖe Zcash network la dzi abe shielded transfer tso asisi ƒe gakotoku me yi asitsala ƒe gakotoku me ene.
4. ZGo léa ŋku ɖe Zcash blockchain ŋu hena asitsatsa la.
5. Le kpeɖodzi atɔ̃ megbe la, ZGo dea dzesi fexexea be enye mamlɛtɔ eye wònaa nyanya ɖekawɔwɔ ɖesiaɖe si do ƒome kplii (Xero, WooCommerce, alo webhook).

Kpeɖodzi atɔ̃ ƒe dzidzenue nye xexlẽdzesi vevitɔ. Nusianu si wowɔ do ŋgɔ la nye fexexe si le edzi yim, ke menye fe si woxɔ o. Ele be nudɔdɔwo dzi wɔwɔ, nudzraɖoƒewo ƒe asitɔtrɔ, kple afɔɖeɖe ɖesiaɖe si womate ŋu atrɔ o le asitsalawo ƒe akpa dzi nalala afɔɖeɖe 5 lia.

ZGo zɔna le egbegbe browser ɖesiaɖe me le kɔmpiuta alo asitelefon dzi, eye womeɖoae ɖe akpa eveawo siaa o. Asisi la hiã Zcash gakotoku; asitsala la hiã Zcash gakotoku kple ZGo akɔnta.

## Akɔntabubu ɖoɖo

Be nàwɔ ZGo akɔnta la, ehiã be nàzã Zcash gakotoku si me ZEC sue aɖe le. ZEC ƒe ga sue si susɔ la xea kɔsɔkɔsɔ ƒe fe si woxena ɖe akɔntabubu ƒe gɔmedzedze ƒe asitsatsa ta. Zcash gakotoku gã ɖesiaɖe wɔa dɔ na esia; kpɔ [ZecHub ƒe Gakotokuwo](https://zechub.wiki/wallets) na tiatia siwo li fifia.

Ðoɖo vevitɔ si wowɔ:

1. Ʋu [zgo.cash](https://zgo.cash/) le web-browser me.
2. Wɔ akɔnta to Zcash gakotoku si le asitsala ƒe dzikpɔkpɔ te zazã me. Ele be gakotoku sia nalé safuiawo ɖe asi. Adrɛs si wotsɔ dea gae mawɔ dɔ o (kpɔ [Vodada siwo bɔ](#common-mistakes)).
3. Kpɔ gakotokua ɖa to gɔmedzedze ƒe asitsatsa suea ɖoɖo ɖa me.
4. Ðo adrɛs si dzi nàxɔe ɖo. Fexexe siwo katã ŋu wotrɔ asi le to akɔnta sia dzi la adze ɖe gakotoku sia me.

Ne akɔnta la nya le dɔ wɔm ko la, asitsala ma ke ate ŋu azã ZGo hena fexexe zi ɖeka (QR-kɔda ɖeka le wɔna si dona me) alo atsɔ ka aƒoe ɖe ɖoɖo si anɔ anyi ɖaa me to Xero alo WooCommerce dzi.

## ZYi kple Xero

[Xero ƒe ŋkɔ](https://www.xero.com/) nye alilikpo me akɔntabubu ƒe mɔnu si dɔwɔƒe suewo kple gãwo dometɔ geɖe zãna. ZGo–Xero ƒe ƒoƒo ɖekae na be asitsala ɖea adzɔxegbalẽvi ɖe go le Xero me, naa asisi la xea fe ɖe eta le ZEC me, eye wònana Xero dea dzesi adzɔxegbalẽvia le eɖokui si be woxee ne asitsatsa la ɖo kpe edzi ko.

Ale si wòwɔa dɔe:

1. Asitsala la wɔa adzɔxegbalẽvi le Xero me abe alesi wòwɔna ɖaa ene.
2. ZGo tsɔa Zcash fexexe ƒe tiatia kpena ɖe adzɔxegbalẽvia ŋu.
3. Asisi la xea fe le ZEC me to woƒe gakotoku dzi.
4. ZGo léa ŋku ɖe [Zcash blockchain](https://z.cash/) na asitsatsa la ta.
5. Le kpeɖodzi atɔ̃ megbe la, ZGo gagblɔa ga si woxe la na Xero, si dea dzesi gaxɔgbalẽvia be woxee.

ZEC la dzena ɖe asitsala la ƒe gakotoku me, ke menye le akɔnta aɖeke si dzi ZGo kpɔ ŋusẽ ɖo alo esi dzi Xero kpɔ ŋusẽ ɖo me o. Akɔntabubu ŋuti nuŋlɔɖi si le Xero me nɔa ɖekawɔwɔ me kple kɔsɔkɔsɔa dzi kpɔkpɔ le eɖokui si.

Ne èdi be yeaɖoe zi gbãtɔ la, wɔ ɖe azɔlizɔzɔ si woɖo ɖi la dzi: [Xero Integration Configuration](https://hedgedoc.vergara.tech/s/4iXC67fmb).

## ZYi kple WooCommerce

Le Internet dzi fiase siwo le dɔ wɔm le [WooCommerce](https://woocommerce.com/) kple [WordPress](https://wordpress.org/), ZGo naa plugin si woɖo ɖi. Plugin la tsɔa Zcash kpena ɖe eŋu abe fexexemɔnu ene le nuƒleɣi eye wòkpɔa nudɔdɔ ƒe nɔnɔmea gbɔ le eɖokui si ne fexexea ɖo kpe edzi.

<img width="672" height="378" alt="ZGo WooCommerce plugin checkout and order flow" src="/content-images/55a791bb-1947-4f55-b5b9-55083be8ed49-2bc8d2571e.webp" />

Sisi tso nuwuwu yi nuwuwu le WooCommerce fiase aɖe me:

1. Asisi la ɖoa gaxɔgbalẽvi gbɔ eye wòtia Zcash be wòanye fexexemɔnu.
2. Plugin la wɔa fexexe ƒe biabiawo eye wòɖea QR-kɔda si le gaxɔgbalẽvia ƒe axaa dzi.
3. Asisi la xea fe tso woƒe gakotoku me.
4. Asitsatsa la kaka ɖe Zcash network eye ZGo dzea ŋkuléle ɖe eŋu gɔme.
5. Le kpeɖodzi atɔ̃ megbe la, ZGo ka nya ta be fexexea nye mamlɛtɔ na plugin la.
6. Plugin la dea dzesi WooCommerce ƒe nudɔdɔa be woxe fe eye wòwɔa nudɔdɔa ƒe nyatakakadzraɖoƒea yeyee.

Ne afɔɖeɖe 6 lia wu enu ko hafi woxea nudɔdɔa. Woateŋu aɖe nɔnɔme siwo do ŋgɔ (nyadzɔdzɔgblɔmɔnuwo, kpeɖodzi gbãtɔwo) afia asisi la be "fexexe si woxɔ, si le kpeɖodzi lalam," gake ele be nudzraɖoƒe, emevava, kple nuwo wɔwɔ le wo ɖokui si le anyime la nalala nɔnɔme mamlɛtɔ.

Plugin la dea dɔdzikpɔkpɔ ƒe dashboard hã WordPress me, afisi asitsala ateŋu alé ŋku ɖe nudɔdɔwo kple ZEC ƒe fexexe siwo va la ŋu le WooCommerce ƒe nudɔdɔ ƒe nukpɔkpɔ si sɔ la xa. Plugin la doa alɔ Zcash adrɛs ƒomevi siwo katã li fifia: Orchard, Sapling, kple Transparent. Asitsalawo siwo xea fe tso gakotoku ɖesiaɖe si wɔ ɖeka kple sea dzi ate ŋu awu asitsatsa la nu.

## Ŋutinuwo

**Non-custodial.** Fexexewo ʋuna tso asisi ƒe gakotoku me yia asitsala ƒe gakotoku me tẽ to asitsatsa siwo ŋu wokpɔ ta na me. ZGo meléa ga la ɖe wo dome gbeɖe o, eye asitsala la léa ŋusẽ blibo ɖe asi le ɣeyiɣi bliboa me.

**Tɔtrɔ ɖe nɔnɔmewo ŋu.** Woateŋu azã ZGo na ŋdɔ ɖeka le asi si woʋuna me, na teƒe si wodzraa nu le ƒe ɖoɖo si anɔ anyi ɖaa, alo abe megbenya na fiase si le internet dzi to Xero alo WooCommerce ƒe ƒoƒo ɖekae dzi.

**Browser-based.** No install le asisi alo asitsala ƒe akpa dzi o. ZGo zɔna le egbegbe browser ɖesiaɖe me le kɔmpiuta alo asitelefon dzi.

**Gakotoku ƒe sɔsɔ.** Zcash gakotoku gãwo, siwo dome esiwo doa alɔ Orchard, Sapling, kple Transparent adrɛs ƒomeviwo hã le, ateŋu axe ZGo ƒe adzɔxegbalẽvi ɖoɖo bubu aɖeke manɔmee le asisi ƒe akpa dzi.

**Nuwɔwɔwo.** Ðekawɔwɔ tẽ kple Xero (akɔntabubu) kple WooCommerce (e-asitsatsa) ƒoa nu tso asitsalawo ƒe dɔwɔwɔ eve siwo bɔ wu ŋu le aɖaka me.

## Vodada siwo bɔ

**Abu nudɔdɔa abe alesi woxee hafi woɖo kpe edzi atɔ̃ ene.** Nyadzɔdzɔgblɔmɔ̃ ƒe asitsatsa mesɔ kple fexexe si ŋu woɖo kpee o. Asitsatsa la ate ŋu ado kpo kpeɖodzi nana alo aɖɔlii kokoko. Le kpeɖodzi atɔ̃ megbe ko hafi ZGo ka nya ta be fexexea nye mamlɛtɔ, eye ɣemaɣi ko hafi woade dzesi nudɔdɔa be woxee le anyime. Ne asitsala aɖe ɖo nudzraɖoƒe alo emevava be wòaʋã le nyadzɔdzɔwo kaka ƒe nudzɔdzɔa me la, fexexe le ametafatafa alo esi do kpo nu ahe nusiwo bu ŋutɔŋutɔ vɛ.

**Afia asi ZGo ɖe asitɔtrɔ ƒe gadzraɖoƒe ƒe adrɛs dzi.** Edze abe Zcash adrɛs ene, gake asitɔtrɔ ƒe gadzraɖoƒe ƒe adrɛswo dzi kpɔkpɔ le asitɔtrɔa dzi, ke menye asitsala lae o. Gaɖɔliƒea léa safuiawo, si fia be gaɖɔliƒea léa ga la ɖe asi, si ɖua susu si ta wozãa dɔwɔnu si menye nudzraɖoƒe o dzi. Ele be gakotoku ƒe adrɛs si woɖo ɖe ZGo me nanye gakotoku si ƒe nuku nyagbe dzi asitsala la kpɔ ŋusẽ ɖo tẽ.

**ZGo wɔwɔ abe gakotoku ene.** ZGo nye fexexe ƒe ɖoɖowɔla, ke menye gakotoku o. Medzraa safuiwo ɖo, meléa ga si susɔ ɖe eme, alo naa asitsala la zãa ga o. Wobia be Zcash gakotoku si le vovo si le asitsala ƒe dzikpɔkpɔ te hafi axɔ ga si ZGo ɖona ɖe mɔ dzi.

## Nyanuwuwuw

ZGo naa mɔnu asitsalawo be woaxɔ Zcash ƒe fewo evɔ womaɖe asi le vidzikpɔkpɔ ŋu o, womanɔ te ɖe domenɔla aɖe dzi o, eye womaɖe asitsatsa ŋuti nyatakakawo ɖe go le dukɔa ƒe kɔsɔkɔsɔ aɖe dzi o. Ðekawɔwɔ eveawo (Xero kple WooCommerce) ƒo nu tso asitsalawo ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ƒe ɖoɖo siwo bɔ wu ŋu; le nu bubu ɖesiaɖe gome la, woateŋu azã ZGo le eɖokui si tso web-kpɔkplɔ ɖesiaɖe dzi.

Le ɖoɖowɔwɔ gome la, mɔa le kpuie: xɔ Zcash gakotoku, wɔ akɔnta le [zgo.cash](https://zgo.cash/), eye nàdze fexexe ƒe biabiawo wɔwɔ gɔme tẽ alo nàde ƒoƒo ɖekae si sɔ la wò kɔmpiuta dzi.
