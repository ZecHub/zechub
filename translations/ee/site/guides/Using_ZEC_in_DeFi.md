## Nu yeyewo dodo ɖe ŋgɔ ƒo xlã Zcash DeFi 

**Layer 1 Egbɔkpɔnu**

Wole tiatiawɔblɔɖewo me kum fifia be woana DeFi dɔwɔwɔwo nate ŋu awɔ dɔ le Zcash ƒe lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe ɖoɖoa me to Layer 1 si li fifia zazã me. Wowɔ esia ƒe tɔtrɔ aɖe le hadome kple JP Morgan le woƒe dɔwɔƒe ƒe blockchain dzi. Tso NU5 me la, mɔnu aɖe li (TZE) be woatsɔ kekeɖenudɔ sia ƒomevi akpe ɖe Zcash ŋu. 

**zkEVM** ƒe dɔ.

Esia ahe native programmability vɛ na Zcash kple EVM-compatible virtual machine si doa alɔ zero-sidzedze kpeɖodzi akɔntabubu. Esia ana Zcash nakpɔ dzidziɖedzi to dɔwɔlawo ƒe habɔbɔ si me tɔwo to vovo wu dzi eye wòado lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe ɖoɖo si me ame ŋutɔ ƒe nyatakakawo takpɔkpɔ ƒe dɔwɔɖoɖowo kple dzesiwo le ɖe ŋgɔ. Esia ana woate ŋu atsɔe asɔ kple L2 ƒe ameŋunyatakakawo gbɔkpɔnu bubu siwo li fifia. 

ECC ye le ŋgɔ na numekuku si yi edzi le Proof-of-Stake & the Cosmos Interblockchain Communication Protocol ŋu. Wole afɔɖeɖe siwo kplɔe ɖo me tom kpe ɖe Ethereum Merge to PoS ƒe dzidzedzekpɔkpɔ ŋu kple nya ɖesiaɖe si ate ŋu ado mo ɖa. 

**ZSA/UDA ƒe**

Zcash Shielded Assets / User Defined Assets le ŋgɔyiyi me kple ƒuƒoƒo aɖe si tsɔ wo ɖokui na ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu. Le NU5 ƒe ɖoɖowɔɖi ƒe ŋgɔyiyi megbe la, wogogo kutsetse geɖe wu. Mɔnuwo na kakaɖedzimanɔamesi & ame ŋutɔ ƒe cross-chain bridging na nunɔamesi siawo si ana woawɔ dɔ aduadu la le dɔ wɔm fifia. Zcon3 ƒe nuƒoƒo tso esia ŋu ƒe kadodo aɖe le ete.
