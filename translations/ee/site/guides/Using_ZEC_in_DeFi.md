Ðekawɔwɔ sia naa Zcash kpɔa mɔ tẽe be wòakpɔ ga si wotsɔ de asi na amewo to THORChain kadodoa dzi, si wɔnɛ be ezãlawo te ŋu léa dukɔa me tɔ ƒe nɔnɔmewo me ɖe asi evɔ mahiã be woazã domenɔla siwo le teƒe ɖeka o.

[THORChain](https://thorchain.org/) nodes ateŋu alé ŋku ɖe Zcash blockchain ŋu eye woana xɔtuɖoɖowo na cross-chain asitsatsa le ZEC kple blockchain nunɔamesi bubuwo dome.

Ðekawɔwɔ sia naa viɖe geɖe Zcash lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ:

- Mɔɖeɖe ɖe cross-blockchain ƒe gazazã ŋu;
- ZEC asitsatsa evɔ meɖoa ŋu ɖe asitsaƒe siwo le titina ŋu o;
- Dɔwɔwɔ aduadu kple blockchain nunɔamesi bubuwo ƒe dzidziɖedzi;

Wole tiatiawɔblɔɖewo me kum fifia be woana DeFi dɔwɔwɔwo nate ŋu awɔ dɔ le Zcash lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe ɖoɖoa me to Layer 1 si li fifia zazã me. Wowɔ esia ƒe tɔtrɔ aɖe le hadome kple JP Morgan le woƒe dɔwɔƒe ƒe blockchain dzi. Tso NU5, mɔnu aɖe li (TZE) be woatsɔ kekeɖenudɔ sia ƒomevi akpe ɖe Zcash. 

**zkEVM** ƒe dɔ

Esia ahe native programmability vɛ na Zcash kple EVM-compatible virtual machine si doa alɔ zero-sidzedze kpeɖodzi akɔntabubu. Esia ana Zcash nakpɔ dzidziɖedzi to dɔwɔlawo ƒe habɔbɔ si me tɔwo to vovo wu dzi eye wòado lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe ɖoɖo si me ame ŋutɔ ƒe nyatakakawo takpɔkpɔ ƒe dɔwɔɖoɖowo kple dzesiwo le la ɖe ŋgɔ. Esia ana woate ŋu atsɔe asɔ kple L2 ƒe ameŋunyatakakawo gbɔkpɔnu bubu siwo li fifia. 

ECC ye le ŋgɔ na numekuku si yi edzi le Proof-of-Stake & the Cosmos Interblockchain Communication Protocol ŋu ECC. Wole afɔɖeɖe siwo kplɔe ɖo me tom kpe ɖe Ethereum Merge to PoS ƒe dzidzedzekpɔkpɔ ŋu kple nya ɖesiaɖe si ate ŋu ado mo ɖa. 

**ZSA/UDA ƒe**

Zcash Shielded Assets / User Defined Assets le ŋgɔyiyi me kple ƒuƒoƒo aɖe si tsɔ wo ɖokui na ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu. Le NU5 ƒe ɖoɖowɔɖi ƒe ŋgɔyiyi megbe la, wogogo kutsetse geɖe wu.
