# Ledger Ɣaɣla [Shielded] ZEC Zãla ƒe Mɔfia

> Lé, xɔ eye dɔ ɣaɣla (ɣaɣla) ZEC kple wò Ledger asikplɔwo dzra mɔ le Ledger Wallet Desktop me.

## Nu si le eme kpuie

* Ɣe 23 September 2026 dzi, Ledger Wallet Desktop doa alɔ ɣaɣla [shielded] ZEC. Zcash account ɖeka si ƒe nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi [transparent] balance kple asitsotso ƒe balance le Ironwood gaƒuƒoƒo me.
* Desktop ko wozã fifia. Woɖe gbe be mobile ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu "va kpɔe tso ɣeyiɣi kpui me".
* Ele be wòanye kple Nano S Plus, Nano X, Stax alo Flex (menye Nano S gbedoxɔtɔ la o), Ledger Wallet Desktop 4.21+ kple Zcash app yeyetɔ.
* Zi **Ɖo ɣaɣla dzi** [Enable shielded] zi ɖeka: wò Ledger doa nukpɔsafui [viewing key] ɖe go ale be desktop app la nakpɔ wò asitsotsowo. Gazazãsafui [spending key]wo medoa mɔkeke la dzi kpɔ o.
* Xɔe ɖe asitsotso ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi (u1...) dzi ne wòate ŋu. Ɖe ZEC tso wò nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi balance me yi asitsotso me la ate ŋu awɔ dɔ, gake ekɔ ɖe eveawo.
* Woate ŋu akpɔ gaɖoɖo ɖe sia ɖe dzi eye woaɖe asi ɖe eŋu le Ledger screen dzi. Kpɔ gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo ɖe afi ma, agbalẽ ƒe dzesi ɖe sia ɖe.

## Gɔmeɖeɖe Vevi [Core Explanation]

Zcash ƒe balance ƒomevi eve le. Nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi [transparent] ZEC (t1 gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo) wɔa dɔ abe Bitcoin ene: Ga ƒe home kple gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo le dukɔ ƒe ŋkume. Ɣaɣla [shielded] ZEC nɔa ɣaɣla gaƒuƒoƒo [pool] me, afi si ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ [encryption] le ame si dɔe, ame si xɔe kple ga ƒe home la dzi ɖoɖonu dzi [on-chain].

Ledger dzra wò gazazãsafuiwo ɖe asikplɔ dzi. Be nàfia ɣaɣla balance, Ledger Wallet Desktop hia Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK). Asikplɔ la ɖe e go zi ɖeka, eye desktop app la tsɔe le gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒo ƒe ɖoɖonu la me di wò ɣaɣla didienuwo. Nukpɔsafui [viewing key] ate ŋu akpɔ wò ɣaɣla ŋutinya, gake mate ŋu azã ga o, eyata nàbu e be enye nya si wòle ŋutɔ.

Afɔku siwo gbɔna la ƒe nuye nye be wòaɖo Zcash app la, nàtsɔ account aɖe akpe ɖe eŋu, nàna ɣaɣla [private] balance la nadɔ, nàxɔ ga, nàɣaɣla nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi ZEC eye nàdo ga tso ɣaɣla balance la me.

## Nukpɔkpɔ gbogblo

Le September 23, 2026 la, Ledger tsɔ ɣaɣla ZEC si wòle eŋutɔ me kpe ɖe Ledger Wallet Desktop ŋu. Azɔ Zcash ƒe akɔnt ta ɖeka me le nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi ƒe balance kple ame ŋutɔ ƒe balance le Zcash ƒe Ironwood gaƒuƒoƒo [pool] me. Wò ame ŋutɔ ƒe sarɔ̃wo nɔ anyi le wò Ledger dzi, eye woɖea gaɖoɖo [transaction] ɖe asi, dea kpɔe, eye wɔnɔa dzesi le mɔkeke la dzi.

**Desktop koe.** Ledger ƒe gbeɖeɖe gblɔ be ɣaɣla ZEC "le ŋuɖoɖo na Ledger Wallet Desktop koe. Mobile ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu mele o le ɣeyiɣi sia me, gake edzɔna kpɔ." Desktop app la hã fia nyatakaka ma. Woɖe kpɔ le October 15, 2026.

## Dzadzraɖo

- Ledger mɔkeke si wɔa Zcash app la: Nano S Plus, Nano X, Stax alo Flex. Zcash app yeyetɔ la womede ɖe Nano S gbedoxɔtɔ la ta o.
- Ledger Wallet Desktop 4.21 alo esia wu, si wòkplɔ tso [ledger.com](https://www.ledger.com) ko. Mɔfia sia zã version 4.24.0.
- Zcash app yeyetɔ le wò Ledger dzi (3.9.4 le ɣeyiɣi si woŋlɔ mɔfia sia).
- ZEC sue aɖe si wòatsɔ ado akpɔe. Woɖe afɔ siwo katã le ete la wɔ le mainnet dzi kple 0.001 ZEC. Gaɖoɖo ɖe sia ɖe xɔ network ƒe adzɔ [fee] sue aɖe (0.00015 ZEC le míaƒe dodokpɔ me).
- Wò nya 24 si tsɔa ŋkuɖoɖo gbɔ la nɔ anyi le agbalẽ dzi. Mègblɔe na komputer alo mẹmae na ame aɖeke o.

## Afɔ le Afɔ ƒe Mɔfia (Ledger Wallet Desktop)

**1. Ðo alo le Zcash app la ɖe yeyetɔ me**
- Ʋu Ledger Wallet, tsɔ wò Ledger kpe ɖe eŋu eye nàʋu eƒe mɔ, eye nàʋu **My Ledger**. Na mɔkeke la naɖo kpeɖeŋu la dzi.
- Di **Zcash** le app ƒe agbalẽ me, eye nàzi **Install**, alo **Update** ne woɖoe xoxo.
- Ʋu Zcash app la le wò Ledger dzi. Screen la gblɔ be "Zcash app la le dzadzraɖo". Yi ɖe mia me va **App info** be nàkpɔ version la.

**2. Kpe Zcash akɔnt aɖe ɖe eŋu**
- Le Ledger Wallet me la, tia **Add account**. Le **Select asset** me la, di Zcash eye nàtia **Zcash (ZEC)**, menye token si woɖe kuɖe eŋu abe Binance-Peg Zcash Token ene o.
- Ne Zcash app la le ʋuʋu me le wò Ledger dzi la, Ledger Wallet afɔ wò akɔnt. De dzesi le eŋu le **Select the accounts you want to add** te, eye nàzi **Confirm**.
- Akɔnt ƒe axa la fia **Available balance**, **Transparent balance**, kple **Private balance**, kpe ɖe **Enable shielded** ƒe button aɖe ŋu.

![Zcash account in Ledger Wallet Desktop showing the desktop-only notice, transparent and private balances, and the Enable shielded button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-01-account-desktop-only.png)

**3. Na ame ŋutɔ ƒe balance nava wɔ dɔ**
- Zi **Enable shielded**. **Enable Zcash private balance** ƒe fesiã la me afɔ ene le: Birthday, UFVK, Device kple Confirmation.
- **Birthday:** Na akɔnt yeye la, wɔ birthday ƒe kɔkɔƒe le egbe ƒe ŋkeke dzi. De dzesi le "This account received shielded ZEC in the past" dzi ko ne ènya be ewɔe kpɔ, elabena scanning tso gaɖoɖowo ƒuƒoƒe [block] 0 me ate ŋu axɔ ɣeyiɣi didi.
- **UFVK kple Device:** Ledger Wallet bia tso wò Ledger be wòado wò Unified Full Viewing Key (UFVK) ɖe go. Xlẽ nu siwo le mɔkeke la ƒe screen dzi eye nàɖe mɔ na e. Ne èkpɔ "Device connection lost" la, kpɔ be Zcash app la le ʋuʋu me, ɖe mɔe enumake, eye nàzi **Retry**.
- Ne èkpɔ **UFVK successfully imported** la, zi **Start sync**. Ne sync la wu la, button la atrɔ ava **Update balance** kple "Last sync" ƒe ɣeyiɣi.
- Nukpɔsafui [viewing key] la nɔ anyi le wò komputer dzi. Ete ŋu afɔ wò ɣaɣla ƒe ga, gake mate ŋu aɖe wo o.

![Enable Zcash private balance window showing UFVK successfully imported and the Start sync button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-02-ufvk-imported.png)

**4. Xɔ ZEC eye nàɖe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesiwo siaa kpɔ le wò Ledger dzi**
- Zi **Receive** eye nàtia wò Zcash akɔnt. Ledger Wallet fia **Address for Zcash 1** (nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi, si dzea egɔme kple t1) kple **Private address for Zcash 1** (si dzea egɔme kple u1). Gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi xoxowo gale wɔ dɔ.
- Wò Ledger fia "Verify Zcash addresses", **Private address** la le axa eve me, eye emegbe la **Public Address**. Sɔ srɔ̃dzesi siaa kple screen la hafi nàma gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi aɖe ɖo, eye nàɖe mɔe le mɔkeke la dzi.
- Tia ame ŋutɔ ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi; zã nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi tɔ ko ne ame si wòle ga dɔm na la meɖo kpekpeɖeŋu bubu aɖeke o.

![Ledger Wallet Receive window showing the transparent address and the private address for Zcash 1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-03-receive-addresses.png)

![Ledger device screens: Verify Zcash addresses, Private address in two pages, then Public Address](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-04-device-verify-addresses.png)

**5. Tsɔ nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi ƒe ZEC yi wò ame ŋutɔ ƒe balance me**
- Zi **Send**. Le **Self transfer** te la, tia **Send to private balance**.
- Ŋlɔ gaƒoƒo aɖe alo zã 25%, 50%, 75% alo **Max**. Kpɔ network ƒe adzɔ la, eye nàzi **Review**.
- Le wò Ledger dzi la, to eme ɖe: "Review transaction to send ZEC", "Transfer from public to private address", gaƒoƒo la, gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi si woɖo ɖe go le axa eve me, adzɔ la, kple expiry height la. Kpɔ be gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi si woɖo ɖe go la sɔ kple wò ame ŋutɔ ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi si le afɔ 4 me, eye nàzi buttons evea le **Sign transaction** dzi.
- Ledger Wallet fia **Transaction sent**. Le gaɖoɖo ƒe nyatakakawo me la, type la gblɔ be **Private (Ironwood)**.
- Ledger Wallet gblɔ be ame ŋutɔ ƒe transfer dea ka wò ɣaɣla ƒe ga kple wò dukɔ ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi. Ne èxɔ ga tẽe ɖe wò ame ŋutɔ ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi me la, womaka wo ɖe eŋu o.

![Send ZEC window in Ledger Wallet with the Self transfer option Send to private balance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-05-send-to-private-balance.png)

![Ledger device review screens for moving ZEC from the transparent balance to the private address, ending with Sign transaction and Transaction signed](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-06-device-review-shielding.png)

- Ne gaɖoɖo la ɖe nyateƒe la, nuwo ƒe ʋuʋuɖi ƒe balance la adzi ɖe anyi eye ame ŋutɔ ƒe balance la ayi dzi. Ɣaɣla ƒe ga yeyewo doa ɖe "maturing" me va se ɖe esime woakpɔ confirmations si de wɔnɔ ɖe wo.

![Zcash account after shielding: transparent balance reduced, private balance increased and shown as maturing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-07-balances-after-shielding.png)

**6. Ðo ZEC tso wò ame ŋutɔ ƒe balance me**
- Ne ame ŋutɔ ƒe balance la te ŋu adɔ ga la, zi **Send** eye nàɖo **Transfer from** ɖe **Private balance**.
- Ðe ame si wòle ga dɔm nɛ la ƒe gaɖoƒe ƒe dzesi ɖe eme eye nàŋlɔ gaƒoƒo la. Àte ŋu akpe ɣaɣlaŋlɔŋlɔ [encryption] ƒe **Memo (optional)** si mele bytes 512 wu o ɖe eŋu, si ame si wòle ga dɔm nɛ la ko ate ŋu axlẽ.
- Kpɔ ame si wòle ga dɔm nɛ la, gaƒoƒo la kple adzɔ la le wò Ledger dzi, eye nàzi buttons evea le **Sign transaction** dzi.

![Sent transaction from the private balance in Ledger Wallet transaction details](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZecHub/zechub/main/site/guides/assets/ledger-zec-08-private-send.png)

## Nu Si Wòele Be Nànya

- Ɣaɣla ƒe ZEC le Ledger dzi le desktop koe fifia (woɖe kpɔ le October 15, 2026).
- Ledger Wallet ƒe ame ŋutɔ ƒe balance zãa Ironwood gaƒuƒoƒo.
- Ledger gblɔ be àte ŋu ama wò gaɖoɖo ƒe xexlẽme ƒe nukpɔ ko ƒe mɔ na tax advisor. Ame sia ame si si wò nukpɔsafui le la ate ŋu akpɔ wò ɣaɣla ƒe xexlẽme, eya ta màe na ame aɖeke negbe ko o.
- Woɖe mɔkeke ƒe screens siwo le mɔfia sia me la kple Ledger ƒe Speculos emulator (Nano S Plus), si wɔa Zcash app 3.9.4 eye wokpe ɖe Ledger Wallet Desktop 4.24.0 ŋu le Zcash mainnet dzi. Ledger si nye mɔkeke ŋutɔ hã wɔa app ma eye efia screens mawo.
