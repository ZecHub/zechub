**Aleke Nàdze Livestream Gɔme**

Zi wòƒe profile ƒe icon dzi, eye dropdown ƒe list aɖe ava do go. Tia "Stream" eye ele be nàkpɔ livestream ƒe tiatia ene. Tia ɖeko esi dze wòƒe hiahiãwo nyuie eye nàzi start stream ƒe button la dzi. Ɖe mɔ na Free2Z be wòazã wòƒe camera kple microphone. Free2Z bia tso asiwò be nàŋlɔ nickname si nèlɔ̃ be nàzã le live stream la me. Emegbe la, zi Join ƒe button la dzi be nàdze live stream la gɔme. Wòƒe subscribers ate ŋu akpɔ live stream la ƒe xɔa me. Àte ŋu akɔpi live stream la ƒe URL tso wòƒe browser me eye nàmae na xɔlɔ̃wo be woakpɔe ƒe xɔa me.
