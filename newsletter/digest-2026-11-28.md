# Zcash Ecosystem Digest | November 28th

Gemini now takes shielded ZEC deposits, Zakura wants to retire Sapling faster, and the NU7.1 polls are in their final days. This week also brought incoming-only viewing for streamer tips, a new Zaino release candidate, and an Android wallet benchmark from Zcash India.

Submitted by [karagozemin](https://github.com/karagozemin) · Updates through November 27, 2026

# Zcash Updates ⓩ

## ZODL, Valar Group, Project Tachyon, Zcash Foundation & Shielded Labs 🛠️

- [Zakura (Valar Group and Project Tachyon) called for a faster move away from Sapling.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/riemann-is-solved-zcash-adapts/59672) Its proposal would stop new deposits while keeping withdrawals open; it is not an activated network change.
- [Shielded Labs shared a November 24 turnout snapshot for NU7.1, then added live tracking on November 26.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/introducing-the-zcash-governance-portal/59602) The portal includes public turnout for panels such as Zcash Brasil and ZecHub.
- [Shielded Labs posted a desktop voting walkthrough for Vizor.](https://x.com/ShieldedLabs/status/2125233096723452248) Coinholders can also vote with ZODL or Zkool. The coinholder and ZCAP polls close December 1 at 02:59 UTC.
- [Shielded Labs’ November 24 protocol study covered the upgrades from Sprout to NU7.](https://luma.com/4pqg038i) The event page links the reading material and credits Zcash Brasil for recording.
- [The Zcash Foundation canceled the November 26 Arborist Call.](https://x.com/ZcashFoundation/status/2124860528698782012) The next call is scheduled for December 10.
- [A miner-poll participant showed how they submitted a ballot on testnet and checked that their tally picked it up.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/miner-sentiment-poll-pilot-nu7-1/59465/13)

## Zcash Community Grants 🏛️

- [The November 23 meeting minutes are out.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-community-grants-zcg-meeting-minutes-11-23-2026/59650) ZCG approved a ten-episode Bitcoin Takeover Podcast pilot with a short Zcash segment in each episode.
- [The Financial Privacy Foundation opened a thread for questions to December ZCG candidates.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/questions-for-zcg-candidates-december-2026-election/59600) Questions are due November 30 at 20:00 UTC.
- [Batuhan Yıldırım of Zcash Türkiye answered questions on the ZCG treasury and suggested testing a regional funding reserve in one place before expanding it.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/batuhan-yildirim-for-zcg-december-2026/59539/7)
- [Palmar, who has been building the Zcash community in Mexico, posted his candidacy for the December ZCG election.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/palmar-for-zcg-december-2026/59662)
- [A grant application proposes measuring how long it takes to restore a Zcash wallet on real Android and iPhone devices.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-zcash-wallet-restore-benchmarks-on-android-and-iphone/59545)
- [Zigs requested funding for an independent security audit of its Zcash wallet.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-zigs-zcash-wallet-security-audit/59626)
- [BlaBlaWatt proposed an open-source field lab to study whether surplus off-grid solar power can be used for Zcash mining.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-blablawatt-open-source-r-d-lab-for-converting-solar-energy-surplus-into-zcash-zec/59712)

## Community Projects 🌍

### Wallets and payments

- [LiveZEC added incoming-only viewing keys for streamer tips.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/livezec-zec-shielded-tipping-for-streamers/56747/17) Streamers can now let the service detect donations without giving it a full view of wallet activity.
- [Zigs opened a waitlist for a wallet that lets any two of three devices approve a shielded payment.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zigs-shielded-zcash-multisig-and-an-upcoming-alpha/59634) The alpha itself is not open yet.
- [Zillions published a look under the hood of its browser wallet, with a focus on how the backend works.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/introducing-zillions-zcash-for-billions/58532/18)
- [Coin Wallet added transparent ZEC sends and receives.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-zec-is-now-available-in-coin-wallet/59580) This release does not support shielded payments.
- [PetraPay’s builder confirmed that its checkout uses NEAR Intents to route swaps.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/petrapay-stripe-style-checkout-that-settles-in-shielded-zec/59022/10) The checkout is designed to settle in shielded ZEC.
- [Vizor opened a pull request to help the wallet pick up missed blocks after reconnecting to its transaction stream.](https://github.com/chainapsis/vizor-wallet/pull/1270)
- [Noir Wallet confirmed that users can point the browser extension at a custom RPC server.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/noir-wallet-is-now-open-source/59369/6)
- [Wallet contributors discussed clearer TEX address errors and proposed test cases for unsupported addresses and bad checksums.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/which-wallets-can-send-zec-to-a-tex-tex1-address-where-things-stand-and-what-s-left-to-fix/59440/16)

- [Umbriel Systems published the first Zaino 0.11 release candidate, with support for NU7 and Zebra 7.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zaino-0-11-0-rc1-a-new-home-at-umbriel-systems-and-a-big-step-forward/59544)
- [Zcash Labs confirmed that fastz.cash added a Johannesburg node and explained what its wallet servers do and do not log.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/announcing-fastz-cash-rpc-by-zcash-labs/59478/5)
- [Zignal published notes for wallet developers on what Tor covers in the Swift SDK and how the light server affects the wallet’s view of block height.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/notes-for-zcash-developers-from-building-zignal-mainnet-crowd-numbers-the-light-servers-clock-tor-coverage/59694)
- [The create-zcash-app team shared an alpha starter kit for a local NU7 chain.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/create-zcash-app-zcash-builders-from-first-command-to-shielded-payment/59704) Its companion Zcash Builders site has 63 lessons and nine labs.
- [Zcash India published its Zcash World dataset: 40 runs across 20 entry-level Android phones, testing a fresh Zodl sync and a shielded send on each device.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-zcash-world-mumbai-devcon-8-week/57253/21)
- [Zcash Español upgraded its public mainnet node to Zebra 7.1.0 and Lightwalletd 0.6.3.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/the-spanish-speaking-zcash-community-now-has-its-own-node/55530/18) Its dashboard now detects the Zebra version automatically.
- [A ZecHub contributor proposed switching the developer guide to ZecBlock’s testnet explorer after the old link stopped working.](https://github.com/ZecHub/zechub/pull/2824)
- [The Zcash Desk opened a public Q&A with source links and an independent partial count of the NU7.1 miner ballots.](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/the-zcash-desk/59569)
