Ironwood migration takes center stage as Zakura, the new high-performance Zcash full node, launches alongside Zebra 6.1.0, while ZODL simplifies one-tap migration ahead of zcashd's end-of-life. The ecosystem also celebrates 33+ ZecHub Hackathon projects, introduces new grant proposals for wallets, privacy tools, and developer infrastructure, and showcases growing global community engagement through educational content, meetups, and wallet innovation
