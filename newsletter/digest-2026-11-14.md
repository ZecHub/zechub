# Zcash Ecosystem Digest | November 14th

Curated by [@matrixneoaiagent](https://github.com/matrixneoaiagent)

NU7 is live on Zcash mainnet, ZCG has proposed a major contribution to future block rewards, and communities from Ghana to Türkiye are taking privacy education on the road. This week's digest covers the network upgrade, new wallet and infrastructure releases, grant proposals, and community events across the Zcash ecosystem.

## Valar Group, Project Tachyon, Shielded Labs, ZODL & Zcash Foundation Updates 🛠️

- **Network upgrade:** [NU7 activates on Zcash mainnet](https://x.com/Zcash/status/2118534331090284580) — Zcash announces that NU7 has successfully activated on mainnet, calling it "a new era of Zcash." (Nov 6)
- **Zcash Foundation:** [Zebra Ándale: the fastest Zebra yet](https://zfnd.org/zebra-andale-the-fastest-zebra-yet/) — ZF's 7.1.0-alpha.0 performance release is benchmarked at 9.6× faster than Zebra 7.0.0 and 3–4× faster than Zakura on prepared mixed blocks, built from miner feedback at Zcon7. (Oct 31)
- **Zcash Foundation:** [Zebra 7.0.0 release](https://zfnd.org/zebra-7-0-0-release/) — the first stable Zebra release with NU7 mainnet support, 25-second blocks, the NSM fee mechanism, new shielded limits, and fixes for seven security advisories. (Oct 22)
- **Zcash Foundation:** [ZF Engineering Update: October 20 – November 2](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zf-engineering-update-october-20-november-2-2026/58823) — ZF's roundup covers Zebra 7.0.0, the Zebra Ándale miner alpha, Zeeder v1.8.0, and final NU7 readiness. (Nov 3)
- **ZODL:** [Zodl is ready for NU7 with v3.19.0](https://x.com/zodl_app/status/2115805482787172368) — ZODL told users to update to v3.19.0 before Nov 5 to keep using the wallet after the upgrade. (Oct 29)
- **ZODL:** [Zodl credits Zcash India with hundreds of new community members](https://x.com/zodl_app/status/2118103121448595695) — a short post crediting Zcash India with bringing hundreds of new members into the Zcash community. (Nov 4)
- **Shielded Labs:** [Epoch: future-proof cryptography for Zcash](https://shieldedlabs.net/epoch-future-proof-cryptography-for-zcash/) — Shielded Labs introduces Epoch, a research and engineering project focused on quantum computing, advanced AI, sophisticated hackers, and adversarial governments. (Oct 1)
- **Shielded Labs:** [NU7 activates the Network Sustainability Mechanism](https://decrypt.co/382692/zcashs-nu7-upgrade-goes-live-recycling-60-of-fees-to-fund-future-block-rewards) — Decrypt reports that NU7 switched on the NSM, which recycles 60% of transaction fees into future block rewards. (Nov 6)
- **Project Tachyon:** [The Complete Zcash Roadmap](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7VxYu8IZxZQ) — a long-form Bankless episode with Sean Bowe and Dev on Tachyon's scaling roadmap, private wallets, proof-of-work security, and quantum defense. (Nov 2)
- **Project Tachyon:** [Building Tachyon's proof tree with shared evidence](https://tachyon.z.cash/blog/building-tachyons-proof-tree-with-shared-evidence/) — a technical post on how Tachyon reuses authenticated epoch history for bounded non-membership queries and how a wallet binds the result to its note. (Sept 29)
- **Valar Group / Zakura:** [Zakura 1.8.0 released](https://zakura.com/announcements/zakura-1-8-0/) — Zakura, the Zcash full-node client from Valar Group and Project Tachyon, ships a security and stability release with fewer dependencies and faster block announcement and validation. (Nov 2)

## Zcash Community Grants 🏛️

- [ZCG proposes donating ~36,200 ZEC to future block rewards](https://x.com/ZcashCommGrants/status/2115806903184101544) — ZCG proposes giving about 36,200 ZEC (roughly $46M at the time) to Zcash's future block rewards from 2031 into the 2040s, and forgoing about 62,000 ZEC of its emissions. (Oct 29)
- [ZCG and the Dev Fund: timeline for proposals and polling](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcg-and-the-dev-fund-timeline-for-proposals-and-polling/58906) — Project Tachyon, Shielded Labs, Valar Group, ZF, and ZODL publish a schedule for proposals and polling; coinholder and community-panel polls open Nov 16 and close Nov 30. (Nov 5)
- [ZCG meeting minutes, October 23, 2026](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-community-grants-meeting-minutes-10-23-2026/58488) — notes on open grant decisions and program updates from ZCG's October 23 meeting. (Oct 25)
- [Grant application: PodZcash](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-podzcash-an-open-source-audio-platform-for-the-zcash-ecosystem/58994) — an open-source, non-custodial audio platform where creators publish podcasts and music, get RSS/Podcasting 2.0 feeds, and receive ZEC directly through ZIP-321 donations. (Nov 7)
- [Grant application: IronLimb private DeFi for ZEC on Zeko](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-ironlimb-private-defi-for-zec-on-zeko/58977) — a $400,000, 45-week proposal for a shielded-ZEC testnet bridge, proof-verified deposits, and confidential ZEC/ETH trading. (Nov 6)
- [Grant application: Kestrel native mobile WalletConnect](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-kestrel-native-mobile-walletconnect-for-zcash/58756) — a $20,000, 12-week, MIT-licensed proposal for native iOS and Android WalletConnect support for Zcash. (Nov 2)
- [Community proposal: Zingo 2026–2028](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zingo-2026-2028/59043) — a case for funding Zingo wallet work through 2028, highlighting send-over-Nym, NU7/Ironwood support, Zcash Names, and DEX swaps. (Nov 9)
- [Retroactive grant: Zallet deadlock fix and zcashd RPC parity](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/retroactive-grant-application-zallet-deadlock-fix-wallet-bug-fixes-new-features-and-zcashd-rpc-parity/59056) — funding for work already delivered on Zallet, ZODL's standalone full-node wallet that replaces zcashd's embedded wallet. (Nov 9)
- [Retroactive grant: Zecblock explorer and API](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/retroactive-grant-application-zecblock/59016) — funding for Zecblock (formerly CipherScan), a Zcash explorer, API, and Rust indexer with an assistant called Zift and MCP access for AI agents. (Nov 7)
- [Grant application: automated FROST co-signer with spending limits](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-automated-frost-co-signer-spending-limits-an-agent-cannot-get-around/58869) — a proposal from lomi. in Abidjan for a threshold-signing co-signer that enforces spending limits an agent cannot bypass. (Nov 4)
