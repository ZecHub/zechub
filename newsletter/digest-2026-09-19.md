## Zcash at a glance

This week, the Zcash ecosystem moved from governance discussion toward concrete implementation timelines. The biggest protocol update was the publication of the **NU7 activation timeline**: testnet activation is planned for **October 6, 2026**, with mainnet activation planned for **November 5, 2026**. The upgrade includes 25-second block spacing, disabling v4 transactions, and NSM integration, while preserving halvings with reintroduction beginning in February 2031.

The week also brought a new round of community funding activity, new wallet and infrastructure work, and continued growth from regional communities and independent builders.

## 1. Valar Group, Project Tachyon, Shielded Labs, ZODL & Zcash Foundation Updates

### NU7 now has a public activation timeline

The Zcash ecosystem published a coordinated NU7 timeline after discussions among Zcash Foundation, Project Tachyon, Valar Group, ZODL, Shielded Labs, Zebra, and Zakura teams.

- **Testnet activation:** October 6, 2026
- **Code completion target:** September 30, 2026
- **Final mainnet activation decision:** October 20, 2026
- **Mainnet activation:** November 5, 2026

The upgrade is expected to affect infrastructure such as full nodes, indexers, and block explorers more directly than ordinary wallets.

[NU7 Timeline](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/nu7-timeline/57655)

### NU7 sentiment results

The Zcash Community Forum published the results of the NU7 sentiment polls. The results included input from ZCAP, coinholders, and regional/community panels. The discussion also records alignment among Shielded Labs, Project Tachyon, ZODL, Zcash Foundation, and Valar Group around February 2031 for the reintroduction of new issuance under the selected interpretation of the polling results.

[NU7 Sentiment Poll Results](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/nu7-sentiment-polls-results/57590)

### Project Tachyon

Project Tachyon continues development of a proposed Zcash upgrade focused on scaling shielded payments and reducing dependencies between components. Its roadmap includes Ragu, new payment-delivery approaches using PIR and post-quantum key exchange, and a redesigned shielded protocol intended for a future test network.

[Project Tachyon Roadmap](https://tachyon.z.cash/roadmap/)

### Shielded Labs governance information

Shielded Labs' governance page documents the Q3 Coinholder-Directed Retroactive Grants Program timeline. The current Q3 coinholder poll runs from **September 17 to September 29, 2026**.

## 7. Why this week matters

Three themes stand out:

1. **NU7 is moving toward implementation.** The ecosystem now has explicit testnet and mainnet dates, giving wallet, node, indexer, and explorer developers concrete milestones to work against.

2. **Funding is becoming more visible and participatory.** Q3 CDRGP voting is live, while new proposals and resubmissions continue to cover wallets, security, infrastructure, education, local adoption, and developer tooling.

3. **The ecosystem keeps expanding at the community level.** Regional communities in Africa, Europe, Latin America, Türkiye, and elsewhere are continuing education, developer, governance, and adoption work while independent builders ship wallets, payment tools, marketplaces, infrastructure, and privacy applications.
