## Italian writing rules to confirm

These are not in the tables above. Write OK, or your correction, after each rule.

1. Sentence case in headings and terms, not English Title Case ("Configurazione fidata", not "Configurazione Fidata"). → 
2. "uno/gli" before z-, s+consonant, ps-, gn- (uno zk-SNARK, gli ZEC). → 
3. Italian word order around names ("la documentazione di Valar Group", not "Valar Group documentazione"). → 
4. English terms kept in Italian take no plural -s (i wallet, gli zk-SNARK). → 
5. Lowercase after a colon, unless a proper name follows. → 
6. Calques to avoid: "una delle uniche" (= one of the only), "con nessuna … richiesta", "componenti mobili" (for moving parts). Add more if you see them. →
